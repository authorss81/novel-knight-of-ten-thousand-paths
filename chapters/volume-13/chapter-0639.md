# Chapter 0639

**Thawmonth the twenty-third, YR 321, a Friday and not a market morning, the third hour at the boards in a lane about four hundred paces long, the ninth of nine days, a copyist who will not put a name in a book, and a chair with nobody in it at the fourth hour.**

The ninth of the nine days was a Friday and the room was shut and the badge-man stood in the open at about the third hour and read the three clauses out twice for anybody who wanted to hear them, which is what he has done at the first of every month for thirty-one years, and four people wanted to hear them and five did not. Aren Kest was at the low end of the boards with his stick for the whole of it and was standing in a lane about four hundred paces long with nine people in it and none of them was talking to him.

Then they tried to find out which road had gone quiet, and they tried for about two hours, and the whole of what they got is this.

They tried it in order. The man of about thirty-one from a road office offered a rider inside four days to any of seven roads in nine counties. The copyist of about twenty-six told him not to and gave the reason again, and the reason was the same reason she gave the day before and she did not soften it because it was the second time.

“**You know seven and you know that one of them has gone quiet and there is nothing in nine counties that joins those two facts, and there is not going to be one, and if I were to help you make it there would be, and then I would have made it, and I would have made it out of a guess.**”

Then they went at it from the other end, which was the copyist's own position, and she answered four or five people in about nine sentences and she said at the start that she had been asked about this four times in two days and was going to answer it once.

“**He said a word in a lane. I did not write it down. I would not have written it down either, and not because it was a secret or because anybody asked me to leave it out. If I had written down the name of a road because a man standing on wet boards said a word to me, then the only thing anybody in nine counties would have about that road would be a line in a book, and a line is a thing you can put beside another line. That is the whole of what I do here and I have done it for four years and a half and I have never been able to do it better than that and I am not going to start on a Friday morning because it would be useful.**”

Nobody told her she was wrong. The badge-man told her so twice, and the second time he said it in a different way.

“**I have spent thirty-one years telling rooms that there is no head in nine counties and that a rule needs one, and I have just said to about nine people that the standing which cost a road its address is the reason a man of about fifty-two is not in this lane on the ninth of nine days. Both of those are true. I have been waiting about thirty-one years to pay a bill in that shape and I want it observed that it has arrived on a Friday, in the open, and that nobody has been cruel to anybody, and that a road has gone quiet anyway.**”

The man of about forty-seven said the thing that the other three clauses cannot be got round, and he said it without any pleasure at all and he was the ninth person to speak and he had not spoken for about four days.

“**Then we will write a rule about roads that can be written to and we will hand it out in nine hands in a market town of about two thousand people, and about four months from now a man will shut a road that nobody can write to and he will break every clause of it and he will break them lawfully, and he will not have heard of any of us. That is what a rule that cannot reach a road is. It is not a small hole. It is the shape of the thing.**”

“**It is what you told us on the fourth day,**” the woman of about thirty-six said, **“**and you were right then as well.**”

“**I have been right five times,**” the man of about forty-seven said, “**and I would like somebody to be allowed to be angry with me about it in about four months rather than told about it now. Write the rule. It is worth more than nothing and it is not worth what we wanted.**”

Nobody thanked him.

At about the fourth hour the badge-man looked at the chair and the chair was empty and he said nothing about it at all, and about four people in that lane had already looked at it and about five had not, and that was the whole of what happened about the chair on the ninth day.

Then the copyist gave the clauses back out, which she had not done before and had said she would not do, and she gave one piece of paper to each of nine hands and she did not keep one.

The handover took about nine minutes and nobody made a speech about it, which is what she had asked for in a room on the second morning and which four of the nine people found difficult and one of them said so.

“**I have been handed things in rooms for about thirty years,**” the man of about fifty-eight with a badge said, “**and I have never once been handed one and not known what I was supposed to do with it. Put your hand on the table. That is all. Nobody has to have read it and nobody has to have agreed with it and nobody has to bring it back on the ninth of the next month. That is the arrangement and it is thinner than a book and it is the first arrangement in nine counties that nine people can be argued with.**”

“**I am not holding a copy and I am not going to be asked to,**” she said. “**I have been asked four times in two days whether I would keep the ninth one and the answer is that there are nine and not ten. That is the entire point of what was agreed on Saturday and anybody who takes a tenth piece of paper out of this lane has taken the thing out of it.**”

The man of about thirty-four with a sheet took his piece, put it inside the satchel, and did not open it again, and he said that he was not going to fold it and he was not going to write anything else on it, because a thing that goes down that lane in a coat in a satchel is worth exactly as much as the words on it and not one mark more.

Then she said the other thing, and she said it standing at the boards in the open, and it is the sentence the ninth day is built on.

“**A rule is not a decision. That is the whole difference and it is worth a lane about four hundred paces long. A decision has nowhere to be challenged. Nine of you have each got a piece of paper and every one of you can be asked about it by anybody at any time, including the ninth of you, including me, including the man with the badge. And that is why nine is a good number and it is not because nine is a good number. It is because nine people can all be argued with and I have never in four years and a half had one thing I could be argued with.**”

Nobody thanked her. The badge-man said that he had been in this work thirty-one years wanting a room to hold a thing and that he had been told twice in about a month that the one thing he had been asking it to hold was the one thing it must not hold, and that he now had nine pieces of paper and that this was the first arrangement in nine counties he had ever watched get made that he could not see the end of, and that he did not intend to say anything else about it.

The woman of about thirty-six who keeps a road in the county where a list of names was burned asked the badge-man, at about the sixth hour, whether there was going to be a tenth day, and he said no, and gave the reason in one sentence and it was the reason he had given on the first of the nine.

“**And on the tenth?**” she said.

“**There is no tenth,**” he said.

“**There is no tenth,**” he said. “**That was the arrangement and the arrangement is the only reason there is anything at all. Nine people who can be argued with is an arrangement. Nine people who can be argued with and then nine more and then nine counties is a different thing and I am not going to call it one until it is in front of me.**”

Nobody thanked him and nobody asked him when that would be.

At about the seventh hour the lane emptied and the key went down the outside stair and about nine people went out into the market street and the man of about fifty-two did not come and nobody sent anything to find out whether he was coming.
