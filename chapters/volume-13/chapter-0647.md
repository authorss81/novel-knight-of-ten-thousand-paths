# Chapter 0647

**Greenmonth the seventh, YR 321, a Sunday and not a market morning, about the fourth hour in a market street with a few carts in it, the room over a weigh-house shut behind its own door, and three people who try to mend a rule from the outside and are each refused by a person and not by the meeting.**

The room was shut, because it is shut on every day but a market morning and this was a Sunday, and a Sunday market street in a town of about two thousand people has about four carts in it and about nine people who are not buying, and nine of them who were not buying had come on purpose.

It is the worst-lit street in the county on a Sunday. The shops are shut and the doors are boarded, and the light comes off the far end of it in a long low sheet that does not reach the boards, and the four carts that were in it stood along the wall out of the way with their shafts down and their loads covered, and they were the only tall things in the street and they made the place look like a yard. A dog went through twice. The gutter that a market morning covers ran, because nobody was in the street to stand on it. About the fourth hour the light began to go and it went early, the way it does when there is cloud behind it, and by the sixth hour the far end of the street and the boards at the low end were the same grey.

Nobody holds a meeting on a Sunday anywhere in nine counties. Everybody who came that afternoon had come on their own time and out of their own reason, and it showed, and the badge-man said so in the first minute, standing where the light was not, that nobody was to write down who had come and that the whole of what the three of them were about to try would be worth exactly nothing if a list of the people who had tried it went into a room. Four people agreed with him out loud and about nine agreed with him by not saying anything.

The woman of about twenty-nine who keeps the room said the standing about the door out loud at the top of the stair before anybody asked her, which she did now on any day anybody came.

“**Shut. Not on a Sunday and not on a Monday and not on a Tuesday. Open Thursday and Saturday from the second hour to the seventh, and that is the whole of it, and I am not going to have it opened for about nine people who asked nicely and I am not going to have it opened for about nine people who did not ask. Down here, in the street, where the whole market can hear, which is the arrangement I said I wanted in the lane and have now got.**”

Nobody thanked her.

Then the three attempts, and each of them was made by a person who wanted the rule to be better and not by anybody who wanted it to be worse, and each of them was refused by one person and not voted on.

The first was the man of about thirty-four who keeps a road in the waterworks country, and it was the best of the three and it was the wrong one.

“**Then make it a fixed day. Not a meeting anybody calls, a day. A day of the month when anybody who keeps a road may stand at these boards and be heard, and a road that cannot be written to gets written to by being written to on that day and the next one and the next one, and in about a year there is a line for it in nine counties and it is in from the day somebody first said it.**”

The copyist of about twenty-six refused it in about eleven words and then explained it for about a minute.

“**A fixed day is a notice. It is a notice that has not been printed and it is a notice that does not need anybody to call it, and a thing that happens on a fixed day is a thing that can be missed, and the road you are describing is missed already. And there is a second thing, which is worse, and I have known it for about two years and I have not said it out loud in a street before. I keep three series and none of them is made out of another. That is correct, and it has been correct for about four years and a half, and I would do it again. And it is the reason that two warnings about one channel went into two different series and into two different places and sat about eleven paces from each other in this building for about nine days and nobody could see them both, because the practice that is right is the practice that hid them. A road that gets a line because somebody said it out loud on a fixed day is a line that gets a line. That is not a road, that is a list with a date on it, and I have spent two years knowing that my own good work is the blind spot and I am not going to be talked out of it by about nine people on a Sunday.**”

It was the longest single thing she had ever said in a street, and she had not planned any of it. She had got as far as the fixed day being a notice, which is the answer anybody would have given, and then the rest had come out of her in the order it came out in, and the last part of it had come out last and had not been said in a room either.

Nobody in that market disagreed with her and about four people looked at the ground and five looked at her.

Then the man of about thirty-one from a road office made the second attempt, which was a second set of books, and the copyist refused it in one sentence and would not say anything else about it.

“**Who would hold the second set?**” the badge-man said, and the man of about thirty-one from a road office said that a road office would hold it, and the copyist said, “**No second set of books in nine counties. Not yours and not anybody's, and I have just told a market street why, and you were standing at the low end of the boards when I told it.**”

Then the woman of about thirty-four who came four days for a sentence made the third attempt, and it was not a rule at all, it was a person.

“**Then somebody write to my road. Not a rule and not a clause and not a form. A letter, carried by a carter, with a person's name at the top of it, saying that on a Saturday in a market town nine people are standing at some boards and asking anybody who keeps a road to come. One letter. And then the road has been spoken to, and it has been spoken to in words it can answer or not answer, and whatever it does next it has done after somebody knocked.**”

Nobody answered her for about as long as it takes to get a cart round a corner.

The badge-man said that a letter with a person's name at the top of it had been obeyed in nine counties for about nine days in Mudmonth and had cost a keeper a road and a count, and that he was not going to be the man who puts a name at the top of anything again in his life, and that if she wanted to write to that road she should write to it in her own hand and carry it herself, and that this was the only arrangement he had and that it was not a good one.

She said that she had already written, in her own hand, and had carried it herself, on the day she had said the sentence, and that nobody in nine counties had answered it, and that she was not complaining, she was saying that the meeting's answer is that nothing goes, and that she had come four days once and was not coming four days again.

Then the man with the page put the page back inside his coat, in front of about nine people, and said why, and it was the reason nobody had expected and it was the reason he had been carrying it for about four months.

“**I am not giving up seven lines. I am putting a page inside my coat in a market street on a Sunday, in front of all of you, because there is no head in this and I will not hand nine counties a list from inside a room with a door on a lane. A list in a room is a decision. A list in a street is an argument anybody can have with. I have been right five times about a load and I have been wrong once about a road and I have never been wrong about the load, and I would rather have a rule that is wrong than no rule, and I have said that out loud twice and I am not going to stop saying it because about nine people have read a page.**”

Nobody thanked him and nobody agreed with him and the page went back inside the coat and did not come out again in that street.

Then Aren Kest said the only thing he said that afternoon, and he said it to the copyist and not to the market, and he said it in about nine words and then stood still.

“**Say it again in nine counties, and not only here. Not the series. The part about the two warnings.**”

He had thought about it for the whole of the walk up the lane. What she had said in the street was not a rule and it was not a refusal of his, and it was going to be a thing that one market had heard on a Sunday afternoon and that about nine counties would not have by Thursday, and there was a version of this in which he said that to the market and turned it into an argument in front of a dozen people, and he was not going to do it, because a thing said to a market is a thing the market is asked to hold, and he was not the man to hand anybody a thing to hold on a Sunday.

He said it to her because she was the only person in the street who could repeat it without it becoming a different claim, and because he had understood, standing four hundred paces off with a barrow of stones in his hands about a week ago, that the two warnings about one channel had been the first honest thing he had heard anybody say in three volumes, and that a person who says a thing like that about her own work in a street is a person who will not be thanked for it and will do it again anyway.

She looked at him, and she said that she had not said it to him and had not meant to say it to him, and that it was in a street and would be in a street on Thursday because a street is the only place she has ever said anything and had it go anywhere.

He said that was the correct arrangement and that he was not going to argue with a person about where she stands to talk.

Then he stood there for about as long as it takes to draw a cart round, and neither of them said anything else, and about four people at the back of that market watched two people not say anything to each other and about five of them were still arguing about the second set of books. He had wanted her to say the words to him. He had wanted it for about four minutes and he had not asked for it, and she had not given it to him, and the two of them went on standing in the same street about a pace apart, and that was the arrangement, and it was the arrangement for the whole of the rest of it.

Then the woman who keeps the room said the last thing of that Sunday, and she said it at about the sixth hour with the light going and about four carts still in the street, and she said it to the boards.

“**Then the two things. The road none of us can write to, and the right to say no. The meeting will answer them out loud, in the street, on a market morning, and it will answer them in front of about nine people and not about nine hundred, and it will write nothing down, and whoever wants to hold what was said will hold it as it was said. I am not going to say when. If I say when then people will come on that day and not on any other, and the whole of what is wrong with this is that about nine people came on nine days and then went home.**”

Four of the nine said they would be there on the next market morning and five said they would be there on both.

Nobody thanked anybody. The register stood at nine questions and no finding went under any of them, and the room over a weigh-house stayed shut all Sunday, and nothing was written about the two things, and the road that is here and unknown sent nothing.

**And the cost of that Sunday is a column and it is nobody's, and a fixed day refused and a second set of books refused and a letter carried by a carter refused and a page put back inside a coat are not in it and none of them has a price.**

**a barrow of gravel at 13, a hand shovel at 20, a coil of new cord at 23, a sack of lime at 29, a bundle of laths at 30, a hurdle at 32, a pail at 33, a hone at 34, a sack of shingle at 37, and a mattock at 38.** Thirteen, twenty, twenty-three, twenty-nine, thirty, thirty-two, thirty-three, thirty-four, thirty-seven and thirty-eight is two hundred and eighty-nine pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and two hundred and eighty-nine pence is six marks and one over.** The copyist went down the lane at the end of it with the returns folded into the front of her apron and the last of the light going off the boards behind her, and the three attempts stayed exactly where they had been made, and on the Thursday the street found out for itself that the fixed day had been refused in a street on a Sunday, which is the whole of the mechanism and the whole of its difficulty, and the woman who had made the third attempt was not there to hear about it and had said she was not coming four days twice.
