# Chapter 0622

**Hearthmonth the twenty-first, YR 320, a Saturday and a market morning, the second hour in a room over a weigh-house, a keeper who has walked four days with wet boots in a bag, and a man of about forty-seven who is right and is not thanked.**

The man who keeps the third road came up the lane at the first hour on a market morning with his boots in a bag, and about nine people were in the market by then and the room over the weigh-house had been open a quarter of an hour, and he had not been to a room in his life. He put the bag down against the wall by the door and stood there.

“**It went in the night,**” he said. “**At the second hour. I was asleep at the first and I heard it and I came out and I have not been back to my own house since because there is nobody in it. I have walked four days. I came because there is a page going round nine counties and I have never seen it and somebody in this county has.**”

The man of about thirty-four with a sheet had it in the satchel and he took it out and did not hand it over, and read out the seventh line, which is the line the third road is on, and read out the sixth, and the fifth, and did not read the other three because the man had asked about a road and not about a page.

Then the man of about forty who keeps the third road said the thing he had walked four days to say, and he said it standing with his hand on the bag.

“**I have a number,**” he said. “**Against my road. And a name against the number, and my name against the number, and it is my job, and I have had it nineteen years and my father had it before me. I am telling you that so that you will not spend a morning deciding whether I am a person. I am a person and I have a number and you can write to me and I will answer, and if I am wrong about the water I will say so in writing and you can hold the writing.**”

“**Then that is the whole of what is different about you,**” the copyist of about twenty-six said, and she said it to the room and not to him.

Nobody in that room said what everybody in it was thinking, and the badge-man said it, because he says things out loud.

“**Then that is the whole of what is different about him, and I have been waiting about four years for somebody to walk into this room and say it, and I want the room to hear what it is. In nine counties there is exactly one road on that page that anybody could write to tonight, and it is the one with a number on it, and it is the one whose keeper has just told us the water came over it. Every other road on that page would have had to be described to us by a person, and none of them has anybody standing on it who is willing to be described.**”

The man of about forty-seven with a page came up the lane at the second hour on foot, the way he came, and he did not come because he had been sent and he said so at the door before he had his coat off.

“**I heard at a gate about nine miles back,**” he said. “**A carter had it from a man who had it from a man who was at the crossing. I have not been told anything and I have not sent anybody and I am here because I have been here every market morning since a page went round nine counties and I am not going to stop coming now that the arithmetic has started paying.**”

“**Your arithmetic,**” the man of about thirty-four with a sheet said.

“**My arithmetic,**” Garvin Slade said. “**I said the works will take seven. I said it has taken seven since a year before any of you were in this room. I said that the fourth of the seven went cold for six days two winters ago and four of the other six went with it inside forty hours, and that the second went cold the winter before that and three went with it. I said both of those were roads with a number on them and a name against the number and somebody whose job it was, and that both of them were on a high flat nobody was watching.**”

Nobody said he was wrong.

“**And on Friday night the third went cold,**” he said, “**and it is not a high flat, and it has got a number and a name and a keeper who has just stood up in this room and said his name can be written down, and I have never in my life been more glad to hear a man volunteer a thing I have been asking nine counties for. So I am going to say the rest of it and nobody is going to like it.**”

“**Say it,**” the man of about fifty-eight with a badge said.

“**Six of the seven are marked to be held. The seventh is on the page with nothing against it. There are seven on the works. There is one thing the works will not take and the thing it will not take has to come off, and the only line on that page with nothing in it against it is the seventh, and by Friday night there is a road with a number on it that has gone cold to prove that the others have been carrying it.**”

“**Then take it off,**” a man near the back said, and it was not the man of about thirty-four with a sheet and it was not anybody who had come up the stair twice.

“**I have said for two months that I would shut one and hold six,**” Garvin Slade said. “**I said it on a page and I said it in this lane and I asked for six marks and I got none, and I did not expect any and I am not going to say again that I expected any. I said the seven were the only reason the seven were seven. I said a road with a figure on it is a weight and a road with nothing on it is not a light thing.**”

“**You said a gap goes first,**” the copyist said.

“**I did,**” Garvin Slade said, “**and I have been wrong about the order and right about the number, and those are the two halves of it and I could not have had one without the other. The gap did not go first. The gap sat on the works for nothing for two months and the roads that had figures on them paid for it, and one of them went on Friday night. That is not a gap going first. That is a gap being carried by everybody, and the roads with figures on it finding out at about the second hour of the night.**”

Nobody thanked him. The man of about thirty-four with a sheet wrote two lines on his own sheet with no head over it and did not read them back, and one of the two was the number of the third and the other was that the man of about forty-seven had said the order was wrong and the number was right, and neither of those was a figure of a road and both of them went down the lane in his coat at the seventh hour and into the satchel.

Aren Kest came up the outside stair at about the second hour and had been four days on a made road with a bar of iron and a lantern, and he came in behind the man of about forty who keeps the third road, and nobody had sent him, and he said his two sentences and sat down.

“**I was at the crossing at the second hour of Friday night and I am the only person in nine counties who has seen the bed of it since Thursday,**” he said, “**and I can tell this room that the water is not coming through the works. It is coming under a stone that is not there. Somebody has been in that channel with a wedge and a bar within a month, and I have not said who on the page and I am not going to say who in this room, because I do not know and because the only two people in this room who could find out are a road office with a schedule and a man of about forty-seven with a page, and both of them would have to go and look at it, and neither of them is going to be asked to.**”

The badge-man said the follow-up question.

“**Then what is the seventh going to be?**”

“**I do not know,**” Aren Kest said. “**And I want that written down as a thing said in this room on the twenty-first of a month, because I have been asked a question in this lane in a month about what I would do and I did not answer it, and I am not going to be asked twice and I am not going to answer twice, and this is me not answering again in front of nine people so that it is on nobody's page but mine.**”

Nobody wrote that down either.

Then the man of about thirty-one from the road office came up the outside stair with a schedule in his hand, and he had not sent for anybody and nobody sent for him, and he said his business standing, which is what a clerk does.

“**My office has had the account of the crossing since the second hour of Friday night,**” he said. “**Not from this room. From the gate, because that is where the account of a road comes from. My office is going to do what a road office does and it is going to send men and materials to the third, and it is going to take the money for them off the works, and I have been told to say so out loud in a room where it is being decided and I am going to say it out loud and then I am going to go downstairs and wait.**”

“**And what is the number?**” the badge-man said, and it was a fair question and it was a question about money and not about a road.

“**I have not got a number,**” the man of about thirty-one said, “**and I have been told to say that I have not got a number as well, and I am saying it, and I would like it noticed that the only two people in nine counties who could produce a number for the seventh line are sitting in this room and one of them keeps three series apart on purpose.**”

The copyist of about twenty-six did not look up from the table.

“**I have said that I could name which of the seven is the gap inside an hour since the ninth day of a month,**” she said, “**and I have said I will not, and I am not going to say it this morning because a road office has sent a man up a stair with a schedule and the second I say it out loud it is a figure in a road office and not a figure in a room, and then it is on a line and then it is on a page and then somebody in nine counties can check it, and nobody in this room is entitled to hand that to anybody.**”

“**I am not asking you to say it,**” the man of about thirty-one said. “**I am telling you that I know you could, and that my office knows you could, and that my instructions do not ask for it, and that if I go back and write that a room in this county holds the answer to the seventh line and will not give it, then in about four months there will be a letter asking for it with something at the top of it.**”

“**Then write it,**” the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps the room said, from the table, without raising her head, “**and put nothing at the top of it, and I will countersign it if the man wants a person and not an office, and that is the whole of what this room can do with it and it is not very much.**”

The man of about thirty-one wrote it and she countersigned it and it went into his coat, and the badge-man said the true thing about what the room had just done, which is that it was the first piece of paper out of this room in four and a half years that anybody in nine counties had asked for, and that the room had given it, and that giving it had cost nothing at all because the room had nothing in it to give.

“**Thirty-one years,**” he said. “**And for four and a half of them I have been putting other men's figures into that room and watching every one of them be used. This morning the first thing a room was asked for was something it had never held. I am not going to stand in a room and call that a victory, and I am not going to call it a failure either, and I am going to go and say the hearing stands at the fourth hour and I am going to say it in the street and not in here, because that is where I say it.**”

Nobody thanked anybody and nobody was thanked, and the man of about forty-seven with a page went out into the market at about the third hour and stood by the weigh-house board for a while with his hands behind him, and about four people in that lane understood that a man had just been right about the shape of a thing in public and had been given nothing for it, and about five did not.

**And the cost of that Saturday is a column and it is the road office's, and a man who walked four days with his boots in a bag and a set of works carrying seven is not in it and neither of them has a price.**
