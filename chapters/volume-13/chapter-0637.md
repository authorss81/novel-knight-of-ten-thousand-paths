# Chapter 0637

**Thawmonth the twenty-first, YR 321, a Wednesday and not a market morning, the fifth hour at the boards in a lane about four hundred paces long, a man of about thirty-one who will not put a figure on the cost, a man of about forty-seven who is right for the fifth time, and a chair with nobody in it.**

Aren Kest was at the low end of the counter boards with his stick from about the fourth hour and stayed there until the lane emptied, and nobody came and stood beside him and nobody said anything to him at all, and that is what the seventh of nine days was like for him. The room was shut again and the badge-man stood in the open on a Wednesday at about the fifth hour and said why, which was that after four days of this the argument had become a thing people in nine counties made a point of being in, and that he would not have a man's reason for standing in the open written down.

“**There is no market and there is no band and there are about nine of you here because you came, and I want that said before anybody uses the word meeting, because there is a room up that stair and we are not in it and the whole of what we have done so far was done in the open on purpose.**”

The man of about thirty-one from a road office had done the arithmetic of the burden clause over two days and on the Wednesday morning he put it on the boards in about nine sentences and then refused to finish it.

“**One closure. A rider out and back about two days. Four men walking to ends of roads, and I cannot tell you how many days because I have not been to the ends of the roads, and I will not tell you a figure for the whole of it either, and I want that written down as a thing I refused to do and not as a thing I was slow about.**”

“**Why refuse?**” the man of about forty-seven said.

“**Because the moment there is a figure on it, my office will do the cheapest thing that satisfies the figure. That is what an office is. If the burden costs forty shillings to discharge then the burden will be discharged for forty shillings, and it will be discharged by a letter to a keeper because a letter to a keeper costs a sheet of paper, and the clause will be satisfied and the road will be off the works, and in about four years somebody will read a return and see that the burden was met and will not read this lane at all.**”

Nobody argued with him about it.

The man of about forty-seven said the fifth thing he had come to say, and he said it before anybody had asked him for it, which he has done every time.

“**You are writing a rule about nine people who are in this lane, and in about four months the man who needs it will not be one of you. He will be in a room with no head over it at the second hour of the night with about nine people in it, and he will have about nine minutes. And I want you to understand that every one of the three clauses will be satisfied. All three. Notice in the road's own words: he will write to the keeper and the keeper's words are the road's words. Witness named by the people who work the road: he will take the keeper. Burden on the man who proposes: that is him, and he will carry it gladly, because carrying it is what he has just agreed to do.**”

He put his hand flat on the boards.

“**There will be a notice and there will be a witness and there will be a burden, all three, all complete, all lawfully done, and the road will be off the works about nine days late and nobody will ever have done anything cruel. I am right and I have been right five times now and I would like somebody in this lane to write down the fifth time in a place that has no head over it so that the next one of me does not have to say it out loud in a lane to be believed.**”

Nobody thanked him. The man of about thirty-four with a sheet wrote on his own sheet that a man of about forty-seven had said he had been right five times and had given no road, and put it in the satchel, and did not read it back.

The woman of about fifty, who keeps a road nineteen years, said the only thing she said that day and she said it to the boards.

“**Then on the day it happens the notice will be read to me,**” she said, “**and I will be the witness, because there is nobody else to stand there, and the burden will be on a man who has agreed to carry it. And I will have been told in my own words by a man who did not come. And I am not making that into a rule and I am not making it into an argument. I am saying that three clauses will be true and none of them will be about me.**”

Then nobody spoke for a while, and the copyist of about twenty-six said the thing about the chair.

She said it flatly and she said it to the room at the boards and not to anybody in particular, and four of them looked at the chair and five did not, and it was the same chair he had put back under the table himself on the third day of the nine.

“**The man of about fifty-two is not here,**” she said. “**He said he would be back on the ninth day and it is the seventh and that is not a thing to be alarmed about, and I am not alarmed and I am not going to make anybody alarmed. I am saying it because nine of us have been sitting in a room and on boards for four days and four of us noticed the chair and five of us did not, and I would like it written down that the copyist noticed, and not by anybody else.**”

Nobody thanked her for noticing. The man of about thirty-one from a road office said that a man in his county had once gone home for two days out of nine and come back on the ninth and nobody in his county had noticed the chair.

“**What is the road?**” the badge-man said.

Nobody answered, and he did not ask a second time, and he said out loud that he was not going to ask a second time and that the reason was not kindness and he gave the reason.

“**Because if nobody knows which road then nobody can tell me that it is all right. If I know which road then in about four days I will be able to send a boy to it, and if I send a boy to it I will have sent a rider to a road that has not sent anybody and has said nothing, and that is the second half of a sheet and I will not build the second half of anything. I would rather sit here with a chair empty for two days.**”

Nobody argued with that either.

The man of about thirty-four with a sheet said that there was one thing he could do about the chair and that he was going to do it and that nobody was to stop him, and what he did was go back to his own sheet with no head over it and write down that a man of about fifty-two who kept a road and would not name it had said he would be back on the ninth day and had not been there on the seventh, and that he had put his chair back under the table himself.

“**That goes down the lane in my coat at the seventh hour,**” he said, “**and it is in words and not a figure, and there is no column in nine counties for a man not being in a lane, and I am aware that the only place this sentence can go is into a satchel and down four hundred paces of lane and into a room in which nobody is anybody's keeper. I have written it anyway, and I would like it noticed that I have written it, because in about a month somebody is going to say that nobody was keeping track.**”

Nobody thanked him and the copyist said that she would not be thanked for it either, because she had not asked and was not going to.

The man of about thirty-four with a sheet then asked a question that had been sitting in that lane since the Tuesday, and he asked it in the plainest voice he has and he asked it of the man of about forty-seven and not of the room.

“**If all three clauses get broken lawfully in about four months, then what has the nine days bought?**”

The man of about forty-seven looked at him for long enough that about four people in that lane understood that he was going to answer it properly.

“**Nothing,**” he said. “**Not one thing, and I want it said by me before anybody else finds it. And I am still for it. Here is the whole of what nine days buys. In about four months a man is going to do something he is not allowed to do, and at present he does it knowing that about nine people in nine counties have not heard of him and think him the only man in nine counties who has ever done it. After these nine days he will do it knowing that nine people had written down what ought to have happened first, and that they wrote it in nine hands and that he can go and read any one of them and argue with it. That is not a rule. That is a fact about what nine people in nine counties now know. And I have spent about four and a half months telling this lane that a gap goes first because nobody is carrying it, and the nine days have not changed a single thing about that, and the next time there will not be a gap and it will go in a week and somebody will be praised for it.**”

Nobody thanked him and about four people in that lane wrote nothing down and about five wrote it down after he had gone.

Berta Lomax read the three clauses out again at the low end of the boards for the people who had come and could not follow them in a hand, and she read them without any weight on them at all, and four people heard them for the first time and one of those four said afterwards that he had not known the second one was about a person rather than about a road.

**And the cost of that Wednesday is a column and it is the road office man's, and about two days of riding and four men walking to ends of roads he refused to put a figure on and a man who said he had been right five times are not in it and none of them has a price.**

**a hay fork at 17, a curry comb at 24, a bucket of bran at 28, a noseband at 31, a hame at 32, a trace chain at 33, a saddle pad at 35, a lantern at 37, a hobnail at 39, and a mattock at 41.** Seventeen, twenty-four, twenty-eight, thirty-one, thirty-two, thirty-three, thirty-five, thirty-seven, thirty-nine and forty-one is three hundred and seventeen pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and three hundred and seventeen pence is six marks and twenty-nine over**, and a room over a weigh-house with its door shut, a deed of thirteen words, three separate series, a register of nine questions, a day-book of nine entries, a fourth head ruled for nothing, a press standing open with no date, a page with six ruled spaces with three in them marked, a satchel with a sheet in it that has no head over it, about four hundred paces of lane, a market town of about two thousand people, a chair put back under a table by a man himself, one closure, a rider two days out, four men, a set of works under an abbey four days off with seven roads through it, and nine counties are seventeen figures of distance and of count and not one of them is a price, and a Wednesday, the fifth hour, four days, nine days, the third day of nine, the seventh day of nine, the ninth day, about nine minutes, two days out of nine, about four days, two days, about four months, and in about four years are thirteen figures of time and not one of them goes in at either end of this column. **A man of about fifty-two who was not in that lane on the seventh day of the nine and a man in a county who once went home for two days out of nine and five of the nine people who did not notice a chair and a man of about thirty-one from a road office and Berta Lomax of the fruit valley and a woman of about fifty who keeps a road nineteen years and a man of about fifty-eight with a badge and a copyist of about twenty-six and four of the nine people who noticed a chair and a woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a room and a man of about thirty-four with a sheet and a man of about forty-seven with a page are twelve figures of persons, and the eleventh of them wrote down a number of times with no head over it and the sixth of them was the notice and the witness and the burden all at once on the same morning.** Nobody thanked anybody and nobody was thanked and nothing went into the register.
