# Chapter 0618

**Hearthmonth the sixteenth, YR 320, a Monday and not a market morning, the second hour to the eighth on a made road four days down, a bridge over a ditch with about nine yards of parapet gone, three men and a boy and a cart, and a woman who has walked about nine hundred paces with a drain.**

There is not a great deal to describe about a bridge with about nine yards of its parapet gone on the low side, and the description of it is that the road itself was sound and the water was not, and that a man who keeps a made road for a living had known about it since the first of a month and had not had anybody to send for, and that on the sixteenth of a month three men came up that made road with a cart and a load of stone and a boy of about seventeen and about two hours of daylight in hand, and they did about nine yards of it before dark, and the wages were counted out on the parapet that had not gone.

Aren Kest was on that road because he had been on it since the ninth, and because there was a boy of about seventeen at the gate of this town who swept a gate and had told a man of about fifty that the water was coming over the road, and because a man of about fifty who lays stone had said he would come after the frost and had come.

The man of about fifty who keeps the made road was on it too, and he was a different man from the man of about fifty who lays stone, and nobody in that party ever confused the two.

At about the fourth hour a carter came down the road with the district's paper in a bag under the seat, and the carter carries paper between gates and has for about nine years, and he had a page with six ruled spaces on it that was not nine days old and had been round part of the district, and he put it on the parapet of the bridge and said that the man who kept the lane four miles on had marked a space and had asked him to bring it on because the man carrying it was four days off and had better things to be doing.

The man of about fifty who keeps the made road read the page standing up, with the cart beside him and three men looking at him, and he read it the whole way through, and then he took out a pencil and put a mark in a space against his own line, and he did it without asking anybody and without any speech at all.

“**Why that one,**” Aren Kest said, and he said it because nobody else in that party was going to.

“**Because I can be found,**” the man of about fifty said. “**I have a figure. It is yards, because it is a made road and nine counties pay for it by the yard and there is a figure against it in a book in a town four days off with a man in it whose name is on the top of the page. If I am wrong about anything on that page, there is somebody I can be told about it and I can say so to his face. That is the only reason I have put my hand there. Put a hand next to mine and you have said nothing at all.**”

“**That is two,**” he said, to nobody, “**and there is a third somewhere and the man with it will not say that, because saying that would be saying a thing about himself that he has not been asked for.**”

Nobody wrote it down, and the carter took the page back and put it under the seat, and it went on round the district, and the man of about fifty who keeps the made road went back to the parapet.

At about the fifth hour the woman of about fifty came round the bend on foot, and she had come about nine hundred paces and she had come on purpose and she was not a stranger to any of them, and she had come because a made road is where a man can be found who cuts ditches, and there is no made road in four days anywhere else to find one on.

“**I want a ditch,**” she said.

“**You have come nine hundred paces to ask for a ditch,**” the man of about fifty said.

“**I have come nine hundred paces to ask for a ditch,**” she said, “**and I am not going to say anything else while there is stone in that cart. I keep a road from the ferry stone at the low end to the ninth milestone at the top. In a wet month the water comes over it in about four places and there is nowhere for it to go, and a ditch about two paces wide along the high side of a road is not a hearing and is not a form and is not a figure. It is a hole that a man with a spade cuts.**”

“**How long,**” the man of about fifty said.

“**About nine hundred paces,**” she said, “**and about nine days of three men, and it will cost a county about four hundred pence, and I have brought you the number because you will not take my word for a number and I would rather you had a figure of mine that is a figure of a thing than one of me.**”

“**Nine hundred paces, three men, nine days,**” the man of about fifty said, “**and I will cut it in the next dry week and there is no hearing about it and there is no paper about it, and I am going to say the thing about it now so that nobody can say afterwards that it was done for you. I am cutting it because the water is coming over a made road and the made road is mine. If you had not come I would have cut it in the thaw and I would have cut it about four hundred paces at a time over two years and got it wrong in about nine places.**”

“**Then it is not a favour,**” the woman of about fifty said.

“**It is not a favour,**” the man of about fifty said, “**and it is not a hearing, and I would like both of those said out loud in front of these three men because in about four months somebody is going to tell one of them that ditches are got by asking.**”

Then the woman of about fifty said the one thing she had come the nine hundred paces for, and she said it while the stone was going on the cart, and she said it to a man of about fifty who was about to cut a ditch and not to the three men.

“**A page wants a mark,**” she said. “**A ditch wants a spade. You have had both of them on this road today and only one of them has a head over it, and the one that has a head over it is going round nine counties, and the one that has not is going to be a hole by the end of next week, and nobody is going to write either of them down except the number of yards of a bridge, which is a figure about a thing and not a figure about anybody.**”

Nobody answered her and nobody thanked her, and the three men put the stone on, and the man of about thirty-two with a limp barrowed from the fifth hour to the eighth hour and was good at it and the boy of about seventeen barrowed beside him and neither of them said the other was good at it, and at the eighth hour the wages were counted out on the sound parapet in coin, and the coin went into nine hands and one of those hands was a boy's and went straight into a pocket, and the man of about fifty who keeps the made road wrote nothing down about the day and told the carter on his way past not to write it down either.

“**About nine yards of parapet on a bridge over a ditch, the fourth of a month to the sixth,**” he said, “**and I will know it and so will the three of them and so will you, and that is where it is.**”

Aren Kest stood in the road at about the eighth hour with his stick in his hand and about two hundred yards of a ditch marked out with pegs that had not been dug yet, and a page had gone past him with a mark in it from a man who could be found, and a drain was going to be a hole in about nine days, and a woman had walked nine hundred paces to be told that a ditch is not a hearing.

He had the argument in his hand, the whole of it, the thing a woman with a barrow had said on a slope in the first week of a month about a figure being a thing a person says out loud in their own place and is wrong in if they are wrong. **And on a made road four days down, at the eighth hour, with nine yards of new parapet and about nine hundred paces of pegged ditch, two people had acted on the shape of it without ever having heard it, and neither of them had been sent, and one of them was a man cutting stone who could be found and about nine yards, and the other was a woman who had walked nine hundred paces to be told that a ditch is not a hearing, and he was not going to be the one who told either of them that he had an argument.**

**And the cost of that Monday is a column and it is the woman of about fifty's, and nine hundred paces of pegged ditch and about nine yards of new parapet and a page with a mark in it are not in it and none of them has a price.**

**a used barrow wheel at two, a hod hook at nine, a bucket at eighteen, a length of chain at twenty-nine, a scupper at thirty, a mason's hammer at thirty-three, a line peg at thirty-five, a whetstone at thirty-seven, a pair of gloves at thirty-eight, and a bushel of lime at forty.** Two, nine, eighteen, twenty-nine, thirty, thirty-three, thirty-five, thirty-seven, thirty-eight and forty is two hundred and seventy-one pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and two hundred and seventy-one pence is five marks and thirty-one over**, and a bridge over a ditch, about nine yards of parapet, about nine hundred paces of pegged ditch, a made road four days down, a ferry stone at the low end, a ninth milestone at the top, nine counties, about nine hundred sheets a month, about a hundred and four in one band, about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month, about eleven inches of drawer, a day-book of nine entries and six ruled spaces are thirteen figures of distance and of count and not one of them is a price, and a Monday, the second hour, the fourth hour, the fifth hour, the eighth hour, nine days, four and a half years and about seven months are eight figures of time and not one of them goes in at either end of this column. **Aren Kest and a woman of about fifty who keeps a road nineteen years and a man of about fifty who keeps a made road and three men who came up with a cart and a boy of about seventeen and a carter who carries paper between gates and a man of about thirty-four with a sheet who is four days off and a copyist of about twenty-six and a man of about fifty-eight with a badge and about nine households at the top end of a road four days off are ten figures of persons, and the second of them walked nine hundred paces to be told that a ditch is not a hearing and the fourth of them is a set and the fifth of them was paid in coin out of nine hands.** Nobody thanked anybody and nobody was thanked and the day was not written down.
