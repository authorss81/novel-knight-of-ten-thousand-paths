# Chapter 0148

The second of Hearthmonth, YR 313, was a Thursday and a market day, and the line came up at about the fourth hour in a gateway on Weir Street with the rain going sideways into the wall opposite, and it came up in a gateway and not in a room because the north end of that line is a yard, and the yard's gate is the north end, and a yard's gate is a place a cart goes through and is not a place a man comes up out of.

The yard was shut. The top hinge was off and the churn was still propping it, which it has propped since a year in which nobody in this county can name the year, and there was a wheelbarrow against the wall with elm shavings in it that had been wet for about a week, and the bench-board under the window had a bill on it with a figure at the top and about four inches of empty space under the figure where a boy of fifteen copies the empty space as well as the number, because that is what the boy does.

Nobody was in the street. It is a market day and it is the fourth hour, and the market is two streets over and about forty men in it, and a man who comes up a line at the fourth hour of a market morning in a gateway on Weir Street is a man nobody in this county has any reason to be looking for, and he was not looked for, and he got to the rooms over the cooper along the water side with his coat over the bag and a stick in his hand that he did not have when he came.

The bell at the Old Toll went at the fourth hour and a half, four hundred yards up the road, the way it goes, and he heard it from the corner of Weir Street and did not turn round, because there is nothing in a bell that tells you whether it is a good day or a bad one and about forty years of standing at the end of that road have taught him that the only fact in a bell is the hour.

He had the fever for three days, the second, the third and the fourth, and it broke on the fifth, and all three of those days are in another man's hands and are not in this account because it was not his to account for.

The boy of fifteen came at the second hour of the second and found him on the floor of the rooms with a tool bag still on and could not make a fire for about an hour and a half and then made one. He held the tools. He carried a scythe edge down to the yard at the third hour of the third for a man who could not lift it and put it on the bench-board under the window and told two people in the gateway and one of them was the man who keeps eels that a man at the rooms over the cooper was not well, and the boy said his own name about the fact and said it as a thing he had been told to say, and the man who keeps eels said there was no hurry, and it was not a hurry.

On the morning of the fourth the boy of the Old Toll, who is seventeen and has been the steward's apprentice for two years and three months and is still not good at it, came down Weir Street with a line in his hand and said that the far end had asked, by the arrangement, whether the man at the rooms over the cooper was carrying anything.

That is the arrangement. A boy of seventeen and a carpenter have rung a bell on that bridge since the twenty-fourth of Fallowmonth and asked after a man who was not there, and the asking is not about the man and the man has never been told it is going on, and it is the one piece of this county's machinery that has been running for four months for a purpose nobody has admitted.

“He’s got a bag and he’s got a stick,” the boy said from the doorway, with the line in his hand and the rain behind him, “and he’s not carrying nothing, and I’m not going to say he’s carrying nothing because that would be a lie, and I’m not going to say I know what’s in it because I don’t know, and that is the fourth time I have asked, and I am not going to ask a fifth time on a Friday, because a Friday is not the day the far end keeps and it would be a wasted ask. It’s a Monday we’re wanted on.”

“All right,” said Aren Kest, from the chair.

“It is a Monday we’re wanted on,” the boy said, as if it needed saying, and went back up the road in the rain.

Then he went back up the road with the line and the rain, and the arrangement went on, and the far end of that line is a coping on a river this county does not have, and a tile on it with a rope on it and no yoke, and nobody looked at it, and the boy of seventeen rang a bell at the fourth hour and a half that day with a carpenter who is a hand and not a keeper, and neither of them is the man at the rooms over the cooper, and nothing in the sound of it is his.

On the morning of the fourth the yard's book had a new line in it.

Nobody saw it happen. The book is on a shelf at the back of the yard where four men have written in it for eleven years, and the man who wrote in it on the morning of the fourth did it in nine seconds flat with a wet nib, and the line is a date and an hour and nothing at all against either, and it is the fifth line in that book in two months with an hour against it and no figure and no name, and the four before it are about a date of somebody's that is not this county's date, and the man who wrote this one is the first hand of the yard and has not been asked about any of the five and will not be.

The boy of fifteen found it at the second hour of the fifth and read it and asked what it was for.

“It’s nothing,” said Tovan Kest. “Put the kettle on.”

He put the kettle on. He has been doing what he was told at a bench-board since he was nine years old and he has never once in two months asked what the other four lines in that book are for, and it is not on a stair and it is not in a gateway and it was not in a room, and nobody in this county will ever know that a yard in a fen town held one end of a line for a hundred and seventeen days because the other end of it was four hundred miles off in a country that is not on any map, and that the holding was an accident of where a gate is, and that a thing set went in a book and the book has it, and there is no instrument in this kingdom that could make that book answer.

The fever broke on the fifth at about the seventh hour and Mara Vey came up two flights of a stair at the eighth with a sheet and a rule, because that is what she brings, and because a body corporate with a door and a number can be sued and a keeper can only be dismissed and she has not told anybody that in three counties and does not intend to start in a room over a weigh-house.

He gave her the numbers before she asked, in the order they happened, flat, the way a man reads out a line of work.

“The fifth of a month, at the fourth hour, on a line made up in nine minutes with the hour against each of the nine. A week and a rolling, and the year there is thirteen rollings long and I was there for all of it, and the year ended on the morning I left. The back is the second of this month and a Thursday, and the fourth hour. The absence is a hundred and seventeen days, which I wrote on the far side on faith before I came, and it is a hundred and seventeen days now, and it is the same figure and the path is nowhere in it.

“The printed figure is three weeks. It has been three weeks in an office's hand since a filing room two years ago and it has not changed and the office cannot say whose figure it is and neither can I. Twenty-one days against a hundred and seventeen. The error is ninety-six days. The error over the absence is about four fifths and the error over the warning is not a thing anybody should calculate, and the path interval is not in it at all and it never has been.

“The fever was three days, the second, the third and the fourth, and it is the second time in this case that a crossing has cost three, and there is a man in this county holding a scythe edge for me and running errands and telling a man who keeps eels that I am not well, and I have not paid him and I am not going to pay him in money.

“The route that was open the whole time is the county with a stone in a field. The route I went out on is the one whose road was closed in four lines of writing in the middle of the year and whose answer was correct, and it is closed and it is not lost, and I still hold two. And the error is in me and it scales with the work, and I wrote that down two volumes ago and I have now proved it twice, and the second time was on a lid with one leg, and the leg is the other half of that sentence and I would like the two of them entered in the same place.”

Then he stopped, and the stopping was in the order it happened, and she wrote the figures down, and the room was quiet for a while.

“There is one more thing,” he said, “and it is not a figure and I am telling you that it is not a figure, which is a thing I have learned to do from you.

“I cannot put an ordinary morning in the order it happened. Not a morning in a county. A morning in a yard on Weir Street on the twenty-ninth of Greenmonth, a Monday, the day I went out, at about the fifth hour. I know the yard and the street and the elm and the shave horse and the boy and the block, and I know the hour, and I know what I was doing at the start of it, and after that it does not run in an order. It is not gone. It is all there. It comes out in whatever order it likes.”

He did not say the rest of it, and the rest of it was four months old and was a rule in a fair copy on a page that cannot be served in a room over a weigh-house, and the third of the three rules ends: an authority that nobody can name the source of is entered as a habit, and a habit is a thing a county does to itself when nobody is looking. She wrote that sentence four months ago and neither of them has ever said whose it is about, and it is about him, and it has been about him since the day she wrote it, and he has not told her that he knows it is about him, and that is the fourth thing in a coat and it is a habit now and not a choice.

She did not ask. She wrote the figures down and she read them back and she did not read the last one back, and then she said, “Ninety-six. I have had ninety-six in this room against a printed three weeks since the middle of the summer and I did not have a figure for the absence to put it against.”

“You had a figure for the absence in a leaf of returns with a printed three weeks at the head of it.”

“I had a leaf,” she said. “I did not have a man who had come up a line and been awake for it.”

And then she asked him one question, and it was the one she always asks, and it made a witness feel foolish in four years and a half and she apologised in plain words for it afterward every time, and this time she did not ask it at all, and that was the ninth, and the ninth was entered on the sixteenth of Longlight in a month four months ago, and there is no tenth entry in that book and there is not going to be one in this year.

She stayed about an hour. She did the thing she does at the fourth hour whatever has come in the bag, which is to go down to the market and look at a pig, only it was the eighth hour and she went anyway, and he heard her say to a man in the lane that she was going to look at a pig, and the man said something about the price of pigs, and she said a number.

The letter went into a bag for the provincial town on the sixth of Hearthmonth, in the evening, in his own hand, with the date and the hour against it, because a thing that is set goes in a book and a thing that is not set goes in a coat, and he had four things in a coat and was not going to make it five.

It is four hundred and eleven words and it is in no book in this county and the only copy of it is in a bag in a mail sack behind a salt loft four hundred yards from a room with a door and a number.

*To Sabin Dain, aid to the register at the Office of Provincial Registration. And to no office.*

*This is a refusal. I am writing it instead of saying it, and I am writing it because a spoken no is a thing that can be repeated by whoever heard it and a written no is a thing that can be produced by the person it was written to, and you are the only person in this business who has ever written the reason down before asking for the thing.*

*One. I am not taking the grade. It is of the grade used in the eastern counties, with a district, with a clerk, with a stipend of eleven marks a year, and with the right to send men. It is a command and it is not a custody. I have been in a county for a year and a rolling in which the man who holds a length of water answers to the people on both sides of the water and to nobody else, and a bench there has governed nothing on purpose for nineteen years, and I have spent two years refusing to be made answerable for a road I keep, and I am not going to be made answerable for a road I do not keep in a rank that has the word warden in it and a command underneath it. I have no argument against a command. I have one against a command that a person appointed by nobody can hold and that a person appointed by a community can refuse.*

*Two. I am not taking the sealed record. A record of myself, kept by your office, produced to any body that asks your office a question about me, not produced to me at any time while it is sealed, the seal yours and not mine. I have spent two years not making a record of that kind and I have been worse at refusing it than I am at building bridges, and this letter is the fourth attempt and the first one that is a document. I have no argument against your office. I have one against a record a person cannot see, and I put that in writing nine years ago in a fair copy and I have not improved on it and I am not going to now.*

*Three, and this is the part that is for you and not for the file. The sheet says the offer was made out in the ninth month of the year three years last past and has been held open since, and that the number of persons to whom it has been made out in that time is not stated on the sheet. I have been about four months thinking about that sentence and I have arrived at the conclusion that it is the only sentence on the sheet that matters, and that the eleven marks and the district and the clerk are the price of not printing it, and that a woman with a filing system and a horror of unnamed things wrote that sheet with the number left off on purpose, and that I am not going to be the one who tells her she did it on purpose, because I do not know that she did, and because if she did then there is a number in her office and if she did not then the number is the sheet's and the sheet is the office's and the office has had three years.*

*Four. There is a warrant of this grade in a sealed envelope in a press in the provincial town. I am not going to ask anybody to go and fetch it. If it is produced to me I will send it back and I would like the return entered whichever way it goes, and if it is not produced then nothing is lost that I can measure.*

*Five. The eastern paths are not to be closed by me. That is not a bargaining position and it is not held against anything, and I will not trade on it. I do not know how to close a road and I am not going to learn how. A county of clay with no office in it, four hundred miles off, had its road closed in four lines of writing in the middle of this year by a bench that asked for it, in a hand that is not a clerk's, and it contained no name, and the fourth line gave back a term I had helped write nineteen years ago, and it was correct, and I read it in a room with about nine people in it and I did not say the fourth line out loud, and that is everything I know about closing a road and it was done by a bench of nine that has been asked four times and has said yes four times and is not a bench.*

*Six. There is an earlier letter of mine in a bag going the other way, written in the middle of the summer, which neither accepts nor refuses this and carries no returnable date and no number and no copy in this county. I am not withdrawing it. A thing that is not withdrawn is a thing that can be produced, and I would like this county to be able to produce both of them.*

*And there is no thanks in this letter, and you did not offer a thing that needed any, and I would rather a woman who writes the reasons down first did not have to be polite to me in a document she is going to keep.*

He read it back once and did not improve it, and put it in the envelope and sealed it with the office's own method, along the left edge, with a knife, because that is the way the envelopes in this case have all been opened since the twelfth of Longlight, and a man does not send a letter in a bag in a way that cannot be seen to have been opened, and every lesson of the last eleven years has been about a seal.

Then he put his hand on the barrow in the yard and could not lift it, and the leg would not take him up the four steps to the rooms, and he got up them on one leg and a hand on the door frame and stood in his own doorway in the dark for about a minute and a half, and the bell at the Old Toll had gone at the fourth hour and a half eight hours ago, and it goes at the fourth hour and a half every day of the year, and there is no second one of it, and a man who has come up a line is not going to be given a second one.
