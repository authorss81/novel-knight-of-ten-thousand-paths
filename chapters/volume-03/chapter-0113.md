# Chapter 0113

The ninth of Rainmonth, YR 313, was a Saturday and it was a market day, and a magistrate of this county who has been one for nine years can tell a market Saturday from a market Thursday in about two seconds and cannot tell you what the difference is, and the difference is that a Thursday is a day the town buys and a Saturday is a day the town decides.

The paper came in with the Saturday post, and the Saturday post is the second bag, and the second bag is the one that comes from a provincial town, and it is eleven days out and eleven days back, and everything in it has been written by a man who is not going to be in the same room as anybody who receives it, and the two sheets in it were dated the twenty-eighth of the month last and the twenty-sixth, which is eleven days and thirteen days, and the thirteen is the one this court would have called a failure of the post and which is in fact a Saturday.

Lysa Fenn opened it at the second hour in a room over a court with one chair at the table that was not hers, and she read it twice, and the second reading was with a rule, and it took about eleven minutes.

---

**It was a form of the Office of Provincial Registration, and it was the fourth form of that series she had seen, and it was the second one that asked for a figure.**

*Proposed count. Object: persons oath-bound in a border county, by the county's own schedule, and by the stones where the county keeps its stones. Date: the twenty-eighth of the month last. Returnable on or before the twenty-second of this month. Person having knowledge: —. How often: —.*

And a ruled space, and the ruled space is the entire instrument, and there is no column headed name anywhere on the sheet, and that is not an oversight, because the Office has been printing this form for nineteen years and has never once had a name on it.

She had beaten the first of these on the ninth of Greenmonth in nine minutes with the form's own construction, and the construction was this: a count of persons is a return of a figure. A figure is not a person. No person is in default. A thing against which nobody is in default is not refused and is not enforced, and it is therefore spent the moment it is answered, and it requires no further returnable date, and there is nobody in this kingdom who can appeal it because there is nothing in it to appeal.

And there was a second sheet in the bag, and it is the only thing this court has ever received from that office that was not asked for, and it is worth knowing exactly what it is.

One sheet. The office's head on it and the number of the instrument on it and a date on it, the twenty-sixth, two days before the form was dated. And it is in a woman's hand, a small upright hand of the kind that has filled in a great many of these and has never once been asked to say whose hand it is.

*Object: as proposed, and dated the twenty-sixth, which is the day before it was asked for. Returned without waiting for an answer, the Office not being in the habit of waiting.*

And in the space for the figure, a figure: nine thousand two hundred and sixty. And under the space, three lines:

*The count is returned for the border district and not for the single county, the district being eleven counties, and the district being a district of the Office and not a body that can be counted.*

*One of the eleven has returned nothing in eleven years and is returned here at the average of its neighbours.*

*This is not an instrument. It requires no answer, it names no person, and it cannot be refused, because there is nothing in it to be refused. It is a figure and it will be used.*

And a magistrate of this county read that sheet three times on a market Saturday and did not put it in a file for thirteen days, which is the longest she has ever sat on anything, and what she could not get past was the second line.

One of the eleven has returned nothing in eleven years and is returned here at the average of its neighbours.

A county that has never sent a return has been returned. Not at nothing, which would have been a hole, and a hole can be looked at. At the average of two counties on either side of it that do send returns, so that the total is smooth and no line on the sheet shows a place where a place ought to be.

And nobody has done anything wrong. There is no officer to be blamed and no duty unperformed. The office cannot say whose figure the average is, in the same way and for the same reason that it cannot say whose figure a printed warning at the foot of a form is, and those are the same fact and this court has now met it twice in a year.

*"**A figure is cheaper than a name**,"* she said, to the empty room, in the voice of a woman reading out a sentence somebody else has said, *"and there is not one instrument in this kingdom that can make it more expensive, and I have been arguing with that sentence since I was thirty-one, and I have just been shown what it looks like when it is finished and put in a bag with a form that is asking me a question I am going to beat in four minutes."*

Then she filed it, because a magistrate who does not file is a magistrate with a document in her house, and in a house it can be lost, and in a file it can be found by somebody who is looking for it, and she would rather it were found by somebody who is looking for it.

**And on the Saturday morning, at about the eleventh hour, in a market that runs in one street with the water on the left of it, the first person in four years and a half came to that court door about a Crown form, and he was not a liar and he was not a claimant, and he was a man of about fifty who keeps the sluice at the upper end of the western channel and who had been told in a market on a Thursday that the Office of Provincial Registration is counting people in a border county.**

He stood in the doorway and did not come in, which is what a person does when they have heard there is a shilling.

"I am not here about a shilling," he said. "I am here because a man at a sluice said it out loud in a market on Thursday and said it was oath-bound persons, and I have a brother nine miles up the western channel, in the last house before the upper lock, and I have not seen him in nine years and he is not a fool, and I want to know one thing. Is this county in it, or is that county in it, or is it a county nobody has heard of."

And she could not tell him, and the not telling took about four seconds, and the four seconds are on the page in her own minute because a magistrate who does not enter a thing she did in a doorway has not done the thing.

"I have a form in front of me that asks for a figure about a border county, and it does not name the county, and there are eleven counties in that district and eleven is more than one, and the district is a district of an office and not a body, and I have not been told which of the eleven it is, and I have written a question about it and the question has gone and the question has not been answered, and I am going to tell you what I can tell you, which is that no person is in default and nothing is pending and I will not be able to tell you in a year either, and you may come back and I will be standing in this room saying the same four sentences, and that is not me being careful. **That is the shape of the thing. A figure with a place on it and nothing to put a person in, and a person comes to the door and asks to be a person in it, and there is nowhere to put him.**"

He thought about it for a while, on the step.

"That is what they said about the register," he said. "In the winter. A man said the whole county was on a roll and there was no name on it anywhere and I said my brother is not on a roll, he is in a bed in his own house, and the man said that is the same thing as far as a form is concerned. **And I have thought about that for nine months and I still do not know whether he was right.**"

"He was right as far as a form is concerned," said Lysa Fenn, "and I would take it further than he did, and I have no power to take it further, and I am not going to pretend otherwise to a man on a step."

He did not give a name, and she did not ask for one, and the name went into the door-book and not into a register, and this court has kept a door-book since the first year and has never had a column in it, and there have been eleven entries in it in four years and a half that are *no name given*, and that is a different book from the one that office is filling, and the minute of it says: *a person was not asked for a name by this court and the reason is that there is no column for it, and the reason there is no column for it is that a column for it would be on a form the Office prints, and this court does not print forms, and that has been the whole of this court's protection since the first year and it is a piece of luck and not a policy.*

And for thirteen days nothing happened, which is what a form of that series does, and on the morning of the twenty-second of Rainmonth, which is a Friday and is not a market day, she answered it in about four minutes and it took her four minutes because the second time is always quicker than the first and she wrote that down on the back of the paper where nobody will find it unless somebody goes looking, which is a thing she has now done twice.

The answer was a figure about her own office, because there is no figure about a border county in that building, and because a figure is a figure, and because she is not going to leave a space empty on a form if leaving it empty is what a space is for.

She took the day-book down off the shelf and went to the register of claims of this office, which is the only book in that room with a spine on it, and she footed it in front of the window, column by column, and she did it in the room, because a magistrate who foots a figure in her head has not footed it.

*The register was begun on the first of Greenmonth of last year, which is the month this county made this office a body, and it is ruled in eleven columns and the columns are the office's own and are not a calendar.*

*48. 51. 39. 62. 55. 44. 71. 58. 66. 43. 60.*

*48 and 51 is 99. 99 and 39 is 138. 138 and 62 is 200. 200 and 55 is 255. 255 and 44 is 299. 299 and 71 is 370. 370 and 58 is 428. 428 and 66 is 494. 494 and 43 is 537. 537 and 60 is 597.*

Five hundred and ninety-seven persons entered in the register of this office since the register was begun, of whom not one is in a border county, and not one of them is oath-bound, and the figure is about this court and not about the object named at the top of the sheet, and she wrote it in the ruled space anyway because the space is a space for a figure.

And she checked it before she sealed it, because a magistrate who foots a figure twice foots it once, and the second footing was in the other order and it came to the same and it took about forty seconds:

*60. 43. 66. 58. 71. 44. 55. 62. 39. 51. 48.*
*60 and 43 is 103. 103 and 66 is 169. 169 and 58 is 227. 227 and 71 is 298. 298 and 44 is 342. 342 and 55 is 397. 397 and 62 is 459. 459 and 39 is 498. 498 and 51 is 549. 549 and 48 is 597.*

Five hundred and ninety-seven both ways, and she wrote the two footings on the sheet in the space under the figure and did not blot the second one, because a figure that has been footed once on a page is a figure anybody can check and a figure that has been footed twice is a figure anybody can check *that the officer checked*, and the difference between those two is the whole of what this court has been for nine years.

**And the second paragraph she did not write.** She had got as far as *I would further observe* and she stopped, and the reason she stopped is worth a line in the minute and got one: *because a second paragraph is a speech, and a speech from this court on a count of persons in a county four hundred miles off is a thing an office can quote, and a quoted speech from a county is a figure with a hand on it, and the whole of what this court has done for four years and a half is not being a hand on a figure.*

Then the sentence, in the room's own form of words, because the form has a place for a reason and the place is under the space:

*No person is in default. Nothing is pending. The instrument is spent upon this answer and requires no further returnable date. The figure given is a figure of this court. It is given because the space is a space for a figure and this court has no figure of the object, and a court that has no figure ought to say so in the space rather than rule it.*

She sealed it and it went into the bag at about the seventh hour, and the bag goes on the Tuesday, and in that same bag on that same Tuesday went a fair copy of six lines of chalk off a board of lath and lime which a woman in a room over a weigh-house had put in on the fourth, and neither of the two things in that bag knew about the other, and one of them had cost four minutes and the other one had been in a press in this county since the sixteenth of the month before with no use on it.

**And a body corporate resolved, on the ninth of Greenmonth last year, with a door and a number and the capacity to sue and to be sued, and a woman in a room over a weigh-house did not hear about the average of two counties this month, and a magistrate of this county is frightened about a sentence she made herself, and the sentence is that a court is not a person, and she has looked for the sentence that lets her keep the first half and drop the second half for a year, and there is not one, and it is written on the back of a paper where nobody will find it unless somebody goes looking.**

And at about the fourth hour that morning a road warden of the Merefen bridge guild went out of this county with a tool bag and a book with four columns on it and no column for a name, and no name was written in any Crown's hand in this county on the twenty-second of Rainmonth, and there has not been one written in this county since the twenty-seventh of Fallowmonth, and that is still the only thing in the case that has cost that office nothing and bought it nothing, and it is the reason the office got further in seven months than it got in eleven years.
