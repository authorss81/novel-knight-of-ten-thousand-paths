# Chapter 0466

**The second ebb after he came down off the made road, and the second hour after the water went out, on a foreshore two miles and about nine hundred paces long where a river runs into the sea, and there is no month on that shore and no day of the week on it and there has been neither for about nine years, and eleven houses stand along it and about nine hundred people use it and four of the eleven have a person in them who answers for it and seven do not, and a man of about twenty-nine who has walked four days off a made road has just asked a question out loud at the seaward end and about nine people are standing inside about nine feet of the answer.**

## One

**The man who asked is not a knight and not a man with a chain and not a man with a sheet, and he is twenty-nine and he came nine days with a letter in a coat and has been to four places in nine counties in the last month and has not found out what he came for.**

He stood about nine paces off the seaward end with his hands empty and asked out loud, at the second hour after the water went out, where about nine hundred people could hear him because there is no other conversation on a foreshore at low water.

**What he had in the way with him was a sheet folded in four that had come away from a factor's table in a fen country, and he did not take it out, and about four people within about nine feet of him saw him not take it out, and about four of those four have said since then that they expected him to.**

“**What do you want.**”

## Two

**The woman of about fifty-one had the driftwood in her hand and she had had it in her hand every ebb for about twenty-two years, and there are twenty-two notches cut in the edge of it, one for each year, and the twenty-second is not cut, and it goes in when the water is off a mark that has not been off in twenty-two years.**

She did not answer for about nine seconds. **About nine people heard the nine seconds and about four of them have said since then that they counted them, and nobody counted them, and there is nobody on that foreshore who counts a person's silence any more than anybody else would.**

**The driftwood is about the length of a forearm and about two inches through and it is grey and it has been in that post for about twenty-two years and it is wedged into a driven post about five inches square, and about four inches of the post above the driftwood is cut off flat, and nobody on that sand knows why it is cut off flat, and about four people have asked her about it and she has said nothing four times.**

Then she said the kind thing first, which is not what she does, and the true thing nine seconds after it, which is.

**The two were not in that order this time. The kind thing came first and the true thing came nine seconds after it, and about four people within about nine feet have said since then that that was a difference and about four have said since then that it was not, and the one who said it was not is the man of about twenty-nine and he has not been asked to say it twice.**

## Three

“**Nobody has asked me one thing in twenty-two years,**” she said, “**and I have never once wanted to be asked for anything, and I want to be asked for a day.**”

“**I want to be asked for a day by somebody and told no. I want it in the winter and I want to still be here after it, and I do not want a stone and I do not want a head on anything, and I want the count of that shore to stay exactly where it is, which is in me and in about nine heads of about nine people and nowhere else at all.**”

**And that is a hundred and five words in two turns of one speech, of which the first is twenty-nine and the second is seventy-six, and it is the whole of what she said, and about nine people heard all of it, and about four of them are the kind of person who will carry it, and one of those four has already carried the other thing she said once, at an ebb, to nobody, in a month he did not name.**

## Four

And then she said the second part of it, which was not part of the answer and was about him and about the four places.

“**You have been to four places that all had somebody's job in them,**” she said. “**You have come four days to a shore where there is nobody's job at all, and you have asked me, and I have answered, and now you will go up the made road again and tell somebody that a woman on this sand wants to be asked for a day.**”

**About nine people were inside about nine feet and all nine of them heard it and not one of them repeated it that day, and about four of them were going to repeat it that night, and one of those four is the woman of about thirty-four with a barrow who took nine words off a handcart about seven weeks back and put them in the front of her shirt and has said no once to a man of about twenty-nine who asked her whether she was going to answer them.**

And the man of about twenty-nine said that he had not come to tell anybody.

And she said that he would, and that everybody does, and that she had said one thing out loud on that shore before today and that the one had gone four ways before the water came back in, and that this one would go further because he had come further.

## Five

The cost of that ebb is a column and it is the man of about twenty-nine's and it is the sixth and he has not folded the six of them up together and has not written a total against any of the six and has a piece of driftwood with twenty-two notches in the edge of it in front of him and did not touch it.

**a loaf at one penny, a second loaf at one, a pound of cheese at four, a pound of wood at three, a tallow candle at two, a room at six, a sheet of paper at one, a quill at one.** One, one, four, three, two, six, one and one is nineteen pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and nineteen pence is under two-fifths of one by one**, and a foreshore is not a road and is not in any column, and about nine hundred people on two miles and about nine hundred paces are not in it and are not a figure of men, and a want that has been said out loud is not in it and is not in any book in nine counties.

**And a woman of about fifty-one was asked at an ebb what she wants by a man of about twenty-nine who is not a knight, and she said that she wanted to be asked for a day and told no, and about nine people heard it and about four of them will carry it, and a woman of sixty-three with a stick and a bowl of brine was sitting at the back of the fourth house four hundred yards up the sand and was not told and will not be told, and the water came in at about the ninth hour and went over about two hundred paces of the seaward half and went back at about the fourth hour of the morning with the wind still against it.**
