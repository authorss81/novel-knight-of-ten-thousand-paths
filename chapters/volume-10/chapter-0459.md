# Chapter 0459

**The twenty-sixth day of Frostmonth, YR 318, a Wednesday and not a market day, and the second hour and the sixth hour and the ninth hour, in one room over a weigh-house with a door on a lane, in a county about two hundred miles off a street of working yards and about four days off a foreshore, where a press has stood open since a day nobody will name, and a woman of twenty-nine is in that room, and a man of thirty-two with a limp is in it and came into it two days ago, and there is a sheet of about nine lines on the bench and about nine of them have figures on them and none of the figures is a name.**

## One

**A letter came in with the cart on the twenty-third day and it is four lines and one of the four lines is a question, and the question is: whether a thing that is a road can be entered in a column that wants a place, a figure of men and a trade, and whether a person can answer for it, and a man of about thirty-four with a sheet wrote it and it came through a clerk of about nineteen in his second year in a town of about two thousand who has never seen the foreshore and will not.**

The keeper of the two figures on the bench that were hers and about seven that were not hers did not answer the letter, and she did not answer it in a sentence or in a paragraph, and she read it twice and then she asked a man in her room a question she has not asked him in about a year.

**She asked him to say a sentence out loud. He did not know why and he said one.**

## Two

“**Say that again,**” she said, and he said it again, and it came out of him the way it had come out of him on a foreshore on the eighth of this month, in front of the same woman with a barrow, and about four hundred people on a coast about four hundred miles off speak it that way every day and have never been asked about it.

Nobody in that room said a word about it and she looked at him for about nine seconds and then she said the thing that is the whole of what a keeper of records is.

“**I have four years and a half of returns in this room,**” she said, “**and about nine hundred a month go out of it into nine counties with a head on them, and if a woman of about thirty-four wrote down the way you just said a sentence then I would have to copy it, and a copy of how a person says a word is a thing about a person that anybody can be shown to a person.**”

“**I know,**” he said.

“**You do not know it,**” she said, “**because you have never had to keep a record and I have, and the difference between the two is about four years and a half and it is not a small one.**”

## Three

**And a man of thirty-two with a limp could not place a room, and the room was a kitchen with a blacked-in hearth and a window that did not shut, and it is a kitchen in a fen town of about four thousand people, four hundred yards up a street of working yards from a yard of nine men, and it is his mother's kitchen and he has not been in it since he was nineteen.**

He was asked about it by nobody. He was thinking about it at about the sixth hour, in a room over a weigh-house, having been asked a question about a column, and what he had in his head was a window that did not shut and a blacked-in hearth and a door off the yard and a woman standing at that door with a bowl in her hand, and every part of that kitchen was correct and about two of the details of it came from a courtyard on the other side of a salt-store.

**He has a page in a notebook with eleven lines on it and no words on any of them, and on the twenty-fourth day he took it out at about the seventh hour and wrote on the eleventh line, which has never been used, and what he wrote was the word for the blacked-in hearth in the courtyard, which is not the word for a blacked-in hearth in a fen kitchen, and the two are the same word in about four hundred languages and neither of them is the word he uses.**

**And he did not cross anything out, and he did not add a date, and he did not add a name, and the eleventh line has never been used before and is used now and there is no twelfth line, and that is the entire entry.**

## Four

**And what he has done in about four weeks is three crossings against a budget of about three a season, and a woman of about fifty-one has not asked him for any of them and a woman of about thirty-four has asked him for two of them and a man of about twenty-nine has paid for none of them and knows about two of them.**

The second one was four days and began on the nineteenth day of this month, in a courtyard, and the third was two days and began on the twenty-fourth, in a room with a stove in it where about nine people were, and nobody on that side asked him any question at all, and the second one he was paid in food and the third one he was not paid in anything and did not ask.

**And the whole of what he has lost in four weeks is this. He cannot tell which of two kitchens he is remembering, and the room over a weigh-house smells like neither of them, and on the twenty-fourth day a woman of twenty-nine said a sentence in an accent that is about four hundred miles from any town she has worked in and did not notice that she had done it and has not noticed yet.**

## Five

The cost of those four weeks is a column and it is his and it is the first one in four weeks and there are about nine lines on a bench in that room and about seven of the nine figures are his and the other two are hers and none of the nine is a name.

**a loaf at one penny, a second loaf at one, a third loaf at one, a pound of cheese at four, a second pound of cheese at four, a pound of wood at three, a second pound of wood at three, a third pound of wood at three, a tallow candle at two, a second tallow candle at two, a third tallow candle at two, a fourth tallow candle at two, two quarts of small beer at two and two, a bushel of oats at four, a second bushel of oats at four, a broom at five, a bucket of tar at eight, a quill and ink at seven, a room at six, a sheet of paper at one.** One, one, one, four, four, three, three, three, two, two, two, two, two, two, four, four, five, eight, seven, six and one is sixty-seven pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and sixty-seven pence is a mark and nineteen over**, and three crossings against a budget of about three a season is not a figure of persons and is not in any column in any county, and nine figures on a bench are not in it and one of the nine is a keeper of records's and about seven of the nine are his and none of the nine is a name.

**And a man of thirty-two with a limp sat in a room over a weigh-house with a door on a lane and could not say which of two kitchens he was in, and a woman of twenty-nine told him that a copy of how a person says a word is a thing about a person, and she was right and he knew she was right and neither of them said one word about the accent, and about nine hundred sheets a month go out of that room into nine counties and about a hundred and four went out on the fixed Saturday, the twenty-second of this month, in one band tied at a corner with a cord, and the two are counts of two different series and are not added and are not converted into one another, and the eleventh line of a notebook has one word on it that is the right word for the wrong room.**
