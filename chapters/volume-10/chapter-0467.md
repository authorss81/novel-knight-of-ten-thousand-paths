# Chapter 0467

**The second ebb after the one where he asked the woman at the seaward end, and the third hour after the water went out, four hundred yards up the sand from the seaward end, at the back of the fourth house on a low wall four feet high that faces the wrong way for the water and faces the light, where a woman of sixty-three with a stick and a bowl of brine has been sitting for about nine years and has not come down to that sand in about nine years, and a man of about twenty-nine who asked a question out loud at the seaward end two days ago has walked up here to ask the same one of her.**

## One

**Nobody introduced them and nobody came with him and the only things between the two of them are nine feet of sand, a bowl of brine and a stick, and nine people on that foreshore can hear both ends of a conversation at once, which is the reason he walked up here at all and the reason he had to.**

She had the bowl of brine beside her on the wall and the crust came off it in one piece when she turned it over, and she had not come down to the sand since she was about fifty-four, and there are about nine hundred people on two miles of that sand and four of them have said out loud that they would not come up this wall either.

**The pan is a foot and a half across and it is set in brick behind the house and the fire under it has not been out since a month in a year she does not give, and the fourth house is one of the four of the eleven with a person in it who answers for it, and the answering is not for the fire and is not for the pan and is for the landing slip at the front door, which is a piece of board four inches wide with nine notches cut in one edge of it, and nine people go past that door for every one that goes in and four of them have ever asked what the board is for.**

## Two

He asked her the same question he had asked at the seaward end, in the same words, and he said before he asked it that he was going to ask her the same thing he had asked the other woman because he was not going to work out a better way to ask a thing after six times.

She did not look at him, and nine seconds went by before she said anything, and four people on that sand counted them afterwards and did not tell anybody they had counted them.

“**No,**” she said.

**And that is one word, and she said it in two seconds, and nine people heard it and four of them thought she had refused the question and four of them did not.**

## Three

Then she went on turning the bowl over for nine seconds and did not say anything else for nine seconds, and the man of about twenty-nine stood at the wall and did not fill them, and four people on that sand have said since then that he could have and did not and none of them has said it to him.

“**I have been at this fire forty-one years,**” she said, “**and you are the fourth person to come up this wall and the first one to ask me that, and nobody has asked me what I want since I was about twenty-two years old, and I am not going to tell you, and I am going to tell you anyway, and I want it understood that those are two different things and that I have not been asked anything else.**”

## Four

“**I want the road to stay exactly as it is.**”

**And that is ten words in one turn of one speech, and it is the whole of what she wanted, and she had said the first part before the want and the first part was longer than the want.**

And he asked her what she meant by exactly, which was the only question he asked her, and she said that she meant that the eleventh house stays the eleventh house and that the ninth keeps its lease and that the seventh keeps not having anybody in it, and that a man of about thirty-four with a sheet comes down that sand when he comes and that he does not get a key for anything, and that in a year or in nine there will be a figure of men against it and she will be seventy and she will have been the oldest person on it since she was twenty-two.

**And he said thank you, and she said that she did not want thanks for an answer she had given away in ten words, and that nine people on that sand heard him say the two words, and that four of them have said since then that they were the correct two words and four of them think they were the wrong two words and that neither of those four has ever said so to him.**

And then she said what she thought of his four days, and it was the same sentence the woman at the seaward end had said to him on the sand four hundred yards below two days before and had said it loudly enough for a woman of sixty-three on a wall to hear every word of it, which is what happens on two miles of hard sand.

**And it was not kind and it was not unkind and it was the first thing anybody on that foreshore had said out loud in about nine years about a thing that came from the mainland.**

“**You have been to four places that all had somebody's job in them,**” she said, “**and you have come up a wall to ask two women who have got nothing, and I would like you to go down and ask the one at the seaward end again, because you did that in front of nine people and she has been alone with it for two days.**”

## Five

The cost of those two days is a column and it is the man of about twenty-nine's and it is the seventh and he has seven of them now and has still not put a total against any of the seven and has not written one of the seven down.

**a loaf at one penny, a second loaf at one, a third loaf at one, a pound of cheese at four, a second pound of cheese at four, a pound of wood at three, a second pound of wood at three, a tallow candle at two, four quarts of small beer at four, a room at six, a bed at four, a second night's bed at four, a pot of cheese at six, a loaf for the road at one, a pound of cheese for the road at four, a sheet of paper at one, a quill and ink at three, a basket at three.** One, one, one, four, four, three, three, two, four, six, four, four, six, one, four, one, three and three is fifty-five pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and fifty-five pence is a mark and seven over**, and forty-one years of a fire that has not gone out is not in it and is not a figure of men, and a want of ten words is not in it and is not in any column in any county and is not going to be in one.

**And a woman of sixty-three with a stick and a bowl of brine told a man of about twenty-nine that she wanted the road to stay exactly as it is, after saying no for two seconds and after saying six sentences of why she was going to answer anyway, and nine people on that foreshore heard the whole of it and four of them are the kind of person who will carry it, and a piece of driftwood with twenty-two notches cut in the edge of it is at the seaward end with the twenty-second not cut, and the water came in at about the ninth hour and went back at about the fourth hour of the morning with the wind still against it.**
