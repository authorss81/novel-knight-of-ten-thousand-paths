# Chapter 0475

**The twenty-third day of Thawmonth, YR 319, a Saturday and a market day, and the third hour and the sixth hour and the eleventh hour, at the seventh of eleven houses on a foreshore two miles and about nine hundred paces long where a river runs into the sea, and there is no month in anybody's mouth on that shore and no day of the week, and four people in the house that nobody there will talk about have been in a fever since the tide before last, and about nine hundred people are on that sand and about nine hundred people are paid twice in seven days in a room four days up a made road, and the two figures are the same number and are not the same people and are not added to one another in any room in nine counties.**

## One

**The house at the seventh of the eleven is one of the seven with nobody standing in it, and a man of about fifty-three with a wagon has never once said why he will not stop at it, and on the third hour of that Saturday four people in it were lying down and one of them was talking, and the talking had a sound in it that nine people on two miles of hard sand could hear, because there is no other conversation on a foreshore at low water and there never has been.**

The woman of about thirty-four with a barrow was the first one across, and she went in and came out in about two minutes, and she did not go in a second time for an hour, and when she came out the second time she had a sheet of oiled paper in her hands that had been round nine pounds of salt nine days before.

She said one thing and she said it to nobody in particular and nine people heard it.

“**That is the fever that came up the road,**” she said. “**It is in a wagon of shell and it is in the seventh house and there is nowhere on this sand to send it and nobody on this sand to send it to.**”

## Two

**And here is the whole of what nineteen years of a road nobody owns has cost, and it did not cost anything for nineteen years and it cost something on the third hour of that Saturday, and the something is four people in a house and no address.**

The thing that arrived is not a word and it is not a song. A word has been up that road nine days and a song has been up it four days, and this went up it in a wagon with nine cart-loads of shell in it and it did not ask anybody.

**A road that nobody owns has no one to tell, and that is the whole of what a keeper is for; the shore said no to a keeper in about nine minutes on five grounds and one of the five was that nobody would be answerable, and the fever has proved the five grounds right and has proved them right the wrong way round, and nine people on that sand have understood that inside one hour and have not said so out loud.**

The woman of about thirty-four with a barrow said it out loud at the sixth hour, to a man of about thirty-eight who rows one of the four boats, and he asked her the question a man asks.

“**So we want one?**”

And she said no, and she said it fast, and she said the rest of it after that.

## Three

“**I have been alone with that for about two hours and I have had it worked out and I want it written down that I worked it out,**” she said. “**A keeper would have given that fever an address in about nine days. I am not saying it would have been a good address. I am saying it would have been one, and a thing with an address is a thing you can be told about, and right now that fever is in a house on this sand that four of us will not name, and it has been there two days, and every one of us who knows about it is standing on a sand telling another one of us about it.**”

“**And that is the cost of what we said no to, and I am not turning about, and if you ask me in a year I will say the same, and the reason I am not turning about is that a man four days up that road is going to want to put a page on it and I have seen what a page does to a number.**”

**And that is a hundred and eighty-nine words in two turns of one speech, of which the first is a hundred and twenty-one, printed in two spans of twenty-eight and ninety-three, and the second is sixty-eight, and a man of about thirty-eight who rows one of the four boats did not agree with her and did not disagree with her and has said since then that he was thinking about a boat and was not.**

## Four

**What happened on that sand between the sixth hour and the eleventh hour was the eleven houses, and nobody on that sand called it that, and it is the only instrument anybody in nine counties has found in five blocks that is not a person.**

The pan at the fourth house was the only fire on that sand that had not been let out in forty-one years, and the woman of sixty-three was at the back of it with her stick and her bowl of brine, and she boiled water and she did not say why and she was not asked.

The house at the ninth has a man in it with a lease. The house at the eleventh has a widow in it with the last of a boat. **Four of the eleven had somebody's work happening in them and seven did not, and the four of them were where a sick person could be taken, and about nine hundred people found that out in about two hours and about four of them had found it out before the third hour.**

Nobody made a list. There is no list and there was no meeting and there is no room on that sand, and about nine hundred people did what they did because eleven houses are there and four of them are standing in.

**And a woman of about thirty-four with a barrow said at the eleventh hour that the four of the eleven had been the answer for nineteen years without anybody putting it in a column, and four people heard her and about nine hundred people had already found it out at once, and the two things happened within about four minutes of one another and neither of them was arranged.**

## Five

The cost of that Saturday is a column and it is the woman of about thirty-four with a barrow's, and it is the first one she has written down, and she wrote it on the back of a handcart docket with a nail, and a fever is not in it and about nine hundred people is not in it and is not a figure of men.

**a barrow with a new wheel at nine pence, a pound of rope at six, a waxed cord at four, a tallow candle at two, a second tallow candle at two, a loaf at one, a second loaf at one, a third loaf at one, a fourth loaf at one, a pound of cheese at four, a second pound of cheese at four, a third pound of cheese at four, a pound of wood at three, a second pound of wood at three, a third pound of wood at three, a quart of small beer at two, a second quart of small beer at two, a third quart of small beer at two, a sheet of paper at one, a second sheet of paper at one, a quill and ink at three, a broom at five, a bucket of tar at eight, a second bucket of tar at eight, a pound of nails at three, a second pound of nails at three, a pair of gloves at two, a second pair of gloves at two, a night's lodging at the fourth house at two, a second night's lodging at the fourth house at two, a pound of candles at four, a second pound of candles at four, and an inkwell at three.** Nine, six, four, two, two, one, one, one, one, four, four, four, three, three, three, two, two, two, one, one, three, five, eight, eight, three, three, two, two, two, two, four, four and three is one hundred and five pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and five pence is two marks and nine over**, and about nine hundred people on that sand and about nine hundred people paid twice in seven days in a room four days up a made road are not in it and are not added to one another, and a fever that came up a road in a wagon of shell is not in it, and four of eleven houses is not in it and four and seven are never added to eleven in a column.

**And four people in a house that nobody on that sand will name were lying down on a Saturday in the worst month to come and about nine hundred people on two miles and about nine hundred paces of hard sand found out what a road nobody owns is for, and about four of them found out before the third hour and about nine hundred found out at once, and nine cart-loads of shell went off four boats into four pans on the tide after, and the fire behind the fourth house had not been out since a month in a year a woman of sixty-three does not give, and the water came in at about the ninth hour and went over about two hundred paces of the seaward half and went back at about the fourth hour of the morning with the wind still against it.**
