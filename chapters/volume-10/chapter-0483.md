# Chapter 0483

**The twenty-eighth day of Mudmonth, YR 319, a Saturday and a market day, and the second hour and the fifth hour and the eighth hour, at the ninth of eleven houses on a foreshore two miles and about nine hundred paces long where a river runs into the sea, and there is no month in anybody's mouth on that shore and no day of the week, and the salt wagon of the man of about fifty-three, nineteen years off and on that sand, came down on the ebb and is standing at the ninth of the eleven with the horses in the water, and the man in the doorway has come out onto the step, which he has not done in nine weeks.**

## One

**A wagon year is about two hundred days and twenty-seven pence a day the wrong way, which is five thousand four hundred pence a year, and the ninth house did not buy for nine weeks, and a sixth of five thousand four hundred pence is nine hundred pence, and both halves of that sum were worked out in nine seconds by a woman of about thirty-four with a barrow in a book in a market town four days off and about nine hundred paces of hard sand from where the wagon now stands.**

She is not on this sand. She has not been on this sand since the fourteenth day of this month, and the number she worked out came back to a carter and the carter brought it up a made road and put it on the ninth house, and the ninth house has had it about a fortnight and has not acted on it.

**A man with nine hundred acres inland has come out onto the step of his own doorway with his hat in his hand and has not said anything, and nine people on that sand have seen him do it and about four of the nine had not seen him come out of that door since a month in a year a woman of sixty-three does not give.**

## Two

The wagon is at the off wheel and the tipping takes nine minutes and the man of about fifty-three is not offering a hand and has not offered one in nineteen years, and the man in the doorway has not come down the step.

“**Three sacks!**” the man of about fifty-three said.

“**I know what three sacks is.**”

“**Then you know I am not asking you for them.**”

“**I have not bought for nine weeks,**” the man in the doorway said, “**and I am standing on my own step in front of nine people, and if you say the figure out loud on this sand then every one of those nine is going to remember it and about four of them are going to be surprised and about four of them are not, and I would rather you said it to the wagon.**”

**And that is eighty-seven words in four turns of one exchange, of which the first is two, the second is six, the third is ten, and the fourth is sixty-nine printed in two spans of seven and sixty-two, and the man of about fifty-three said the first three turns without stopping and the man in the doorway said the fourth without stopping either, and nine people heard the whole of it and four of them were carrying a basket or a barrow and none of the four said anything afterwards.**

## Three

**And what the man of about fifty-three did about the figure is the whole of what that tide is about, and he did not say it to the wagon and he did not say it at all, and nine people on that sand worked out on their own in four minutes that nobody was going to be told.**

He put his hand on the near rim, which has been cut down twice, and he looked at the water for about as long as it takes a man to say nine, and then he counted the sacks on the wagon out loud one at a time the way a man does when he is not sure and gets it right.

“**One,**” he said, and he put his hand on the first. “**Two.**” And then he stopped and did not say three, and four people were waiting for it and four were not, and the man in the doorway stayed on the step and did not come down and did not go in.

“**You did not buy for nine weeks,**” the man of about fifty-three said, “**and I did not lose nine hundred pence. You did not buy for nine weeks and I did not lose nine hundred pence. A man said a thing about a wall on a made road four days up and a carter heard it and you are the careful sort, and I have not got a figure for what that is, and the only figure I have got is the one a woman wrote in a book with a barrow standing at the end of a quay, and she wrote it wrong, and she wrote it wrong on purpose, and she is the only person I have ever met who got a thing right by getting it wrong on purpose.**”

**And that is a hundred and twenty-eight words in three turns of one speech, of which the first is one, the second is one, and the third is a hundred and twenty-six printed in two spans of seven and a hundred and nineteen, and the man of about fifty-three has not said a hundred and twenty-six words out loud on that sand in nineteen years, and the man in the doorway came down the step about halfway through the third one and stood at the off wheel and did not touch anything.**

## Four

And the ninth house bought three sacks, and it bought them at nine pence a sack, and it had bought them at nine pence a sack for eleven years, and the man with nine hundred acres inland did not argue about the figure and did not remark on the nine weeks and has not remarked on the nine weeks to anybody since.

**He said one thing and he said it to the man of about fifty-three and not to the nine, and it was that he had not stopped buying because of the man with the sheet, and that he had stopped buying because a wall of stone had appeared at a hundred paces from the landward end of a road that has no line on it, and that a careful man does not put his salt on a road that has just been told about.**

“**I would have gone on buying at nine pence a sack if the wall had not gone in,**” he said. “**I have got a boat and four sacks of salt in it and I have had them since a month in a year I am not going to give. I have been putting the cart down and picking it up for nine weeks, and the ninth of the eleven has been standing still for nine weeks, and nobody on this sand has put that on anybody and I am not going to either.**”

“**That is the first time in nine weeks you have said a thing about the nine weeks out loud,**” the man of about fifty-three said, “**and you have said it to me and not to the nine, and I am not going to say it back to the nine, and that is the arrangement and it is nine weeks old and it has cost the two of us a figure nobody has said.**”

**And that is a hundred and fifty-eight words in two turns of one exchange, of which the first is ninety-one printed in two spans of eighteen and seventy-three, and the second is sixty-seven printed in two spans of nineteen and forty-eight, and a woman of about thirty-four with a barrow is not on that sand and was not told and would not have thanked anybody, and a man of about thirty-two with a limp is not on that sand either and has not been told and has said nothing about the ninth house in about a fortnight.**

## Five

The cost of that Saturday is a column and it is the man of about fifty-three's, and it is the second one he has written down in nineteen years and it is on the back of a handcart docket with a nail, and nine hundred pence is not in it and the salt is not in it and the money is not in the salt twice.

**forage for two horses at eighteen pence, tar and shoeing at six, a farrier at three, the driver's food at five, a second loaf at one, a third loaf at one, a quart of small beer at two, a tallow candle at two, a sheet of paper at one, a second sheet of paper at one, a quill and ink at three, a pound of nails at three, a broom at five, a night's lodging at the fourth house at two, a second night's lodging at the fourth house at two, a pound of wood at three, an inkwell at three, a tin of oil at two, a hook at one, a bucket of tar at eight and a pound of candles at four.** Eighteen, six, three, five, one, one, two, two, one, one, three, three, five, two, two, three, three, two, one, eight and four is seventy-six pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and seventy-six pence is a mark and twenty-eight over**, and nine weeks and nine hundred pence and a sixth of a wagon year are not in it, and four men who came down that sand on a Friday with a crowbar and a shovel and a sledge and a barrow are not in it and are not a figure of men, and three sacks a trip at nine pence a sack is not in it and is a price the ninth house has paid for eleven years without once arguing about it.

**And the ninth of the eleven bought three sacks on the twenty-eighth day of Mudmonth after nine weeks of not buying, and the man in the doorway came down the step halfway through a hundred and twenty-six words and stood at the off wheel, and nine people on that sand worked out in four minutes that nobody was going to be told a figure, and the two of them said the arrangement out loud and it is nine weeks old and it has cost them something that is not on a docket with a nail on it, and a man with nine hundred acres inland has a boat and four sacks of salt in it and a lease on a house that stood still for nine weeks while a wall of eleven paces took the tide over it twice a day, and about the ninth hour the sea came up over the salt in the wagon bed and over the sand the man of about fifty-three was standing on, took about two hundred paces of the seaward half, and went out again some time before four in the morning.**
