# Chapter 0480

**The nineteenth day of Mudmonth, YR 319, a Thursday and a market day, and the second hour and the fifth hour and the ninth hour, on a foreshore two miles and about nine hundred paces long where a river runs into the sea, and Mudmonth is the worst month for roads, and there is no month in anybody's mouth on that shore and no day of the week, and a cart of wood that comes up a made road on a fixed day was due at the landward end before the light and is not there, and four people knew it would not be there and about nine hundred people found out at the fifth hour.**

## One

**The water is off about four hours out of twelve on that sand and it has been about four hours out of twelve all winter, and Mudmonth is not shorter than any other month. Mudmonth is soft.**

The last half mile of the made road at the landward end is over ground that was a hard bank in Thawmonth and is nine inches of black sand in Mudmonth, and a loaded cart that goes down on the ebb sits in it, and a cart that is sitting in it does not come back on the flood.

**A man of about twenty-three who rows one of the four boats worked that out in about four minutes at the second hour, standing at the top of the sand with a boot in the black, and two people on that sand had worked it out before him and about nine hundred had not.**

The first had been the man of about fifty-three with a wagon, at the second hour, before the light, to nobody. The second had been the woman of about thirty-four with a barrow, who had been up the made road and back twice in nine days and had said so to a man of about thirty-two with a limp on one of those two trips.

He was the third to say it out loud and he said it to a man of about forty who was standing at the boat land with a basket, and the man of about forty had been on that sand for eleven days and had not heard it said by anybody.

“**The last half mile of that road is not a road in this month,**” the man of about twenty-three said, “**and a cart that goes down in it is not coming back on the flood, and I have been in it up to here yesterday and I could have told you at the time.**”

“**I have known it three years,**” the man of about forty said, “**and I did not say, because the last three years it has not come to that.**”

“**Then say it now,**” the man of about twenty-three said, “**and do not wait to be asked twice!**”

**And that is eighty-two words in three turns of one exchange, of which the first is forty-eight printed in two spans of fourteen and thirty-four, the second is twenty-two printed in two spans of six and sixteen, and the third is twelve printed in two spans of four and eight, and the man of about forty has said since then that he had not wanted to be the man who says a thing like that in a month when everybody is short.**

The fourth of the four was a woman of sixty-three at the back of the fourth house, who said nothing at all and put a third armful of wood on a fire that has not been out since a month in a year she does not give.

## Two

**And then about nine hundred people found out at once, at about the fifth hour, and the way they found out was that a cart came round the head of the bay at the fifth hour with about half a load on it and no driver on the box, and a driver walking beside one wheel.**

There is a piece of driftwood about the length of a forearm and two inches through wedged in a driven post at the seaward end, with twenty-two notches cut in the edge of it, one for each year, the twenty-second not cut, and it goes in when the water goes off a mark that has not been off in twenty-two years.

**The water did not go off that mark on the first of Thawmonth and it did not go off it on the nineteenth of Mudmonth either, and the twenty-second notch is still not cut, and nine people on that sand understood at the fifth hour that the year had turned and had not been marked, and about nine hundred people at the fifth hour understood that there was no wood coming.**

## Three

**What about nine hundred people do when there is no wood coming on a foreshore in Mudmonth is what they did in a winter about four years ago, and in a winter about nine years before that, and the arrangement for it is not written down and has never been written down and is eleven houses.**

Four of the eleven have somebody's work happening in them. Seven of them do not.

**And a wood fire does not care whose house it is in, and that is the whole of the instrument, and it took four years ago and about nine years before that to be sure of, and about nine hundred people found it out at the fifth hour and about four of them had found it out before the third hour.**

Nobody made a list. There is no list and there is no room and there was no meeting. **The seventh of the eleven is one of the seven and it had four people in it lying down in Thawmonth with a fever in it, and it had nine people in it by the fourth hour on a first of a month because there was a cart that was not coming, and it had nine people in it again on this afternoon, and the man of about fifty-three has never said why he will not stop at it and did not say on this afternoon either.**

## Four

**Aren Kest was on that sand from the seventh hour and was not asked for anything, and he carried one armful of wood up from the boat land to the fourth house and put it down, and a woman of about twenty-seven with a basket said thank you to him and not to anybody else, and he said that she was welcome, and that is the whole of what he did on that sand in three visits and he is not the reason four of eleven houses are standing in.**

He did not say anything about a price and he did not say anything about a keeper and he did not ask the man of about fifty-three anything at all, and the man of about fifty-three did not tell him anything, and four people saw the two of them not do a thing and three of the four have said since then that they were waiting for one of them to start.

**And nine cart-loads of shell went off four boats into four pans on the ebb before the cart was sighted, and the man of about fifty-three counted every one of them and sent no boat back empty and told nobody why, and about four of the nine hundred people were carrying wood at the same time, and the tide came in at about the ninth hour and went over about two hundred paces of the seaward half and went back at about the fourth hour of the morning with the wind still against it.**

## Five

The cost of that Thursday is a column and it is the man of about twenty-three's, and he is the third of the four who worked it out and he has four of them in a fold in the inside of a coat and has not joined them up, and nine hundred people and eleven houses and four cart-loads are not in it and are not a figure of men.

**a sheet of paper at one penny, a second sheet of paper at one, a quill and ink at three, a loaf at one, a second loaf at one, a third loaf at one, a pound of cheese at four, a second pound of cheese at four, a pound of wood at three, a second pound of wood at three, a tallow candle at two, a second tallow candle at two, a quart of small beer at two, a second quart of small beer at two, a third quart of small beer at two, a room at six, a bed at four, a second night's bed at four, a handcart for the day at four, a basket at five, a broom at five, a pound of rope at six, a pair of gloves at two, a second pair of gloves at two, a pound of nails at three, a bucket of tar at eight, an inkwell at three, a pound of candles at four, a second pound of candles at four, and a third pound of candles at four.** One, one, three, one, one, one, four, four, three, three, two, two, two, two, two, six, four, four, four, five, five, six, two, two, three, eight, three, four, four and four is ninety-six pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and ninety-six pence is two marks exactly**, and four of eleven houses and seven of eleven houses are not in it and are never added to eleven in a column, and about nine hundred people on two miles and about nine hundred paces of hard sand are not in it, and nine inches of black sand over a hard bank is not in it and is not a rate.

**And on the nineteenth day of Mudmonth a cart of wood came round the head of the bay with half a load on it and a man walking beside one wheel, and four people on that sand had known before the light and about nine hundred found out at the fifth hour, and four of the eleven houses are standing in, and the fire behind the fourth house has not been out since a month in a year a woman of sixty-three does not give, and a piece of driftwood in a post at the seaward end has twenty-two notches cut in the edge of it, one for each year, the twenty-second not cut, and four boats went out on the ebb and all four of them came back with sand on the boards, and the water came in at about the ninth hour and went over about two hundred paces of the seaward half and went back at about the fourth hour of the morning with the wind still against it.**
