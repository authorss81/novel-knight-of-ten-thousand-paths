# Chapter 0469

**The twenty-ninth day of Goatmonth, YR 318, a Wednesday and not a market day, and the fourth hour and the seventh hour and the eleventh hour, at the landward end of a foreshore two miles and about nine hundred paces long, in front of a weigh-house and a slipway and about four hundred yards of quay in a port of about nine hundred people, where three people have come down the made road four days to find out what their own three rooms will carry, and a man of about thirty-two with a limp is standing at about the two hundredth pace with a stick he cannot lean on and did not come down that road with anybody.**

## One

**Three rooms, and they are three rooms and they are not a body and there is no chair in this either, and what came down the made road was four clauses about a wagon in a channel and not one word about anything else.**

The four clauses are the ones that about nine people agreed on in about three hours in a room over a weigh-house that is four days' walk from this quay and about four of the nine have said since then that it is not an agreement and about four have said since then that it is the whole of one.

Each of the three rooms has to say it out loud at its own end to anybody who asks. That is all it has to do and that is all any of the three was asked to do.

**The three of them who came down that made road walked it together at the start and not at the end. Two of them had come from the same end and had walked it together for about nine years as a matter of course. The third had come from the other end and had walked about four days of made road alone each way and had got used to being alone on a road about two days before anybody thought to ask him whether he wanted company.**

## Two

The room at the landward end will carry it and has already said it out loud once, on the morning after the meeting, to a carter who had come off the sand at the wrong hour and did not want to be charged for the half of it.

**The room at the seaward end will carry it and will say it at the next ebb, and the woman of about fifty-one at the seaward end is not in that room and has not been told and will say it herself if anybody asks her.**

And the port four days off will not carry it, and nobody in that port refused anything, and the reason it will not carry it is not a reason anybody could have argued with.

It is that the sentence says **nobody is paid**, and a weigh-house is a room where about nine hundred people in that port are paid twice in seven days, and a man standing in that room cannot say four clauses about a wagon out loud without making three of them a lie.

**The man from the port four days off said that himself and nobody asked him for it, and he said it standing in about four hundred yards of quay with the tide out, and the man of about forty-eight who keeps that weigh-house stood about nine feet off and agreed with him about nine seconds later and gave one reason, which was that about nine hundred people in that port know his name and about four of them know his face and neither of those is the same as knowing what he said.**

## Three

**And the man of about twenty-nine asked the man of about thirty-two with a limp whether he was needed, and he asked it in the open at about the seventh hour with about nine people standing about nine feet off, and nobody had discussed it and nobody had arranged it.**

The man of about thirty-two with a limp had come down that road four days behind him and had not said why and had not walked with him and had not asked him for anything, and about nine people on that made road had seen the two of them on four days of walking and about four of them had understood that they were not together and about four had not thought about it at all.

## Four

“**I am going to say a thing and it is not a no,**” he said, “**and you may have it.**”

“**There is one thing I can do on this coast that nobody else alive can do, and it is not on your sand, and it is four days up that made road and it is about a mark and eleven paces of stone and a figure of men on a sheet of paper with a fourth head on it that nobody has filled in.**”

“**If you want that carried, then I am needed, and if you do not want it carried then I am not, and I have not asked which and I am not going to. And I have been asked to hold a thing four times in about seven months and I said yes to one of them and it is not this one, and the reason is that a man who stands in the middle of three places is a man who can be sent for to all three, and this shore is not the middle of anywhere, it is the end of about nine hundred paces of hard sand.**”

**And that is a hundred and nine words in the last of three turns of one speech, and the whole of what he said was a hundred and ninety-one words in the three of them, and it is the fifth question anybody has put to him about a place in about seven months and the first of the five that has been answered with an offer instead of a refusal, and nobody on that sand improved on it and about four of the nine have said since then that an offer is the same as a no and about four have said since then that it is not.**

## Five

The cost of that Wednesday is a column and it is the man of about thirty-two with a limp's and it is the first one he has kept since a market morning on a made road between a town of about four thousand people and a port of about nine hundred, and he does not know what it is for.

**five loaves at a penny, a pound of cheese at four, four pounds of wood at three, two tallow candles at two, three quarts of small beer at two, a room at six, five nights' bed at four, a handcart for the four days down at four, a handcart for the four days back at four, a broom at five, a bucket of tar at eight, two sheets of paper at one, a quill and ink at three.** One, one, one, one, one, four, three, three, three, three, two, two, two, two, two, six, four, four, four, four, four, four, four, five, eight, one, one and three is eighty-three pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and eighty-three pence is a mark and thirty-five over**, and three rooms and two of them carrying four clauses and one of them not is not in it and is not a figure of men, and about nine hundred paces of hard sand is not in it and a foreshore is not a road.

**And a man of about thirty-two with a limp was asked at about the seventh hour on two miles and about nine hundred paces of hard sand whether he was needed and gave an answer that is not a yes and is not a no and offered to carry one sentence about a wall four days up a made road to anybody who wants it, and about nine people heard the whole of it and about four of them have said since then that the offer was the honest half and about four have said since then that the refusal was the useful half, and the man of about twenty-nine has both of them and has written down neither, and the three rooms carry four clauses between them and one of them carries nothing, and the water came in at about the ninth hour and went back at about the fourth hour of the morning.**
