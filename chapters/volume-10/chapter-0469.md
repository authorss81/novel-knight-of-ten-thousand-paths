# Chapter 0469

**The twenty-ninth day of Goatmonth, YR 318, a Wednesday and not a market day, and the fourth hour and the seventh hour and the eleventh hour, at the landward end of a foreshore two miles and about nine hundred paces long, in front of a weigh-house and a slipway and four hundred yards of quay in a port of about nine hundred people, where three people have come down the made road four days to find out what their own three rooms will carry, and a man of about thirty-two with a limp is standing at about the two hundredth pace with a stick he cannot lean on and did not come down that road with anybody.**

## One

**Three rooms, and they are three rooms and they are not a body and there is no chair in this either, and what came down the made road was four clauses about a wagon in a channel and not one word about anything else.**

The four clauses are the ones that nine people agreed on in three hours in a room over a weigh-house that is four days' walk from this quay and four of the nine have said since then that it is not an agreement and four have said since then that it is the whole of one.

Each of the three rooms has to say it out loud at its own end to anybody who asks. That is all it has to do and that is all any of the three was asked to do.

**The three of them who came down that made road walked it together at the start and not at the end. Two of them had come from the same end and had walked it together for nine years as a matter of course. The third had come from the other end and had walked four days of made road alone each way and had got used to being alone on a road two days before anybody thought to ask him whether he wanted company.**

## Two

The room at the landward end will carry it and has already said it out loud once, on the morning after the meeting, to a carter who had come off the sand at the wrong hour and did not want to be charged for the half of it.

**The room at the seaward end will carry it and will say it at the next ebb, and the woman of about fifty-one at the seaward end is not in that room and has not been told and will say it herself if anybody asks her.**

And the port four days off will not carry it, and nobody in that port refused anything, and the reason it will not carry it is not a reason anybody could have argued with.

It is that the sentence says **nobody is paid**, and a weigh-house is a room where about nine hundred people in that port are paid twice in seven days, and a man standing in that room cannot say four clauses about a wagon out loud without making three of them a lie.

**The man from the port four days off said that himself and nobody asked him for it, and he said it standing in four hundred yards of quay with the tide out, and the man of forty-eight who keeps that weigh-house stood nine feet off and agreed with him nine seconds later and gave one reason, which was that about nine hundred people in that port know his name and four of them know his face and neither of those is the same as knowing what he said.**

## Three

**And the man of about twenty-nine asked the man of about thirty-two with a limp whether he was needed, and he asked it in the open at about the seventh hour with nine people standing nine feet off, and nobody had discussed it and nobody had arranged it.**

The man of about thirty-two with a limp had come down that road four days behind him and had not said why and had not walked with him and had not asked him for anything, and nine people on that made road had seen the two of them on four days of walking and four of them had understood that they were not together and four had not thought about it at all.

**He had said his name once, on the first day, to a carter who had asked it, and the carter had not asked it a second time, and eight days on a foreshore is long enough for a man to find out that nobody has any use for his name. He was called Aren, and the man of about twenty-nine had used it twice, and nine people standing nine feet off had not heard either time.**

He had a stick in his right hand that he was not leaning on. He had been holding it the way a man holds a thing he has decided not to put down, and getting up off the shingle where he had been sitting took him four seconds longer than a man of thirty-two with both legs would have taken, and he did it without the stick and without hurry, and nine people standing nine feet off saw all of it and none of them said anything, because on a foreshore you do not say anything about how a man gets up off the shingle.

**His left palm was warm. He put it against his coat for a moment and then took it out again and let it hang, and it had been warm since the fourth hour and it had been warm on the four days of the road as well, and he had told nobody that, and there was nobody on that sand he could have told.**

## Four

“**I am going to say a thing and it is not a no,**” Aren said, “**and you may have it.**”

**He said it in a coast accent, and he did not know he had said it that way, and the man of about twenty-nine heard it and did not notice, and a woman of twenty-nine in a room over a weigh-house two hundred miles off has not noticed yet.**

“**There is one thing I can do on this coast that nobody else alive can do, and it is not on your sand, and it is four days up that made road and it is about a mark and eleven paces of stone and a figure of men on a sheet of paper with a fourth head on it that nobody has filled in.**”

“**If you want that carried, then I am needed, and if you do not want it carried then I am not, and I have not asked which and I am not going to. And I have been asked to hold a thing four times in seven months and I said yes to one of them and it is not this one, and the reason is that a man who stands in the middle of three places is a man who can be sent for to all three, and this shore is not the middle of anywhere, it is the end of about nine hundred paces of hard sand.**”

**And that is a hundred and eight words in the last of three turns of one speech, and the whole of what he said was a hundred and ninety words in the three of them, and it is the fifth question anybody has put to him about a place in seven months and the first of the five that has been answered with an offer instead of a refusal, and nobody on that sand improved on it and four of the nine have said since then that an offer is the same as a no and four have said since then that it is not.**

## Five

The cost of that Wednesday is a column and it is the man of about thirty-two with a limp's, and he wrote it standing up, and it is the first one he has kept since a market morning on a made road between a town of four thousand people and a port of about nine hundred, and he does not know what it is for.

**five loaves at a penny, a pound of cheese at four, four pounds of wood at three, two tallow candles at two, three quarts of small beer at two, a room at six, five nights' bed at four, a handcart for the four days down at four, a handcart for the four days back at four, a broom at five, a bucket of tar at eight, two sheets of paper at one, a quill and ink at three.** One, one, one, one, one, four, three, three, three, three, two, two, two, two, two, six, four, four, four, four, four, four, four, five, eight, one, one and three is eighty-three pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and eighty-three pence is a mark and thirty-five over**, and three rooms and two of them carrying four clauses and one of them not is not in it and is not a figure of men, and about nine hundred paces of hard sand is not in it and a foreshore is not a road.

**And a man of about thirty-two with a limp was asked at about the seventh hour on two miles and about nine hundred paces of hard sand whether he was needed and gave an answer that is not a yes and is not a no and offered to carry one sentence about a wall four days up a made road to anybody who wants it, and nine people heard the whole of it and four of them have said since then that the offer was the honest half and four have said since then that the refusal was the useful half, and the man of about twenty-nine has both of them and has written down neither, and the three rooms carry four clauses between them and one of them carries nothing, and the water came in at about the ninth hour and went back at about the fourth hour of the morning, and Aren went back up the made road behind him, four days ahead of nobody, with his name said twice in eight days and no use for it.**
