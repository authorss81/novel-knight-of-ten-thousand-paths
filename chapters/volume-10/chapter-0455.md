# Chapter 0455

**The twelfth day of Frostmonth, YR 318, a Wednesday and not a market day, and the second hour before light and the seventh hour and the eleventh hour, in a long low building with a single door and a stone floor and about four feet of headroom under the beams, behind a weigh-house at the landward end of a foreshore, where the road had been under about nine hundred feet of water since the second hour of the afternoon and would not be out again until the eighth hour of the morning, and there are nine people in that building and none of them works there, and a man of thirty-two with a limp is in it.**

## One

**The building is a salt-store and it is the one place on that foreshore where a door can be stood in twice a day and still be shut at both ends of the water, and a woman of about thirty-four put a bench against the inside of it on the morning of the third day and moved it back on the morning of the twelfth and it is the same bench and it is still there.**

Nobody in that building works there, and the nine of them were in it on the third day of the month and everything below the next ten lines happened on that morning, and what is printed here is the twelfth. It is shut from the sixth hour to the eleventh in a good half of the year and from the fourth hour to the ninth in the rest of it, and the people standing in it are the people of four houses and of a yard, and nine of them came because a woman of about thirty-four told them at the seaward end at low water on the third day and told them again on the fourth and did not tell anybody on the fifth.

A man of about twenty-nine came with them and was the only one of the nine who had not been on that foreshore before that morning, and he is not one of the people of the four houses and nobody asked him to come and he came.

**What the nine of them were told, in a building with a stone floor and about four feet of headroom, is a sentence, and the sentence is that there is a man at the landward end who has been on this foreshore once and who wants to stand in a place he has not stood in, and that the asking is the asking and not the reason, and that anybody who wants to hear the reason can hear the reason and about four of the nine did.**

## Two

A woman of about thirty-four with a barrow said the reason, and it is the whole of the case for letting him in, and it took her about four minutes and she did not raise her voice at any point in it.

“**He is not from here and he is not one of the four, and that is the only reason I am letting him, because the four would have made a mess of this in about nine days and you know the four and so do I, and the four are good at what they are for and the four would have had a vote about him in a week. I am letting a man in who is nobody, because a man who is nobody cannot be asked whether he thinks he ought to be let in, and that is the only argument I have got and it is not a good one and I am giving it in a shed in front of nine people.**”

Then she asked him three questions and he answered all three the same way, which was that he would do whatever the nine of them told him to do about the four houses and the seven, and that he would not be one of the four and would not be one of the seven either, and that if anybody on that side of the door wanted him to stop after nine days then he would stop after nine days.

“**Shut it behind you,**” she said.

**And that is four words and they are about the door and not about him, and she said yes before she said them, and about four of the nine people in that building did not agree and said so out loud and were not overvoted because there was nothing to overvote, because nobody was keeping a count.**

## Three

**The room on the other side of the door is a courtyard with a wall about nine feet high and a well in it and about forty people in it, and there is no Wednesday on that side of the door and has not been for about nine hundred years, and about nine of the forty looked at him at once, and the woman of about thirty-four on that side of the door was not the same woman and had never met the one on this side of the door and had been given a description of her by a man who had been across that sand twice and could not tell her much.**

He was nine days on that side.

**What nine days does is not dramatic and is not a gift and is not a thing anybody can report. It is this: at the end of nine days there is a thing he does with his hands that he did not do before, and a way of standing on sand that he did not have, and a handful of words in a language that is not his and is spoken by about four hundred people. He learned to read a tide off a post the way the woman on this side of the door reads one, and he is not going to be able to stop doing it, and nobody on that side of the door thinks it is strange and one of them said it was a good thing to learn in nine days and he is going to carry that sentence for the rest of his life and it is the only one of the nine days he would keep if somebody let him keep one.**

The people on that side did not reset and he was not asked to help and he did about nine days of ordinary work with about nine of them, which was carrying shell and mending a wall of stone about nine feet high, and it is a different wall from the one of stone on the foreshore and there is no connection between the two and nobody has looked for one, and he was paid in food and not in coin and he did not ask in coin.

## Four

**And on the twelfth day, at about the eleventh hour, a man of thirty-two with a limp came back through a door in a salt-store at the landward end of a foreshore, and there were nine people in that building and one of them had moved a bench back about two hours before and none of them had moved it after.**

The woman of about thirty-four on this side of the door looked at him and did not ask him one question about where he had been.

“**You are standing like our own,**” she said.

**And that is six words, and she said them in the accent of that side of the door, and he heard them in the accent of this side of the door, and both of those were true at once and about six people in that building noticed one of the two.**

He looked at where his feet were. Both of them were on a stone floor and about a foot apart and both of them were pointing at the door.

“**No,**” he said.

She did not argue with it and she looked at her own feet for a moment and then she said, “**They are the same as yours,**” and that was the end of the whole of it, and about six of the nine in that building noticed and about four of the six had noticed something else, which was the accent, and one of those four was the man of about twenty-nine, who had been on the foreshore eleven days and had not heard him speak before the ninth day of a month and had heard him speak on about thirty occasions since.

**Nobody said a word about the accent. That is the second cost, and it is a thing and not a figure, and it is not going to be.**

## Five

The cost of those nine days is a column and it is his and it is the first one he has kept since a room over a weigh-house with a door on a lane, and it is in his own hand and there is no second hand in it and there is no notebook open anywhere in it.

**a loaf at one penny, a second loaf at one, a pound of cheese at four, a second pound of cheese at four, a pound of wood at three, a second pound of wood at three, a tallow candle at two, a second tallow candle at two, a quart of small beer at two, a bushel of oats at four, a broom at five, a room at six, a second room at four.** One, one, four, four, three, three, two, two, two, four, five, six and four is forty-one pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and forty-one pence is under one by seven**, and nine days of somebody else's food is not in it and was carried at somebody else's cost and there is no figure in this kingdom on which a man is entered as having eaten nine days of another shore's bread.

**And a man of thirty-two with a limp came back through a door in a salt-store at about the eleventh hour on the twelfth of a month and stood with his feet pointing at the door, and nine people were in that building and one of them had moved a bench back about two hours before, and about four of the nine heard an accent that had not been in that yard nine days before and none of them said a word about it, and about four hundred people on a coast about four hundred miles off speak it every day and have never once been asked whether they would like to stop, and a man of about twenty-nine has not written any of it down and has not been asked to.**
