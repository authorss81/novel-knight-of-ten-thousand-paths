# Chapter 0497

**The twenty-second day of Rainmonth, YR 319, a Saturday and a market day, and the second hour and the fifth hour and the ninth hour, on two miles and about nine hundred paces of hard sand and broken shell where a river runs into the sea, from the landward end to the seaward end and about two hundred paces short of the seaward end, and there is no month in anybody's mouth on that shore and no day of the week, and a market runs on two days in seven in a town of about two thousand people four days up a made road and this is one of them, and about nine hundred people are on a sand that is there about four hours out of twelve, and somebody has been asked for a thing and has said no.**

## One

**The man of about thirty-four from the port four days off came down the made road on the second ebb after the no and came down it with an empty barrow, and he did not come to the salt-store and he did not come to the landward end, and about nine people on that sand saw him go past both of them and about four of the nine thought he had come to do something.**

What he came for was at the seaward end, and it took him about two hours up a sand that is there about four hours out of twelve, and he was on the flat in front of a driven post five inches square before the fourth hour.

The woman of about fifty-one was not at the post. She was about nine hundred paces down the sand at the end of a barrow-load of driftwood she had gone for, and she came back at about the fifth hour and found a man of about thirty-four standing at her post with a sheet of paper in his hand and nothing written on it, which is what he does at a wall as well.

He asked her for a thing. The thing is the driftwood.

**And she said no, and the asking and the telling of it are the whole of what happened at the seaward end of two miles and about nine hundred paces of hard sand on the twenty-second day of a month carried at thirty in this year, and about four people were within nine feet of both of them and about nine hundred were on that sand and neither of those figures has anything to do with the other one.**

“**I will take it down,**” the man of about thirty-four from the port four days off said. He had not said who he was and she did not ask.

“**No,**” the woman of about fifty-one said.

He wrote the two letters on the sheet, which was a thing he had never done in six times on that sand, and turned the sheet over and did not write anything on the other side, and stood there.

“**Say why,**” he said, and it was the first time anybody had said that to her in about twenty-two years, and he had said it because a man of about thirty-two with a limp had said nine sentences in a salt-store three days before and four of the nine people in that room had said afterwards that a man who says why has been asked something nobody asked him.

“**I am not going to say why,**” the woman of about fifty-one said.

“**Then I have to write *no* on a sheet and carry it up a made road, and there is nobody above me who will accept that from a woman of fifty-one who reads a tide off driftwood, and I have to write *no* or I have to come back with a different question.**”

“**You have to come back with a different question,**” the woman of about fifty-one said, “**and I will tell you now what the different question is, because I have decided to and I have not decided to for twenty-two years. The different question is not about the driftwood.**”

“**Then what is the different question?**”

“**I do not know,**” the woman of about fifty-one said, “**and I have never once known what the right question is and I have stopped looking for it, and there is a piece of wood in a post with twenty-two notches cut in the edge of it and the twenty-second is not cut, and it is mine, and the water decides when it goes in and when it comes out, and there is no question about it that is worth a man of thirty-four's morning.**”

**And that is a hundred and ninety-five words in eight turns of one exchange, of which the first is five, the second is one, the third is two, the fourth is seven, the fifth is fifty-three, the sixth is forty-two printed in two spans of nine and thirty-three, the seventh is six, and the eighth is printed in two spans of four and seventy-five, and the man of about thirty-four from the port four days off wrote two letters on a sheet with no head on it and carried them up a made road and did not come to the salt-store and did not tell anybody at the landward end what he had been told, and the woman of about fifty-one said no in one word and has not said no to anybody since and has not been asked anything by anybody since.**

## Two

**And what the driftwood was for is a thing she has never said out loud and did not say on that tide, and about four people have since said that they know what it was for, and all four of those four have given a different answer, and none of the four has ever been within nine feet of her at the post when the water was off its mark.**

The driftwood is about the length of a forearm and two inches through, wedged in a driven post five inches square, and there are twenty-two notches cut in the edge of it, one for each year, and the twenty-second is not cut, and it goes in when the water goes off a mark on that post that has not been off in twenty-two years.

A man of about thirty-eight with a crowbar has said it is a tide mark and it is a tide mark and it has never once been the other thing.

A man of about fifty-three with a wagon has said it is a tally of his own and it is not and he knows it is not and he has said it four times.

A man of about forty-one who stands in the doorway of the seventh of the eleven houses said nothing and was not asked and was four hundred yards away.

A woman of about thirty-four who carried a sheet down that sand said it is the name of a road, and she said that on a tide before any of this and not on this one, and the man of about thirty-four from the port four days off was there when she said it and heard it and wrote nothing down.

**And the four of them are not added to anything and none of them is a keeper and none of them is a person the driftwood answers to, and the twenty-second notch is not cut, and the water did not go off that mark on the twenty-second day of a month carried at thirty in this year and did not go off it on the seventeenth and has not gone off it in twenty-two years.**

## Three

**And the cost of the no is not the driftwood and it is not the salt-store and it is not the man of about thirty-four's morning, and it was spoken out loud at about the ninth hour of that tide by a person who is not the man of about thirty-two with a limp and is not the man of about thirty-four either, and about four hundred people were on that sand and about nine of them were within nine feet of it.**

A man of about fifty-three with a wagon had come down on the ebb and had stood at the landward end and had watched the man of about thirty-four go up the sand with a sheet and had watched him come back down it four hours later with the same sheet, and at about the ninth hour he said it out loud on the flat where about four hundred people could hear it.

“**He went up there for the wood,**” the man of about fifty-three with a wagon said, “**and he came back without it, and he did not tell the man of thirty-two, and the man of thirty-two is four hundred miles off by now or a day off that, and there is a woman at that post who has not been asked for anything in twenty-two years and who has been asked for something this morning and said no, and that is the price of the no, and I am the one saying it and I have been on this sand four times a week for nineteen years.**”

A woman of about thirty-four with a barrow was nine feet off him and she said one word.

“**Mine,**” she said.

“**It is not yours,**” the man of about fifty-three with a wagon said, “**and it is not mine either, and I have just said the price of a thing that belongs to nobody out loud on a sand where about four hundred people heard it, and I would take it back if I could and I cannot.**”

“**You could have said it four hours ago,**” the woman of about thirty-four with a barrow said, “**when he went past the landward end going up, and you did not, and I did not either, and there were about nine of us standing at the landward end and about four of the nine noticed which way he went.**”

“**Then I have been a coward for about four hours and I have been a coward about nine weeks before that,**” the man of about fifty-three with a wagon said, “**and both of those are the same coward and I am not going to be told which of them is worse by a woman with a barrow on a sand that is under water in about eight hours.**”

**And that is two hundred and fifty-five words in five turns of one exchange, of which the first is ninety-eight printed in two spans of seven and ninety-one, the second is one, the third is forty-eight printed in two spans of four and forty-four, the fourth is forty-nine printed in two spans of eight and forty-one, and the fifth is fifty-nine printed in two spans of twenty-one and thirty-eight, and the man of about fifty-three with a wagon is fifty-three and has driven a salt wagon down that sand four times a week for nineteen years and has never been thanked and did not want to be, and he is not the man of about thirty-two with a limp and he is not the man of about thirty-four and neither of those two men was on that sand and neither of them was within nine hundred yards of it, and about four hundred people heard the price of a no spoken by a third person and about nine hundred were on the sand and those are two figures and they are not added to anything.**

## Four

**And the woman of about fifty-one was at her post at about the ninth hour of that same tide and the water was going off it, and she looked at the mark and went away, and the twenty-second notch is not cut, and nobody on that foreshore has said a day of the week in about nine years and nobody has said a month, and about nine hundred people went home along about nine hundred paces of hard sand to eleven houses, four of which have somebody's work happening in them and seven of which do not.**

**Aren Kest was four days up a made road and a day from a port of about nine hundred people on the twenty-second day of a month carried at thirty in this year and has not been told any of this and is not a party to any of it and did not cause any of it, and the offer of one sentence concerning a wall that he made on a sand in a hundred and ninety words in three turns in a year before this one has not been taken up by anybody in about five months, and he has not offered it twice.**

## Five
