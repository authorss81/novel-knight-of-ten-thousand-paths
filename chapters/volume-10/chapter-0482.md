# Chapter 0482

**The twenty-sixth day of Mudmonth, YR 319, a Thursday and a market day, and the fourth hour and the seventh hour and the eleventh hour, four days off a coast where a river runs into the sea, in a market town of about two thousand people where a market runs on two days in seven and this is one of them, at the back of a street four hundred yards long, on the first floor over a chandler's shop, in a room with one window and a table and six chairs and no fire lit, where a piece of paper with three heads filled in and a fourth head empty is lying on the table with a seal beside it, and a man of about thirty-four with a sheet has come four days up a made road to look at it and has not touched it.**

## One

**A deed that wants a signature and a form that wants a name are two different pieces of paper and they have been on the same table in nine counties for four months and nobody has confused them out loud yet, and the reason nobody has is that they are not the same shape.**

The deed is a foot and a half long and it is folded in three and it has a place for one signature at the bottom and the place for the signature is ruled and it says nothing else. The form is the same size and it has four heads cut into it, and three of them are filled in, and the fourth is empty, and the fourth is the one that has been empty since the middle of a month in Wolfmonth.

**A place, filled in. A figure of men, filled in. A trade, filled in. And at the foot of it, in a second and later hand, a line for the name of whoever is going to answer for the road between the two ends, and that line is the one that has been empty longest.**

Nobody has put a name in the fourth head, and a clerk or a copyist in a room like this one might say out loud that the fourth head ought not to exist and be right about it and not be agreed with, and nobody has said it in this room, and nobody has said the opposite either, and both of those silences have been there since the middle of a month in Wolfmonth.

## Two

A man of about forty-seven is the clerk of the works and he has the deed, and a woman of about thirty-one is the copyist and she has the form, and neither of them has ever held both at the same time because a man of about thirty-four with a sheet came in at the fourth hour and put his own paper on the table between them and would not let either of them pick up the other's.

“**That is mine and I will not have it read,**” he said, and the clerk of the works said that nothing on the table needed to be read, and the man of about thirty-four said that was as well.

**And then he said the thing that nine people in nine counties have said about that form and never once to each other, which is that a form with four heads on it and a deed with one ruled signature at the foot of it are the same piece of work with the faces turned, and that one office has been holding two of them for four months without noticing.**

“**Your deed wants a signature and it will get one inside nine days, because a signature is a thing an officer can give and an officer can be asked for it twice. Your form wants a name and it will not get one in nine days or nine years, and the reason is not that nobody wants to sign it. The reason is that the fourth head wants a person from two miles of sand and there is nobody on that sand who answers for a road.**”

**And that is ninety-seven words in two turns of one speech, of which the first is ten and the second is eighty-seven, and the clerk of the works did not agree and did not disagree and the copyist wrote down the opening ten words and then stopped, which is a thing she has done before.**

## Three

**And what the man of about thirty-four with a sheet does not do, in a market town of about two thousand people on a market morning, is the whole of what that room is about, and it is that he does not fill it in, and he does not say that anybody else has, and he does not offer.**

He had offered something on the twenty-ninth of Goatmonth on a sand, at about the seventh hour, in about a hundred and ninety words in three turns, and the offer was that if anybody wanted one sentence carried about the wall then he was needed and if nobody did then he was not. Nobody took it up. **Nobody has taken it up in the eighty-six days since, and eighty-six days is two months carried at thirty and twenty-six days over, and there is no third asking of anybody and no list and no fourth head with anything in it.**

“**I am not asking for it,**” he said. “**I want that said in this room while the two of you are looking at me, and it will not be said again in this room, and if I had asked for it you would have had to answer it and neither of you could.**”

**And that is fifty-one words in one turn of one speech printed in two spans of six and forty-five, and he said it standing, and the copyist's hand stopped for about two seconds and then went on writing, and after the two seconds she said the thing she had been not saying since the middle of a month in Wolfmonth, and she said it to the window and not to him, and it was the only sentence anybody in that room said that the man of about thirty-four did not answer.**

“**The fourth head ought not to be on it,**” she said. “**It is the only one of the four that asks for a person and not for a thing that can be counted, and a form that asks for a person is a form that is going to sit still.**”

“**It ought not to be on it,**” the clerk of the works said, “**and it is on it, and it has been on it since the middle of a month in Wolfmonth, and I have cut three of the four heads on about nine hundred forms in twenty-two years and I have never cut that one, and I am not going to start now.**”

“**That is you agreeing with me.**”

“**That is me telling you what I am not going to do,**” he said, “**and it is not the same, and I have not got the morning to explain the difference to a man who has come four days for it.**”

**And that is a hundred and twenty-five words in four turns of one exchange, of which the first is nine, the second is forty-six printed in two spans of seven and thirty-nine, the third is six, and the fourth is sixty-four printed in two spans of twelve and the rest of the sixty-four, the last of those two spans being a figure that is not set down**, and the copyist was right about it and nobody agreed with her, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet did not take either side and wrote nothing down and has not said since which of the two he thought was right,** and the clerk of the works looked at the fourth head for nine seconds and then looked at the window, and nine people on a market morning in that town saw a man put four folded sheets on a table and not open them.**

## Four

And then a woman of about twenty-six with a basket came in for candles and the clerk of the works said that candles were downstairs and she went down, and in the four minutes she was gone the man of about thirty-four with a sheet said the last thing he said in that room, and the room had two other men in it and both of them wrote it down and one of them has it still.

“**There is a wall of eleven paces at a hundred paces from the landward end and it is not a boundary and nobody on that sand has ever called it one, and there is a woman there who has been asked what she wants once in twenty-two years and answered in a hundred and three words, and there is a man there of sixty-three who has not been asked one thing in forty-one years, and neither of them is on this paper and neither of them is going to be, and the fourth head is going to stay empty because the fourth head wants one of them and neither of them has been asked and neither of them is going to ask.**”

**And that is a hundred and twenty-two words in one turn of one speech and it is the longest speech a man of about thirty-four has made in a room since the twenty-ninth of a month in the year before this one, and the clerk of the works wrote all of it down, and the other of the two men wrote down the words *fourth head* and *stays empty* and nothing else, and the copyist did not write it down at all and has said that she was not asked to.**

**And nobody in that room said the fourth head ought not to exist, and nobody said anybody had filled it in somewhere else, and nobody thanked the woman of about thirty-four with a barrow on that sand who asked whether the fourth head was still empty twelve days ago, and the press in a room over a weigh-house two hundred miles off stands open with no date anybody will give and nobody shuts it.**

## Five

The cost of that Thursday is a column and it is the man of about thirty-four's, and it is the fourth one he has kept and he has not written a total against any of the four and he does not know what any of them is for.

**a quill and ink at three, a sheet of paper at one, a second sheet of paper at one, a third sheet of paper at one, a fourth sheet of paper at one, a fifth sheet of paper at one, a pound of candles at four, a pound of wood at three, a second pound of wood at three, a broom at five, a bucket of tar at eight, a room at six, a bed at four, a second night's bed at four, a third night's bed at four, a pound of rope at six, a pound of nails at three, a pair of gloves at two, a second pair of gloves at two, a waxed cord at four, an inkwell at three, a handcart for the day at four, a loaf at one, a second loaf at one, a pound of cheese at four, a bushel of oats at four, a brush at two, a knife at two, a basket at five, a second brush at two, a tallow candle at two, a second room with a fire in it at eight, a second broom at five, a second pound of candles at four, a second pound of cheese at four and a third loaf at one.** Three, one, one, one, one, one, four, three, three, five, eight, six, four, four, four, six, three, two, two, four, three, four, one, one, four, four, two, two, five, two, two, eight, five, four, four and one is a hundred and eighteen pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and eighteen pence is two marks and twenty-two over**, and a deed that wants a signature and a form that wants a name are not in it and are two pieces of paper, and a fourth head is not in it and is empty, and eleven paces of stone and about nine hundred people on two miles and about nine hundred paces of hard sand are not in it, and about nine hundred people paid twice in seven days in a weigh-house room in that town four days off are not in it and are not the same people and are not added to the other nine hundred.

**And a form with three heads filled in and a fourth head empty lay on a table on the first floor over a chandler's shop for about seven hours on a market morning in a town of about two thousand people and a man of about thirty-four with a sheet did not fill it in and did not offer, and a clerk of the works wrote down a hundred and twenty-two words that were said in that room, and a copyist wrote down ten of them, and neither of them was asked to be a keeper of anything, and nobody in nine counties has put a name in the fourth head, and the water went off about four hours out of twelve on two miles and about nine hundred paces of hard sand that morning as it goes off about four hours out of twelve twice a day, and the fourth head is empty, and it is going to be empty on the last day of this month and on the first of the next one.**
