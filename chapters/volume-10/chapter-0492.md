# Chapter 0492

**The fifteenth day of Longlight, YR 319, a Thursday and a market day, and the second hour and the sixth hour and the tenth hour, in one low room behind a weigh-house at the landward end of a sand of two miles and about nine hundred paces, in a port of about nine hundred people, in a building whose beams give a man four feet of headroom and whose floor is stone and whose door is the only one, where there is no month in anybody's mouth and no day of the week, and three rooms are in that one building for the second time in a hundred and fifty-one days, and a man of about thirty-two with a limp is in it and is not the reason it is happening.**

## One

**The salt-store is shut from the sixth hour to the eleventh in a good half of the year and from the fourth hour to the ninth in the rest of it, and the three of them came on the tide before the water and went on the flood after it, and about four hundred yards of quay and a slipway were between that building and the water and nobody used the quay.**

Nobody arranged it. Nine people had heard about it four days ago and four of them had told one other each, and that is the whole of how three rooms came to be in one building, and there is no list in nine counties that says who told whom.

A man of about thirty-four from the port four days off was already inside when the door opened and had been inside for about a quarter of an hour. A man of about sixty with a slipway at the landward end came in second. A woman of about thirty-four with a barrow came in third and shut the door behind her, which she has not done on any tide since the middle of a month in Wolfmonth, and did not say why.

**And the three of them had been in one building once before, on the fourteenth of a month in Hearthmonth, a hundred and fifty-one days back, in a room over a weigh-house four days up a made road, for three hours, and they had agreed on four clauses about a wagon in a channel and on nothing else at all.**

“**This is the second time,**” the man of about sixty said, to nobody, and put his hand flat on the stone floor as if the floor were the thing that needed convincing.

“**It is,**” the man of about thirty-four from the port four days off said.

“**It is a hundred and fifty-one days.**”

“**It is a hundred and fifty-one days,**” the man of about thirty-four from the port four days off said, “**and before that it was forty-one years and a barn nine miles off, and before that nobody living, and if you want a round figure for it then a century is a round figure and a century is not a fact.**”

“**Whose century?**” the woman of about thirty-four with a barrow said.

“**My father's,**” the man of about sixty said, “**and he was wrong about his own age once, so I would not build a room on it.**”

**And that is eighty-four words in six turns of one exchange, of which the first is five, the second is two, the third is seven, the fourth is forty-eight printed in two spans of seven and forty-one, the fifth is two, and the sixth is twenty printed in two spans of two and eighteen, and the man of about sixty with a slipway at the landward end put his hand on the floor to make a point about a floor and the point was about a date, and the man of about thirty-four from the port four days off said the same date twice and the woman of about thirty-four with a barrow shut the door behind her and has not said why and is not going to.**

## Two

**And a man of about thirty-two with a limp came through that door at about the sixth hour with a stick he is not leaning on, and there were three people in that building and none of them had come for him, and he was not asked to sit down and he sat down on a bundle of rope because it was the only thing in the room that was not stone.**

He had been on that foreshore eleven days in the last month. He was the reason for none of it. Three people had arranged to be in a building on a tide and a man who was not one of the three came through the door of it, and about four of the nine hundred people in that port would have said afterwards that he was the reason, and all three of the people in the room knew he was not, and none of them said so out loud, because saying so would have been a fourth thing.

“**You are four days up,**” the man of about sixty said, after a while.

“**I am four days up,**” Aren Kest said.

“**And you did not come down for this.**”

“**I did not come down for this. I came down for a tide and I am on that tide because I am four days up and I have no business here.**”

“**That is not a reason,**” the man of about thirty-four from the port four days off said.

“**No,**” Aren Kest said. “**It is a thing I did not do, which is a different kind of thing from a reason, and I have noticed that about myself about nine times this year and I have not got used to it.**”

**And that is ninety-three words in seven turns of one exchange, of which the first is five, the second is five, the third is eight, the fourth is thirty-one, the fifth is five, the sixth is one, and the seventh is thirty-eight, and the man of about thirty-four from the port four days off said the sixth turn in one word and did not apologise for it, and Aren Kest is not the reason the three rooms are in that building and said so in the plainest words available to him and was not thanked for saying it because saying it was not a service.**

## Three

**And what the three of them had come about was the four clauses, and the four clauses had carried twice in a hundred and fifty-one days and one of the three rooms could not carry them at all, and the woman of about thirty-four with a barrow said the whole of that in thirty-one words and the other two did not improve on it.**

“**The landward room says all four,**” she said, “**and the seaward room says all four, and the port four days off says three and says why it cannot say the fourth, and there is no second meeting and there is no day, and there has not been a day since the middle of a month in the year before this one and nobody has asked for one.**”

“**Then it is working,**” the man of about sixty said.

“**It is working the way a pan works,**” the woman of about thirty-four with a barrow said. “**A pan works because there is a fire behind it. Nobody asked the fire to be there and the fire is not paid and the fire does not attend anything, and if the fire goes out the pan stops and nothing has been decided about it.**”

“**That is a very good sentence,**” the man of about thirty-four from the port four days off said, “**and it is a sentence about a pan, and I want to know what it is a sentence about before I carry it up a made road.**”

“**It is a sentence about four clauses,**” she said, “**and the four clauses are about a wagon in a channel, and the pan is a sentence about a different thing, and I have put the two next to each other because they were in the same room and I have about four minutes.**”

**And that is two hundred and seven words in six turns of one exchange, of which the first is printed in two spans of six and fifty-nine, the second is four, the third is eight, the fourth is forty-six, the fifth is thirty-three printed in two spans of six and twenty-seven, and the sixth is fifty-one printed in two spans of seven and forty-four, and the man of about thirty-four from the port four days off has carried four clauses up and down a made road four times and has never once carried the pan sentence and has not been asked to and did not offer, and nobody in that building thanked the woman of about thirty-four with a barrow and she did not want to be thanked and has said so twice in about four months.**

## Four

**And a man of about thirty-two with a limp sat on a bundle of rope in a building with about four feet of headroom and listened to three people say a thing that was not his, and he said four words in the whole of the hour and the fourth of them was a number, and the number was the age of a building.**

“**How old is it?**” he said, at about the tenth hour.

“**The floor is older than the door,**” the man of about sixty said, “**and the door is older than me, and the beams are older than the floor, and I have never once found out a figure for any of it and I have been in and out of this building for forty-one years.**”

“**Then a century is a round figure,**” Aren Kest said, “**and it is not wrong, and it is not a fact, and the two of those are not the same and nobody in this room is going to be able to tell you which of the two you have got.**”

**And that is ninety-nine words in three turns of one exchange, of which the first is four, the second is forty-eight printed in two spans of seven and forty-one, and the third is forty-seven printed in two spans of seven and forty, and the man of about sixty with a slipway at the landward end has carried the number about a century in his mouth since before the man of about thirty-two with a limp was born and has never had anybody take it off him until a man he had met twice said it could be either, and he did not thank him and did not tell him he was right, and has told two other people since that a man four days up said a thing about a century, and neither of the two asked what.**

## Five

The cost of that Thursday is a column and it is the man of about thirty-four's from the port four days off, and it is the fifth one he has carried up and down a made road and he has never joined any two of them together, and four clauses are not in it and are not a rate.

**a room by the day at six, a second room by the day at six, a bed at four, a second bed at four, a load of wood at three, a second load of wood at three, a tallow candle at two, a second tallow candle at two, a quart of small beer at two, a pound of candles at four, a coil of rope at six, a sheet of paper at one, a second sheet of paper at one, a third sheet of paper at one, a pen and ink at three, a besom at eight, a bucket of nails at three, a barrel of pitch at eight, a barrow for the day at four, a loaf at one, a quarter of cheese at four, a pear at one, a second pear at one, a knife at two, a scraper at two, a bushel of oats at four, a second bushel of oats at four, a mallet at three, a second mallet at three, and a stop for a boot at three.** Six, six, four, four, three, three, two, two, two, four, six, one, one, one, three, eight, three, eight, four, one, four, one, one, two, two, four, four, three, three and three is ninety-nine pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and ninety-nine pence is two marks and three over**, and three rooms in one building is not in it, and a hundred and fifty-one days is not in it, and a century that is a round figure and not a fact is not in it, and eleven houses about four hundred yards up a sand that is there about four hours out of twelve are not in it and are not a figure of men, and a mark is forty-eight pence and a road that is not a toll road has no rate printed on it anywhere in nine counties and none was printed here.

**And three rooms were in one building at the landward end of two miles and about nine hundred paces of hard sand for about three hours on the fifteenth day of a month carried at thirty in this year, and they said four clauses about a wagon and they said a sentence about a pan, and neither of those is a keeper, and a man of about thirty-two with a limp sat on a bundle of rope through all of it and said four words and three of them were a date and the fourth was a question about the age of a floor, and the door was shut at the ninth hour and the water was over about two hundred paces of the seaward half from the sixth hour and it was off the sand again before four in the morning.**
