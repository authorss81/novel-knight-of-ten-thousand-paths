# Chapter 0471

**The second day of Thawmonth, YR 319, a Saturday and a market morning, and the fourth hour and the seventh hour and the eleventh hour, in a market town of about two thousand people four days off a coast where a river runs into the sea, where a market runs on two days in seven and this is one of them, at the back of a street four hundred yards long in a counting-house with a counter and a bench and a fire that is out in summer, and a man of about forty-four is behind that counter with a book he has kept in one hand for twenty-two years and a boy of about sixteen is taking down what he is told, and a man of about twenty-nine is four days up a made road with eight folded sheets in a coat and has not come in yet.**

## One

**The trade in that room in the fourth hour of that market morning is a cart-load of shell, and the only price in the whole of it that is written down anywhere in nine counties is six pence a cart-load, and it has been six pence since before the boy at the counter was born.**

The shell comes off two miles and about nine hundred paces of hard sand and broken shell where a river runs into the sea, nine cart-loads a day off four boats into four pans, and it goes to a lime kiln four days off that coast, and the kiln pays six pence a cart-load out of a book and has never once paid a different one.

The man of about forty-four said the six pence without being asked and said it the way a man says the price of bread.

“**It is not that I set it,**” he said. “**I have never set it. It was six pence in my father's time and it was six pence when I came in and there is a kiln four days off with a book and the book is the price, and I am the man who pays it and I have been arguing with a book for twenty-two years.**”

## Two

**A cart-load of shell is worth nine pence to anybody who will burn it himself, and he knows that the way he knows the price of everything, which is that he has offered it nine times and been beaten nine times, and he has never had it in writing and never will.**

And then the boy of about sixteen, who had been taking down the six pence and the cart-loads and the days, put two figures on one line of a sheet with a rule across it, which he was not supposed to do.

He had got them off two different sheets and he had not read the headings, and one of them was a figure of people on a coast and the other was a figure of people in a port four days up a made road, and both of them said nine hundred.

“**They are the same number?**” the boy said.

The man of about forty-four took the sheet and looked at it for a long time, and the sheet was wrong and he knew it was wrong before he had finished looking, and he left it on the counter where it was.

“**They are the same number and not one person is in both of them,**” he said. “**About nine hundred people on that sand and about nine hundred people in that port, and I have been buying from one and selling to the other for twenty-two years and I have never once had them on a line together, and you have just put two numbers on one line where there is nothing on the line to compare, and a clerk in my house in nine counties is going to take a sheet like that and add the two and be wrong in a way that will outlast both of us.**”

**And that is a hundred and seven words in one turn of one speech printed in two spans of fourteen and ninety-three, and both are counted, and the boy of about sixteen heard both and is the only person in that room who will still have them in a month.**

## Three

And the man of about twenty-nine came in at the eleventh hour, four days off a made road and wet through at the knees, with eight folded sheets in the inside of a coat that he has not joined up and has never written a total against.

He did not sit down and he put the sheets on the counter and did not open them, and four people in that street saw a stranger put eight folded sheets on a factor's counter and not open them.

“**Six pence,**” he said. “**That is the number on the page. What is the number in the mouths.**”

The man of about forty-four said nine, and then said that he had known the number in the mouths for about twenty years and had never been able to prove it to anybody who would put his name to it, and that this was the first time in twenty-two years that anybody had walked into that room and asked him the two questions in the wrong order.

**And then the man of about twenty-nine asked him the third question, which is the one nobody had ever asked him, and it was about a rate on a road four counties off that nobody collects and nobody can check.**

“**There is a rate on a road four counties off,**” he said. “**It is not a rate anybody set. It is not in your book. It works because nobody can check it, and that is the whole of why it works, and I am not going to put it in a column and neither are you.**”

“**I have been on the wrong end of one,**” the man of about forty-four said.

**And that is nine words in one turn of one speech, and he had been on the wrong end of a rate nobody can check for eleven years and had never been able to say so to anybody, and a rate nobody can check is a rate nobody can complain of, and that is the whole reason it works.**

## Four

**And the man of about forty-four said the thing that has been true of that trade for twenty-two years and that nobody had said out loud, at about the seventh hour, to a boy of about sixteen and a man of about twenty-nine and four people who were buying nothing.**

“**I have been the middle of that trade for twenty-two years and I have never had to answer for a penny of it, and I have always thought that was a good arrangement, and it is not a good arrangement, it is an accident, and an accident with a book in it is a keeper. The day somebody writes down what a cart-load of shell is worth, that page is the keeper, and the person who keeps the page is the person who answers, and neither of them will be a man on a sand.**”

“**You have come four days with a request for a road that is there when he wants it,**” he said, “**and you have a book with a price in it and no name on the price, and those are the same want with the coat off.**”

**And that is a hundred and thirty-nine words in two turns of one speech, of which the first is ninety-five and the second is forty-four, and it is the first time in twenty-two years that anybody in that street had said anything about that trade that was not a price, and a boy of about sixteen wrote it out in the middle of a line he was not supposed to be writing on and neither man stopped him.**

## Five

The cost of that Saturday is a column and it is the man of about forty-four's, and it is the first one he has written down in his own book in about nine years, and it is in the back and not in the front, and the boy of about sixteen saw him write it and said nothing.

**a quill and ink at three pence, a sheet of paper at one, a second sheet of paper at one, a pound of cheese at four, a loaf at one, a second loaf at one, a pound of wood at three, a tallow candle at two, a room at six, a bed at four, a second night's bed at four, a quart of small beer at two, a second quart of small beer at two, a brush at two, a pound of candles at four, a handcart for the two days at four, a basket at five, a broom at five, a bucket of tar at eight, a pound of rope at six, a knife at two, a second knife at two, a second broom at five, a pound of nails at three, a second pound of nails at three, a pair of gloves at two, a second pair of gloves at two, a waxed cord at four, a second waxed cord at four, an inkwell at three.** Three, one, one, four, one, one, three, two, six, four, four, two, two, two, four, four, five, five, eight, six, two, two, five, three, three, two, two, four, four and three is ninety-eight pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and ninety-eight pence is a mark and fifty over**, and two figures of about nine hundred people are not in it and are not added to one another, and a rate on a road four counties off that nobody can check is not in it and is not in any book, and twenty-two years of a book kept in one hand is not in it and is not a figure of men.

**And a man of about forty-four wrote ninety-eight pence in the back of his own book on a market morning in a town of about two thousand people and said out loud that a page with a price on it is a keeper, and four people who were buying nothing heard him say it and two of them have it as a road and the other two have it as his own trade, and a boy of about sixteen put two figures of about nine hundred people on one line of a sheet and was told they were the same number and not the same people, and the man of about twenty-nine did not open his eight folded sheets, and a kiln four days off a coast where a river runs into the sea went on paying six pence a cart-load out of the same book it has paid out of for twenty-two years, and about nine hundred people went on getting across two miles and about nine hundred paces of hard sand four times out of twelve, and the water came in at about the ninth hour and went back at about the fourth hour of the morning.**
