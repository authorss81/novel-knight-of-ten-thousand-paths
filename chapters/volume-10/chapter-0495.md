# Chapter 0495

**The seventeenth day of Rainmonth, YR 319, a Monday and not a market day, the third ebb after the one where the water came over eleven paces of stone, and the fourth hour after the water went out, at the seaward end of that sand, where nobody says a month or a day of the week, and three rooms have sent a woman down a made road and a day off a boat to say that the coast has terms, and the terms are on the coast's own paper and the fourth head on that paper is filled in and it is a different head on a different document from the fourth head of a form in a market town four days up, which is empty and stays empty.**

## One

**What came down that sand on the seventeenth day of a month carried at thirty was a sheet of paper with four heads cut into it, folded in three, and the sheet is a foot and a half long and it is not the same size as the form in the market town four days up and it was not cut in the same town, and about four people on that sand said so out loud before anybody asked them to.**

The first three heads are the heads anybody would expect, because they are the heads on every form anybody has: a place, a figure of houses, and a trade. The place is **two miles and about nine hundred paces of hard sand where a river runs into the sea.** The figure of houses is **eleven.** The trade is **salt, and glass, and grain, and preserved fruit, and four boats.**

The fourth head is a line for a name, and it is filled in.

**And the name in it is a word, and the word is one the coast has said out loud for about nineteen years, and it is the word for the thing the water leaves when it goes off that sand, and the woman of about fifty-one who reads a tide off a post at that end said the word twice on that tide and did not know the woman carrying the sheet had heard her say it and had not asked her anything.**

“**That is not a name,**” a man of about thirty-eight with a crowbar said, from nine hundred yards off, at about the fourth hour, to nobody, and about four people heard it.

“**It is the name of the road,**” the woman who carried the sheet said, and she was about thirty-four and had four days of a made road and a day of a boat behind her and had a barrow at the landward end with nothing in it, and she had not been asked to carry anything and had not been paid and had not been thanked.

“**It is the name of a thing that is under water twice a day,**” the man of about thirty-eight with a crowbar said, “**and a name that is a thing is not a keeper, and a keeper is a person, and I have been putting timber against a wall for two months and I would know a keeper if one came down that sand.**”

“**Then you have seen nine hundred people go over that wall,**” the woman who carried the sheet said, “**and not one of them stopped for it, and not one of them asked who keeps it, and I have been four days on a made road and a day on a boat to be told what a coast's terms are and the terms are the name of the road and the shape of the water and the number of houses.**”

**And that is a hundred and thirty-nine words in four turns of one exchange, of which the first is five, the second is seven, the third is fifty-five printed in two spans of fourteen and forty-one, and the fourth is printed in two spans of eleven and the rest of the seventy-two, and the man of about thirty-eight with a crowbar came nine hundred yards on a sand that is there about four hours out of twelve to say five words and then stayed, and the woman who carried the sheet did not know the man of about thirty-two with a limp was on that sand and he did not tell her and she did not ask and neither of them has said the word in the fourth head out loud on any tide since.**

## Two

**And the terms were three, and they were read out four times that tide on a foreshore where nobody has said a day of the week in about nine years, and about nine hundred people were on that sand and about four of the nine hundred were within nine feet of the woman reading and about nine of the nine hundred did not stop.**

The first term is that **the road is a road while the water is off it and a place with water on it while the water is on it, and the two of those are one thing and not two.**

The second term is that **the eleven houses are the road's keeper, and the four that have somebody's work happening in them are the four that keep it, and the seven with nobody in them are the way out of the weather, and the road has been kept that way for about nineteen years and has not been agreed to by anybody.**

The third term is that **the water is the only thing on that coast that decides anything, and it decides twice a day, and it is not paid and it does not attend anything and it cannot be asked.**

“**Who is the keeper?**” a man of about forty-one with a basket said, at about the fifth hour, and he had crossed the sand that day with nine baskets and had not once thought about it.

“**There is not one,**” the woman who carried the sheet said. “**That is the second term. That is the whole of the second term. If you have understood it, you have understood the sheet.**”

“**I have understood the sheet,**” the man of about forty-one with a basket said, “**and I have understood it about nine seconds after I asked the question, and I asked it because I have been carrying baskets over that road for about nine years and I wanted to say the word out loud once.**”

“**You may say the word,**” the woman who carried the sheet said. “**Nobody is going to give you one to say. If somebody gives you one to say then this is a form with a name on it and it is not the form on this sheet, and I will know, and so will you, and so will the man who wrote it.**”

**And that is a hundred and nineteen words in five turns of one exchange, of which the first is four, the second is twenty-three, the third is printed in two spans of five and forty, the fourth is five, and the fifth is fifty-one, and the man of about forty-one with a basket asked a question he had wanted to ask for about nine years and was told there was no answer and was not thanked for asking and did not want to be, and the woman who carried the sheet has said the second term out loud four times on that sand and has not said it a fifth time because about nine hundred people had already heard it four times.**

## Three

**And what the coast's terms do not have is the thing that every form anybody has carried up and down that made road for four months has had on it, and the not-having of it is the whole of the difference, and about nine people on that sand said a version of that out loud in about four minutes and none of them said the version that was best.**

A man of about thirty-four from the port four days off had come down on the day before and had read the sheet twice and had said nothing about it to anybody, and at about the sixth hour he said one thing to the woman who carried it.

“**There is no name on the fourth head,**” the man of about thirty-four from the port four days off said.

“**There is a word on the fourth head,**” the woman who carried the sheet said.

“**A word is a name that has not asked anybody's permission,**” the man of about thirty-four from the port four days off said, “**and I say that as a man who has carried four clauses about a wagon up and down a made road four times and has never once had to say that, because nobody has ever asked me for a fifth clause.**”

“**Then you will not say it about that word either,**” the woman who carried the sheet said, “**because there is nobody on this sheet for you to say it about. There is a place, and there is a figure of houses, and there is a trade, and there is a word for the road. That is four. I have not put a person on it and neither have the three rooms that sent me, and the three rooms will be asked about it and they will say no, and you may write that down and take it up a made road and nobody will stop you.**”

“**And the form in the market town four days up?**”

“**Is a different piece of paper with a different head on it,**” the woman who carried the sheet said, “**and that head has been empty since the middle of a month in the year before this one and it is going to be empty on the twenty-fifth of this month, and nobody in nine counties has put a name in it and nobody is going to and I have not come down this sand to say that anybody has.**”

**And that is two hundred and forty-nine words in six turns of one exchange, of which the first is eight, the second is eight, the third is printed in two spans of eleven and forty-one, the fourth is ninety-nine printed in two spans of ten and eighty-nine, the fifth is ten, and the sixth is printed in two spans of twelve and sixty, and the man of about thirty-four from the port four days off said the sentence about a word and a name and then took it back off himself in the next turn, and the woman who carried the sheet said the difference between two pieces of paper on a foreshore and did not raise her voice at any point in it, and about four people heard the whole of it and about nine heard the first two turns.**

## Four

**And a woman of about fifty-one was at that seaward end at about the fourth hour with the driftwood that is the length of a forearm and two inches through, in a driven post five inches square, and she read the mark on that post for about as long as it takes a man to say nine, and then she went back up the sand, and the twenty-second notch is not cut, and the water did not go off that mark on the seventeenth day of a month carried at thirty in this year and has not gone off it in twenty-two years.**

She was not asked what the word on the fourth head was. She was not asked what her want was. Nobody on that sand has asked her either in twenty-two years and about four people have wanted to and have not, and one of those four is the woman who carried the sheet and she did not ask.

**And a man of about thirty-two with a limp was about two hundred paces landward of that post at the fourth hour and at the sixth hour and at the eighth hour, and he was the hundred and ninth person over that sand that tide, and the hundred and ninth is not the first, and he did not read the sheet and was not asked to and did not ask.**

## Five

The cost of that tide is a column and it is the woman of thirty-four's who carried the sheet, and it is the first one she has ever kept and she kept it on the back of a handcart docket with a nail she found at the landward end, and a word on a sheet is not in it.

**a sheet of paper at one, a second sheet of paper at one, a third sheet of paper at one, a fourth sheet of paper at one, a fifth sheet of paper at one, a sixth sheet of paper at one, a quill and ink at three, a second quill and ink at three, a knife at two, a second knife at two, a stop for a door at two, a second stop for a door at two, a pound of candles at four, a tallow candle at two, a second tallow candle at two, a broom at five, a bucket of tar at eight, a pound of nails at three, a waxed cord at four, a second waxed cord at four, a room at six, a bed at four, a second bed at four, a loaf at one, a pound of cheese at four, a pear at one, a second pear at one, a third pear at one, a quart of small beer at two, a bushel of oats at four, a handcart for the day at four, an inkwell at three, a second inkwell at three, and a pair of gloves at two.** One, one, one, one, one, one, three, three, two, two, two, two, four, two, two, five, eight, three, four, four, six, four, four, one, four, one, one, one, two, four, four, three, three and two is ninety-two pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and ninety-two pence is a mark and forty-four over**, and a word on a sheet is not in it and is not a name, and a place and a figure of houses and a trade are not in it and are three heads, and eleven houses about four hundred paces long with four of them somebody's work and seven of them nobody's are not in it and are never added to eleven in a column, and four days of a made road and a day of a boat are not in it.

**And a coast set its own terms on the seventeenth day of Rainmonth in this year, four days before anybody was going to be asked anything about them, and the terms are that the road is a road while the water is off it, that eleven houses are the keeper and four of the eleven do the keeping, and that the water decides twice a day and is not paid, and the fourth head of the sheet carried down that sand has a word on it and not a person, and the fourth head of a different form on a different paper in a market town four days up has been empty since the middle of a month in Wolfmonth and was still empty, and the sea came over the seaward half at about the ninth hour and took about two hundred paces of it and was off it again before four in the morning.**
