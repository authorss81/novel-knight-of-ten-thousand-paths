# Chapter 0476

**The twenty-eighth day of Thawmonth, YR 319, a Thursday and a market day, and the second hour and the sixth hour, on a foreshore two miles and about nine hundred paces long where a river runs into the sea, at a hundred paces from the landward end, and there is no month in anybody's mouth on that shore and no day of the week, and a man of about thirty-four with a sheet has come down that sand for the fifth time since the middle of a month in Wolfmonth and there is another man with him, and four people on that sand saw the two of them arrive and three of the four have not said so to anybody.**

## One

**Eleven paces of stone and about two feet high stands at a hundred paces from the landward end with a second footing course nine inches lower, and about four inches of sand has banked against the far side of it in four months, and it took the water over it twice that day exactly as it has twice a day since the four days it took to lay.**

The man of about thirty-four came down on the ebb and stood at the stone and did not go within nine feet of it, which is the same as the four times before and is the only thing anybody on that sand could say about him.

He had a sheet of paper on a board and he wrote on it for about twenty minutes, and he did not look up more than four times, and four people were within nine feet of him the whole of the time and all four of them were doing something else at the same time.

**He has been on that sand four times and he has not said why he comes when he comes, and nobody has asked him, and on this fifth time there was a second man, and the second man is the first person in about nineteen years to stand on that sand who was not there to buy, to sell, to carry, to boil, or to count a tide.**

## Two

**The second man is about twenty-eight and he carries nothing. He has no chain and no rod and no sheet and he does not speak, and he did not stand at the stone. He stood about forty yards inland of it and looked at the sand.**

He looked at the sand for about twenty minutes the way a man looks at a road he has not been on, and then he looked at the eleven houses, one at a time, from the landward end to the seaward end, and four people on that sand followed where he was looking without knowing they were doing it.

**A man of about fifty-three with a wagon was at the seventh of the eleven and did not follow him, and has said since then that he did not follow him because he has never once in nineteen years looked at his own sand from the landward end.**

Nobody said anything to either of them.

## Three

**The only conversation on that sand that tide was about a jug of water, and it was between a woman of about twenty-seven with a basket and a man of about thirty-four, and four people heard it, and it is the whole of what nineteen years of a road with a stone on it has produced in the way of contact.**

“**Is there water I could have?**” he said, “**and I am not asking you to fetch it. If there is a jug with anything in it I can come to it.**”

She gave it to him out of a jug with the handle broken off.

“**It has been broken since I came,**” she said, “**and I have not had it mended, and you are welcome to it.**”

He said thank you, and she said nothing, and he drank about half and gave the jug back and wiped the rim on his sleeve.

**And that is forty-nine words in two turns of one exchange, of which the first is twenty-nine printed in two spans of six and twenty-three and the second is twenty printed in two spans of seven and thirteen, and four people heard it, and the man of about thirty-four did not ask the second man whether he wanted any and the second man did not ask and did not look over, and the woman of about twenty-seven has said since then that she noticed that, and that she has been thinking about it for nine days, and that she has not worked out what she noticed.**

## Four

**And then the man of about thirty-four said one thing to the woman of about twenty-seven that four people heard, and it was not about the water and it was not about the stone.**

“**Has anybody ever come down this sand twice in a year?**”

“**I have been here two years and I cannot answer for anybody else,**” she said. “**A man of about fifty-three comes four times a week and has for nineteen years, and you have been five times in about two months, and those are the only two I know of.**”

“**I am the second of those,**” he said, “**and I did not know about the first one was there until this morning, and I have written it down.**”

**And that is eighty-four words in three turns of one exchange, of which the first is eleven, the second is forty-seven printed in two spans of thirteen and thirty-four, and the third is twenty-six printed in two spans of six and twenty, and the three are all printed and the sentences around them are part of what is being counted, and the man of about thirty-four said thank you again and went back to his board, and the second man had already gone inland and was not on the sand, and nobody had seen him leave and four people had not known he was there until he was not.**

**That is the fifth time and there is a different man, and nothing on that sand is scheduled, and the eleven paces of stone took the tide over it twice that day, and the only thing that has happened in two months that has not happened in nineteen years is that two men came down a sand that nobody owns and one of them was not there for the stone.**

## Five

The cost of that Thursday is a column and it is the man of about thirty-four's, and it is the fifth of them, and he has five of them in a fold in a coat and has never joined them up, and the second man's day is not in it because the second man has not been asked and has not offered.

**a sheet of paper at one penny, a second sheet of paper at one, a third sheet of paper at one, a fourth sheet of paper at one, a fifth sheet of paper at one, a quill and ink at three, a loaf at one, a second loaf at one, a third loaf at one, a pound of cheese at four, a pound of wood at three, a tallow candle at two, a second tallow candle at two, a quart of small beer at two, a second quart of small beer at two, a night's lodging at a lodging house at four, a second night's lodging at four, a third night's lodging at four, a pair of boots mended at five, a second pair of boots mended at six, a handcart for the four days at four, a basket at five, a broom at five, a pound of rope at six, and a pound of nails at three.** One, one, one, one, one, three, one, one, one, four, three, two, two, two, two, four, four, four, five, six, four, five, five, six and three is seventy-two pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and seventy-two pence is a mark and twenty-four over**, and eleven paces of stone and a second footing course nine inches lower and about four inches of sand in four months are not in it, and four of eleven houses and seven of eleven houses are not in it and are never added to eleven in a column, and a man of about twenty-eight who carries nothing is not in it and is not a figure of men.

**And a man of about thirty-four with a sheet came down that sand for the fifth time and there was a different man with him and four people saw it and three of the four have not said so, and a woman of about twenty-seven gave a man half a jug of water out of a jug with a broken handle and has been thinking about what she noticed for nine days, and eleven paces of stone stood at a hundred paces from the landward end and took the water over it twice, and the water came in at about the ninth hour and went over about two hundred paces of the seaward half and went back at about the fourth hour of the morning with the wind still against it.**
