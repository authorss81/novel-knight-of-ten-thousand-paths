# Chapter 0454

**The eighth day of Frostmonth, YR 318, a Saturday and a market morning in a county about two hundred miles off a street of working yards where a market comes in off six villages, and it is not a market morning in this yard and never has been, and the first hour and the fifth hour and the ninth hour, in a slipway and a yard behind a weigh-house in a port of about nine hundred people at the landward end of two miles and about nine hundred paces of hard sand where a river runs into the sea, and the yard is shut twice a day for the water, and about four hundred people are on the sand and about five hundred are in four houses and a yard, and a man of thirty-two with a limp is in it, and a woman of about fifty-one is in it, and a man of about twenty-nine is in it and has a letter in his coat.**

## One

**A market is a day where people are gathered who would not otherwise be in the same street. A market morning is the word for the day of the week that a market happens on, and a man of thirty-four with a sheet has put that word on about nine pieces of paper in nine counties, and on a foreshore two miles and about nine hundred paces long there is no such word and never has been.**

The market on that foreshore begins when the water goes out and ends when it comes back in, and it ends the same way twice a day, and a wagon that is on the sand at the moment it stops is a wagon that stands on the sand for about six hours with its shafts down and nobody near it, and that is the ordinary condition of trade on a road that is not there all the time.

**The man of about twenty-nine said the word *Saturday* out loud at about the fifth hour, in a yard, to two people, because he had been on that sand about five days and had heard the word and could not help it, and a woman of about thirty-four with a barrow-load of shell on her hands stopped and looked at him.**

“**Say that again,**” she said.

“**I said it because it is a market morning,**” he said. “**Back up the coast, four hundred miles.**”

“**It is a market,**” she said, “**and it has been a market since before the wall, and you are going to keep saying that word and I am going to keep telling you that it has no morning.**”

## Two

**And a man of thirty-two with a limp is in that yard and is not from that coast and has been on that sand for three days and has not been asked one thing by anybody, and about nine people in that yard have worked out that he is not from here and about four of the nine have worked out that he is not staying, and neither of those is written down and one of the nine is the man who sells the glass and he has said nothing to anybody about either.**

He had not been on the sand long when he said it. It was the ordinary thing to say to a woman who is standing in the middle of a market with a barrow in front of her and about nine hundred people behind her.

“**Move her along and I will take the next one,**” he said.

**And that is ten words in one turn of one speech and it is the only sentence he has said on that foreshore in an accent that is not his, and about six people heard it, and one of the six was a woman of about thirty-four with a barrow and she heard it the way the foreshore hears it and he heard it the way Merefen hears it and the two of those are not the same sound.**

The woman of about thirty-four repeated it back, in the ordinary way, the way you repeat a thing to a stranger to be sure he has said it, and it came out of her in about the accent of a foreshore and it was exactly what he had meant, and it was not the sound that had been in his mouth.

**And he heard both of them at once, about four seconds apart, and about a year of a man's life in a fen town came out of his mouth in somebody else's mouth, and the man of about twenty-nine was the only other person who noticed and he noticed the accent and not the sentence and he has not said so and is not going to.**

Nobody said anything about it. That is the whole of the first cost and it is the cheapest of the three in a month of this and it is the only one nobody can see coming.

## Three

The market is nine hundred people and about four hundred of them are on the sand and about five hundred are in four houses and a yard, and the other seven of the eleven are on the seaward half and are shut.

What is on the sand at about the sixth hour on a day when the water is going out is this. A barrow of shell. About nine cart-loads of it a day, off four boats, and it goes into the pans of four families. A wagon of salt out of the landward end, and the wagon waits at the far end of the sand for the tide and the driver sleeps in the wagon and is not paid extra for the six hours. **A load of glass that came nine days on a cart and is going to be a pane in about nine houses on a coast four hundred miles the other way, and the man who sold it is standing beside it because a thing that has come nine days is not left.** Preserved fruit in eleven baskets, of which nine are bought before the salt is off and two are not.

And a woman of about fifty-one with a piece of driftwood and twenty-two notches cut in the edge of it, standing at the seaward end of the sand, where there is nothing on it and nothing to sell.

**She is not selling anything and she is not waiting for anything and she is at the seaward end because at the seaward end you can see whether the water is coming, and she is the only person in nine hundred who needs to know and cannot see the sky from where she is standing because of a bank.**

## Four

The man of about twenty-nine got his letter out of his coat about the seventh hour and asked whether anybody on that foreshore had ever seen a keeper, and the yard got quiet in the ordinary way a yard gets quiet.

A woman of about thirty-four with a barrow said the word *keeper* back to him in the tone of somebody handling a coin.

“**We have got four of them,**” she said. “**That is what the four houses are. And the seven that nobody owns are the ones that get used, and if you put a keeper at the seaward end then you have got five, and if you put a keeper at the landward end you have got six, and at some point in about nine years somebody is going to want to know why two of the four are keepers and five of the others are not.**”

“**Who are the four,**” the man of about twenty-nine said.

“**A woman of sixty-three with a pan and a fire that have not gone out since she was twenty-two,**” she said, “**a man with nine hundred acres inland and a lease on a piece of shingle, a widow with the last of a boat, and a man who is not here today and owns the fourth house and is not a good man and nobody will say so out loud on this sand.**”

**And the woman of about fifty-one was at the seaward end and heard all of that and did not come over, and about nine people saw that she did not come over, and about four of the nine understood what it was.**

## Five

The cost of that Saturday is a column and it is the man of about twenty-nine's, and he has been eleven days on this coast and has eaten at four of the eleven houses and slept at five, and the five are not a figure of men and are not on any list.

**a loaf at one penny, a second loaf at one, a pound of cheese at four, a second pound of cheese at four, a tallow candle at two, a second tallow candle at two, a third tallow candle at two, a quart of small beer at two, a bushel of oats at four, a broom at five, a handcart hired for the day at eight.** One, one, four, four, two, two, two, two, four, five and eight is thirty-five pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and thirty-five pence is under one by thirteen**, and about four hundred people were on that sand and about five hundred were in that yard and those four houses on that Saturday and none of them is a figure of men and none of them went on anything, and about nine cart-loads of shell and a wagon of salt and eleven baskets of preserved fruit and nine panes of glass are not in the column and are somebody else's day.

**And a man of about twenty-nine said a word for a day of the week in a yard on a foreshore where there are no days of the week, and was told twice by two different women that he had got it wrong, and a man of thirty-two with a limp said ten words in an accent that is about four hundred miles from any town he has worked in and heard the same ten words come back at him out of a foreshore, and the six people who heard it did not say one word about it, and about nine hundred people went home along about nine hundred paces of hard sand to eleven houses, four of which have a keeper and seven of which do not, and the water came back over all of it at about the tenth hour and the wall of stone at a hundred paces from the landward end did not move.**
