# Chapter 0473

**The twelfth day of Thawmonth, YR 319, a Tuesday and not a market day, and the second hour and the fifth hour and the tenth hour, at the ninth of eleven houses on a foreshore two miles and about nine hundred paces long where a river runs into the sea, and there is no month in anybody's mouth on that shore and no day of the week, and a salt wagon that has been off and on that sand for nineteen years has gone down on the ebb and is standing at the ninth of the eleven with the horses in the water, and nine people are standing nine feet off it, and four of them are the kind of person who will carry what is said.**

## One

**The wagon is broad-wheeled and the near rims have been cut down twice and the tipping takes nine minutes, and the man with nine hundred acres inland stands in the doorway of the ninth of the eleven and watches it and has never once offered a hand.**

He has been buying salt off this wagon for eleven years and he has never once asked what the wagon costs and the carter has never once said, and this is the arrangement, and neither man has ever called it an arrangement.

**The carter is fifty-three. His name is Tobin Kernick and he is on this sand four times a week and he is not keeping anything and nobody has asked him to.**

He said the number himself, at the fifth hour, standing at the off wheel with the sacks still on it, and he said it because a man of about twenty-nine had asked him a question on a previous tide and had gone four days up a made road with it, and there had been a question in the asking that had never been answered.

## Two

“**Three sacks a trip,**” he said. “**That is what I am paid and it is not money, it is three sacks, and the ninth house has paid nine pence a sack for eleven years without once arguing about it, and that is the only time in nineteen years I have ever put the salt and the money side by side, and neither of them is ever going in a book.**”

**And that is sixty-eight words in one turn of one speech printed in two spans of four and sixty-four, and nine people were standing nine feet off it and four of them heard the arithmetic and four of them heard a man of fifty-three saying a thing out loud that he had kept in his head, and nobody on that sand had known that he knew it.**

“**A trip is three sacks at nine pence, and that is twenty-seven pence,**” he said. “**And a day of that wagon is two horses on sand and tar and a farrier and my own food, and a day of that wagon costs me fifty-four pence, and it has cost me that every day for nineteen years, and I have never once had a whole day off it that was not a day the water was in, and not one of them was mine to choose.**”

**And that is eighty-three words in one turn of one speech printed in two spans of thirteen and seventy, and both are counted, and the man of about fifty-three has said the number fifty-four out loud on that sand for the first time in nineteen years and a man with nine hundred acres inland heard every word of it from a doorway.**

## Three

**And then he said the part that is the answer to a question nobody on that shore had thought to put, and he said it to the ninth of the eleven and not to the man standing in the doorway, and nine people heard it and the water was two hours off going back.**

“**A day is twenty-seven pence the wrong way. I get about two hundred days a year when the water is off long enough, and twenty-seven pence short on two hundred days is five thousand four hundred pence a year, and five thousand four hundred pence is two hundred and seventy days of a person living at twenty pence a day, and two hundred and seventy days is nine months.**”

“**I have been running this wagon nineteen years and it has cost somebody nine months of a winter every year, nineteen times, and I know exactly which four of the eleven it has been going into, and I could not tell you who paid it, and neither can the man standing in that doorway, and if you want the answer to that then you have come to the right sand and the wrong question, and the answer is that nobody paid it and everybody did!**”

**And that is a hundred and fifty-four words in two turns of one speech, of which the first is sixty-nine and the second is eighty-five, and the man of about fifty-three has not said a sentence of that length out loud on that sand in nineteen years and has said one of them about himself, and the man with nine hundred acres inland went inside nine seconds before the end of the second one and shut the door and did not open it again that tide.**

## Four

**The woman of about thirty-four with a barrow was nine feet off and had a sheet of oiled paper wrapped round nine pounds of salt in the front of her barrow, and she asked him the one question that nine people on that sand heard and four of them have said it was the only one anybody asked.**

“**Who do you think paid it?**”

And the man of about fifty-three said that he had never asked, and that a man who does not ask is a man who does not get to be angry, and that he had been not asking for nineteen years and had got very good at it.

“**If the man at the ninth house had known,**” he said, “**he would have said nothing and paid a penny less, and that is how it works, and I am not complaining about it and I am not saying it is a discovery. I am saying that you are the only one on this sand who has asked me and can be asked at all.**”

**And that is sixty-nine words in two turns of one exchange, of which the first is six and the second is sixty-three printed in two spans of nine and fifty-four, and both are printed, and a man of about fifty-three has said sixty-three words out loud to a woman with a barrow on a foreshore in about nineteen years and had not said the last forty-five of them about himself.**

**And four of the nine said that it was the truest thing said on that sand in a year, and four of them said that it was a man of fifty-three managing what happened next, and the woman of about thirty-four with a barrow said that both of those were the same sentence said by two people who did not know each other, and she has said that she is the only one of the four who has said anything about it, and the woman of about fifty-one at the seaward end was not told and does not want to be told.**

## Five

The cost of that Tuesday is a column and it is the man of about fifty-three's, and it is the first one he has written down in nineteen years and he wrote it on the back of a handcart docket with a nail, and the salt is not in it and the money is not in it twice.

**forage for two horses at eighteen pence, tar and shoeing at six, a farrier at three, the driver's food at five, a second loaf at one, a third loaf at one, a quart of small beer at two, a second quart of small beer at two, a tallow candle at two, a second tallow candle at two, a pound of cheese at four, a second pound of cheese at four, a sheet of paper at one, and a quill and ink at three.** Eighteen, six, three, five, one, one, two, two, two, two, four, four, one and three is fifty-four pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and fifty-four pence is a mark and six over**, and three sacks a trip and nine pence a sack and twenty-seven pence are not in it and are not added to it, and nineteen years of a salt wagon is not in it and is not a figure of men, and five thousand four hundred pence a year is not in it and is a price on one shore and a wage on another and is the same number twice over and nobody on that sand has ever seen the second copy of it.

**And a man of fifty-three stood at the off wheel of a wagon at the ninth of eleven houses and said out loud that the wagon had cost somebody nine months of a winter every year for nineteen years, and a man with nine hundred acres inland went inside and shut a door, and nine people heard it and four of them will carry it, and nine cart-loads of shell went off four boats into four pans the next tide, and the fire behind the fourth house has not been out since a month in a year a woman of sixty-three does not give, and a piece of driftwood at the seaward end has twenty-two notches cut in the edge of it, one for each year, the twenty-second not cut, and the water came in at about the ninth hour and went over about two hundred paces of the seaward half and went back at about the fourth hour of the morning with the wind still against it.**
