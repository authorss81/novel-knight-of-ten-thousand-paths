# Chapter 0474

**The eighteenth day of Thawmonth, YR 319, a Monday and not a market day, and the fourth hour and the seventh hour, at a cart shed nine miles up a made road that runs four days to a foreshore where a river runs into the sea, with a trough and a fire ring and nine people and eleven carts, and a woman of about twenty-four who has been at the pans on that sand for two years has walked up that road with a bundle on her shoulder and has said one word nine times since she came in, and a man of about twenty-nine who gave the road its reason is nine feet off with a sheet against the wall, and a man of about thirty-two with a limp is sitting on the trough.**

## One

**The word is the working, and on that sand it means the four hours out of twelve when the water is off, and about nine hundred people have been using that word for about nineteen years, and it has never once meant a shift.**

She had said it nine times in about an hour and the ninth of the nine was to a carter of about forty who had a cart on the stones and had four men standing behind him who were not to be paid until Thursday.

“**Are they on the working?**”

And then he said it back to her, the way a man says a word he has just taken in and has not looked at yet, and there were nine people at that shed and about four of them turned round, and the woman of about twenty-four did not hear it and could not have stopped it.

**And a word that has meant four hours of a tide since before she was born has meant a shift since before anybody at that shed was born, and both of those are true, and nobody at that shed had ever heard the first one.**

## Two

The man of about twenty-nine said the true thing about the word, and he said it to the fire ring and not to the woman, and he had been carrying it up that road for nine days and had not known he was carrying it.

“**I came down that road with a request for a road that is there when I want it,**” he said. “**And nine people on a shore said no to it in about nine minutes on five grounds, and one of the five was that nobody on that shore would ever be answerable for it, and I have written all five down and I have told them on the way up, and the one that keeps coming back is the one about being answerable.**”

“**And I have worked it out since, and it is not the reason they gave, and the reason they gave is a good one and I would have given it myself.**”

**And that is eighty-one words in the first of two turns of one speech, printed in two spans of eighteen and sixty-three, and the second of the two turns is thirty-one words, and he counted the first of the two on the back of a sheet folded in four because he counts things, and did not write the number down, and the woman of about twenty-four heard the second half of it and has said that she was one of the nine who said no and that the reason it was not the reason they gave is not a thing to be settled on a made road.**

## Three

**The true reason is a page, and it came out of a salt factor's yard four days further on, and the man of about twenty-nine said it once at a cart shed and never said it again to anybody for the rest of that week.**

“**A permanent connection is a fixed price, and a fixed price is a book, and a book is a person who answers for it, and that is all a keeper is. So the shore said no for the reason you would give, which is that nobody would be there to answer, and the true reason is that somebody would be there to answer, and that is the whole of what they were refusing and they did not have the word for it and neither did I, and I found it out about a cart-load of shell.**”

**And that is ninety-six words in one turn of one speech, and it is the third time anybody has said it out loud, and the other two were said by a man of about forty-four behind a counter four days further on and by a man of about thirty-two with a limp who was standing nine feet off when the second of the two was said, and the man of about thirty-two with a limp is sitting on the trough at this moment and has not said a word, and four people at that cart shed have carried it and two of them have already been asked about it by a man off the road four days further on and neither of them has given him anything.**

Then the woman of about twenty-four sang.

## Four

**She sang it because somebody at that shed asked her if they had a song on that sand, and it is four lines and the last line of each one turns back on the first, and it is about a pan and the crust on the top of it, and she sang one line of it in a metre that is not the metre it is sung in four hundred yards of that sand.**

“**And the crust comes off in one piece,**” she said, and then she sang the rest of it and the rest of it had a fifth line, and the fifth line is not from that coast and belongs to a kiln four days off and came up this road inside a wagon of shell.

**Nobody at that shed could have said where the fifth line came from and nobody asked, and about four of the nine have said that it is the best line in it, and the woman of about twenty-four has said that it is not hers and that she would not take it back out now.**

The man of about thirty-two with a limp sat on the trough for the whole of it and said nothing, and after about an hour he asked her one question, which was whether anybody on that sand had asked her what she wanted, and she said that nobody had and that she had not been on that sand long enough to be asked, and that the oldest person on it had not been asked one thing in forty-one years and had not complained about it.

**And then he said that he was sorry, and she said that he was not to, and neither of them said one word more about it, and the carter of about forty paid four men on the fixed day and did not know whose fault the working was.**

## Five

The cost of that Monday is a column and it is the man of about twenty-nine's and it is the fourth of them, and he has four of them in a fold in the inside of a coat, and a word and a song are not in it and cannot go in it, and he has still not written a total against any of the four.

**a loaf at one penny, a second loaf at one, a third loaf at one, a pound of cheese at four, a pound of wood at three, a tallow candle at two, a room at six, a bed at four, a second night's bed at four, a quart of small beer at two, a second quart of small beer at two, a sheet of paper at one, a second sheet of paper at one, a third sheet of paper at one, a fourth sheet of paper at one, a quill and ink at three, a handcart for the day at four, a basket at five, a broom at five, a pound of rope at six, a pair of gloves at two, a second pair of gloves at two, a night's lodging at the cart shed at two, a second night's lodging at the cart shed at two, a farrier at three, a pound of nails at three, a pound of candles at four, a second pound of candles at four, a second broom at five, and an inkwell at three.** One, one, one, four, three, two, six, four, four, two, two, one, one, one, one, three, four, five, five, six, two, two, two, two, three, three, four, four, five and three is eighty-seven pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and eighty-seven pence is a mark and thirty-nine over**, and a word that has changed its meaning twice and a song with a fifth line in it are not in it, and about nine hundred people on two miles and about nine hundred paces of hard sand are not in it and are not a figure of men, and nine miles of made road are not in it and nobody on it can be found about.

**And a woman of about twenty-four said the word the working nine times at a cart shed and a carter of about forty said it back meaning a shift, and a man of about twenty-nine said out loud why a shore had been right to refuse him, and a man of about thirty-two with a limp asked one question and was told not to be sorry, and four people at that shed carried it four days up a road to a port and eleven carts went down it, and the crust came off in one piece.**
