# Chapter 0486

**The seventeenth day of Greenmonth, YR 319, a Thursday and a market day, and the second hour and the fifth hour and the ninth hour, at a hundred paces from the landward end of a foreshore two miles and about nine hundred paces long where a river runs into the sea, where a man of about thirty-four with a sheet is standing nine paces off eleven paces of stone about two feet high with a second footing course nine inches lower on it and about four inches of sand banked against the far side, and this is the sixth time he has been on that sand since the middle of a month in Wolfmonth, and there is no month in anybody's mouth on that shore and no day of the week, and a woman of about thirty-four with a barrow has come up nine hundred paces to ask him the question four people on that sand have wanted to ask since the first of a month in a year a woman of sixty-three does not give.**

## One

**A man of about thirty-four with a sheet has been on that sand five times and has never once said why he comes when he comes, and on the fifth of them there was a second man with him, about twenty-eight, carrying nothing, who did not stand at the wall and looked at the eleven houses one at a time, and this is the sixth time and there is no second man and nobody has said anything about the fifth time to anybody.**

He came down on the ebb; he has a sheet of paper on a board and he has not written on it yet, and he has been standing nine paces off the stone for four minutes, and nine people on that sand are inside nine feet of him and all nine of them are doing something else.

**He is not a Crown officer and he does not say that he is and nobody on that sand has ever asked him and nobody in the four days up a made road has ever asked him either, and what he is, as far as four people who have thought about it can tell, is a man in a room of nine who is given a place to go to and a sheet to write on and comes when he is sent.**

## Two

**And the question was asked at about the fifth hour by a woman of about thirty-four with a barrow, and she asked it in seven words, and it is the first time in two months that anybody has asked that man anything at all.**

“**Why do you come when you come?**”

He did not answer for about as long as it takes a man to count seven, and four people on that sand thought he was not going to, and then he turned round, which he had not done, and put the board down on the shingle.

“**I am sent!**” he said, and four people on that sand heard the one word and understood it, which is more of them than understood anything else he said that tide. “**There is a place on a sheet and my name is not on it and neither is anybody's, and a man in a room with a seal decides how often the place is looked at, and he decided it, and I came, and I will come again when he decides it again. That is the whole of it. It is not a thing anybody asked me to keep to myself. It is a thing nobody has asked me.**”

“**Then who decided it?**”

“**I do not know. I have never been told and I have never asked and I am not going to ask, because if I ask I am a man who is asking, and right now I am a man who is looking at a wall.**”

**And that is a hundred and thirty-seven words in four turns of one exchange, of which the first is seven, the second is eighty-one printed in two spans of three and seventy-eight, the third is four, and the fourth is forty-five, and nine people on that sand heard all of it and about four of them heard it twice because the wind took the first seven words and she said them again and the second time she said them louder, and neither time did the man of about thirty-four with a sheet ask her to say them again.**

## Three

**And the answer was not a conspiracy and it was not nothing, and that is the whole of what happened at the fifth hour of the seventeenth day of Greenmonth, and four people on that sand have said, since that tide, that it was the smaller of the two things they expected and one of those four has said that she expected the larger.**

He picked the board up off the shingle and wrote on it for about eleven minutes, and he wrote four things, and the four things were how long the wall is and how high it is and how far the sand is banked against the far side of it and how many tides have gone over the top of it, and he wrote the date at the top of the sheet and there is no head on the sheet and he has never put one there.

“**I will tell you what I have not found out in six times,**” he said, to nobody in particular and to her in particular. “**I have not found out whether the fourth head on that form wants a person or wants a word. Nobody has ever told me which and I have now been six times and asked nobody and there is a room of about nine where the answer probably is and I am not going to look for it.**”

“**That is six times of a man not looking,**” the woman of about thirty-four with a barrow said.

“**That is six times of a man not looking,**” he said, “**and it is the only thing about those six times that is mine, and I would rather it was that than a story.**”

**And that is a hundred and eleven words in three turns of one exchange, of which the first is seventy printed in two spans of thirteen and fifty-seven, the second is nine, and the third is thirty-two printed in two spans of nine and twenty-three, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet said both of his out loud on a foreshore that has never had a question like it asked on it, and the woman of about thirty-four with a barrow is not going to be thanked for it and said so before anybody thought of it and has said it twice.**

## Four

**And nothing else happened on that tide that anybody has written down, and the second man of about twenty-eight who came on the fifth time was not on the sand on the sixth, and nobody looked for him, and the four people who followed him with their eyes on the fifth time did not follow anybody on the sixth because there was nobody to follow.**

Nine cart-loads of shell went off four boats into four pans on the ebb before the wall and nine people watched four boats go out and four of the nine were the same four who have said, since that tide, that the answer was the smaller of the two things they expected.

**And the man of about fifty-three with a wagon was at the seventh of the eleven and did not hear any of it and was not told, and a man of about forty-one who stands in the doorway of the seventh of the eleven houses was about two hundred paces seaward and heard the first seven words and not the rest, and the woman of about fifty-one at the seaward end was not told and does not want to be told, and a woman of sixty-three at the back of the fourth house was not told and put a fourth armful of wood on a fire that has not been out in forty-one years.**

## Five

The cost of that Thursday is a column and it is the man of about thirty-four's, and it is the sixth of them and he has six in a fold in a coat and has never joined two of them up, and a wall of eleven paces and a second footing course nine inches lower and about four inches of sand in four months are not in it and are not a figure of men.

**six sheets of paper at six, a quill and ink at three, a second quill and ink at three, a pound of candles at four, a second pound of candles at four, a pound of wood at three, a bucket of tar at eight, a broom at five, a second broom at five, a pound of rope at six, a waxed cord at four, a pair of gloves at two, a second pair of gloves at two, a pound of nails at three, an inkwell at three, a loaf at one, a second loaf at one, a third loaf at one, a pound of cheese at four, a room at six, a handcart for the day at four, a basket at five, a knife at two, a second knife at two, a brush at two, a bushel of oats at four, a second handcart for the day at four, a second room with a fire in it at eight, a pair of mitts at two, a tin of oil at two.** Six, three, three, four, four, three, eight, five, five, six, four, two, two, three, three, one, one, one, four, six, four, five, two, two, two, four, four, eight, two and two is a hundred and nine pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and nine pence is two marks and thirteen over**, and six times is not in it and is a figure of one man, and about nine hundred people and eleven houses and four of eleven and seven of eleven are not in it and are never added to one another in a column, and the fourth head of a form is not in it and is empty and is in a room over a weigh-house two hundred miles off.

**And a man of about thirty-four with a sheet was on a foreshore for the sixth time since the middle of a month in Wolfmonth and was asked why he comes when he comes by a woman of about thirty-four with a barrow, and said that he is sent and that he does not know who sends him and is not going to ask, and wrote four things on a sheet with no head on it and dated it at the top, and nobody has thanked either of them, and there is no second man on that sand this time, and the wall took the water over it twice that day, and at about the ninth hour the water came over eleven paces of stone for the second time that day and lay on the top course for two hours, and about two hundred paces of the seaward half went under it, and it was off the stone again before four in the morning.**
