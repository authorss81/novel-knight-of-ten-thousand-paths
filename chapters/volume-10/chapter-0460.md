# Chapter 0460

**The thirteenth day of Hearthmonth, YR 318, a Saturday and a market morning, and the fourth hour and the seventh hour and the tenth hour, on a made road between a town of about four thousand people and a port of about nine hundred, four days off a foreshore, where about nine carts stand in a yard and a market comes in off six villages, and there is one man of thirty-two with a limp on that road at about the seventh hour and there is nobody in a room anywhere on it, and about four hundred yards up a street of working yards from the town end of that road a man of twenty-five owns a yard of nine men and a bench-board four feet off the ground and a press at the end of it that cannot be served with, and he is at work and has not been told that the man on the road is on it.**

## One

**Seventeen days after a woman of twenty-nine said in a room over a weigh-house that a copy of how a person says a word is a thing about a person, a man of thirty-two with a limp walked four days from a port and stopped on a made road at about the seventh hour of a market morning, and about nine carts went past him and about nine of them were loaded and about four of the nine were going to the port and about five were coming away from it.**

He stood on the verge for about nine minutes. He had a stick and a coat and about four things in the coat, and one of the four is a sheet of nine inches by six with three lines and no head on it, and one of the four is a notebook with eleven lines on a page and a word on the eleventh of them, and nobody has asked him for either and he has not offered either.

**What he did on that verge for about nine minutes is not a decision and is not a revelation and is not in a notebook. It is that he tried to remember which of two kitchens he had been in on the twenty-fourth day, and he got it wrong twice, and on the third attempt he got the window right and got the door wrong, and he left it alone.**

## Two

A woman of about thirty-four with a barrow came up the road behind him about two minutes after he stopped, and she had come nine days and she had a barrow-load of stone on it, and she was going the other way.

“**You have been on a beach,**” she said.

He said that he had.

“**You are standing like a man who has been carrying something for nine days,**” she said, “**and you have not got anything, and that is the only thing I have ever seen you do that was wrong.**”

**And that is thirty-five words in one turn of one speech, and it is the second thing she said to him on that road, and she said it in the accent of a foreshore, and he answered it in the same accent in a sentence about the tide and not about himself, and about nine people going past heard the two of them and about four of the nine looked at each other and about none of the nine said anything.**

“**There will be water over that wall this afternoon,**” he said, “**and it will be off it again by the eighth hour of the morning.**”

**And he did not hear it. On the eighth of a month a woman of about thirty-four with a barrow said one sentence of his back to him to be sure he had said it, and he heard the two of them about four seconds apart and they were not the same sound, and that was five weeks earlier, and the sound that came out of him on this road was the sound he meant.**

He asked her one question and it was about the man with a chain, and she said the surveyor was coming back on the ninth day with a second chain and a longer rod, and that the man with the sheet came with him about half of the time, and that neither of them had ever been on that sand at the fourth hour of a morning, and then she went.

## Three

**A market morning on a made road four days from a foreshore is a market morning, and about nine hundred people came in off six villages, and one of the things that happens at one is that anybody can put up a stall, and a man of about thirty-two with a limp did not put up a stall and did not say why and was not asked.**

He bought a loaf and a bushel of oats and a broom and a bucket of tar and paid for them, and he put them in a barrow that he hired for the day, and then he loaded nine stones on top of them, because the yard had nine of them stacked against a wall and nobody was going anywhere with them, and he stood in that yard for about an hour with a barrow-load of nine stones and a broom and a bucket of tar on top of them.

And a man of about thirty-four with a sheet was in that yard.

**Nobody spoke to anybody. The man with the sheet was at the far end of a wall with a man of about thirty-eight, and about nine of them had come out of a building about four days off with a load of stone that was going to a foreshore. The man of thirty-two with a limp was in the same yard with nine stones and a broom and a bucket of tar, and none of it was his and all of it was hired.**

They were about ninety feet apart for about an hour and neither of them went in the other's direction and about nine people in that yard noticed the two of them and about four of the nine have said since that they were waiting for one of them to do something and about none of the nine saw anything happen.

## Four

**And what he did with the days between that Saturday and the Saturday after was walk four days to a foreshore four days off with a hired barrow and a barrow-load of stone on it, and be on that sand for two days, and walk four days back, because a wagon could not have gone and a barrow could, and because he had hired a barrow, and because nobody on that foreshore had asked him to and about nine of them had watched him do it and about four of them had understood that he was not being paid and about nine of them had understood that he was not asking to be.**

He walked the four days down with the barrow and was on that sand for two days and he walked the four days back, and the wall is eleven paces long and about two feet high and it had a second footing course on it when he left, and the second course is nine inches lower and it is bedded on sand that is wet twice a day.

**And a woman of about fifty-one was at the seaward end with a piece of driftwood in it and about twenty-two notches cut in the edge, and she said, “**Go and tell him I have not cut it.**”**

**And that is nine words and it is the whole of what she said to him on the last day, and he did not write them down and he is not going to, and about six people on that foreshore heard her say them and none of the six has repeated any of them and one of the six is the man of about thirty-four with a sheet, who has been on that sand four times since the middle of a month in Wolfmonth and has never once been on it at a time when a woman of about fifty-one was at the seaward end.**

## Five

The cost of that Saturday is a column and it is his, and it is the third one in about a month and it is the largest of the three, and he has kept a column on four days in seven since a room in a township of about nine hundred people asked him to leave it.

**four loaves at a penny, two pounds of cheese at four, four pounds of wood at three, four tallow candles at two, three quarts of small beer at two, three bushels of oats at four, a broom at five, a bucket of tar at eight, two rooms at six, a sheet of paper at one and a quill at one.** One, one, one, one, four, four, three, three, three, three, two, two, two, two, two, two, two, four, four, four, five, eight, six, six, one and one is seventy-seven pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and seventy-seven pence is a mark and twenty-nine over**, and ten days of carrying somebody else's stone for nothing is not in it and is not a figure of men, and a wall with a second footing course on it is not in it and is nine inches of stone and a day of four men, and a piece of driftwood with twenty-two notches in it is not in it and is not a record of anything anybody has asked him for.

**And a man of thirty-two with a limp walked four days from a port to a made road on a market morning and stood on a verge for about nine minutes and got a kitchen wrong twice, and a woman of about thirty-four with a barrow said thirty-five words to him in an accent that is about four hundred miles from any town he has worked in and he answered in the same accent in a sentence about the tide, and about nine carts went past and about nine hundred people came in off six villages, and two men were about ninety feet apart in a yard for about an hour and neither of them went in the other's direction, and about ninety feet is not a figure of men and is not in a column and is not in any book in nine counties, and the twenty-second notch in a piece of driftwood is still not cut and a woman of about fifty-one says the water has not been off that mark in twenty-two years and the wall has a second course on it and the water goes over both of them twice a day, and there is nobody in a room anywhere on that made road.**
