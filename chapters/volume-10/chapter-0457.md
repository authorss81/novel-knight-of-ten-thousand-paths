# Chapter 0457

**The thirteenth ebb and the second hour after the water went out, on a foreshore two miles and about nine hundred paces long, where there is no month and no day of the week and the water has been off and on about sixteen thousand times in the life of a woman of about fifty-one, and eleven houses stand along it, and about nine people are standing at the landward end by a pan and a fire, and the fourth of the four houses is behind them, and a man of thirty-two with a limp is in it and is not one of the nine and is not keeping anything.**

## One

**The nine did not gather. They came out to the pan over about an hour, in ones and twos, because it is about two hours past the ebb and there are nine hundred people on two miles of sand and about nine hundred people have work.**

They are not a council. There is no chair. **There is a woman of sixty-three with a stick and a bowl of brine. A woman of about fifty-one with a piece of driftwood and twenty-two notches cut in the edge of it. A woman of about thirty-four with a barrow. A man of about forty-one with a barrow. A man with nine hundred acres inland and a lease on a piece of shingle. A man who owns the fourth of the eleven houses and is not a good man and who came anyway. And three men of between about thirty and about fifty who are the families that boil salt.** That is nine and it is not a council and there is no chair.

**And the man of about twenty-nine came with them, and he was not asked and he is not one of them, and he stood about nine paces off for an hour and forty minutes and did not say one word, and about four of the nine noticed him and about none of the nine counted him.**

The subject was the second side of a letter.

## Two

The woman of thirty-four put it on the wall by the pan, folded once, and did not read it out and said nothing about it, and then the man with nine hundred acres read it out himself and read it badly, and then nobody spoke for about four minutes.

“**It is not a road,**” the man with nine hundred acres said. “**It is three roads and a middle, and there is a room two hundred miles back that will give the middle a name, and a man has been on this sand twice with a chain and he is coming back with more chain, and the three of you want him to sign something.**”

Nobody said the man of about twenty-nine was in the room. He was nine paces off and they all knew.

The woman of sixty-three did the thing she has not done for forty-one years, which is to be asked nothing and to say a thing about herself.

“**I have been at this fire forty-one years,**” she said, “**and I have never once wanted that road to be a different road. And in a year from now, or in nine, there will be a man on that sand with a piece of paper with my trade on it and a figure of men on it, and I will be asked to be answerable for the road past my own door, and I will be about seventy, and there will be a person who can produce me.**”

**And nobody argued with her and nobody agreed with her and about six of the nine said afterwards, in ones, that she had said the true thing, which is what people on a foreshore do at the second hour after the water goes out and it is not agreement and it is not a vote.**

## Three

**And then the nine refused it, and it took about nine minutes, and the nine reasons are not the same reason and none of the nine is wrong, and a man of thirty-two with a limp was asked once and answered and was not asked twice.**

The man with nine hundred acres refused because a middle with a name on it is a middle that can be given a second name, and he has a lease on a piece of shingle that is worth about nine times what it was worth before a wall went in a hundred paces from the landward end.

The woman of about thirty-four refused because she has a market on that sand and the market is four houses and she has never in nine years put the other seven into it, and putting the seven in is the first step and the first step is the whole of the danger, **and about nine people on that foreshore had watched her do it once and about four of them had understood it the first time and one of those four is the man of about twenty-nine and he has not understood it yet and nobody has explained it to him.**

The woman of about fifty-one refused, and her reason is the one she has been carrying about two hundred paces for twenty-two years.

“**If the four ask the seven for one thing then the seven are eight,**” she said, “**and the four are still four, and after that there is a count of eleven on a piece of paper and the eleven has a head on it, and the head says men.**”

And then the man of about forty-one with a barrow, who is not the man with the nine hundred acres and is not the woman of about thirty-four, refused in nine words in one turn of one speech.

“**I would rather be sent for than be found,**” he said.

**And that is the last of the reasons and it is the one on that sand that has nothing to do with the tide, and he is the only married man among the nine and the only one of the nine who did not say a word after it.**

**And a man of thirty-two with a limp was asked by the woman of about thirty-four whether he would hold the middle, and he said no.**

“**I will not stand in the middle of three places,**” he said. “**I said so to a woman with a pen about a month ago and I am not going to say it twice for money.**”

**And that is thirty-four words in one turn of one speech, and it is the fourth time in about seven months that somebody has asked him to hold a thing, and of the four he said yes to one and no to this one, and the other two were offers he made and did not get, and the four were a word in a song, a form with three heads, a door in a salt-store, and this.**

## Four

**And a woman of about fifty-eight who sweeps a yard behind a house of assizes in a township of about nine hundred people, nine days off the landward end, has not been on that foreshore in about nine years and sent nine words, and they came nine days back on a handcart, and about nine people on that foreshore know that those words exist and about four of them know the whole of them and nobody is going to carry an answer to them.**

He read the nine words out at about the third hour and there was a fire going and about nine people were close enough to hear.

“**Tell him the yard will outlive all of us,**” he read.

**And that is nine words and it is what she sent, and about six people on that foreshore had said the same thing in about nine years without ever having said it to a man with a chain, and none of the nine at the pan had said it out loud and one of them has been the last one for about nine years.**

A woman of about thirty-four took the nine words and folded them and put them in the front of her shirt, and she was asked by the man of about twenty-nine who had the letter whether she was going to send an answer, and she said no, and she said it in a way that was not unkind, and he did not ask again.

**And the wall of stone at a hundred paces from the landward end is eleven paces long and about two feet high and took four days, and a man of thirty-four with a sheet and a surveyor of about forty-four have both been on that sand since the middle of a month in Wolfmonth and neither of them knows that nine people on a foreshore said no to a piece of paper, because nobody wrote it down and nobody wrote anything down and there was no month to write it against.**

## Five

The cost of that ebb is a column and it is the man of about forty-one with the barrow's and it is the shortest one any of the nine has kept and there are about nine of the nine who kept one that day and nobody compared them.

**a loaf at one penny, a second loaf at one, a third loaf at one, a pound of cheese at four, a second pound of cheese at four, a pound of wood at three, a second pound of wood at three, a third pound of wood at three, a tallow candle at two, a second tallow candle at two, a third tallow candle at two, a fourth tallow candle at two, a fifth tallow candle at two, a sixth tallow candle at two, a bushel of oats at four, a second bushel of oats at four, a broom at five, a bucket of tar at eight, a handcart hired for the day at four.** One, one, one, four, four, three, three, three, two, two, two, two, two, two, four, four, five, eight and four is fifty-seven pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and fifty-seven pence is a mark and nine over**, and nine people refusing a piece of paper on a foreshore is not in it and is not a figure of men and is not in any column in any county, and about nine hundred people went home along about two miles of hard sand to eleven houses and the water came in over about two hundred paces of the seaward half at about the ninth hour.

**And a man of thirty-two with a limp was asked for the fourth time in about seven months to hold the middle of a thing and said no in thirty-four words, and a woman of about fifty-one refused a piece of paper for the reason she has been carrying about two hundred paces for twenty-two years, and a woman of sixty-three with a pan said a thing about herself out loud for the first time in forty-one years, and nine words from a yard nine days off came back the same way they went, on a handcart, unanswered, by a woman of about thirty-four who was not asked to answer them and did not, and there is no month on that foreshore and there is no day of the week on it, and a piece of driftwood in a bundle at the seaward end has twenty-two notches cut in the edge of it and the twenty-second is not cut yet and it goes in when the water is off a mark that has not been off in twenty-two years.**
