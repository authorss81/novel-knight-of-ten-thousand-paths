# Chapter 0478

**The eighth day of Mudmonth, YR 319, a Sunday and not a market day, and the second hour and the sixth hour and the ninth hour, in a room over a weigh-house two hundred miles off a coast where a river runs into the sea, with a door on a lane, where about nine hundred sheets a month go out of that room into nine counties and about a hundred and four go out in one band on a fixed day, and a woman of twenty-nine has been four years and a half in that room for fourpence a year out of a deed of thirteen words, and a man of about twenty-three has brought a band back and put it on the bench and gone down four steps.**

## One

**The round does not go out on a Sunday and came back on the second day before, and one sheet out of about a hundred and four has a number on it and no head on it, and the number is about eleven hundred.**

She read it standing up, in nine seconds, the way she reads everything in that room, and she did not sit down and she did not go to the press, which has been standing open since a day in a year nobody in that room will name and which nobody shuts.

**About eleven hundred people are on no roll and no sheet and no list, and have been for longer than she has been in that room, and there is a figure of them in a county two hundred miles off that came back on a band on a Saturday, and there is no place in that room where a figure like that can go.**

## Two

**A register takes a question. A day's book takes a thing nobody asked. Neither of them takes a number without a place attached to it, and about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month come into that room and about nine hundred sheets go out of it, and those three are not the same number and none of them is derived from another.**

She said that out loud to a man of about twenty-three who had not yet gone down four steps, and she said it the way she says things in that room, which is twice and then once more if the first two were not listened to.

“**A figure is not a question,**” she said. “**A question is a thing somebody has to answer. That is the whole of what it is for, and that is why it has a head on it, and if I turn that number into a question then I have made one I have to answer, and I am not going to be the person who made it.**”

“**Then I will take it back down,**” the man of about twenty-three said.

“**You will take it back down,**” she said, “**and it will go out on the fixed day in the band with the hundred and three others, and in a year it will come back with a different number against it, and the different number will be a figure too, and nobody will ever be able to say where the first one came from.**”

**And that is a hundred and thirty-two words in three turns of one exchange, of which the first is sixty-four printed in two spans of six and fifty-eight, the second is seven, the third is printed in two spans of six and fifty-five, and the hundred and thirty-two is the whole of the three of them together, and the man of about twenty-three counted the hundred and thirty-two on his fingers on the steps and has said since then that he counted them because she made him wait and not because he cared, and the two of them have not agreed on which.**

## Three

**What she did with the sheet was nothing, and the nothing took four seconds, and it is the fourth time in four years and a half that she has done nothing to a sheet, and the first three were not the same nothing and this one is the fourth.**

She put it back into the band in the order it came in, which was the fourth from the cord, and she tied the cord, and she wrote the day on the outside of the band in a hand that a clerk in nine counties can read.

**And the register stands at seven questions and no findings, and the day's book stands at nine entries of a thing nobody asked, and she did not open either, and a tenth is not a gift and an eighth is not a correction.**

There is a drawer about eleven inches deep under the bench and it holds about four hundred returns and one of them holds a question of nine words that came off a road two days off a coast and that nobody in nine counties is going to answer, and she did not open the drawer, and the drawer is not opened on that Sunday and has not been opened in about two months.

## Four

**And then the man of about twenty-three asked her the question a man asks a woman alone in a room on a Sunday, which is whether anybody is going to do anything about the eleven hundred.**

She said it to the door and not to him, and she said it in five words, and she had already said the same five words twice that morning, and she has not been thanked for either saying and is not going to be.

“**A figure, not a question,**” she said.

“**You have read it,**” he said, “**and you have written the day on the band, and in about nineteen days it will be back in that county on a cart with a different number on it.**”

“**Yes,**” she said. “**And that is the twentieth time that has happened and I have been here for four of them. You may go down the steps.**”

**And that is sixty-four words in three turns of one exchange, of which the first is five, the second is thirty-four printed in two spans of four and thirty, and the third is twenty-five printed in two spans of one and twenty-four, and it is a Sunday, and a market runs on two days in seven in the town under that weigh-house and this is not one of them, and about nine hundred people were paid twice in seven days in the floor below and about four hundred and nine parish rolls came in that month and about nine hundred sheets went out, and none of the three is a figure of about eleven hundred, and the press stood open, and nobody shut it.**

## Five

The cost of that Sunday is a column and it is the woman of twenty-nine's, and it is the fourth of them, and about eleven hundred people are not in it and cannot go in it and are not a figure of men, and a deed of thirteen words is not in it and fourpence a year is not in it.

**a quill and ink at three pence, a second quill and ink at three, a sheet of paper at one, a second sheet of paper at one, a third sheet of paper at one, a fourth sheet of paper at one, a fifth sheet of paper at one, a pound of candles at four, a second pound of candles at four, a pound of wood at three, a second pound of wood at three, a bucket of tar at eight, a broom at five, a second broom at five, a pound of rope at six, a waxed cord at four, a pair of gloves at two, a second pair of gloves at two, a pound of nails at three, a second pound of nails at three, a loaf at one, a second loaf at one, a third loaf at one, a pound of cheese at four, a second pound of cheese at four, a third pound of cheese at four, a tin of oil at two, a second tin of oil at two, a pound of salt at four, a second pound of salt at four, a third pound of salt at four, a fourth pound of salt at four, a room at six, a bed at four, a second night's bed at four, a third night's bed at four, an inkwell at three, and a second inkwell at three.** Three, three, one, one, one, one, one, four, four, three, three, eight, five, five, six, four, two, two, three, three, one, one, one, four, four, four, two, two, four, four, four, four, six, four, four, four, three and three is one hundred and twenty-two pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and twenty-two pence is two marks and twenty-six over**, and about nine hundred sheets a month and about a hundred and four in one band and about four hundred and nine parish rolls are not in it and are not added to one another, and about eleven hundred people are not in it, and seven questions with no findings and nine not-askings are not in it and neither nine is added to the other.

**And a woman of twenty-nine read a figure of about eleven hundred people in nine seconds on a Sunday and put the sheet back into a band at the fourth place from the cord and tied the cord and wrote the day on the outside of it, and a man of about twenty-three went down four steps, and the register stands at seven questions and no findings and the day's book stands at nine, and the press stood open, and nobody shut it, and nobody thanked her, and nobody thanked her twice.**
