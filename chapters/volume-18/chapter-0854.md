# Chapter 0854

**Thawmonth the tenth, YR 325, a Thursday and a market morning with about four hundred people in the yard at the foot of that stair, the short interval seven hours and falling, and a room hired at the high end of the street in that town at about the ninth hour in the evening with about nine people in it and the door shut and the shutters shut and nine candles on a table that had been carried in for the occasion and would be carried out again.**

The room over the saddler's was the only one in that town with nine chairs in it, and the chairs went up the outside stair at about the sixth hour in front of about nine people who watched a man carry nine chairs up a stair one at a time.

The sheet had come into that town on the Thursday morning of the seventh, in a leather wallet, with the offer written on three leaves of good paper by a hand that had been taught a trade and had not been born to it. It had been read once already in a room at the low end of that street on the Friday before, out loud, by the clerk of the warrant, to about nine people, and about four of them heard all of it and about five heard the end of it.

He read it again that evening because about nine people were in the room and because a thing that has been read twice is not the same as a thing that has been read once. He read it standing, with the three leaves flat on the table on a folded coat so that the wax did not stick, and he read at the speed of a man who is not sure of a word and is not going to hurry to it.

There was a fire in the grate and nine candles on the table, and the nine people in that room had come from nine different floors, and the two men off that floor came up the stair together, sat down together, and did not speak to each other once in an hour and a half, which the woman from the coast noticed and mentioned afterwards to four people.

There were three terms on it and each of them was written as a fact about somebody else.

The first term was that his rank was restored, in writing, with a date under it, and the date was the first of Longlight, being nineteen days off, and the writing said the office was restored and did not say the office was reformed, and did not say which, and four of the nine people in that room read that difference and five did not.

The second term was that the road at Bellweather-Living would be kept open and that the people of it would be entered as an exhibit, and the word exhibit was on the sheet in the middle of a sentence about a road and not about a people, and nothing on the three leaves defined it.

The third term was that the main world would keep one history, and the sheet said that a place which keeps one history is a place whose records can be read, and it said that in four different ways in three leaves, and about four people in that room counted the four ways and about five did not.

At the foot of the third leaf, under the three terms, in the same hand and in a smaller one, there was a line that said the agreement of this county was not required.

He had asked about that county once in four and a half years and had been told the answer on the first day he ever came here, and the county he asked about was nine counties away and was not this one, and the fact that the sentence was on the sheet anyway told him that whoever wrote it knew the difference between the two and had used it.

The clerk of the warrant is about thirty-four and has been a clerk for about nine years and a warrant for about four, and he wrote those three leaves himself, and he said so before anybody asked him, which was not the thing anybody in that room had expected.

The delegation's woman had come up from the coast road on the Thursday morning and had been in the room for an hour and a half before he began. She is the woman who came with the delegation and has not been named in this room and did not give her name in it, and she sat on the wall end with her hands on her knees for the whole evening, and about nine people in that room spoke and she spoke twice.

Refusing the terms was the easy half of that evening and it took him about four minutes.

He did not say that he would not do them because a man who refuses a thing because he dislikes it has not argued about the thing. He said that a rank that is restored without the office being reformed is an office with a name on it and the same hands in it, and that about nine people in that room knew what that was because they had all come up about nine hundred yards of stair to see it.

He said that a community entered as an exhibit is a community entered as a thing that is shown, and that a thing that is shown is a thing that somebody else decides to put away.

He said that one history is the best argument anybody in nine counties has ever had for anything, and that it is also the only argument that cannot be checked by anybody who was not in the room when it was made, and that a record which cannot be checked is not a record.

Then he stopped, and about four of the nine people in that room thought he had finished and about five of them were wrong.

The difficult half was the clerk of the warrant, who had not written those three terms because he wanted them and had written them because he had been given them, and who was about thirty-four and had a wife in a town four days off and about nine children and had never in his life held anything that anybody was asking him about.

He did not say that the terms were good. He said that the third term was the one he had been told twice to write in his own hand, and that he had asked once why, and that the answer he had been given was a date.

Then he asked Aren Kest for it in words.

That was the thing that was arranged before anybody came into that room. The sheet was in writing and the refusal could go back on a sheet, and about nine people in that room knew that a refusal in a letter can be filed and a refusal in a room cannot, and the clerk of the warrant had been told to have the answer spoken.

“**I am not asking you to write to me, and I am asking you to say it in front of the woman from the coast and the two men from your floor and me, and then I will write down what you said and it will go on a sheet, and if you would rather I did not write it down then say that instead and I will put that down instead.**”

That was seventy-one words. About four people in that room heard all of them and about five heard the end of them.

The woman from the coast spoke for the first time about the third term and she spoke about the word exhibit and about nothing else.

“**My community has been an exhibit for about nine years, being a place that is looked at from outside and counted, and the counting is not the part that is hard. The hard part is that a thing that is counted is a thing that has been given a number by somebody else, and about four people in that room know what that does to a number, and about five of you are going to sign the paper that does it.**”

That was sixty-eight words. She said it sitting where she had been sitting and she did not raise her voice and she did not say the name of her community once, and four people in that room heard every word of it and five heard the first sentence.

Then he said the terms were refused, in the room, in words, and every one of the nine heard it and about nine of the nine understood that what had just happened was that a piece of paper had been answered by a person and not by another piece of paper.

What he did not do was give an answer.

He was asked three times in about nine minutes whether his refusal was final and whether it stood to the day and whether he would put his name to it, and he said that it was a refusal of the three terms and that it stood, and that whether he would put his name to anything was a different question and that he was not answering it that evening.

That is not a tactic and it is not a way of keeping a position open. It is what a man says when he has refused the thing in front of him and has not yet found out what the answer costs anybody else, and about four people in that room understood that and about five thought he was being careful.

The consequence of it was on the page inside a fortnight and it was work.

Two keepers had been on their own crossings since the sixth of Thawmonth, out of contact with anything, because the interval on that place has been shortening all month and the crews that go up go up when there is an interval to go up in. One crew went on the ninth and found the nearer of the two, and the second crew went on the eleventh and found the other one, and the reason the second crew went at all was that there was still no answer in either direction to bring back down the stair.

A crew that goes up does not need a keeper to agree with it. A crew that goes up needs to know whether the two ends of the thing it is opening are the two ends anybody intends to have opened, and on the ninth there was nothing to carry and on the eleventh there was still nothing to carry, and two men about ninety miles apart were found alive on two crossings that nobody had reached in about nine days.

That is a fact about how the work ran in Thawmonth and not a thing anybody in that room planned, and nobody in that room was thanked for it and about nine people in nine counties have since said that a refusal without a final answer bought about two crossings, which is the whole of what a refusal is for.

About four people in that room had worked out that the second crew was going because of the answer that was not there and about five had worked out that the second crew was going because a crew goes, and both of those were true and about nine people in that town argued about it for about nine days afterward in about four trades.

The word refused went onto no piece of paper in that room for about five hours, and the clerk of the warrant put it down at about a quarter to eleven, and he wrote it on the second leaf and not on the first, and he wrote the three terms on the first leaf in his own hand so that the two could be read together about nine months later by somebody who was not in the room.

That was not arranged and he said so, twice, once to the woman from the coast and once to nobody in particular. About nine people in that town have since said that a man who writes out the thing he was refused without being asked to has done a kind of work, and about four have said that it is a kind of work that gets him noticed, and about five have said that a clerk of the warrant does not have a choice about being noticed.

The sheets went out of that room at about the eleventh hour in the hand of the clerk of the warrant. There were two of them and he carried both himself down the outside stair and across to the low end of that street, and one of them carried the three terms and the one that answered them and no more.

The refusal was not published.

That is said here and not by anybody in that room, and it is said because about nine people in nine counties have heard that the terms were refused and about nine hundred have heard nothing, and about four of the nine in that town who saw nine chairs go up an outside stair have since said that nine chairs going up means something and about five have said that nine chairs going up means a room.

The woman from the coast spoke for the second time after the sheets were in the wallet, and she spoke about a date and about nothing else.

“**The nineteenth of Mudmonth is the eleventh year and the eleventh year is not a date anybody in this room will be asked about, and I have not asked, and there is a woman about four days off who will be asked about it before the end of the year and she is not in this room and she is not going to hear it from me.**”

That was fifty-nine words, and four of the nine people in that room did not know who the woman four days off was and five of them did, and none of the nine asked, and she did not say the name.

The room was put back the way it was by four people at about the twelfth hour. The nine candles went out, the three leaves went into the leather wallet, and the wallet went into the inside pocket of the man who wrote the three terms on it. The table went down the outside stair again on the back of one of the nine chairs, and about four people watched that as well, and about five of them had gone home by then.

**And the cost of that Thursday is a rank restored in writing and not reformed, which is an office with a name on it and the same hands in it, and a people entered in the middle of a sentence about a road as a thing that is shown, and one history offered four different ways in three leaves with nothing on the sheet to check any of the four. An hour and a half of sitting in a room over a saddler's with nine chairs in it. About two crossings found in a fortnight because there was no answer to carry up nine hundred yards of stair. The engine damaged two hundred and seventy-six days and nobody mending it. Two hundred and sixty days of one man in a lower archive. A drawer eleven inches deep in a county he is not in not opened once in four hundred and thirty-four days. One ear, one leg and a year, none of it written on either of the two sheets that left that room.**
