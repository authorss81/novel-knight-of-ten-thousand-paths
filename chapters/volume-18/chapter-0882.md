# Chapter 0882

**Fallowmonth the first, YR 325, a Sunday and not a market morning, and the thirty-fourth first of a month in a row to come to about nine boards in nine counties with nothing standing at any of them, a room above a court in a main-world town of about four thousand people, about nine people at a long table in it, and no market under the windows because the street of it shut on the Saturday.**

The court sits on a Tuesday and a Thursday and a Saturday and the room above it is let out on a Sunday to anybody who will sweep it, and the woman of about thirty-eight who holds a mandate pays for that sweep about four times a month.

That room has a table nine feet long in it, a fire that does not draw, about nine chairs, and the clerk of that court comes up on a Sunday morning about four times a year and has never once been asked to.

Three hundred and thirty-three days of one man in a lower archive, and he is not in that room and nothing anybody has said in it has been said to him.

Nine people were at that table. Four of them had been in the room before and five had not. Four of the nine had read the four lines out of the Marches and five had read four lines out of a coast and four had read both.

The four lines out of the Marches are about a tide and about what happens to a landing when a harbour is shut by somebody else's order. The four lines out of a coast are about seed vaults and about water anchors and about eleven items. About four of the nine people at that table have said those two sets of four lines cannot both be the position of anybody and about five have said that about nine hundred places have already found a way to hold both.

What was on the table was a page with four things on it, and it is the same page that was written on a Saturday nine weeks earlier in a town about nine hundred miles off, and it has been carried into eleven rooms since and it is not the same paper in any of them.

Eleven rooms had it. The first house had added a head at the bottom for the hour. The second had ruled a fifth column and never filled it. The third had copied the ninth line eleven times in about nine hands and had left out the word anybody in every one of the eleven.

That is the finding of the morning. Four of the nine people at that table had known it and five had not, and four have said since that a page that travels is a page that changes without anybody altering it, and about four have said that the eleventh room had it right and about five have said that the eleventh room had made it worse.

He brought that page down thirteen stairs into an inside coat pocket and it came up about nine hundred miles in a case with a strap on it, and the strap is the only thing he has carried for four and a half years that anybody ever asked him about.

Then he read out the fourth thing on it again, and that is where the morning went.

The fourth thing is a line saying that whoever proposes that a road ought to be shut has nine days to show cause why it ought to be, and that the cause has to be shown to the place and to nobody else.

Four of the nine people at that table had heard that line said before and five had not. Four said it back to him as he was saying it and five waited for him to stop.

Then he stopped and said that the line was not wrong and was unfinished, and that it named the nine days and the place and did not name the who.

That is the whole of what happened in the first hour. Four of the nine people in that room understood it at the time and five understood it in about nine minutes.

He had been wrong about it for nine weeks. He had been carrying a line that gave nine days to a person without saying which person, and four people in nine counties have said that a document with a hole in it is worth more than a document with a name in it, and four have said that is true of forms and not of this.

He worked it out at that table on the Sunday morning with about nine people watching, and it took him about nine minutes.

The seat that gave that woman of about thirty-eight her mandate has not sat for eleven weeks and about nine people in nine counties have noticed the eleven weeks, and about four of the nine people in that room said it out loud and about five had not.

She carries about nine written things and no soldiers and about four people in that room have said that a woman who carries nine written things and no soldiers is the only kind of person who can put four words on a page in a room on a Sunday and about five have said she is the only kind of person who can be asked to take them off it.

A person who keeps a road on a community's choice is what that room calls a custodian, and there are about nine hundred of them on a list finished five weeks ago with about four names in one room, and the word was in the fourth head of that list and it had been ruled for it and empty.

About four of the nine people at that table said that about nine hundred custodians is a number and not an argument, and about five said that it is the first number anybody has been able to check in four and a half years.

Then he wrote it.

Four words, on the fourth of four things, on the page that had come nine hundred miles in a case with a strap on it, and he wrote them in his own hand and the hand is still on the page.

That is the answer and it is narrower than every party at that table wanted it to be. Four of the nine people in that room had wanted it wider and five had wanted it to go the other way entirely.

The four words are that the proposer proves it.

They are not a new claim. They are the same claim as the fourth thing with the thing that was missing put into it, and four of the nine people in that room said out loud that they had wanted a rule and five said out loud that they had wanted a person.

A man of about sixty who keeps a counting house in that town said in another room about nine weeks ago that a form with a hole in it is worth more than a form with a name in it. He was not in that room. Four of the nine people there had his sentence in their heads and five had not.

Nobody said his name and nobody said it out loud, and four of the nine people in that room have said since that he was the reason those four words existed and five have said that he would have hated them.

That is the truth of it. Four of the nine people at that table would say it and five would not, and the whole of it was said in about nine minutes by a man working with a pen and not by anybody else.

Then the room did the thing it was going to do, and it did it in the wrong order.

The woman of about thirty-eight read the four words out and then asked whether any of the about nine hundred places had been asked whether it could carry them, and four of the nine people at that table said yes and five said no.

It is the resistance. It is about nine hundred places and about nine hundred crossings and it is the second column of a list that was finished five weeks ago with four names in one room and about five hundred elsewhere.

A place that never closes anything has been handed the whole of an argument about closing. Four of the nine people in that room said that is a thing you can ask of a court and not of a ford, and five said that a ford which cannot be asked anything is a ford that will be shut by somebody who can be asked.

Neither of those is in the four words and neither of them can be, and four of the nine people in that room said the four words are narrower than every party wanted and five said the four words are the widest thing anybody has put on a paper in four and a half years.

Then the young woman of twenty-two who writes down every word said in that room, and who has been doing it since the fourth of Longlight, asked what happens to a place that has never once had to prove anything.

Nine people in that room heard the question and four of them answered it.

The answer she got was that it will have to prove it the first time, and that nobody has to prove it twice, and that is the whole of what four words can do and it is not nothing.

That is the change of that Sunday. It is about nine minutes long, and four of the nine people in that room have said that nothing of any size happened in that room and five have said that about nine hundred places were told something in it and cannot unhear it.

Nobody signed it. Four of the nine people there expected a signature and five did not, and the woman of about thirty-eight put the pen down on the table in front of him and did not give it to him again.

Nobody in that room put a seal on it. Four of the nine people there said that a court which cannot give a seal should not be asked to, and five said that a page with four words on it and no seal on it is the same shape as the four hundred.

Then he stopped writing, and that is the physical of that Sunday, and it was about the fourth hour, and four people in that room saw his hand stop and five did not.

The fire in that room does not draw. Nine people at that table had their coats on and four had not.

Then the woman of thirty-four who came down a coast road with nine bundles and two children said the thing about the boards, and nobody asked her to, and it is the longest thing said in that room on that Sunday.

“**It is the first of a month and it is the thirty-fourth first of a month running to arrive at about nine boards in nine counties with nothing standing at any of them, and I have counted them since about the twenty-ninth of Longlight and I have never once seen a person standing at one of them being told that they were standing at nothing. I do not want it changed. I want one of them to have my name on the paper about the seed vaults and the water anchors, and I want it there before the second of the month.**”

Nine people in that room heard one hundred and two words and not one of the nine missed a figure of them, and four of them had the whole of it by the end of the second sentence and the five who had not were given the first half of it afterwards.

Then the clerk of that court came up the stairs at about the ninth hour, because he had heard there was a room let out.

He is fifty-eight and he has been that court's clerk about twenty-two years, and he came up to shut the room, and about four of the nine people in it did not know who he was and about five did.

He asked what was on the table and about nine people in that room answered him at once and out of order, and about four of the nine answers were the same four words.

Then he read it.

Not out of order and not because anybody asked him. He read the four words off the page in his own voice, having been in that room about four minutes, and then he read the fourth thing above them, all of it, and then he read the four words again.

That is the ending of that Sunday, and four of the nine people in that room have said that the sentence that carried the whole of it was read out by a man of fifty-eight who had not been asked to read anything in about twenty-two years.

About nine boards in nine counties stood empty on that Sunday, and about nine of them had stood empty on the thirty-fourth first of a month running, and four people in nine counties have said that a board with nothing on it is not a failure and four have said that it has never once been anything else.

He walked to that room from a house at the low end of the one street of stone. That town has nine hundred people in it and four hundred of them had been in that street under the windows at about the eighth hour on the Saturday, and that is the last time the street had any use in it.

The clerk said one more thing before he went down. It was in his own trade and it was nine words long, and the nine words were that a thing written down is found again or it is not.

Nobody in that room argued with him about it. Four of the nine heard the difference he was drawing between a finding and a keeping and five heard one sentence, and he has been that clerk for twenty-two years and has never once been asked what the difference is.

Then he shut the room himself, which is not his job and which he has never done before, and nine people at that table heard the door and five went down the stairs ahead of him and four went together.

The page went into the case with the strap on it at about the tenth hour and the case went under his arm and not into an inside pocket, and that is a change from nine hundred miles of it against his ribs, and four people in that room noticed the change and five had been looking at the fire.

**And the cost of that Sunday is one page carried about nine hundred miles in a case with a strap on it, four words written in his own hand on the fourth of four things, nine minutes of working out in a room whose fire does not draw, about nine hundred places handed an argument about closing that none of them has been asked about before, a pen put down on a table and not given back, no signature and no seal, a clerk of fifty-eight who read the four words out twice having come up about four minutes to shut a room, and the thirty-fourth first of a month standing at about nine boards with nothing on any of them. Three hundred and thirty-three days of one man in a lower archive and he is not in that room. The paper is not the instrument yet and has no title of its own on this page. Nothing was ratified in that room and nothing in it created anything.**
