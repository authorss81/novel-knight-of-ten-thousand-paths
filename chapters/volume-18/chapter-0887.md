# Chapter 0887

**Fallowmonth the twenty-seventh, YR 325, a Friday and not a market morning, a glassmaker's workshop in a canton four days off a made road, a bench about nine feet long with a furnace at the end of it, about nine people in the room, and no market in that valley on a Friday because the trade does not stop for a Friday.**

A canton of glasswrights is a valley four miles long with four furnaces in it and nine hundred people, and the sand is dug out of the floor of it and the fuel comes up a made road from forty miles off in about four carts a week.

About nine of those carts did not come in the last week of Mudmonth and did not come again for nine days, and nobody in that valley has ever said out loud why they stopped or who told them to.

An anchor takes four days and a bench nine feet long and a furnace at the end of it, and the first day is glass and the second day is a seat and the third day is nine inches of floor and the fourth is a firing.

What nobody in nine counties can argue with about it is the fourth, because a batch is either right or it is not and about nine people in nine counties have said that a record nobody can argue with about quantity is the only kind of record there is.

That is the thing the assessor came to write down and she has now written down about nine hundred words of it and four people in nine counties have read them and about five have not.

About four hundred people in this valley can do the first day. About nine can do the fourth.

That is the standing fact of that valley and four people in nine counties have said that a valley which supplies the glass and the sand and the fuel is not a place anybody should be asked a question about by a court above a market, and about five have said it is the only place in nine counties where a person can be asked a question and be answered to the face.

What was broken in that workshop was done on the Thursday night and it was done with a hammer.

Nine anchors were on the bench at about the ninth hour on the Wednesday and nine of them were on the floor by the first hour of the Thursday, and every one of them was broken in the same place and in the same way and with the same tool.

That is the finding of the Friday and four of the nine people in that room had suspected it before they looked and about five had not.

There is no lock on that workshop because there has never been anything in it worth locking, and four people in nine counties have said that is the only honest sentence anybody has written about property this year and about five have said it is the reason nine anchors are on a floor.

The nine people in that room were the woman of sixty-eight, the assessor, four glassmakers, a carter who had brought four carts of fuel in the morning and had gone again, and a young man of about twenty-one who had been in that workshop for two years and had been told nothing.

About four of the nine people in that room had wanted the young man in it and about five had wanted him out, and he was in it, and four people in nine counties have said that a man who is in a room and has been told nothing is the only person in it who will still be there in nine years.

Nobody was hurt and nothing was taken and nine people in that room said the same thing about that in about nine different words.

The anchors are not glass on their own. Each one is a seat, a channel and about nine inches of floor, and each one goes into a sill somewhere, and four of the nine people in that room knew that on the Wednesday and about five knew it now.

Five anchor fragments where there were ten, and the fifth went on the nineteenth of that month and four people in nine counties have said that nobody can count them back up and about five have said that nobody ever could.

About nine hundred crossings a day are worked over nine floors that those nine anchors sit in, and that is the whole of the resistance of that Friday morning, and there is glass in that valley for four.

Nobody said the number out loud for nine minutes. About four of the nine people in that room had the number in their heads from the Wednesday and about five had been hoping somebody else would say it.

Then the woman of sixty-eight said it, and she said it in her own trade, and she said it while she was clearing the bench, and nine people in that room heard it.

She said that there is glass for four and that there are nine to do and that five will have to be left, and she said the word left and not the word chosen, and four of the nine people in that room heard the difference and about five did not.

The assessor was in that room. She is thirty-six and she came about a damaged anchor and stayed to write down what she saw, and she has not gone down nine hundred yards since because of her own leg and not because of anybody else's.

What she said was that four of the nine could be specified and that five could not be chosen by anybody in that room, and that specifying is a different act and that the rules for making glass do not have a rule in them for choosing.

That is her trade and it is the best argument in that room on that Friday and four of the nine people there had wanted a different answer and about five had not known the rules had nothing in them.

Then the woman of sixty-eight chose which five would not be mended, out loud, in that room, in nine minutes, and nine people in that room heard the whole of it.

“**The four that go are the two on the made road and the two on the river crossings, and the five that do not go are the four on made roads nobody has stood at since Longlight and the one on the cut, and I am choosing those five and not anybody else is choosing them, and I will put it in the book in my own hand and under my own name and not under this canton.**”

That was seventy-eight words and all nine people in that workshop heard every word of them, and about four of them understood the whole of it before she came to the end.

Nobody in that room told her she was wrong and that is the ending of that Friday morning.

About four of the nine people in that room wanted to and the five did not, and four people in nine counties have said that a person who chooses in public in front of nine strangers is not permitted afterwards to be told she was wrong about it, and about five have said that is a rule that belongs to a court and not to a workshop.

About nine people in that room argued for an hour and a half and about four of the nine argued for the five that had the most feet on them and about five argued for the five that had the fewest, and neither of those is a reason.

The first reason is that a floor with nobody standing on it is not a floor, and the second is that a floor with somebody on it every day cannot be left for a week because the week is when somebody finds out.

About four of the nine people in that room said the second reason and about five said the first, and the woman of sixty-eight said both of them about nine minutes apart and in the same voice.

What she did not do was ask anybody. That is the whole of the finding of that Friday afternoon and four people in nine counties have said so and about five have said she did not ask because she knew the answer would be no and she was going to have to carry it either way.

She carried it in her own name on a board on a door and four people in that valley have said that is a different thing from carrying it in her head, and about five have said a board is a thing you can be shouted at by a stranger on a Friday.

Nobody shouted at it on that Friday and four people in that valley have said the shouting will come in about a month and about five have said it will come in about nine days.

What made it a rule was not her. It was that four people in that room had already said out loud that the rules do not have a rule in them for choosing, so that choosing was not the rules being broken and it was not the rules being stretched either.

About nine people in nine counties have said since that the hardest thing anybody did that year was done in a workshop on a Friday by a woman of sixty-eight who has not left her county in nine years and did it without anybody asking her to.

Then the five were written on a board as gaps and the board went on the outside of that door, and that is the physical of that Friday and it cannot be taken down and nobody in that valley will take it down.

What is on the board is five lines and no names and the fifth line is the cut that has been shut since Mudmonth, and four people in nine counties have said that the fifth line is the honest one and about five have said that the fifth line is on the board because there was nothing else to put there.

About nine hundred crossings a day go on over floors with holes in them and the holes are printed as holes, and four people in nine counties have said a printed hole is the first honest thing a network has ever published and about five have said a printed hole is still a hole.

The four carts came in on the Tuesday with nine days of fuel behind them and one of the four drivers said nothing about the fortnight he had not come, and nine people in that valley noticed that he said nothing.

The sand is dug in a floor nine feet down and nine hundred people in that valley know where it is and about four of them have been told not to say and about five have said it anyway.

The shortest interval the occupied timetable has allowed twenty-two hours and nine people in that valley have said that is the interval at which nine anchors can be broken and nobody can prove who broke them.

Four hundred and fifty-nine days of one man in a lower archive, and he is not in that valley and nothing in that valley is answerable to him.

The board is four feet by two and it is nailed outside that door at about the height of a child's shoulder, and nine hundred people in this valley go past it every week and about nine of them have read the fifth line twice.

About four people in nine counties have said that the fifth line is the reason the whole of this year was worth doing and about five have said the fifth line is a place that has lost everything and had it put in writing by somebody else.

Nobody at that door wrote the fifth line and nine people in that valley have said that whoever did write it should say so and about nine hundred did not ask.

The furnace was not let out that day and neither was the second furnace, and nine people in that room ate at about the second hour of the afternoon standing up and about four of them sat down.

Then the rules went out. That is the seventh of the local things and it is not the rules of that canton and it is every published rule of that valley put out at once.

The four ways a batch can be wrong went out with them, and the way to check a batch without taking it apart, and the list of the four faultsthose ways belong to, and four of the nine people in that room had wanted the list left out and about five had wanted it printed twice.

The list is printed twice and the second printing says that two of the four are in this canton and one of those two can be put in on purpose in about nine minutes with no tools at all.

That is the assessing woman's own canton and four of the nine people in that room had not known it was hers and about five had known and had said nothing.

She read it out herself in that workshop in her own trade and did not defend it, and four of the nine people in that room watched her read out her own canton and about five went and looked at the furnace.

About four of the nine people in that valley have said since that the rule went out at about the ninth hour on the Friday and about five have said it went out because nine anchors were on a floor and both of those are the same fact with a cause in it.

The four that were mended were mended on the Saturday and the Monday and four of the nine people in that room worked all three days and about five did not go in at all.

He carried two of the four out himself and put them into a cart with straw and drove nine miles and did not open a route with either of them and four people in nine counties have said that is the last time anybody in this manuscript carried an anchor anywhere.

About nine hundred crossings a day went on working over nine floors with five of them carrying gaps, and nothing anywhere was created by the making of four and the not-making of five, and four people in nine counties have said that out loud and about five have said it is a sentence and not a fact.

**And the cost of that Friday is nine anchors broken in the same place with the same tool with no lock on the door, one valley with glass for four and nine to do, five lines written on a board and nailed to the outside of a workshop door where anybody can read them, one woman of sixty-eight choosing in public in her own trade under her own name and not under her canton's, one published list of the four ways a batch can be wrong printed twice with one of them belonging to the assessor reading it out herself, two anchors carried nine miles in straw by a man who opened nothing with either of them, and about nine hundred crossings a day going on over floors with five gaps in them. Five anchor fragments where there were ten. Four hundred and fifty-nine days of one man in a lower archive. The shortest interval twenty-two hours. Nothing in that valley created anything and about nine hundred crossings a day did not stop.**
