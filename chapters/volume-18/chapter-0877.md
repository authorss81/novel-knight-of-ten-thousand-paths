# Chapter 0877

**Rainmonth the twenty-sixth, YR 325, a Sunday and not a market morning in a main-world town of about two thousand people, a room above a court that does not sit on a Sunday with a long table and about nine people in it, three of whom walked to get there, and about nine hundred people along nine miles of made road who are not in that town.**

The court sits on a Tuesday and a Thursday and a Saturday and it has not sat on a Sunday since about the twenty-second of Longlight, and the room above it is let out on Sundays to anybody who pays for the sweep, and the woman of about thirty-eight pays for the sweep four times a month.

That town has a market on a Saturday and on a Sunday the street of it is shut by about the fourth hour, and nine hundred people along nine miles of made road know that and about nine hundred more do not.

Three people walked into that room on the Sunday morning. Two of them had walked four days and one of them had walked nine, and all three of them came on the Saturday on foot and slept on the floor of the room above a weigh-house in a town four miles back.

What they carried was nothing. One of them had a copy of a toll roll, one of them had nine letters written by about nine different people in about four hundred houses, and one of them had nothing at all and said so twice in a room full of people.

What each of them wanted is a different thing and about four of the nine people in that room had worked out what it was by the fourth hour and about five had not.

The woman of forty-four wanted the nine days written down with the date on it in a place that people could go and look at.

The man of sixty wanted nothing at all and had come to stop the other two being turned into something they had not said, and about four of the nine people in that room understood that at the time and about five understood it about nine minutes later.

The third one wanted to be in a room where somebody said out loud that a place which cannot show what it was has still been a place.

They are not delegates and they are not on anybody's list. They came because a court above a market sent a printed sheet up a made road in the ordinary post and it said that anybody who could show a place where a road had been shut and had cost somebody their own record was to come and say so in a room.

About nine hundred people along nine miles of made road read that sheet and about four came, and four people in that town have said the sheet found the right three and five have said that three is a low number and four have said that three is a high number when three of them have walked nine days.

The three are from three regions, and the regions are about nine hundred miles apart, and about nine hundred miles of coast, and about nine hundred yards of a corridor.

What each of them put on that table was the same shape of thing.

The sheet that brought them was printed in that same room above a court on a Thursday, four pages, with a seal on it that the court owns, and nine hundred people saw it and four came.

A region is not a province, and a region keeps the record of itself on its own anchor, in its own hand, in its own building, in its own hand, in its own building, and nine hundred people in a region can go and look at what that region was on any given day for four hundred years.

Then a path closed. Not a road and not a bridge. A path, once, for nine days, in an argument about a toll and a cull and a sheet nailed to a frame.

Then the anchor stopped answering and everything written on it after that date became a thing that is written down and cannot be read back.

About four of the nine people in that room understood it the moment the first of the three said it and about four understood it by the third.

That is the evidence and it is good and it is against him, and four people in nine counties have said that a man who says a thing is against his own position is usually doing it to soften it, and four have said that in this case nobody has softened anything.

Aren Kest came into that room on the Sunday morning with the intention of getting rid of it.

That is on the page because it is what he said to the woman of about thirty-eight at the door of that court and she repeated it out loud to four people and five of them did not think it was the right thing to have come for.

He had a thing worked out, on the two days of that made road, and it was nine lines long and it said that a loss of record is not a loss of road and that a region which cannot show what it was has not been invaded and has been interrupted, and that the two are different, and that four hundred years of a region's own account is a thing worth nine days of a road.

About four of the nine people in that room found the nine lines good on the first reading and about four found that they did not answer the thing that had been put on the table, and about four found that they answered it well and were about choosing which of the two findings to say out loud.

The woman of about forty-four who walks four days was the one who broke it.

She said that her region had lost its own anchor at about the seventh hour on a Wednesday and had it back on the ninth day and that for those nine days nobody in four hundred houses could say what had been agreed on the Thursday.

She said that the agreement on the Thursday was about eleven things and that she knows it was eleven and knows the room and the hour and knows that four men were in it, and that she cannot give one of the eleven back to anybody.

About four of the nine people in that room heard the whole of that and about four heard the end of it and had to be told the first part.

About four of the nine people in that room have said since that the man of sixty is the only one of the three who came to lose something, and four have said that a person who comes to lose something is harder to argue with than a person who comes to win something, and about four have said that neither of those is a finding at all and that all three of them are about the man of sixty and not about the three regions.

Then the man of sixty said the other half of it and he said it in his own trade, and four of the nine people in that room understood all of it at once.

He said that his region has nine hundred written things after the date and nine of them can be read back and the other nine hundred cannot, and that a court case in about four years will turn on one of the eight hundred and ninety-one, and that whoever wins it will have won it because his side kept a copy and the other side did not.

About four of the nine people in that room had not thought about the second nine hundred and about four had thought about it before the first of the three spoke.

Nobody in that room asked the third one a question for about nine minutes, and four of the nine people there noticed the nine minutes and five did not, and about four of the nine people in that room have said since that they noticed because they were waiting for a question and about four have said because they were not.

The third of them is from a place that is not a region and is not a town and has not sent anybody to that court in nine years.

About four people in that room have said it is a city of eleven thousand people four days off a coast and about four have said it is not a city and is a set of nine streets.

Four people in nine counties know that it refused a rescue nine years ago and five know that it said no in nine words and went on saying no for four hours while nine hundred people stood outside it, and four know that it has said nothing at all since, and that the silence is nine years old and is not a wound and has never once been described as one.

Nobody in that room asked anybody anything about it. Four of the nine people there had wanted to and four had not and four had noticed that the other four had wanted to.

That is what the man in the lower archive meant on the Thursday, and four of the nine people in that room had worked that out before it was said and four worked it out after.

Then he read the three returns out loud.

There are three of them and they are four pages each and nine hundred words apiece and he read all twelve pages in a room above a court on a Sunday morning in about nine minutes, and nine people in that room heard every word of them, and about four of them had the whole of each return before he came to the end of it and four did not.

What was in the returns is the working out. Where the anchor was on the day before, where it was on the day after, what the region wrote on the day before, what the region wrote on the day after, and how many of those things can be read back.

The number that is in all three of the returns and four people in that room understood before he read it out is that a region can show what it was and cannot show what it has been since, and that this is not going to change while the arrangement stands.

The ninth part of the fourth return is about him and four and a half years, and it is four lines long, and it is the only part of any of the twelve pages that is about anybody who is in that room.

It says that where an anchor and a person disagreed between the second hour and the sixth hour for about four years, the person's answer was taken, and that where there was no person the anchor was taken, and that about nine hundred times in four and a half years those two were not the same and that he has never found the list.

He read the twelve pages out loud and did not read the ninth part of the fourth, and four of the nine people in that room had read it in advance and about four had not.

Then he stopped trying to explain it away.

That is the whole change of that Sunday and four of the nine people in that room have said that it happened about nine minutes before the room expected it to and about four have said it happened when the fourth page of the second return was out of his hands and not before.

What he said was in his own trade and he said it to the nine people in that room and not to the three who had walked, and four of the nine people there heard all of it and four heard the first half.

“**Those three places are right and nine hundred people on that coast road are right, and I have been on that coast road, and I am not going to stand in this room and tell anybody they are not. Separation costs a place its own account of itself and it costs it nine days to find out and it never finds out at all, and I have been looking for a hole in that for four days and there is not one and I am not going to make one.**”

That was ninety words and nine people in that room heard every one of them and not one of them wrote any of it down.

About four people in nine counties have said that is the first time in four and a half years that man has said out loud in a room that a thing is true against him, and about four have said it is not against him at all and that it is against the arrangement and that he is the arrangement.

The woman of thirty-eight put the three returns in a pile and squared the edges of them against the table, which she does four times a day, and then she asked him one question and it was the only question anybody asked that afternoon.

She asked whether the paper he is holding open makes that more likely or less.

Then he answered it in one sentence, and nine people in that room heard the whole of it and four of them have written it down since in their own books and not in any record.

“**Less likely in the next nine years and more likely in the ten after that, and the second of those is a thing about which I have nothing at all to tell you.**”

That was thirty-three words and it is the last thing said in that room and it did not finish anything.

About four of the nine people in that room have said that the three who walked will go back by the same road they came, and about four have said that nine hundred miles is not far when a thing has been carried the whole way and is very far when nobody has carried anything back.

About four of the nine people in that room have said that those two sentences are the whole of that Sunday, and that the three who walked will not agree among themselves about which one of the two is the true one.

Then he gave the three copies away, one to each of the three who had walked, and kept the ninth part of the fourth return, which is the only part that is about him, and four of the nine people in that room saw him keep it and about four did not know that there was a fourth return.

**And the cost of that Sunday is three people walking four days and four days and nine days to stand in a room above a court on a Sunday, about eleven things agreed on a Thursday by four men in four hundred houses that nobody can now give back to anybody, nine hundred written things in one region of which nine can be read back, one city of eleven thousand people that said no nine years ago and has said nothing since and that nobody in that room asked about, and one man of thirty-two standing in a room and saying out loud that the risk is real. The engine damaged four hundred and twelve days and nobody mending it. The bleed six hundred and twenty-one days old and not cured and not explained and no character in that room has noticed it. Eight anchor fragments where there were ten. One ear, one leg, and a year, and not one of the three of them is a fourth return.**
