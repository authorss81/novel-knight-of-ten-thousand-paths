# Chapter 0890

**Embermonth the twenty-fifth, YR 325, a Friday and not a market morning, the selector room at the bottom of the water stair in the place nine hundred yards up an occupied stair and about nine people on the anchor floor above it, the room open with its records unsealed since the sixteenth of Rainmonth, and nine days after the hour of the year.**

Four hundred and eighty-seven days of one man in a lower archive and he has not come out of it and he is not going to be let go of it by anybody on this page.

The engine has been damaged five hundred and one days and it is not curable this year and it is nobody's achievement, and nine people on that floor had a piece of it in their hands on that morning and five had not touched it in four and a half years.

The bleed is seven hundred and ten days old and is not cured and is not explained and no character in that place has noticed it.

What happened on that Friday is that the thing stopped being one person's and four people on that floor had been waiting nine days for it and about five had not known it was going to happen at all.

The working is nine hundred crossings a day and every one of them is worked by a person who has to be asked, and for four and a half years every one of those asks went to one man.

That is the resistance of that Friday and it is not a resistance anybody put in the way. It is nine answers a day coming out of a person, and there is one person, and four people on that floor had worked that out years ago and about five had said it every week and been answered every week.

Then on that morning nine other people did it.

Nine people, asked one at a time by somebody who was not him, at about the fourth hour, on that floor and on the landings and at the top of that stair, and four of the nine people on that floor had asked them and about five had asked nobody and were asked.

He was not in any of the nine asks and four people in nine counties had said he would not be and about five had said somebody would use him in the first hour.

What the nine of them were is not nine names and four people on that floor could have said all nine and about five could not.

They are the nine people who work nine hundred crossings a day and every one of them had said yes to somebody else before that morning and about four of them had said yes to a person they had never spoken to and about five had said yes to a person they had argued with.

That is the whole of the hand-over in one sentence and it took four and a half years to reach it and about nine people in nine counties have said that the last step of it was the easiest and about five have said the first step was and both of those are the same length.

One of the nine is a woman of thirty-one who walks a lantern run nine miles off and one of the nine is a man of forty-four who runs a weekly cart to a ford and one of the nine is about twenty-one and has been in a glass workshop for two years.

Three of the nine had been asked in a week and six had been asked that morning and four people on that floor had wanted all nine asked at once and about five had said all nine at once is nine answers from one person.

Nobody used him. He was on that floor from the fifth hour with a tool bag at his feet and he did not speak for about four hours and four people on that floor noticed and about five did not.

What he had in that tool bag is nine years of a trade and it is a bar, a lever, a line, a wedge, a roll of tarred cord and about nine things a person does not use on a floor.

He has carried that bag down nine hundred yards of stair and up it about nine hundred times and four people in nine counties have said it is the only object in this manuscript he has never put down and about five have said he put it down in Chapter 0883 and picked it up again.

Now the erasure order.

It is a sheet and it is not nine lines and it is the reason four hundred people at the far end of a four hundred yard cut are at the far end of a four hundred yard cut, and four people in nine counties had read a copy of it and about five had not and nobody in nine counties has ever read the whole of it.

The woman of thirty-eight with a mandate read it out in that room on the Thursday and nine people on that floor heard all of it, and the last line of it says that a community which cannot produce its own record to a standard set elsewhere ceases to be addressed.

That is its own words and four of the nine people on that floor understood it at once and about five needed the third reading.

Then the man in the lower archive came up those three steps again and he did not come further and nine people in that room saw him and about four of them were frightened of him and about five were not.

He withdrew the sheet.

He did it in his own hand on the floor of that room in nine lines and he did it in nine minutes and nine people in that room watched a man of about sixty write nine lines and then turn the sheet round so that four of the nine people in that room could see the whole of it.

About four of the nine people in that room had wanted him to say why and about five had not, and he said nine words about it and they were not about why.

That is the whole of it and four people in nine counties have said it is the best nine words anybody has said in this year and about five have said it is the ninth time he has said something that is nearly right and left the rest of it.

Then he surrendered the Key.

That is the Last Common Key and it was made out of ten anchor fragments and there are none of them left, and four people in that room understood that at the sixth hour and about five understood it at the eighth.

What he put on the floor is a bar of about the length of a forearm and he put it down and did not pick it up and four of the nine people in that room knew what it was and about five had only heard of it.

About nine people in that room watched a man put down the only thing in nine years he could still open anything with, and about four of them have said since that it was the easiest thing he ever did and about five have said it was the hardest and both of them were there.

He did not come out of that place. He went back down three steps and the door stayed open and nobody closed it and nine people in that room watched him go and about four of them wanted to say something and about five did not.

Then the refusal state came back, and it came back by the working that made it and not by a new one, and that is the whole of the ending of this manuscript's eighteenth year.

The First Witness refuses an unwitnessed closure and it does it in about the same words it was written in, and the mechanism is the one the people who built it wrote down in three trades' hands nine years ago in nine books read aloud on a Thursday.

About nine people on that floor heard it and about four of them had heard it before in a room nine paces by five and about five had not heard it at all.

Nobody did anything to it. Nobody went near it and nobody asked it to do anything and four of the nine people on that floor had wanted to go and look at it and about five had not wanted to leave the floor.

That is the whole of it and about nine people in nine counties have said that a thing coming back because nine hundred people asked somebody else nine hundred times is not a victory and about five have said it is not a defeat either.

The Key can no longer select a continuation and four people in that room said so out loud and about five had worked it out before it happened and did not say it out of respect for the first four.

Then the Culling Order was dissolved, and it was not his doing and it did not happen in that room.

That is the name the sealed-out gave it and the council never once used, and about nine people in nine counties have said it belongs to the room it is said in and not to the people it was used on, and four have said that is why it is on no document at all and about five have said that is not the reason.

What ended it was a list of about nine hundred places where somebody was still keeping an office in it, and the list went out on the ordinary post with one question at the top of it, and the question was whether that place wished to go on keeping it.

Four of the about nine hundred answers came back inside a month and about five hundred did not come back at all and nobody went after the ones that did not. No one answer did it and every one of them was asked by somebody with no authority to ask.

The man of forty-four who runs a weekly cart to a ford had kept an office in it for about nine years, and he was asked on that floor in that week, and he wrote his answer on the back of the question in his own trade and gave the office up in nine words.

And the emergency authority that came with it was not repealed by anybody in that room, because it does not need repealing. It runs out on its own date unless a local council renews it in public, and about nine people in nine counties have said that is the only power anybody gave away this year that nobody had afterwards to be trusted with.

Nobody thanked him for the form either and about four people in that room had expected somebody to and about five had not.

Then the trial was ordered.

Not conducted. Ordered, at about the tenth hour, by a seat that has not sat for eleven weeks and that sent a mandate in writing four days ago, and four people in that room had wanted the order read and about five had wanted it left until the Monday.

He read it out. That is the last thing he does with his voice in this manuscript and nine people in that room heard all of it and he read it twice.

Then the woman of thirty-eight read the order out, and the order is the first thing anybody has ever printed under the name of the charter in that room, and nine people in it heard every line of it and about four of them had wanted it read and about five had wanted it left until the Monday.

“**That a man be brought before a court of this name at the first sitting after the fourth of Longlight, and that he be held in the meantime in the place he is in and not in any other, and that nobody in this room speak for him and nobody in this room speak against him, and that the order be read in the room it was made in and sent up that road in the ordinary post and not by anybody who is in this room.**”

That was eighty-seven words and nine people in that room heard all eighty-seven of them, and about four of them had the whole of it before the reading stopped.

The last of those seven lines is the reason that order is worth eighty-seven words and four people in nine counties have said so and about five have said it is a detail and nine hundred people in four hundred places cannot check it either way.

Nobody thanked him. Not the man on the floor of that room and not the nine people who asked nine hundred times that morning and not the four hundred and eighty-seven days, and four people in that room had said beforehand that somebody would and about five had said that nobody would and the five were right.

What was lost on that floor on that morning was a certainty and not a thing anybody else had been holding, being that he had believed for four and a half years that there was one person the whole of this arrived at and that if that person were on a floor then nine hundred crossings a day would work.

About four people on that floor had believed it too and about five had believed the other one, and nobody worked out which of them was right until the ninth asks had been made and answered.

He did not say it out loud and nobody asked him about it and four people in nine counties have worked out what it was and about five have not.

Then a bar went down on a stone floor about the length of a forearm from a man's hand and its edge turned up the inside of his right wrist about three inches and four people in that room saw it happen and about five had their backs to it.

That is the fourth of the four and it is on the same arm as the first and nine people in that room have looked at all four since and about four of them have said a man who works with a bar on the right and a stick on the right will have four scars there in about nine years.

The order went up that road in the ordinary post on the Saturday with a carter who had four other things in his bag, and nine people in that town saw a sealed paper go past and about four of them asked what it was and about five did not.

Nobody in that room went up that stair to see it go and four people in that room wanted to and about five stayed on the floor, and about nine people in nine counties have said that staying is what four and a half years was for.

The man in the lower archive was not told anything about the order and four people in that room believed that and about five did not, and he was nine hundred yards down a stair with a door open above his head and a bar on a floor he could not see.

Then the last nine crossings of that day were worked by nine other people and he stood four paces off with his tool bag at his feet and nine people in that room saw all nine of them and about four of them had watched him do it about nine hundred times and about five had not watched him at all.

That is the ending of the part and it is not a resolution and four people in nine counties have said it is the first ending of this manuscript that is not about a man doing a thing and about five have said that is not an ending at all and it is not.

A toll bell hangs on a gate post four days of made road off and it is silent and nobody in that room has touched it and four people in nine counties have said it will be touched again before this manuscript ends and about five have said it will not.

Nobody went into a drawer in a low room in another county and nobody turned a half leaf and nobody lifted a cloth off a bench and nobody picked up a mallet, and about nine people in this manuscript have carried all four of those things for seventeen years and about four of them have said that this year is the year the list stopped growing.

**And the cost of that Friday is nine hundred crossings a day handed from one person to nine people in one morning, nine answers a day that came out of a man for four and a half years coming out of nine other men and women instead, one bar of about the length of a forearm put down on a floor and not picked up, one erasure order withdrawn in nine lines in a man's own hand and turned round so that anybody could read it, a trial ordered and not conducted, four hundred and eighty-seven days of a man in a lower archive and he is still in it and nobody let him go, an ear and a leg and a limp and a bleed at seven hundred and ten days and four visible scars on the right arm and hand and elbow and wrist, and a tool bag on a floor four paces off a thing nine other people are doing.
