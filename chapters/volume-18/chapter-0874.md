# Chapter 0874

**Rainmonth the eleventh, YR 325, a Saturday and a market morning in a coast town of about nine hundred people, a room above a boat-yard with nine chairs and a long table in it and about nine people at that table, about four hundred people on the quay below from the eighth hour buying what is on nine boats, and the bleach six miles off in her hand.**

Six writers produced six fragments in six rooms above that boat-yard and every one of them wrote at a table by a window with the tide going out below it, and the room this morning is one of the six and the window is the window and the tide is going out.

She came up on the Monday, three days ahead of the delegation, and she is a steward of Bellweather-Living and her name is Bryenna Faal and she is fifty-four, and four of the nine people in that room had known for two days that she was here and five found out when she sat down.

She is out of the delegation's order. That is the whole of her position and she stated it in nine words on the Monday and has not restated it since.

About four of the nine people in that room have said that a person sent ahead of a delegation and out of its order is a person the delegation has sent to be useful and not to speak. About five have said she is a person the delegation sent ahead because she will say the thing first and be contradicted later, which is what stewards are for.

What she had with her was one sheet of paper with four lines on it in her own hand, and she brought it into that room folded and did not open it for about nine minutes.

The woman who leads that delegation is about thirty-nine and came in on the Thursday with four others and has not asked the steward for anything once.

About four of the nine people in that room have noticed that the leader has not asked her for anything and about five have noticed that the leader has not once said what the delegation thinks.

Aren Kest came up that coast road on the Wednesday with a lamp and a stick and about nine feet of cord, and he got in at the fourth hour and slept on a boat, and four people in that town have said that a man who sleeps on a boat in Rainmonth is a man in a hurry and five have said he was not in a hurry and had come early.

What he wanted on that Saturday was her four lines on the sheet of terms before anything else went on it.

Bellweather-Living is not a ruin and it is not a reconstruction and it is not a copy of anywhere, and four people in nine counties have said they could not tell you what it is instead and five have said that a place which cannot be described is a place nobody has described in the wrong month.

Four hundred and nine people live there and about nine hundred yards of a stair in another place is nine days ride from any town in this county, and the road out of it has been open and shut in the same fortnight twice this year.

What is on the sheet of terms is four terms from four places and no names on any of them, and four of the nine people in that room have said that is the correct way round and five have said it is a way of writing that makes four places indistinguishable from each other after nine days.

The first term is eleven lines long and it is about seed vaults and water anchors and it is the only term on that sheet with a number of items in it, and the second is four lines about a tide and about what happens to a landing when a harbour is closed by somebody else's order.

The third is two lines and nobody in that room can say which place it came from, and four of the nine people there have read it four times and five have not been able to find anything in it, and it is on the sheet in a hand that is not any of the six hands in the six rooms.

The woman of forty-four who writes out what other people have to agree to said the thing about the terms sheet on the Friday and she said it in her own trade.

She said that a term with a place in it is a term that can be argued with by somebody who knows the place, and that four terms with no places in them are four terms that can only be argued with by the five people in this room.

About four of the nine people at that table agreed with her out loud and about five did not and one of the five said that four places which cannot tell each other apart on one sheet is four places which have agreed, and the woman of forty-four said that it is four places which have agreed and cannot prove it.

Two men of that delegation want a term about the road out of their own place and one woman wants a term about who speaks for the nine communities in a room like this one, and neither of the two is on the sheet and about four of the nine people in that room have noticed that neither has been written and about five have not.

Then the steward of Bellweather-Living opened the sheet.

She read four lines of it out loud in that room and about nine people at that table heard all four of them and about four of them understood what the four lines were and about five understood that they were being read and not what they were.

She read four lines and they were four lines and about four of the nine people at that table could tell by the end of the third one that it was not about trade at all.

That was the first reading. It is in the record of that room as having happened and the words are not in this page, and about four of the nine people in that room have said since that what she read on the Saturday was not the condition and was a shorter and blander thing, and about five have said it was the condition and that it was read twice.

What the room did with the four lines was take them apart. Two men of the delegation said that the first line was a trade matter and not a political one and that trade matters went to the coast towns and not to a room above a boat-yard.

The woman of forty-four said nothing. The young woman of about twenty-two who writes down every word that is said in that room wrote down all of it, and four of the nine people at that table had noticed that she had not written a question mark on anything for two days.

The man of about thirty-eight who is an officer of a court that has a mandate and carries no army had come up that coast road on the Friday with about nine written things on him and no soldiers, and about four of the nine people in that room have said that he asked for the four lines to be put in as the delegation's position and about five have said that he asked for them to be put in as hers.

He is right about the difficulty and about four of the nine people in that room said so out loud, and a guarantee given in a room by a person dies with that person, and that is the whole of the trouble with the four lines and it is why they came here.

Then somebody asked the steward whether the delegation stood on the four lines.

That is the moment of that Saturday morning and about four of the nine people in that room have said that the answer was correct and that the thing that came after it was the fault.

She said that she does not speak for the delegation.

Four of the nine people in that room heard the whole of that and five heard the first three words and thought the rest of it was going to be corrected.

Nobody corrected it. The woman who leads the delegation did not correct it, and the two men did not, and four people in that town have said since that nobody corrected her because everybody in that room had wanted somebody to say it for nine weeks and nobody had.

What she said after that was in her own trade and it is short.

She said that the four lines are hers, and that they were written by her in a room about nine days of coast road from here, and that the delegation has not read them and will not read them before it leaves unless somebody makes it, and that she is not going to be the person who hands the delegation a position.

About four of the nine people at that table understood the whole of that and about five understood the first sentence of it and were still on the second when she stopped.

He understood both halves of it, four of the nine people in that room understood that he had, and five understood only the half about her hand.

He had come that coast road to write down a dead man's promise on somebody else's behalf and had got a live woman's four lines and a room where everybody was waiting for her to say what nine hundred other people thought.

Then the young woman of twenty-two asked her one question and it was the only question anybody asked that morning about the four lines.

She asked whether a condition written in one person's hand is a condition of the community.

Four of the nine people in that room held their breath and five did not know there was anything to hold their breath about.

What the steward said was that it was written in the community's own words and that her hand is the only reason it is on any paper at all, and that if the delegation's own hand were on it the delegation could withdraw it and if hers is on it the community cannot, because she cannot speak for the community either and can only be asked by it.

About four of the nine people in that room had not thought of that and about four had and said so afterwards.

Then he did the thing he came that coast road for, and he did it in front of nine people, and he did not ask anybody's leave.

He asked for a clean sheet, and he wrote the four lines out on it in his own hand, word for word, and he did not change one word of them, and he put that clean sheet down in the middle of that long table in front of the sheet of terms and put his hand flat on it and took his hand away.

About four of the nine people in that room understood what that was and about five understood that something had been put on a table.

He did not sign it. About four people in that town have said that a man who copies another person's condition out in his own hand and does not sign it has put his name on it by the copying and about five have said he did exactly the right thing.

Then the woman of forty-four ruled nothing and wrote nothing, and she put her own hand flat on the sheet he had written and left it there.

What happened next is the ending of that Saturday.

Nine people in that room have described it since and four of them have described it the same way, and the four are the four who were not looking at the sheet when the young woman of twenty-two put her pen down.

The steward of Bellweather-Living picked up the sheet that had her own four lines in another man's handwriting, and she read them out loud in that room from the top, and about nine people at that table heard every one of them.

“**We will open this road to trade. We will not be held by it, and no Crown and no council and no person living may hold it, and no promise made by a person who is dead is a promise made to us. And nobody may write down a thing about this place and carry it out of it unless one named person here has read it first.**”

That was sixty-eight words and she read them in her own trade and slowly, and about four of the nine people at that table understood the second sentence before she came to the end of it and about five understood the whole of it afterwards and not before.

Nobody in that room corrected her for it.

That is the whole finding of that Saturday. Four of the nine people in that room have said since that it is the least of the four things that happened at that table and about four have said that it is the first time in about nine weeks that a person has said a thing in that room and had all of it taken as hers.

About nine people in that town eat at the delegation's table that night and about four hundred do not, and about nine hundred along that coast road do not know there is a delegation in the town at all.

The delegation did not correct her and did not agree with her and about four people in that town have said that the two of those are the same thing this month, and about five have said the delegation will raise it in a week.

He had copied a condition and made himself the second copy of it in the room where she had said that a copy needs a named person to have read it, and he had left it on the table and had not carried it out of anywhere, and he was aware of all three of those facts at the same time and did not know what any of them was for.

The four lines went on the sheet of terms fourth, after a term about a seed vault and a term about a tide and a term nobody could account for.

Four of the nine people in that room have said that going fourth is a decision and five have said that it went fourth because there was nowhere else for it to go. Nine people in that room have read the sheet of terms since and nine hundred people along nine miles of coast road have not heard that there is a sheet at all.

Nine people on that quay bought what was on nine boats between the eighth hour and the eleventh and nine hundred people along nine miles of coast road are not in that town and will not hear about any of it.

**And the cost of that Saturday is one coast road and a boat and one sheet of terms with four terms on it and about four hundred people on a quay, one guarantee that survives whoever signs it written out word for word in a second person's hand and signed by nobody, one steward's condition read twice in one room by two different people and not corrected once, and about four hours of the delegation's week given to a sheet somebody else had written. The bleed six hundred and six days old on the inside of one wrist and not cured and not explained and no character in that room has asked him about it. Nothing about an ear, a leg or a year can promise anything to whoever has to stand behind whoever signs.**
