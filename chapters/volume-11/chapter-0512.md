# Chapter 0512

**The twenty-seventh day of Fallowmonth, YR 319, a Monday and not a market day, and the second hour and the fifth hour and the eighth hour, in a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people four days up a made road from a coast, and the second set of four ruled heads came back up that lane from a county two hundred miles off on the Sunday, the clerk who brought the bundle slept in that town, and about nine people are in the room at the fifth hour on the Monday, and one of the four sheets in the bundle has five lines on it and not four, and the fifth line has no head on it and nobody ordered it.**

## One

**The four sheets came back in a bundle with nine returns in it, and the nine returns are the week after the nine, and about four hundred and nine parish rolls came in that month and this bundle is not a part of that figure and is not derived from it and the two of them are never added in that room.**

The four heads on the four sheets are the four heads; a place, a figure of people, a trade, and a fourth head that is ruled and empty. The fourth line on the second set is about a sixteenth of an inch narrower than the three above it, on purpose, and the reason has been given once in that room to about four people and to nobody else, and the reason is that a word put in it in one county will not fit in the other.

**And on one of the four there is a fifth line, and it is ruled from the same left-hand edge to the same right-hand place, and it is the same weight, and it has no head cut into the wood above it, and it is empty.**

**Aren Kest was in that room from the second hour to the eighth and was not asked anything at either of them, and he read the fifth line and said nothing about it, and about four people in that room have it that he was looking for something to be wrong with and about five have said that he was waiting to see whether anybody would say it out loud, and the two of those are not the same thing and one of them is not wrong.**

Nobody had ordered it. The rule on it is a woman of about forty-six with grey in her hair who is a clerk in the county two hundred miles off and who came up the lane on the Sunday with the bundle and slept in that town and has been in that room since the second hour this morning.

## Two

“**I ruled it,**” the woman of about forty-six with grey in her hair said, at about the second hour, before anybody asked her.

“**Why?**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**Because the book in my county has five columns this week and the sheet has four, and I am not going to be the clerk who has a sheet that does not match his book,**” the woman of about forty-six with grey in her hair said, “**and that is the whole of the reason and it took about as long as a woman takes to say nine.**”

“**And the head?**”

“**There is no head,**” the woman of about forty-six with grey in her hair said. “**I did not cut one and I was not asked for one and I would not have known what to cut if I had been asked, because I have been in this work about twenty-six years and I have never in all my life had to invent a head.**”

**And that is one hundred and sixteen words in five turns of one exchange, of which the first is three, the second is one, the third is fifty-six printed in two spans of thirty-five and twenty-one, the fourth is three, and the fifth is fifty-three printed in two spans of four and forty-nine, and the woman of about forty-six with grey in her hair is not a villain and is not a keeper of anything and is not on a list, and she ruled a fifth line in about four minutes on a sheet that came two hundred miles and she has said that it was the book that was short and not the sheet that was long.**

## Three

“**Then it is not a copy,**” the man of about thirty-four said, at about the fifth hour. He had been in the room since the second hour and had said nothing until then.

“**It was a copy on Monday,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, “**and it is a copy with a fifth line on it now, and those are not the same object, and I want somebody in this room to notice that they are not the same object before anybody has written anything else on it.**”

“**It is a line,**” the man of about thirty-four said, “**and a line is not a road and a line is not a keeper and a line is not a person.**”

“**It is the fifth line on a sheet in nine counties that every clerk with a book is going to copy,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, “**and in about four years a man is going to hold up two of them and say that one is a copy and one is not, and he will be right, and the difference between them will be a line that a woman of about forty-six ruled to make a sheet match a book.**”

“**Then she should not have ruled it.**”

“**She should not have,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, “**and I am not going to say that to her, because she has told me why and the reason is that a book with five columns and a sheet with four is a worse thing than a fifth line, and she has been a clerk for about twenty-six years and I have been whatever this is for about six weeks.**”

**And that is two hundred and twenty-six words in six turns of one exchange, of which the first is six, the second is forty-nine printed in two spans of six and forty-three, the third is twenty-five printed in two spans of four and twenty-one, the fourth is seventy-five printed in two spans of twenty-one and fifty-four, the fifth is seven, and the sixth is sixty-four printed in two spans of four and sixty, and about four of the nine people in that room agreed with the man of about fifty-eight and about five did not, and the woman of about forty-six with grey in her hair was not asked a second question and did not offer one and has not thought about it since about the sixth hour.**

## Four

**And the copyist of about twenty-six was at the side table with a straightedge and a knife and a pot of paste, and she had taken the fifth line off with the knife she rules with in about four minutes, and the shaving of paper off the edge of it is about the width of her little finger, and the sheet has four lines on it again and the four are the four that went into that bundle on the Monday, and nobody could tell that off the sheet and one woman can.**

She said one thing about it and it was not about the fourth head and it has not been said twice.

“**I took a line off a sheet that came two hundred miles,**” Nesta Braith said, “**and I did it in four minutes with the knife I rule with, and if anybody in nine counties wants to know who ruled the fifth line then two people can answer and one of them is not me.**”

“**Why did you do it?**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**Because you said out loud that a copy with a line on it is not a copy,**” Nesta Braith said, “**and I would like the two counties to be able to say that they are the same object, and I would like that more than I would like to be right, and I have been right once.**”

**And that is one hundred and ten words in three turns of one exchange, of which the first is fifty-one printed in two spans of twelve and thirty-nine, the second is five, and the third is fifty-four printed in two spans of seventeen and thirty-seven, and the fifth line came off a sheet that came two hundred miles and the cut edge of it is not a straight edge and nobody in nine counties has looked at it and nobody in nine counties is going to, and the shaving is not in the cost of that Wednesday and is not in anybody's book.**

**And the line that came off is empty, and it was empty before it came off, and nobody in that room put a word in it and nobody in that room can say that anybody has put a word in it, and a line that is ruled and then taken out again is not a fifth head and is not the fourth head and is a third thing, and there is no head anywhere in nine counties for a third thing.**

## Five

The cost of that Wednesday is a column and it is the woman of about forty-six's, and it is the first one she has ever kept, and she keeps it on the back of a return, and two hundred miles is not in it.

**cheese at four, a cake of ink at three, a second pen-nib at one, a pot for the paste at five, a rule of brass at nine, a second waxed cord at two, a second broom at five, a second packet of pins at one, a night's bed at four, a knife at two, a pound of candles at four, a packet of pins at one, a leather strap for the bag at two, a pen-nib at one, a bucket of tar at eight, a waxed cord at two, a second cake of ink at three, a second ream of paper at nine, ink at three, a quill feather at one, a second bucket of tar at eight, a second quill feather at one, a broom at five, and a ream of paper at nine.** Four, three, one, five, nine, two, five, one, four, two, four, one, two, one, eight, two, three, nine, three, one, eight, one, five and nine is ninety-three pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and ninety-three pence is a mark and forty-five over**, and a fifth line is not in it, and a sixteenth of an inch is not in it and is a difference in a ruled line and is not a figure of persons, of money, of days or of distance, and about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month are not in it, and about nine hundred sheets a month are not in it, and about four hundred at the end of a made lane are not in it, and the four of those are not added to one another.

**And on the twenty-seventh day of Fallowmonth in this year a clerk of about forty-six with grey in her hair came up a made lane on the Sunday before with nine returns and four ruled heads in a bundle, and ruled a fifth line on one of the four on the Monday morning so that the sheet would match a book, and a copyist of about twenty-six took the line off again with the knife she rules with in about four minutes, and the fourth head of that sheet is empty and it was empty before and it is empty now, and the copy is a copy again, and about four people have said that the taking off was the right thing and about nine have said that a line cut off a sheet is not a line that never was on it, and nobody has taken the sheet out of the room and nobody is going to.**
