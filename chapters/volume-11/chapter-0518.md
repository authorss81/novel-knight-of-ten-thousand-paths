# Chapter 0518

**The eighth day of Wolfmonth, YR 319, a Sunday and not a market day, and the seventh hour and the ninth hour and the eleventh hour, in a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people four days up a made road from a coast, and the cart came back up that lane at about the seventh hour with a return in it, and the fourth head on that return is not empty, and it is the first return in nine counties in about four years with a word in the fourth head, and the room has been shut on Sundays for about four months and about nine people are in it.**

## One

**The word is one word. It is not a person's name and it is not a keeper's name and it is not the name of a road and it is not on a sheet on a flat stone at the landward end of a coast four days off, and it is written in a hand that about four people in that room say is a hand they have seen before and about five say is a hand they have not.**

The county is the county whose return came back in Harvestmonth with the fourth head cut out clean with a knife and a quarter inch of the paper taken away with it. **The two of those are two acts by two men in one county in about four months, and the room knows that it knows the county and does not know either man, and about four people in that room have said that the county did both and about five have said that a county is a building and a clerk and about nine hundred and forty returns a year, and the two sets have not merged and are not going to.**

The fourth head on the Crown's form lying on the table in that room is empty, and it is empty on every form in that town, and it has been empty for about four months, and nobody filled it.

**Somebody filled one on a different piece of paper, in a different county, about four years after the head was cut, and that somebody is a woman of about fifty-three who takes in washing and who is standing at the back of the room with her hands wet.**

## Two

“**Who filled it in?**” the man of about fifty-eight said, at about the ninth hour.

Nobody answered him for about as long as a man takes to say four, and the woman of about thirty-four said one sentence.

“**Do not ask her that,**” the woman of about thirty-four said, “**and I am not saying she did not do it, I am saying that the moment you ask her that in this room then the only thing this sheet is, for the rest of its life, is a thing a woman was made to account for, and it was a thing she was told to fill in.**”

“**By whom?**”

“**You have just asked the wrong question twice,**” the man of about thirty-four with a satchel said, “**and the first time you asked it she did not answer and about four people in this room heard you not ask it, and that is the whole of what has happened on that paper.**”

“**I am the officer in this room.**”

“**You are a man with a badge in a hired room,**” the man of about thirty-four with a satchel said, “**and a badge is a badge, and the question was not about the officer and it was never about the officer.**”

**And that is one hundred and fifty words in six turns of one exchange, of which the first is four, the second is sixty-two printed in two spans of five and fifty-seven, the third is two, the fourth is forty-three printed in two spans of eight and thirty-five, the fifth is seven, and the sixth is thirty-two printed in two spans of eleven and twenty-one, and a man of about fifty-eight asked a question in a room of about nine people and was told twice, by two different people, in front of the other seven, that he had asked the wrong one, and nobody argued with the man of about thirty-four with a satchel and about four of the nine argued with the woman of about thirty-four and about five did not.**

## Three

“**I filled it in,**” the woman of about fifty-three said, at about the ninth hour, and Aren Kest was about nine feet from her and did not move and did not look at her and looked at the sheet, before anybody asked her anything else, and she did not come forward and she did not raise her voice.

“**You do not have to tell us that.**”

“**I know I do not,**” the woman of about fifty-three said, “**and I am telling you because you have all been not asking me for about four minutes and I would rather be a woman who said it than a woman who was not asked. I was told to fill it in. I have wanted it stopped since the day the head was cut out of one in our county and nobody said anything, and I wanted it stopped when I was told to fill it in, and both of those are true and you can put them on one line or you can put them on two.**”

“**Who told you?**”

“**I am not going to tell you that,**” the woman of about fifty-three said, “**and you did not ask it, and we have both just found out that we did not ask it, and that is the first thing today that has gone the way it should.**”

**And that is one hundred and fifty-eight words in five turns of one exchange, of which the first is four, the second is eight, the third is one hundred and two printed in two spans of five and ninety-seven, the fourth is three, and the fifth is forty-one printed in two spans of eight and thirty-three, and a woman of about fifty-three who takes in washing said out loud in a room of about nine people that she was told to fill in a head and that she wanted the same head stopped, and she was not thanked, and she was not asked to explain, and she was not convicted, and nobody in that room has any power to convict anybody, and the room did not pretend to have any.**

## Four

**And the room did one thing about it, and the thing it did was copy the sheet, and the copy was made by a copyist of about twenty-six in about four minutes and the copy has the word on it, and the copy is now in two counties and the original is in this room on a table.**

The man of about fifty-eight read the one word out loud twice and did not explain it and did not say what it was for. **Nobody in that room has asked him what it means and about four have said it means there is nothing there and about five have said it means whatever the person who wrote it needed it to mean on the day, and about nine people in nine counties will read this word inside about four years in a room that does not exist yet and will not know whose hand it is.**

**And the rule that the fourth head stays empty was never the office's to keep. That is a fact about nine counties and not about this room. In about four years a clerk in a county cut a line out of a form with a knife because he did not want the question, and in about four months after that a woman of about fifty-three put one word in a fourth head with a pen because she was told to, and neither of the two of them is an office and neither of them is a villain and neither of them has been asked anything by anybody with power, and the fourth head on the Crown's form in this town is empty and has not been filled by anybody in this room.**

**And a sheet on a flat stone at the landward end of a coast four days off has a word in a fourth head of its own, and it is a different document, and it is in a different county, and the two documents have been put next to each other in this paragraph on purpose so that it can be said that they are two.**

## Five

The cost of that Sunday is a column and it is the woman of about fifty-three's, and it is the first one she has ever kept, and she keeps it on a washing board, and a word is not in it.

**cheese at four, a bucket at eight, a second boiler's coal at nine, a night's straw at four, a second night's straw at four, a broom at five, a third boiler's coal at nine, a third broom at five, a stone of tallow at five, a fourth boiler's coal at nine, a second broom at five, a third night's straw at four, a fourth night's straw at four, a second wringer at nine, a third bucket at eight, a second bucket at eight, a bundle of kindling at four, a second bundle of kindling at four, a wringer at nine, a pound of candles at four, a line and a peg at two, a boiler's coal at nine, a second pound of candles at four, and a third pound of candles at four.** Four, eight, nine, four, four, five, nine, five, five, nine, five, four, four, nine, eight, eight, four, four, nine, four, two, nine, four and four is a hundred and forty pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and forty pence is two marks and forty-four over**, and one word is not in it, and about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month are not in it, and about nine hundred sheets a month are not in it, and about a hundred and four in one band are not in it, and the three of those are three counts of three different series and are not added to one another, and a knife and a pen are not in it and are two acts by two people in one county in about four months.

**And on the eighth day of Wolfmonth in this year a return came up a made road with a Sunday cart with one word in a fourth head, and it is the first one in nine counties in about four years, and a woman of about fifty-three who takes in washing said out loud that she was told to fill it in and that she wanted it stopped, and nobody asked her by whom and nobody asked her why and she was not thanked and was not convicted, and the fourth head on the form in the room where she was standing is empty and nobody in that room filled it, and the tide decides twice a day and is not paid and is not on any of this.**
