# Chapter 0513

**The second day of Embermonth, YR 319, a Saturday and a market day, and the first hour and the third hour and the seventh hour, in a market town of about two thousand people four days up a made road from a coast, in a hired room over a saddler's, and a man of about fifty-one in a grey coat is carrying a letter he wrote on a Monday and has not taken out, and about nine people are in that room at the third hour, and the thing he is about to say is worse than the thing he said before it and he is going to say that first.**

## One

**He took the letter out at about the third hour and put it on the table face down, and then he turned it over, and then he put it face down again, and about four of the nine people in that room put those two turns first when they tell it and about five leave them out.**

The letter is a letter to nine counties. It is one page. It has the four names of the four people who are to read the returns in this room at the top of it, which is the offer he made in a room over a weigh-house about two months ago and which has been on the table ever since, **and the four names have not been written on anything in this county and are not written on that page.**

What is written on the page is a sentence offering to send the four names to the nine counties, so that a road may complain to a person.

**And the fourth head on a return in this town is empty, and it is empty on about ninety of them now, and the offer on that page is not about the fourth head and is about every other head on the same sheet.**

## Two

He said it in nine sentences and he did not stop in the middle of one of them, and the copyist of about twenty-six wrote all nine down because she has a sheet and not because she was asked.

“**This is worse than the offer I made before, and I want that said in this room before anybody else says it, because I have been about four months building a thing that is supposed to be small and I have found out that the smallness was in the room and not in the letter. If I send the four names to nine counties then a woman in a lane has four names she can be walked to, and a clerk in a county has four names he can put at the top of a sheet, and that sheet will be a different kind of sheet from the one we have now, because ours is about roads and that one would be about us. A name is not a small thing, and I have said so in this room and I have been right about it. I would have a thing I could say in a room, which is that a person can complain, and I would have it because I wrote the four names down myself, and not one of those four people is on a road and not one of them has ever been on one. I have not decided to send it and nobody has to agree with me today. I have not been asked for it by anybody, and I have spent about nine days going looking for a reason to send it, and the reason I have found is that it is the only thing I have got. That is a bad reason and I have been holding it for about nine days. If it is not sent then the four names stay off every sheet in this county, which is a thing about four people and not about a road. I would like somebody in this room to tell me a better one before the Friday.**”

Nobody in that room said anything for about as long as a man takes to say nine.

**And that is three hundred and twelve words in nine sentences in one turn of one exchange, and the copyist of about twenty-six wrote all nine sentences down on a sheet with no head over it, and the man of about fifty-one in a grey coat did not read them back and did not ask her to, and about four of the nine people in that room agree with him and about five do not, and the two sets do not overlap at all, and the four names are still off every sheet in this county and the letter is still on the table face down.**

## Three

“**Say the part you have not said,**” the woman of about thirty-four said.

“**The part I have not said is that a road that can complain to a person is a road that has been found,**” Gault Prentice said. “**Not found out about. Found. There is a difference and it is about nine words wide and it is the whole of what I have been arguing about for four months, and I have just argued against myself in front of nine people, and I would do it again, because the alternative is a return with an empty fourth head and nobody to put anything in it and about four hundred people at the end of a made lane who find out about it from a shop.**”

“**Then why are you offering it?**”

“**Because I would rather be found than be right,**” Gault Prentice said, “**and I have not got a better sentence than that and I have had about four months.**”

**And that is one hundred and forty-nine words in four turns of one exchange, of which the first is seven, the second is one hundred and ten printed in two spans of twenty-three and eighty-seven, the third is six, and the fourth is twenty-six printed in two spans of nine and seventeen, and a man of about fifty-one in a grey coat said out loud in a room of about nine people that he would rather be found than be right, and about four of the nine have it that it is the first thing anybody in that room has said that they did not already think of, and about five have said that a man who would rather be found than be right has just handed nine counties a way of finding a road, and the two sets do not overlap.**

## Four

**And a man of thirty-two with a limp was in that room from the first hour to the seventh and was not asked one question, and what he said was nine words, and it was said to the table and not to the man in the grey coat.**

“**A county clerk can find a man in about four years.**”

“**Can he?**” the man of about thirty-four said.

“**A letter with a name on it goes into a county, and a county has a list of who receives letters, and the list is a list of houses,**” Aren Kest said, “**and I have been in nine counties for about four years asking a different question and I have learned that one thing about all of them, and it is that a county knows where every man in it sleeps.**”

**And that is eighty-one words in three turns of one exchange, of which the first is eleven, the second is two, and the third is sixty-eight printed in two spans of twenty-nine and thirty-nine, and nobody wrote any of it down, and a man of thirty-two with a limp is a person a room can write down, and about four people in that town have it that he did not have to be in that room on that Thursday and about nine have said that he was in it because a form says nobody is to be sent to find out and he came anyway, and the two are not added and neither of them is wrong.**

**And the letter went back into the grey coat at about the seventh hour, and the four names are not on it and the fourth head of a return in this town is empty, and the page in the grey coat says a thing that was not done, and a thing that was not done is not a rule and is not a refusal and does not have a head on it.**

## Five

The cost of that Thursday is a column and it is the man of about fifty-one's, and it is the second one he has kept in about four months, and four names are not in it and a letter that was not sent is not in it.

**a waxed cord at two, a bucket of tar at eight, a bundle of cords at two, a third sheet of paper at one, a second small beer at two, a fourth copy of the four heads at one, a rule at four, a candle at two, a cart to the made road at nine, a second copy of the four heads at one, a broom at five, a second sheet of paper at one, a pot of tea at three, a third copy of the four heads at one, a knife at two, a quill and ink at three, a second rule at four, a pony's hire at nine, a sheet of paper at one, a second knife at two, cheese at four, a second broom at five, a second cheese at four, a second pony's hire at nine, a copy of the four heads at one, a stamp at three, a second bucket of tar at eight, small beer at two, and a second pot of tea at three.** Two, eight, two, one, two, one, four, two, nine, one, five, one, three, one, two, three, four, nine, one, two, four, five, four, nine, one, three, eight, two and three is a hundred and two pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and two pence is two marks and six over**, and about four hundred at the end of a made lane are not in it, and about nine hundred on a sand four days off are not in it, and about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month are not in it, and the three of those are not added, and a person who can be found at a house is not in it.

**And on the second day of Embermonth in this year a man of about fifty-one in a grey coat put a letter on a table in a hired room over a saddler's and said out loud that it was worse than the offer he had made before, and said so in nine sentences, and the room did not agree with him and about four of the nine people in it thought he was right, and the letter went back into his coat at about the seventh hour with four names still off it, and the fourth head of a return in that town is empty and is not in the letter and is not in the column, and the tide decides twice a day and is not paid and is not on any of this.**
