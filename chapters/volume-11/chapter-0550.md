# Chapter 0550

**A man of thirty-two with a limp was not in a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people on the twenty-fifth of Mudmonth, YR 320, a Saturday and a market day, at the second hour and the fifth hour and the ninth hour, and the finding that room made in a month carried at thirty is in that room's book on a page with no head over it, and the fourth head of the Crown's form on the table by that door is ruled and empty, and about nine people were in it, and he had said to nobody that he would be there and nobody had asked him.**

He came up the lane at about the third hour because he had decided before the second hour that he would come up the lane, and there was a market in the street and he was in it, and he was not upstairs.

A man of about twenty-nine with a handcart knew him from a street and said the name out loud, twice, and then said a third thing that was not the name, and Aren Kest stood in a market and let a market town say his name and did not say it back, and the woman who sells fish near the weigh-house looked at him for about as long as a woman takes to say nine and went back to her board.

That is the whole of where he was and it goes first because it is the last thing in that county anybody would have said out loud about him.

Nobody in that room said anything about where he was. The clerk of about twenty-two wrote the day at the top of a fresh page and did not write the year anywhere on it, and the year had been Thawmonth on the first of the month and Mudmonth all the way through and nobody in that room said which of the two it was, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet wrote the year out on a bare leaf with no head over it and ruled nothing and put it face down.

Then the man of about fifty-eight put the finding in the book, and it had been off the book since the second day of a month carried at thirty, and it went in on the thirteenth of Mudmonth with a pen and not a knife.

**He said out loud that he was not cutting a second head over it in eleven years, and that the room's book has one head cut into the wood over one line in it and that head was cut for a different thing, and that the finding is in the book and is under a head that is not its own, and that those are two separate facts and that a clerk in about four years is going to find a sentence under a head that was cut for something else and is going to have to decide on his own what it is about, and that is the correct condition for it.**

Nobody argued with him and nobody agreed with him out loud.

Then the man of about thirty-four with a sheet said the part that had not been said, and he said it flat, and he said it to the table and not to anybody.

“**A rule about procedure is not a promise about what anybody will do,**” he said, “**and it has been in that book for twelve days and nobody in nine counties has done one single thing differently because of it, and I have been in nine counties since the eighth of a month carried at thirty and I have watched, and that is not a complaint about anybody in this room. It is the second half of the finding and the second half is the one that will still be true in about four years.**”

Nobody in that room thanked him for saying it and he did not ask and no one in nine counties has ever heard him say that he wanted to be thanked for it.

The man of about fifty-eight said the whole of what the room can do, and he said it in about four sentences and it is the same sentence he read off a leaf in this room on the second day of a month carried at thirty, and the words are the same words and he did not improve on them.

“**An order may not act on a road without that road's own answer to a question put to it in its own words. That is the whole of what this room has found. It is worth more than the whole of what anybody in it came for, and about four of the nine people here think that is a rule and about five think it is a sentence a room says about itself in a room where nobody is anybody. Both of those are about this room. Neither of them is about a road.**”

**And he said out loud what the room cannot do, and it took about as long as a man takes to say nine, and nobody in that room had asked him to say it and nobody wrote it down.**

He cannot put a city back. He cannot open anything. He cannot send anybody to find out, and printed at the foot of a return in the same ink as the rest of it is a line that says nobody is to be sent to find out, and it has said that for about a hundred and forty years and nobody in nine counties has ever taken it as a joke. He cannot fill the fourth head, and he is not going to, and no office is going to, and **a rule that says a thing may not be done is a different object from a thing being not done, and the difference between those two is the whole of what a room of about nine people can spend.**

There is a place on a shelf that nothing anybody in that room did in five months has touched, and about four hundred people at the end of a made lane four days off the other way and about four hundred that went under a second head of a return in Harvestmonth are the same figure and are not the same figure, and no figure of either of them is in that room's book and no figure of either of them is in the column at the end of this page, and nothing that room found has anything to do with a shelf.

**And the man of about fifty-one in a grey coat said one thing about the last day, and it was the only thing he said in that room in about a month, and nobody thanked him for it and he is not a villain and he is not a knave and nobody in nine counties has ever been told whose plan any of it is.**

“**There were about nine people in this room on the second day of a month carried at thirty and there are about nine people in it now, and the difference between those two rooms is a sentence and a piece of foolscap with four lines on it, and I have spent five months building a thing that was supposed to be small, and what has happened to it is not that it got bigger. It is that it got about a man in it. I have said out loud in this room that the smallness was in the room and not in the county and I am not going to take it back and I am not going to say it again.**”

Nesta Braith wrote it on a bare leaf, ruled no head over it, ruled nothing under it, and read none of it back.

**And nobody in that room agreed with him out loud and nobody argued with him out loud, and it is the first time in five months that anybody in that room has said a thing out loud about the man of thirty-two with a limp, and the man of about fifty-eight said out loud that he had not been asked to say it and had said it anyway.**

Then the man of about thirty-four with a sheet did the thing that is the last thing anybody in that county did with a piece of paper, and nobody thanked him for it.

Half a leaf came back.

It is half of a leaf because a man of thirty-two with a limp cut one in half with a knife in a room over a weigh-house on the eighth of a month carried at thirty and gave one half to the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps that room and kept the other, and said out loud that it was so that neither of them had all of them, and she said the reason was not a reason and he said that it was a precaution, and neither of them said one word about it again.

**This is the half he kept. The man of about thirty-four with a sheet carried it down a made road himself in the last week of Thawmonth, and nobody sent him and he was not sent, and a man who goes down a made road with a sheet in his coat is a man going down a made road and a man who is sent is a man somebody sent, and that distinction is the whole of what those nine leaves were for.**

It went to the landward end of a coast four days off and a day off a boat and it came back up nineteen days or about nine more on the sixth of Mudmonth, and a man of about thirty-four with a sheet took it out of a bundle with nine returns in it and put it on the table in a hired room over a saddler's and put it face down.

It has been on that table face down for nineteen days.

**Nobody in that room has turned it over. The man of about fifty-eight has not turned it over and he is the man with a chain of office that is a badge. The copyist of about twenty-six has not turned it over and she has a knife. The clerk of about twenty-two has not turned it over and it is his job to enter things. The man of about fifty-three with a barrow has not turned it over and he is the only person in that room who has ever been on the wrong side of a line. Nobody has asked anybody else to and nobody has been asked.**

“**It is half a leaf,**” the woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key said, “**and it is not a form and it is not the form and it is not a return and it is not on a bundle in nine counties, and it has four heads on it with a question under the fourth one and the question is under the head and is not in it, and the fourth head is ruled and empty and has been empty since a room over a weigh-house ruled it, and no office has filled it and no person has filled it and nobody in nine counties is going to.**”

Nobody thanked her and nobody argued with her out loud and she is not on it and it is not a keeper and it is not a register and it has no day.

Then the man of about thirty-four with a sheet said one thing about the nineteen days and it is the only thing he said about it and he is not going to say it again.

“**I have carried that half a leaf down a made road and back and I have not turned it over, and I have had nine days in a market town in which about four hundred people have been saying a man's name in a street because a sheet with his name in a fourth line went round a market, and I have not turned it over because the second time I turned a page over in this county a man with a barrow read a man's name out loud at a rail of a common and about four hundred people at the end of a made lane found out a name they had not asked for. I am not going to be the reason it happens twice.**”

Not one person in that room said so out loud, and not one person in that room said no to him either.

Then the room did the thing it does on the last day of a month carried at thirty, and it did it at the ninth hour to about four people instead of about nine, and the difference is five, and a thing said out loud to four people on the ninth hour of a market Saturday is worth exactly as much as a thing said out loud to nine.

“**The hearing stands,**” the man of about fifty-eight said. “**It is not adjourned and it is not refused and it is not retold and it is not closed, and there is no bench in this room and there has never been a bench in this room and there is nobody in this county who is sentenced to anything by anything said in it, and I am going to go on saying that on the first day of every month until somebody stops me, and nobody has stopped me.**”

**The Crown's form on the table by that door is still carrying its fourth head ruled and empty: empty at the second hour, empty at the ninth, and empty again on the Monday the bundle goes. No office in nine counties has put a word in it, no person in that room did, not one head on any form in that room was filled that day, and nobody there is going to fill one.**

One lane at the end of a made road still has its three names on it and still has three names and no more: the Green Lane by the rail of a common, the Long Lane at the eleventh of eleven houses, the Chapel Way at the fourth house. Nobody has been asked which is true, nothing has been put on a calendar about choosing, and the room has not taken one over. A lane holds three words for itself; that is not the same as a room settling a question.

A shut road four hundred miles off is not reopened and is not scheduled and nobody was sent to it, and about nine people are on the wrong side of it, and about that road nobody in this county has used the word, and in five months nobody here has used it about one thing.

Four days off, at the seaward end of a sand, a woman of about fifty-one is at a post with a piece of driftwood. **She has cut twenty-two notches in the edge of it. The twenty-second is not cut. The water has not been off that mark in twenty-two years and a notch there is a year and not a tide, and nobody in this county has been to her and nobody out of it has either, and about nine hundred people on that sand are on no roll and no sheet and no list and about eleven hundred more are on nothing, and there is nobody on that sand in this account and no month and no weekday has come out of a mouth on it in nine counties in five months.**

**And nothing was opened, and nobody was sent, and a woman of about forty-four who sells fish asked the man of about fifty-eight one question on the sixteenth of Mudmonth and got an answer, and Aren Kest was in a market on the three days after that with his name being said out loud around him by about nine people he had never met, and he did not go up the stairs and nobody in that market knew he was the man in the fourth line until somebody said it, and about four of them said it and about five said something else, and the two sets do not overlap.**

**And the cost of that Saturday is a column and it is the man of about thirty-four with a sheet's, and it is the fifteenth one he has kept in about nine years, and a face-down half leaf is not in it and nineteen days is not in it and a man of thirty-two with a limp being in a market instead of upstairs is not in it.**

**a sheet of bladder at three, a skein of thread at six, a stop for a bottle at eleven, a wedge of soap at thirteen, a second wedge of soap at seventeen, a lamp for a door at nineteen, a piece of chalk at twenty-two, a bundle of tow at twenty-six, a lamp for a gate at thirty-one, a chain of three links at thirty-seven, and a sheet of oiled paper at forty-one.** Three, six, eleven, thirteen, seventeen, nineteen, twenty-two, twenty-six, thirty-one, thirty-seven and forty-one is two hundred and twenty-six pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and two hundred and twenty-six pence is four marks and thirty-four over**, and half a leaf is not in it and a face-down thing is not in it, and nineteen days is not in it, and neither the four heads nor the fifth line is in it, and that fifth line is not a fourth head, and a rule about procedure is not in it because a rule is not a thing anybody buys, and about nine people in that room are not in it, and the about four hundred at the end of a made lane and the about four hundred that went under a second head of a return in Harvestmonth are one figure twice over without being one figure, so neither of them is priced here; and the about nine hundred on a sand four days off and the about two thousand in this town are two more, of two other things, priced at neither end, and twenty-two notches and twenty-two years are the same number under two names, and neither of those names is a price in this column, and two hundred miles off there is a room where three series are counted and none of the three is counted from another: about nine hundred sheets a month go out, about a hundred and four go out in one band, about four hundred and nine parish rolls come in, and about nine people on the wrong side of a shut road four hundred miles off are not in it, and a letter with four names still off it is in a coat in that room and is not in it.

Nobody in that room turned the leaf over on the ninth hour and the man of about fifty-eight went down the stairs and out into a street where a market was finishing, and the flat box with the bare leaves in it is not in the room's book and the finding is in the room's book, and those are two different places for a thing to be, and nobody in nine counties has said which of them is better.
