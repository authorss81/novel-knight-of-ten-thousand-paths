# Chapter 0519

**The ninth day of Wolfmonth, YR 319, a Monday and not a market day, and the first hour and the fourth hour and the eighth hour, in a market town of about two thousand people four days up a made road from a coast, in a hired room over a saddler's, and the cart that goes up that road went at about the first hour with nine returns in it, and a clerk of about twenty-two was asked at about the fourth hour for a book that is not the room's book, and said no in nine words, and nobody argued with him.**

## One

**The book has about two hundred leaves in it and it has been his for about nine months, and it is not the room's book and it is not a copy of the room's book and nobody told him to begin it.**

About nine of its leaves have something on them. Eight of the nine are figures of carts on market mornings in this town in a season, and the ninth is a list of the four saddles in a shop nine doors from a weigh-house with the price of a new one against each and the name of the man who made the last one against two of the four.

“**I want that on the table,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, at about the fourth hour, “**and I want it now, and it is going into the room's book because the room's book is what goes up the road.**”

“**It is not the room's book.**”

“**It has been in this room every day for nine months.**”

“**It has been in my coat every day for nine months,**” the clerk of about twenty-two said, “**and being in a room and being in a coat are two different things, and about four months ago you wrote down a man's name in the back of a book of carts and he did not read it out and you did not ask him to, and that is the same kind of thing and I noticed it and I have not said anything about it since.**”

**And that is one hundred and twenty-five words in four turns of one exchange, of which the first is twenty-nine printed in two spans of six and twenty-three, the second is six, the third is eleven, and the fourth is seventy-nine printed in two spans of eleven and sixty-eight, and the man of about fifty-eight did not answer it and did not take the book and did not ask again, and about four of the nine people in that room have it that the clerk of about twenty-two was defending a thing that is not worth a book and about five have said that the man of about fifty-eight wrote a name down on a Sunday and did not read it out and the two of those are the same act done by two men in the same room.**

## Two

**Aren Kest was in that room from the second hour and he had been standing at the one window that is open since the fourth hour, and he turned round when the man of about fifty-eight said the last thing, and he said nothing, and about four people in that room have it that he could have said one sentence and about five have said that he was right not to, and he has not said which of the two he thought, and nobody asked him.**

“**Last thing,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, at about the fifth hour. “**Hand it over.**”

“**It is mine, and I would rather keep it.**”

Nobody in that room said anything for about as long as a man takes to say four, and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel, who had been at the back of the room since the second hour, did not say anything either, and the woman of about thirty-four did not say anything, and the copyist of about twenty-six went on with what she was doing.

“**You have heard him,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, to about nine people, “**and I am not going to ask a second time, and I am not going to treat it as a refusal of the room, and it is not a refusal of the room. It is a man of twenty-two declining to give up nine leaves that he began by himself, and I have been in a room like this about four months and I have not once had a person say no to me in it, and I am going to write down that it happened and the day it happened on and nothing else.**”

“**Not the leaves,**” the clerk of about twenty-two said.

“**Not the leaves,**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

**And that is one hundred and nineteen words in five turns of one exchange, of which the first is five printed in two spans of two and three, the second is nine, the third is ninety-nine printed in two spans of four and ninety-five, the fourth is three, and the fifth is three, and the nine words are the shortest refusal anybody has said in that room in about four months, and the five before them are two words and a comma and a question, and nobody in that room argued with either of them and about four of the nine have said that it is the first thing in four months that went the way it should and meant it and about five have said that a room where a man can refuse is not a room, and the two of those are not added together and neither of them is a rule.**

## Three

“**I counted nine market mornings in it,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, at about the sixth hour, to the clerk of about twenty-two, “**and I wrote that down in a room on a Sunday in Fallowmonth, and I have been wrong, and I would rather find that out from you than from a man in four years.**”

“**It is eight market mornings,**” the clerk of about twenty-two said. “**The ninth leaf is the four saddles. I counted it a market morning because I had it in my hand and the nine was the number I was thinking of, and a man of fifty-eight counting leaves in a room at the end of a long day is a man who counts the shape of a thing and not the thing itself.**”

“**Eight,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, “**and I am going to take the nine out of my own book, because a number in a book of carts is a number somebody will use.**”

“**You will leave it.**”

“**I will not leave it.**”

“**Then it is your book and not my nine,**” the clerk of about twenty-two said.

**And that is one hundred and fifty-four words in six turns of one exchange, of which the first is forty-one printed in two spans of seven and thirty-four, the second is sixty-seven printed in two spans of five and sixty-two, the third is twenty-eight printed in two spans of one and twenty-seven, the fourth is four, the fifth is five, and the sixth is nine, and a man of about fifty-eight was told by a man of about twenty-two that he had counted the shape of a thing and not the thing, and a man of about fifty-eight said he would take a number out of his own book, and a man of about twenty-two told him he would not, and the two of them did not agree and about four people in that room have it that the clerk was right and about five have said that the chairman was going to do it, and the two sets do not overlap at all.**

## Four

**And the cost of that Monday was said out loud by a man of about fifty-one in a grey coat at about the eighth hour, and he looked at the man of thirty-two with a limp before he began, and the man of thirty-two with a limp was about nine feet from him and said nothing, and about nine people in that room heard all of it.**

“**Here is what it costs, and I am going to say it once and I am not going to be thanked for it. A room with a book in it that the room does not have is a room that cannot say what it knows, and a room that cannot say what it knows is a room that will be asked in about four years by somebody with a warrant, and there will be nine hundred sheets a month in it and about four hundred and nine parish rolls and about a hundred and four in a band, and the man asking will be right and the room will not be able to put a number on the four things he is asking for, because one of them is nine leaves in a coat and there is no head in nine counties for nine leaves in a coat. I would rather have that number than the book, and he will not give it to me, and that is the cost and it is his and not mine and I am not going to call it anything else.**”

Nobody said anything for about as long as a man takes to say four.

“**Then we are all four of those things,**” the clerk of about twenty-eight with a form said.

**And that is one hundred and ninety-five words in two turns of one exchange, of which the first is one hundred and eighty-seven and the second is eight, and a man of about fifty-one in a grey coat said a thing about a warrant that has not been issued and may never be issued, and nobody in that room believes that a warrant will never be issued and nobody in that room said so, and the four counts he named are four counts of four different things and are not added to one another and none of them is a figure of a person.**

## Five

The cost of that Monday is a column and it is the clerk of about twenty-two's, and it is the first one he has ever kept, and he keeps it on the back of a leaf that is not one of the nine, and a book of about two hundred leaves is not in it and about nine leaves with something on them is not in it.

**a second bucket of tar at eight, a pot of tea at three, a third broom at five, a second stone of tallow at five, a third pound of candles at four, a third ream of paper at nine, a fourth night's bed at four, ink at three, a second pound of candles at four, cheese at four, a waxed cord for the bundle at four, a third cheese at four, a broom at five, a second broom at five, a second ink at three, a second farthing at one, a third bucket of tar at eight, a second night's bed at four, a third farthing at one, a pound of candles at four, a ream of paper at nine, a stone of tallow at five, a second cheese at four, a second cake of ink at three, a fourth pound of candles at four, a cake of ink at three, a bucket of tar at eight, a third night's bed at four, a night's bed at four, a farthing at one, a second ream of paper at nine, a second pot of tea at three, and a fifth pound of candles at four.** Eight, three, five, five, four, nine, four, three, four, four, four, four, five, five, three, one, eight, four, one, four, nine, five, four, three, four, three, eight, four, four, one, nine, three and four is a hundred and forty-nine pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and forty-nine pence is three marks and five over**, and nine leaves are not in it, and about nine hundred sheets a month are not in it, and about a hundred and four in one band are not in it, and about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month are not in it, and the three of those are three counts of three different series and are not added to one another and are not derived from one another, and the day's book in a room two hundred miles off is at nine and is not in it, and the count under a fourth step of an outside stair is nine and is not in it either, and the two nines are not the same nine.

**And on the ninth day of Wolfmonth in this year a clerk of about twenty-two said no in nine words to a man of about fifty-eight in a hired room over a saddler's and nobody argued with him, and a man of about fifty-one in a grey coat said out loud what that costs, which is that a room with a thing in it that the room does not have is a room that cannot be asked, and about four people in that room thought the clerk was right and about five thought the chairman was going to do it, and neither of them was thanked, and a cart went up the made road at about the first hour with nine returns in it and the four ruled heads went with it, and the tide decides twice a day and is not paid and is not on any of this.**
