# Chapter 0549

**In a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people, on the sixteenth of Mudmonth, YR 320, a Thursday and a market day, between the second hour and the ninth, a sheet of foolscap with four ruled lines on it went round this market for eight days before anybody knew what was happening with it, and on the eighth day a woman of about forty-four who sells fish came up that lane at about the fifth hour and asked the man of about fifty-eight one question, and he answered it, and about nine people heard the answer, and about four hundred people in that town can now say where a man of thirty-two with a limp sleeps.**

Nobody had done anything. That is the finding of the seventh day and the copyist of about twenty-six wrote it on a bare leaf with no head over it and ruled nothing and did not read it back.

The sheet went into the market on the Thursday in a bundle with nine returns in it and a man in a saddler's shop put it on a board, and on the Saturday a woman who sells fish near the weigh-house asked the man who keeps the board what was on it, and he said it was paper, and she read it standing up, and about four people read it standing up on the Saturday, and by the Tuesday about four hundred people in this town of about two thousand had read a piece of foolscap with four lines on it standing up in a street.

**A finding is worth more than the whole of what anybody wanted and the reason is this and the reason is that a person can carry it. A rule in a room's book is a thing about four people. A sheet of foolscap with four lines on it is a thing a woman who sells fish can carry about a market for eight days and put down in front of a stranger, and about four hundred people have carried it and about nine hundred sheets a month cannot be carried by anybody because they are in a bundle in a cart.**

Nobody in nine counties ordered it. Nobody filled a head on it. Nobody cut a line out of it. The four lines are four lines and the fourth of them has a man's name in it and it is not a fourth head, and the Crown's form on the table by the door has four heads and the fourth of them is empty and has been empty for about five months and nobody in this county has filled it and no office has and nobody in that room did and nobody in nine counties is going to.

**And the woman of about forty-four who sells fish was not thanked and was not made a villain and was not made a symbol and was not made a witness and was not put on anything, and she asked one question and she got an answer and she went back to the market.**

“**Is it true?**”

“**I wrote it,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, “**and I have been in this room four months and about four of the nine people here would tell you that the parts of it that are about this room are the parts I can check, and about five would tell you that the parts of it that are about nine counties are the parts nobody in this room can check, and both of those are true and I am not going to pick one in front of you.**”

“**That is not an answer,**”

“**It is the only answer I have,**” the man of about fifty-eight said. “**I have a chain of office round my neck and it is a badge and it is not a thing that makes a thing true, and I have sat in this room long enough to have worked out what half the people in it say to each other on the stair, which is that a head with a name over it is a thing a clerk is given to fill in, and the other half say that a sheet with a man's name in a line on it is a thing a person can be shown, and I have written about nine hundred returns' worth of paper in eleven years and I have never once had any of it read out loud in a street, and I am not going to pretend to you that this is the same thing as that.**”

**And the man of about fifty-one in a grey coat said one thing about the eight days and about the woman who sells fish, and it is the only thing he said in that room in about four weeks, and he did not say whose plan the inquiry is and did not say who he answers to, and nobody thanked him and he was not thanked and he is not a villain and he is not a knave.**

“**Seven days,**” Gault Prentice said. “**Four hundred people in a market town of about two thousand have read a piece of paper standing up and about nine of them have been in this room and the rest of them have not, and the rest of them do not know there is a room, and there is no head in nine counties for the rest of them and there is not going to be one, and that is the thing I have been building towards for about five months without knowing I was building towards it, and I would like somebody to write that down and I would like it written without a head over it.**”

Nesta Braith took a bare leaf, ruled no head over it, ruled nothing under it, and read none of it back.

Then the woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key asked the one question in that room that nobody had asked and she asked it to the man of about fifty-eight and not to the man in the grey coat.

“**What happens to him?**”

“**What happens to whom?**”

“**The man whose name is in the fourth line,**” she said. “**He is a man a town can find. About four hundred people have said in this market that he is the reason the rest of it stands up and about four hundred have said he is the reason anybody should have believed any of it in the first place, and the two sets do not overlap, and neither of them is in this room, and about four months ago a man of thirty-four with a sheet asked him one question and he said one word, and the word was no, and that was the whole of the answer, and nobody has asked him a second thing since and he has not been in this room for about four weeks and nobody in this room has sent for him.**”

The whole of that room was quiet for about as long as a man takes to count four.

“**I am not going to say what happens to him,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, at about the sixth hour, “**because the honest answer is that I do not know, and because the second honest answer is that a room that says what happens to a man is a room that has started keeping a book of people, and I have written about four leaves with no head over them in five months and I have a rule of my own which is that I do not write anybody's name on one, and a name went into a fourth line this month on a sheet of foolscap and I did not rule it and I am not going to take it out and I am not going to say anything about it in this room.**”

**And nobody thanked him and nobody argued with him and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet wrote all of it on a bare leaf with no head over it and ruled nothing and read nothing back, and the man of about fifty-three with a barrow said one thing and it was about a rail of a common and not about a man.**

“**A woman who sells fish put a piece of paper in front of a man in a street and the man said *I wrote it*, and about four years ago a man in a lane in another county was asked a question about a form and said nothing at all, and both of those men are alive, and I have been walking about nine roads in this county for about nine years and I have never once seen the first thing done and I have seen the second thing done about nine times.**”

**And nobody in that room agreed with him out loud and nobody argued with him out loud, and it is on the page because half of them hold that a man with a barrow is the only person in that room who has ever been on the wrong side of a line, and the other half hold that a man with a barrow is a man who reads pages out loud at a rail, and that is a different thing and neither of them is wrong.**

Aren Kest was not in that room. He had not been in it for about four weeks and nobody in that room said anything about where he was and nobody sent for him and the clerk of about twenty-two wrote the day at the top of a fresh page and did not write the year anywhere on it.

**And about four hundred people in this market town of about two thousand can now say where a man of thirty-two with a limp sleeps, and that is a figure of persons and it is not a figure of anybody in this room, and that room has heard the finding called worth more than the whole of what anybody wanted, and it has heard the finding described as a thing that has put a man's name in a line on a piece of paper that four hundred people have carried about a market, and it did not choose between those two and is not going to, and it did not vote and will not.**

A woman of about fifty-one is at a post on a sand four days off with a piece of driftwood in her hand. Twenty-two notches are cut in the edge of it and the twenty-second is not one of them, the water has not been off that mark in twenty-two years, and what a notch counts there is a year and not a tide. Nobody has been sent to that coast, and about nine hundred of the people on that sand are on no roll and no sheet and no list, while about eleven hundred more are on nothing at all. A shut road four hundred miles off is shut, is not scheduled to be anything else, and nobody was sent toward it, and about nine people are the other side of it.

**And the cost of that Thursday is a column and it is the man of about fifty-one in a grey coat's, and it is the fourth one he has ever kept, and eight days in a market and four hundred people standing up is not in it and a man whose name is in a fourth line is not in it.**

**a bootlace at two, a third broom at seven, a pot of paste at nine, a waxed cord at fourteen, a ream of paper at seventeen, a second pound of candles at nineteen, a sheet of oiled paper at twenty-five, a case of quills at twenty-seven, a pound of beeswax at thirty-five, and a night's bed at thirty-nine.** Two, seven, nine, fourteen, seventeen, nineteen, twenty-five, twenty-seven, thirty-five and thirty-nine is a hundred and ninety-four pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and ninety-four pence is four marks and two over**, and four days is not in it and was not a figure of days in this column and is a figure of days on the page, and eight days is not in it, and about four hundred people standing up in a market is not in it, and about two thousand people in this town is not in it, and a room two hundred miles off sends out about nine hundred sheets a month and about a hundred and four in a band and takes in about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month, and those three are counted separately, are never added to one another here, and are not in this column at either end, and the about four hundred at the end of a made lane four days the other way are not in it, and they are not the about four hundred who read a sheet in this market, and those two are one figure and are not one figure, and go in at neither end of this column, and about nine hundred on a sand four days off is not in it, and the twenty-two notches are years, and those are one number under two names, and neither of the two is a price in it, and a letter with four names still off it is in a coat in that room and is not in this column and is not in any column in nine counties.

The woman of about forty-four who sells fish went back down the lane at about the sixth hour and told about nine people in the market that the man with the badge said he wrote it, and it went round the market from there a good deal faster than she carried it, and by the ninth hour about four people in this town had stopped saying the finding was about roads and had started saying it was about a man, and the man of about fifty-eight heard that at about the ninth hour from the woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key and said out loud that it was going to go on saying that for about four years and that there was nothing in nine counties he could do about it and that he was not going to pretend otherwise.
