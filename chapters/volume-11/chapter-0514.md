# Chapter 0514

**The twenty-first day of Embermonth, YR 319, a Thursday and a market day, and the second hour and the fifth hour and the ninth hour, in a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people four days up a made road from a coast, and a letter came up that lane at about the second hour that had been sent nineteen days earlier from a room over a weigh-house with a door on a lane about two hundred miles off, and the letter is one page and it does not answer the question that was put to it on the twenty-ninth day of a month carried at thirty.**

## One

**The question went up the made road on the second day of Embermonth and it was put in writing and it was four sentences long and the last of the four was about nine miles long, and the answer came back on a Monday with the cart and it is one page and it is about four words long, and about nine people in that room understood in about four minutes that nothing had come back.**

There are four names at the top of a question in that room, written in a hand, and a sentence under them offering to send them to nine counties, and a man of about fifty-one in a grey coat who said out loud that the offering was worse than the thing he had offered before. **Aren Kest was in that room at the fifth hour and read the three sentences twice and was not asked one question and did not ask one, and about four of the nine people in that room have it that he read it twice on purpose and about five have said that he read it twice because he could not sleep, and nobody has asked him which of the two it was.**

**And the page that came back is not a refusal and it is not an answer and it is not a rule, and it does not say no, and the room has been in the same state about it for about nineteen days and has not been in it before about anything.**

## Two

**The page has three sentences on it and nothing else on it, and the copyist of about forty-four, who has been copying for about nineteen years and who has not been asked to copy anything since the second hour on a day in Harvestmonth, was asked to copy it at about the fifth hour, and she read the three sentences out loud before she wrote any of them down, and she is the only person in that room who has read them twice.**

“**A place is not a road until somebody has been asked to call it one, and the asking is the road going on paper, and it cannot be taken back off the paper, and nobody who has not been asked has agreed to it. A figure is not a figure of people until somebody has said which hour it was counted at, and a figure of people that does not name an hour is a figure of a room. A road that can complain to a person is a road that has been found, and the finding is the thing the road was trying to avoid.**”

Nobody in that room said anything at all for about as long as a man takes to count four.

**And that is one hundred and six words in three sentences in one turn of one exchange, printed as one span, and it is the whole of what came back down that made road in nineteen days, and it does not mention the four names, and it does not mention the fourth head, and it does not say whether the two sets of four heads are a standing arrangement or a thing that happened twice, and nobody in nine counties has asked her and she has not offered.**

## Three

“**Then what do I tell the four?**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**That they are still not written down,**” the woman of about thirty-four said.

“**That is what I said to them in a letter two months ago and it is the same sentence with the months changed,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, “**and I have now sent a question up a made road and had three sentences come back that do not answer it, and I am going to have to say something to a room in about four days, and the something is going to be about four sentences long because that is how long the questions are.**”

“**Then say four sentences about the three,**” the man of about thirty-four said, “**and not one of them is about the four names, and the four names will stay off every sheet in this county for as long as nobody in nine counties puts them on one.**”

“**And if somebody puts them on one?**”

“**Then a county has done it,**” the man of about thirty-four said, “**and a county is not a man, and you have already had that conversation in this room once and it took nine turns and it was the correctest thing anybody said in nine weeks.**”

**And that is one hundred and eighty-two words in six turns of one exchange, of which the first is seven, the second is seven, the third is eighty printed in two spans of twenty-three and fifty-seven, the fourth is forty-one printed in two spans of seven and thirty-four, the fifth is seven, and the sixth is forty printed in two spans of six and thirty-four, and a woman of about thirty-four said a sentence in that room that she had said in it before, and nobody noticed except about four people, and about five did not, and the man of about fifty-eight has four days and has not used any of them.**

## Four

“**A letter is a form that walks,**” the man of about thirty-eight with a satchel said, at about the ninth hour, to the page and not to anybody in the room.

Nobody asked him what he meant and about four people in that room worked it out in about nine seconds and about five have said that it took them until the Friday and have stopped saying that they had it in nine seconds.

“**It goes out and it comes back,**” he said, “**and it says at the bottom of most of them that nobody is to be sent to find out, and this one came back having been walked about two hundred miles by about nine different men who did not know what was in it and could have read it in about four minutes in a kitchen, and none of them did, and that is not because of the ink. The reason none of them did is that it was folded once and it was in a bundle with nine returns and a bundle is a kind of wall.**”

“**Then the wall is the answer,**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**The wall is about half of it,**” the man of about thirty-eight with a satchel said, “**and the other half is that a form does not stop being a form when it stops moving.**”

**And that is one hundred and forty-three words in four turns of one exchange, of which the first is seven, the second is one hundred and five printed in two spans of seven and ninety-eight, the third is six, and the fourth is twenty-five printed in two spans of seven and eighteen, and a man of about thirty-eight with a satchel said in a hired room that a wall is about half of the answer and a man of about fifty-eight said that the wall is the answer, and the room went on working after that until about the tenth hour and the two of them did not settle it and did not try to.**

## Five

The cost of that Monday is a column and it is the keeper of a room over a weigh-house with a door on a lane about two hundred miles off, and it is the eighth one she has kept in four and a half years and it is in the same book as the seventh, and this room did not see it and does not know what is in the book and is not going to be told.

**a second bootlace at one, a second pot of tea at three, a cord at two, a cake of ink at three, paper at nine, a bootlace at one, a knife at two, a second broom at five, a pound of candles at four, a straightedge at two, a second bucket of tar at eight, a fourth night's bed at four, a second paper at nine, a night's bed at four, a candle at two, a broom at five, a pot of tea at three, a third night's bed at four, a rider's day at three, cheese at four, a second knife at two, a second rider's day at three, a rule of brass at four, a second cord at two, a second night's bed at four, a bucket of tar at eight, a second candle at two, a second cheese at four, a second pot for the paste at five, and a third rider's day at three.** One, three, two, three, nine, one, two, five, four, two, eight, four, nine, four, two, five, three, four, three, four, two, three, four, two, four, eight, two, four, five and three is a hundred and fifteen pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and fifteen pence is two marks and nineteen over**, and three sentences are not in it, and about two hundred miles is not in it and is a distance and not a figure of persons, of money, of days or of sheets, and nineteen days are not in it, and about nine hundred sheets a month and about a hundred and four in one band and about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month are three counts of three different series and are not in it and are not added to one another.

**And on the twenty-first day of Embermonth in this year a page came up a made road after nineteen days and put three sentences on a table in a hired room over a saddler's, and the three sentences did not answer the question and were not a refusal, and about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month still come in at a room two hundred miles off and about nine hundred sheets a month still go out of it and about a hundred and four still go out in one band, and the fourth head on the outgoing sheet of that room is still ruled and still empty and has been for four and a half years, and nobody in nine counties has been told and nobody was thanked.**
