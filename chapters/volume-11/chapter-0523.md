# Chapter 0523

**The twenty-first of Wolfmonth, YR 319, a Saturday and a market day, and the first hour and the fifth hour and the tenth hour, in a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people, and a woman of about sixty-three who has been in nine counties asking about forms since the third year she kept an inn came up the lane with a piece of paper in a flat box, and about nine people were in that room at the fifth hour, and the paper is not a Crown form and is not off a press in this town.**

The box is a deal one about two feet long and the paper inside it is a single leaf folded in three and it is not a return, because it has no heads cut into it and no lines ruled under them and no words cut into wood anywhere on it, and it is about the size of a hand.

Nobody in that room knew what it was for about four minutes, and the woman of about sixty-three sat down before she said anything at all and put it on the table face up and did not open the third fold.

“**I have had that in a flat box in a house in this town for about eleven months,**” she said, “**and I have taken it out four times and I have shown it to one person and that person was a lawyer and he said he did not know what it was either and he charged me a shilling and told me nothing, and I am not a woman who keeps boxes of paper and I kept it because my mother kept it, and I am telling you that first because you are going to write my name against it in about a minute and I want the shilling on the same line as my name and not the other way round.**”

“**What is it?**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**Open the third fold,**” the woman of about sixty-three said.

The man of about fifty-eight opened the third fold and read it and put it down and did not say anything and read it again, and the room watched him read it twice, and he went through it fast the first time and slow the second time, and one of the two people who had a view about it has said the difference was in his eyes and the other has said there was no difference at all and the difference was in the room.

Then he pushed it across the table to the woman of about forty-four who has been copying for about nineteen years and said three words.

“**Read it out.**”

She read it out. It is nine words and the man of about fifty-eight had read it twice already and about nine people in that room heard it once and a man of about thirty-four with a sheet heard it and wrote it down, and the copyist of about twenty-six wrote it down too and the two of them wrote the same nine words in the same order and neither of them looked at the other.

*Nothing shuts but two who did not propose it.*

Nobody in that room said anything for about as long as a woman takes to count four.

“**That is a refusal,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, and his hand had gone flat on the table and stayed there, and he said the next part of it to the table and not to the woman of about sixty-three. “**And a refusal is the oldest thing anybody in nine counties can put a date to, because it is the only kind of writing that is older than every other kind. A paper that asks a question can be made by anybody who wants something answered. A paper that refuses is a thing a person was made to write, or a thing a person had to write to keep their own hands where they were. That is on a piece of paper and it is the only piece of paper in nine counties that anybody can call old, and I am not going to say the word for it in this room until somebody has to decide something.**”

“**You are going to have to say it,**” the woman of about thirty-four said. “**There is a man of about fifty-three with a barrow standing at the back of that room who has a form in his hand with a fifth line on it and a stroke through the fifth line, and he has come up a lane twice to show it to you, and in about four days there is going to be a man in this town who comes up that lane and asks what the stroke means, and you will not be here and I will be here, and I am not going to stand in front of that form and say a thing I do not understand.**”

The man of about fifty-eight looked at her for about as long as it takes a man to say four.

“**Then I will say it,**” he said, “**and I want it said by me and not by her and not by the man with the barrow, and I want the room to hear it in the order it is going to be read in a book, and the order is the nine words first and the name second, and the name is one word and it is not a person and it is not an office and it is not in any form in nine counties.**”

And he said it. He said the nine words again first, out loud, the whole nine, and then he said the one word, and the room heard it once, and a man of about thirty-four with a sheet wrote it down and a copyist of about twenty-six wrote it down and the woman of about sixty-three who brought the paper in a flat box did not write it down at all and put her two hands flat on the box.

*The Compact.*

**That is the word. It is printed once in this room and it is printed once on the page, and the man of about fifty-eight said it once and did not say it again that day, and the copyist of about twenty-six wrote it once, put the leaf face down under a second leaf, and has not looked at it again.**

Nobody in that room thanked anybody and nobody in that room was thanked, and the woman of about sixty-three said out loud that she was not going to be thanked and would rather be asked, and nobody asked her, and the man of about fifty-eight said that he was not going to have the room settle anything about the word on that Saturday, and that anybody who wanted to say what that word was to nine counties could say it in his own county and not in this one.

**Aren Kest, a man of thirty-two with a limp, was at the back of that room against the wall by the one window that is open, and he had come in at about the fifth hour and had not said a word all afternoon, and the copyist of about twenty-six has said that she was about nine feet from him and that neither of them looked at the other and that both of them were listening to the same thing, and that a man who is listening to a word he has heard before and a woman who is listening to a word she has not heard before are not in the same room, and that neither of them was in the wrong one.**

**And nobody in that room agrees about whether printing it was right.**

About four of the nine people in that room think it is a rule. They think the oldest thing in the works is a refusal and that a refusal is a rule and that the rule says a thing may not be closed unless two people who are not the person proposing it are present, and that a paper with a stroke through a line and a paper with nine words on it in the same hand are the same piece of paper seen from two ends. About five think it is a sentence somebody liked the sound of. They think nine words in a hand nobody can date is a thing a person wrote because it pleased him on an afternoon in a house in this town, and that the man of about fifty-eight has wanted a rule since the first hour he came into this room and has found one he can hold.

The two sets do not overlap at all and neither of the two is wrong and the room did not vote and the man of about fifty-eight said out loud that a vote would have been a finding and there has not been a finding in this room in four months and there is not going to be one on a Saturday.

**And two of them did something about it, and it is the reason that Saturday was not only a Saturday, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet folded the paper in three again, put it in the flat box, and asked the woman of about sixty-three for the box for about four minutes so that he could write the nine words on the inside of the lid, and she gave him the box and did not ask what he was writing and has not asked since. The copyist of about twenty-six got up and went to the one window that is open in that room and stood at it for about as long as a man takes to say nine and then came back and sat down, and nobody followed her and nobody said anything to her about it, and the two of them said the same thing on that Saturday by not saying it, and neither of them was thanked.**

**And the cost of that Saturday is a column and it is the man of about fifty-eight's, and it is the second one he has kept in this room, and a word said out loud once in a room of about nine people is not in it.**

**a box of quills at twenty-three, a night's straw at nineteen, a broom at eight, a cake of ink at seventeen, a rule of brass at thirteen, a ream of paper at eleven, a pot of tea at five, and a jar of paste at fifteen.** Twenty-three, nineteen, eight, seventeen, thirteen, eleven, five and fifteen is a hundred and eleven pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and eleven pence is two marks and fifteen over**, and a word said out loud once is not in it, and nine words are not in it, and a flat box about two feet long is not in it, and two people who are not the person proposing something are not in it and were never counted by anybody in nine counties, and about nine hundred sheets a month, about a hundred and four in one band, about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month: three series, three counts, none of them derived from another, none of them in the column, and none of them added to any other.

The box went home down the lane with the woman of about sixty-three at about the tenth hour, and the man of about fifty-eight locked the room and walked to the rail of a common and back and did not say the word again, and the copyist of about twenty-six put the leaf face down under a second leaf and has not looked at it.
