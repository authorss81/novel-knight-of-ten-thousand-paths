# Chapter 0524

**The twenty-fifth of Wolfmonth, YR 319, a Wednesday and not a market day, and the second hour and the sixth hour and the ninth hour, in a hired room over a saddler's, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet had the two sheets in front of him side by side and had put a third thing on the table between them, and the third thing is a strip of paper about two inches wide with a sentence on it in a hand of about a hundred and forty years ago, and the sentence is nine words long and it is a selection.**

It is not a copy. It is a strip, cut along one edge with something blunt, and the cut has gone into the paper a little at one end and not at the other, which is what happens when a man cuts a strip with a pair of scissors that have one blade shorter than the other and has not had them looked at since the day they were bought.

“**That came off the front of the second copy,**” the man of about thirty-four said. “**I took it off on the Saturday after the word was said, in about four minutes, with a knife, and I did not ask anybody, and I am telling the room before the room asks me, because if I do not say it now then everything else anybody says in here this morning is said over the top of a thing I did.**”

Nobody in that room said anything at all for about as long as a man takes to say four.

“**Say the rest of it,**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**The rest of it is that the strip was folded in with the copy and not pasted on,**” the man of about thirty-four said, “**and that whoever folded it in folded it in the wrong way up, and that when I unfolded it this morning I read it and it took me about as long to read as it takes a man to say four, and I have been sitting in this room for about two hours with it on the table in front of me.**”

**And the sentence on the strip is nine words and it is shorter than the sentence on the paper the woman of about sixty-three brought in a flat box, and it is the sentence that has been going out into nine counties ever since.**

*A thing once asked is a thing twice agreed.*

“**It is shorter,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, and he had not read it twice, and he said that as though it were a fact about the length of a strip of paper and not about anything else. “**It is a sentence about a thing being asked and a thing being agreed and it is nine words and it is the shape of a thing that can be carried out by anybody. And the other one is a sentence about a thing not being closed, and the other one cannot be carried out by anybody, and the other one wants two people who are not the person proposing, and a man who has cut a head out of a form with a knife in one county of nine does not have two people who are not the person proposing anything, because the head was out before anybody came to the counter.**”

The man of about thirty-four said that he had come up that lane on the Monday with a question and had got a different question, and that he was not complaining, and that the question he had come with was whether a fifth line was a rule, and the question he had got was whether a rule can be one sentence long and still be a rule, and that the two questions are not the same and that he had put the first one down in the room in Fallowmonth and it was not answered then and it was not answered now.

**Nobody in that room can say who made the second cut.**

“**Then we are where we were,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, “**in the room, with a strip of paper and a form, and no name on either.**”

“**We are not where we were,**” the man of about thirty-four said, “**because in Fallowmonth we had a line and now we have a line and a sentence, and a line with a stroke through it is a question somebody asked, and a sentence underneath it is an answer somebody wrote, and the answer is the thing that has been going out into nine counties for about a hundred and forty years, and a stroke through the fifth line is the only part of that paper that anybody can still be got to swear to.**”

“**A county is not a man,**” the woman of about thirty-four said, and about four of the nine people in that room said it after her, out loud, inside about nine seconds, and it is the second time in four months that sentence has been said in that room and it was as correct the second time as it was the first.

“**And the man who made it is dead,**” the copyist of about twenty-six said, from the side table, without getting up and without being asked, and it is the third time anybody in that room has said a thing she was not asked for and it is the first time she has said one about a man who is not in the room. “**He is dead and he was not a keeper and he is not a villain and he had about nine years in a county office in the middle of the last century, and none of those four things is a name, and the room is going to have to do without a name.**”

“**How do you know he is dead?**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**Because the hand is about a hundred and forty years old,**” Nesta Braith said, “**and because whoever wrote the answer wrote it as a thing that needed no name on it, and a man who needs no name on a thing is a man who has never in his life been asked for one.**”

Nobody argued with her and nobody thanked her and the man of about fifty-eight wrote the four things down on a leaf with no head over it — *dead, not a keeper, not a villain, no name* — and the leaf has no head on it and the four things are not a finding and he said so out loud at the ninth hour.

**Aren Kest, a man of thirty-two with a limp, was in that room from the second hour to the ninth and said four sentences, and none of the four was about the strip, and the copyist of about twenty-six wrote one of them down and left the other three where they were, and has not said which three.**

“**A man who puts a stroke through a line and does not sign it has done one of about four things,**” Aren Kest said, “**and the four are: he did not want the question asked, he did not want it asked badly, he was told to go through the line, and he was frightened. The first two are the ones in that room this morning and I cannot get from here to the other two and neither can anybody in it.**”

“**Then what is the finding?**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**There is no finding,**” Aren Kest said. “**There is a strip of paper and four reasons and about nine people in a room and one of the reasons belongs to a man who is dead and one of the reasons belongs to a man in a county two hundred miles off who has never heard of this room, and a room that has four reasons and about nine people in it is a room that is going to be asked in about four years to say which one it had believed, and the answer at that point will be whichever one the asker likes.**”

Nobody in that room agreed with him and nobody in that room argued with him and the man of about fifty-eight said that he was going to write down the four reasons on a second leaf with no head over it and that the second leaf was going to stay face down, and the copyist of about twenty-six said that a thing written on a face-down leaf is a thing that has been written down and hidden by the same person in the same hand, and that there is no head in nine counties for that, and he said he knew.

**And a selection is a refusal with the two witnesses taken out, and a thing with two witnesses taken out is a thing one person can do alone on a Tuesday.**

The man of about fifty-three with a barrow said that at about the sixth hour and said it to the strip and not to anybody, and then said the second half of it, and about nine people in that room heard both halves and the copyist of about twenty-six wrote the second half down because the first half was the kind of thing that is heard once and then argued with instead of written.

“**A man who wanted to close a thing could not close it with that strip in front of him and the two witnesses in the room,**” the man of about fifty-three said, “**and a man who wanted to close a thing can close it with my strip in his own hand on a Tuesday, in a room with nobody else in it, and he does not have to want it twice, and the paper on the wall of every room in nine counties does not have a head that says two, and if it did he would go and find a Tuesday.**”

**And the strip went back into the flat box, and the flat box went home with the woman of about sixty-three, and the second copy of the copy of the sheet stayed on the table by the door, and the man of about fifty-eight with the chain of office round his neck said that it was going into the Monday bundle in a fold of its own with nothing written on the outside of it, and the copyist of about twenty-six said she was not going to write a head over it and he said he was not going to ask her to, and neither of them said the word again.**

**And the cost of that Wednesday is a column and it is the man of about thirty-four with a sheet's, and it is the eleventh one he has kept in about nine years, and a strip of paper about two inches wide with a sentence on it is not in it.**

**a cheese at five, a cake of ink at seventeen, a broom at nine, a ream of paper at twenty-one, a stone of tallow at six, a rule of brass at thirteen, a pot of paste at fifteen, a night's bed at eleven, a pound of candles at eight, a bun at five, and a bundle of kindling at seven.** Five, seventeen, nine, twenty-one, six, thirteen, fifteen, eleven, eight, five and seven is a hundred and seventeen pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and seventeen pence is two marks and twenty-one over**, and two inches is not in it, nor the nine words, nor the hundred and forty years, nor the nine years a man spent in one county office, nor any Tuesday, and the four hundred who live at that lane's far end and the nine hundred on that sand are two separate counts and the column holds neither and makes no sum of them, and a fifth line is not in it and is not a fourth head and is not a person, a sum, a period or an inch.
