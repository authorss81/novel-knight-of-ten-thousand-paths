# Chapter 0540

**The first of Thawmonth, YR 320, a Monday and not a market day, and the first hour and the fourth hour and the ninth, in a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people, and the year turned on that day and nobody in that room said so, and the cart went up the made road at the ninth hour with a bundle on it and about nine returns in the bundle.**

The clerk of about twenty-two wrote the day at the top of a fresh page in his own book, the way he has written the day at the top of a page since the twenty-fifth of Harvestmonth, and he did not write the year anywhere on it, and the year had turned on that morning without anybody in that room noticing, and there is no head in nine counties for the year either.

About five people were in the room at the first hour. The man of about fifty-eight was at the head of the table. The woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key was at the key end. The copyist of about twenty-six was at the side table with the straightedge. The clerk of about twenty-two was at the near end with his own book open. The man of about thirty-four with a sheet was at the press-corner with the bundle.

That is all, and that is how it has been for about four months: a room that is a room, a chair with a badge on the man in it, a woman at a key end who keeps the key, a woman with a knife, a clerk with a book of about two hundred leaves of which about nine are written on, and a man with a sheet who asks for nothing.

Aren Kest was not in that room and had not been in it for eleven days and had not sent anything and had not come up the lane, and nobody in that room said anything about where he was or whether he was all right, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet said after the others had gone that he had been asked by two people at the rail of a common whether the man was all right, and that he had said he did not know and had not offered to find out, and that a man who says he does not know about a thing he can walk a mile and a half to ask about is a man who has understood something about being found, and that he was not going to say it out loud in the room because the room has a rule about what it is for.

**The record is in public.** It has been read out loud once, in an upper room over a tallow chandler's, to about four hundred people, and a copy of its first page has gone out on the board in a saddler’s shop and down the made road since, and the man in that shop has never been told what is on the copy and nobody has sent anybody to tell him. It is four pages and a head, and the head has a name in it that the man wrote himself, and it took him longer than a man with a good hand would have needed. It is true. It is complete in the only sense a man can judge, which is against his own knowledge, and the last line of the fourth page says that he does not know five things and is not going to be asked about them in a room by anybody with a badge, and that is the last line, and it is a list and it is not a flourish.

At about the fourth hour the man of about fifty-eight put his pen down and said the thing that had to be said in a room that first day of a month, and he said it to about five people instead of about nine.

“**The hearing stands,**” he said. “**It is not adjourned and it is not refused and it is not retold and it is not closed, and it is going to be said in this room on the first day of every month until somebody with a reason stops saying it. A thing said out loud to five people on the first day of a month is worth exactly as much as a thing said out loud to nine, and the difference is four, and I have spent eleven years in this county learning to add people to a room and I have never once learned to do the other thing.**”

Nobody in that room said anything, and the copyist of about twenty-six wrote what he said on a bare leaf with no head over it and did not rule anything over it, and did not read it back, and turned it over, and nobody asked her what was on the other side, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet said that was the correct arrangement and that he was not going to improve on it.

**And the hearing stands, and it was said out loud, and nobody in that room agreed with it and nobody in that room argued with it.**

**The fourth head of the Crown’s form on the table by that door is empty.** It has been empty for about four months and it is empty this morning, and nobody in nine counties has filled it and no office has filled it. The word in a fourth head on the coast’s own sheet is a word in a different county on a different document cut with a knife out of a flat stone, and the two of them have not been in one room since the twenty-fourth of Frostmonth, and on the day they were it was said out loud in front of nine people that they are two documents in two counties and that nobody in that room had the right to say which of the two was older.

**The three words for one lane are still three.** The Green Lane at the rail of a common, used by about four people who live at the end of it. The Long Lane at the eleventh of eleven houses, used by six, and said by a woman of about twenty-seven who is thanked by nobody and is on no sheet. The Chapel Way, given by a man of about seventy in the fourth house and carried on a copy of a return whose first head a person in a kitchen filled in with a pen and a borrowed straightedge in about nine minutes. Nobody was asked which of the three is the true one, nothing has been scheduled about choosing, the room has not adopted one, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet wrote down what the woman of about twenty-seven said at the eleventh of those eleven houses on a bare leaf on that Saturday and read none of it back to anybody.

**A shut road four hundred miles off is not reopened, and is not scheduled, and nobody was sent.** About nine people are on the wrong side of it and not one of them has ever come into this room, and nobody in that room got up on the first day of a new year and said one word about them. The man of about fifty-eight wrote in the room’s book at the fourth hour that nothing in the inquiry this month concerned any road that is shut, and that a line in a book saying so is not a finding and is not a rule, and that nothing has been cut into the wood over it, and that he had decided to leave it unruled — and would hear from anybody in that room why that was cowardice before the month was out.

**And a coast four days off from this town has eleven houses along two miles and about nine hundred paces of hard sand and about nine hundred people on it, and about eleven hundred people on no roll and no sheet and no list, and a woman of about fifty-one at a post at the seaward end of that sand with twenty-two notches cut in the edge of a piece of driftwood. The twenty-second is not cut. The water has not been off that mark in twenty-two years, and the notches are years and are not tides, and nobody has been sent to that coast by anybody in this county or out of it, and she has not been asked and has not been thanked and has not been written to.**

**And the cost of that Monday is a column and it is the clerk of about twenty-two’s, and it is the third one he has ever kept, and a year is not in it.**

**a bound book at forty-one, a pot of tea at five, a candle at six, a bundle of kindling at twenty, a sheet of blotting paper at thirteen, a knife at twenty-one, a night’s straw at thirty-one, a bootlace at two, a bit of chalk at three, a bowl of soup at fourteen, a sheet of oiled paper at twenty-five, and a farthing at one.** Forty-one, five, six, twenty, thirteen, twenty-one, thirty-one, two, three, fourteen, twenty-five and one is a hundred and eighty-two pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and eighty-two pence is three marks and thirty-eight over**, and the year is not in it, and the day at the top of the page is not in it, and the four pages are not in it, and the five things nobody will be asked about are not in it, and the two names in the back of a book of carts are not in it, and about nine returns in the bundle are not in it, and about nine people in this room in Hearthmonth are not in it and about five people in it this morning are not in it either, and those two are not added, and about four hundred at the end of a made lane are not in it, and about two thousand in this town are not in it, and about nine hundred sheets a month out of a room two hundred miles off are not in it, and about nine hundred on that sand are not in it, and none of those is derived from any of the others.

He shut the book of about two hundred leaves and put it inside his coat at about the ninth hour, and the bundle went down to the cart and up the made road, and the man of about fifty-eight locked the room over the saddler’s, and the woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key turned the key and went down the stairs, and the year was Thawmonth and it was the first and nobody in that room wrote it down.
