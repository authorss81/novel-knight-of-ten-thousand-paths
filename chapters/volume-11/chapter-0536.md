# Chapter 0536

**On the fourth of Goatmonth, YR 319, a Tuesday and not a market day, at about the fourth hour in a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people, a man of about thirty-eight with a satchel asked a question he had asked in that room in Frostmonth, and this time somebody in the room tried to answer it, and the answer was wrong, and he did not argue with it.**

He had said in Frostmonth that if the answer was that nobody knew then the answer was that nobody knew and he would carry that down the road instead of a rule and it would do the same work. He had been up and down that lane in about nine years and nobody in this room had argued with him then and nobody argued with him now.

“**Is a rule still a rule when one man changed it in a hand?**”

He said it at about the fourth hour and he said it in front of the other eight and he put it the same way, flat, with the satchel between his knees.

And a woman answered it, which is a thing that had not happened in that room in four months.

“**It is,**” the woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key said. “**And it is a better rule than the one we had, and here is why: a rule that a man can put his own name under is a rule that can be checked. Every rule anybody in nine counties has got has got nobody's name on it. That is what has been wrong with all of them since the day the four heads were cut. I have watched that room over the weigh-house send about nine hundred sheets a month into nine counties for four and a half years with a die on them and not one clerk in nine counties able to say whose die it was or who was meant to answer it, and you cannot check a thing that nobody can be shown. He can be shown. That is the whole difference and it is a real one.**”

Nobody in that room argued with her for about as long as a man takes to count nine.

Then the man of about thirty-four with a sheet said why she was wrong, and he said it in three sentences and he did not raise his voice, and he was right, and about four of the nine people in that room agreed with him and about five did not, and the two sets did not overlap, and the room did not vote.

“**A rule binds people who did not agree to it, and this binds one man, and one man is not a rule, he is a witness. And a rule that can be checked by one man's memory stops being checkable the day he dies, and on the day after that the only thing anybody has is a piece of paper that a man wrote about himself and that nobody else has ever read out loud. And the moment you call it a rule you have to have somebody to apply it, and there is exactly one person in nine counties who can apply that one, and he is the man it is about, and he has not said in this room that he will do it.**”

Nobody in that room agreed with him out loud. Nobody in that room argued with her out loud either. The copyist of about twenty-six wrote both of them on the same bare leaf with no head over it and ruled nothing and put the leaf face down, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet said he had seen her do it and that he was not going to say what was on the other side of it, and she said that was the correct thing to say.

The man of about thirty-eight with a satchel did not argue with either of them. That is the thing that was different about that Tuesday and it is the whole of what he did.

“**In Frostmonth I asked for a rule and there was not one,**” he said, “**and I said I would carry the other answer down the road instead and that it would do the same work. It did. I have been down nine counties since Frostmonth and every road office in every one of them has a sheet with four heads on it and not one of them can tell me whose die cut the heads, and every clerk in nine of those rooms said some version of *it is the form we have always used*, and not one of them said it in a way I could write down and take to a magistrate. So I did not carry a rule down the road. I carried nobody knowing, and it did the same work.**”

“**And now?**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**And now I will carry this one down the road instead,**” the man of about thirty-eight with a satchel said, “**and what I will carry is a room in which a man of thirty-two read out four pages about himself in front of about four hundred people, and a woman said a rule that can be checked is a better rule, and a man said a witness is not a rule, and neither of them was thanked, and both of them were right, and the woman was wrong. I will not tell them which was which. I will tell them what was said, in the order it was said in, and that is what a man in a satchel is for.**”

He said that last part as though it were a piece of information about the weather, and nobody thanked him for it and he did not ask to be thanked, and the man of about fifty-eight said out loud that the room was not going to rule on the woman of about thirty-four's answer and was not going to rule on the man of about thirty-four's either, and that a room which has two sets in it has not got a finding, it has got a room.

Then the man of about fifty-three with a barrow did a thing at about the seventh hour that was a consequence of which of the two sets he was in, and nobody asked him which set it was and he did not say.

He asked the man of about thirty-four with a sheet to write the first page out for him in his own hand, on a bare leaf, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet wrote it out and gave it to him without asking what it was for, and the man of about fifty-three with a barrow folded it into four and put it inside his coat against his chest and said that there is a rail of a common about a mile and a half from that room with about four people standing at it on a market morning who have never in their lives been asked anything by anybody, and that he is going to stand there on the Thursday and read one page out loud, and that nobody is to come with him.

Nobody in that room agreed with that and nobody argued with it, and the woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key said that if he read it out at a rail then the rail would have a version, and that a version is a thing that cannot be got back into a page, and the man of about fifty-three with a barrow said that a page that cannot be read out loud is a page about four people, and that is fine for a page about four people and is not fine for that one.

Aren Kest came into that room at about the seventh hour and stood at the end of the table through the last of it with his stick in his right hand, and nobody said anything to him and nobody asked him anything and nobody made room for him and a man of about thirty-four with a sheet moved a leaf out of the way so that the press-corner was not shut, and he stayed until about the eighth hour and then went out and down the lane, and nobody in that room has said since why he came in at the seventh hour rather than the first, and he was not asked.

**And the cost of that Tuesday is a column and it is the man of about thirty-eight with a satchel’s, and it is the thirteenth one he has kept in about nine years, and a question that got an answer when nobody expected one is not in it.**

**a satchel strap at twenty-one, a night’s bed at twenty-nine, a pot of tea at fourteen, a waxed cord at seven, a cake of ink at twenty-three, a sheet of oiled paper at nineteen, a bridle buckle at seventeen, a farthing at one, a half loaf at two, a thimble at six, and a candle at eleven.** Twenty-one, twenty-nine, fourteen, seven, twenty-three, nineteen, seventeen, one, two, six and eleven is a hundred and fifty pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and fifty pence is three marks and six over**, and the four pages are not in it, and the two sentences are not in it, and the leaf with both of them face down on it is not in it, and the about four hundred in an upper room over a tallow chandler's on the twenty-ninth of Hearthmonth are not in it, and about four hundred at the end of a made lane are not in it, and the two of those two are the same figure and are not the same figure and go in at neither end of this column, and about nine people in that room are not in it, and about two thousand in this town are not in it, and about nine hundred sheets a month in a room two hundred miles off are not in it.

The man of about thirty-eight with a satchel went down the lane at about the eighth hour and stopped at the door and turned round and said one more thing to the room, and it was not about the record and it was not about the rule, and it was that he had come up that lane for about nine years on the business of a man who could see his face and not his voice, and that he had got the voice on the Thursday, and that he was not going to be told what to do with it.
