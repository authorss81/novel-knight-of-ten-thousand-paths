# Chapter 0547

**In a county of about nine thousand people, in a room over a weigh-house with a door on a lane about two hundred miles up a made road from a coast, on the second of Mudmonth, YR 320, a Thursday and a market day, from the second hour to the seventh, four lines came up that lane on the Monday in a bundle with a return on top of them, and the four lines are a question that nobody in nine counties asked, and the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps that room read them twice and put them face down and did not answer them and has not answered them.**

The four lines are these, and they are in a hand that is not one person's, and they were written by somebody who put four heads and four lines under them on a piece of paper the size of a return and left it on a flat stone at the landward end of a coast four days off, and about four days and about nine more got it up a made road to a market town four days off the other way, and about nineteen days and about nine more brought a copy of it up a made road to this lane, and nobody has signed it and nobody has ever written down who wrote it.

*You have nine hundred and about four trades and one word. Who is in the room when you answer? Nobody asked us. We are asking you.*

**The room did not answer it, and the reason the room did not answer it is on the page and the reason is not that the question is bad, and the question is a good question and the man of about fifty-eight in a town four days off said so out loud in a room in Frostmonth and said it in the voice of a man who is going to have to defend it in about four months.**

The woman of about twenty-nine said one thing about it at about the fourth hour and it was the same thing she has said about it every time it has come up a lane, and she did not say it twice and she was not thanked for saying it.

“**It is addressed to whoever opens it, and in nine counties the answer to a question like that is whoever is nearest, and I have got a press standing open and a door on a lane and I am the nearest, and if I answer it then the answer is a woman in a room two hundred miles off from the sand it was written on, and a woman in a room is a person and a person can be found, and the sand has about nine hundred people on it and about eleven hundred more on no roll and no sheet and no list, and I have not answered it and I am not going to.**”

Nobody in that room agreed with her out loud and nobody argued with her out loud, and the woman of about thirty-one with a pen copied the four lines out for the fourth time in a hand that she said out loud had decided before she did, and she said that again and nobody asked her a second time.

**And then the two of them had an exchange at about the fifth hour and it is on the page because a figure of a person standing four lines from another figure of a person is the defect no instrument in this project owns, and a woman of about twenty-nine and a woman of about thirty-one in one exchange is exactly that, and the room is going to say so about itself and nobody in it is going to be able to do anything about it.**

It went like this. The woman of about thirty-one with a pen had the sheet of foolscap on the table with the four heads on it and the fourteen words under the fourth one, and she asked a question about the copying of a figure under a second head, and the woman of about twenty-nine answered it, and in the answering a figure came out of one of them and a figure of a person came out of the other, and the two of them were four lines apart on the page and neither of them was a figure of a person who could be counted by anybody in that room.

“**I have got a figure of nine hundred on three returns from a sand four days off,**” the woman of about thirty-one with a pen said, “**and the figure is the same on all three and I have never seen anybody count the nine hundred and I am not going to.**”

“**I have got about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month coming into this room,**” the woman of about twenty-nine said, “**and none of them has ever asked this room a question and every one of them has a head over it and about four hundred of them are the same head.**”

**And that is a figure of nine hundred standing four lines from a figure of four hundred and nine, and they are not one figure and they are not a sum and neither of them is a figure of persons, and the copyist of about twenty-nine did not put them together and the woman of about thirty-one with a pen did not put them together and the two of them have never in four and a half years put any of the three series of that room together, and neither of them has said so out loud today and neither of them is going to.**

The woman of about thirty-one with a pen said the thing that came out of the exchange and it took about as long as a woman takes to say four.

“**A form does not ask a place its name. It asks a person, and the answer it gets is the name the person has, and a place that has three names has three people, and that is not a fault in the form, that is what a form is, and the fault is that nine counties of forms were cut with a head that asks for one and there is no head in any of the nine that asks how many.**”

Nobody in that room said anything and the press stood open and nobody shut it and nobody dated it.

Then Aren Kest, a man of thirty-two with a limp, who had come up the lane on the Wednesday and was in the room from the second hour, said the finding and he said it in about four sentences and he said it to the four lines and not to anybody, and nobody wrote it down, and it is on the page because he said it out loud in a room with about nine people in it and about four of them have it since that he was right and about five have said that a man who is in nine counties for about four years is a man who has an answer before the question, and the two sets do not overlap.

“**A road is not a place with a figure of people in it. A road is a thing a person uses and describes, and the description is made by the person who is standing on it, and the reason a return asks for a place and a figure of people and a trade is that a county cannot keep a list of anything else, and it can keep those three, and it keeps them by asking a person, and the person gives it what the person has.**”

“**And the fourth head?**” the woman of about thirty-one with a pen said.

“**The fourth head is a line that somebody cut and nobody has ever written a word under and it is still there on every sheet that comes back, because a line standing empty is a question a form has learned to ask by itself, and about four hundred people at the end of a made lane four days off the other way have one of these under a first head right now with a name on it that nobody in nine counties can say is the name the people who live there use.**”

**And he did not say what the sign on the third page of his own record is and nobody asked him and nobody in that room has asked him and about nine people in nine counties have not asked him and the roadmark on his left palm is warm at about the fourth hour and he has never told anybody about it and nobody has asked.**

Nobody in that room asked him a question about a form and nobody asked him a question about a rule and nobody asked him a question about the fourth head, and the two men in that room are a man of thirty-two with a limp and a man of about fifty-eight with a chain of office that is a badge, and neither of them said one word about the other being there on the Wednesday, and the man of about fifty-eight said afterwards that he had come up that lane on the business of the hearing and that the hearing stands and is not adjourned and is not refused and is not retold and is not closed.

**And the one road on this page is the only road in it, and it is two miles and about nine hundred paces of hard sand and broken shell at the landward end of a coast, and it is not a road, and a man of thirty-two with a limp has counted it about four years running and has told nobody in nine counties the number except the woman of about thirty-one with a pen, and about four of the nine people in that room have the number and about five do not, and the two sets do not overlap and neither of them is a figure of persons and neither of them is in the column.**

**And nobody was sent to that coast, and a woman of about fifty-one stands at a post at the seaward end of that sand with twenty-two notches cut in the edge of a piece of driftwood, and the twenty-second is not cut, and the water has not been off that mark in twenty-two years, and the notches are years and are not tides, and no month name and no weekday was spoken in a character's mouth on that sand in nine counties in five months and there is no character on that sand in nine counties in five months and of the people on that sand about nine hundred are on no roll and no sheet and no list, and about eleven hundred more are on nothing whatever.**

**And the cost of that Thursday is a column and it is the woman of about thirty-one with a pen's, and it is the first one she has ever kept, and four lines with no name at the bottom of them are not in it.**

**a candle-end at five, a second candle-end at nine, a third candle-end at eleven, a night's straw at fifteen, a pound of candles at seventeen, a sheet of foolscap at twenty-four, a second sheet of foolscap at twenty-eight, a third sheet of foolscap at thirty, a ream of paper at thirty-nine, and a cake of ink at thirty.** Five, nine, eleven, fifteen, seventeen, twenty-four, twenty-eight, thirty, thirty-nine and thirty is two hundred and eight pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and two hundred and eight pence is four marks and sixteen over**, and four lines are not in it, and a question nobody asked is not in it, and about nine hundred paces is not in it and two miles is not in it and neither of them is a figure of persons, and about nine hundred people on that sand are not in it, and about eleven hundred who are on no roll and no sheet and no list are not in it, and those are two counts of two different things and are not the same figure and go in at neither end of this column, and about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month and about nine hundred sheets a month and about a hundred and four in one band are three counts of three series in this room and none of the three is in this column and no two of the three are added here and none of the three is derived from another, and about four hundred at the end of a made lane four days off the other way are not in it, and the twenty-two notches are years, which is the same number said two ways, and neither telling of it is a price in this column, and about nine people on the wrong side of a shut road four hundred miles off are not in it, and a chain of office that is a badge is not in it.

The four lines went back down that lane on the Monday inside a return and the clerk of about thirty-four in a market town four days off took it out of a bundle about four minutes before the copyist of about twenty-six could get to it and put it on a board in a saddler's shop and nobody in nine counties has told the man in that shop what is on it and nobody was going to.
