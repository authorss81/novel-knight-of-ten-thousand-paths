# Chapter 0546

**Two things came up a made road in the same week in the last days of a month carried at thirty and neither of them is a form, and the second of them came up on foot in the hands of a woman of about fifty-three who takes in washing on the twenty-seventh of Thawmonth, YR 320, a Saturday and a market day, and she was in a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people from the second hour to about the fourth and was not thanked and was not made a witness and was not made anything.**

The first thing was a sheet of foolscap. It came up the made road on the Thursday in a bundle with nine returns in it and it went through about nine hands in a saddler's shop and the man in that shop put it on his board and nobody told him what was on it and nobody has told him since.

**The sheet has four heads on it. A place, a figure of people, a trade, and a fourth, ruled and empty. Under the fourth head a person has written one sentence in a hand that is not a press and not a die and not a copy of anything anybody in this county has made, and the sentence says that the fourth head is empty here too and has been for four and a half years and that nobody has told her why, and it is fourteen words, and it is the same fourteen words that went up a made road in Rainmonth in the year before this one from a room over a weigh-house with a door on a lane about two hundred miles off, and it has been in nine counties in five months and nobody has filled the head it is under and nobody has cut the line out of it and nobody has said who is to answer it.**

The second thing came up the lane on the Saturday morning on foot and a woman of about fifty-three who takes in washing was carrying it, and she carried it about four miles and it is a returned form from a county two hundred miles off this one, and it has four heads on it, and three of them are filled, and the fourth of them has one word in it, and she is the woman who put it there, and she has not changed her account of that since the eighth of a month carried at thirty in the year before this one and she was not asked about it on that morning and nobody asked her about it.

“**Do not ask her that,**” the woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key said, before anybody had, and she said it to the room and not to the man of about fifty-eight, and it is the fourth time in five months that she has said that sentence in that room and she has never once said it twice in a day.

Nobody asked her.

Then the woman of about fifty-three said one thing and it was not about the word, and then she sat down and was not asked anything else for about two hours, and the two hours were spent in a room where about nine people were talking about a line with a stroke through it and she was in the room and nobody turned to her and nobody used her and nobody put her in front of anybody.

“**I have wanted that stopped since the day the head was cut out of one in our county and nobody said anything,**” she said, “**and I wanted it stopped when I was told to fill it in, and both of those are true, and I am not going to say anything else about it today and I would like somebody to write down that I said it and that it was in a room and not in a street.**”

**The copyist of about twenty-six wrote it on a bare leaf with no head over it and ruled nothing and did not read it back, and nobody thanked the woman of about fifty-three for saying it, and she was not thanked before she left and was not thanked after and has not been thanked, and she is not a witness and is not a villain and is not a symbol and is not a subject, and her word stays where it is on a piece of paper in a county two hundred miles off and it is going to stay there.**

Then the woman of about thirty-one with a pen said the thing that is the reason that afternoon happened, and she had come up that lane on foot with the cart behind her and she had not said what she was in town for, and what she said was about a line and not about the woman.

“**There are two things in that room that are not the form and about nine people in it have been treating them as though there were one, and I have been in a room two hundred miles off for two years and a half and I have never been in a room with both of them in it, and I want to say what is different about them and it is one difference and it is small and nobody in nine counties has ever said it out loud.**”

She put her hand flat on the table on the side where the sheet of foolscap was and did not move it.

“**This one has a stroke through a line. That one has a word in a head. A line somebody has been through is not an empty line and it is not a filled one, and that is not a distinction anybody in nine counties has ever put on a page, and it is the whole of my trade and I have been copying for two years and a half in a room with a door on a lane and nobody has ever paid me for the difference.**”

“**Then what is it?**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**It is a line somebody asked about,**” the woman of about thirty-one with a pen said. “**A clerk in about four years will pick that sheet up and see a line with a stroke through it, and the stroke will mean to him that somebody wrote no, and it will be true that somebody wrote no, and it will not be true that anybody asked, and the two of those are the same event seen from two ends and one of the ends has a man's name on it and the other end has nothing on it at all.**”

The copyist of about twenty-six said one thing and it was not about the fourth head and it was not the same sentence she said in Wolfmonth.

“**I have ruled four thousand lines,**” Nesta Braith said, “**and I have copied about four hundred of the ones with a stroke through them and I have never once been asked what one of them meant, and this morning I have been asked, and I have been asked by a person who takes in washing, and I am not going to decide what that makes the two of us and I would like it minuted that I have not decided it.**”

Nobody in that room agreed with her out loud and nobody argued with her out loud, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet wrote it on the same bare leaf as the other things and ruled nothing over it and read nothing back.

**And nobody in that room filled any head on any form, and the fourth head of the Crown's form on the table by that door is still ruled and still empty, five months now, and the word in the fourth head of the returned form is a woman's and it came off her own pen in a county two hundred miles off, and the two of those are two documents in two counties and one of them is not the form, and the sheet of foolscap with the fourteen words under the fourth head is a third document and it is in a third county and it is not the form either, and a piece of stone a foot and a half long with four heads cut into it and a word in the fourth is in a room in another county and it is a fourth document, and those are four documents in four counties and none of the four is the Crown's form and the page says so before anybody in that room can say it.**

**And a line somebody has been through is not an empty line and is not a filled one, and a fourth head with a woman's word in it is neither an empty head nor a rule, and a fifth line with nobody's name against it is not a fourth head, and a printed line at the top of a form is a printed line and not a ruled one, and the four heads of a Crown form are four heads and the fourth of them is empty and no office has filled it and no person in that room filled it and nobody in nine counties has.**

Aren Kest, a man of thirty-two with a limp, was in that room from the second hour to the ninth and was asked nothing, and he said three things, and about four of the nine people in that room have it that the third of the three was the best of the three and about five have said that a man who has been in nine counties for about four years says things that are all the same length, and the two sets do not overlap, and nobody thanked him and he did not ask.

**Nothing in that room was opened on that Saturday and nobody left it. Four hundred miles off, a shut road is exactly as shut as it was, nothing has been put on a list to look at it again, nobody has been sent toward it, and about nine people are on the side of it that is not this side. No one in this county has used the word about that road, and in five months no one here has used it about anything.**

**A woman of about fifty-one stands at a post on a sand four days off, and there are twenty-two notches in the edge of a piece of driftwood in her hand and not a twenty-third, and the water has not been off the highest of them in twenty-two years, and what they count is years. Nobody has been sent to that coast. Of the four documents named above, two are not that coast's, and one of those two is the sheet on a flat stone at its landward end, and it did not come into that room today and it has not come into it since the twenty-fourth of Frostmonth.**

**And the cost of that Saturday is a column and it is the woman of about fifty-three's, and it is the second one she has ever kept, and she keeps it on a washing board and a word she wrote in a fourth head in a county two hundred miles off is not in it.**

**a sheet of blotting paper at two, a pot of tea at five, a second pot of tea at eight, a third broom at nine, a sheet of foolscap at fifteen, a second sheet of foolscap at sixteen, a pound of candles at twenty, a rule of brass at twenty-six, a bundle of kindling at thirty, and a stone of tallow at forty.** Two, five, eight, nine, fifteen, sixteen, twenty, twenty-six, thirty and forty is a hundred and seventy-one pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and seventy-one pence is three marks and twenty-seven over**, and a word in a fourth head is not in it, and a line somebody has been through is not in it and is not a fourth head and is not a fifth line, and the fourteen words under a fourth head in a room two hundred miles off are not in it, and a room two hundred miles off counts three series that do not come off one another: about nine hundred sheets a month, about a hundred and four in a single band, and about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month coming in. No two of the three are added here and not one of them is in this column, and the about nine hundred on a sand four days off is not in it, the about four hundred at the end of a made lane four days the other way is not in it, the about two thousand of this town is not in it, and the three of them are three separate things that go in at neither end of this column, and four miles walked with a form in a hand is not in it, and the twenty-two notches and the twenty-two years are one figure told two ways and neither of them is in this column, and a washing board is not in it because a washing board is a thing a woman owns and is not a thing a room is charged for.

The woman of about fifty-three went down the lane at about the fourth hour and she did not take the returned form with her, and it is on the table in a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people and it is not in a book and it is not in the room's book and the clerk of about twenty-two did not enter it and did not write a year on it and did not write anything on it at all, and the woman of about thirty-one with a pen went back down that lane on the Monday with the cart and told nobody what she had come up for and was not thanked for coming and was not thanked for going.
