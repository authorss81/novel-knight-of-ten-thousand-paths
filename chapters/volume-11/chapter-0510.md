# Chapter 0510

**The nineteenth day of Fallowmonth, YR 319, a Sunday and not a market day, and the ninth hour and the eleventh hour, in a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people four days up a made road from a coast, and the room is shut on a Sunday and about four people are in it because the cart for a weigh-house two hundred miles off goes on Mondays and comes back on Sundays, and the second set of four ruled heads went into that cart at about the eleventh hour, and about four hundred people at the end of a made lane about a mile and a half from that room are on a form and have not been told and are not on anything else.**

## One

**About four people are in that room on that Sunday and they are the man of about fifty-eight, the man of about fifty-one in a grey coat, the copyist of about twenty-six, and a man of thirty-two with a limp, and the two nailed windows are still nailed and the open one is the one that has always been open.**

“**I am not going to say it again,**” Nesta Braith said, at about the ninth hour, when the man of about fifty-eight asked her the question he had asked her in the same room six weeks before.

“**I did not ask you,**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**You have been building toward it since you came in,**” Nesta Braith said, “**and I am not going to give you the satisfaction and I am also not going to give the fourth head a name, because a name on it in a county that has never had one is worse than a line.**”

“**Then what are you going to do with it?**” Gault Prentice said.

Nesta Braith took the four sheets out of the bundle and squared them and said one sentence, and the sentence is sixty-five words long and it was done in about four minutes with the same knife and the same rule.

“**I am going to rule the fourth line a sixteenth of an inch narrower in the second county than in the first, so that a word put in it in one of them will not fit in the other, and a man in four years with nine counties of forms will be able to tell which county a word was written in without being told.**”

**And that is one hundred and seventy words in six turns of one exchange, of which the first is eight, the second is five, the third is fifty-one printed in two spans of ten and forty-one, the fourth is nine, the fifth is seventy-two printed in two spans of sixty-five and seven, and the sixth is twenty-five, and the fourth head is empty in both sets and is a sixteenth of an inch narrower in one of them, and about four people in that room understand what she has done and about nine have said since the first hour that they do not and neither of those is a criticism.**

## Two

**And a woman of about thirty-one with a pen, in a room over a weigh-house with a door on a lane about two hundred miles off, sent four forms in the bundle with the return for the cart on the Monday, and the four forms have the same four heads on them and the fourth one is ruled narrower than the three above it by about as much as a sheet of paper moves in a wet week.**

“**I have ruled the fourth one narrower,**” the woman of about thirty-one with a pen said, in a letter of four sentences that came up that lane on the Wednesday, “**and the letter does not say why, and the head is empty, and I have not been asked to say why and nobody will be.**”

**And what the four sentences add up to is nine counties, two sets of four heads, two knives, two rules, and one difference of about a sixteenth of an inch, and the difference is not a figure of persons and is not a figure of money and is not a figure of days and is not added to anything, and nobody has been told the reason in either county and the man of about fifty-one in a grey coat read the letter and said one word about it and the word was not a question.**

## Three

**And a man of thirty-two with a limp was asked one question in that room on the Sunday, at about the eleventh hour, and he answered it once, and the answer is about a thing he did and not about a form, and all four of the four people in that room wrote it down and about nine hundred people in nine counties have not been told it and will not be.**

“**Can a form be stopped?**” the woman of about thirty-four said, and she had come up the lane from the weigh-house at about the fourth hour and had not said anything at all until then.

“**I have stopped one,**” Aren Kest said. “**Once. Six years ago and a little, and the witness I had for it was false, and it did not open again for about three weeks, and about nine people on the other side of it were never told that I had done anything at all. I have not done it since. I am not going to stand in this room and tell you that I never will, because that sentence is about me and you asked me a question about a form.**”

“**Then that is a no,**” the woman of about thirty-four said.

“**That is not a no,**” Aren Kest said, “**that is a thing that happened once and a thing I will not promise. If you want a no from me about a form, go and ask a man who has never stopped one, and there are about nine of those in nine counties and I cannot name one of them and neither can anybody in this room.**”

“**Why not?**”

“**Because I have not met them,**” Aren Kest said, “**and I have been in nine counties for about four years asking a different question.**”

**And that is one hundred and eighty-three words in six turns of one exchange, of which the first is five, the second is eighty-seven printed in two spans of four and eighty-three, the third is five, the fourth is sixty-three printed in two spans of five and fifty-eight, the fifth is two, and the sixth is twenty-one printed in two spans of six and fifteen, and the man of about fifty-eight wrote the whole of it down in his own book and not in the room's book, and the copyist of about twenty-six copied the whole of it into the second bundle without being asked, and there are now two copies of it in two counties and one in a third, and none of the three has a head on it and the fourth head of a form is still empty.**

## Four

**And what changed on that Sunday is not a decision and not a rule and it is not a settlement, and it is that a man of thirty-two with a limp is a person a room can write down, and the man of about fifty-eight wrote him down at about the eleventh hour in the back of a book of carts, under nine market mornings of figures and above a list of four saddles, and it took him about as long as it takes a man to say four and he did not read it out.**

Nobody saw him do it except the copyist of about twenty-six, who has said since the ninth hour that she saw it and that she said nothing and that she was right to say nothing, **because a man who asks to be written down is a different man from a man who is written down, and there is no form in nine counties that has a head for the difference.**

**And the form's fourth head is empty in two counties and is a sixteenth of an inch narrower in one of them, and about four hundred people at the end of a lane are on a return and about nine hundred people on a coast four days off are on two returns, and the two of those figures are not added, and the eleven houses on that coast and the eleven of a lane in a town of about two thousand are two elevens and are not the same eleven.**

## Five

The cost of that Sunday is a column and it is Aren Kest's, and it is the seventh one he has kept since the twenty-fifth day of a month carried at thirty in the year before this one, and an answer given in a room is not in it and a name in the back of a book of carts is not in it.

**a sheet of paper at one, a quill and ink at three, a second quill and ink at three, a knife at two, a second knife at two, a third knife at two, paste at two, a pot for the paste at five, a rule at four, a second rule at four, a week's bed at nine, a second week's bed at nine, a bucket of tar at eight, a second bucket of tar at eight, a third bucket of tar at eight, bread at one, a second bread at one, a third bread at one, cheese at four, a second cheese at four, a third cheese at four, a broom at five, a second broom at five, a waxed cord at four, an inkwell at three, small beer at two, a second small beer at two, a third small beer at two, a pot of tea at three, a second pot of tea at three, a candle at two, a bootlace at one, a farthing at one, a second farthing at one, a third farthing at one, a fourth farthing at one, a fifth farthing at one, and a sixth farthing at one.** one, three, three, two, two, two, two, five, four, four, nine, nine, eight, eight, eight, one, one, one, four, four, four, five, five, four, three, two, two, two, three, three, two, one, one, one, one, one, one, and one is one hundred and twenty-three pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and twenty-three pence is two marks and twenty-seven over**, and two marks is ninety-six pence and twenty-seven pence over, and about four hundred people at the end of a lane is not in it, and about nine hundred people on a coast four days off is not in it, and thirty-one nights and thirty-four nights are not in it and are two counts of two different things and the difference of three is not in it and has not been found.

**And on the nineteenth day of Fallowmonth in this year, which is a Sunday and not a market day, a copyist of about twenty-six ruled a fourth line a sixteenth of an inch narrower in one county than in another and told nobody why, and a man of thirty-two with a limp was asked one question in a room with about four people in it and answered it once and said that a thing which happened once six years ago is not a promise, and a man of about fifty-eight wrote his name in the back of a book of carts at about the eleventh hour and did not read it out, and the cart left for a weigh-house two hundred miles off at about the eleventh hour with four ruled sheets in it, and the fourth head on those sheets is empty, and it will be empty when they arrive, and about four hundred people at the end of a lane do not know it is on paper and about nine hundred people on a coast four days off do not know it either, and five paths are open and none of them has a keeper, and the driftwood at the seaward end of that coast has twenty-two notches cut in the edge of it and the twenty-second is not cut, and the water has not been off that mark in twenty-two years.**
