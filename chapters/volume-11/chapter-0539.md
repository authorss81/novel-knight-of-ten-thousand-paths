# Chapter 0539

**Goatmonth the twentieth, YR 319, a Thursday and a market day, and the first hour and the ninth, in a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people, where a man of thirty-two with a limp was asked one thing and it was not about a form, and he said one word to it, and that was the whole of the answer, and about nine people were in that room.**

The thing that had happened in the market since the twenty-ninth of Hearthmonth was not a thing anybody in that room had done. A story had gone round a town of about two thousand people and turned four heads into four roads, and a man with a limp had a name at the top of a page because of a Saturday in Hearthmonth when nine people in a hired room had found that there was no head in nine counties for a record of a man's own wrongdoing and had ruled one.

About four hundred people came to the chandler's on the twenty-ninth, and a person in the market who says he was in that room will tell you that a good many of them have been saying since, in the inns and at the ends of counters, that he is the reason the rest of it stands up, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet says he is not going to count it.

They are right. He is the reason it stands up. Nobody has found a better reason and nobody is going to, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet wrote that down on a bare leaf on the day of the reading and has not shown it to anybody.

And the ones who said it are not all of the people who came, and the ones who said it are not the same people who said he is the reason anybody should have believed any of it in the first place, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet wrote both of those on the same leaf and ruled nothing over them and did not count either, and said in the end that a count of who believes what in a market is a number about a street and is not a finding.

Then the man of about thirty-four with a sheet asked him.

“**Are you going to correct them?**”

“**No,**” Aren Kest said.

That was the whole of it. He did not say why, and the room did not ask, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet looked at him for about as long as a man takes to count four and then wrote two words on a bare leaf and turned the leaf face down, and did not read them back, and did not read them to him, and has never told anybody in nine counties what the two words are.

Nobody in that room said anything else about it. The copyist of about twenty-six went back to the straightedge. The clerk of about twenty-two shut his own book of about two hundred leaves and did not open it again that day. The man of about fifty-eight said that the record was public and that a man who has published a thing may say what he likes about it in public, and that the room was not going to sit in judgement of what a man chooses to be true about, and that the room had better get back to the returns because the cart goes on the Monday and there were about nine of them on the table.

Then the room got back to the returns.

**And the cost of that was in the man of thirty-two and not in the room, and it is one line and there is no more of it than one line: he made himself findable on a Saturday in Hearthmonth and about four hundred people have found him, and being found is the thing he bought, and he did not know it would be this.**

Nobody in that room put a hand on him and nobody in that room said anything kind to him, and the man of about fifty-one in a grey coat looked at him once at about the fourth hour and then went out into the market and did not come back in until the seventh, and has not said why, and was not asked.

The man of about thirty-four with a sheet did one thing that afternoon because of what had been said in the street, and it was not about Aren Kest, and nobody thanked him for it. He took the leaf with the story on it — the leaf with the two sets on it and nothing ruled over either — and he copied out the first page of the record on the back of it in his own hand, and wrote on the top of it that it was a copy of the first page of a record made by a man of thirty-two with a limp, and then he carried it down to the man in the saddler's shop and gave it to him and said to put it on the board with the bundles, and the man in the saddler's shop put it there and has never been told what it was and nobody has told him and nobody is going to.

That is the whole of what the second week of Goatmonth did with a record of a man's own wrongdoing, and the man of about fifty-eight wrote none of it in the room's book, and the line in the room's book still says what it has said since the seventh of Hearthmonth, that a rule was changed by one man in a hand and that nobody in that room knows who and nobody in that room is going to find out, and the head cut into the wood over that line has not been cut any deeper.

**And the cost of that Thursday is a column and it is the man of thirty-two’s, and it is the ninth one he has kept since the twenty-fifth day of a month carried at thirty in the year before this one, and a name at the top of a page and a story in a street are not in it.**

**a ferrier’s spur at twenty-seven, a pot of tea at five, a sheet of foolscap at seven, a bundle of kindling at nine, a cake of ink at nineteen, a night’s bed at eleven, a jar of paste at twenty-three, a cord at two, a wick at sixteen, a stone of tallow at fifteen, a rule of brass at thirteen, and a paper of pins at twenty-eight.** Twenty-seven, five, seven, nine, nineteen, eleven, twenty-three, two, sixteen, fifteen, thirteen and twenty-eight is a hundred and seventy-five pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and seventy-five pence is three marks and thirty-one over**, and the name is not in it, and the two words on the face-down leaf are not in it, and the copy of a first page on the back of a leaf is not in it, and the about four hundred who came to the chandler's are not in it, and the about four hundred who live at the end of a made lane are not in it, and the two of those two are the same figure and are not the same figure and go in at neither end of this column, and about two thousand in this town are not in it, and the story is not in it, and thirty-one nights still stand against five roads and thirty-four on a chalk board over a yard stair two hundred miles off and the difference of three is not in it and has not been found, and the notebook has eleven lines and no twelfth and did not come out of his coat.

He went down the lane at about the ninth hour with the stick in his right hand and the column in his coat and the record under the flat weight with the returns, and the man of about fifty-eight locked the room behind him, and the cart went up the made road on the Monday with about nine returns in it and a copy of a first page on top of them in a saddler's shop, and nobody in that room read the two words out.
