# Chapter 0506

**The thirteenth day of Harvestmonth, YR 319, a Saturday and a market day, and the second hour and the sixth hour and the ninth hour, in one room over a weigh-house with a door on a lane about two hundred miles up a made road from a coast, and four of the five returns that came back to a town of about two thousand people four days off that coast have been copied out twice in that room in the last three days, and the copies were made by a woman of about thirty-one with a pen who was told to make them and did, and the second set of copies is not in that town.**

## One

**What came up the lane on the Monday was four copied sheets in one packet with a line at the top of each of them in the hand of the man of about fifty-one in a grey coat, and the line says that they are copies and that the copies were made in a hired room in a town of about two thousand people and that nobody in that town has been asked whether a copy of their form may be made, and the four of them were made anyway, and the packet did not say so.**

The fifth return, the one with the fourth head cut out of it with a knife, was not copied. **That is a fact about a man of about fifty-one who has been arranging an inquiry for about four months and it is the first thing he has done in that time that he has not written a line about in his own book, and about four people in that room have noticed the omission and about nine have not.**

## Two

“**Say the thing you would have to have said to me on Thursday,**” the woman of about thirty-one with a pen said, at about the second hour, to Mara Vey, “**so that it is written down, and then say the four sentences after it, and I will rule a line and put both on it.**”

Mara Vey looked at the four copied sheets on the table for about as long as it takes a man to say four.

“**Controlled disclosure is another kind of the same silence.**”

The woman of about thirty-one with a pen wrote it down and ruled a line under it and did not say anything.

“**A room is not the people who are in it, and it is not smaller when you count the people who are in it, and it is not smaller because you have written four names at the top of a sheet. It is the same size. It is the same size with four names on it.**”

“**That is one,**” the woman of about thirty-one with a pen said, “**and you said you had four.**”

“**That is one, and it is the only one of the four that is about a room, and the other three are about what you would have to do to the sheet, and I am going to say them in the order I would have to do them in, which is the order they were done in.**”

“**Go on.**”

“**First I would have to agree that four names at the top of a sheet is a thing anybody can find out about, and it is not, because there are about nine hundred sheets a month out of this room and about a hundred and four of them go in one band on a fixed day and about four hundred and nine parish rolls come in at this door, and if I put four names at the top of a sheet then in about four months somebody in this county will ask me who the four are, and I will have to write them down.**”

“**And if you do write them down?**”

“**Then there are four people in this county who can be found at a house, and one day in about a year one of them will be asked something by somebody who wants a copy of a form, and there will be a road in this county that a man in another county has heard the name of.**”

**And that is three hundred and forty-one words in nine turns of one exchange, of which the first is thirty-eight printed in two spans of thirteen and twenty-five, the second is nine, the third is fifty-six, the fourth is nine printed in two spans of three and six, the fifth is fifty-seven, the sixth is two, the seventh is a hundred and five, the eighth is seven, and the ninth is fifty-eight, and the woman of about thirty-one with a pen ruled one line and put nine words on it and left about two-thirds of it blank, and the blank is not a figure and is not counted, and about four people have read that line since and about nine have not.**

## Three

**And the other two of the four sentences were said on the Monday and not on the Saturday, and the second of them is the reason the two sets of copies are in two counties and not in one.**

“**Second,**” Mara Vey said, on the Monday, at about the sixth hour, “**is that the copy of a form is a better thing than the form, because the form goes out and comes back and the copy stays where it was written, and a thing that stays where it was written cannot be said to have found anybody out.**”

“**It can,**” the woman of about thirty-one with a pen said. “**It is in this room and this room is one room with a door on a lane.**”

“**Then make two,**” Mara Vey said, “**and put one in a town of about two thousand people four days off a coast and one in this room, and let the two of them be the same four heads, and let nobody in either town know that the other one exists, including the people who keep the rooms.**”

“**That is a controlled disclosure with two rooms in it.**”

“**That is a form that goes out and comes back and lands in a place where nobody was looking,**” Mara Vey said. “**And I have not decided to do it and I have not said no to it, and if I did it I would tell the woman of about thirty-one with a pen in about four months and I would tell her before anybody else, and that is the third sentence and it is the only one of the four that is about me.**”

“**And the fourth?**”

“**The fourth is that a woman of twenty-nine has four and a half years of one room and a woman of twenty-nine in another county has about four months of one room with a different man in it, and the two of us are the only two people in nine counties who have looked at four forms with four heads on them, and if we do not say this to each other in the same month then somebody else will do it in about a year and will not be two women with a lane each.**”

**And that is three hundred and twelve words in seven turns of one exchange, of which the first is one, the second is forty-nine printed in two spans of forty-seven and two, the third is seventeen, the fourth is fifty-four printed in two spans of three and fifty-one, the fifth is ten, the sixth is eighty-two printed in two spans of nineteen and sixty-three, and the seventh is ninety-nine printed in two spans of three and ninety-six, and the woman of about thirty-one with a pen wrote all seven turns down on the same sheet and did not rule a line under any of them and has the sheet still, and it is not in that town and it is not on a list.**

## Four

**And the second set of copies was made on the Thursday and on the Friday, and it consists of four ruled sheets with four heads on each of them, and there is not one figure, place, trade or name on any of the four, and the four heads on the second set are the same four heads as the first set and are cut by the same hand with the same knife and are not the same width.**

“**You have ruled them tighter,**” the woman of about thirty-one with a pen said, on the Friday.

“**They are the same rule,**” Mara Vey said. “**They are tighter because the second set is going to be read by somebody who has not seen the first one, and a wider line looks like a space for a word and a narrower line looks like a rule, and I would rather it looked like a rule.**”

“**And if somebody puts a word in it anyway?**”

“**Then there will be a word in it in one county and not in the other, and that is the first thing in nine counties that anybody can compare,**” Mara Vey said, “**and I am not going to be the one who says that is a good idea, and I have not said it is a bad one, and if you write either of those two sentences down I will ask you to leave the room and you can come back in about four days.**”

**And that is one hundred and fifty words in four turns of one exchange, of which the first is five, the second is five, the third is fifty-eight printed in two spans of forty-nine and nine, and the fourth is eighty-two printed in two spans of twenty-nine and fifty-three, and nobody has written either of those two sentences down and the woman of about thirty-one with a pen has said since Monday that she thought about writing one of them down, and about four people in that county have heard that she thought about it and about nine have not.**

## Five

The cost of that Saturday is a column and it is Mara Vey's, and it is the seventh one she has kept in four and a half years, and four copied sheets and four ruled sheets and nine words on a ruled line are not in it.

**a ream of paper at nine, a quill at two, a second quill at two, ink at three, a second ink at three, a knife at two, a second knife at two, paste at two, a pot for the paste at five, a rule at four, a second rule at four, a straightedge at two, a second straightedge at two, a night's bed at four, a second night's bed at four, a third night's bed at four, a fourth night's bed at four, bread at one, cheese at four, a second cheese at four, small beer at two, a broom at five, a second broom at five, a bucket of tar at eight, a second bucket of tar at eight, a bootlace at one, a farthing at one, a second farthing at one, a third farthing at one, a fourth farthing at one, and a fifth farthing at one.** nine, two, two, three, three, two, two, two, five, four, four, two, two, four, four, four, four, one, four, four, two, five, five, eight, eight, one, one, one, one, one, and one is one hundred and one pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and one pence is two marks and five over**, and two marks is ninety-six pence and five pence over, and four counties and five counties and nine counties are not in it, and about nine hundred sheets a month and about a hundred and four in a band and about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month are three counts of three different series and are not in it and are not added to one another, and the fourth head is empty on both sets of four and is not in it.

**And on the thirteenth day of Harvestmonth in this year a woman of twenty-nine in a room over a weigh-house with a door on a lane two hundred miles up a made road from a coast said nine words and then four sentences, and a woman of about thirty-one with a pen ruled a line under the nine words and left about two-thirds of it blank, and four forms were copied twice into two counties, and about four hundred and eighty people are on a piece of paper in a town four days off that coast and about nine hundred people on a coast four days further off have a return with a word under its first head and nothing under its fourth, and neither of those figures is on anything the other one is on, and the tide decides twice a day and is not paid and is not in this column.**
