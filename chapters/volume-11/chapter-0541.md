# Chapter 0541

**At about the fourth hour on the second day of Thawmonth, YR 320, in a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people, on a Tuesday and not a market day, from the second hour to about the ninth, and about nine people were in that room, and a man of about fifty-eight with a chain of office round his neck said out loud the whole of what this room can do, and it took him about four minutes, and the copyist of about twenty-six wrote all of it and ruled nothing over it.**

The man of about fifty-eight read it off the leaf before he said it, and then he put the leaf face down, which is a thing he has not done in this room in four months and which the clerk of about twenty-two noticed and nobody else did.

“**An order may not act on a road without that road's own answer to a question put to it in its own words.**”

That is the whole of it and it is on a bare leaf with no head over it and it is not in the room's book, because the room's book has one head cut into the wood over one line in it and that head was cut for a different thing and the man of about fifty-eight said out loud that he was not going to cut a second head in eleven years over a sentence he had said out loud in a room on a Tuesday.

Then he said the other half, and he said it to the room and not to the leaf.

“**And a rule about procedure is not a promise about what anybody will do. I am saying that out loud in about nine people and I want it in the same breath as the first thing and not on a second leaf, because in about four years somebody will read the first thing on its own and it will sound like a promise and it is not one, and the reason it will sound like a promise is that it is the shape of a promise, and a shape is the only part of a rule that travels.**”

Nobody in that room agreed with him and nobody argued with him, and the copyist of about twenty-six wrote both sentences on one leaf and left about two-thirds of it blank, and the blank is not a figure and is not counted.

**And then the room found out that saying a thing is not the same as being able to do it, and it took about as long as a man takes to say nine.**

The man of about thirty-four with a sheet did that part, and he did it out loud, and he did it in the order he did it, which is the order a man walks a road in.

“**You cannot put that sentence into practice anywhere in nine counties and I have been trying to do it since the eighth of a month carried at thirty in the year before this one, and here is every part of it that does not work.**”

He put his finger on the table and did not move it.

“**One. There is no head in nine counties for a question. I have nine counties of forms in this room over four months and every one of them asks a place, a figure of people, a trade, and the fourth, and not one of them asks anybody anything, and a form that does not ask a question cannot carry an answer, and a leaf with no head over it can carry anything and can be shown to nobody.**”

Nobody said anything.

“**Two. There is no head in nine counties for an order. There is a chain of office round the neck of the man who said it and the chain is a badge and a badge is not a word, and the word order is not on any paper in this room, and I have asked twice in four months and been told twice that it is a county matter.**”

The man of about fifty-eight said that was right and that he had said it himself.

“**Three, and this is the one that stops me.**”

The man of about thirty-four with a sheet looked up for the first time in about four minutes.

“**You cannot ask a road a question in its own words. You can ask a person. A person will tell you what he has heard said about a road, and what he has heard said about a road is what somebody told him, and the somebody is in this room or in a house in this town, and about four months ago nine people in this room were told that a record says where four roads are, and the four came from four heads counted by a man with a loaf of bread under his arm.**”

**Nobody in that room started the story and about four of the nine have told it since and about five have not, and the two sets do not overlap, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet did not count either and said a count of who believes what in a market is a number about a street.**

“**And four. I have been down nine counties since the eighth of a month carried at thirty and I have asked about nine hundred returns a month and about four hundred and nine parish rolls and about a hundred and four in a band, and I have asked every road office in nine counties how a road answers a question put to it in its own words, and not one of them has a procedure for it, and not one of them has a head for it, and eight of them said some version of *it is the form we have always used*, and one of them wrote that down for me and dated it and I have it in the satchel.**”

**And the room sat with the fourth of those, because it is the only one of the four that anybody can be shown.**

The man of about fifty-three with a barrow said one thing at about the seventh hour, and it was not about the sentence, and nobody thanked him for it and he did not ask.

“**You have just said in one sentence that nobody in nine counties can do the thing you have just said should be done, and you have said it out loud, and about four of us think that is a rule and about five of us think that is a man finding a room where he does not have to go and find out, and both of those are about the room and neither of them is about a road, and I am the one who said in Harvestmonth that a page that cannot be read out loud is a page about four people, and I would like somebody to say whether this sentence can be read out loud, because I will read it out loud if it can.**”

**And nobody told him he could and nobody told him he could not, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet wrote the whole of it on the same bare leaf and ruled nothing, and the room did not vote, and the man of about fifty-eight said out loud that a vote would be a finding and there has not been a finding in that room in five months.**

Then the man of about thirty-four with a sheet said the thing that is the reason the sentence is worth what it is worth, and he said it flat, and he said it to the leaf and not to the man of about fifty-eight.

“**It is worth more than the whole of what anybody in this room came for, and I have been in this room four months and I came for a rule, and a man of about fifty-eight came for a room, and a woman of about twenty-nine two hundred miles off came for nothing to be sent anywhere, and a man of thirty-two came for a thing nobody would ask him about, and what we have got is a sentence that says an order may not act on a road without the road's own answer, and that is not what any of us asked for, and it is the only thing in five months that is worth the paper, and I would like it minuted that I said so and that I am not going to say it twice.**”

The clerk of about twenty-two wrote it and said out loud that it was the second thing he had entered in the room's book in about four months and the first was on the twenty-fourth of Frostmonth.

**Nobody agreed with him out loud and nobody argued with him out loud, and the copyist of about twenty-six wrote what he said on the same leaf and put her hand flat on the blank part of it, and the man of about fifty-three with a barrow got up and went out of the room for about as long as a man takes to say four and came back in and said nothing, and nobody asked him where he had been and he was not asked, and nobody in that room thanked him for offering to read a sentence out loud at a rail of a common on a market morning, because he did not offer it twice.**

Aren Kest, a man of thirty-two with a limp, was in that room from the second hour to the ninth and was asked nothing at all, and he was asked nothing about a form and nothing about the fourth head and nothing about the sentence, and he said four things and none of the four was about the sentence and nobody wrote any of the four down.

**And nothing was opened, and nobody was sent, and no head was filled in on any form by anybody in that room or in this county, and the fourth head of the Crown's form on the table by that door is ruled and empty and will be empty on the Monday.**

Five months is how long that head has stood empty and it is still standing empty, and not one of the nine counties has put anything under it and not one office has either. **A word does sit in a fourth head on a piece of the coast's own, but that is a different county and a different sheet and the word was cut into a flat stone with a knife, and the two objects have not shared a room since the twenty-fourth of Frostmonth, and on the one day they did, about nine people heard it said out loud that there are two documents and two counties and that nobody present was in a position to say which of them came first.**

The three words for one lane are still three. The Green Lane at a rail of a common, the Long Lane at the eleventh of eleven houses, and the Chapel Way at the fourth house, and nobody was asked which of the three is the true one, nothing has been scheduled about choosing, and the room has not adopted one.

Nobody was sent anywhere on that Tuesday or on any of the four days before it. A shut road four hundred miles off stays shut and is not on a list of things to be looked at again, and about nine people are on the far side of it. **About that road no person in this county has used the word, and about anything at all in this county in five months nobody has.**

Four days off from this town there is a woman of about fifty-one at a post at the seaward end of two miles and about nine hundred paces of hard sand. She has cut twenty-two notches into the edge of a piece of driftwood and the twenty-second of those has not been cut, and the sea has not been off that mark in twenty-two years. A notch on that wood is a year and is not a tide. Nobody in this county has been to that coast and nobody out of it has either.

**And the cost of that Tuesday is a column and it is the man of about fifty-eight's, and it is the fourth one he has kept in that room, and a sentence that is worth more than what anybody wanted is not in it.**

**a sheet of blotting paper at one, a bit of chalk at two, a night's bed at four, a thimble at eight, a wick at ten, a cake of soap at eleven, a box of quills at twelve, a stone of tallow at fifteen, a sheet of oiled paper at nineteen, and a rule of brass at thirty-one.** One, two, four, eight, ten, eleven, twelve, fifteen, nineteen and thirty-one is a hundred and thirteen pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and thirteen pence is two marks and seventeen over**, and a sentence is not in it, and a head cut into wood is not in it, and a chain of office is not in it because a chain of office is a badge and a badge is not a thing anybody buys, and about nine people in that room are not in it, the about four hundred who live at the end of a made lane are not in it, and neither is the about nine hundred on a sand four days off, and the about two thousand of this town are not in it either, and those are three separate counts of three separate things and none of the three is in this column and no two of the three are added here, and a room two hundred miles off keeps three tallies that are not made out of one another, about nine hundred sheets a month going out, about a hundred and four going out in one band, about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month coming in, and not one of the three of them is in this column, and the twenty-two notches are years and are not in it, and the four hundred miles is not in it, and the four months in which nobody filled a head are not in it.

The man of about fifty-eight locked the room over the saddler's at about the ninth hour and went down the stairs and out into a street where a market was not going, because it is a Tuesday, and the leaf with both sentences on it and about two-thirds of it blank went into the flat box with the other bare leaves and the flat box is not in the room's book, and nobody in nine counties can be shown it, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet said the condition for it was correct and that he was not going to say it twice.
