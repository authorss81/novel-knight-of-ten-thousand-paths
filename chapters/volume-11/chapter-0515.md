# Chapter 0515

**The twenty-eighth day of Embermonth, YR 319, a Thursday and a market day, and the first hour and the fourth hour and the seventh hour, in a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people four days up a made road from a coast, and a third return has come up that lane from that coast with a boat, and the three filled heads on it are the same as the three on the other two, and the fourth head is empty on it as it is on the other two, and nobody on that coast has been told that any of the three has ever been here.**

## One

**The word under the first head is the same word. The figure under the second head is the same figure, and the figure is about nine hundred, and the third head carries the same four trades in the same order. The two are in the same hand and the two are not in the same ink, and this third one is in the same hand as the two before it and in a third ink.**

Aren Kest was in that room at the fourth hour and at the seventh and he is the man who has read about four hundred and nine returns in two days, and he looked at the third one for about as long as a man takes to say nine and then said that the three dates were the day the boat came and that a clerk cannot help that and that the clerk should not have to try.

The clerk of about twenty-two has entered a date on all three. **The three dates are nine days apart and every one of them is the day the boat came; nobody in nine counties has been able to say by how much the day the boat came is not the day the thing under the first head happened, because nobody on that coast writes a month or a day and has not for about nine years.**

**And a fourth document exists on that coast and it is a different document.** It is a sheet on a flat stone at the landward end, and it is a foot and a half long, and it has four heads cut into it, and the fourth of them holds a word, and the word is the coast's own name for the road. **A Crown form with a fourth head ruled and empty in a market town four days up a made road and a sheet on a flat stone with a word in a fourth head are two documents in two counties and they are not the same document and they are not added to each other and nobody in that room has ever seen the second of them.**

## Two

“**It is the same road,**” the woman of about thirty-four said, at about the fourth hour.

“**I know it is the same road,**” the man of about thirty-four said. “**What I want to know is what the clerk is going to write in the first column when there are three of the same road in it.**”

“**Three,**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**Three,**” the man of about thirty-four said, “**and a man in about four years who has a whole county's worth of returns is going to count that road three times, and the three are not three roads, and the clerk is the only person in nine counties who could tell him that, and the clerk is twenty-two and he is going to count them because that is what the column is.**”

“**Then it goes in the book with no head on it,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, “**the way the carts went in last Thursday.**”

“**You have got about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month coming into a room two hundred miles off,**” the woman of about thirty-four said, “**and about nine hundred sheets a month going out of it, and neither of those is a number of roads, and if you put three of the same road on a bare leaf as well then in about four years there are two places in nine counties where that road is written down and one of them has a head on it.**”

**And that is two hundred and six words in six turns of one exchange, of which the first is five, the second is thirty-four printed in two spans of seven and twenty-seven, the third is one, the fourth is sixty-five printed in two spans of one and sixty-four, the fifth is nineteen printed in two spans of eleven and eight, and the sixth is eighty-two printed in two spans of twenty and sixty-two, and a man of about fifty-eight has now ruled two bare leaves in about four weeks, one for carts and one for a road, and neither of them has a head and neither of them has a keeper and about four people in that room have said that a leaf is where a thing goes to be forgotten and about five have said that a leaf is where a thing goes to stop being chosen.**

## Three

“**Nobody is going,**” the man of about thirty-eight with a satchel said, at about the fifth hour, and about nine people in that room agreed with him and about four did not, and the four were not asked and are not going to be asked.

“**There is a clerk of about thirty-four in this county who has walked about nine roads in eleven years,**” the woman of about thirty-four said, “**and the room decided in Harvestmonth that a clerk goes down a road rather than a road being asked, and that clerk is in this room.**”

“**I know it,**” the man of about thirty-eight with a satchel said, “**and I have not said the clerk should not go, and I have said that a clerk going down a road four days off a made road to a coast is a clerk going down a road, and the difference between that and this is about nineteen days of waiting and a boat.**”

“**And the form says nobody is to be sent to find out.**”

“**The form says nobody is to be sent to find out,**” the man of about thirty-eight with a satchel said, “**in the same ink as everything else on it, and the room decided in Harvestmonth to send somebody, and the two facts have been in the same room since the first hour of the first day and about nine people had not put them together until about the tenth hour of the eighteenth day of that month and I have not put them together since.**”

**And that is one hundred and ninety-two words in five turns of one exchange, of which the first is three, the second is forty-five printed in two spans of nineteen and twenty-six, the third is fifty-six printed in two spans of three and fifty-three, the fourth is twelve, and the fifth is seventy-six printed in two spans of eleven and sixty-five, and nothing was decided on that Thursday about the coast, and nothing was scheduled, and no boat was hired, and the twenty-second notch in a piece of driftwood at the seaward end of that sand is not cut and the water has not been off that mark in twenty-two years.**

## Four

**And the third return is the second object about that coast in this county, because the copy of the first one went up a made road on a Monday in a bundle with nine returns in it, and the copy of this one will go on the next Monday, and the two counties are two counties and the two copies are two copies and neither of them is the return.**

The copyist of about twenty-six copied the first one in about four minutes. She copied the second one in about four minutes. **She copied this one in about nine minutes, and about four of the nine people in that room have said that nine minutes is a long time for three lines, and about five have said that she was not copying the lines.**

“**What were you copying?**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**The word,**” Nesta Braith said, “**and I have copied it four thousand times in about four counties and I have never had to decide whether it is a place or a name, because on the copies it is a word and a word does not have to be either. Today somebody read it out loud as a place and about four people agreed, and I copied it after that, and my hand had already decided.**”

“**And the hand is not yours,**” the woman of about thirty-four said.

“**The hand is not mine,**” Nesta Braith said, “**which is the entire reason I have spent four years being careful about lines.**”

**And that is one hundred and one words in four turns of one exchange, of which the first is four, the second is seventy-two printed in two spans of two and seventy, the third is six, and the fourth is nineteen printed in two spans of five and fourteen, and a copyist of about twenty-six has said a thing about her own hand out loud for the first time in this room, and nobody agreed with her and nobody disagreed, and about nine hundred paces and eleven houses and twenty-two notches are not on a page anywhere and are not in a room four days off and are not in the cost of that Thursday.**

## Five

The cost of that Thursday is a column and it is the copyist's, and it is the first one she has ever kept, and she keeps it in a pocket, and a word under a first head is not in it.

**a pounce pot at two, a third quill at two, a cake of ink at three, a second scrap of linen at one, a second bread at four, cheese at four, a third knife at two, a third pot of tea at three, a pot of paste at three, a knife at two, a second cake of ink at three, bread at four, a bootlace at one, a brush at two, ink at three, a second pot of tea at three, a second quill at two, a pound of candles at four, a second rule at four, a second broom at five, paste at two, a straightedge at two, a second cheese at four, a pot of tea at three, a third pound of candles at four, a second knife at two, a broom at five, a pot for the paste at five, a third cheese at four, a second straightedge at two, a scrap of linen at one, a bucket of tar at eight, a quill at two, a second pound of candles at four, a second bootlace at one, a second brush at two, paper at nine, a rule at four, and a second ink at three.** Two, two, three, one, four, four, two, three, three, two, three, four, one, two, three, three, two, four, four, five, two, two, four, three, four, two, five, five, four, two, one, eight, two, four, one, two, nine, four and three is a hundred and twenty-four pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and twenty-four pence is two marks and twenty-eight over**, and about nine hundred on a sand four days off is not in it, and the third return is not in it, and the word under the first head is not in it, and the three returns from that coast are three returns and not three roads and are not added to the about ninety returns that came in under the wrong question in Harvestmonth, and about four hundred at the end of a made lane are not in it either.

**And on the twenty-eighth day of Embermonth in this year a third sheet came up a made road from a coast four days off with a word under a first head that is the same word and a figure under a second head that is the same figure and a fourth head that is empty, and the clerk of about twenty-two wrote a third date on it and the third date is the day the boat came, and nobody on that coast has been told, and nobody was sent, and the copyist of about twenty-six copied the word in nine minutes instead of four and said out loud that her hand had decided before she did, and a Crown form and a sheet on a flat stone are two documents and the tide decides twice a day and is not paid.**
