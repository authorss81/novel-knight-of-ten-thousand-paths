# Chapter 0509

**The second day of Fallowmonth, YR 319, a Thursday and a market day, and the second hour and the sixth hour and the tenth hour, in a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people four days up a made road from a coast, and a woman of about twenty-seven walked up the lane that runs past the weigh-house and into that room at about the second hour with a sheet of paper in her hand, and the sheet of paper is a copy of a return that went out of that room on the twenty-fifth day of a month carried at thirty, and there is a figure of about four hundred on it and the figure is the people who were in a lane on a Tuesday.**

## One

**She found it in this town and not in her lane. A man of about thirty-four who is a saddler has a shop nine doors from the weigh-house, and a return went past that shop inside a bundle on a cart on the Thursday and he took a copy of it out of the bundle and read it standing up and carried it up the lane on the Monday, and about four people in that town have told four different ways of how a return came to be in a saddler's shop and not one of the four has been asked.**

She is the woman at the eleventh of eleven houses at the end of a made lane about a mile and a half from that room, and there are about four hundred people at the end of that lane, and the third head on the return says what is carried on it, and the third head names four trades, and one of the four is not carried on that lane at all and got there from a county office.

**She is not angry. About nine people in that town have said since Monday that they expected her to be angry and about four of the nine have said that what she did was worse than being angry, and the four and the nine do not overlap at all.**

## Two

“**Who is the four hundred?**” the woman of about twenty-seven said, at about the second hour, to the room, and put the copy of the return on the table face up.

“**There are about nine people in this room and I do not know, and the clerk of about twenty-two might.**”

“**It is a figure of people in a lane on the Tuesday of the twenty-fourth of a month carried at thirty, and a man of about forty-one with a cart brought the form in himself, and I wrote down the figure that he gave me, and I was told in the book to write it down.**”

“**By whom?**” the woman of about twenty-seven said.

“**By the form,**” the clerk of about twenty-two said.

The woman of about twenty-seven looked at the clerk of about twenty-two for about as long as it takes a man to say four.

“**Then it is the form that sent a man of about forty-one up that lane to count us, and I am not angry about the counting, and I am angry about the line, and there is a line on that paper and it is empty and it has got my lane's name on it above it and about four hundred of my neighbours under another line and four trades under a third one, and the fourth line is empty, and it is the only line on that paper that nobody put anything in, and it is the only line that anybody in this room has read twice.**”

“**I ruled it,**” Nesta Braith said, from the side table, without getting up.

“**I know you ruled it,**” the woman of about twenty-seven said, and did not look round, and about four people in that room looked at the side table and about five did not.

**And that is two hundred and one words in eight turns of one exchange, of which the first is five, the second is twenty, the third is fifty-six, the fourth is two, the fifth is three, the sixth is a hundred and seven, the seventh is three, and the eighth is five, and a woman of about twenty-seven read a form out loud in a room of about nine people and the longest of the eight turns was a hundred and seven words and she did not raise her voice at any point of it, and about four people in that room have written down one word out of it and the word is *line* and about five have written down *line* and about nine have written down nothing.**

## Three

**And the cost of that Thursday was spoken by a man of about fifty-eight who is not Aren Kest, and the first thing he said was two words and he said four sentences after it, and about nine people in that room heard all of it and about four of the nine have repeated the two words since and about five have not.**

“**It works,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, at about the sixth hour. “**That is what I have to say. It works. I have been in this room eighteen days trying to make a thing that cannot be found out about and a woman of about twenty-seven has come up a lane with it in her hand.**”

Nobody said anything for about as long as it takes a man to say four.

“**I am not going to say that is a reason to stop,**” the man of about fifty-eight said. “**It is a reason to write down what a form is, and it is a reason to stop putting a figure under a head that does not say whose, and it is a reason to have somebody whose job it is to be the person a lane can complain to.**”

“**That is a keeper,**” the man of about thirty-eight with a satchel said.

“**That is a name on a form,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, “**and I have not got one and I have said so in this room, and if somebody in nine counties writes one down then it will be a man in nine counties and a woman in a lane can walk up a made road and stand in a room and be listened to, and that is not nothing, and I am not going to pretend in here that it is nothing because it is not what I came for.**”

**And that is one hundred and ninety-eight words in four turns of one exchange, of which the first is forty-six printed in two spans of two and forty-four, the second is twelve, the third is fifty-four printed in two spans of fifty and four, and the fourth is eighty-six printed in two spans of seven and seventy-nine, and a man of about thirty-eight with a satchel said the word for it out loud and was not answered and has not been answered, and that word is not printed in any book in that room and is not in the cost of that Thursday.**

## Four

**And in the week after that Thursday, nine counties sent back about nine returns each, and in the week before, four counties out of nine had sent about nine, and the difference is not a decision and is not a panic and is not anybody's doing, and it is the ordinary thing, and about four people in that town have said that the ordinary thing is the whole of the danger.**

The man of about thirty-eight with a satchel put it in his own book on the Friday in a line of eleven words, and the eleven words are these: **a form is a thing that finds out, and the more it finds the better it works.**

**And about nine hundred sheets a month go out of a room two hundred miles off with four heads on them and the fourth one empty, and about a hundred and four of those go out in one band on a fixed day, and about four hundred and nine parish rolls come in at that door, and the three of those are counts of three different series and are not added, and none of them is a figure of a lane and none of them is a figure of a coast and none of them is a figure of a town of about two thousand people, and the three of them are not converted into one another anywhere in nine counties.**

## Five

The cost of that Thursday is a column and it is the woman of about twenty-seven's, and it is the first one she has ever kept, and it is on the back of a saddler's receipt with a nail she found at the end of the lane, and a form with a figure of about four hundred on it is not in it.

**a sheet of paper at one, a quill and ink at three, a knife at two, a second knife at two, a rule at four, paste at two, a pot for the paste at five, a night's bed at four, a second night's bed at four, a third night's bed at four, a fourth night's bed at four, a pound of candles at four, a second pound of candles at four, a loaf at one, a second loaf at one, a third loaf at one, a fourth loaf at one, cheese at four, a second cheese at four, a third cheese at four, small beer at two, a second small beer at two, a third small beer at two, a broom at five, a second broom at five, a bucket of tar at eight, a second bucket of tar at eight, a waxed cord at four, a second waxed cord at four, an inkwell at three, a second inkwell at three, a bootlace at one, a pot of tea at three, a cord at two, a farthing at one, a second farthing at one, a third farthing at one, a fourth farthing at one, and a fifth farthing at one.** one, three, two, two, four, two, five, four, four, four, four, four, four, one, one, one, one, four, four, four, two, two, two, five, five, eight, eight, four, four, three, three, one, three, two, one, one, one, one, and one is one hundred and sixteen pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and sixteen pence is two marks and twenty over**, and two marks is ninety-six pence and twenty pence over, and about four hundred people at the end of a lane is not in it, and about nine hundred people on a coast four days off is not in it, and about two thousand people in a town of about two thousand is not in it, and the three of those are not added, and the lane is not the coast and the coast is not the town.

**And on the second day of Fallowmonth in this year a woman of about twenty-seven walked up a lane past a weigh-house and into a hired room with a copy of a return in her hand and read a hundred and seven words of it out loud, and a copyist of about twenty-six said she had ruled the fourth line and the copyist is about twenty-six and is not on a list and has said one thing out loud in two counties in a month, and the room was told in two words that it works, and nine counties sent back about nine returns each in the week after, and the fourth head of the form in two counties is empty and the fourth head of a sheet on a coast four days off holds a word, and those are two documents and are not the same document, and the tide decides twice a day and is not paid and is not on any of this.**
