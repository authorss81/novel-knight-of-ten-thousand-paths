# Chapter 0530

**About nine people were in a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people on the seventh of Hearthmonth, YR 319, a Wednesday and not a market day, at the second hour and again at the fifth and the ninth, and the room had arrived at one thing on purpose. The one thing was not a decision about a road and was not a decision about a form, and it is the first time in eleven years that anybody in that county has decided to do a thing on purpose and said out loud that they were doing it.**

It took from the second hour until about the seventh hour and it was not quick and it was not clever, and the man of about fifty-eight with the chain of office round his neck did most of it and not all of it, and the first hour of it was the two of them going over what the room had, which is four returns with four heads on them from a coast four days off, none of them with a date on it that anybody on that coast wrote, a sheet of paper off a press with four heads cut into a wood about four counties away, a second copy of a copy of that sheet in a flat box that came out of a chest of waste paper, a leaf with no head over it with two figures of carts on it and a road with no name on it and three bare leaves made in the last thirty days, a strip of paper about two inches wide with nine words on it in a hand about a hundred and forty years old, a piece of stone a foot and a half long with a word cut into its fourth head, three words for one lane on two sheets, and a woman of about fifty-three's return with one word in its fourth head.

Nobody in that room put a figure of people to any other figure of people while they did it, and about four hundred and about nine hundred and about two thousand and about nine hundred sheets a month and about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month were said out loud as separate things and none of them was added to another.

Then the man of about fifty-eight said the thing that had been in the room since the second hour of Frostmonth and had not been said.

“**We have found that a rule was changed by one man in a hand,**” he said. “**That is what is on the table and it is not a guess. There are two nine-word sentences and one of them cannot be carried out by anybody and the other one can, and the second one is on the strip and the first one is on the paper in the flat box, and the second one is later and shorter and the hand that made it is a hand that has been through a line, and the man who wrote it is dead and was not a keeper and is not a villain and has no name, and we have found that out in about nine days and we are not going to find out who he was.**”

Nobody in that room said a name. The man of about thirty-four with a sheet said one sentence and it was about the county and not about the man.

“**A county is not a man,**” he said, “**and he was in a county, and there are nine of them, and there is no head in any of the nine for a man inside a county, and that is not an excuse, it is the shape of the thing, and I have said it in this room once before, in Embermonth, and the woman of about thirty-four has said it in this room twice and is not the same man and is not in the wrong, and I would like it minuted that this is the third time and not the first.**”

“**It is minuted,**” the clerk of about twenty-two said, and wrote it, and it is the only thing the clerk of about twenty-two entered in the room's book in about nine days.

**Aren Kest, a man of thirty-two with a limp, was in that room for all three of the hours and did not say a word, and the copyist of about twenty-six has said that she was not surprised, and that the reason she was not surprised is that a man who has asked a room to put his name next to another man's in a book that goes up a made road has nothing left to say to that room for about a year, and that she would not have known that in advance and knows it now.**

Then the man of about fifty-eight said the second thing.

“**And I am not going to write down who,**” he said. “**I am going to write down what we have found, in the room's book, in my own hand, with the day on it and the hour on it, and it is going to say that a rule was changed by one man in a hand and that nobody in this room knows who he was and that nobody in this room is going to find out, and I am going to rule a head over that line, which is the first head I have ruled in this room in eleven years, and I am ruling it because the four of us who have been putting things on bare leaves since Fallowmonth have made a mess and the mess has a cause and the cause is that there is no head anywhere in nine counties for a thing we were not allowed to write down, and a rule that is not written down is not a rule, it is a habit, and a habit of four people in a hired room dies when the four of them are not in the room.**”

Nobody argued with him and the copyist of about twenty-six said one thing and it was not about the fourth head.

“**You have just said the reason I have not been thanked for four years,**” Nesta Braith said. “**And I am not going to say thank you, and I want it on the record that I did not say thank you, and I want the record to say why: because if a thing I did gets a head cut into the wood over it then it stops being a thing I did and starts being a rule, and I have a rule of my own and it is that I do not have rules.**”

The man of about fifty-eight wrote what she said on a bare leaf and did not rule a head over that one either.

**And about four of the nine people in that room think writing it down is right, and about five think it is a way of not doing the other thing, and the two sets do not overlap.**

The four think that a finding nobody can check is a thing a man says in a corridor, and that the finding is a rule and the rule is a refusal, and that a refusal which is not written down is a refusal a man's nephew can talk his way round at a table in about four years.

The five think that a room which has just worked out who cannot be found, and has said so out loud, and has then ruled a head over the saying of it, is a room that has made a finding about its own limits and dressed it as a finding about the world, and that the head cut into the wood over that line is the fourth head of a form nobody in nine counties can fill in.

The room did not vote. The man of about fifty-eight said out loud that a vote would be a finding and there had not been a finding in that room in four months and there was not going to be one on the ninth hour of a Wednesday in Hearthmonth, and that the decision he had made was the only one he was entitled to make in that room and he had made it in his own hand and in his own name and nobody else had voted on it and nobody else needed to.

**And the man of about fifty-one in a grey coat said the thing that is the cost of it, out loud, at about the ninth hour, and nobody thanked him for it and he has not said this room anything else since the ninth of Wolfmonth.**

“**You have written down that you do not know,**” Gault Prentice said, “**and you have put a head over it, and in about four years a man in this county is going to read that line and take it for a rule about the road, and it is not a rule about the road, it is a rule about this room, and rooms get read as roads by people who are not in them, and I have spent about four months building a thing that was supposed to be small and I have found out again that the smallness was in the room.**”

Nobody argued with him and nobody thanked him and the copyist of about twenty-six wrote what he said on the same bare leaf and left about two-thirds of it blank.

**And the cost of that Wednesday is a column and it is the woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key's, and it is the second one she has ever kept, and the first one she kept was in Wolfmonth and she keeps it in the back of a bad copy, and this one she keeps on the back of a bill for a lock, and a finding is not in it and a refusal is not in it and a head cut into wood is not in it.**

**a pot of tea at three, a ream of paper at thirty-one, a broom at nine, a box of quills at twenty-three, a farthing at five, a cake of ink at nineteen, a night's bed at eleven, a jar of paste at twenty-seven, a bundle of kindling at seven, a stone of tallow at fifteen, a bootlace at two, and a rule of brass at thirteen.** Three, thirty-one, nine, twenty-three, five, nineteen, eleven, twenty-seven, seven, fifteen, two and thirteen is a hundred and sixty-five pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and sixty-five pence is three marks and twenty-one over**, and eleven years is not in it, and nine days is not in it, and the two hundred miles between this room and the room over the weigh-house is not in it, and about nine people in that room, about four hundred at the end of the made lane, about nine hundred on that sand and about two thousand in this town are four counts of four different things and none of the four is in this column and no two of the four are added, and the outgoing counts of that other room belong to it and not to this column: rolls in at four hundred and nine a month, sheets out in a band of a hundred and four, sheets out in all at about nine hundred, three tallies kept in three separate books by three separate people, none of them derived from any other.

**And three documents are on that table and none of them is the form.** The fourth head on the Crown's form in this market town is empty and has been empty for about four months and nobody has filled it. One woman's word is in a fourth head on a returned form, and the word is the woman's, and a woman of about fifty-three who takes in washing was told to put it there and has not been thanked and has not been asked to explain and has not been convicted. A struck-out line is on a copy of a copy, and the stroke is in a hand that has not been signed, and nobody has filled the struck-out line and nobody can say that anybody has. Those are three documents and they are in three counties and they are not the form, and the form is on the table by the door with four heads and three of them filled on some sheets and the fourth empty on all of them.

**And the cart goes up the made road on the Monday. The inquiry is not adjourned and is not refused and is not retold and is not closed. The line is in the room's book in one hand with a day and an hour against it, and the man who wrote it is going to be asked about it in about four years by somebody he will not know, and that is what he bought it for.**

**The hearing stands.**
