# Chapter 0525

**The twenty-eighth of Wolfmonth, YR 319, a Saturday and a market day, and the second hour and the fifth hour and the ninth hour, in a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people, and two things came up the made road from a coast four days off in the same fortnight, and one of them is a question that nobody in nine counties asked.**

The first thing was a sheet of paper with four heads cut into it and nothing in the fourth head, which is the fourth return to come up that lane from that coast and is the ordinary thing, and the clerk of about twenty-two entered the day the boat came against it in the way he enters the day the boat comes against everything, and the woman of about forty-four who has been copying for about nineteen years copied it in about four minutes and did not say anything while she did it.

**The second thing was not a form.** It is about the size of a return and it is the same paper as the returns and it has four heads cut into it by somebody with a knife and not by a press, and there is a word in the fourth head, and under the word, in a smaller hand, four lines and no signature.

The four lines are a question.

*You have nine hundred and about four trades and one word. Who is in the room when you answer? Nobody asked us. We are asking you. A road that answers a question about itself is a road that has been found, and we know that, because one of us wrote it to you and not to anybody else.*

Nobody in that room said anything for about as long as a man takes to count nine.

“**Who is the one of you?**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**There is no one of us,**” the man of about thirty-four with a sheet said, who had come in at about the second hour and had read the four lines twice before anybody else had read them once. “**It says *one of us*. That is a hand that is not one person. That is about nine people who have all read it and all four of them left the pen alone for a day and then one of them asked whether they should sign it and somebody said no, and the reason somebody said no is this, and the reason somebody said no is that a question with a name at the bottom of it is a letter and a question with no name at the bottom of it is a question, and the difference between those two things has been the whole of this room for four months.**”

Nobody argued with him and nobody thanked him.

**Aren Kest, a man of thirty-two with a limp, had come in at about the fifth hour and had read the four lines before anybody else had finished the second one, and he did not read them out, and the clerk of about twenty-two said afterwards that he had watched the man of thirty-two read them and had watched him read the four words on the return underneath them, in one go, head to foot, about the length of a man saying nine.**

He said one thing about the four lines and it was not about the coast and it was not about the question, and nobody asked him for it and nobody argued with it.

“**A man who has been copying for nineteen years would have written *who is in the room when you answer* and stopped there,**” Aren Kest said, “**and the second sentence is the reason those four lines are worth a boat. It is the only sentence anybody has sent up that road that says what a room is for, and it was written by somebody who has never been in a room, and the two of those are not the same and the second one is the rarer.**”

“**You are telling nine counties that a stranger knows more about this room than we do,**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**I am telling you that you have a sheet on your table with a man down a lane who says a road that answers a question about itself has been found,**” Aren Kest said, “**and that he is right, and that you have had that on your table since the sixth of Harvestmonth and you have not written it down, and I am not going to say the rest of it in this room because the rest of it is about me and you asked me a question in Fallowmonth that I did not answer.**”

Nobody in that room asked him the rest of it.

“**They have not said a month or a day in about nine years,**” the woman of about thirty-four said. “**That is on the three returns and it is on the four lines and the clerk here has entered the day the boat came against all four of the sheets and every one of the four is the day the boat came, and nobody in nine counties can say by how much the day the boat came is not the day any of this was written, and the four lines on that page were written by somebody who has a way of counting things and the way of counting things on that sand is not ours and is not ours to correct.**”

The man of about thirty-four with a sheet said that he had looked at the four lines for about as long as it takes a man to say nine and that the reason he had looked at them that long was that the four lines were the first thing anybody has sent up that road in four months that is a question instead of an answer, and that a question which nobody asked cannot be answered by a man with a badge without turning the man with the badge into the person who asked it.

“**Then we do not answer it,**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**We do not answer it,**” the man of about thirty-four said, “**and I want that written on a leaf with no head over it, and I want it written that the reason is not that the question is bad.**”

“**It is a good question,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, and he did not say it as though it settled anything, and he said it the way a man says a thing he is going to have to defend in about four months.

The clerk of about twenty-two asked one question that afternoon, and the copyist of about twenty-six wrote it on a leaf with no head over it, and the leaf is on the table and the question is on the leaf and not in any book.

“**If it is a question and nobody asked it, then who is it addressed to?**”

“**It is addressed to whoever opens it,**” the woman of about thirty-four said, “**and the trouble with that is that in nine counties the answer to a question like that is whoever is nearest, and in four months that is going to be a man with a badge in a hired room, and the man with the badge is going to be right, because he is the one who opened it.**”

“**Then the room does not open it,**” the man of about thirty-four said, “**and the bundle goes up on the Monday with the return on top of it and the four lines underneath, and a man in a saddler's shop in this town opens a bundle about nine times a month and he opens it for the twopence and he takes the top sheet off and puts it on his board and hands the rest down, and a question that nobody asked goes through about nine hands before it reaches a person and every one of those nine hands is a bundle is a kind of wall.**”

Nobody argued with him, and the man of about fifty-eight said the bundle would go as the man of about thirty-four had said it, and said it was the fourth thing a person had told him in four months that he had done without arguing, and did not say who the other three were, and nobody asked.

**And nobody in that room sent anybody to that coast, and nobody has been sent in four months, and the form at the bottom of a return says in the same ink as everything else on it that nobody is to be sent to find out, and the twenty-second notch in a piece of driftwood at the seaward end of that sand is not cut, and the water has not been off that mark in twenty-two years.**

The man of about fifty-eight did one thing about the four lines and it was the ordinary thing and it was not an answer. He had the second return and the third return and the fourth return and the four lines laid out in the order they had come up the lane, and the copyist of about twenty-six copied the four lines in about nine minutes instead of four, and said out loud, as she had said out loud in Embermonth, that her hand had decided before she did.

“**You did that on the third return,**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**I did that on the third return,**” Nesta Braith said, “**and I am not going to tell you what my hand is doing today, and if you ask me again I will tell you the same sentence twice and then I will stop, and that is three sentences and I have said two of them in two counties and I would like to keep the third one for myself.**”

Nobody asked her again.

**And nothing of that coast was in that room but four returns and four lines and a question.** The eleven houses were not in the room. The woman of about fifty-one at the post was not in the room. The about nine hundred people on that sand are on no roll and no sheet and no list and the room did not put them on anything, and the tide decides twice a day on that sand and is not paid, and it decides twice a day in this town as well, and nobody in that room wrote either of those down.

**And the cost of that Saturday is a column and it is the clerk of about twenty-two's, and it is the second one he has ever kept, and he keeps it on the back of a leaf that is not one of the nine, and four lines with no signature on them are not in it.**

**a box of quills at fifteen, a bun at four, a jar of paste at twenty-three, a bundle of kindling at ten, a cake of ink at nineteen, a pot of tea at five, a ream of paper at seventeen, a pound of candles at eight, a rule of brass at thirteen, and a night's bed at eleven.** Fifteen, four, twenty-three, ten, nineteen, five, seventeen, eight, thirteen and eleven is a hundred and twenty-five pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and twenty-five pence is two marks and twenty-nine over**, and the sand's nine hundred is not in it, and the three series of that other room — nine hundred sheets a month, a hundred and four in one band, four hundred and nine parish rolls a month — are three separate counts of three separate series and none of the three is in this column and no two of the three are added, and the four hundred at the end of the made lane are not in it, nor the nine years, nor the twenty-two years, and the notches are years and are not tides and are not in it, and a question nobody asked is not in it.

The bundle went up the made road on the Monday morning with the return on top of it and the four lines underneath, and a man in a saddler's shop in that town opened it about four minutes before the copyist of about twenty-six could get to it, and nobody has asked him what he found and nobody was going to.
