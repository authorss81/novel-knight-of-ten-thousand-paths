# Chapter 0548

**In a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people, on the ninth of Mudmonth, YR 320, a Thursday and a market day, between the second hour and the ninth, a finding went up a made road on that same morning on a sheet of foolscap that is not a form and is not a return and is not off a press in this town, and it went in the fourth line of that sheet, and the fourth line of that sheet is a fourth line and not a fourth head, and it is on a different paper from the Crown's form and it is in a different hand from the Crown's form, and the page says so now, before anybody in that room can say it, and a head is a shape and not an order.**

The sheet is foolscap and it is the same foolscap nine returns a month come out of a press on about two hundred miles off, except that this one was not off a press. The copyist of about twenty-six ruled four lines on it at about the second hour with a straightedge and a knife, and she ruled them herself and nobody ordered it and nobody asked her to and she said one sentence about it before she did it and the sentence was that four lines is not four heads and that a head is a cut in a die and a line is a thing a person makes.

**Nobody in that room said the fourth head ought not to be there, and nobody in that room said the fourth head ought to be filled in, and the fourth head of the Crown's form on the table by that door is ruled and empty and was empty at the second hour and is empty at the ninth hour and will be empty on the morning the bundle goes, and no office in nine counties has filled it and no person in that room filled it and nobody filled any head on any form that day.**

The four lines are the finding. The first line is the finding about procedure, which is a rule and not a promise. The second line is the reason a rule about procedure is not a promise, which is that a rule about procedure is only binding on somebody who has been told what the procedure is, and about nine hundred sheets a month go out of nine counties of presses and not one of them prints the procedure anywhere. The third line is the consequence, which is that a man who is going to act on a road without the road's own answer to a question put to it in its own words is not breaking a rule and does not know he is doing a thing, and that about four years ago a man in a lane in another county did exactly that and about nine people on the far side of it were never told, and the man who did it is in this room and is not the man of thirty-two with a limp.

**And the fourth line is the finding with a man's name in it, and the name is the man of thirty-two with a limp's, and it is the only one of the four lines that has anybody's name on it, and it is not in a head, and there is no head over it, and the copyist of about twenty-six ruled nothing above it and ruled nothing below it and the line is a line.**

Here is the fourth line as it went up the made road, and it is on the page because about nine people in that room saw it before it went and about four of them can write and about five cannot.

*The room in a hired room over a saddler's in this town has found that an order may not act on a road without that road's own answer to a question put to it in its own words, and has recorded on the first page of a record made by Aren Kest, a man of thirty-two, a crossing he held for about a day and a night about nine people let over were not told about, which is the only instance in nine counties anybody in this room knows of.*

**And the fourth line of that sheet is a fourth line on a different paper in a different hand, and it is not the fourth head of the Crown's form, and it is not a fifth line, and the fourth head of the Crown's form is still empty, and nobody has filled it and nobody is going to and no office has and no person in that room did.**

The man of about fifty-eight ruled the line himself with a knife he has never used and it showed, and he said out loud that he had done it because a rule nobody has written down stops being a rule and becomes a habit, and four people's habit does not travel out of a hired room on its own, and that about four of the nine people in that room think that is right and about five think that a room which has just worked out what it cannot find and has then ruled a line over the saying of it has made a finding about its own limits and dressed it as a finding about the world.

**Nobody in that room voted. The clerk of about twenty-two took his hand off the pen and laid both hands flat on the open book and left them there, and the copyist of about twenty-six, who had had a knife in her hand since the second hour, put the knife down on the sill and did not pick it up again that morning, and the man of about fifty-three with a barrow got up and put his coat on over his shirt and did not say where he was going, and nobody in that room asked him.**

Then the man of about thirty-one with a pen said one thing about the fourth line and it is the only thing anybody said about it that day, and he said it in a room over a weigh-house two hundred miles off and it came up a made road on the Wednesday in a letter of one page and the letter is on the table.

“**You have put a man's name in the fourth line of a piece of paper that is going to nine counties and the name is the man of thirty-two with a limp, and about four of the nine people in this county have said that the man is the reason the rest of it stands up, and about four hundred people who came to the chandler's on the twenty-ninth of a month carried at thirty have said that the man is the reason anybody should have believed any of it in the first place, and neither of those two sets of people is going to be in this room, and the two sets do not overlap, and the man has not been asked which of the two he thinks is right and has not been asked and will not be.**”

Nobody thanked the man of about thirty-one with a pen for writing that and he did not ask to be thanked and he is on no sheet in nine counties and he is not a keeper of anything and he is not in the room over the saddler's.

**And nobody thanked Aren Kest for his name being in the fourth line, and he did not ask to be thanked, and he is not thanked before he leaves and is not thanked after, and the fourth line does not make him the reason anything works and the man of about fifty-eight said so out loud in front of about nine people and nobody argued with him and the copyist of about twenty-six wrote it on a bare leaf with no head over it and ruled nothing.**

Then the man of about thirty-four with a sheet did the thing that is the cost of the whole of what that room has done since the second of a month carried at thirty and nobody thanked him for it, and it is a thing nobody in that room could have done and about four of them had thought of doing and about five had not.

“**I have carried the first page of that record down about nine counties in a satchel since Goatmonth and I have read it out loud twice at a rail of a common and once in a bar at a crossroads and I never once read the man's name, and I have been asked about four times in about nine years why I carry a form around and I have not answered and the reason I have not answered is that a man who carries a page with another man's name at the top of it is a man who can be shown that name, and a name that can be shown is a name that can be said.**”

“**Then stop carrying it,**” the man of about fifty-three with a barrow said.

“**No,**” the man of about thirty-four with a sheet said, “**because the other half of that is that a road anybody can find out about can be shut, and about four hundred people at the end of a made lane four days off the other way are on a form and were not asked, and the only reason anybody in nine counties knows there is a fourth head on it at all is that about four hundred unregulated copies of a return went down a made road on a cart in a bundle about nine times a month and every one of them was made by a person in a kitchen and not by an office, and I have been the man who carried the first of them and I am going to keep carrying it and I am not going to be thanked for it and I am not going to say it twice.**”

Nobody in that room agreed with him out loud and nobody argued with him out loud.

**Nobody opened anything in that room on that Thursday and nobody walked out of it. The shut road four hundred miles off is not on anybody's list and is not going on one, and nobody in this county has set off toward it, and about nine people are on the other side of it than the side this room is on. Nobody here has used the word about that road, and in five months nobody in this county has used it about one thing.**

**On a sand four days off a woman of about fifty-one is at a post with a piece of driftwood in her hand, and the edge of it has twenty-two notches in it and the twenty-second is the one she has not cut, and the water has not come off that mark in twenty-two years, and each of them is a year and not a tide. Nobody has been sent to that coast. The sheet of foolscap that went up a made road that morning is not the sheet on a flat stone at the landward end of that sand: two documents, two counties, and neither of them has been in the same room as the other in this county since the twenty-fourth of Frostmonth and neither of them is going to be.**

**And the cost of that Thursday is a column and the column is the man of about thirty-eight with a satchel's, who has carried the satchel for about nine years, and it is the thirteenth one he has kept, and a man's name in the fourth line of a piece of foolscap is not in it.**

**a pot of tea at four, a stone of tallow at six, a second stone of tallow at nine, a night's straw at ten, a cake of ink at eleven, a third cake of ink at thirteen, a sheet of foolscap at fourteen, a second sheet of foolscap at sixteen, a third sheet of foolscap at thirty, a bundle of kindling at thirty-three, and a pound of candles at forty.** Four, six, nine, ten, eleven, thirteen, fourteen, sixteen, thirty, thirty-three and forty is a hundred and eighty-six pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and eighty-six pence is three marks and forty-two over**, and a name is not in it, and a fourth line is not in it and is not a fourth head and is not a fifth line, and a head is a shape and not an order and a shape is not in a column, and about four hundred who came to a chandler's and about four hundred at the end of a made lane are two counts of two different things and are not added, and about nine hundred on a sand four days off and about two thousand in this town are two more and are in neither end, and the four hundred and nine parish rolls a month and the nine hundred sheets a month and the hundred and four in a band are three counts of three series in a room two hundred miles off and none of the three is in this column, and the twenty-two notches and the twenty-two years are one figure told two ways and neither of them is in it, and about nine counties is not in it, and about nine days is not in it.

The bundle went up the made road at about the ninth hour with nine returns in it and the sheet of foolscap on the top of them, and a man in a saddler's shop in this town took the top sheet off that bundle about four minutes before the copyist of about twenty-six could get to it, and put it on his board, and handed the rest down, and nobody in nine counties has told that man what is on it and nobody was going to.
