# Chapter 0517

**The fifth day of Wolfmonth, YR 319, a Thursday and a market day, and the first hour and the third hour and the sixth hour, in a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people four days up a made road from a coast, and a woman of about thirty-four who has come into that room about nine times in about four months walked in at about the first hour with a sheet of paper in her hand and put it on the table face up and asked whether it was allowed, and the sheet is a copy of a return and the copy is bad.**

## One

**It is bad in four ways; the four ways are all the same way, which is that the copyist is not a copyist.**

The four heads are on it and the third one is under the second one, because the sheet she copied from had a crease in it and the crease ran through the column. The figure under the second head is there and it is **about four hundred**, and the trade under the third head is there and it is one of the four, and the fourth head is ruled and empty, and nobody in that room told her to rule it.

“**I copied that at a table in my own kitchen,**” the woman of about thirty-four said, “**and I did it in about nine minutes with a pen and a straightedge I borrowed, and I am not a copyist and I have never been one, and I would like to know before I go back whether I have done something wrong.**”

“**Who gave you the sheet you copied from?**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**A man in a saddler's shop took a copy out of a bundle on a cart and gave it to a woman at the end of a lane, and she gave it to four other people, and four of those four gave it to four more, and I am the ninth person to have had it and I do not know how many more there are and I am not going to try to find out because finding out is what the sheet says not to do.**”

**And that is one hundred and forty-nine words in three turns of one exchange, of which the first is fifty-four printed in two spans of ten and forty-four, the second is eight, and the third is eighty-seven, and about four hundred people live at the end of that lane and about four hundred is the figure on the sheet and the two are not added to each other, and nobody was thanked.**

## Two

“**It is not allowed,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, “**and it is not forbidden, and those are two different sentences and I am going to write both of them down and then one of us is going to have to say which one the room means.**”

“**That is not an answer.**”

“**It is the first honest thing anybody in this room has said to you in about four months,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, “**because a copy is not a Crown thing. There is no head anywhere in nine counties for a copy. There is no head for a form that goes out and comes back, and there is a head for about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month that come in, and there is not one single word in about four months of instruction about a woman in a kitchen with a pen.**”

“**Then I have not done anything wrong.**”

“**You have not,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, “**and I have just told a room of about nine people that a piece of paper has walked out of a bundle in a saddler's shop and into four hundred kitchens and that nobody in nine counties has a head for it, and about four of the nine people in this room have just understood that, and about five of the nine have not.**”

**And that is two hundred and ten words in five turns of one exchange, of which the first is forty-one printed in two spans of four and thirty-seven, the second is five, the third is ninety printed in two spans of eighteen and seventy-two, the fourth is seven, and the fifth is sixty-seven printed in two spans of three and sixty-four, and a chairman of about fifty-eight with a chain of office round his neck said out loud in a room of about nine people that there is no head anywhere in nine counties for a copy, and the chain of office is a badge and is not in this sentence and is not in the cost of that Thursday.**

## Three

**And the woman of about thirty-four brought a second sheet, and that is the whole of what she came in with, and the second sheet is the same return with a different first line on it.**

“**That one is not the same,**” she said. “**The first line on that is not the word on yours.**”

“**What is it?**”

“**The other word,**” the woman of about thirty-four said, “**and nine of us at the end of that lane have compared our copies, and about nine of the nine have found that we do not all have the same first line, and I have brought two, and both of them are true, and I cannot find anybody who can tell me which one is the right one, because the man who would know the word your clerk wrote is at the rail of the common four miles off and the woman who would know the word on my second sheet is at the eleventh house and has never been to the rail.**”

“**Then you have two copies of one road and one line to put them in,**” the man of about thirty-four with a satchel said, who had been at the back of that room since the third hour, “**and no person in nine counties who can be asked about both of them.**”

“**That is what I am telling you,**” the woman of about thirty-four said, “**and I have told it to a room and about four of the nine people in it have understood it and about five of them have asked me which word I would rather had been on the original.**”

**And that is two hundred words in five turns of one exchange, of which the first is seventeen printed in two spans of six and eleven, the second is three, the third is one hundred and six printed in two spans of three and one hundred and three, the fourth is twenty-nine printed in two spans of fifteen and fourteen, and the fifth is forty-five printed in two spans of seven and thirty-eight, and a woman of about thirty-four brought two pieces of paper into a room and the two of them disagree about a place and both of them are right and the room did not choose and did not schedule choosing and about nine people at the end of a made lane are not on any sheet in nine counties except this one and they are not a list and they are not a district.**

## Four

**A man of thirty-two with a limp was in that room from the third hour and was not asked one question, and what he said was to the two sheets and not to anybody, and it was three turns long and the first of the three is twelve words.**

“**A copy of a form finds out more slowly than the form.**”

“**Slower?**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**Later,**” Aren Kest said, “**and in a different direction, and by a longer road, and nobody has to sign anything, and that is the only reason nine counties have not found out what nine counties are doing.**”

**And that is forty-seven words in three turns of one exchange, of which the first is twelve, the second is one, and the third is thirty-four printed in two spans of one and thirty-three, and nobody wrote any of it down and the woman of about thirty-four packed her two sheets back into her own bag and did not leave them,** **and about four people in that town have it that the two of them were the only two people in that room who understood what had happened and about nine have said that neither of them has a job, and those are two sets that do not overlap and neither of them is a compliment.**

**And nobody was thanked. The copyist of about twenty-six looked at the two bad copies for about as long as a woman takes to say four and then went back to a third return from a coast four days off, which is a different document from these two and is not the same document as the sheet on a flat stone at the landward end of that coast, and she did not say so and nobody asked her.**

## Five

The cost of that Thursday is a column and it is the woman of about thirty-four's, and it is the first one she has ever kept, and she keeps it in the back of the bad copy, and a pen and a straightedge she borrowed are not in it.

**a bootlace at one, a second broom at five, a second bed at four, a third bed at four, a third pot for the paste at five, a bed at four, a fourth bed at four, a second cord at one, a broom at five, bread at four, a second paper at nine, a second bucket of tar at eight, cheese at four, a second cheese at four, a cord at one, a third cheese at four, a second rule at four, a bucket of tar at eight, ink at three, a third paper at nine, a rule at four, a pot for the paste at five, paper at nine, a third rule at four, a sixth bed at four, a pound of candles at four, a fifth bed at four, a second ink at three, and a second pot for the paste at five.** One, five, four, four, five, four, four, one, five, four, nine, eight, four, four, one, four, four, eight, three, nine, four, five, nine, four, four, four, four, three and five is a hundred and thirty-three pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and thirty-three pence is two marks and thirty-seven over**, and two words for one lane are not in it, and about four hundred at the end of a made lane are not in it, and about four hundred on the second copy of the figure under the same head is not in it either, and the two about four hundreds are not added to each other, and a form with a fourth head ruled and empty is not in it, and a fifth line is not in it and is not a fourth head.

**And on the fifth day of Wolfmonth in this year a woman of about thirty-four copied a return for herself in about nine minutes at a table in her own kitchen with a borrowed straightedge, and brought it into a hired room over a saddler's and asked whether it was allowed, and was told that it is not allowed and is not forbidden and that those are two different sentences, and about four hundred people at the end of a made lane have each got a copy now and about nine of them have compared them and the nine of them do not agree about a place and both of them are right, and nobody in nine counties has a head for a copy, and the tide decides twice a day and is not paid and is not on any of this.**
