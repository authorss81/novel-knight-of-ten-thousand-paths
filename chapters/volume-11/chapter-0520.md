# Chapter 0520

**The thirteenth day of Wolfmonth, YR 319, a Friday and not a market day, and the second hour and the sixth hour and the tenth hour, in a market town of about two thousand people four days up a made road from a coast, in a hired room over a saddler's, and the room has been shut on Sundays and open on six other days since the twenty-third day of Fallowmonth, and a Sunday went and a Monday went and four days have gone since, and this is a Friday, and it is the last of them, and nothing has been decided in that room since the second day of Embermonth and nothing is going to be decided before the room shuts for the day.**

## One

**What went up the made road on the Monday was nine returns and four ruled heads, and one of the four ruled heads had a fifth line on it, and the fifth line was ruled in about four minutes on the Monday morning by a clerk of about twenty-eight with a form who was matching the sheet to a book and who has never heard of the fifth line that came off a different sheet in a different county in Embermonth.**

Nobody ordered that fifth line. It is ruled from the same left-hand edge to the same right-hand place. It has no head over it. **It is empty, and it was empty when it went, and nobody in nine counties can fill it and nobody in nine counties has.**

**And in about four years a man with nine counties of forms in his hands will be able to tell which county a line was ruled in, and he will not be able to tell who ruled it, and there is no head anywhere in nine counties for either of those facts.**

## Two

**Two copies of one return are now in two counties; they do not agree about the first line, both of them are right, and this is not a decision and it is not a resolution and it is not going to become either.**

One of the two is in a hired room over a saddler's in this town, and it is a bad copy written in about nine minutes at a table in a kitchen with a borrowed straightedge, and it says the name that a man of about forty-one with a cart knew on the day in Harvestmonth when he walked half of a made lane and came back with a figure of about four hundred and four trades.

The other of the two went up a made road on a Monday in a bundle and is now two hundred miles off in a room over a weigh-house with a door on a lane, and it says the other name, and the woman who wrote it wrote it because six people at the end of that lane have never once used the first one.

“**Which of the two goes in the book?**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**The one that was in this room first,**” the woman of about thirty-four said, “**and the other one does not get burned, and the two of them are not going to be compared, and if anybody in nine counties ever wants to know what a place is called at the end of a made lane then there are two answers and they are both on paper and neither of those two answers is wrong.**”

“**That is not a record.**”

“**That is the first thing anybody in this room has recorded in about four months,**” the woman of about thirty-four said, “**and I would like somebody to rule a line and write on it that it is a record and not a mistake, because in about four years it is going to look like a mistake and I am not going to be in that room at all then.**”

Nobody ruled a line and the man of about fifty-eight said that he would rule one himself on the Monday and did not.

**And that is one hundred and forty-four words in four turns of one exchange, of which the first is eight, the second is sixty-eight printed in two spans of eight and sixty, the third is five, and the fourth is sixty-three printed in two spans of fifteen and forty-eight, and a woman of about thirty-four asked for a ruled line to say on it that a thing was a record and not a mistake, and did not get one, and about four people in that room have it that she was asking for the only thing in four months that anybody in that room has actually wanted.**

**And a man of about fifty-three with a barrow can be found at the rail of a common at the landward end of that lane, and a woman of about twenty-seven can be found at the eleventh of eleven houses, and a clerk of about thirty-four has walked the whole mile and a half and can be asked about both, and the road cannot be found at either and is not going to be.**

## Three

**A man of thirty-two with a limp was in that room at the sixth hour and at the tenth hour and said seven sentences in all, and none of the seven was about anything that had happened to him, and about four of the nine people in that room heard all of them and about five heard six.**

“**The two copies are not an error,**” Aren Kest said. “**A man who fills in a form is not lying when he writes the name he already knows, and the form told him to write the name the people at the end of the road use, and the fault is not the man's and it is not the woman's. The fault is a head cut into a piece of wood with one line under it for a place that has two words on it, and that head was cut before any of us were born and it will be cut long after. A copy of a form is the only form in nine counties nobody has ever thought to regulate, and there are about four hundred people at the end of that lane with one each, and about nine of them have put two side by side, and the nine of them do not agree. And the word in the fourth head of one returned form is a woman's and it came off a pen in a county that is not this one, and the fourth head on the form on this table is empty, and those are two documents, and one of them is not the form.**”

“**Then what is the room for?**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**The room is where the two copies meet,**” Aren Kest said, “**and they have not met, and I do not know that they ever will, and I am not going to tell this room that a man of thirty-two with a limp is the one who will make them meet.**”

**And that is two hundred and fifty-eight words in three turns of one exchange, of which the first is two hundred and five printed in two spans of seven and one hundred and ninety-eight, the second is six, and the third is forty-seven printed in two spans of eight and thirty-nine, and a man of thirty-two with a limp was not asked a question and answered nothing and said seven sentences that were not about him, and about four people in that room have it that he was careful and about five have said that a man who is careful in a room is a man who is managing what a room thinks of him, and the two sets do not overlap and neither of them is right.**

## Four

**And the hearing has not been adjourned and has not been refused and has not been retold and has not been closed, and the register of a room two hundred miles off is at seven questions and no findings, and the day's book in that room is at nine entries of a thing nobody asked, and the count on a page under a fourth step of an outside stair is nine, and a bench has been asked nine things in about nineteen years, and none of those four is added to another and none of them is a figure of a person.**

The corridor's rule is bad and its rate is not printed. About eleven hundred people are on no roll and no sheet and no list. The two villages are not back on a road. **A shut road four hundred miles off is not reopened and is not scheduled and nobody has been sent to it, and about nine people are on the wrong side of it.**

The city is on a shelf. The valley is unreachable. Nothing has been opened. The twenty-second notch in a piece of driftwood at the seaward end of a coast four days off is not cut, and the water has not been off that mark in twenty-two years, and about nine hundred people on that sand have three returns with a word under the first head of each and an empty fourth head on each, and no month and no day has been said aloud on that sand in about nine years.

**And the fourth head on the Crown's form on the table in that room is empty, and it is empty because nobody has filled it, and the word in the fourth head of a return that came up a made road in Wolfmonth belongs to a woman of about fifty-three who takes in washing, and those are two documents in two counties and one of them is not the form.**

## Five

The cost of that Friday is a column and it is the man of about thirty-eight with a satchel's, and it is the tenth one he has kept in about nine years, and two copies of one return are not in it and a fifth line is not in it and a road is not in it.

**a cab to the made road at nine, a second pound of candles at four, a third cheese at four, a second bucket of tar at eight, a pound of candles at four, a second quill and ink at three, a rule at four, a pot of tea at three, a quill and ink at three, a third cab to the made road at nine, a second rule at four, a stone of tallow at five, a second stone of tallow at five, a second cab to the made road at nine, a second week's bed at nine, a third broom at five, a third pot of tea at three, a second cake of ink at three, a cake of ink at three, a week's bed at nine, a broom at five, a third pound of candles at four, a second cheese at four, a second broom at five, a third bucket of tar at eight, a bucket of tar at eight, a pot for the paste at five, a second pot of tea at three, cheese at four, and a nail for the board at one.** Nine, four, four, eight, four, three, four, three, three, nine, four, five, five, nine, nine, five, three, three, three, nine, five, four, four, five, eight, eight, five, three, four and one is a hundred and fifty-three pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and fifty-three pence is three marks and nine over**, and about four hundred at the end of a made lane are not in it, and about four hundred on a return are not in it, and about two thousand in this town are not in it, and about nine hundred on a sand four days off are not in it, and about nine hundred sheets a month and about a hundred and four in one band and about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month are not in it, and none of those is added to another, and a sixteenth of an inch is not in it and is a difference in a ruled line and is not a figure of persons, of money, of days or of distance.

**And on the thirteenth day of Wolfmonth in this year two copies of one return were in two counties and did not agree about a place, and both of them were right, and a man of about fifty-three with a barrow could be found at one end of a made lane and a woman of about twenty-seven could be found at the other, and the road could be found at neither, and the fourth head on the form on the table in that room was empty and the word in the fourth head of a returned form belonged to a woman of about fifty-three who takes in washing, and those are two documents and one of them is not the form, and the hearing stands, and the tide decides twice a day and is not paid.**
