# Chapter 0507

**The eighteenth day of Harvestmonth, YR 319, a Thursday and a market day, and the first hour and the fifth hour and the tenth hour, in a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people four days up a made road from a coast, and five counties out of nine have answered a return, and about nine people are in that room at the fifth hour, and the question that is in that room at the fifth hour is not how many roads there are and has not been since the first hour.**

## One

**The man of about fifty-eight put the question himself at about the first hour, and it is thirteen words printed in two spans, and about four of the people in that room had already asked it in another room in about nine years and about five of the nine had never thought of it.**

“**How many roads can be written on a return,**” he said, “**without somebody walking them?**”

Nobody answered for about as long as it takes a man to say nine. **And the man of about thirty-eight with a satchel wrote the question on the top of a leaf in his own book, and it is the only question in that book that is not about carts, and the copyist of about twenty-six saw him write it and did not say anything and has said since the first hour that she wanted to copy it and was not asked and was not going to ask.**

## Two

“**That is a different question from the one you have been asking,**” Gault Prentice said, at about the fifth hour, “**and you have known it was a different question since the first hour, and you have let four hundred returns come in under the other one.**”

“**I have let about ninety come in,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, “**and four of the ninety are the same return twice and I have said so in the book.**”

“**Then the number is about ninety and not four hundred.**”

“**The number is about ninety,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, “**and about nine counties have sent in about nine returns each and I have not been told whether the nine are a list or a sample and I have not asked, because if I ask then the nine is a list, and if it is a list then it is a thing I have made, and I have enough things I have made.**”

“**You have one thing you have made,**” Gault Prentice said, “**and it is on a board outside a weigh-house in this town and about nine people copied an address off one of the two sheets on it and about four of the nine do not know which one they copied.**”

The man of about fifty-eight took off the chain of office and put it on the table, which about four people in that room have said since was the first time anybody had seen him do it.

“**Then the thing I have made is worse than I thought,**” he said, “**and I will take it down on Saturday, and I will do it in the room and not in the street, and about four hundred people in this town will walk past a board with nothing on it and about nine of them will have copied something off it.**”

**And that is two hundred and forty-eight words in six turns of one exchange, of which the first is thirty-eight printed in two spans of twelve and twenty-six, the second is twenty-five printed in two spans of seven and eighteen, the third is ten, the fourth is sixty-eight printed in two spans of five and sixty-three, the fifth is forty-seven printed in two spans of seven and forty, and the sixth is sixty printed in two spans of eleven and forty-nine, and about four counties and about nine counties and about ninety returns and about four hundred people are four different counts and none of them is added to another in that room and about nine returns a county is not a list and has not been turned into one.**

## Three

**And a second return came up that lane from that coast on the Tuesday before this one, and it is the second one that has ever come back from that coast, and the three filled heads on it are the same as the three filled heads on the first one and the fourth head is empty on it as it was on the first one, and the two are in the same hand and the two are not in the same ink.**

The first head on both is the same word, and it is the word the people at the end of that place use for the place, and about four people in that room can read it as a place and about four of the nine say it is a name and about one of the four has said nothing at all.

The second head on both is **about nine hundred.** The third head on both is four trades. **The fourth head on both is empty, and there is no instruction in nine counties that says what goes in it, and a clerk of about twenty-two has entered a date on both and the two dates are nine days apart and neither of them is the day anything happened.**

“**They are the same road,**” the woman of about thirty-four said, at about the seventh hour, “**and the clerk has to write down which one of the two is the true one, and he cannot, and if he writes down neither then the book has a road in it twice and a man in about four years will count the road twice.**”

“**Write down both,**” the man of about thirty-eight with a satchel said.

“**Write down both?**”

“**Write down both,**” the man of about thirty-eight with a satchel said, “**and write down that they are the same road and that you do not know which is which, and then a man in about four years who has a whole county's worth of returns can look at the two and see the one that comes back a third time, and the third one is the road and the other two are a road somebody told us about.**”

**And that is one hundred and twenty-seven words in four turns of one exchange, of which the first is five, the second is forty-nine printed in two spans of forty-six and three, the third is three, and the fourth is seventy printed in two spans of three and sixty-seven, and a man of about thirty-eight with a satchel has carried a book of carts for about nine years and has just given an instruction about a book of returns and does not know whether he is right and did not wait to find out.**

## Four

**And what the room decided on the Thursday at about the tenth hour is that a clerk goes down a road rather than a road being asked, and about nine people were in that room and about four of the nine voted and about five of the nine did not vote and there was no count taken and no minute of the vote exists, and the clerk is a man of about thirty-four who has walked about nine roads in this county in eleven years and is not a keeper of anything and is not on a list.**

**And the return itself says, at the bottom, in the same hand and the same ink as everything else on it, *Nobody is to be sent to find out*, and the room has decided to send somebody, and the two of those facts have been in the same room since the first hour of the first day and about nine people had not put them together until the tenth hour of the eighteenth day of a month carried at thirty.**

## Five

The cost of that Thursday is a column and it is the man of about fifty-eight's, and it is the second one he has kept in about a month, and the chain of office is not in it and is a badge and not an instrument.

**a sheet of paper at one, a second sheet of paper at one, a third sheet of paper at one, a quill and ink at three, a second quill and ink at three, a knife at two, a second knife at two, a rule at four, a second rule at four, paste at two, a pot for the paste at five, a second pot for the paste at five, a loaf at one, a second loaf at one, a third loaf at one, cheese at four, a second cheese at four, small beer at two, a second small beer at two, a third small beer at two, a broom at five, a bucket of tar at eight, a second bucket of tar at eight, a week's bed at nine, a second week's bed at nine, a waxed cord at four, a second waxed cord at four, an inkwell at three, a bootlace at one, oil for the lamp at one, a farthing at one, a second farthing at one, a third farthing at one, a fourth farthing at one, and a fifth farthing at one.** one, one, one, three, three, two, two, four, four, two, five, five, one, one, one, four, four, two, two, two, five, eight, eight, nine, nine, four, four, three, one, one, one, one, one, one, and one is one hundred and seven pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and seven pence is two marks and eleven over**, and two marks is ninety-six pence, and about ninety returns and about four hundred people and about nine returns a county and about four counties are four counts of four different things and are not in it and are not added, and a word under a first head and about nine hundred under a second head and nothing under a fourth head are three things on one sheet and are a return and are not in it.

**And on the eighteenth day of Harvestmonth in this year a man of about fifty-eight in a hired room over a saddler's took a chain of office off and put it on a table and said the thing he had made was worse than he thought, and a room of about nine people decided that a clerk goes down a road rather than a road being asked, and the form in front of them says at the bottom in the same ink that nobody is to be sent to find out, and the clerk is a man of about thirty-four who is not on a list and is not going to be, and a form is a thing that finds out, and about nine hundred people on a coast four days off have two returns and a word under the first head of both and nothing at all under the fourth, and the tide decides twice a day and is not paid and is not on any of this.**
