# Chapter 0543

**A market was going on in the street below the one open window of a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people for the whole of the eleventh of Thawmonth, YR 320, a Thursday and a market day, from the first hour to the eighth, and a letter went up a made road on the Monday morning with a sheet of oiled paper round it and no names on it, and that letter has been on the table in a room in a market town of about two thousand people four days up a made road from a coast since the sixth of Harvestmonth, and the four names are still off it, and nobody in nine counties has sent them.**

There are two sets of four ruled heads in nine counties and that is the whole of what was on that table.

One set is in this room. The other is in a room over a weigh-house with a door on a lane about two hundred miles off. **Nobody outside those two rooms knows the other one exists, and the two sets are not copies of one another, and the two sets are not a return and not a form and not a book and not a register, and neither of them has a name, and neither of them has a keeper, and neither of them has a day.**

They are four heads. A place. A figure of people. A trade. And a fourth, ruled, empty.

**The second set's fourth head is about a sixteenth of an inch narrower than the three above it, and the first set's fourth head is not, and that is the whole of the difference between them, and the reason was given once in this room, to about four of the people in it and to nobody outside it, and the reason is that a word written in that head in one county will not go into the other, and a man in about four years with nine counties of forms in his hands will be able to tell which county a word was written in without being told.**

Then the offer was answered, and it was answered on the twelfth of a month carried at thirty in the year before this one and it has been on the table ever since, and the answer is that the four names go to nine counties so that a road may complain to a person, and the four names are not written on anything in this county and are not written on that page and have not been written on anything in five months.

Nobody thanked the man who offered it and he was not thanked when he made it and has not been thanked since, and he is a man of about fifty-one in a grey coat and he did not say whose plan it is and has never said whose plan it is and did not say who he answers to.

The room went over the offer again on the eleventh of a month carried at thirty and it took about as long as a man takes to say nine, and nobody argued about whether it was good, because about four of the nine people in that room think it is the best thing anybody in nine counties has put on a table in five months and about five think it is the worst, and the two sets do not overlap and the room did not vote and has never voted about it and is not going to.

The man of about thirty-four with a sheet put the two sets side by side on the table at about the fourth hour. He had the one from this county and the one from the weigh-house, and he put them about two inches apart, and about two inches is everything and about a sixteenth of an inch is nothing, and he said the reason out loud and nobody stopped him.

“**These two things will be in nine counties in about nine days and there will be no head in any of the nine that says where either of them came from, and that is not a fault in them, it is the reason they are worth having, and if you put a head on either of them then within a year somebody will be asked to fill it and a man who fills it will be a man who has been given a thing to do and a road that does not want to be found will be a road that has been given a form to refuse on.**”

“**Then they are nothing,**” the woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key said.

“**They are nine leaves,**” the man of about thirty-four with a sheet said, “**and a leaf is not nothing, and a leaf with four heads on it and a question written under the fourth one by a person is the only document in this county that can go out and come back with something in it that nobody in an office chose, and a leaf is not an institution and a leaf has no keeper and a leaf has no day, and anybody in this room who wants to make it one of those three things can go and do it and I will not stop them and I will not write a second line on either of them.**”

The copyist of about twenty-six was at the side table with the straightedge and she said one thing, and it was not about the fourth head and it was not the second time she has said the other thing.

“**I ruled the fourth line in that other county a sixteenth of an inch narrower and I have not told anybody in this county why and I am not going to, and I want it written down that I have not told this room why and that some of you have worked it out between you and that the rest of you are not going to be told, and that is not a rule, it is a thing I did in a room in another county in Fallowmonth and nobody asked me to.**”

Nobody argued with her and nobody thanked her and nobody agreed with her out loud, and the man of about fifty-eight wrote it on a bare leaf with no head over it and said out loud that he was not going to cut a head over it either, and that a leaf with five things on it and no head is going to be the most useful object in that room and that nobody was to be allowed to say so.

**And the answer to the offer was that the four names go up the made road to nine counties in a sheet of oiled paper with nothing on the outside of it, and that is the whole of the answer, and it is a worse answer than the letter it came in, and the man of about fifty-one in a grey coat said out loud that it was worse and that he was not going to argue with it and that he would rather be argued with than not.**

Nobody in that room agreed with him out loud and nobody argued with him out loud, and the woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key said one thing about the four names and it was about the size of the room and not about the names.

“**Four names at the top of a sheet that goes into nine counties is about four doors, and about nine hundred sheets a month go out of one room two hundred miles off on a fixed day, and there are about nine counties and a person in one of them can be walked to in about nine days on a made road and a cart, and I have been in this room four months and I have been the only person in it who has ever been walked to, and I am not going to say anything else about that and I am not going to say it again.**”

Nobody thanked her for saying it, and the man of about fifty-one in a grey coat looked at the table for about as long as a man takes to count four and then said one word and the word was not a name and was not a sentence.

**And that is the whole of what the offer got and the four names are still off the letter and the letter is going up a made road in a sheet of oiled paper with nothing on the outside of it, and a man in a saddler's shop in this town opens a bundle about nine times a month and takes the top sheet off and puts it on his board and hands the rest down, and about nine people in nine counties will hold a piece of paper with four names at the top of it in about nine days and about nine of those nine will be people who asked to be named, and the other half of that figure is not a figure of persons and is not in that figure.**

Aren Kest, a man of thirty-two with a limp, was in that room from the first hour to the eighth and was asked nothing about a form and nothing about the fourth head and nothing about the two sets, and he said three things, and half of that room has it that the first of the three was the reason the room got to the answer at all, and the other half has said that a man who says three things and is right about all of them is a man who has been in nine counties for about four years, and neither half is wrong, and nobody in that room thanked him and he was not asked to be thanked.

**And nothing was opened and nobody was sent, and the fourth head of the Crown's form on the table by that door is ruled and empty and has been empty for about five months, and no office has filled it and no person has filled it and nobody in that room filled any head on any form.**

**Four days off, at the seaward end of a sand of two miles and about nine hundred paces, a woman of about fifty-one stands at a post with twenty-two notches cut into the edge of a piece of driftwood, and the twenty-second of them has not been cut, and the sea has not been off that mark in twenty-two years, and a notch there is a year and not a tide, and no person in this county has been to that coast and no person out of it has either, and she has not been asked and has not been thanked and has not been written to.**

**And the cost of that Thursday is a column and it is the man of about thirty-four with a sheet's, and it is the fourteenth one he has kept in about nine years, and two sets of four ruled heads and a difference of about a sixteenth of an inch and four names still off a letter are not in it.**

**a second pound of candles at one, a bun at two, a third broom at three, a piece of chalk at five, a second stone of tallow at six, a sheet of oiled paper at eight, a second pot of paste at eleven, a cake of ink at thirteen, a ream of paper at nineteen, a pound of beeswax at thirty, and a night's straw at thirty-three.** One, two, three, five, six, eight, eleven, thirteen, nineteen, thirty and thirty-three is a hundred and thirty-one pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and thirty-one pence is two marks and thirty-five over**, and two sets of four heads are not in it, and a sixteenth of an inch is not in it because a sixteenth of an inch is a difference in a ruled line and is not a figure of persons, of money, of days or of distance, and the four names are not in it and about nine days is not in it, and a room two hundred miles off takes in about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month and sends out about a hundred and four in a band and about nine hundred sheets a month, and those are three separate series counted three separate ways, and not one of the three of them is in this column and no two of them are added here, and about four hundred at the end of a made lane are not in it, and about nine hundred on that sand are not in it, and about two thousand in this town are not in it, and those are three counts of three different things and go in at neither end of this column.

The letter went up the made road on the Monday in a sheet of oiled paper with nothing on the outside of it, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet put it in the bundle himself at about the eighth hour and wrote the day at the top of a page in the room's book and did not write the year anywhere on it, and the year was Thawmonth and it was the eleventh and the clerk of about twenty-two did not say so, and nobody in that room said which year it was except the man of about fifty-eight, who said it out loud to an empty room at about the ninth hour and then locked the door and went down the stairs.
