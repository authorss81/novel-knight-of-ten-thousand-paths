# Chapter 0503

**The fourth day of Harvestmonth, YR 319, a Thursday and a market day, and the second hour and the fifth hour and the ninth hour, in one room over a weigh-house with a door on a lane about two hundred miles up a made road from a coast, and a man of about fifty-one in a grey coat comes up that lane at about the second hour on foot with a satchel and does not send a rider, and he is in that room for about six hours, and about nine hundred sheets a month go out of that room, about a hundred and four of them in one band on a fixed day, and about four hundred and nine parish rolls in at the door, and those are three series and the three of them are not added.**

## One

**The man of about fifty-one in a grey coat said his own name once, at the second hour, and did not say whose plan it was and did not say who he answered to, and about four people in that room have since said that they knew exactly who he was and about nine have said they did not, and neither of those is a fact about him and both of them are facts about the county.**

He put the satchel on the table, took out four returns, and set them in a row with the heads at the same angle, and then he read the sheet of Mara Vey's paper out loud from the top, including the fourteen words under the fourth head, and he read the fourteen words twice and put it down and did not say anything about them for about as long as it takes a man to say nine.

“**I have come up a lane on foot,**” he said, “**because I wanted to see the room before I wrote anything else about it, and I have now seen it, and it is smaller than I was told it was, and that is not a complaint, it is the reason I came.**”

“**It is one room,**” Mara Vey said.

“**It is one room with a door on a lane,**” the man of about fifty-one in a grey coat said, “**and I have been building that sentence for about four months, and you have just made it shorter than mine, and I would like to know whether you did that on purpose.**”

“**I did it because it is the whole of it,**” Mara Vey said, “**and I have four and a half years of a room that is one room with a door on a lane and I am not going to add a clause to it for a visitor.**”

**And that is one hundred and forty-one words in four turns of one exchange, of which the first is fifty printed in two spans of eight and forty-two, the second is four, the third is forty-two printed in two spans of ten and thirty-two, and the fourth is forty-five printed in two spans of ten and thirty-five, and the man of about fifty-one in a grey coat did not answer the question he had asked and has not answered it and the woman of about thirty-one with a pen wrote down the word *smaller* and nothing else and it is the only word in that room's book for that day.**

## Two

**And he made the offer at about the fifth hour, and it is a real offer and it is not a trick, and he is right about the danger it is built against, and the thing he is wrong about is a number.**

“**Here is what I have got,**” Gault Prentice said. “**Nine counties. About nine hundred returns a month out of rooms like this one, and about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month coming the other way, and I am not adding those two and neither are you. The thing that is coming is a form with four heads on it, and three of them can be filled in by anybody who has walked the road, and the fourth one cannot be filled in by anybody at all, and what I want is for all of it to come to one room in this town and be read by four people who have put their names at the top of a sheet.**”

“**Four,**” Mara Vey said.

“**Four,**” Gault Prentice said, “**and no more, and the names go at the top, and anybody in nine counties who wants to see one of those sheets has to come and ask one of the four of us and say why, and we write the reason down.**”

“**And the four hundred people?**” the woman of about thirty-one with a pen said, from the far end of the room, and she had not spoken in about two hours.

“**Which four hundred?**”

“**On the one that came back on the Thursday before,**” the woman of about thirty-one with a pen said. “**There is a figure of forty-one against a road nine miles off and there was one of thirty-eight against another and they are not the same road. That is eighty-nine people on two sheets and neither of them has been told.**”

“**Eighty-nine,**” Gault Prentice said, and he did not correct the figure and he did not add to it, and he looked at the two returns on the table for about as long as it takes a man to say four.

“**Yes,**” he said, “**and in about nine months, if this goes the way it is going, there will be a great many more than eighty-nine, and you are asking me how to stop that and I do not have an answer that does not involve a room.**”

**And that is two hundred and sixty-nine words in eight turns of one exchange, of which the first is a hundred and nineteen printed in two spans of six and a hundred and thirteen, the second is one, the third is forty-four printed in two spans of one and forty-three, the fourth is five, the fifth is three, the sixth is fifty-one printed in two spans of ten and forty-one, the seventh is one, and the eighth is forty-five printed in two spans of one and forty-four, and the man of about fifty-one in a grey coat put his own arithmetic next to another person's arithmetic in the same sentence and did not combine them and the room has not combined them since.**

## Three

**And a man of thirty-two with a limp was in that room from the third hour and he was there for six hours and he said about nine sentences in all of them, and three of the nine were the name of a road and none of the nine was about anything that had happened to him.**

Aren Kest had come up the lane on the Tuesday because there is a press in that room and there is nothing in nine counties that will tell a man what a return says about a road once it has been folded twice, and he had read about four hundred and nine of them over two days and had not said so to anybody, and the woman of about thirty-one with a pen had brought him a cup at the fourth hour without asking whether he wanted one.

**He and Mara Vey had not been in a room together since a Tuesday in Rainmonth in the year before this one and there is a thing between them that neither of them has said in either direction, and it is not said here, and it is said on the page once and not said again.**

“**You have read about four hundred of those returns,**” Gault Prentice said to him, at about the fifth hour. “**Then tell me what one of them is for.**”

Aren Kest looked at the return on the table for about as long as it takes a man to say four.

“**A road is a place, a figure of people, and what is carried on it,**” he said, “**and the form asks for those three because a county cannot keep a list of anything else, and the fourth head is not a fourth thing about a road. It is a line. Somebody cut it, and nobody has ever written a word under it, and it is still on every sheet that comes back, and a line that is there and empty is a question that a form asks on its own.**”

“**Who cut it?**”

“**I do not know,**” Aren Kest said, “**and I have been in nine counties asking about four years and I have asked a man who has been in this county for about thirty-one years and he does not know either, and I have stopped saying that as a thing that is being kept from me, because I have never found anybody who was paid to keep it.**”

**And that is one hundred and seventy-three words in five turns of one exchange, of which the first is nine, the second is twenty-four printed in two spans of nine and fifteen, the third is seventy-three, the fourth is three, and the fifth is sixty-four printed in two spans of four and sixty, and about four people in that room heard the whole of it and about nine of the nine heard the last turn of it, and Gault Prentice did not write anything down and did not say that he would and did not say that he would not.**

## Four

**And he is right about the danger, and the room agreed with him about it, and the man of about thirty-eight with a satchel from a board outside a weigh-house in a town four days off this coast is the reason and is not in the room.**

On the first of a month carried at thirty in the year before this one a man of about thirty-eight with a satchel came into that room, asked for a return on a road he named, was told there was one, was told there was no charge, and copied it out in about nine minutes at a table by the door, and nobody stopped him and nobody thought to and the sheet went out under his arm into a street.

**That is the whole of his case and it is a good one and Mara Vey has never denied it and did not deny it on that Thursday, and a man of about fifty-one in a grey coat did not put it in front of her as an argument, he put it on the table in the second hour and let it sit there for six hours.**

“**You have not asked me the question,**” Gault Prentice said, at about the ninth hour, when the light had gone out of the lane and the lamp was lit and the two of them were the only people left in the room.

“**I decided not to ask it,**” Mara Vey said.

“**That is worse than asking it,**” Gault Prentice said. “**I can argue with an answer.**”

“**You can,**” Mara Vey said, “**and I have about four and a half years of not having asked it, and you have been in this room six hours, and you have not noticed that you did not ask it either.**”

**And that is sixty-two words in five turns of one exchange, of which the first is seven, the second is six, the third is six, the fourth is eight printed in two spans of six and two, and the fifth is thirty-five, and the man of about fifty-one in a grey coat sat in a room over a weigh-house until the ninth hour with a lamp going and did not ask the question, and did not notice that he had not asked it until a woman of twenty-nine told him he had not noticed, and he wrote one line in a book at the tenth hour and the line is nine words and it is the only line in that book about the lane.**

## Five

The cost of that Thursday is a column and it is Gault Prentice's, and it is the first one he has kept in about four months, and it is a man's column and not an office's, and four names at the top of a sheet are not in it.

**And on the fourth day of Harvestmonth in this year a man of about fifty-one in a grey coat walked up a lane on foot and sat in one room over a weigh-house for about six hours and was not told the question he had come to ask, and a man of thirty-two with a limp who had read about four hundred and nine returns in two days said about nine sentences in a room and three of them were the name of a road, and the fourteen words under the fourth head of a sheet of her own paper are on a table in a hired room over a saddler's in a town of about two thousand people four days off a coast, and the fourth head on that sheet is empty, and the road in it is not a toll road and has no rate printed on it in nine counties, and nobody is going to find that out from this column.**
