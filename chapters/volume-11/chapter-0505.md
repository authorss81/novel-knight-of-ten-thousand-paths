# Chapter 0505

**The eleventh day of Harvestmonth, YR 319, a Thursday and a market day, and the first hour and the fourth hour and the eighth hour, in a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people, and five returns have come back to that room in eleven days out of nine counties that were asked, and four of the five have a fourth head on them and empty and the fifth has had the fourth head cut out with a knife, and about nine people were in that room at the fourth hour and about four hundred were in that town.**

## One

**Five counties out of nine answered in eleven days. Four of the five sent back the form with the three heads filled in and the fourth head ruled and empty, and the fifth sent back a form with the fourth head cut out clean with a knife and a quarter inch of the paper taken away with it, and the man of about fifty-one in a grey coat laid all five out on the table on the Thursday morning and did not say anything about any of them for about as long as it takes a man to say nine.**

The four that came back whole are not the same paper. One of them is a foolscap sheet off a press in a town of about nine hundred people and the ruled lines on it are about a quarter inch apart, and one of them is a sheet off a press in a town of about four hundred people and the lines are about an eighth of an inch apart, and the four heads on them are cut into the wood and not printed, and the cutting is the same cut on all four, and the man of about fifty-one in a grey coat put the four heads of the four forms next to each other on that table and about nine people in that room could see that the head at the top of the fourth line is the same word in four counties with four different knives.

**And the fifth form is a different thing entirely and it is the only one of the five on which anybody has done anything, and what they did was take the fourth head off it.**

## Two

“**Somebody cut that out,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, at about the fourth hour, and put his finger on the fifth form and did not touch it.

“**Yes,**” Gault Prentice said.

“**Do you know who?**”

“**No,**” Gault Prentice said, “**and I have asked, and I have been told that it is a county matter, and I have been told that twice by two different men in two different counties in about a fortnight, and both of them said it the same way, which is the way a man says a thing when he has been told to say it that way.**”

“**Then the county did it,**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**A county is not a man,**” the woman of about thirty-four said. “**A county is a building and a clerk and about nine hundred and forty returns a year and whatever the clerk was told. If somebody cut a line out of your form with a knife then a man did it and he is somewhere in four counties and he is not a villain and he did it because somebody told him a line that is there and empty is a question and he did not want the question.**”

“**You cannot know that.**”

“**I cannot know it,**” the woman of about thirty-four said, “**and that is exactly the sentence, and you have just said it yourself without noticing that you had said it.**”

**And that is one hundred and eighty-nine words in nine turns of one exchange, of which the first is four, the second is one, the third is four, the fourth is sixty-three printed in two spans of one and sixty-two, the fifth is five, the sixth is six, the seventh is seventy-eight, the eighth is four, and the ninth is twenty-four printed in two spans of four and twenty, and the fifth form came from a county of about four hundred people and the man who cut the line out of it is not in that room and is not a villain and has never been asked and does not know that a form of his is on a table four days up a made road.**

## Three

**And the man of about thirty-eight with a satchel, who is in that town about twice a month on that business, said the thing that the room had been circling for about nine minutes, and he said it to the four forms and not to the man of about fifty-eight.**

“**There are four of those on that table and one of those,**” the man of about thirty-eight with a satchel said, “**and the way to find out whether the fourth head is in every form in nine counties is to ask nine counties for their forms, and the asking is nine counties getting a piece of paper with four heads on it and three of them filled in.**”

“**That is what we did,**” Gault Prentice said.

“**That is what you did to five counties,**” the man of about thirty-eight with a satchel said, “**and four of them have sent a form back with a figure against it, and about eighty-nine people on two of those are on paper and do not know it, and if you ask the other four you will have nine counties, and every road in nine counties can fill in the first three heads if somebody walks it, and about one road in nine of them is a road nobody has ever had a clerk down.**”

“**You are telling me I should not find out,**” Gault Prentice said.

“**I am telling you that finding out is a form going out,**” the man of about thirty-eight with a satchel said, “**and a form is a thing that finds out, and I have said that sentence in this room before and I am saying it again because it is the only sentence in it that I am certain of.**”

**And that is two hundred and eight words in five turns of one exchange, of which the first is fifty-nine printed in two spans of twelve and forty-seven, the second is five, the third is eighty-five printed in two spans of eight and seventy-seven, the fourth is nine, and the fifth is fifty printed in two spans of twelve and thirty-eight, and a man of about thirty-eight with a satchel has said the sentence about a form three times in about nine years and has never once said it in a room that was writing it down, and the clerk of about twenty-two wrote it down this time and did not know that he had done it until the copyist of about twenty-six took the leaf out from under him.**

## Four

**And a fifth return came up that lane on the Friday before this one and it came from a coast four days off and it is the first return that has ever come back from that coast and it is not one of the nine counties and it was not asked by anybody in this room.**

The return has the same four heads. The first head is filled in and what is written under it is a word, and it is a word that about nine people in that room cannot read as a place, and about four of the nine can read it as a place and about four of the nine say it is a name.

The second head is filled in with a figure and the figure is **about nine hundred**, and about four people in that room have said since Friday that a figure of nine hundred people on a road four days off is not a figure of a town and is not a figure of a market and is a figure of a place where somebody counts. The third head is filled in with four trades. The fourth head is empty.

**And there is no date on it, because nobody on that coast writes a month or a day and has not for about nine years, and the clerk of about twenty-two had to enter a date and he entered the day the boat came, and the day the boat came is not the day the thing under the first head happened, and nobody in that room can say by how much, and about nine hundred people on that sand are not in the room and about four of the nine hundred are within nine feet of it and about nine of the nine hundred are not.**

## Five

The cost of that Thursday is a column and it is the man of about thirty-eight with a satchel's, and it is the ninth one he has kept in about nine years and the first one with a cab on it, and four counties and five counties and nine counties are not in it.

**a cab to the made road at nine, a second cab at nine, a loaf at one, a second loaf at one, cheese at four, a second cheese at four, a third cheese at four, a pound of candles at four, a second pound of candles at four, a quill and ink at three, a knife at two, a second knife at two, a rule at four, paste at two, a pot for the paste at five, small beer at two, a broom at five, a bucket of tar at eight, a night's bed at four, a second night's bed at four, a third night's bed at four, a fourth night's bed at four, a bootlace at one, a farthing at one, a second farthing at one, a third farthing at one, a fourth farthing at one, and a fifth farthing at one.** nine, nine, one, one, four, four, four, four, four, three, two, two, four, two, five, two, five, eight, four, four, four, four, one, one, one, one, one, and one is ninety-five pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and ninety-five pence is a mark and forty-seven over**, and four counties is not in it and five counties is not in it and nine counties is not in it and the three are counts of three different things and are not added, and a quarter inch of paper cut out of a form in a county of about four hundred people is not in it, and about nine hundred people on a coast four days off is not in it and is not a figure of persons in a town.

**And on the eleventh day of Harvestmonth in this year five returns lay on a table in a hired room over a saddler's, four of them with a fourth head ruled and empty and one of them with the fourth head cut out with a knife, and about nine people were in that room and about four hundred were in that town, and the two figures are not added, and a return came up the lane from a coast four days off with a word under its first head and about nine hundred under its second and nothing at all under its fourth, and a word that is a place and a word that is a name are not the same word and nobody in that room can say which one it is, and the tide that makes that place a road or a place with water on it is not in this column and decides twice a day and is not paid.**
