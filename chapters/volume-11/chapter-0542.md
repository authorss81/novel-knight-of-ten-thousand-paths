# Chapter 0542

**In one room over a weigh-house with a door on a lane about two hundred miles up a made road from a coast, a county of about nine thousand people, on the eighth of Thawmonth, YR 320, a Monday and not a market day, from the second hour to the ninth, the two of them were in that room for about seven hours together for the first time since a Tuesday in Rainmonth in the year before this one, and a woman of about thirty-one with a pen was in it for all of it, and neither of the two said one word about the four and a half years, and this is the one time either of them has said it and it is not going to be said again.**

The room is one room. There is a press in it that stands open and has stood open since a day nobody will give. There is a register in it with seven entries and every one of them is a question and none of them is a finding. There is a day-book at nine entries of a thing nobody asked. There is a drawer about eleven inches deep that is not open. And the outgoing sheet of that room has carried a fourth ruled line with no head over it for four and a half years, and nobody in nine counties has ever told her what it is for, and she has asked twice and been told twice that it is not hers to cut.

**What they built in that room on the eighth of a month carried at thirty is the first thing anybody has put in nine counties that nobody keeps.**

It is not a register. It is not a roll. It is not a book with a head over it and a name under the head. It is about nine leaves of foolscap with the same four heads ruled on each of them in the same hand, and every one of them has three of the four heads filled and the fourth ruled and empty, and every one of them is in a different county, and nobody knows where six of the nine are, and the man of thirty-two with a limp and the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps that room each know where two.

“**That is nine sheets,**” the woman of about thirty-one with a pen said, looking down at them on the table, “**and a sheet is not a thing and nine of them are not a thing, and I want it said in this room that I have not made an institution this afternoon and I am not going to.**”

“**Nobody has,**” the woman of about twenty-nine said.

“**I have made nine copies of a form,**” the woman of about thirty-one with a pen said, “**and that is a Tuesday, and it is what happens in about four hundred kitchens at the end of a made lane a mile and a half from a market town four days off, and I have never once been told that I am not allowed to and I am not going to be told now, and I have not signed them and I am not going to sign them, and a copy with nobody's name on it is a copy.**”

Nobody in that room thanked her, and she was not thanked later and has not been thanked since, and she is a woman of about thirty-one with a pen and she is on no sheet in nine counties and she is not a keeper of anything.

Then the man of thirty-two with a limp did the part that is his and it took him about as long as a man takes to say four, and he did not explain it and nobody asked him to.

He took nine leaves and wrote nine questions on them, one to each, and every one of the nine questions was the same question in the same words, and Aren Kest wrote them in the same hand he had written the four pages of a record in, and the words were these.

*What is this road called, and who lives at the end of it, and what is carried on it, and what is the name you use for it?*

**And the four heads are on the leaf above the question and the question is under the fourth head, and the fourth head is ruled and empty, and the question is not an answer to it and does not go in it, and every one of the nine leaves is a form with four heads on it and a question written under the fourth one by a person and not by an office.**

“**That is the first instrument anybody has found in five blocks,**” the man of thirty-two with a limp said, “**and it is not an instrument, and I want that said in this room, and I want it said by somebody who is not me.**”

“**It is a leaf with a question on it,**” the woman of about thirty-one with a pen said, “**and a leaf is a leaf, and the four heads on it are the four heads and I ruled them this afternoon in about nine minutes with a straightedge I borrowed from the man who keeps the key to the door of this room, and I did not put a fifth line on any of them and I want it noticed that I could have and that nobody asked me not to.**”

Nobody noticed it except the man of thirty-two with a limp and he did not say so.

**And then the two of them did the second thing together, and the second thing is the whole of what the two of them put on a table, and it took about as long as a man takes to count nine, and it was not said out loud until the woman of about twenty-nine said it out loud.**

The office in this county can refuse to show a road office in another county a return. The office in this county cannot show a room four days up a made road a coast a thing nobody has asked it for. **What the office can do is hold, and a thing that is in one office is one thing, and a thing that is in nine counties in nine hands is nine things and one of them is a question.**

“**This is what I have got and I am not asking for it,**” the woman of about twenty-nine said. “**Nine leaves and a question under the fourth head in each, and a man takes a question down a made road to a coast four days off, and a man on a coast puts a hand on a piece of paper and writes the answer in his own words and his own hand, and the answer goes back up nineteen days or about nine more, and about four weeks is the whole of the round trip and a cart is a fortnight and the cart goes on a fixed day, and nobody has to be sent because a man who goes down a made road with a sheet in his coat is a man going down a made road, and a man who is sent is a man somebody sent.**”

**And that is the challenge to the office and it is not a challenge to any person, and it is not a refusal of anything, and the woman of about twenty-nine did not restate what she wants, because what she wants has not been restated in four and a half years and was not restated on the eighth of a month carried at thirty and has not been restated since.**

The man of thirty-two with a limp said the thing about it that is true and it is about nine counties and not about a woman.

“**A question under a fourth head on a leaf is the only one of about nine documents in this county that goes out and comes back with something in it that nobody in an office chose, and the reason it is the only one is that every other one of them asks a person to fill a head, and a filled head is a thing an office can read and a question is a thing an office can only forward, and forwarding it is what an office is for.**”

“**Then it is a form,**” the woman of about thirty-one with a pen said, from the far end of the room, where she had not spoken for about two hours.

“**It is a form,**” the man of thirty-two with a limp said, “**and I am not going to call it anything else, and I have been in nine counties for about four years and that is the first form anybody has sent out that could come back with a person in it who was not asked, and the fourth head on it is empty and it is going to stay empty, and a question written under a head by a person in a room two hundred miles off is not in it, and no office has filled it and no office is going to.**”

Nobody in that room agreed with him and nobody argued with him, and the woman of about thirty-one with a pen wrote all nine questions out again in her own hand on a second leaf and did not read them back, and she said that she had done that four times in four years and about nine days ago and that this is the first one where the second copy is the same as the first.

“**They are the same because there is one of me,**” she said, “**and not because anybody told me to.**”

**And nobody thanked her for that and nobody thanked the woman of about twenty-nine and nobody thanked the man of thirty-two with a limp, and the man of thirty-two with a limp was in that room for about seven hours and said about nine sentences in all of them and about four of the nine were about counties and not about anything that had happened to him.**

Then he did a thing at about the seventh hour that is not a speech and that nobody asked for, and it is the only thing in two years that two people did at the same time without agreeing on it first.

He took the ninth leaf, which was the only one of the nine that had not gone anywhere, and he cut it in half with a knife, and gave one half to the woman of about twenty-nine and kept the other, and said one sentence.

“**That is so that neither of us has all of them, and I am not going to say why and you are not going to ask me, and the two halves are two and not one, and a person in nine counties who wants to know what this is cannot be shown the whole of it in one room, and that is the whole of what I have.**”

“**That is not a reason,**” the woman of about twenty-nine said.

“**It is a precaution,**” the man of thirty-two with a limp said, “**and you have kept a room with a door on a lane for four and a half years and you have never once had to decide whether to lock the door, and I have been in nine counties for about four years and I have never once been able to decide whether to stay, and we are not going to talk about that today and I would like the leaf cut.**”

**And nothing has said any more about that since and nothing ever will, and the two of them did not say one word about it then and have not said one word about it in the forty days since, and a leaf about nine inches by six with three lines and no head has been in an inside pocket and has not come out.**

Nobody was sent. Nobody was sent from that room or from this county or from the room four days up a made road, and in the same ink as the rest of it, at the foot of a return, there is a line that says nobody is to be sent to find out, and the man of thirty-two with a limp went out of that room at about the ninth hour and down the lane and got onto a cart that goes to a market town and he has not been back to that lane in about four days and nobody has sent for him.

Four days off there is a sand of two miles and about nine hundred paces with eleven houses along it and about nine hundred people on it, and about eleven hundred more are on no roll and no sheet and no list. At the seaward end of that sand a woman of about fifty-one stands at a post with a piece of driftwood in her hand. **Twenty-two notches are cut in the edge of it and the twenty-second is not cut, the water has not been off that mark in twenty-two years, and what they count is years. The half leaf went down a made road to the landward end of that coast on the Monday in the hand of a man of about thirty-four with a sheet, and in about nine years nobody on that sand has written a month or a day out loud and nobody asked them to.**

**And the cost of that Monday is a column and it is the woman of about twenty-nine's, and it is the ninth one she has kept in four and a half years, and nine leaves and a question under the fourth head of each is not in it.**

**a farthing at one, a bootlace at two, a third pot of tea at three, a fourth broom at four, a wedge of bread at five, a pound of candles at six, a sheet of foolscap at eight, a second night's bed at nine, a pot of paste at eleven, a rule of brass at fifteen, a hank of yarn at twenty, and a knife at thirty-seven.** One, two, three, four, five, six, eight, nine, eleven, fifteen, twenty and thirty-seven is a hundred and twenty-one pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and twenty-one pence is two marks and twenty-five over**, and nine leaves are not in it and a question is not in it and a form is not in it and a form is not a thing anybody buys, and the three series of that room — about nine hundred sheets a month, about a hundred and four in one band, about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month — are three separate counts of three separate series and none of the three is derived from another and no two of the three are added here, and the about nine hundred on that sand are not in it, and neither is the about four hundred at the end of a made lane four days the other way, and those two are counted apart and go in at neither end of this column, and the twenty-two notches and the twenty-two years are not in it and are not the same figure and are not added, and the four and a half years is not in it, and a deed of thirteen words and fourpence a year are not in it and fourpence a year is not in this column.

The press in that room stood open all day and nobody shut it and nobody dated it and nobody in that room said when it was opened, and the nine leaves went out at the ninth hour in nine separate hands and about four of the nine people who carry them do not know what is on them and about five do, and a woman of about thirty-one with a pen washed her knife and put it away and was not thanked.
