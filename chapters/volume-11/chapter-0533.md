# Chapter 0533

**At about the fourth hour on the seventeenth of Hearthmonth, YR 319, a Saturday and a market day, a word came up out of the second page of a man's record of himself in a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people, and the word was a word a press had been printing at the head of every form in nine counties for longer than anybody in that room had been copying.**

The second page is about nine lines long and it took him a day and a half to write and he said so on the top of it, and then he put it on the table face up and did not read it, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet picked it up and read it out, and Aren Kest corrected him once, in one word, and did not correct him again.

The page says that the account came up the made road on a cart with the forms, and that the cart was going to nine counties, and that the account was on top of the load in an oiled-paper cover, and that he signed for the cart because a man signs for the cart. It says that he read the account while the cart was being unloaded. It says that the account gave a day and an hour and a figure and a cause, and that the cause was the word that has been at the head of every form in this county since before the woman of about forty-four took up the work, and the word is *none*.

“**I had a question about it at the time,**” the man of about thirty-four with a sheet read, in the voice he uses for a form and not the voice he uses for anything else, and he got to the end of the ninth line and looked up, and Aren Kest said one word at him and the word was *load*, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet wrote the word in the margin and went on.

Nobody in that room said anything.

The woman of about forty-four who has been copying for about nineteen years got up and took the form off the top of the pile, and held it up by the two corners, and did not say anything at all, and turned it round so that the room could see the top of it.

Above the four heads, in letters smaller than the four heads, there is a printed line. It is not ruled. It is set in the same sort as the line at the foot that says that nobody is to be sent to find out, and it sits in the die that cuts the four heads, in one piece with them, and it is the line that everybody in nine counties reads and nobody in nine counties has ever asked about.

*Fever in this parish: none since the last return.*

“**That is in the die,**” the woman of about forty-four said. “**Not on the sheet. In the die, cut with the four heads, in one piece, and the four heads have not changed in nineteen years and neither has that.**”

“**A printed line is not a ruled line,**” the man of about fifty-eight said. He had the form in his hand and was looking at the top of it from about the distance at which a man reads a thing he has stopped being surprised by. “**A fifth line is not a fourth head and a printed line is not a fifth line, and I want the room to hear me say that, because in about four months somebody in this county is going to find a mark on a form and call it a fifth thing, and there is a fifth line in this county that was ruled in Fallowmonth and taken off a sheet in about four minutes, and there is a fifth line going out in the Monday bundle that nobody ordered and nobody cut. This is none of those. It is type.**”

Nobody argued with him.

Then the man of about thirty-four with a sheet asked the question that the page had been waiting four days for, and he asked it in the plainest words he had.

“**Is the word in your account the same word?**”

“**It is the same word,**” Aren Kest said. “**Same spelling. Same type. I have the account and I have the form and I have put them side by side on a table in this room and I am not going to say anything else about it, and what I am going to say is the rest of the second page and then you can all decide what you think of me.**”

And the rest of the second page was that he had held on to the account and had put it in a box, and that the box was not in this room and had not been opened, and that about nine people on the other side of the crossing had been told nothing at all about any of it, and that he had not been on the far side since, and that the record did not say where the fever came from and would not, and that he was the man who was standing on the crossing and could not tell and would not guess.

The man of about fifty-three with a barrow said the thing the page had not said.

“**Then the mistake you are putting in there is not the crossing.**”

“**No,**” Aren Kest said.

“**The crossing is a thing a man of twenty-one did with a piece of paper that was not complete, and a man of thirty-two can look at that, because he has had the whole of the time since to look at it with. The word is a different thing. You took a word off a cart because the cart was going out, and you have had the whole of the time since not asking what it means, and there is no way to put that on a page that anybody can check.**”

“**There is,**” Aren Kest said, “**and it is the second page, and the second page says *I had a question about it at the time and I did not put it to anybody*, and that is checkable, because a man who did not have a question cannot write down having had one, and a man who is lying about it will write it a different way.**”

Nobody in that room said whether they thought he had one, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet wrote the second page out in full on a third bare leaf, and when he had finished he read the one line back and nobody stopped him, and the line is the line about the question, and he read it exactly as it is written and not as it might have been written.

Then the copyist of about twenty-six did a thing at about the sixth hour that nobody had asked her to do and nobody thanked her for, and she took the form off the table by the door, and held it up again, and said one sentence, and went back to the straightedge.

“**Nine hundred sheets a month go out of a room two hundred miles off into nine counties, a hundred and four of them in a single band on a fixed day, and four hundred and nine parish rolls come back into that same room in a month. Those three are three series in three separate books and none of them is derived from another. The word is at the top of every one of them, and the word is not one of the counts and is not counted by anybody.**”

And the man of about thirty-four with a sheet said the finding out loud, and it was his and not the man of thirty-two's, and the room did not vote on it and the copyist wrote it on a fourth bare leaf and ruled nothing over it.

“**A man who has recorded the mistake he made with a piece of paper has to go and record the mistake he made with a word,**” he said, “**and he cannot do the second one by writing more, because the second one was not a not-writing. The second one was a word he took off a cart. And the word is still going out.**”

Nobody in that room thanked him and nobody in that room asked him where the word was printed, because the woman of about forty-four had already told them, and had told them it was in the die, and that a die is cut once.

**And the cost of that Saturday is a column and it is the man of about fifty-three with a barrow's, and it is the first one he has ever kept, and a printed word in a die is not in it.**

**a barrow-mend at thirty-one, a cake of soap at six, a hank of twine at sixteen, a bowl of broth at five, a sheet of oiled paper at fourteen, a nail at two, a stone of tallow at nineteen, a wick at three, a farthing at one, and a jar of pitch at six.** Thirty-one, six, sixteen, five, fourteen, two, nineteen, three, one and six is a hundred and three pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and a hundred and three pence is two marks and seven over**, and the word is not in it, and the die is not in it, and the cart is not in it, and nineteen years of a press in four counties is not in it, and nine hundred sheets a month and a hundred and four in a band and four hundred and nine parish rolls a month are three series in three separate books in a room two hundred miles off and none of the three is in this column and no two of the three are added here, and about nine hundred on a coast four days off is not in it, and about four hundred at the end of a made lane is not in it, and about two thousand in this town is not in it, and the three of those are three counts of three different things and go in at neither end of this column.

The man of about fifty-three with a barrow took the form back to the press-corner himself and put it on the pile face up, which nobody had done in four months, because the pile has been kept face down so that the head of a sheet is never the first thing a clerk sees when he takes the top off a bundle, and the man of about fifty-eight looked at him do it and said nothing, and let it stand, and did not rule a head over it.
