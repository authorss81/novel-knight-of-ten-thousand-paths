# Chapter 0580

**The board off the middle terrace, the ninth hour of the set sitting, a leaf with four lines under a fourth head, about nine people in the open air, a woman of about thirty-eight who has asked to be the answer for a road and has not been told she may, and a plank of green wood lying on the grass in the light a hundred yards up the slope.**

The question was asked at the ninth hour in the open and it was not answered at the ninth hour, and nothing was answered afterwards either, and nobody has been scheduled to answer it and nobody has said they will.

She said it standing away from the board, about nine paces from it, which is a thing a person does when they have decided that the board is not theirs.

“**Will anybody in this valley say out loud that the water has been let out at the foot of a wall and put back in at the head of it?**”

About nine people were within earshot. That is a figure of people on a slope at an hour and not a figure of this valley, which is eleven households on about eleven terraces, and the two are not added.

Nobody answered.

A man of about forty-four said that he had a tree on the second terrace that would want looking at, and that was a true thing and it was not an answer. A woman of about thirty-one said that her block was not on the four lowest and that she had better be at the gate. The man of about sixty of the Standing said that he had been asked a fair question and that he was not going to answer it, and he said why, and nobody thanked him for the why.

“**Because in about nineteen years the only questions that have been put to that board have been ones somebody in this valley already knows the answer to, and if I answer this one then the next person will put me a question and the one after that will put me a question, and in about four years there will be a person in this valley whose job is to know things and to say them out of a face he was given at the table when he was small. I have watched the shape of that. I do not want to be the beginning of it and I will not be the end of it either, and I am not going to hand the ending to anybody in this forty yards either.**”

Nobody argued with him. Four people in that valley have put it about that the man of about sixty refused a fair question for a fair reason and nine have put it about that he refused it because he did not know, and the two sets do not overlap and nobody asked him and he has not said.

The woman of about thirty-eight stood there for about as long as a man takes to say nine and then said the last two things of that hour, and they are on nobody's sheet and about four people heard both.

“**Nobody in this valley has told me I was wrong since I said it out loud, and I said it out loud twice, and I am not going to say it a third time until somebody has actually done it.**”

“**Then do not,**” the man of about fifty-one said, and he was nine paces off and he had said it before she had finished.

Nobody thanked him for stopping her. Nobody thanked her for saying it.

Then the woman of about forty-four, who keeps that board, did the thing she does at the ninth hour, which is to look at what is on it and say what is not on it, out loud, to whoever is standing there.

“**There is a leaf on this board and it has four divisions on it and three of them are filled and the fourth is ruled and empty, and there are four lines written under the fourth in her own hand, and there is one word in the margin in a different ink, and I did not rule the fourth and I am not going to rule it now and I am not going to take the leaf down. A division on a board in a valley with no office in it is a division. It is not a head. It is not a fifth line and it is not a fourth head and it is not going to be a finding, and I am going to say that now so that nobody in this valley can put a fifth thing on my board and say I let them.**”

Nobody thanked her.

**And nobody filled a head. Nobody ruled one. Nobody cut a line above the fourth and nobody wrote anything on it, and the fourth head of the Crown's form is about four counties off and has been ruled and empty for about six months, and there is no office in nine counties whose business it is to fill it, and there is no person in this valley who can, and a question under a head is not an answer to it and does not go in it.**

**And the record is open. The answer is not made. A woman of about thirty-eight asked a room at the second hour of a sitting in the swell to be the answer for a road and she has not been told she may, and she has asked in the open twice since and the second asking was not about her, and nobody has told her either.**

Then somebody asked what is to be done about the carter of about forty, who went out of this valley in the swell sitting with two sacks of pears on a cart and has not come back in, and the fruit of that day is on no road in nine counties. Four people on that ground had that question between them within about as long as a man takes to say nine and about nine terraces is the answer, and it was not a discussion and nobody put it to a vote.

“**The lane will do what it does,**” the woman of about thirty-eight said, “**and so would the bar, and I have asked the fourth of the four things to a carter of about nineteen who came in on his own answers and I did not report him to anybody, and that is the whole of the administration of this crossing and there is not a word of it on a sheet.**”

Nobody thanked her. Nobody has asked her since whether she would report the other one.

And the boy of about fifteen rang it at the fourth point, for the penny, and the ninth hour went the way the ninth hour goes, and nobody asked him why he had rung it and he has not been asked.

**And the cost of that hour is a column and it is the woman of about forty-four's, and a question nobody answered is not in it and a plank on the grass is not in it and neither of them has a price.**

**a cake of ink at two, a quill at five, a stick of sealing wax at nine, a sheet of foolscap at twelve, a pot of paste at sixteen, a night's straw at nineteen, a tallow candle at twenty-three, a leather grip at twenty-six, a scraper at twenty-nine, and a case of quills at thirty-one.** Two, five, nine, twelve, sixteen, nineteen, twenty-three, twenty-six, twenty-nine and thirty-one is one hundred and seventy-two pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and seventy-two pence is three marks and twenty-eight over**, and nine paces and a hundred yards and nine people and eleven households on eleven terraces and four sets of people and nine households on four terraces are six figures of six different things and none of them is a price, and six months of an empty head and twenty-two winters and the ninth hour and the fourth hour and the second hour are five figures of time and none of them is a price, and every one of them is at neither end of this column. **Four heads and four divisions and a fourth head and a fifth line ruled on a Monday morning in a market town by nobody who ordered it are four shapes and not one object, and none of them is the Crown's form, and the fifth line is a fifth line and is not a fourth head and is not a sixth.**

Nobody thanked anybody.

**And nobody has paid anything yet.** The plank is still on the grass in the light a hundred yards up the slope and the ground under it is filled in and the flat stone is on top of the loam and not on top of the plank, and the water at the top of the Long Set is where it was on the morning the record came out, and four miles of stone about two paces wide with a lip on the high side are still going down that slope with about nine yards of lip out over a bed that has gone, and about nine households on the four lowest terraces have not been asked anything and have not answered anything and are the people who will pay for it and are not the people who were wrong, and a bar of wood is lying in the grass at the foot of the Low Lane about nine terraces further down, and it was never lifted, and nobody has asked whether it should be.
