# Chapter 0554

**In a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people, on the seventh of Greenmonth, YR 320, a Thursday and a market day, between the second hour and the sixth, a leaf about nine inches by six came out of a satchel that a man of about fifty-three had not put down for two days, and there was a mark at the top of it and there was nothing else on it, and the mark is where a branch of one tree was joined to the wood of another, and about nine people looked at it for about as long as it takes a man to say four, and nothing was entered in any book.**

The leaf is not paper from a press. It is the size a leaf is. It has been folded twice along the middle and pressed flat and it has been carried in a satchel for about nine days, and it smells of a cold room and of earth, and the fold has gone soft at both ends.

The mark at the top of it is about two inches across and it is not a drawing and it is not a seal and it is not a stamp, and two people in that room took it at first for a drawing of a tree and a man of about thirty-four with a satchel took it for a mark a person makes with a hot thing, and both of those are wrong, and that is said here before anybody in the room can say it, and the true one is on the next line.

**It is a scar in a tree. It is where a branch of one kind of tree was cut and a branch of another kind was fitted into the cut and bound and left, and the wood has grown round it for about nine years, and the join is sound, and whoever made that mark made it with a knife and some bark and about nine years of waiting and did it to a living tree.**

Then the woman of about thirty-one with a pen said the thing her trade says about it, and it took her about as long as it takes a woman to say four, and nobody in that room interrupted her and nobody thanked her.

“**I have been copying for four and a half years in a room with a door on a lane, and I have never once in four and a half years had a sheet handed to me that nobody had told me was a copy of something. Every sheet I have ever copied was a copy of another sheet or of an entry in a book or of a thing a man said and wrote down. This is the first piece of paper anybody has given me that I cannot swear to, and I want that minuted in a room and not in a street, and I am not going to tell the rest of this room what I think it is because what I think it is belongs to the trade and not to nine people on a Saturday.**”

Nobody thanked her and nobody agreed with her out loud and nobody argued with her out loud, and several people in that room privately thought she was being precious about a piece of paper and have not changed their minds since.

**And all five of the documents came together on that table in one breath, because anybody in nine counties who has been following any of this has been asked four times in five months to hold four of them at once, and it is done here in the open.**

The Crown's form is on the side table with four heads ruled on it and the fourth of them empty, and it has been empty for about five months, and no office in nine counties has filled it and no person has filled it. The sheet of foolscap with four ruled lines and a man's name in the fourth of them went into this market in Mudmonth and is not a form and has never been a form. The returned form from a county two hundred miles off has four heads on it and a word a woman put there herself in the fourth of them. The piece of stone a foot and a half long with four heads cut into it is in a room in another county. **And a leaf about nine inches by six with a mark at the top of it and nothing under it came out of a satchel on a Thursday. That is five documents in five places and not one of the five is the Crown's form, and the fourth head of the Crown's form is still ruled and still empty, and the fifth line ruled on a Monday morning in a room in this town by nobody who ordered it is a fifth line and is not a fourth head and is not a sixth.**

Nobody in that room argued about any of that and nobody had to, because the man of about fifty-eight read it out of the copyist's mouth and not his own, and he said afterwards that he had not written it and had not thought of it and that it was the best thing anybody had said in that room in about four months, and that he was not going to thank her for it either.

Then the man of about fifty-three asked that the leaf be entered in a book, and he asked it properly, and he gave his reason, which was that a thing in a room that is not in a book is a thing that the next man in this room cannot use.

**And the man of about fifty-eight refused, in about nine sentences, and he was right, and nobody in that room agreed with him out loud and nobody argued with him out loud.**

“**A return is a question a county asked. Every sheet in a bundle in a cart behind this building is a question somebody in this county put to a person, and the answer comes back with the question's name on it and it goes into a column and the column is the reason the county can find out anything at all. This is not that. This is a mark that a person made in a tree, and no county in nine counties asked for it, and if I enter it in a book then in about four years there will be a line in a book in this county that says a place exists, and there will be a clerk who will have to decide on his own what kind of place, and if he decides that on his own then it will not have been me who sent him.**”

Nobody thanked him. Gallow said that was fair and then said the other thing, which is that a man who will not put a thing in a book is a man who knows exactly what happens to places that are only in books, and that he had been in this room four days and had not been given a book to write in either.

Then the woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key asked the one question in that room that nobody had asked, and she asked it to the leaf and not to the man, and it took about as long as a woman takes to say four.

“**Who made the mark?**”

“**Somebody who has made it every year for as long as anybody can remember,**” Gallow said. “**That is what I was told. It is done once a year at the time the fruit sets and it is done by whoever is holding the knife and nobody argues about who holds the knife.**”

**And the woman of about thirty-four said out loud that a thing a person makes every year is not a mark at all, and then said it the other way round, and the other way round is how it comes out of her, which is that it is not a mark and it is a name, and a name on a leaf with nothing under it is a person saying yes to whatever a reader brings to it. About four of the nine people in that room understood her the first time and about five did not, and the four of them did one thing afterwards because of it and the five of them did another, and neither of those two things was said out loud. She is not a keeper of records and she is on no sheet in nine counties and she keeps a key.**

“**That is a signature,**” she said, “**and a signature on a leaf with nothing under it is a person saying yes to whatever a reader brings to it, and I am not going to be near it and I am not going to hold it.**”

Nobody thanked her. Nobody argued with her. The copyist of about twenty-six took a bare leaf, ruled no head over it, ruled nothing under it, wrote one sentence, and read none of it back.

**And the cost of that Thursday is a column and it is the woman of about thirty-one with a pen's, and it is the second one she has ever kept, and a leaf with nothing on it is not in it and is not the same as a leaf with a strike through a line on it and is not a fourth head and is not a fifth line.**

**a pot of paste at one, a quill at two, a second quill at three, a candle-end at five, a second candle-end at seven, a bundle of kindling at nine, a sheet of foolscap at twelve, a third candle-end at fourteen, a night's straw at twenty-six, and a pound of candles at thirty-two.** One, two, three, five, seven, nine, twelve, fourteen, twenty-six and thirty-two is one hundred and eleven pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and eleven pence is two marks and fifteen over**, and four heads and an empty fourth head are not in it, and a mark in a tree is not a price and is not in it, and about nine years is a figure of time and is not a figure of persons, and the five documents in five places are five documents and not one figure and go in at neither end of this column, and about nine hundred sheets a month out of a room two hundred miles off and about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month into it are two counts of two series and neither of them is in this column and neither is derived from the other.

At about the sixth hour the leaf went back into the satchel and the satchel went up to the room over the inn, and the fourth head of the Crown's form was still on the side table with nothing in it, and the copyist's bare leaf went on the same side table with a sentence on it and no head over it, and nobody put the two near each other and nobody turned either of them over.
