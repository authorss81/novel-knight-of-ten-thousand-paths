# Chapter 0572

**The Low Gate, the sixth hour of the bar sitting, a carter of about thirty with an empty cart, a woman of about fifty-five with a board, nine paces off a woman of about thirty-eight with a barrow, and a word that nobody in that valley had ever heard said out loud on a slope until that evening.**

The carter came up the Low Lane with an empty cart and one wheel that had begun to say something in the first mile, and the woman of about fifty-five was on the stool inside the bar with the board face up on her knees.

She asked the four things in order, the way she has asked them since before the carter's father was in his trade.

“**What is this lane called?**”

“**The Low Lane.**”

“**Who lives at the end of it?**”

“**Nobody I have any business with.**”

“**What is carried on it?**”

“**Nothing today. I am going up for a load of hurdle and I am going down again empty and if you will let me past I will not stop to talk and I have come out of a place they call the quiet.**”

He said the last part to the woman of about fifty-five and he said it the way a man says a thing he has said about nine times in about nine counties, and then he got on with the cart.

Nobody thanked him. The woman of about fifty-five did not write anything on the board, and the fourth of the four went unasked, and the bar of wood was where it has been lying for about thirty years and a cart with a bad wheel went under it and up the lane.

“**What did that man say?**” the woman of about fifty-five said, to the woman of about thirty-eight, who was nine paces off with a barrow.

“**He said a word.**”

“**I have kept this bar for about thirty years and I have asked four things of everybody who comes up this lane,**” the woman of about fifty-five said, “**and I have never once written down a thing a person said that was not an answer to one of the four. I have not got a head for it and I am not going to rule one.**”

The woman of about thirty-eight put the barrow down.

“**He said they call it the quiet. That is the word. That is all of it, one word, and he has been about nine counties carrying it about and he has not the least idea what he was carrying.**”

Then she asked the carter two things before he was out of earshot, and he answered both of them at a cart's width, standing in the lane, with his hand on the rail.

She asked where he had got it, and he said a man with a sheet had said it in a market town about four days of carting off, reading it out of a paper.

She asked what the paper was, and he said it was a report, and that it had about four hands of writing on it, and that it was the kind of writing that wants reading out loud, and that he could not tell her whose report it was because he was not asked and did not stay to find out.

“**Can anybody in that town produce it?**”

“**No,**” he said. “**I asked a man standing next to me whether that was the paper's name and he said it was a word for a place nobody can find, and then he asked me what I wanted it for.**”

The carter went up the lane. Nobody stopped him. Nobody has ever stopped him, and he did not know that he had said anything, and about nine people on that lane heard him say it and about nine did not and the two figures are not added to each other and neither of them is a figure of the people who live on this slope.

**And the word went up the slope at about the ninth hour in the mouth of a woman of about thirty-eight, and she said it once, out loud, standing at the head of the Long Set with eleven people within nine paces of her, and she said it to nobody in particular, and it was the first time in about twenty-two years that anybody in that valley had heard it.**

She did not say it twice and she has not said it again, and four people on that slope have told other people on that slope that they understood it, and four have told other people on that slope that they did not understand it and could not be got to, and the two sets do not overlap and nobody has asked either of them a question about it.

Nobody thanked her.

The man of about thirty-one was the only one of them who asked her where she had got it, and she told him, and he said the thing that made the word a matter of standing rather than of weather.

“**There was a paper that went out of this valley in the year the strip was let go,**” the man of about thirty-one said, “**and it had about four hands of writing on it and it went out with a party and it did not come back, and my father's father was on the Standing in that year and he never spoke of it to my father and my father never spoke of it to me. That paper is the one that man read out of, or it is a copy of it, or it is a thing about it. I have been in that room nineteen years and I have never been shown one line of it and I have asked twice.**”

“**And can it be produced?**” the woman of about thirty-eight said.

“**No,**” the man of about thirty-one said, “**and I have stopped asking, which is a different thing and I would like it noticed that it is a different thing.**”

The man of about sixty was asked whether he had ever seen the word in writing and said no, and then said the half of it that is the whole of it, and nobody argued with him about either half.

“**I have been in that room about nineteen years and I have never used that word about this place in my own mouth and I am not going to start now, because a word that came to you from outside is not a name for anything, and if it were a name then it would be a name that four counties gave us, and we did not ask them for it. Whatever that word means, it is not what this is.**”

“**Then what is it?**” the man of about thirty-one said.

“**It is a word somebody else uses so that they do not have to use the hard one,**” the man of about sixty said, and would not say what the hard one was, and nobody has asked him twice.

Then a woman of about seventy came down from the Wet Set with a lamp, because the light had gone, and the word had gone up the slope before her and she had heard it from about nine terraces down, and she stopped and said one thing about it and went on to the press-house.

“**I have a name for this place,**” she said. “**It is not that, and it is not a word anybody from outside can use, and I am seventy years old and I am not going to give it to a stranger with a limp. If it is in a paper four counties off then it is in their paper and not in my mouth, and a thing in their paper is a thing they can write anything into, and I have said that once already about a leaf and nobody asked me then either.**”

Nobody asked her then either.

Nobody thanked her.

**And the cost of that sitting is a column and it is the woman of about fifty-five's, and a word is not in it and a bar of wood lying in grass for about thirty years is not in it and neither of them has a price.**

**a twist of tarred twine at two, a thimble at six, a pot of paste at nine, a whetstone at thirteen, a leather grip at seventeen, a piece of sailcloth at twenty-one, a jar of oil at twenty-four, a second jar of oil at twenty-eight, and a coil of withes at thirty-eight.** Two, six, nine, thirteen, seventeen, twenty-one, twenty-four, twenty-eight and thirty-eight is one hundred and fifty-eight pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and fifty-eight pence is three marks and fourteen over**, and the sixth hour and the ninth hour and thirty years and nine days of carting and nine days back and four hands of writing are six figures of time and of distance and of size and not one of them is a price, and nine people on the lane and nine who did not hear it and four of the eleven on the slope and four who could not be got to are four figures of four different sets of people on one day and none of them is a figure of this valley, which is eleven households on eleven terraces, and none of the four goes in at either end of this column. **A board with four things written at the top of it in a hand used for about thirty years is a board and not a form, and a head is not a division and a division is not a head, and the fourth of the four things on that board has been scrubbed out, put in again and scrubbed out a second time, and it cannot be read by anybody on that slope now, and she has not asked for it to be traced.**

The lamp went down to the press-house and the bell was rung at the fourth point of the sitting by a boy of about fifteen who is paid a penny for the four of them, and the word was not said again that day, and about four hundred people in a market town of about two thousand can say where a man of thirty-two with a limp sleeps and about nine people on that slope know that he is somewhere above the bar, and the two figures are not one figure and are not added.
