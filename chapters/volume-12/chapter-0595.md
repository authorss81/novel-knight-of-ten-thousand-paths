# Chapter 0595

**Embermonth the first, YR 320, a Monday and not a market day, the second hour in a lane outside a weigh-house, a key, a room, a press standing open, and a man of about fifty-eight with a badge who says the hearing stands.**

The lane behind the weigh-house was empty at the second hour, and the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a room over the weigh-house stood in it with the key in her hand, and the copyist of about twenty-six stood beside her with a satchel, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet stood further down by the board, and about nine people had come for the hearing and none of them had come for anything else.

The man of about fifty-eight with a chain of office that is a badge came down the lane on foot and stopped in the street and not in the room, and he said out loud what he says on the first of every month, and the only first of a month inside these weeks is this day, which is a Monday and not a market day, and he said it where everybody could hear it and nobody could mistake where it was said.

“**The hearing stands,**” he said. “**There is no bench in the room. Nobody is sentenced to anything. The room opens on market mornings at the second hour and shuts at the seventh, and the key stays in the lane because the door has to be locked from the lane while there are people on the stair.**”

None of the nine had come for it and none of them left because of it, and they stood the way people stand when they have come for something else and found this instead, patient and unsatisfied, and the lane held them without crowding, and the morning stayed cool.

Upstairs the room held what it holds. A register at seven entries, all questions and no findings. A day-book at nine entries. A press standing open with no date anybody will give. A drawer about eleven inches deep holding about four hundred returns. The three series went out and in as they always had, and none was derived from another, and this ground adds none: about nine hundred sheets a month out into nine counties, about a hundred and four in one band on a fixed day, about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month in.

The woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key locked the door from the lane while the copyist went up, and stood in the lane because the key is in the lane and she is in the lane, and she said what the room had cost her, which was a place in it for four weeks and now a place out of it on sitting days, and she said it was a place and not a punishment, because a punishment would mean somebody did it to her.

“**There is a difference between a Thursday and a market Saturday,**” she said, “**and I have worked it out and nobody has thanked me for working it out, and the door stays open on market mornings for one more week, and it is his and not mine and he will not put it on me.**”

The copyist wrote on a bare leaf with no head over it and said again that she is not going to write the fourth head twice, because once is a copy and twice is a habit, and the pen went back in its case and the case was shut, and nobody put a name at the top of anything that morning.

Two hundred miles off a valley mended stone, and the two of them did not repair the breach in a scene, an aside, a document or an apology, and they were not in one room with a third person in it, and neither of them said one word about the four and a half years, and the room over the weigh-house and the slope above the Low Lane stayed two places with a made road between them and no crossing made that day.

At the seventh hour the room shut, and the key came back up the lane, and the register stayed at seven and the day-book at nine, and the press stood open, and the drawer held its four hundred, and the hearing stood with no bench in it, and that was the whole of the first of the month, and the lane emptied the way lanes do when there is nothing more to wait for, and the key hung where it hangs, and the room above settled into quiet with the leaf on the table and the form on its side table, and nobody stayed to watch it.

**What that morning cost is a column, and it is the woman's who keeps the key, and a place in a room and a hearing that stands are not in it and neither of them has a price.**

**a hand file at eighteen, a cake of soap at four, a bucket at twenty-seven, a tallow candle at nine, a sieve at twenty-three, a hank of yarn at six, a wooden rule at thirty-one, a whetstone at fifteen, a leather strap at five, a nail at twenty, and a jar of oil at twelve.** Eighteen, four, twenty-seven, nine, twenty-three, six, thirty-one, fifteen, five, twenty and twelve is one hundred and seventy pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and seventy pence is three marks and twenty-six over**, and a drawer about eleven inches deep and about four hundred returns and about nine hundred sheets and about a hundred and four in one band and about four hundred and nine parish rolls and seven questions and nine entries and a bare leaf and a fourth head and nine counties are ten figures of distance and of count and not one of them is a price, and the second hour and the seventh hour and the first of a month and a Monday and four weeks and four and a half years and a Thursday and a market Saturday and one more week and a fixed day are ten figures of time and not one of them goes in at either end of this column. **About nine people in a lane and a woman of about twenty-nine who keeps the room and a woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key and a copyist of about twenty-six and a man of about fifty-eight with a badge and a man of about thirty-four with a sheet are six figures of persons and are not added to one another.** Nobody thanked anybody.
