# Chapter 0556

**A pot of loam about a foot across came up a made road into a market town of about two thousand people on the eleventh of Greenmonth, YR 320, a Monday and not a market day, at about the fourth hour, in the hands of a woman of about thirty-eight who had walked nine days, and there was a scion in it of about nine inches tied with a cord, and the scion came off the oldest tree in the lower part of a valley that four days and about nine of hard going from the nearest made road, and about nine people in a hired room over a saddler's looked at it, and it did not open anything.**

It came on the cart at about the fourth hour and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet got it off the cart and put it on the table and asked the driver where it had come from, and the driver said the woman had, and the woman was standing in the lane with her boots in her hand.

Nobody had sent her. Nobody had been sent to that valley. At the foot of every return in this county, in the same ink as the rest of the printing, there is a line that says nobody is to be sent to find out, and it has said that for about a hundred and forty years, and **she walked nine days down a road nobody sent her down and came nine days back up it with a foot of earth from a tree in it and she is on no sheet in nine counties and she is not on any column.**

Then the woman came in and put her boots under the bench and said she would say the thing she had come to say and would not say it twice, and the man of about fifty-eight asked her to say it and not to say it twice, and she said it.

“**The letter says the oldest tree in the Long Set is dead. It has been dead twenty-two years. That is in the letter and the letter is written in four hands and every one of the four of them wrote it down because they were told it by somebody who had stood in front of the tree.**”

“**How do you know it is not?**” the man of about fifty-three said, and he was not unkind about it and he was not believing her and he did not pretend to.

“**Because I cut the scion off it in the second week of the set sitting and it was on the tree that morning and it is on my board now,**” she said, “**and the man who was in that valley with you has stood in front of it and seen nothing in the bark, and both of those are true and I have not come nine days to make one of them stop.**”

**And the man of about thirty-eight with a satchel said the thing that three people in that room had been feeling for about four days, and he said it to the pot and not to anybody.**

“**A tree that has been dead twenty-two years and a tree that was cut from this year are two trees, and one of them has to be a lie, and the lie is either in a letter written in four hands by people who were not in the valley, or it is in the head of a woman who walked nine days with a foot of earth. I am not going to decide which and I would like somebody to write down that I did not decide it, because in about four days everybody in this room is going to have decided it and about half of them are going to be wrong.**”

Nobody thanked him and nobody decided it in that room on that day and about four people in nine counties decided it in the next four days and the four of them are not a figure of persons and not one of them was in that room.

**Then she corrected the letter, and she did it flat, and she had it on her board.**

“**The letter says the Long Set is nine terraces. It is eleven. The Long Set is the name for the whole lower slope and it has eleven terraces and the count is eleven and it has been eleven since before the fruit was planted in that order. Nine is how far a man gets in a day who has walked the slope once and written down what he saw at the end of it, and a man who has done that is not wrong and he has written a figure that is a figure about his walking, and about four of the eleven people in this county would call that the Long Set because they have read the letter.**”

Nobody thanked her. The woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key said that eleven terraces and a day's walk are two figures of two different things and that the letter had put them under one head, and the copyist of about twenty-six wrote it on a bare leaf with no head over it and ruled nothing and read none of it back.

Then the man of about fifty-eight asked her one question, and it was not about the tree and not about the terraces and not about the letter, and it is the whole of what that room did on that Monday.

“**Who told you to send it?**”

“**Nobody,**” she said. “**The Standing did not tell me to send it and I did not ask them and they have not been told that I have sent it and I do not know what will happen to me at the end of the week when this comes back, and I have not thought about that for nine days because I have been walking, and I would rather not start now.**”

**And the man of about fifty-eight said out loud what a thing like that is, and it is the whole of that afternoon's business and it is nine sentences and nobody wrote them down.**

“**Then it is not a gift and it is not a duty and it is not on behalf of anybody, and I am going to say the three things I know about it and then I am going to stop. It is a thing a person did. It came up a made road in a cart and it has not cost anybody anything and it is a foot of earth out of a valley with a tree in it that four people in nine counties have now described in two ways. I am not going to put it in a book and I am not going to rule a head over it and I am going to put it on that table, and it will stand there, and if anybody in this room knows what a foot of earth from a place is for, they can say so, and if nobody in this room knows, then the room will have stood in front of a thing it cannot use, and that is a Sunday's work in a room that is shut on Sundays.**”

Nobody thanked him. Nobody argued with him. About four of the nine people in that room wanted to say what it was for and about five did not, and the two sets do not overlap and the room did not vote.

**And the cost of that Monday is a column and it is the man of about thirty-four with a sheet's, and it is the sixteenth one he has kept in about nine years, and a foot of earth out of a valley is not in it.**

**a pot of paste at two, a lump of chalk at four, a sheet of oiled paper at six, a pound of candles at seven, a second pound of candles at nine, a night's straw at thirteen, a bushel of loam at sixteen, a second bushel of loam at twenty-three, a stone of tallow at fifty-five, and a pair of shears at fifty-seven.** Two, four, six, seven, nine, thirteen, sixteen, twenty-three, Fifty-five and Fifty-seven is one hundred and ninety-two pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and ninety-two pence is exactly four marks**, and eleven terraces is a figure of ground and not a figure of persons and is not in this column, and the nine in the letter is a figure about a man's walking and is not in it, and nine days there and nine days back is a figure of time and is not in it, and the twenty-two years is a figure of time and is not a price, and the two halves of that room are the same nine people counted twice under two different opinions and neither of those counts is a figure of anybody and neither of them goes in at either end of this column.

The pot stood on the table by the side window until about the eighth hour, and at about the seventh hour the woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key asked the room one practical question, which was what a person waters a thing like that with, and nobody in that room could answer it, and she said *that is the first question anybody has asked about it and it is the right one*, and went and got a can from the yard and watered it herself, and nobody thanked her and she is not a keeper of anything.
