# Chapter 0582

**The Long Set from the top down, the ninth hour of the swell sitting, four sets of people asked the same question in the same voice on the same morning, about nine hundred drying cloths going over a line, about nine hundred picking cords going into a bundle, the fruit not six weeks off being picked, and a slate with three names on it carried from house to house by a man who keeps the water.**

They asked it in the same voice because the woman of about thirty-eight insisted on that, and she was right, and the man of about fifty-one carried the slate and read out what the three of them had written and did not put a word of his own on the end of it.

The question is on the slate under the three names, and nobody added to it.

What is your share of four miles.

That is the whole of it. Not what can you afford and not what will you agree to and not what do you think ought to be done about the water at the foot of a wall. What is your share of four miles. A person can say a number or say they have not got one and both of those are answers and both of them are allowed, and that is the first thing she said out loud before anybody answered anybody.

The nine hundred cloths went over the line while it was said. The woman of about fifty-eight who turns them does about nine hundred in a sitting and does not stop for anybody, and she has turned about nine hundred cloths through the whole of the asking and nobody has said that she stopped, because she did not.

The first set to be asked was the middle of that Long Set, which is where the woman of about thirty-eight keeps her four blocks and where the man of about thirty-one has his mother's house on the second terrace, and it said yes.

Nine said yes at that one and nobody wrote how many there were, because the man of about fifty-one does not count people and has said out loud that a man who counts people in this valley is a man who has started something.

“**Yes,**” the man of about thirty-one said, “**and I will say what it is and I will say it now and not in a week, and it is not a big number and it is not nothing.**”

Nobody asked him for a figure. She asked him for one and he said he would put it in writing and she said write it in your own hand and not on the slate, because a slate is a finding and a share of four miles is not a finding, and he said he understood that and did it.

The second set was the top of that Long Set, which is the man of about fifty-one's, and it said no, and the reason it gave is the best reason anybody gave all morning and it is not an excuse.

“**I have nine terraces above the water and no money in a hand at the end of a pick,**” he said. “**Not none. Some. Enough for a barrow-wheel and a new hoe handle and about four days of bread, and I have counted it, and if I put a share of four miles on this slope I take it off the fruit that is not picked yet, and the fruit is not picked yet for about six weeks, and the water stops before that. So it is no, and I am not asking you to think less of me for it, and I am not going to be asked again this month.**”

Nobody thanked him. Nobody argued with him. The woman who is his neighbour and has been his neighbour for nine years stood on the grass and let the whole of it go past without saying one word in her own mouth about it, and about four people on that slope have noticed that she let it go and nine have noticed that she did not say one word in the oldest voice either.

The third set was the four lowest terraces, and it said yes before anybody had finished the question, and the woman of about thirty-one who keeps them was the one who said it, and she is one of about nine households and four of the others are her neighbours and none of the four of them was asked separately and all four of them are in her answer.

“**Yes,**” she said. “**Before you have finished saying it. I have had about nine days to work out what I was going to say and I have said it to my four neighbours and none of the four of them said no and one of them said it was a mad thing and one of them said nothing and one of them said she would rather not and that is three of the four and I am the fifth and I have not asked the four of them to change and I have not asked the four of them whether they would rather not, because if I do that then I have to stand there and watch, and I have watched a person do that in this valley and I am not going to ask it.**”

Nobody said that was fair. Nobody said that was not fair either. The man of about fifty-one read out the third answer and then he did the thing that made it a refusal and he did it without stopping, and he has not said since whether he did it well.

“**Your share is the smallest and it is the dearest and I am not going to argue with you about that in front of these people, and I have written it down and I have not softened it.**”

The fourth set was the settlement at the middle terrace, where the board is, and it said no, and the reason it gave is a reason about what a record is and it is the sharpest thing said in this valley since the ground was opened.

“**I will not fund a thing three people wrote on a slate in a field,**” the woman of about forty-four who keeps that board said, and she said it to the man of about fifty-one and not to the woman of about thirty-eight, and she said it about four times. “**A record read by four people and not by the valley is not a record the valley can fund. It is not that I doubt the three of you. Two of you I have known since I was a girl and one of you is a boy. It is that in about twenty-two years this valley did one thing with a piece of writing and the thing it did was put it in the ground, and every one of us was better off for it except the nine households who were not asked, and I am not saying that out loud to make anybody feel bad, I am saying it because I am the woman who keeps a board and a board is the place where you say the thing everybody is thinking.**”

“**And the nine households are us,**” the woman of about thirty-eight said.

“**The nine households are not the argument,**” the woman of about forty-four said. “**The nine households are the bill.**”

Nobody answered her. The man of about fifty-one stood on the settlement green with a slate in his hand and about nine people round him and the ninth hour going the way the ninth hour goes, and he read out the fourth answer to everybody, and the boy of about fifteen rang the bell at the fourth point for the penny and nobody asked him why he had rung it and he has not been asked.

The woman of about sixty-four was at the head of that Long Set tying cords and she heard the whole of it, and she tied about nine hundred in a sitting of which about a hundred and forty are hers and are paid in fruit and not in money, and she said one thing at the end of it and it was not about the money.

“**I have walked round that old tree at the eighth hour for thirty years,**” she said, “**and I have never once counted the stones, and I am not going to start now because somebody wants a number off a slate. You want four miles mended. Go and mend four miles.**”

Nobody thanked her. The woman of about fifty-eight turned the last of the cloths and said nothing at all, and about four people have taken the view that she said more by not saying it, and nine have taken the view that a woman who turns nine hundred cloths in a sitting has not got the standing to say anything about a channel, and the two sets do not overlap and she has not been asked.

**Two of the four said yes and two of the four said no on one morning at the ninth hour, and no one in this valley was asked to change, and nobody has said out loud that either of the two who said no ought to say yes, and the man of about thirty-one has put his own share in writing in his own hand and it is not a large figure and it is not nothing, and the woman of about thirty-one's share is the smallest and the dearest and has been on the slate since and the slate is in the man of about fifty-one's coat.**

**Nobody has been told what happens next, because nobody in this valley has said what happens next, and the water at the foot of that Long Set is nine days from being somebody else's problem and about nine households are the problem.**

**That morning's cost is a column and it is the woman of about thirty-eight's, and two sets of people refusing each other in one hour is not in it and neither refusal has a price.**

**a drying cloth at three, a picking cord at six, a willow basket at eight, a jar of oil at eleven, a whetstone at thirteen, a coil of rope at sixteen, a cake of tallow at eighteen, a nail at twenty-one, a hand file at twenty-four, a round of bread at twenty-eight, and a skin of glue at thirty-three.** Three, six, eight, eleven, thirteen, sixteen, eighteen, twenty-one, twenty-four, twenty-eight and thirty-three is one hundred and eighty-one pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and eighty-one pence is three marks and thirty-seven over**, and four miles and about nine terraces above the water and about six weeks and nine households on four terraces and about nine hundred cloths and about nine hundred cords and about a hundred and forty of them hers are seven figures of distance and of count and of time and not one of them is a price. **The ninth hour and the eighth hour and thirty years and nine days and four days and twenty-two years are six more figures of time and none of them is in this column at either end, and nine people on a green and nine said yes at one set and four said no at two of them and ninety people twenty-two winters ago are four separate counts of persons, are not to be added to one another, and do not go in at this column, and the boy was paid his penny on the four points and the penny is not in it.** Two of the four said no on the same morning. Nobody thanked anybody.
