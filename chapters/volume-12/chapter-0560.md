# Chapter 0560

**A hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people was shut on the twenty-fourth of Greenmonth, YR 320, a Sunday and not a market day, and about four people were in it, and the floor of it had been washed, and nobody in nine counties knows who washed it and nobody was thanked, and a leaf about nine inches by six with four ruled heads on it went out of that room in the coat of a woman of about thirty-eight who had walked nine days to get there and was going to walk nine days back with a pot of earth in it that was not hers.**

Nobody opened that room for about two hours. It was shut because it is shut on Sundays and it had been shut since before the man with the badge came to it, and the rule was broken on that Sunday at about the seventh hour by a man with a chain of office round his neck that is a badge, who let four people in through a door he had the key to and did not call it a sitting and did not write anything down.

**The four of them were the man of about fifty-eight, the woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key, the copyist of about twenty-six, and a woman of about thirty-eight who keeps a block of ground in a valley four days and about nine of hard going from the nearest made road, and the last of those four did not come in first and did not come in last and was not asked which she would prefer.**

The pot was on the table by the side window because it could not be carried anywhere else until the cart came, and the cart comes on a Monday and goes on a Saturday, and the woman was going on the Saturday, and there was therefore a pot of earth standing in a shut room over a saddler's in a market town for about nine days, and nobody in that room ever decided who was looking after it.

**And the floor of that room has not been washed since the eighth of Wolfmonth, YR 319, and it was washed on that Sunday, and about four people saw the bucket and the wet boards and nobody asked, and nobody has said since, and the man of about fifty-eight said one thing about it out loud and it is nine sentences and it is the reason the rule was not broken for him.**

“**A rule that is broken in a room is a thing the room can wash. I did not break this rule and I want that written down somewhere, because I am the man with the badge and if anybody in nine counties hears that the floor of this room was got up on a Sunday they will come here and look for the man who ordered it, and there is no man, and there has not been a man since the eighth of a month carried at thirty, and it has taken about four months for this room to be able to say that at all and I am not going to have it be about me.**”

Nobody thanked him. The copyist of about twenty-six wrote it on a bare leaf, ruled no head over it, ruled nothing under it, and read none of it back.

**And the man of about fifty-eight had been holding one question for about nine days and he asked it at about the eighth hour of a Sunday in a shut room with nobody in it but the four of them, and it was the only question he has asked in five months that was not about a form, and he asked it to the woman who had walked nine days and not to the copyist and not to the woman with the key.**

“**I have a badge and a room and a book with one finding in it under a head that is not its own, and I have been sitting here for five months waiting for a road to send me an answer, and I am not going to insult you by pretending that what I want is for you to take a piece of paper home. Here is what I want. I want to be able to put in that book, in a line that a clerk in about four years can read without a man standing next to him explaining it, that a place exists, that somebody there was asked, and that the somebody who was asked is a person and not an office. That is three things. I have wanted them for about five months and you are the first person in nine counties who could put one of them in my hand.**”

Nobody thanked him for asking. She did not answer for about as long as a man takes to say nine and she looked at the pot on the table by the side window while she did it, and then she said three sentences and they are here.

“**Then you will have to ask me at the gate instead of in a room, because at the gate nobody is watching, and I will answer a question at a gate. I will not answer one in front of nine people, and I will not answer one that has to be written down by somebody else, and if you want three things in a line in a book then the second of them has to be mine and not yours.**”

“**That is correct,**” the man of about fifty-eight said. “**And I am not going to argue with it and I am not going to repeat it, and I would like it written down that a woman in a shut room on a Sunday told a man with a badge how to do his job and nobody thanked her and I did not take notes.**”

The copyist of about twenty-six wrote it on the same bare leaf as the floor and ruled nothing over either of them and read none of it back.

Then the leaf was made, and it was made the way they were made, which is that it takes about nine minutes and two people and neither of them is paid.

Nesta Braith ruled four heads on a leaf of foolscap about nine inches by six, and the fourth of them is about a sixteenth of an inch narrower than the three above it, and nobody has ever been told why, and the copyist in that room is the only person who knows and has not told anybody in that county and is not going to.

Three of them were filled with a pen. A place, in the words of the valley and not in the words of the letter, which are not the same words, and the difference between them is the whole of what she did in nine days. A figure of people, which is about eleven households on about eleven terraces and is a figure about a slope and not a figure of persons, and the eleven households and the eleven terraces are one figure told two ways and are not added to each other. And a trade, which is grafting.

**And the fourth head is ruled and empty and the question is written under it and not in it, and a question under a head is not an answer to it and does not go in it, and no head was cut over it and no office has filled it and nobody in that room filled it and nobody in nine counties has.**

**And the question under that head is the one that went under the fourth head of nine leaves in a room two hundred miles off in Thawmonth, in the same nine words, and the copyist said out loud that she was not going to write those nine words twice in one week because once is a copy and twice is a habit, and everybody in that room agreed with her about that, and nobody in that room has agreed with her about anything else.**

The woman of about thirty-eight read it herself anyway, twice, and did not say that it was the right question and did not say that it was not, and then she put the leaf inside her coat against her chest and said that she would take it on the cart and not be sent with it, and nobody in that room thanked her for that either and nobody argued with her about it.

**And the pot went down to the yard and was put on the cart by the man of about thirty-four with a sheet, who is not sending anybody and has said so out loud, and the cart goes on a Saturday, and the Saturday is in about three days, and the woman will be on it and so will the leaf and so will the earth.**

Then the door was shut again and the four people went out into a street where there was no market, because it was a Sunday, and about eleven people who had come up a made road were still asleep in that town of about two thousand, and the market was not on and would not be on until the next morning, and nobody in that town said anything about a certificate, and nobody in that town has said anything about a certificate since.

**And the cost of that Sunday is a column, and it belongs to the woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key, and it is the sixth she has ever kept, and a floor that somebody got up on a Sunday is not in it and nobody is thanked for it.**

**a bucket at four, a stick of lye at seven, a second broom at nine, a bundle of kindling at eleven, a pound of candles at twelve, a night's straw at thirteen, a pot of paste at fourteen, a sheet of oiled paper at sixteen, a stone of tallow at eighteen, a bootlace at nineteen, a ream of paper at twenty-two, and a second night's bed at thirty-two.** Four, seven, nine, eleven, twelve, thirteen, fourteen, sixteen, eighteen, nineteen, twenty-two and thirty-two is one hundred and seventy-seven pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and seventy-seven pence is three marks and thirty-three over**, and a leaf is not a price and is not in this column, and four people and about nine people and eleven households and eleven terraces are four figures of three different things, and a figure of a person is not a figure of households and a figure of households is not a figure of terraces, and not one of the four is in this column, and the difference of about a sixteenth of an inch in a ruled line is a difference in a ruled line and is not a figure of money, of persons, of days or of distance and is not in it.

Upstairs, where it had been since the sixth of a month carried at thirty, the half leaf was face down on a table with a question under its fourth head that nobody in nine counties has answered and that nobody in nine counties turned over on that Sunday or on any other, and the Crown's form was on the side table with four heads on it and the fourth of them empty and about five months old and still empty, and the pot was gone down to the yard, and the floor of that room was wet at the boards by the door and would be dry by morning.
