# Chapter 0558

**A market went on in the street of a market town of about two thousand people on the sixteenth of Greenmonth, YR 320, a Saturday and a market day, from the first hour to the ninth, and one of eleven people who had come four days said one word out loud that about nine people in that market town had not heard said in a street in about five months, and she said it once and did not say it again, and a thing said once in a market by a person who has said it before in about four other places is a different object from a thing said once in a room by nine people, and about four hundred people stood up and read four ruled lines off a board in that street in Mudmonth and none of those about four hundred were in that market that day.**

The word is **continuation.**

She said it at about the third hour, standing at the end of the weigh-house board with her hands behind her back, to a man of about twenty-nine with a handcart who had asked her what it was like where she had been, and he asked her the way people ask, which is as though the answer were going to be about the weather.

“**It is a continuation,**” she said. “**It is not another county and it is not another province and it is four days and about nine of hard going from a made road, and none of that is what the word means. It means the same country with the argument taken out. I am telling you because you have counted carts on that street for about nine years and you are going to have to decide in about four years whether you are willing to be part of what is done to this one, and a man who has counted carts has a better head for it than a man who has read a letter.**”

The man of about twenty-nine with a handcart looked at her for about as long as a man takes to say four and then asked her what the word meant.

“**I am not going to say it twice,**” she said, “**and I would rather you did not want it twice than that I were a person who said it twice. If you cannot hear it once properly you will not hear it twice properly either, and I have stood at the end of a place for about a fortnight and I would not like anybody in it to hear a thing like that carelessly.**”

Nobody thanked her. Nobody repeated the word. **Nobody in that market repeated it and nobody has repeated it since, and about four people in that market have said, since the word, that they heard it and about four have said they did not and the two sets do not overlap, and the word is not in any column and is not in any book in this county.**

And the man of about fifty-eight with a chain of office that is a badge heard it from about nine paces and did not ask her what it meant and said out loud, to the people standing round that board and not to her, that he was not going to ask her and why.

“**Because I have a chain of office round my neck that is a badge and it is not a thing that makes a question good or bad, and because that room over the saddler's has spent five months not asking anybody a second thing, and because a man who asks a stranger what a word means has stopped keeping a book of answers and started keeping a book of words, and I have seen what that looks like in about four years and I did not like it and I am not going to be the one who starts it in this county.**”

About half of that little crowd took it for caution and the other half took it for being afraid, the halves do not overlap, and nobody voted on which it was.

Then the man of about fifty-three who had brought the letter said the thing that ends the possibility, and he said it out loud and flat and to the ground in front of him, and he did not soften it and about half the people round that board believed him and the other half did not and neither of the halves has said so out loud.

“**Then the certification cannot be given. I have known that since a woman told me on a Monday that a valley has eleven terraces and not nine, and I have not said it since, and I am saying it now so that nobody in nine counties can tell me afterwards that I came up a made road and asked a man to sign something. A road may be described, or opened, or refused, or certified, and in every one of those four things it may be done by its own answer and by nothing else. That valley has not had an answer since before the man you are all talking about was born. I asked about it before I came up this road and nobody in nine counties could tell me what an answer is in that place, and I have been asking since, and the answer to my asking is that nobody here knows.**”

Nobody thanked him and nobody argued with him.

“**Then who is the answer there?**” the woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key asked.

“**I do not know,**” Merritt Gallow said, and it is the first thing he has said, since that Monday, that he had not written down beforehand.

**And then he said the other half of it, and it is the worst thing anybody said in that market town in about five months, and it is true, and that is said here rather than left to the four of them in a corridor an hour afterwards.**

“**I was sent to find out whether there is one. I will tell you that I was sent, and I will not tell you by whom, and I have been in this county for four days being pleasant and useful and I have not been dishonest once and I have not said out loud that anybody in this county is the reason there is no answer there, and there is a woman from that valley standing in this market at about the ninth pace from that door who may be the answer and may not be, and nobody has asked her and nobody is going to ask her today, and I am not going to ask her today either, because I have brought eleven people a long way and I am not going to spend her in a street.**”

Nobody thanked him for any of it and nobody called him a knave and nobody in that market has yet been told whose plan the letter is.

**And then the man of about fifty-one in a grey coat said the only thing he said in that market in about four weeks, and it was not about the letter and it was not about a certificate.**

“**There are about nine people in the room over the saddler's on a Wednesday and about four hundred people in this street on a Thursday, and I have spent five months at a thing that was meant to stay small, and what has come of it is not that it is any bigger. It is that a woman said a word in a street and about nine people in my room have spent the morning deciding what to do about a word instead of about a letter. I said out loud in that room that it was small because the room was small and not because the county was, and I am not taking that back and I am not here to say it again.**”

Nobody thanked him and he was not thanked and he is not a villain and he is not a knave and he did not say whose plan anything is.

**And the cost of that Saturday is a column and it is the man of about fifty-one in a grey coat's, and it is the fifth one he has ever kept, and a word said once is not in it.**

**a bootlace at two, a knob of beeswax at six, a sheet of cartridge paper at twelve, a pound of tow at fifteen, a sheet of vellum at twenty-six, a pound of soap at thirty, a stone of chalk at thirty-one, and a second stone of chalk at thirty-two.** Two, six, twelve, fifteen, twenty-six, thirty, thirty-one and thirty-two is one hundred and fifty-four pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and fifty-four pence is three marks and ten over**, and one word is not a price and is not in this column, and both of the about four hundreds in this county, which are the one that stood up in this street in Mudmonth and the one out at the made lane, are a figure and are not a figure and are in this column at neither end, and eleven people is a figure of persons and is not in it, and about nine paces and about nine people are two figures of distance and of persons and neither of them is a figure of anybody and neither of them is in it, and a road four days and about nine off is not in it, and the about nine hundred sheets a month and the about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month are two counts of two series in a room two hundred miles off and neither of them is in this column and neither is derived from the other.

Then the man of about thirty-four with a sheet followed her up to the room over the inn and asked her one question at the stair, and it was whether the answer to a valley is a person or an office, and she said that she did not know and that she would not guess in a stair, and he wrote none of it down and asked her nothing else.

At about the ninth hour the market went down and about nine people carried four ruled lines away from a board they had all read in Mudmonth, and none of the nine carried anything else out of that street that day, and the word a woman said at the third hour went with the nine who heard it and with nobody else, and about four hundred people did not hear it and about nine of the four hundred had heard the word from somebody else since and have said so and have not been asked where they heard it.
