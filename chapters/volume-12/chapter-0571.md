# Chapter 0571

**The settlement at the middle terrace, the first hour of the bar sitting, a board on a wall with four divisions ruled on it and three of them filled, a leaf with four lines under a fourth head and one word in the margin, and a woman of about thirty-eight who asked four people a question about herself and is going to ask about nine people a question about the ground.**

The first work of the bar sitting is carrying the dead wood off the top of the second terrace and down to the foot of the slope, and it is the first hour and there are eleven people on that ground between the board and the top of the step.

The leaf is on the board where she left it. Three divisions filled and the fourth ruled and empty, four lines under the fourth in her own hand, and the word **noted** in the margin in a different ink and not in the same one. Nobody has filled the fourth and nobody has ruled a line above it and nobody has taken the leaf down.

**And nobody has told her she may. Four people were asked at the second hour of a sitting and two of them were not at the board and one of those sent a child, and the fourth was there and said nothing for about as long as a man takes to say nine, and that is the whole of what came back to her.**

She came to the board at about the eighth hour and not at the second, and the woman of about forty-four who keeps it noticed the hour before she noticed the woman.

“**You asked at the second hour,**” the woman of about forty-four said, “**and I was here, and I have not got anything to write on the strength of it, and I would like to know what you are going to ask me now.**”

“**I am going to ask a different question,**” the woman of about thirty-eight said, “**and I am going to ask it with about nine people on this ground instead of four stools in a room, and the difference between the two askings is not the hour. The first one was about me. This one is not.**”

“**Then ask it.**”

She asked it. Nobody thanked her and nobody interrupted her and about four of the people carrying wood stopped where they were for about as long as a man takes to say four and then carried on.

“**Is there anybody in this valley who will let a person dig at the foot of the oldest tree in the Long Set?**”

The man of about thirty-one of the Standing said the first thing.

“**That is the ground the oath is on.**”

“**It is the ground the oath is on,**” the woman of about thirty-eight said, “**and I did not know that when I asked it, and I have not been told it since I arrived, and I am not asking you to lift an oath. I am asking whether anybody will let a person dig.**”

“**That is the same thing with less in it.**”

“**It is not, and you know it is not, and you have been in that room about nineteen years and you know the difference between a thing you may not do and a thing nobody will let you do.**”

Nobody argued with her. The man of about sixty of the Standing said the other half, and he said it slowly and he said it twice, which he does not do.

“**It is not a block and it is not yours and it is not mine, and there are about four people on this slope who could say yes to it this morning and every one of them is about thirty or less and about half of those four hold ground inside nine yards of it. I have been on this Standing for about nineteen years and there has not been a question put to it in my lifetime, and before me for about four years, and I am telling you the reason there has not, and it is not that nobody would answer you. It is that nobody has ever thought there was anything to answer.**”

Nobody thanked him for that. She asked him one question and it was the only one she asked anybody that hour.

“**Then somebody has thought one now.**”

“**Yes,**” the man of about sixty said, “**and I have been sitting here for about four minutes wishing that it was about a different thing.**”

Then the woman of about forty-four asked her to write down what she had asked, so that it could be carried up to the man of about fifty-one who keeps the water at the top of the Long Set, and she said no to that and gave the reason, and the reason is a fact about the room she is sitting in and not about the woman who asked her.

“**A written question is a question to an office, and there is no office in this valley, and I have found that out in about nine days and it has cost me a journey of nine days each way. I am not writing it down and I am not asking him to be told. I am asking him in the open the same as I asked you, and if he says no then he has said no in front of about nine people and not on a sheet that somebody can put away.**”

“**You want him to have to answer where we can hear it.**”

“**I want him to have to answer at all,**” she said, “**and I have been in this valley nine days and I have learned that the two of those are not the same thing and I did not know that anywhere else.**”

Nobody thanked anybody.

**And the man of about thirty-one said the thing that made it a different asking and not a better one, and he said it while he was still standing up.**

“**A question about you can be refused in a room and the refusal stays in the room. A question about that tree cannot, and that is the whole of what you have done and I do not think you have thought about the half of it where you cannot get the answer back.**”

“**I have thought about that half,**” the woman of about thirty-eight said. “**I thought about it on the way up the lane. I would rather be refused in the open than permitted in a room, and I said that once already about something else and I am not going to say it twice.**”

Aren Kest was about nine paces off for the whole of that hour and did not say one word, and the woman of about thirty-eight looked at him once in the middle of it and did not ask him a question either, and nobody on that slope has said since whether that was two people keeping out of each other's way or two people who had nothing to add to it.

So it was not carried up to anybody, and she went and stood at the top of the second step and said it again to the man of about fifty-one, who was nine terraces up with a foot-rule in his hand, and he heard his own name and came down about nine terraces' worth of a slope in about as long as a man takes to boil a kettle, and stood at the bottom of it.

Nobody has told him what to say. He has not said it.

**And the four lines under the fourth head are on the board, and the fourth head is ruled and empty, and there is no office in nine counties whose business it is to fill it and no person in this valley who can.**

Nobody ruled a head. Nobody filled one. Nobody cut a fifth line over it and nobody read the four lines back out loud.

**And the cost of that sitting is a column and it is the woman of about thirty-eight's, and a question about the oldest tree is not in it and a tree is not in it and neither of them has a price.**

**a stick of chalk at three, a pot of paste at six, a bootlace at eleven, a sheet of oiled paper at sixteen, a hoop of willow at eighteen, a length of tarred cord at twenty-one, a pruning knife at twenty-seven, a stone of loam at thirty-three, and a jar of oil at thirty-nine.** Three, six, eleven, sixteen, eighteen, twenty-one, twenty-seven, thirty-three and thirty-nine is one hundred and seventy-four pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and seventy-four pence is three marks and thirty over**, and the first hour and the eighth hour and the second sitting's second hour and nine days and nine days back are five figures of time and of distance and not one of them is a price, and eleven people on the ground and four of the nine who were asked at the second hour and four who stopped carrying wood are three figures of three different sets of people on one morning and none of them is a figure of this valley, which is eleven households on eleven terraces and is one figure of a slope told two ways, and neither half of it goes in at either end of this column. **Four heads and four divisions and a fifth line and a fourth head and a mark at the top of a leaf about nine inches by six are five shapes and not one object, and none of them is a price, and the word in the margin is not in this column and is not a head and is not a fifth line.**

The board stayed on the wall off the middle terrace with the leaf on it and nothing on the fourth, and the man of about fifty-one stood at the foot of the second step for about as long as a man takes to say nine and did not say one word about a tree, and four people in that valley have put it about that asking it out loud was a foolish thing that made a hard afternoon longer, and four others have put it about that it was the first question anybody had asked about anything on that slope in about twenty-two years, and the two sets do not overlap and nobody asked either of them how they knew.
