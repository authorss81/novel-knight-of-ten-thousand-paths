# Chapter 0564

**The head of the Long Set, the seventh hour of the swell sitting, about nine paces below the top of the slope, a woman of about thirty-eight with a spade and a root that has come up through a path, a man of thirty-two with a limp standing on the other side of the hole, and the first thing anybody in that valley has said to him that was not in answer to a question.**

Her name is Ilsa Wend and she has been in that valley all her life and about nine people in it could have said the name out loud and none of them did.

She did not wait to be asked. He came up the slope at about the seventh hour to ask her one practical thing about the block of ground, and she was already talking, and she had been talking to the root.

“**I am going to lose this tree,**” she said. “**I know that. The root has come up through the path and the path is where eleven people walk to the top every sitting of the year, and I can put the path a foot to the left or I can take the tree out at the root and let the wound close, and if I take it out at the root in the swell sitting then the tree has a hole in it all the way through the year and it will be carrying fruit in the pick, and a tree carrying fruit with a hole through it puts its weight on the wrong side of itself in about nine years. I am going to lose the tree.**”

She levered the spade and came out with a root the thickness of a wrist.

“**I am doing it in the swell sitting anyway, because in the pick there are nine people on this slope who can be spared and in the swell there are not, and I have four blocks of ground and one of them is in a place where the fruit is about two years off being worth anything and I would rather lose a tree I have had since I was nine than lose a year of a block I am not going to hand on.**”

Nobody thanked her. He asked his question, which was where the water came off the second terrace, and she answered it in about four sentences, and it was a good answer and it was not the answer he had come for.

**And then she said the other thing, and she said it without being asked about anything, and it is the whole of what that sitting was for.**

“**I would like to change my mind about something and I have not been able to for four years, and you are the first person I have said it to who was not going to answer me, and I am going to say the rest of it now and you are not going to write it down.**”

“**I am not going to write it down,**” he said.

“**I know. That is why I am saying it. I said the first part of it in a street in a market town four days' walk from here to about four people near a fish board and they did not hear it properly and I have thought about those four people every day since, and I have thought about this more, and the difference is that you cannot hear it properly either, and that is the whole of what I have wanted for four years.**”

She put the spade in and took it out again and wiped her face with the back of her wrist.

“**I grafted four years ago in the second week of the set sitting. I took a variety the Standing called a bad risk and I put it on a block that had never held anything but the ordinary kind, and it took, and the Long Set ate better in those two years than the Wet Set ate in a bad year, and at the end of the second year the Standing told me I had done it wrongly. Not that the graft was wrong. That I had done it wrongly, because I had not asked.**”

“**What is it you would change your mind about?**” Aren Kest said, and it was the only question he asked in that sitting, and he asked it because she had not asked him not to.

“**That. I would like to be able to say that a thing I did was wrong without it also turning out that I am the one who has to move. That is all of it. I have wanted that for four years and it is a small want and I know it is a small want, and it is the only one I have.**”

**And the oldest voice in that valley came out of her mouth when she said the next part, and he heard it, and she heard him hear it, and neither of them said anything about it.**

“**They were pleased,**” she said, in a voice about four generations old, calm and even and not hers. “**They would be pleased if I asked them. They would be pleased the way a room is pleased. And being pleased is not the same as being allowed, and I worked that out on my own at about nineteen and I have never had it in front of anybody who could have told me I had it wrong, and that is not the same as being right, and I have said that in a street already.**”

Then she went back to her own voice, in the middle of a sentence, with no sound of a change at all, and the change is the only thing in that sitting that nobody in that valley could have pointed at afterwards.

“**There was a strip of ground at the foot of the Long Set. It asked to be let go. About ninety people went out on it, and that is the figure I have and it is a figure about what people said afterwards and not a figure anybody counted, and I was about sixteen and I was asked to go and I said yes and I did not go, and I have not told anybody that in four years and I have told you because you are a stranger with a limp and a stranger with a limp is the safest person in nine counties and about four of them are.**”

Nobody thanked her.

“**I am not going to tell you where it was done,**” she said, “**and it is not because I do not trust you. It is because there is a place on this slope where a thing is not said out loud, and I have known about the place for thirty years and I have never been near it, and I am not going to take a man from off the hill to it, and if you go looking for it on your own then you will find it in about a day because there is one old tree on this slope and everybody knows it, and when you stand at that tree somebody will come out and ask you what you are doing, and I would like to have said this to you first.**”

Nobody argued with her. He did not say that he was sorry, and the reason is that he has not done anything to be sorry about in that sitting, and the reason he has not done anything is that she did not ask him for anything and the whole of the last hour has been her holding a spade and talking.

**And she asked him one question at about the eighth hour, and it was not about a road and not about the cut, and it was the first question anybody in that valley had asked him.**

“**What did you do before you came up here?**”

“**I looked at things under water for eleven years,**” he said, “**and I have been asked about it about nine times since I came and I have given about nine different answers and none of them was that one, and I would like you to know that I noticed while I was saying it and I noticed afterwards and I did not stop.**”

Nobody thanked her.

**And the cost of that sitting is a column and it is the woman of about thirty-eight's, and a root that has come up through a path is not in it and a tree is not in it and neither of them has a price.**

**a spade-handle at two, a bootlace at four, a stick of tar at six, a rooting-knife at nine, a sheet of cartridge paper at twelve, a knife-scabbard at sixteen, a barrow of loam at twenty-one, a second barrow of loam at twenty-five, a willow withe at twenty-nine, a stone of chalk at thirty-one, and a cake of beeswax at eighteen.** Two, four, six, nine, twelve, sixteen, twenty-one, twenty-five, twenty-nine, thirty-one and eighteen is one hundred and seventy-three pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and seventy-three pence is three marks and twenty-nine over**, and about ninety people who went out on a strip of ground twenty-two years ago is a figure of a past and not a figure of anybody standing in that valley and is not a price, and eleven terraces and a path with a root in it are two figures of a slope and neither of them goes in at either end of this column, and about four people near a fish board in a market town four days' walk off are a figure of persons in a different county and are not in this column, and the four years and the nineteen years and the thirty years and the four days' walk are four figures of time and of distance and none of them is a price, and the change in a woman's voice in the middle of a sentence is not in this column and cannot be.

The hole was filled in at about the ninth hour and the root was burned at the foot of the slope and the smoke went up past the High Set and nobody in that valley looked up at it, and he went down to the drying floor with about a half of what she had said still in him, and about half is not a figure of anything and is not in any column.
