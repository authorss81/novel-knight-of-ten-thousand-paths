# Chapter 0578

**The settlement at the middle terrace, the ninth hour of the set sitting, a plank of green wood lying on the grass in the light with marks cut into it, a board on a wall with four divisions ruled on it and three of them filled, and the first argument this valley has had in about twenty-two years, going on now and not stopping, and nobody in it angry.**

Nobody has decided to have it. It came the way weather comes.

It is at the ninth hour, because that is when the hands are off the ground and standing about, and about nine households on the four lowest terraces of the Long Set live in that hour more than in any other, and what the ninth hour has done in six days is put nine people in the same forty yards of grass with a plank in it.

**And the finding does not make anybody angry, and the volume of it does not make anybody angry, and about four of the nine people standing there would have said a fortnight ago that there is nothing here to be angry about.**

The man of about fifty-one said it first and he said it plainly and to everybody.

“**I opened that ground and I would open it again, and I have thought about it for about four days and I have not found a reason to be careful about it, and I am going to stop finding reasons to be careful about it. There is a water mark on a piece of wood under the oldest tree that says the water was let out at the foot of a wall and put back in at the head of it on the day the strip was let go, and that is the reason four miles of stone want mending, and I have carried a fault I could not say for about nine years.**”

“**That is a mark,**” the man of about sixty said. “**Nobody has said what it is a mark of.**”

“**Then somebody will,**” the man of about fifty-one said, and that was the whole of it and he did not raise his voice at any point of that block.

Nobody thanked him for saying it out loud, and nobody thanked him in any other way, and he did not go near the plank again that hour.

The woman of about thirty-eight said her half and it was a different half and it was not smaller.

“**I dug that ground. I did not expect to and I have not decided about it. I would open it again and I would rather it had not been opened, and both of those are true and I have said them in that order on purpose.**”

Nobody argued with her. The man of about thirty-one of the Standing asked the question that has been asked in that room for about nineteen years in one version or another.

“**Then which is it?**”

“**The record was opened for the water and not for us,**” the woman of about thirty-eight said, “**and that is what I want argued about, and I have not argued about it in four days because I have been busy saying a different true thing about a voice coming out of my mouth. He opened it because a wall is going. I opened it because I was about sixteen and I said yes to about ninety people and I did not go, and there was nothing in the ground that had my name on it and there is nothing in the ground now and that is not why I dug.**”

“**That is your business and not mine,**” the man of about fifty-one said, “**and the water is the water.**”

“**It is, and it is mine too, because it is four terraces below my ground and about nine households live on them, and I have four blocks and one of them is in a place where the fruit is about two years off being worth anything.**”

“**Then you have said it,**” the man of about fifty-one said, “**and that is the end of it.**”

And then she said the thing she had said in a market town four days' walk off to about four people near a fish board, and she said it out loud in the open on a working morning in front of about nine people, and it was not in the oldest voice in that valley and half of the nine noticed that.

“**I would like to be told I was wrong. That is all of it. I have wanted it since I was about sixteen and I have wanted it out loud, in the open, from somebody who knows, and I am not going to be told it by a man in a room and I am not going to be told it by a man who agrees with me.**”

Nobody told her. The man of about fifty-one said the reason and he said it as though he had thought about it for about nine minutes and had arrived somewhere he did not like.

“**I do not think you were wrong and I am not going to tell you that you were not, because I did not ask to go and I did not go either and I was about nine years old, and a man who was nine has nothing to say to a woman of thirty-eight about whether she should have gone. If I say it, it will be me being kind, and you have told us in a market that being pleased is not the same as being allowed, and you were talking about a room and you are talking about me.**”

“**I had hoped you would say it,**” the woman of about thirty-eight said, and nobody in that valley has asked her what she expected him to do instead.

Nobody thanked him. Nobody thanked her.

**And the two people who opened it are the two who disagree most, and they were asked about it by four separate people in about an hour, and each of them gave the same answer twice, and neither of them gave the other's.**

Then the woman of about thirty-one, who keeps a block that was thinned wrong for a season by a man of about forty-four who does not mind, said the thing that the nine households have been not saying for about twenty-two years, and she said it without any heat at all.

“**Nine days ago that plank was in the ground and my four neighbours and I were not losing anything, because there was nothing on anybody's sheet and a thing that is not on a sheet is a thing you can be wrong about privately. It is on the grass now. Four people in this valley can read a bit of it and every one of the four is above us on this slope, and the water question is about us and the question is about them, and we are going to be the ones who pay for it whatever anybody says in this forty yards today.**”

Nobody argued with her. The man of about forty-four said that the peace had been worth a year and that the strip of ground had not been worth one, and he said both of those in one breath and would not be drawn on either.

The woman of about seventy came down from the Wet Set at about the ninth hour and stood at the edge of it for about as long as a man takes to say nine and said nothing at all, and nobody asked her why, and about nine people have since said that she was the only person in that valley who had said nothing about it and nobody has asked her.

The man of about thirty-one said one thing about the future that is the only instruction anybody gave that day.

“**Whoever stands up there and reads that plank out loud has to read it in their own voice. Not in the one everybody in this valley was given at the table. There is a woman of about thirty-eight who can hear the difference and she has told me in front of nine people that she cannot stop it coming out of her, and if the first reading of it is done in that voice then it will be the same document as the one that went in the ground and we will have opened a hole for nothing.**”

Nobody thanked him.

**And the woman of about forty-four who keeps the board wrote nothing at all on that board that hour, and ruled nothing, and cut nothing, and the leaf with four lines under a fourth head is still on it and the fourth is still ruled and still empty and there is no office in nine counties that can fill it and nobody in this valley has tried.**

**And a man of thirty-two with a limp was somewhere between the end of the made road and the foot of this lane and has not been on this slope in about nine days, and two people have said so and nobody has seen him and nobody has been asked.**

**And the cost of that hour is a column and it is the woman of about thirty-eight's, and about nine households on four terraces and a year lost off a block of ground and about twenty-two years are three figures of three different things and none of them is a price, and wanting to be told you were wrong is not in this column and cannot be.**

**a basket at four, a length of cord at eight, a needle at twelve, a stone of chalk at fifteen, a jar of paste at nineteen, a sheet of cartridge at twenty-two, a knee-cap at twenty-six, a cake of ink at twenty-nine, a grindstone at forty, and a ream of paper at forty-one.** Four, eight, twelve, fifteen, nineteen, twenty-two, twenty-six, twenty-nine, forty and forty-one is two hundred and sixteen pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and two hundred and sixteen pence is four marks and twenty-four over**, and four miles of stone and the fourth hundred pace and nine yards of lip and four terraces and nine households and eleven households on eleven terraces and ninety people twenty-two winters ago and nine days under water are eight figures of eight different things and none of them is a price and none of them goes in at either end of this column. **A mark cut into a plank is not a head and a head is not a division and a division is not a fourth line, and the fourth head of the Crown's form is ruled and empty about four counties off, and nobody has filled it and nobody is going to.**

Nobody thanked anybody.

**The water is on the table in about nine households' worth of words and nobody has paid anything yet, and nobody has said out loud who pays, and the four sets of people who would have to find the money between them have not been asked, and two of them have not heard of it at all.**
