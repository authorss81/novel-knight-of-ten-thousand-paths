# Chapter 0588

**The middle of the Long Set, the eighth hour of the pick sitting, about nine hundred drying cloths on about nine hundred feet of line on the only flat ground within four hundred paces of the fourth hundred pace of the cut, a woman of about fifty-eight who turns them, about nine people standing about, and a decision taken in the open by a person who was not asked to take it and is not a keeper of anything.**

Nobody argued about it, and that is the fact about this morning that four people have put out since and nine have put out a different version of, and the two sets do not overlap.

The fourth hundred pace of the cut is the only part of those four miles that has to be right, and about nine people cannot do it, and the man of about fifty-one said so out loud at the top four days ago so that nobody could say afterwards that they had not known. The two sets that said yes have money. The money buys men. There are not nine men within four days and about nine of hard going who can lay that bed, and there are four, and three of the four are the man of about fifty-one and the man of about forty-four who holds the second hoe and a carter of about nineteen who came in on his own answers, and the fourth is a man of about sixty-four on the second terrace who last laid a course in this channel before the oath.

Getting to that fourth hundred pace takes barrows of stone and about nine barrows of spoil to every four paces, and barrows need flat ground to run on, and the only flat ground within four hundred paces of it is a strip about nine hundred feet long at the middle of that Long Set, and the strip has about nine hundred drying cloths on it.

The woman of about fifty-eight said the arithmetic out loud. She is not a keeper of anything and has never put her name to a board and has never been asked to.

“**Four hundred paces of barrow run and about nine hundred feet of ground and about nine hundred cloths. That is the whole of it and there is no second way of doing it and I am not going to stand here and pretend there is.**”

Nobody else was going to say it and the man of about fifty-one was not going to say it either.

“**Then they come down,**” the woman of about fifty-eight said.

Nobody thanked her for saying it. He did not argue and did not agree, and all he said was that the cloths were not his, and it was the shortest thing said on that strip of ground that day.

“**No,**” the woman of about fifty-eight said, and she said it fast, and then she said the rest of it slowly, which is a thing she has done about four times in as many years and never in front of anybody. “**The cloths are not yours and that is exactly why it is mine. If the cloths were yours I would be asking you to put nine hundred of your own on the ground for a channel that is going to keep working for you for the next thirty years, and I would be asking you in front of nine people, and you would have said yes because you are that sort of man and you would have been right to and I would have taken it. The cloths are not yours. About four fifths of them are the fruit of people who cannot get at a barrow run and one of them is mine, and the person who should decide is the one who turns them, and that is me, and I have not decided anything in my life.**”

Nobody argued with her. About nine people were standing about. The boy of about fifteen was one of them, and what he has said about it is that he was there and that nobody argued, and four people have said that is the single most frightening thing he has ever watched and nine have said that it is what a place looks like when nobody has a vote and everybody has an opinion and one of them finally says the sum out loud.

“**How long,**” the man of about thirty-one of the Standing said, and he said it as a man asking a room a question and not as a man of the Standing, and he wrote the answer on a slate because that is what they do now.

“**A day,**” the woman of about fifty-eight said. “**Four people can take nine hundred cloths off nine hundred feet in a day if they start at the third hour and do not stop, and there are about nine of us who can be spared off that pick for one day because the pick on the middle is nine days from being ready anyway. And they go into the press-house and under the middle terrace wall, and the ones under the wall are the ones that come out damp.**”

“**How many of the nine hundred come out dry,**” the man of about thirty-one said.

“**About two in three,**” she said, “**and I have turned nine hundred cloths at the eighth hour for about nine years and I can tell you that without counting a thing, and I have just told you a number I have not got and I am telling you now that I have not got it, and you can write that down or you can not.**”

He wrote it down. The slate is not the finding and the finding is not the slate and the board off the middle terrace has four divisions ruled on it and three filled, and a division is not a head and is not a fifth line, and nobody has filled a fourth head and there is no office in nine counties that can fill it.

**And the woman of about thirty-one on the four lowest terraces was four terraces down under about nine days and a morning of water, and about a fifth of that fruit was on those lines, and she was not in that forty yards, and nobody went down and told her, and the cloths came down at the third hour and were under the middle terrace wall by the ninth hour, and she was told at about the ninth hour of the day after.**

That is the cost and nobody took it out of anybody's hand. The woman of about fifty-eight did not come down. He did not come down either. The man of about thirty-one of the Standing wrote the number of the two in three on a slate and did not write the name of whose fruit it was, and the woman of about thirty-eight read the slate and put it face down on the grass, which is a thing she has never done to a slate.

“**Do not,**” she said, when he reached for it.

“**It is not finished.**”

“**It is not mine to finish and it is not yours and it is hers and she is under water four terraces down and nobody told her. Take it down to her in the morning. Not now. Now it is about four hours dark on that row and you would be standing in her light with a slate.**”

Nobody thanked her for that. The man of about thirty-one of the Standing carried the slate down the next morning and put it on a wall by the fourth terrace and came away, and the woman of about thirty-one read it and said one thing, which was that two in three was about right and that she had said in a market four days and about nine off that being pleased is not the same as being allowed and that this was the first time in her life she had watched the two of them come apart with a number in her hand.

**Nobody was angry. That is the other fact about that eighth hour and it is not a small one: about nine people stood on a working morning and watched nine hundred cloths of somebody else's fruit come down off the lines and not one of them was angry, and about four of the nine have given as their reason for not being angry that nobody in that forty yards had done anything wrong, and about nine have given as their reason that it was worse than angry, because a thing that nobody can be angry about is a thing that has already been agreed to and nobody knows who agreed to it.**

The man of about sixty of the Standing was told at the ninth hour, on the settlement green, by the man of about thirty-one, and he asked two questions and answered neither of them and said nothing else about it.

“**Was I asked,**” he said.

“**No.**”

“**By whom.**”

“**By a woman of about fifty-eight who turns the cloths and who is not a keeper of anything and who has never been asked your advice on a working morning in her life.**”

“**Was it right.**”

The man of about thirty-one of the Standing said that he did not know and that he was not going to argue for it, and the man of about sixty stood on the green and looked at the fourth terrace for a while and then said the other thing, which is the only instruction he has given anybody in about three weeks.

“**Then there is no room to put it in, and that is not a rule and it is not a precedent, and in about four years somebody is going to come to me and say a woman once did a thing on her own and that is the beginning of it, and they will be right, and I am not going to be the one who says they are wrong, and I am not going to be the one who says they are right either. Do not write this down. I have asked and I have been told no and I am not going to say another thing about it.**”

Nobody thanked him for that and nobody argued with it. The slate went into a pocket and the finding with three names on it is on a stone at the head of that Long Set, and the two are not the same object and the two have never been in the same hand.

**What that eighth hour cost is a column, and it is the woman of about fifty-eight's, and a third of about nine hundred cloths and a day and a pick and a season are not in it and not one of them has a price.**

**a bench peg at two, a coil of rope at six, a nail at nine, a plane at thirteen, a jar of oil at sixteen, a whetstone at nineteen, a hank of string at twenty-five, a stick of chalk at thirty, a trowel at thirty-three, a pot of tar at thirty-five, and a wooden wedge at thirty-nine.** Two, six, nine, thirteen, sixteen, nineteen, twenty-five, thirty, thirty-three, thirty-five and thirty-nine is two hundred and twenty-seven pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and two hundred and twenty-seven pence is four marks and thirty-five over**, and about nine hundred cloths and about nine hundred feet of line and four hundred paces and about nine barrows to four paces and the fourth hundred pace and three hundred paces and thirty years and about nine years are eight figures of count and of distance and of time and none of them is a price, and the third hour and the eighth hour and the ninth hour and the day after and a day and about four days and twenty-two years are seven more figures of time and none of them is priced in this column. **About nine people standing in forty yards and about nine households on four terraces and about ninety people twenty-two winters ago and three names on a slate and a man of about sixty-four on the second terrace are five separate counts of persons and none of them is to be added to another, and the penny is not in this column either.** Two in three was not counted. Nobody thanked anybody.
