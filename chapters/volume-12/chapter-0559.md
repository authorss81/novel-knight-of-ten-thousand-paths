# Chapter 0559

**In a hired room over a saddler's in a market town of about two thousand people, on the nineteenth of Greenmonth, YR 320, a Tuesday and not a market day, at about the fourth hour, a thing a place had sent and a person had carried for nine days stood in a pot on a table and did not open anything, and at about the sixth hour a man of thirty-two with a limp told a woman of about thirty-eight from a valley that the valley could not speak, in a lane behind the room with nobody else in it, and he did not say that he was sorry, and she did not say that he was.**

The fourth hour is the hour his left palm does the thing it has done at that hour for eleven years and he has never told anybody about it, and nobody in that room asked him about it and nobody in that room noticed it and the pot stood on the table through all of it with the cord tied round the scion and a can of water put in it on the Monday by a woman who is not a keeper of anything.

The man of about fifty-eight read the letter aloud again at the fourth hour, the whole of it, four hands, and then he read out the one paragraph that nobody had read out in that room, which is a paragraph in the second hand about a channel of about four miles that has not been mended since before the year before last and cannot be.

Then the woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key said the thing, and it is the whole of that afternoon, and it is nine sentences, and nobody wrote them down.

“**You have the only thing anybody in nine counties has had out of that place in twenty-two years and you have not asked anybody there a question. A thing arrives and it is a thing that has arrived. A question is the only thing in this county that has ever come back with a person in it that nobody in an office chose, and we have made nine of them, and some of them have come back, and one of them came back cut in half, and you have a pot with a tree in it and you have not asked one single question of a woman who kept a piece of bark on her belt for nine days so that she would have something of her own place in her hand.**”

Nobody thanked her and nobody argued with her and the man of about thirty-eight with a satchel said *that is true* out loud, and nobody thanked him for saying it and he did not ask.

Then they did the thing that is the only instrument this county has, and it was done in about nine minutes by two people who have done it before.

Nesta Braith ruled four heads on a leaf of foolscap about nine inches by six. Three of them were filled by the man of about fifty-eight with a pen and not with a knife. **The fourth one was ruled and left empty and no head was cut over it and no head was cut in the wood above it, because there is no wood in that room and because a clerk in about four years who finds a sentence under a head that was cut for something else has to decide on his own what it is about, and that is the correct condition for a sentence.**

The three that were filled say this: a place, in the words of the letter, and the words of the letter are not the words of the valley; a figure of people, and it is the figure off the leaf, which is not a figure of people and is a figure of one woman's walking; and a trade, which is grafting.

**And the question went under the fourth head and it is the same question that went under the fourth head of nine leaves in a room two hundred miles off in Thawmonth, in the same words, because a question that is written twice and is the same both times is one question and not two, and nobody has to sign it and nobody has to carry a knife.**

*What is this road called, and who lives at the end of it, and what is carried on it, and what is the name you use for it?*

Nobody read it back. Nobody thanked anybody.

Then at about the sixth hour he went out into the lane behind the room and she was already in it, because she had been in the lane for about an hour with her boots in her hand and the pot at her feet, and there was nobody else in that lane and nobody came into it and nobody has said since that anybody was in it.

**And he told her. He said it flat and quickly and he told her in a lane instead of in the room on purpose and he did not say why and the reason was in the room and the reason was nine people.**

“**It is not going to be given. And I am not going to sign it, and that part is easy, and the hard part is the reason. A road may only be certified by its own answer and that valley has not had one in twenty-two years, and it is going to have to be asked whether it wants one, and I have decided that today without asking you, and I decided it in nine minutes in that room and I want you to know how long it took.**”

“**You have told nine counties that my place cannot speak,**” she said. “**And you told me in a lane to keep it out of that room, and you were right to keep it out of that room, and both of those are true, and you did not ask me whether you would, and you have had nine days.**”

Nobody argued with her. He did not say that he was sorry, and the reason he did not is that apologising is naming the thing he should have done, and what he should have done was ask her at the gate on the Monday before the pot was even on the cart, and he had about nine days, and he cannot say that out loud without saying also that he had the days and used them, and he did not say either of those things.

“**What did they ask me? Nobody in this county has sent anybody to that valley and nobody is going to. Nobody in this county has asked me a single question and I have come nine days on a road with a foot of earth in it because I am the only one of the eleven who was asked anything, and I answered it, and I have not been thanked for it, and that is correct.**”

Then she sat down on the kerb and put her boots on and did not cry and was not looked at, and at about the seventh hour she said the other thing, and it is the last thing she says that day and she said it to the lane.

“**I am going back anyway. I would like a sheet, and I would like to write it myself, and I would like it to be one of the four you have just made, and I will carry it and I will not be sent. Carrying it is the one thing I know how to do and it is the one thing nobody has ever asked me to do, and I would like somebody to write down that I said that in a lane and not in a room.**”

Nobody thanked her. Nobody wrote it down on that day.

**And the cost of that Tuesday is a column and it is the man of thirty-two's, and it is the eleventh one he has kept, and a lane behind a room is not in it.**

**a night's straw at four, a bit of chalk at seven, a sheet of oiled paper at nine, a stone of tallow at eleven, a third pound of candles at thirteen, a second broom at sixteen, a ream of paper at nineteen, a case of quills at twenty-two, a pot of paste at twenty-nine, and a coil of tarred rope at thirty-nine.** Four, seven, nine, eleven, thirteen, sixteen, nineteen, twenty-two, twenty-nine and thirty-nine is one hundred and sixty-nine pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and sixty-nine pence is three marks and twenty-five over**, and nine days is a figure of time and is not a price and is not in this column, and nine minutes is a figure of time and is not in it, and twenty-two years is a figure of time and is not in it, and nine leaves in a room two hundred miles off are not in this column, and the ones that came back and the one that came back cut in half are three separate objects and not one figure and none of them is a figure of persons, and the two figures of about four hundred that this county keeps, which are the one that carried a sheet in Mudmonth and the one that lives at the end of the made lane, are the same number and are not the same number, and neither of them is in this column and neither of them has a price.

At about the ninth hour the woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key put the pot on a board by the door and a note on the board that said what was in it, and about nine people in that county now know that a woman from a valley is going home on a cart on the Saturday, and some of them know it because they were told and some of them do not, and neither of those is a figure of persons and neither is added to anything.
