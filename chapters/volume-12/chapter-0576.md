# Chapter 0576

**The first of Harvestmonth, YR 320, a Thursday and a market day, from the second hour to the seventh, a market town of about two thousand people, a weigh-house with a board outside it, a hired room over a saddler's, a woman of about thirty-four who keeps a key, and a man of about fifty-eight with a chain of office that is a badge who says one thing out loud on the first of a month and is not thanked for it.**

He said it in the street outside the weigh-house and not in the room, because he has said it in the street every first of a month since the room began and about nine people standing there hear it and nobody in that market town of about two thousand has ever come to a sitting for it.

“**The hearing stands. There is no bench in the room. Nobody is sentenced to anything by me or by anybody in that room, and there is a form on the side table with four heads on it and the fourth of them is ruled and empty and has been about six months, and I am the man who says that out loud on the first of every month and I do it because a thing that is true only when somebody says it is not true.**”

Nobody thanked him. A woman selling eggs asked him whether he wanted one and he said that he did not and she put the basket down again.

**And the room over a saddler's was open that morning and had been open since the second hour, and it is shut on Sundays and it was washed on a Sunday in Greenmonth by somebody, and nobody in nine counties knows who washed it and nobody was thanked, and he has said out loud more than once that he did not do it and does not know who did.**

Four people were in it: a woman of about thirty-eight from a river city who had come down on the cart and was going back up it and had asked to be in the room while it was open and had been let in; a copyist of about twenty-six; a woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key; and the man of about fifty-eight.

Nobody asked her why and she did not say.

**And the woman of about thirty-four who keeps the key was not in that room for one hour of that morning, and it was the first hour since the room began that she had not been in it, and this is the whole of what happened to her and it is the cost of that Thursday.**

She had been in every sitting since the room began and she had never once been outside it while it was doing anything, and the reason was that the door has a lock and the lock is a good one and the key is hers and the only way to open that door from the lane is with the key in your hand.

“**From the first of a month the room opens at the second hour on market mornings and shuts at the seventh,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, “**because there is now a thing in nine counties that wants this room open, and I am not going to pretend the nine counties can come on any morning they like, because a market town is where people are on market mornings. So the door will be open from the lane while there are people on the stair, and that means the key is in the lane, and that means the person who keeps the key is in the lane.**”

Nobody thanked him for saying it out loud before he did it.

“**You have told me about it before you have done it,**” the woman of about thirty-four said, “**and I want it noticed that you did, and I am not going to thank you for it, because thanking a man for telling you first is a way of making him feel better about doing it.**”

“**That is fair.**”

“**How long?**”

“**Four weeks. Then it goes back to what it was, if nobody has said anything that makes it wrong.**”

“**And if somebody says something that makes it wrong?**”

“**Then it is mine and not yours and I will not put it on you.**”

She stood in the lane with the key for about as long as it takes a woman to say four, and then she said the other thing.

“**I have kept that room since it was a room with a table in it and a boy came and swept it out on the first day, and I have been inside it for every hour of it. I am not saying I am not willing to stand in a lane. I am saying that a place in a room is a thing I had and I have not got, and nobody is wrong, and I would like it written down that it was a place in a room and not a punishment, because a punishment would mean somebody did it to me.**”

Nobody wrote it down. The copyist of about twenty-six wrote it on a bare leaf and ruled no head over it and read none of it back, and then she said the thing she had said once already and had been asked about once and had not repeated since.

“**I am not going to write the fourth head twice.**”

“**You have said that.**”

“**I am saying it again because a room that opens on market mornings is a room that gets asked the same thing nine times a week, and once is a copy and twice is a habit, and a habit in a room like this one is how a line gets filled in by somebody who has never once asked a road anything.**”

Nobody thanked her. Nobody argued with her.

The woman of about thirty-eight from a river city said one thing in that room and it was about a word and not about the room, and about four people in that town had heard her say it once in a market in Greenmonth and about nine had not, and the two are kept apart and neither of them is a figure of the people in the room.

Then the room did what a room does between the fourth hour and the seventh, which is nothing that anybody will afterwards be able to describe.

**And upstairs, where it had been since a day in a month carried at thirty, the half leaf lay face down on a table. There is a question written under its fourth head and nobody in nine counties has answered it, and nobody turned it over that morning, and nobody has been booked to turn it over, and nobody has said out loud how long it has been lying there, which is the one figure the thing itself does not give up. The Crown's form was on the side table with three heads filled and the fourth empty and about six months old and still empty. And the pot of earth that stood in this room for about nineteen days in Greenmonth is four days and about nine the other way up a lane in a hired room over a saddler's, watered by a woman who is not a keeper of anything, and it goes home with the woman who sent it.**

Nobody thanked anybody.

**And the cost of that Thursday is a column and it is the woman of about thirty-four's, and a place in a room is not in it and a key is not in it and neither of them has a price.**

**a key-file at three, a broom at eight, a skin of oil at twelve, a sheet of paper at sixteen, a pot of tar at nineteen, a bootlace at twenty-one, a night's straw at twenty-five, a pound of tallow at twenty-seven, a hank of yarn at twenty-nine, and a bundle of kindling at thirty.** Three, eight, twelve, sixteen, nineteen, twenty-one, twenty-five, twenty-seven, twenty-nine and thirty is one hundred and ninety pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and ninety pence is three marks and forty-six over**, and four weeks and six months and five months and nineteen days are four figures of time and none of them is a price, and the fourth head and the fifth line and the four heads of a return and the four divisions on a board in a valley four days and about nine off are four shapes and not one object and none of them is a price, and the about four hundred who carried a sheet out of a market in Mudmonth and the about four hundred at the end of a made lane about a mile and a half from this town are one number and are not one number and go in at neither end of this column.

Nobody thanked her, and she kept the key, and she stood in a lane from the second hour to the seventh with her hand on it and did not go in, and four people in that market town of about two thousand have put it about that she was let out of a room she had never left and nine have put it about that a room with its door open is still a room and that a woman in a lane has been given nothing, and the two sets do not overlap and she has not been asked which she agrees with, and Aren Kest came down that lane on his way out, slept one night under a bed he paid for, and walked out onto the coast road before the second hour, and nobody in that town knows and nobody in nine counties has been told that he is about nine miles along a made road with a stick and a bad leg and no money for a cart.
