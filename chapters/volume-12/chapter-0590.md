# Chapter 0590

**The head of the Long Set, the ninth hour of the last day of the pick sitting, a finding with three names on it lying on a stone, about four hundred paces of stone about two paces wide with a lip on the high side, a barrow standing on the low side, water coming down a channel that is not mended, and the turn of the five sittings with nothing tidied.**

The work stopped because the work stops, and nobody stopped it, and the difference between those two things is the whole of what happened in about five weeks in this valley and nobody in it will be able to say it more clearly than that.

What had been done is not a thing anybody can point at from the head of that slope. About four hundred paces of bed of four miles had been laid at the fourth hundred pace, from the top down, by four men and about nine others, and it is good work, and the man of about fifty-one said so out loud once and did not say it twice, and the man of about forty-four who holds the second hoe said it was the best course he had laid in about nineteen years and then went and did not talk about it. The top three hundred paces is wrong and is going to stay wrong until a year that nobody in this valley has arranged for. Between the three hundred and the four hundred there is about a hundred paces nobody has touched at all.

About nine yards of the lip at the fourth hundred pace is new stone on a bed that has gone, and the new stone is holding, and the line of chalk that a man cut across that lip in the set sitting is still on it under about four inches of new course and cannot be seen from the top any more, and nobody cut a second one, and the finding that three people wrote on a slate is on a stone at the head of that Long Set and has not been carried to anybody.

**The water is back where it was.** It came down the channel at about the seventh hour on the first morning and stood in the first line of trees by about the eighth, and it spread from the first terrace to the second in about a day and a half and to the third in three and to the fourth in five, and it went back off the four lowest terraces at about the fifth hour of a day after it had stood on the fourth of them for about a day, and the ninth was a morning as well as a day and that is on a slate in this valley and is not in a book. The four lowest terraces have their water, and the top of the Long Set has its water, and the channel between them is what it was before anybody on that slope touched it, except for about four hundred paces of it, which is better than it was and is not enough.

The woman of about thirty-one was on the second of the four lowest at about the fifth hour with about nine people and about four neighbours, and the water was going off her own ground at about the rate it goes off, and she said one thing about it which is the only thing anybody said that morning that was not about stone.

“**There it is,**” she said. “**I have got the water back on four terraces in a turn of the five and I have lost a fifth of the pick off the bottom two and about a third of what was drying on the middle, and I am going to be picking in about three weeks and I am going to have about two thirds of what I had, and I am going to have it in a year when the four hundred paces of new bed has been under a winter of this water, and it will be better then than it is now.**”

Nobody thanked her. Nobody said that was a good thing to have said, and the man who keeps the water, who had walked up from the fourth hundred pace for it and had not been asked to walk up from the fourth hundred pace for it, said that it was true and would not say whether it was good.

The four sets were asked on one morning at the ninth hour and two of them said yes and two of them said no, and the order of the asking was yes, then no, then yes, then no, and nobody was asked to change and nobody has changed. The money was found. The two sets that said yes found it and the two that said no did not, and the man of about thirty-one of the Standing put his own share in writing in his own hand and it was not a large figure and it was not nothing, and the woman of about thirty-eight, who keeps four blocks on that middle, gave one with his and has not said out loud what her part of it came to and has not been asked, and the share of the woman of about thirty-one who keeps the four lowest was the smallest of the four and the dearest of the four, and it has not been said out loud by anybody what it came to per household and she has not said it and has not been asked.

That money is gone. It bought about four hundred paces of bed, about nine hundred feet of barrow run, about nine hundred cloths off about nine hundred feet of line in one day, a month of bread for about nine men and a boy's penny on four points of a sitting, and about four days of a carter, and it did not buy four miles and it was never going to and the two sets that said no were not wrong about that and have not been asked whether they were right.

**Nobody has said thank you in this valley since the plank came out of the ground, and nothing in this paragraph is an exception.** About four people in the Long Set have put it about that the woman of about thirty-eight got somebody else to open a hole and then got herself told she was wrong by a man with a stick, and about nine have put it about that the woman of about thirty-one counted the water with a stick and about nine stones and put the number on a slate, and the two sets do not overlap and neither of them has been asked and neither of the two women has been asked and neither of them has asked.

The plank of green wood is on a stone at the head of that Long Set with its marks uppermost, and the hole under the oldest tree is filled in with its own loam and a flat stone on top of the loam and not on top of the plank, and about nine yards of grass has grown up round the edge of the flat stone, and the man of about fifty-one said out loud on the day it came out that it is not going back in the ground, and nobody in this valley has said where it ought to be kept, and the woman of about seventy came down at the ninth hour on the day it came out and stood at the edge of about nine people and said nothing at all, and nobody asked her why, and she has not said.

**And the answer is not made.** A woman of about thirty-eight asked to be it in a market town four days and about nine off and asked again in the open, and the second asking was about a hole in the ground and she was let, and being let is not the same as being told, and nobody has told her. The name this valley uses for a person who may speak for a road is a person and not an office, and the answer is made and not found, and a woman who holds a block of ground cannot be the answer for the ground, and she is the one who has said that out loud about herself, and it is the reason she cannot do it and it is on nobody's sheet.

**Nobody has filled a fourth head.** The Crown's form is on a side table in a room over a saddler's in a market town four days and about nine off, with three heads filled and the fourth ruled and empty, and it has been about six months, and the fourth head of a foolscap sheet is ruled and empty and has four lines written under it in a hand that is not an office's, and the fourth head of a returned form from a county two hundred miles off carries a woman's word and is not this, and the fifth line on a sheet in a market town was ruled on a Monday morning by nobody who ordered it and is a fifth line and is not a fourth head and is not a sixth, and the board off the middle terrace has four divisions on it and three filled and a division is not a head. They are five documents in five places and none of them is the Crown's form, and the plank is a sixth object and is not any of them, and the half leaf is face down upstairs in that room and nobody has turned it over and nobody has been booked to turn it over and nobody has said how long it has been there, which is the one figure the thing itself does not give up.

The turn of the five sittings came at about the ninth hour, which is when it comes, and the bar sitting begins, and the year is a turn of the five and no month is a sitting and no sitting is a month, and a season in this valley is not a season in a market town and the two are not converted anywhere on this slope.

**And the bar of wood is still lying in the grass at the foot of the Low Lane about nine terraces further down, and it was never lifted, and nobody has asked whether it should be, and a carter of about forty went out of this valley in the swell sitting with two sacks of pears and has not come back in, and the fruit of that day is on no road in nine counties, and the answer to the question about him was that the lane will do what it does.**

Nobody thanked anybody.

**The cost of a turn of the five is a column, and it is nobody's, because the column belongs to whoever paid for the day and the day was paid for out of two sets' money by about nine households and the column below is the woman's of the four lowest, and she did not pay for it, she was paid for it, and that is a different thing and it is not a better one.**

**a pail at two, a bucket at five, a mat at nine, a cake of tallow at twelve, a hand file at sixteen, a nail at twenty, a jar of oil at twenty-three, a sieve at twenty-seven, a whetstone at thirty-one, a night's straw at thirty-five, and a leather grip at thirty-nine.** Two, five, nine, twelve, sixteen, twenty, twenty-three, twenty-seven, thirty-one, thirty-five and thirty-nine is two hundred and nineteen pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and two hundred and nineteen pence is four marks and twenty-seven over**, and about four miles and about two paces wide and about four hundred paces done and three hundred paces wrong and about a hundred paces untouched and the fourth hundred pace and about nine yards of lip and about nine hundred feet of line and about nine hundred cloths and about two thirds of a pick and about four terraces and about eleven terraces and eleven households and about ninety people twenty-two winters ago are fourteen figures of distance and of count and not one of them is a price, and the fifth hour and the seventh hour and the ninth hour and nine days and a morning and a turn of the five and five sittings and three weeks and a year and about nine years and twenty-two years and about nineteen years are twelve figures of time and not one of them goes in at either end of this column. **The three names on a slate and two sets that said yes and two sets that said no and about nine households on four terraces and four neighbours and about nine people at the ninth hour on the day the plank came out and a carter of about forty and a man of about thirty-two with a limp are eight figures of persons and are not added to one another, and the penny for the bell was one penny for four points and is not in this column.** Nobody thanked anybody.
