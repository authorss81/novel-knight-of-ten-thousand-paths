# Chapter 0598

**The middle terrace at the third hour of the bar sitting, a delegate chosen without a vote, a written refusal of the right to be thanked, a document about ground where every argument was once put under a tree, and nobody angry.**

The middle terrace lay quiet at the third hour, and the settlement stood with its doors open, and the board off the middle terrace stood by the wall with four divisions ruled on it and three filled, and a division on a board in a valley with no office in it is a division and is not a head and is not a fifth line, and everybody who stood there knew it.

The woman of about forty-four who keeps that board stood beside it, and the man of about sixty of the Standing stood opposite, and the man of about thirty-one of the Standing stood between, and the woman of about sixty-four who ties about nine hundred picking cords stood with her hands empty, and about nine people of the settlement stood round, and nothing was voted, because nothing in this valley is voted.

“**Somebody has to go down,**” the man of about thirty-one said. “**The low roads are making a gathering and our ground is wanted at it, and a person going is better than a leaf going, because a leaf with nothing under a mark says yes to anything a reader brings to it.**”

“**I will go,**” the woman of about sixty-four said. “**I keep nothing and I am on no roll, and I can be refused by my own valley, and if I am refused I will carry it back.**”

Nobody asked for another name. Nobody wrote her name at the top of anything. The man of about sixty asked the three questions he always asks and answered none of them himself, and the woman of about forty-four ruled nothing new on the board, and the delegate stood made and not found.

Then the delegate did the first thing a delegate of that valley does, and she did it out loud, and she was believed, which is the resolution and it is not a victory.

“**Our right to refuse contact stays explicit,**” she said. “**Write it so. If the gathering will not have us on those terms, we do not come. And write this beside it, that I refuse to be thanked for coming, and that no thanks in that room is owed to this valley.**”

The woman of about forty-four wrote it on a bare leaf in her own hand, and the refusal to be thanked for coming went down in ink, and it was not softened, and nobody thanked the delegate, and nobody in that settlement said the valley should be glad to be asked.

On the wall behind them lay a document about a place where every argument was once put in the ground, written by four hands twenty-two winters gone, and it is in the room and it is not the Crown's form, and the five documents are in five places and none of them is the Crown's form, and the plank is a sixth object and is not any of them, and the slate with three names on it is a seventh and is a finding and not a document an office can hold, and the leaf the delegate carries is an eighth and is her own refusal and is not any of them.

The man of about sixty looked at the delegate for a long time, and he did not say whether going was right, and he would not say, and that refusal stood beside her insistence the way two true things stand in this valley without being added.

“**Go,**” he said at last. “**And come back refused or received, and either will do.**”

Nobody was angry. About four of the nine put it about that the valley had sent its oldest counter to do its youngest thing, and about five put it about that it had sent the one person nobody would miss at the lines, and the two sets did not overlap and she was not asked which of the two she thought she was, and the leaf dried in the sun with the ink dark on it, and the board stood by the wall with its divisions quiet, and the settlement went back to work with the sound of fruit being handled, and the delegate stood a while looking up the slope she had lived on all her days, and then went in to pack nothing, because she carries her slate and her hands and needs nothing else.

**What that hour on the terrace cost is a column, and it is the woman's who keeps the board, and a delegate unwaged and a refusal written down are not in it and neither of them has a price.**

**a leather strap at fifteen, a rushlight at four, a cart tire at thirty, a hank of twine at seven, a seed sieve at twenty-four, a cake of soap at ten, a scythe at twenty-seven, a jar of oil at thirteen, a nail at twenty-one, a whetstone at sixteen, and a hand file at nineteen.** Fifteen, four, thirty, seven, twenty-four, ten, twenty-seven, thirteen, twenty-one, sixteen and nineteen is one hundred and eighty-six pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and eighty-six pence is three marks and forty-two over**, and four divisions on a board and three filled and three questions and about nine hundred cords and four hands of writing and five documents and five places and a sixth object and a seventh and three names and an eighth are ten figures of distance and of count and not one of them is a price, and the third hour and the bar sitting and twenty-two winters are three figures of time and not one of them goes in at either end of the column. **A woman of about forty-four who keeps the board and a woman of about sixty-four who ties the cords and a man of about sixty and a man of about thirty-one of the Standing and about nine people of the settlement and about four of the nine and about five of the nine and two sets that did not overlap are eight figures of persons and are not added to one another.** Nobody thanked the delegate and nobody thanked anybody.
