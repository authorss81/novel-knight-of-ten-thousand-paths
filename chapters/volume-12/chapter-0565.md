# Chapter 0565

**A room off the middle terrace with a board on one wall and four stools, the ninth hour of the swell sitting, a leaf about nine inches by six with four ruled heads on it and four lines written under the fourth of them in a woman's hand, four people of the Standing and a fifth stool with nobody on it, and a man of thirty-two with a limp standing by the door.**

The four lines under the fourth head are these, and nobody in that valley has read them and the woman who wrote them read them to about four people at about the fourth hour and has read them since to nobody.

*Nobody in this valley has been asked anything for about twenty-two years. I am asking on my own account. The name I use for the person who may speak for a road here is a person and not an office. I have not been made one.*

**And that is not a name for a road and it is not a promise of peace and it is not a list. There is no name in it. It is a woman saying out loud that a place has not asked anybody anything for twenty-two years, and a road in nine counties has never answered instead of being described before this and this one has.**

The three heads above the fourth are filled and they were filled on a Saturday in a market town four days' walk off and they are in the words of this valley and not in the words of a letter, and the fourth of them is still ruled and still empty, and the four lines are under it and not in it, and a question under a head is not an answer to it and does not go in it.

Nobody filled a head. Nobody ruled a head. Nobody cut a line above the fourth one, and there is a woman of about seventy in this valley who said at the gate on the first day that this is a leaf with four divisions and not four heads, and a division and a head are not the same shape and are not the same object, and two of the people in this room were standing at that gate when she said it, and the two of them have said different things about what she meant since, and neither of them has been asked.

“**It is not an answer,**” the man of about sixty said, in the fourth hour, on the second day.

“**No,**” the woman of about thirty-eight said.

“**It is not an answer because the answer is made and not found. That is the whole of it and it is not a new thing and I am not inventing it to be unkind. There has not been an answer in this valley since before I was born and there was one when my father's father was a boy and it was made, in a room, by about nine people, out loud, and it took the better part of a sitting, and the woman who was made it did not choose it and was not asked whether she would.**”

Nobody thanked him and nobody argued with him and the woman of about thirty-eight did not sit down.

“**Then what is required of me,**” she said, “**so that the four lines under that head stand?**”

“**You have to be made,**” the man of about sixty said. “**And to be made you have to be put to us, and to be put to us somebody has to propose you, and I will tell you what the proposing has been since I have been a grown man in this room, which is that nobody has been proposed to this board in my lifetime and before me for about four years, and that the reason nobody is proposed is not that we do not want an answer, it is that an answer is a person and a person has to be free of the ground, and the ground of this valley is the only thing any of us have got.**”

“**Say that again and say it plainly,**” the woman of about thirty-eight said, “**and do not do it in the voice, because I can hear the voice and I have been hearing it for about nine years and I have never once been able to stop it coming out of me.**”

**And the man of about sixty said it plainly, and it is the worst sentence anybody said in that valley in twenty-two years, and it is true, and nobody has said since whether it is right.**

“**A woman who holds a block of ground cannot be the answer for the ground. That is the rule and it has been the rule for longer than anybody can walk back. If you are the answer then you do not hold anything here, and the reason for that is that a person who holds ground is a person who can be moved off it, and a person who can be moved off it will find that out before anybody else does, and that is how this valley got into the state it is in.**”

Nobody thanked him. The woman of about thirty-eight stood in the middle of that room for about as long as a man takes to say nine and then she said two sentences and they are the last thing said in that room in that sitting.

“**Then I have written four lines under a head I am not allowed to have written under, and the four lines are true, and the three heads above them are in my words, and I am going to leave the leaf on that board and I am not going to take it back and I am not going to unwrite it, and if the board is a room and the room is a rule then the rule has my four lines sitting on it and I would like somebody to move the board.**”

Nobody moved the board.

**And then a man of about thirty-one who sits at that board said the other half out loud, and he said it to the man of about sixty and not to the woman, and it is said out loud here before anybody in that room can be fair about it.**

“**You have just told her that the only thing standing between this valley and a person who can speak for a road is that she owns a piece of it. I would like somebody to explain to me why that is a rule and not a way of keeping this valley exactly as it is, and I have been in this room for about nineteen years and I have never once asked, and I am asking now and I am not asking her.**”

Nobody thanked him. The man of about sixty said *that is fair* and then said the rest of it, which is the sentence that a valley of this shape always says.

“**A person who has answered once can be told by this room that she has not. That has been available since before the putting-away and it has never once been used, and I am telling her it is available, and I am telling her I am not going to use it, and I want it written in whatever you write things in that I said that out loud before she had to find out.**”

Nothing was written. The woman of about forty-four who keeps the board wrote the single word **noted** in the margin of the leaf, in a different ink, and ruled nothing over it and read none of it back, and the fifth stool stayed empty for the rest of that sitting and for the sitting after.

**And a man of thirty-two with a limp stood by the door for the whole of it and did not say one word, and two people in that room have said, without being asked, that he should have said something, and one other has said that a man who has been in a room for an hour and a man who asked the question in a lane in a market town about four days' carting away are two different men and the second one was the one who was wrong, and the room did not vote.**

Nobody thanked anybody.

**And the cost of that sitting is a column and it is the man of thirty-two's, and a leaf with four heads on it is not in it and a single word in a margin is not in it and neither of them has a price.**

**a stick of chalk at three, a thimble at six, a cake of ink at nine, a sheet of foolscap at twelve, a case of quills at sixteen, a pot of paste at nineteen, a night's straw at twenty-three, a pound of tallow at twenty-seven, a ream of paper at thirty-one, and a sheet of vellum at thirty-seven.** Three, six, nine, twelve, sixteen, nineteen, twenty-three, twenty-seven, thirty-one and thirty-seven is one hundred and eighty-three pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and eighty-three pence is three marks and thirty-nine over**, and four heads and a fourth head ruled and empty are two things and a shape, and four lines under a head are four lines and are not a finding and are not a count, and twenty-two years and about ninety people and a fifth stool and four people who thought a rule was a way of keeping a place as it was are five figures of five different things and none of them is a price and none of them goes in at either end of this column, and a mark in a tree in another valley four days' walk off is not in this column and a leaf about nine inches by six is not a price and not a figure of persons.

The board stayed where it was with a leaf on it and the word **noted** in the margin, and the woman of about thirty-eight went out and did not take the leaf, and about nine people in that valley knew before the sitting was over and about four did not know until the fourth hour of the next day, and the two sets do not overlap and nobody asked which of them was in the room.
