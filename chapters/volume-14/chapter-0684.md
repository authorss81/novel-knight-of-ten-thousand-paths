# Chapter 0684

**Wolfmonth the twentieth, YR 321, a Thursday and a market morning, the second hour in the market street at the boards with about four hundred people in it and two hundred of them stopped, a man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract standing at the high end of the boards with about nine of his men behind him, and a sentence said out loud in a market street that nobody softens afterwards.**

The man of about fifty-two came up the market street on foot from the low end of the made road with about nine men behind him at a distance that had been agreed on the way, which is the way a man walks nine men who are not his family and who are being paid by the day.

Nobody thanked him for walking them and four people in that market street had worked out inside four seconds that about nine men who work a road had come into a market street, and five worked out nothing at all.

He had kept four miles of made road under contract for about nineteen years on the far side of this town, going out to a stone quarry and back, and it is a good four miles and it has nine men on it in Frostmonth and four men on it in the middle of the summer, and every one of the nine is named in a book that the county does not keep and that he keeps in a box in a hut at the quarry end.

Nobody thanked him for the box and four people in nine counties have said that a contractor's book of names is a head with a different name on it, and five have said that it is nine wages and no more than nine wages, and neither of those two sets has ever spoken to the other.

Then he said what he had come to say, and he said it in the open, and he said it once, and he did not say it again for the rest of that morning and for the rest of the month.

“**The second clause wants a witness. It says a witness is somebody named by the people who work the road, and I have read it and I have had it read to me, and I am not going to give it one. I have nine men on that road and four of them have been on it since the middle of Rainmonth and I am not going to stand in a market street and name one of them for a thing nobody has shown me is going to happen.**”

Nobody said anything and the whole of that market street had come to a stop where it stood.

“**I said no on a Friday and a man in a livery read it back to me and it is on a page in a book he carries in a coat, and that is one no. This is the second no and it is a different no and I want the two of them not put in the same place. The first one was me. This one is me on behalf of nine men, and I have asked none of them, because if I ask them then I am a man who asks, and I have been a man who asks for about nineteen years and I do not want to find out what I am when a thing is on a page.**”

Nobody thanked him and four people in that market street had heard the whole of the difference between the two noes and five had heard a contractor say that he did not want to find out what he was.

Then he said the other half of it, and it is the sentence, and it is nine counties' sentence and it is his, and nobody in that market street had heard it before that morning.

“**And I will say the rest of it, because I have had nineteen years and a book of nine names to think about and nine people in this street are about to say the wrong thing about it. The man with the badge put a rule in this county about four and a half years ago and the rule is about a road being shut. A shut road has to be proposed by somebody. A burden only falls on a man who proposed. That is a good rule and it is the best thing anybody in nine counties has ever made. And in four and a half years not one road has been shut, and the man who said a road might be shut has not shown that it may be shut, and I am not giving a man a name for a rule about a thing that has never happened to anybody.**”

Nobody thanked him and nobody said anything and two hundred people in that market street had heard every word of it, and nine of them had understood all of it, and five had not understood it at all.

Then the woman of about thirty-one who keeps the smith's house at the junction came down off the boards and asked him a question, and she had come four miles for it and she said so before anybody had asked her.

“**Then what happens to a man in a coat who asks me for a witness and I send him to you.**”

Nobody thanked him and the man of about fifty-two looked at her for about as long as it takes to turn a cart, and four people in that market street watched a contractor work out an answer to a question in his own trade and five watched a man be slow.

“**He writes down that you sent him to me,**” the man of about fifty-two said, “**and he reads it back to you, and he says that it is very good of you, and you will think about it for a fortnight and you will not get past it. That is what happens. I have watched it happen to two other people in nine counties and I am telling you now so that when it happens you will know that somebody told you.**”

Nobody thanked her and four people in that market street had heard a man describe a thing that is going to happen to a woman before it happened, and five had heard a man be kind in the middle of a refusal.

“**Can I write it down,**” she said, which was not the question anybody expected and was not a thing about four and a half years of this county has ever been asked at those boards.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about fifty-two said that she could write down whatever she liked and that it would make no difference, and she said out loud that she knew that, and four people in that market street heard a woman ask a question she already knew the answer to and five did not.

Nobody thanked him and four people at the boards had heard a man of fifty-two stand in a market street and refuse to name a witness, and five had heard a man say that the whole apparatus of nine counties rests on a thing that has not happened.

Then the man of about thirty-four with a satchel said the question that about nine people in that street had come to ask, and he asked it once.

“**Then what is the witness clause for.**”

Nobody thanked him and the man of about fifty-two did not answer that at all, and four people in that market street had expected him to answer it and five had not.

Then the badge-man asked the second question, and he asked it out loud in the open, and about two hundred people in that market street heard it.

“**Then which of the two is it. Is a clause that cannot be used the best thing this county has built in four and a half years, or the end of it?**”

Nobody thanked him and nobody answered him and about nine people in that market street stood still, and four of them had been waiting four months to hear one of those two said out loud by a person who is not a clerk, and about five had not.

Then the woman of about thirty-six who keeps a road in the county where a list of names was burned said the true thing about it, and she said it standing up, and she has not stood up at those boards since the first of Embermonth.

“**It is for the four and a half years in which nine counties got a name on a road by somebody standing in a lane in the rain and being wrong in public,**” she said. “**That is all it has ever been used for. It has been used about nine times in four and a half years and four of those nine were a man of about twenty-six naming a man of about seventeen, which is the whole trade, and every one of those four cost somebody a month of being spoken about in a market. And this morning a man of about fifty-two has said that he will not do it, and that is the second time a road has said no to the only clause that any of the seven of us have ever used.**”

Nobody thanked her and four people in that market street had heard a woman say what a clause is for and five had heard a woman say that a clause had stopped being used.

“**And I am not going to say whether that is the best thing that has happened in four and a half years or the end of the only thing any of us have built,**” she said, “**because a man of about thirty-four in a coat asked a question on a Thursday in Fallowmonth and nobody could answer it then either, and I have had a month to sit with not knowing and I have got used to it and I am not going to give it up to make a better afternoon.**”

Nobody thanked her and two hundred people in that market street had heard a woman refuse to say whether something was a good thing, and nine of them had noticed that the refusal was the same shape as the refusal of the man of about fifty-two and five had not.

Then the badge-man said the thing that about nine people in nine counties had been waiting about four months to hear him say in a market street, and he said it in nine words, and then he said the second half, which nobody had asked him for and which is the reason the first half is on the page.

“**Then it will be asked in this street and not in a room four hundred miles off.**”

Nobody thanked him and four people at the boards had heard a man of fifty-eight take a sentence that had been said once in a market street and five had heard nothing at all.

“**I am going to say that man's words again in this street, in the same words, because in about nine days a serjeant of a captain of about forty-four is going to arrive somewhere with a sheet and four men and a question, and nine people in nine counties are going to be asked which of the two they want: that the second clause is a good rule, or that the man with the badge said a road might be shut. I am not going to let anybody in nine counties be asked that in a room. I am going to have it said in a market street on a Thursday, twice, and both times in the words of a man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles under contract.**”

Then he said it again, out loud, in that market street, in front of about two hundred people, and he said it in the man's own words and he did not improve them and he did not put a clause in after it, and four people at the boards heard a man of fifty-eight read another man's sentence back to a market street and five heard a man repeat himself.

Nobody thanked him and nobody thanked the man of about fifty-two and nobody in that market street said one word that made the sentence smaller, and nine people have said since that it should not have been said twice and about five have said since that twice is what it took, and the sentence was not softened by anybody afterwards and it is not going to be.

Then the copyist of about twenty-six said one thing, and she said it in the flat way, and she said it to nobody in particular and about nine people at the boards heard it.

“**That is the first question anybody has asked a road at those boards in about four and a half years and there is nowhere in my room to put the answer, and I am not going to rule anything about it, and I said on Sunday that I would not rule a fifth head and I have not and I am not going to.**”

Nobody thanked her and about four people at the boards had heard a keeper of records say out loud that the room could not hold a thing that had just been decided, and about five had heard a woman be exact.

Then Aren Kest said four sentences at the low side of the boards and about nine people in that market street heard all four and about four of them understood what they were for, and nobody thanked him and he was not thanked for the first three of them at the time and has not been thanked for the fourth since.

“**I have got nine hundred words on a table in a room over a saddler's and one of the nine clauses is about a witness and the sixth clause is the one that is wrong, and I am not going to say anything about either of them this morning because a man of about fifty-two has just said the truer thing and he has said it in a market street, and if I had said it he would have been a man who had been asked for it and he would have had to answer me.**”

Nobody thanked him and about two hundred people in that market street had heard a man of thirty-two stand down and about five had heard a man admit that somebody else had said a thing better.

“**I want one thing said about the sixth clause and I am going to say it once. It is wrong and a woman of about thirty-four walked nine days to tell me and she went back up a made road without a copy of it. I am not going to change it. I have said that in this street and I am saying it again so that a man of about fifty-two who has just refused to name nine men can hear that the man who wrote nine hundred words asking for a name on each side of a thing has not changed his mind about needing one.**”

Nobody said anything to that and about two hundred people in that market street stopped what they were doing, and about four people at the boards looked at the ground and about five looked at the boards, and the woman of about thirty-six said nothing at all and was not argued with.

Then a man of about thirty-eight who puts boards on the inside of gates said the practical thing out loud, and it was not a hard thing, and about nine people in that market street had not thought of it.

“**Then the six clerks in this county have got a clause they cannot use, and there are about nine gates on a lane two days off where a man in a livery has been writing on boards since the middle of Rainmonth, and if any of the people on that lane is asked for a witness then not one of them is to be named, and that is a thing you can say in a morning before you have had your breakfast.**”

Nobody thanked him and about four people in that market street had heard a man turn a refusal into an instruction that a person could carry and about five had heard a man make it small, and about four people have said since that it was the only useful thing anybody said on that Thursday and about five have said since that it was the whole of the problem in one sentence.

Nobody said anything and the market went on at about the fourth hour, and two hundred people went back to what they were doing, and nine people at the boards stayed.

The rest of that market street was doing what it does on a Thursday and nobody had stopped for more than about four minutes at a time. A carter of about forty-one was at the low end of the boards with a horse he could not get a load for and had been standing there since the second hour, and the copyist of about twenty-six counted his horse at the boards in front of nine people at about the third hour, which is a thing she does about four times a month and has done since the middle of Harvestmonth, and the carter put nothing in and was not argued with. A woman of about thirty-three who sells wool two streets off had come in late and had heard about four minutes of it and got the rest from a man of about twenty-six who told it wrong, and about nine people in that street have said since that the man of about twenty-six told it the way he wanted it and about five have said he told it the way it was, and nobody has asked him.

Nobody thanked anybody and about four people in that market street knew that a carter had been counted in front of nine people and about five did not, and the copyist said out loud, in the flat way, that a horse that is counted is a horse and not a wage, and that a wage is a different book and there is no series in nine counties for wages.

What happened at about the fourth hour was that nine men walked out of a market street.

They came down off the boards in a line about four paces apart, the way they had come up, and they went out of the market street at the low end and up the lane toward the made road, and about two hundred people in that market street watched nine men who work a road walk out of it, and about nine of those two hundred could have told you afterwards exactly what had happened and about five could not.

Nobody thanked them and the man of about fifty-two went out last and stopped at the low end of the boards on his way and said one sentence out loud to about nine people, and he said it to the boards and not to the badge-man and not to the man with the stick.

“**I will be at the quarry end at the fourth hour tomorrow and I will be on that road on Monday and the nine of us will be on it on Monday, and that is not a thing for anybody in this street to write down.**”

Nobody thanked him and about four people at the boards had heard a man say that his road was not a thing to be written down, and about five had heard nine men go back to work on a Thursday because it was Thursday.

**And the cost of that Thursday is one clause out of about nine hands that nine people in this county have used nine times in four and a half years and cannot use again on the road that asked, and about nine men who walked out of a market street at the fourth hour, are not in it and none of them has a price.**

**a stub of chalk at 4, a wicket at 21, a pound of nails at 24, a barrow wheel at 27, a bushel of lime at 29, a hurdle at 30, a gate socket at 34, a hanglock at 35, a shovel at 36, and a mattock at 37.** Four, twenty-one, twenty-four, twenty-seven, twenty-nine, thirty, thirty-four, thirty-five, thirty-six and thirty-seven is two hundred and seventy-seven pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and two hundred and seventy-seven pence is five marks and thirty-seven over.** The badge-man did not go out to the low end of the boards to shake the man's hand and about four people in that market street noticed that he did not and about five did not, and he said afterwards, in a lane, to a man of about thirty-eight who put boards on gates, that he had wanted to and that there was nothing in nine counties that would have made it a thing he could do, and the man of about thirty-eight said that was the correct arrangement and went home.
