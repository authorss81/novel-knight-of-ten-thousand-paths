# Chapter 0696

**Hearthmonth the twenty-second, YR 321, a Wednesday and not a market morning, about nine people in a market street with no market in it and the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door, four days after a bridge went down on a made road four miles off, and a man of about twenty-eight who has come down four days of made road on his own account.**

A Wednesday is not a market morning, and the boards in that market street stood in an empty street for the fourth day of a bridge having gone down, and about nine people came to them, which is four more than come on a Wednesday and fewer than came on the Saturday after the captain was there.

Nobody thanked anybody and about four people in that market street had come on that Wednesday to ask about the bridge and about five had come to ask whether the eleven persons were going to be given up on a Friday, and about four people in nine counties have said that the two questions are one question and about five have said that they are not and that nobody in nine counties is going to settle it.

Nobody thanked anybody and the made road out of that market town was carrying almost nothing on that Wednesday, and about four people in nine counties have said that the fourth day of a bridge going down is the day the road empties, because a road empties the day after a thing happens on it and not the day, and about five have said that a road that empties on the fourth day was always going to empty and that the bridge is not why.

Nobody thanked anybody and the deck at that crossing can be seen from the made road at about nine hundred yards, because the lane comes down off the high side and the low end of the deck is on the low side of the bank, and a man standing on the made road with his back to the crossing can see nine planks lying at an angle and a length of oak standing in the water, and about nine people in nine counties know exactly what those two shapes are and about five cannot tell them from a cart that has come off a bank, and that is the whole of what anybody outside nine counties is going to get out of the coast of returning roads, and there is nobody up there to ask.

Nobody thanked anybody and the fourth day of a thing is not a day anybody has anything to do on, and the weigh-house weighed two loads of lime and nothing else, and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that this was the quietest week he had had since the middle of Longlight and that he had not been asked for a figure out of any of it, and nobody thanked him.

Then the man of about forty-one who keeps a cart came down the made road on the Monday, four days with a load of nothing coming up and a load of boards coming down, and he had been up past the crossing and had seen the deck lying at the low side of it, and on the Wednesday morning he stood in that market street at about the ninth hour and said out loud, to about nine people, that everybody on the coast of returning roads already knew who took a bridge down, and that he did not say a name because he did not have to, and that everybody up that road carries for a living and always have and there are nine of them and a man of thirty-two would not get a bridge down on a crossing where nobody in nine counties can name a road.

Nobody thanked him and about two hundred people had not been in that market street and four of the nine who were had heard the whole of what he said and five had heard a man say everybody knows.

Then a man of about twenty-eight came down the lane at about the tenth hour with a pack on his back and a stick in his hand, and about nine people in that market street knew his name, which is Tovan Kest, and four of them had last seen him on a Thursday in Frostmonth standing at the top of that made road with a barrow he was not going to be paid for, and five had not seen him since the middle of Longlight.

Nobody thanked him and he is a carrier, and a carrier on that road walks a route between path communities and market towns with goods and comes back with goods, and about nine people in nine counties have said that about nine carriers walk that road and that none of them has ever been asked for anything by anybody in a coat, and that four of the nine have been asked for a witness and have said no and that nobody in nine counties knows that but the four.

Nobody thanked him and he was not thanked for the four days and nobody asked him what he had come for, and he went to the low end of the boards and stood there, and about four people in that market street had recognised him and about five had not.

Then the man of about forty-one who keeps the cart said it to him directly, in the open, in front of about nine people, and nobody stopped him.

“**Then it is you and your eight and everybody that walks up that coast, and you can say so here, and I will write it down.**”

And the man of about twenty-eight said no out loud, in the open, in front of about nine people, and nobody thanked him and nobody argued with him, and he said it once.

“**No.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a carrier refuse a thing offered to him in the open and about five had heard a man say one word.

“**And I am going to say why in my trade and not in anything else, and you have asked me in front of nine people so I will say it in front of nine people,**” he said. “**A path community is nine houses and a set of crossings and a road that is not made. It has no head. It has never elected anything, it has never named anybody, it has never kept a paper, and there is nobody in it that a man can give an order to, because there is nobody in it to give an order to. I have walked that coast since I was about nine. There are about nine of us who carry and there are about nine hundred people in nine counties who know what we eat and none of them knows where we slept on Tuesday.**”

Nobody thanked him and about four people in that market street had heard a description of a path community in the trade of a man who walks one, and about five had heard a man talk about himself in the third person.

“**And I am going to tell you the thing that makes it not an army and not a thing you can put a bridge down. If we were an army then somebody could tell us where to be on the eighteenth and we would be there on the eighteenth. We are not told where to be. We are told by the people at the ends of the roads what is wanted there, and what is wanted at the coast on Tuesday is what is wanted at the coast on Tuesday. Nobody up that road had a reason to be at your crossing at the second hour on a Saturday, and if you had asked us on the Thursday we would have said no, and we would have said it up the lane from the other end of it, and you would have heard us say it four days later than you did.**”

Nobody thanked him and four people in that market street had heard a carrier say that his own trade has no way to reach a crossing on a Saturday, and five had heard a man give nine people a reason.

Then the man of about forty-one who keeps the cart tried the second thing, and he tried it in the open, and nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him.

“**Then it is the man of thirty-two and he is up that road every day of his life and there is no other explanation and I will write that down too.**”

Then the man of about twenty-eight refused that one out loud in the open in front of about nine people, and it took him about twice as long as the first one did, and nobody thanked him and nobody argued with him and about nine people in that market street heard both refusals and have never since been able to separate them in their own minds.

“**No, and I will not let that be said either, and the reason is dated and it is not mine.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a man refuse twice in about nine minutes and five had heard a man refuse once.

Then he put his hand inside his coat and took out two leaves of paper, and both of them were written on one side and folded once, and he did not unfold the first one and he read from it out loud in that street in the open, and nobody thanked him.

“**On the eleventh of Hearthmonth, at a place called Nine Elms on that coast, nine houses have written this and two of them have signed it and the other seven have put a thumb on it. It says that the houses on that road have not asked anybody to shut a road and will not shut one, that no house on that road has been asked by any man in a coat to do a thing at a crossing, and that if a thing is done at a crossing on that coast by a person from that coast then the houses will say so in writing before the fourth day and not after it.**”

Nobody thanked him and about four people in that market street had heard a thing written eleven days before a bridge went down about a bridge going down, and about five had heard a man read out a date.

“**That is the eleventh. The bridge went on the eighteenth. I have it because I carry paper up that road twice a month and I was paid nine pence for it in Fallowmonth and I have been carrying it since the eleventh and I am the only person in nine counties who has it.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had understood the whole of why a man reads a date out loud before he reads anything else, and about five had heard a man be careful.

“**And I am going to say the hard half of it, because you have offered me two things to refuse and I have refused both and neither of them was the hard one. The hard one is that if I stand here and say that man did it and no man in nine counties can say he had the right, then I am making a finding, and a finding is a thing with a head on it, and nine of my own houses wrote on the eleventh that the roads on that coast shut nothing, and if I put a finding on a thing about a bridge then nine houses have shut something in a matter of nine days and every one of them will be told about it by somebody.**”

Nobody thanked him and about four people in that market street had heard the whole of why a carrier would not name the man who took a bridge down, and about five had heard a man talk about nine houses.

“**So I am not going to say who did it. I am going to read what nine houses wrote on the eleventh, and I am going to put the date beside it, and anybody in this street who wants to know about the crossing can go and stand at it and look at the deck, and a deck is a thing that anybody can look at and a man is not.**”

Nobody said anything and nobody thanked him and about nine people in that market street had heard a man refuse to name a man in the open on purpose, and about four of them had understood that a refusal to name a man is a thing you do for the man as well as against him, and about five had understood only that a carrier did not want his own houses in trouble.

Then the man of about forty-one who keeps the cart said one thing and it was not an argument, and nobody answered it.

“**Then I am going to write that nobody told me, and that is going to be in my book on Friday, and it is going to say your name and the date and nothing else.**”

“**Write that,**” the man of about twenty-eight said. “**Write that in front of nine people and put the time on it, and it is the only line in it that is worth one.**”

Nobody thanked him and nobody thanked the carter and the man of about forty-one wrote it down and read it back and nine people heard it read, and about four people in nine counties have said since that it is the only page written in nine counties in about four and a half years that says a thing that happened and does not say whose fault it was, and about five have said that it is a page about a cart.

Aren Kest was at the high end of those boards for the whole of it with his stick in his right hand and did not speak once, and nobody thanked him and nobody looked at him, and about four people in that market street had worked out that the man who had read out a date on the eleventh was reading it for somebody and about five had not worked out anything about it at all.

Then a person in that market street asked the man of about twenty-eight a question, and it was asked out loud and in the open and nobody stopped her and about nine people heard it.

“**Then what is the second one?**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a woman ask about a second leaf and about five had not known that there was one, because it had gone back inside a coat at the same moment as the first one had, and nobody had seen it come out.

“**It is about a different thing, it is dated the ninth, and it is not about a bridge, and I am not going to read it in this street,**” the man of about twenty-eight said. “**And I am going to tell you why I am not going to read it, because you have asked me in front of nine people twice and I am not going to stand here with a paper in my hand and be asked a third time. That one is not mine to read. It came to the house it came from on the ninth and I carry it and it is not addressed to me and it is not addressed to anybody, and when the people it is addressed to decide that it should be read then it will be read, and not one day before, and not in this street on a Wednesday because there are nine people standing in it.**”

Nobody thanked him and about four people in that market street had heard a carrier refuse to read a paper in public and say that it was not his to read, and about five had heard a man be careful with a thing he was carrying.

Then the man of about forty-one who keeps the cart said that a man who will not read a paper has usually read it, and the man of about twenty-eight said that that was true and that it was the reason, and nobody thanked either of them and about nine people in nine counties have said since that the only time in about four and a half years that a person in this county has said out loud that they had read something and did not want anybody to know is the twenty-second of Hearthmonth in a market street with no market in it, and that nobody has ever asked him about it again.

Nobody thanked anybody and nine people in that market street had noticed, in nine seconds flat, that the man of about twenty-eight had been four days up a made road and could not have been at the crossing on the second hour of the eighteenth, and had said what was under the deck and how long it took and that a prop of oak about four inches through was still standing in the water at the low side, and nobody in that market street asked him how he knew, and about four people have said since that the not asking was the loudest thing that happened in that street on the twenty-second of Hearthmonth and about five have said that a carrier walks a road and knows what is on it, which is also true, and that neither of those two sets has ever spoken to the other.

Then the market street did the ordinary half of a Wednesday with no market in it, which is that about nine people went home in about nine minutes and four of them went up the lane to their dinners and five went down it, and the boards stood in it with nothing on them.

Nobody thanked anybody and four people in that market street went on talking about a date and five went on talking about nine houses, and four people in nine counties have said since that the whole of what a date in a market street does is make a thing unarguable without making it true, and five have said that the whole of what nine houses does is make a thing countable without making it right, and that a man of about forty-one wrote one of them down on a Wednesday and that is the only document either of them has ever had.

Nobody thanked anybody and the two leaves went back inside the coat, and the room over the weigh-house stayed shut behind its own door, and the second leaf was not read out in that street and has not been read out anywhere, and about nine people in nine counties know that there are two of them and about five know where the second one came from.

**And the cost of that Wednesday is a man of about twenty-eight who has come four days on foot to say a date out loud, a carter's book with one line in it that says nobody told me, and two leaves of paper in a coat where only one of them has been read in a market street, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
