# Chapter 0669

**Fallowmonth the fifth, YR 321, a Saturday and a market morning, the second hour at the boards in a market street with about four hundred people in it and the room over the weigh-house open behind it, a woman of about forty-four who keeps a crossing four hundred miles off coming through the market town on her way back and going straight back again, and a thing said in a market street that nine counties cannot refuse and cannot return.**

She came in at about the second hour with a bag and about nine days on her and the road on her, and nobody in that market street knew her, and four hundred people watched a person come through a market on a Saturday the way they watch anybody who has been on a road.

She was about forty-four and she had been working on a boat on the day before yesterday and had not been in a town of two thousand people for six days, and she said so at the boards before anybody asked her anything, in nine words, the way a person says a thing that establishes what they are and does not want it used against them.

Then the copyist of about twenty-six asked her, in the flat way, whether she was on the market town's business.

“**I am on my own business and it is about nine days each way and I am going back this afternoon,**” the woman said. “**And I have come through here because there is a made road and there is a market and there is a man with a badge who stands at boards, and I have been told about him by a person who has been here, and I am not going to pretend I have not come on purpose.**”

“**Say what you have come for,**” the badge-man said, and he said it in about nine words and then he stood back about nine paces, which he does about four times a year.

The woman of about forty-four said the thing that a market street of about four hundred people heard on the fifth of Fallowmonth, and it was about a minute and a half long, and she said it without stopping, and she said it in the way of a person who has had nine days to get the order of it right.

“**We have your sheet. It came on the ordinary road with the ordinary post and there were two of them and a covering letter about a drain. Nobody in our council has been asked anything and nobody in our council has instructed anybody and I am not a council, I keep a crossing and I have said so in four places this morning.**”

“**Then say the rest,**” the badge-man said.

“**On the second day after the sheet came, our council met, and it did not take nine hours, it took nine minutes, and it did one thing. It resolved that if anybody in nine counties asks a city of ours which place or road a paper of the Crown is meant, this city will answer, and will name the place, and will not refuse to name it.**”

About four hundred people in that market street stopped what about two hundred of them were doing, and four people at the boards understood the whole of what had been said and five understood half of it.

“**Read that back,**” the copyist said, in the flat way.

The woman of about forty-four read it back, and she read it exactly as she had said it, and about four hundred people heard a thing said twice.

Then she said why, and the why is the third thing, and it is worse for this county than an army would have been.

“**Nine counties has a rule about roads being shut,**” she said, “**and four people in this market know what it is and about two hundred have not been told. It is a good rule. I have read what your woman read out loud in a market street on the twelfth of Longlight and I have read it since, because our crossing keeps its count in crossings open and weeks shut and it is the same count, and I am not going to tell you that your rule is a small thing because it is not.**”

Nobody said anything and four people at the boards had understood half of that and five had not.

“**So here is the part you will not like,**” the woman of about forty-four said. “**A city that was never severed has just decided that it will speak about nine counties in a room that nine counties cannot get into, and that it will name a place, and that nobody asked it to and that it did not ask. We are not doing it for you. We are doing it because your paper says that any person asked which place or road is meant shall answer, and we are the only people in the world who can answer it, and we would rather say the name ourselves than have it taken out of us in a room.**”

Then she stopped, and about four hundred people in a market street on a Saturday morning stood in the rain and worked it out, and about four of them worked it out all the way to the end and about five worked it out halfway.

“**And I am going to say the last of it, and I have said it to four people on the road and I am saying it here because a man with a badge said out loud in a market street on the twentieth of Longlight that if anybody ever wanted to know whether we would help them, they should come and stand at a crossing and ask, and be prepared to be told no, and not write the no down.**”

“**I have read that,**” she said. “**And our council read it, and it did one thing about it, and the one thing was this. We are not going to be asked again this year, and we are not going to come, and we are not going to be moved. And we are not going to be quiet.**”

Nobody thanked her.

A man of about twenty-nine in the crowd at about nine paces said, out loud, whether the city had thought about what would happen if it named a place.

“**Yes,**” the woman of about forty-four said, without turning round, and about four hundred people in that street heard a woman answer a question she had not been looking at. “**We thought about it for nine days. If we name a place then a man with a book writes down a place, and a place in a book is a place a column can be sent to, and we have not asked your county a single question and we are not going to start now by asking them where the works stand. If we do not name a place then somebody else names it, in a room, from a map made nine years ago, and there is a mark at a place called the ninth milestone and a mark at a place called the ferry stone on it, and between them four miles, and seven other marks that are not roads at all.**”

“**How do you know about the map?**” the copyist asked.

“**Because a captain of about forty-four read a clause of it out loud at a board in the rain in a town two days off on the nineteenth of Rainmonth, in front of nine people, and it went round the coast on the ordinary road, and four people in this market have heard it and nine have not,**” the woman said, and the badge-man said out loud that she was right and that he had been in that street and had not written it down, and that about four people understood that the two of them were saying that a thing said at a board can be carried four hundred miles on an ordinary post.

“**So you have read the sheet and you have heard the captain and you have not read anything else,**” the badge-man said. “**Is there anything else?**”

“**There is a woman in your county who read a paper out loud in a market street on the twentieth of Longlight and said we would not come, and we have had that read to us four times on the road by people who were not in the street,**” the woman of about forty-four said, “**and I have not got a copy and I have not asked for one, and I would like to say that in about four hundred yards of this market street there is a copy and that nobody has sent it to anybody and that I have not been told who has got it.**”

Nobody thanked her and the copyist of about twenty-six said, at about nine paces, that she had it, and that it was a quarter of it, and that there was a captain's copy with nine pages and a seal on the fold in a coat two days off, and that nobody in nine counties had been given the whole of anything.

Then the badge-man said the true thing about it, and it took him four seconds, and four hundred people heard it and nine people at the boards understood all of it.

“**Then a city has offered nine counties something that nine counties cannot refuse,**” he said, “**and I have been standing in this street for about a year building a rule about a thing being done to a road, and four and a half years before that I was building a rule about a man naming a person, and in a month of it I have been handed a thing that is neither. It is not a thing being done to a road. It is a thing being said about a road, by somebody four hundred miles off, in a room none of you can get into, before anybody here has been asked anything, and I have not got a clause about that, and I have not got a clause about an army either, and I would like about nine people in this market to notice that this is the first thing anybody has brought to this county that cannot be said no to, and that it was not brought by a Crown officer and it was not brought by a man in a livery, and that it came in on the ordinary post with a covering letter about a drain.**”

Nobody said anything and four hundred people in that market street understood that a thing had arrived that they could not refuse, and nine people at the boards understood that the man with the badge had not said what to do about it.

“**So what is it, then?**” a man of about thirty-three said at the high end of the boards. “**Is it an army?**”

“**No,**” the woman of about forty-four said. “**And it is not us saying we will not be one, which is what your woman said in this street on the twenty-fifth of Longlight and which I have read, and both of those are things a person can say out loud in a market and be held to. This is a room. If a thing is said in a room about a county, then that county is a thing that is being talked about, and about nine people here would like it very much to be a thing that is being talked to somebody, and I have come nine days to say that it is not going to be, and that I have not got a say in that and neither have you, and I am sorry for it and I am not going to soften it because I have got a crossing to be at on Friday.**”

Nobody thanked her.

Then the copyist of about twenty-six said the thing about the room, and she said it to about four hundred people, and about nine of them heard the whole of it.

“**I keep a book with nine questions in it and no finding under any of them,**” she said, “**and I have said out loud in this street that a thing roads write to is a head at the top of a thing, and a man with a badge has a badge and no head and will not make one, and between the two of us there is a room with about eleven inches of drawer in it. And a city four hundred miles off has just resolved to name a place in a room. And a name in a room is a head at the top of a thing, and I have said that sentence in this street four times, and it has taken me four and a half years to learn, and I would like nine people to notice that I have learned it and that it is the first thing in about a month that I could have used.**”

“**Then unlearn it,**” the badge-man said, in the voice he uses. “**I have had four and a half years and a badge and a street and I have not unlearned it, and a woman of about forty-four has come nine days to tell us about a room, and I am going to say the only thing I have got, and it is that nine counties cannot refuse what a city says about it, and that a county has never in nine counties refused anything that anybody had actually asked it, and that in about a year the whole of this county will be able to remember the first morning it was asked something it had not been asked before.**”

Nobody said anything to that and four people at the boards looked at the boards and five looked at the door the woman of about forty-four had come in at.

Then somebody in the market said the sentence that the second half of that autumn is going to be about, and nobody has been able to establish since who said it, and four people have given four different trades for it, and nine people in nine counties still agree on the words.

“**Then the question is whether to ask them.**”

Nobody thanked anybody and nobody answered it and four hundred people in a market street on the fifth of Fallowmonth went home with it.

Aren Kest was four miles down the same lane at that hour, at the low end of a woman's four miles, with a bar in his hand and four other men and about nine feet of a thing that was not a ford and was not a crossing and was a length of approach he had been asked to look at by a man of about fifty-one who had come to find out whether the water was still going over the top of the cut.

The man of about thirty-four with a sheet in his coat came down the lane at about the sixth hour on foot and got him, and he was out of breath and he did not apologise for it and he said the thing in about nine sentences without a figure in it.

“**A woman came through the market at the second hour from a city four hundred miles off and said out loud at the boards that her city has resolved to name a place in a room, and that nobody asked it to, and that it is not going to be quiet, and the badge-man said that nine counties cannot refuse a thing a city says about it, and about four hundred people heard it, and it is the fourth thing said in this market about this paper and the first one not said by anybody who works for the Crown.**”

“**You have come down here to tell me that,**” Aren Kest said.

“**I have come down here to tell you that,**” the man of about thirty-four said, “**and I want you to hear me say that I did not send for you and would not have, and that is not a complaint and it is a fact about the shape of this county, and four people in this market noticed me not sending and five did not, and I would rather you had that from me now than worked it out in a fortnight.**”

Nobody said anything and four men in that lane looked at the ground and five looked at the water going over the top of the cut at the low side.

“**You are about five hours late,**” Aren Kest said. “**She is going back this afternoon and the crossing is nine days off and I can be at that crossing in about nine days on a stick, so I am not late for the crossing. I am late for the market. Whatever the four hundred people in that street are going to think about it for the next four months, I am not in the room where they think it, and there is no arrangement in nine counties that would have put me in that room, and I have been the man a thing arrives for for four years and this is the first thing I have wanted to arrive for and I cannot get there.**”

“**No,**” the man of about thirty-four said. “**You cannot, and the reason you cannot is the same reason I did not send, and it is worth about nine words and you can have them for nothing. There is no head. And the woman who came through said the second thing that matters and I have not said it yet.**”

“**Say it.**”

“**That they have said we will not be asked again this year,**” the man of about thirty-four said, “**and that in about nine days a woman who keeps a crossing four hundred miles off is going to be asked by somebody, and that it will not be the Crown, because the Crown asked and got a no. And that the only people in nine counties who can get there are four, and one of them is a man of about fifty-eight with a badge and no head who said out loud in a market street that he will not ask anybody anything for a month, and that as far as I can tell you are the four.**”

**And the cost of that Saturday is a column and it is nobody's, and a resolution passed in nine minutes in a room nine counties cannot get into and four people who could get to a crossing in nine days on a stick are not in it and none of them has a price.**

**a saddle at 27, a bridle at 28, a second chain at 29, a whetstone at 30, a sheet of canvas at 31, a thumb stall at 32, a pound of cheese at 34, a firelock at 35, a quire of paper at 37, and a box of inks at 39.** Twenty-seven, twenty-eight, twenty-nine, thirty, thirty-one, thirty-two, thirty-four, thirty-five, thirty-seven and thirty-nine is three hundred and twenty-two pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and three hundred and twenty-two pence is six marks and thirty-four over.** The woman of about forty-four went back up the made road at about the fourth hour with her bag and about nine days on her the other way, and the boy of about nineteen who runs carts on that lane saw her go and counted her out of the market and into the lane, and said afterwards that she had not looked at the boards once on her way through and had looked at the gate twice. Nobody asked him why he had looked at a gate. He said he did not know.

Then the man of about thirty-four went up the lane to the boards and stood at the high end of them for about an hour with his sheet in his coat and did not take it out, and about four hundred people went home out of a market street with a question in it that nobody in nine counties had standing to answer, and nine people in nine counties already knew what the question was and had been asked nothing about it, and four hundred yards up a made road a yard was loading a sill and nobody in that yard had heard any of it.
