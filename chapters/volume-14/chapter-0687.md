# Chapter 0687

**Frostmonth the sixth, YR 321, a Saturday and a market morning, the second hour in the market street at the boards with about four hundred people in it, a man of about fifty-one who owns a field on the low side of a cut four miles off walking into a market street on purpose, and a thing that nine counties has and has not used being named out loud by a person who is not anybody's clerk.**

The man of about fifty-one came in at the market gate at about the eighth hour and went down the street at the pace of a man who has decided to be in a place, and he got to the boards at about the second hour and nine people in that market street had seen him coming before he got there and five had not.

He has a want that has nothing to do with any of this, which is that he would like somebody to know what a barrow drain costs, and he asked in Fallowmonth for it to be said in a market street and not in a lane, and a woman of about thirty-eight with a bar told him he would not be thanked for it and would not be argued with, and four people in nine counties have said that was correct and five have said it was unkind and nobody has ever said which of those two is right.

Nobody thanked him and nobody argued with him and he was not thanked for the four miles and he did not ask to be.

He said the thing that about nine people in that market street had not known the county had, and he said it without any preamble, and he said it once, and four people understood it and five did not.

“**Nine counties has got a thing it has not used,**” the man of about fifty-one said. “**It is not a figure and it is not a body of men and there is not a name at the top of it, and I am not going to say it twice and I am not going to be asked to say it twice, and I have walked four miles to say it once.**”

Nobody said anything and about two hundred people in that market street had stopped what they were doing.

“**About nine people in nine counties will get up and go to a gate when a man with a book asks them a question, and will say no in the open, in daylight, where anybody going past can hear it, and then will write down where they were and what they said and the day, and leave the paper where a person can find it. That is the whole of what this county has made in four and a half years. Not the hearing. Not the three clauses. The nine.**”

Nobody thanked him and four people in that market street had heard a man say that the county's real product is nine people and five had heard a farmer list a county's assets.

Then he said the part that made about nine people in nine counties put down whatever they were holding.

“**Nobody has ever asked them to do it as a body. Nine of them, on one morning, at nine gates, because they were asked. And there has never been an ask, and there is not going to be an ask, and I want nine people in this street to work out for themselves why there is not going to be an ask.**”

Nobody said anything and two hundred people in that market street were standing still, and the man of about fifty-one waited at the boards for about as long as it takes to turn a cart, and nobody filled it.

“**Because the asking is a head,**” he said. “**A man with a badge who says come to the gate on Tuesday is a head. A room that says it is a head. A book that says it is a head. Nine counties has spent four and a half years making itself a thing that cannot be got at and this morning I am telling four hundred people the reason, and the reason is that the one thing this county could do if it were asked to do it is the one thing it has arranged for about four and a half years that nobody can do.**”

Nobody thanked him and four people in that market street had heard a man explain nine counties' whole shape in about nine minutes of standing still, and five had heard a man say that a thing cannot be asked to do the one thing it can do.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about fifty-one stood at the boards and let about nine people look at him, and he said out loud who the nine were, because he had thought about it for a month and because a thing nobody can name in a market street is a thing nobody can argue with in a market street, and he wanted it argued with.

“**There is a woman of about thirty-nine at a gate two days off who has said no once. There is a man of about thirty-eight who puts boards on the inside of gates and who wrote down one answer and did not write it in his book. There is a woman of about thirty-six who keeps a road in a county where a list of names was burned and who reads four clauses and points at one. There is a woman of about thirty-eight with a bar on a mile and a half. There is a man of about fifty-eight with a badge and no head. There is a man of about thirty-four with a satchel. There is a woman of about forty-nine who keeps a kitchen on the made road. There is a man of about thirty-two with a stick who wrote nine hundred words and got one of the nine wrong. And there is me, and I own a field.**”

Nobody thanked him and four people in that market street had heard nine people described by their trades and not one by anything else, and five had heard a man of fifty-one put himself in a list of nine.

“**That is the whole of it,**” he said. “**Nine people, no head, no page, no series, and one of the nine has a lever and the other eight have a habit. I have been at those boards four times this month and I have said four things and I would like it noticed that all four of them were about what this county cannot do and not one of them was about what it can.**”

Nobody thanked him and nobody argued with him and two hundred people in that market street had heard nine people read out by a man of fifty-one, and four of them had counted, and five had not.

Then a woman of about sixty with a basket and a stick, who has done forty runs out of this market town and who was at the boards on the third hour, said the thing about it that nobody else in that market street had thought of, and she said it out loud, and nine people heard it.

“**Then what is it worth if nobody can ask for it?**”

Nobody thanked her and the man of about fifty-one said no in about four seconds, and four people in that market street had expected him to have an answer and five had not.

“**It is worth what a barrow drain is worth,**” he said, “**which is nothing on any day until the day somebody needs it, and on the day somebody needs it there is no arrangement, no head, no page, and no way of telling anybody to be there, and nine people will be there anyway because they have always been there. That is what this county has. It has not got a lever. It has got nine people and a habit.**”

Nobody thanked him and nobody said anything and two hundred people in that market street had heard a man of fifty-one compare a county to a drain and five had understood what he meant.

Then the copyist of about twenty-six said the whole of the rest of it, and she said it flatly, and it is the second cost of this volume said out loud in a fourth mouth, and nobody thanked her.

“**And there is nowhere in nine counties to put it,**” she said. “**A return is a thing a district asks for and gets. A roll is a thing people are on. A sheet going out to a district is a thing a person in a room sends. Nine people saying no at nine gates on one morning is not a return and is not a roll and is not a sheet, and I have three series and they have been correct for about four and a half years and there is not one line in any of them about a barrow drain or a boy who went with a man in a livery on a Wednesday, and there is not going to be one, and I am not going to rule a head for it.**”

Nobody thanked her and four people in that market street had heard a keeper of records say that her own work cannot hold the best thing her county has, and five had heard a woman be exact about her own limits.

Then the man of about thirty-four with a satchel asked the question that the man of about fifty-one had been walking four miles to be asked, and he asked it out loud, in the open, and two hundred people heard it.

“**Then ask them.**”

Nobody said anything and nine people in that market street understood the whole of what that sentence was and five had not.

“**You are asking me to say it, so I will say it and I will not do it,**” the man of about fifty-one said. “**I will not carry it up four miles to a man who has a badge, and I will not carry it up four miles to a room, and I will not carry it into a county where there are not nine people in it. And I would like about nine people here to notice that a man of about thirty-four has just been told no in a market street by a man with a field and that nobody in this street thinks that is an argument for doing it anyway.**”

Nobody thanked him and four people in that market street had heard a man refuse to carry a thing, and five had heard a man of fifty-one name what being asked to carry a thing is, which is being made into a head with legs.

Then the badge-man said the one thing he says about that, and he said it in nine words, and nine people in that market street had waited four months for him to be asked something he could not answer with a fact, and nobody thanked him.

“**Then nobody asks, and the nine turn up, and I am not going to be thanked for any part of it.**”

Nobody thanked him and two hundred people in that market street had heard a man of fifty-eight say that the correct procedure is to do nothing, which is the second time he has said that in nine months, and four people at the boards had noticed that and five had not.

Then the cost of it was said out loud, and it was said by a woman of about thirty-three who keeps a chandler's shop at the low end of the boards, and she has said about nine things in four and a half years that turned out to be exactly right and five that did not, and nobody has ever been able to tell which is which before the fact.

“**Then say what it costs, because nine people in this street have heard that we have a thing and four of them have understood that a thing that cannot be asked for is a thing that has to be paid for by somebody anyway,**” the woman of about thirty-three said.

Nobody thanked her and she said it in about nine words and nobody at those boards interrupted her.

“**It costs the woman of about thirty-nine on a lane two days off, who said no at her own gate four feet before she was asked and who cannot promise to say it twice. It costs four of about nine households at a crossing nine miles off who have stopped going there and cannot be got to say so. It costs a boy of about fourteen who is in no danger. And it costs four people on a Friday at a cut on a mile and a half who come out for nothing and are not thanked for it, which is the only one of the four that anybody in this county has ever said out loud.**”

Nobody thanked her and four people in that market street had heard a woman of thirty-three put a price on the county's best asset in the only currency this county has, and five had heard a chandler say that a drain nobody can ask for is paid for by four people in the wet.

Nobody said anything and the man of about thirty-one who keeps a house and a hundred acres off the made road said no out loud, and he had walked in at about the ninth hour and had said nothing until then, and nobody had asked him anything.

“**I will not put my name to a list of households that work at the gate when the crews are on the road,**” the man of about thirty-one said. “**I have five people and four of them are under the age I am going to describe, and my load goes to market on Thursdays, and if I am at the gate on a Thursday then my load does not go and I do not get paid, and I have said no and I am not going to be argued with, and whoever asked me had no right to ask me and asked me anyway.**”

Nobody said anything and the man of about thirty-four with the satchel said that he had not asked him and that the man of about thirty-one had been asked by a woman of about twenty-four who keeps a copy-book four doors up, and that the woman of about twenty-four said so, and that she was standing in the street at the time, and that she said yes, she had asked, and she had known what it cost, and she had asked.

Nobody thanked her and four people in that market street had heard a woman of about twenty-four admit to asking a thing she had no right to ask, and about five had heard a man hand her the whole of it in front of four hundred people.

“**I asked because I copy things for nobody,**” the woman of about twenty-four said, “**and I wanted to be in a thing. That is the whole of the reason and it is a bad one and I am not going to dress it up in a market street, and about four people have told me since the first of Wolfmonth that it is a bad reason and about five have told me it is the only reason anybody in this county has ever had for anything.**”

Nobody thanked her and nobody argued with her and the man of about thirty-one said that he was not going to be on a list and that the argument was over, and that he had a load to send, and he went.

Nobody thanked him and the market carried on at about the fourth hour, and the two hundred went back to what they were doing, and about nine people stayed at the boards and did not go home.

What happened at about the fourth hour was ordinary and it went on for two hours and nobody ordered it. A wain went up the lane to the ford with a chain on a handcart and a boy of about nine walking beside it, and the man of about sixty-two with the wain came back down the made road with three horses and no load and did not stop. The weigh-house weighed two loads of lime and a load of wool, and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people who were in and about five who were not, that the whole town had been busy for a fortnight and that not one person in it had asked him for anything, and about four people in the weigh-house heard that and about five did not.

Nobody thanked him and about four people in that market town have said since that a man who keeps a weigh-house is the only man in it who can be certain of how much has come past, and that he has never once used it, and about five have said that it is not his to use.

Then the thing that happened at about the sixth hour was that a man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up said one sentence out loud at the low end of the boards, and it is the whole of what nine counties has got in its head on the sixth of Frostmonth, and about nine people in that market street heard it and about four of them asked him for the list and about five did not.

“**I have got nine names in my head and I am not going to write them down.**”

Nobody thanked him and about two hundred people in that market street heard a man refuse to write nine names down and about nine people heard where the names are and about four asked him for them and about five did not, and he wrote nothing, and about nine people in nine counties know that the nine names in that market town are in a man of about thirty-three's head and are not on a board, and about five have said that a head is not a series and about four have said that a head is the only kind of record that cannot be got at.

**And the cost of that Saturday is nine people who will say no at a gate and cannot be asked to, four households at a crossing nine miles off who cannot be got to say they have stopped, and a list of nine names in a man's head that nobody has written down, are not in it and none of them has a price.**

**a sheet of foolscap at 16, a bit of chalk at 23, a bucket at 27, a gate stop at 28, a line of chalk at 30, a hurdle at 32, a hayfork at 34, a collar for a horse at 35, a tether ring at 36, and a stable door bar at 40.** Sixteen, twenty-three, twenty-seven, twenty-eight, thirty, thirty-two, thirty-four, thirty-five, thirty-six and forty is three hundred and one pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and three hundred and one pence is six marks and thirteen over.** The man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up put his cloth down off his board at about the seventh hour, which he has not done on a market morning in about four years, and about four people in that market street noticed that he had put it down and about five did not, and Aren Kest, who had been at the high end of the boards with his stick in his right hand for the whole of it and who had said nine words at about the fourth hour, went up the made road on foot after him and caught him at the top of the rise and said one sentence, and the man of about thirty-three said he was not writing it down either, and neither of them said anything else, and about nine people in that market town saw a man of thirty-two and a man of about thirty-three walk up a made road together and not write anything down.
