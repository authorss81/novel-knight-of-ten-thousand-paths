# Chapter 0689

**Frostmonth the thirteenth, YR 321, a Saturday and a market morning, the second hour in the market street at the boards with the hearing in the street and four hundred people in it and two hundred of them stopped, a captain of about forty-four with a sealed warrant and a serjeant and four men, and the difficulty of a paper that has no place in it to be wrong in.**

Halvard Rees came up the market street on foot with the serjeant on his right and four men behind them, and he was not in a cloak, and nobody in that market street had ever seen him in a cloak.

Nobody thanked him and four people at the boards had noticed the cloak and five had noticed the four men behind him, and the four men were half a pace behind the serjeant and the serjeant was half a pace behind the captain, which is what a column of about four looks like in a market town and is not what a column looks like anywhere else, and nine people in nine counties have said the four men are the part that frightened them.

There is no horse in that market street. That is the first thing nine people noticed and four of them have said out loud and five have not, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a captain of about forty-four who walks into a market town of two thousand people with four men and a chain and no horse at his back is a man who has thought about what he is doing to a market street, and that about five people in nine counties have said that a man who cannot ride cannot be followed at a gallop, and that both of those things are the same thing.

Nobody thanked him and four people at those boards had worked out that the four men behind the serjeant were not there as a show and five had not.

He has been doing this since the eighth of Embermonth. He came into this county on that day with a serjeant and four men and a chain and a list, and he has gone on doing it in this county every day since except two, and four people in nine counties have worked out that the two days are the two days a thing happens that is not a road, and five have worked out nothing.

He put the sheet on the boards himself, which about nine people at the boards noticed and five did not, and he did not fold it and he did not put it away, and the list of counties in order was on the boards of this market town for the second time in about nine weeks.

Nobody thanked him and nobody in that market street read it, because it is a list of counties and not a name, and two hundred people had seen it once.

Then he said the thing he had come to say, and he said it the way he says everything, which is that he gives the reason before he gives the answer and does not soften the answer and does not read it faster than he has to.

“**Three things and then I will stop talking. One. I have been at this for nine weeks and two days and I have taken one road in one county at a time, off a list of about thirty, and I have not read the last twenty-one counties on it and I am not going to pretend that I have. Two. A serjeant of mine has been somewhere every three days since the middle of Fallowmonth with a sheet and four men, and he does not have my warrant, he has a copy of a line of it, and he does the same thing I do and he does it because I told him to and he would rather be at it than be here. Three. I have nine men on a road in this county and nine I cannot spare, and I have said the number twice and I am saying it a third time in front of four hundred people because I would like it written down that a captain of about forty-four has said out loud in a market street that he cannot spare his own men, and nobody has ever asked me to.**”

Nobody said anything and two hundred people in that market street had heard a captain say the hardest thing on his list out loud, and nine of them understood it and five understood that he had wanted it understood.

Nobody thanked him and four people at the boards had heard a professional say the thing about his own difficulty in the order that makes it useless to him, and five had heard a man read out a number twice.

Then he said the fourth thing, and it is the fourth thing, and he had not said it in that street before, and nine people in nine counties have said since that it is the most important thing anybody from the office four hundred miles off has said in nine months.

“**The instrument is not the difficulty. The shape is.**”

Nobody thanked him and two hundred people in that market street had heard a man of forty-four say that a paper is not the problem and four of them had understood what he meant and five had not.

“**I want to be exact, because about four of the people standing in this street understand words and five do not, and I have been read back at a gate in about nine counties and I would rather be understood. My paper is legal. Nobody in this county has ever said in public that it is not legal and I have waited five weeks for somebody to say it and nobody has. My paper is competent. It is the work of about nine hands and it is sealed and it is signed and a man in a livery at a gate can put his hand inside his coat and produce it, and he does, and he does it well.**”

Nobody thanked him and nobody said anything and two hundred people in that market street had heard a captain of forty-four say that his own paper is good.

“**And it has no place in it. A warrant that names a place can be refused at a gate. I have been refused at a gate and I did not take anybody's neck off for it, because that is what a gate is for. This one does not name a place. It says wherever the works are, and a thing that says wherever is not a thing that can be argued with at a gate, because there is nothing at the gate to argue about. It has no end. A man cannot be wrong about a thing with no end in it.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had understood all of that and five had heard a captain explain that the problem is that he cannot be wrong.

“**And that is the whole of it, and I have not come to your boards to be liked and I am not going to be thanked, and I would like about nine people to notice that a thing nobody can be wrong about is a thing nobody can be argued with, including the people in it, and that is the shape, and the shape is worse for me than the paper is.**”

Nobody thanked him and two hundred people in that market street had heard a captain of about forty-four say that the shape of his own paper is the difficulty and four of them had understood why that is a thing a man says out loud in public.

Then he said the other thing, and it is the fifth thing and it is the one about four people in this market town have been waiting nine weeks for.

“**I have been wrong about a ford. On the Sunday in the first week of Embermonth I put a chain on a ford four miles down a lane and a woman of about fifty came down to it in the rain and told me what her four miles were and what the cost was, and she was right, and I was wrong, and the man of about fifty who keeps the field has heard me say that in this street and I have not asked him to say it again.**”

Nobody thanked him and four people at the boards had heard a captain of forty-four say a thing about himself that nobody in nine counties had asked him to say, and five had heard a man own a mistake.

“**That is the only thing I have had in nine weeks that anybody in this county could use against me. I have been thinking about it since the Sunday and I have arrived somewhere and I am going to say it in a market street, and four of you will understand it and five will not. I would like nine more.**”

Nobody said anything and two hundred people in that market street had heard a captain of about forty-four say that he would like nine more mistakes, and nine of them had understood, and five had heard a man ask for the wrong thing.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel asked the question that nine people at those boards had come to ask, and he asked it once.

“**Then what would you like us to do?**”

Nobody thanked him and the captain said the true thing about it in fourteen words and did not add to it, and four people at the boards had heard it and five had not.

“**Nothing. I would like you to go on doing exactly what you are doing.**”

Nobody said anything and two hundred people in that market street had heard a captain of forty-four tell about nine people in nine counties to go on doing what they are doing, and four of them understood that it was not a favour and five understood that it was.

Then the woman of about thirty-six who keeps a road in the county where a list of names was burned said the one thing that about nine people in that market street had been waiting for her to say, and she said it standing up again, which she has now done twice in nine days, and nobody thanked her.

“**I have been at these boards four times since Fallowmonth,**” she said, “**and every one of the four times somebody has asked me for a name, and I have not got one, and I am not going to get one, and I have said that four times and I would like it noticed that a captain of about forty-four has never once asked me for one. He has asked about a ford and a chain and whether a road may be shut. Nobody in a coat has ever asked me for a name and I have been in nine counties in about four years and that is the whole of what I have to say to a man with a warrant.**”

Nobody thanked her and four people in that market street had heard a woman of thirty-six say that a Crown officer had not once asked her the one thing nine hundred words and nine people in this county keep asking her for, and five had heard a woman say that a man with a paper was the only person in a coat who had not asked her for anything.

Then the badge-man said the thing that about nine people in nine counties had been waiting four months to hear him say to a captain of about forty-four, and he said it in twenty-eight words, and he said it in the street and not in a room, and nobody thanked him.

“**Then you are the only man in nine counties who has never asked, and I have no clause for you, and I am not going to write one.**”

Nobody said anything and two hundred people in that market street had heard a man of fifty-eight say out loud that he had no clause for a captain, and four of them had understood that it was the first thing he had ever said in a market street that was not a warning, and five had not.

Nobody thanked him and the captain said that he had not been asked on purpose, that he had not known there was a thing to ask, and that if anybody in nine counties wants him to ask for a name he will ask for a name and will be refused and will write it down, and that he had done that twice at a gate this month and had read both back.

Nobody thanked anybody and about four people at the boards had heard a captain say that he will ask for a name if anybody in nine counties wants a name asked for, and about five had heard a man say that he will be refused.

Nobody thanked him and about four people at the boards had heard a woman of thirty-six say that nobody in a coat has ever asked her for a name, and about five had heard a woman of thirty-six say it to a captain instead of to a copyist, and nobody at the boards has been able to establish since which of those two things she did it for.

Then a man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up asked the question that about nine people in that market street had been carrying since the middle of Harvestmonth, and he asked it out loud and in the open, and nobody thanked him for asking it and about five people in that market street have said since that it was the only question anybody asked that morning that was not about a paper.

“**Then what is the county for, if not for the roads.**”

Nobody said anything and two hundred people in that market street had stopped what they were doing for the third time that morning, and the captain of about forty-four did not answer it, and the badge-man did not answer it, and the copyist of about twenty-six did not answer it, and about nine people in that market street understood that all three of them had heard the question before and about five had not.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about thirty-three waited at the low end of the boards for about as long as it takes to turn a cart, and nobody answered him, and he said out loud that that was the correct arrangement and went back inside his shop, and about four people in that market street heard a man of thirty-three take the absence of an answer as an answer and about five heard a man walk away from a question.

Nobody thanked him and about four people in that market street had worked out that a question a man asks in a market street and does not get answered is a question the county has already answered, and about five have not.

Aren Kest was at the high end of the boards for the whole of it and the captain of about forty-four looked at him twice, and on the second time he said one word, which was a name, and about two hundred people in that market street heard a Crown captain say a name that nobody in nine counties had heard in about eleven years except from a piece of paper, and about nine of them understood that and about five did not.

Nobody thanked either of them and the man of thirty-two said nothing back, and about four people in that market street had come to those boards that morning to hear what a man who wrote nine hundred words would say to a captain with a warrant, and about five had not, and he said nothing, and the badge-man said afterwards, in a lane, that that had been the correct arrangement and that about nine people in nine counties would have preferred the other one.

Then the serjeant said nothing and the four men said nothing and about two hundred people in that market street had watched about four men of a column stand in a market for about a quarter of an hour and do nothing at all, which about nine people have said is the most frightening part of a column and about five have said is the only decent part.

Nobody thanked anybody and the market carried on at about the third hour, and the two hundred went back to their own business, and about nine people stayed at the boards and watched a list of counties go down a lane.

What happened at about the fourth hour was at the low end of the boards, in the open, in front of about two hundred people who had not gone home, and it is on the page because about nine people in nine counties have said that a captain of about forty-four and a man of about thirty-one with a small book disagreed about a page in public, and that four of those nine understood the whole of it.

The man of about thirty-one in a livery put the small book on the boards and opened it and wrote, and he wrote the way he writes, which is that he wrote down what was said and not what was meant, and he read it back to about nine people and about two hundred heard it.

Nobody thanked him and the captain came down off the high end of the boards and stood at the low side of it and looked at the page for about as long as it takes to turn a cart, and then he said the true thing about his own difficulty, and about four people in that market street understood it and about five did not.

“**Leave a space.**”

Nobody thanked him and the man of about thirty-one said no, and he said it in the ordinary way, and about nine people in that market street heard it.

“**A page has no space for an answer. A page has a line for what was said and a place for the county and the gate and the day and my name, and if I leave a space on it then in about four years a man in a room reads the space and thinks a person was there and did not answer, and the person may have been there and said everything, and the space is a thing I put in with a pencil in about four seconds and I cannot take it out in about four years.**”

Nobody thanked either of them and the captain said one sentence, and he said it out loud, and about two hundred people in that market street heard a captain of about forty-four agree with a man of about thirty-one in front of four hundred people.

“**You are right. I have been asking a man to invent a thing a page cannot hold, and I have been doing it for nine weeks, and I would like about nine people to notice that the difficulty I came to this market street with is not a shape. It is a pencil.**”

Nobody thanked him and about four people in that market street had heard a captain of about forty-four say that his difficulty is a pencil and about five had heard a man admit something in a market street on a Saturday.

**And the cost of that Saturday morning is nine weeks of one road in one county at a time off a list of about thirty that nobody has read past the ninth, a serjeant's three days between places with a sheet and about four men, and nine men a captain of about forty-four cannot spare out of a thing nobody in nine counties can be wrong about, are not in it and none of them has a price.**

**a horse collar at 15, a sheaf of warrants at 29, a tally board at 30, a wax seal at 31, a mile of cord at 32, a length of chain at 34, a hand lantern at 35, a serjeant's book at 36, a ration of oats at 37, and a camp kettle at 40.** Fifteen, twenty-nine, thirty, thirty-one, thirty-two, thirty-four, thirty-five, thirty-six, thirty-seven and forty is three hundred and nineteen pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and three hundred and nineteen pence is six marks and thirty-one over.** The captain left the list of counties on the boards of that market town and went down the lane at about the fourth hour with the serjeant and about four men, and the small book went inside a coat, and the small book had a page in it with a line on it and a place for the county and the gate and the day and his own name and nothing else, and about nine people in that market street watched a man of about forty-four go down a lane with a sheet he could not fold and four men who had stood in a market for a quarter of an hour doing nothing.
