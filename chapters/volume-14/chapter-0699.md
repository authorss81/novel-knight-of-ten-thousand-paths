# Chapter 0699

**Goatmonth the first, YR 321, a Friday and not a market morning and the first of a month, with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and the boards standing in an empty market street, about nine people in it, and nothing at the top of it.**

A first of a month in this county comes whether or not anybody is in the street for it, and the first of Goatmonth is a Friday, and there is no market on a Friday, and this is the tenth of the twelves that the man of about fifty-eight named out loud in an empty market street on the first of Hearthmonth, and about four people in nine counties have said since that he was right the tenth time and about five have said that being right ten times in a row about a thing you announced is not the same as having fixed it.

Nobody thanked anybody and about nine people came to those boards at about the ninth hour and about five did not, and the nine were not the same nine who came on the first of Hearthmonth, and about four people in nine counties have said that this is the whole of what a first of a month is and that a thing which has nine different people standing in it four and a half years running is not a thing anybody attends.

Then the badge-man did not speak.

Nobody thanked him and a man of about fifty-eight with a badge and no head stood at the low end of the boards from about the ninth hour until about the eleventh with his bar against his leg and said nothing at all, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this was the first first of a month in four and a half years on which that man said nothing, and about four people have said that a man who says nothing on a first of a month has run out of warnings, and about five have said that he has been saying the same true thing for four and a half years and that a true thing said ten times is not a warning and is not a finding and is not nothing.

Nobody thanked anybody and the copyist of about twenty-six was not there, and about four people in that market street had noticed the room standing shut with the bolt still on from the Saturday and about five had not noticed anything at all, and the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps that room had not come down the thirteen stairs and nobody sent for her and nobody asked why.

Nobody thanked anybody and then three people said the whole of what had happened, one at a time, in the open, to about nine people, and each of them said it and stopped and nobody filled the space after any of them.

The man of about thirty-four with a satchel said the first, and he had it on a sheet and he read it and did not improve it.

“**The column turned south on the twenty-first of Hearthmonth. It is on a made road four days off this market town and it is not coming back up it in this weather. A captain of about forty-four has been in this county for fourteen weeks and five days. He took about nine counties off a list of about thirty and he did not take the ferry city and he did not take the works and he did not take one road in nine counties, and he did not take a gatehouse, and there is a wall on a made road at nine miles that four hundred people in this street could point at on Saturday.**”

Nobody said anything and about nine people in that market street had heard the whole of fourteen weeks in four sentences and about five of them had not understood that a thing not taken is a thing somebody decided.

Nobody thanked him and the sheet he read it off had come up that made road on the Thursday with a man of about thirty-one in a livery, and it is a copy of the list of thirty counties in order, and about nine of the thirty are struck through with a single line each and the striking is not neat and was not meant to be, and about four people in nine counties have said that a struck line on a list is the only thing an office four hundred miles off has ever done to a county that it could not do anything else to, and about five have said that a struck line is a thing that is put on a page and not on a person and that is the difference that matters.

Nobody thanked anybody and about twenty-one counties are still on that list with nothing against them, and nine people in nine counties have said since that nine of those twenty-one have not been read since the eighth of Embermonth, and that a county which has not been read is a county nobody is working on.

Nobody thanked him and a question was asked in that market street at about the tenth hour, out loud, in the open, by the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up, and nobody stopped him.

“**Then what happens on the first of the next month?**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard the question and about five had heard ten words.

And the badge-man said nine words back to it and it is the last thing anybody said in that street that day.

“**Nothing happens on the first of the next month.**”

Nobody thanked him and a second question was asked in that market street at about the tenth hour and thirty minutes, out loud, in the open, by a person who has no standing in anything at all, and nobody stopped her.

“**Then what is the ninth of the month for.**”

Nobody said anything and the badge-man did not answer that one, and about four people in that market street had worked out that it was the only question of that morning he did not have an answer to, and about five had worked out nothing, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the first of a month is still there and still comes and that a day with nothing at the top of it is not a day anybody has to come to, and that this was never in doubt and that about four people in nine counties have said that a thing which is never in doubt is not worth four and a half years, and five have said that it is worth four and a half years because in four years somebody will want to know whether it was ever in doubt.

Nobody thanked him and about four people in that market street had heard a man of fifty-eight tell nine people that nothing is going to happen on a day that four and a half years ago was a standing arrangement, and about five had heard nothing happen, and about nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the whole of the resolution of this county and that a thing which stands in a street on the first of every month has been told by the man who built it that it will not stand in that street on the first of any month to come unless somebody with standing says so, and that nobody in nine counties has standing, and that this is not a decision and is not a finding and is a thing a man of fifty-eight said out loud in an empty market street and then went home. and about five of them had not understood that a thing not taken is a thing somebody decided.

Nobody thanked him and about four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that a thing has come off a Crown list and not gone onto one, and about five have said that about nine counties of about thirty have not been looked at since the eighth of Embermonth and that nobody in the office four hundred miles off has read the last twenty-one and that the last twenty-one are now the whole of the remaining business.

Then the woman of about thirty-nine who keeps the second gate from the top of this made road said the second, and she had walked in and had not come to the boards before, and she said it standing in the street.

“**My gate is open. It has been shut since the first of Hearthmonth and it is open this morning and I have not shut it and I am not going to shut it again, and I said no before anybody asked me twice and I have said it four times this month and the fifth time is not going to be different from the first.**”

Nobody thanked her and about four people in that market street had heard a woman put a thing back the way it was, and about five had heard a woman close something.

Then the badge-man said the third, and it is the last thing he says at a set of boards in a market street in nine counties, and he said it in ten words, and he said it at about the eleventh hour with four people in that street and five not.

“**Nobody can call the next meeting and nobody needs to.**”

Nobody thanked him and about four people in that market street had heard a man say that the arrangement has no next meeting and does not need one, and about five had heard ten words, and nobody in that street asked him how the arrangement gets going again and he was not asked.

Nobody thanked anybody and then the thing that happened in nine counties between the eighth of Embermonth and the twenty-ninth of Hearthmonth was said plainly on this page, because nobody in that market street said it and somebody has to.

The badge-man's two hours of silence at the low end of those boards are worth putting down, because nine people in nine counties have said since that a man saying nothing for two hours is the hardest thing he has ever done and that four and a half years of his life has been spent learning to say true things out loud in market streets, and about five have said that a man who says nothing for two hours in a market street with nine people in it has told those nine people more than he has ever told them and that nobody has ever been able to write down what.

Nobody thanked him and what happened in those two hours was this. Four people came up to him one at a time and asked him things and he answered nothing. Five people stood at a distance and waited. Nobody wrote anything down. Nobody left, because about four people in that street have said that leaving is what a person does when a man has stopped talking, and about five have said that leaving is what a person does when a man is tired, and that on the first of Goatmonth nobody in that market street could tell which of the two was happening and that about nine people have chosen not to decide which and that this is the closest this county has come to a decision in about four and a half years. and about five have said that a hearing that decides nothing is not a decision and is not a resolution and is a thing that stands in a street, and that this is the fourth and a half year it has been a thing that stands in a street, and that nobody has ever come to a market street to be told that.

The march came into nine counties and went out of it again and it did not take one road and it did not take the ferry city and it did not take the works, and that is the whole of what nine counties did about it, and not one person in nine counties has ever said the word for what that is, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the reason nobody has said it is that the word has a shape and the shape belongs to a room four hundred miles off and not to a market street, and about five have said that the word would have to be said by somebody and that there is nobody in nine counties who is entitled to say it, and that a person who is not entitled to say a word about a thing that has happened is not obliged to say it.

Nobody thanked anybody and nothing about that is better than what it was in August, and nobody in that market street said that it was, and a wall on a made road at nine miles that four hundred people in a market street can point at is a worse thing for every person in nine counties than a wall that four hundred people walked past, and the nine counties knows it, and about four people in nine counties have said out loud since the twenty-fifth of Hearthmonth that they would give the whole of the last four and a half years back to have the wall shut again and about five have said exactly the same thing and that neither set has ever spoken to the other.

Then the copyist of about twenty-six came down the thirteen stairs at about the eleventh hour and unlocked the door and opened it and stood in the lane while the room let the cold off it, and she came out again with a leaf in her hand and nothing on it, and about four people in that lane had seen her come down and about five had not.

Nobody thanked her and she said the only thing she said in that lane that afternoon, and it is fourteen words, and it is what the hearing is.

“**It certifies nothing, it has never certified anything, and it will not start now.**”

Nobody thanked her and about four people in that lane had heard a keeper of records say the whole of what a hearing is in fourteen words, and about five had heard fourteen words, and the register in that room stands at ten questions with no finding under any of them and a fourth ruled head empty for about eleven months and no fifth head ruled and the day-book at eleven entries, and nobody asked her about any of that and she did not mention it.

Nobody thanked anybody and the room was shut again at about the twelfth hour and the bolt went on and the boards stood in an empty market street with nothing on them, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the first of Goatmonth was the tenth of those twelves and that on the ninth of the ten four people stood in the street and on this one nine did, and that neither figure is one anybody has agreed on, and that the nine who came on the first of Goatmonth were not the nine who came on the first of Hearthmonth, and that about four people in nine counties have said that a thing which nine different people turn up to is a thing nobody attends and five have said that a thing which four people turn up to is a thing with four people in it.

Nobody thanked anybody and the people who are not in that street are four and they are named here, because a resolution that only lists what was present is not a resolution.

The captain of about forty-four is on a made road four days off this market town going south in the wet with a list of about thirty counties in order that went up the made road in a handbasket on the Saturday after he turned and that is in the hired room over a saddler's in this market town under a cloth on the same table as nine hundred words that nobody has withdrawn, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a list of counties and nine hundred words under one cloth is the nearest this county has ever come to a document and five have said that it is a basket and a cloth and that neither of them has a head.

The woman of about thirty-nine at a gate two days off is at her gate, and her gate is open, and she is not in that street and nobody sent for her and nobody sent anybody to her.

The delegation that came eleven days on a cart in the middle of Longlight went home along that road in the middle of the summer and about four people in nine counties have said since that it got home and about five have said that nobody has asked and that not asking is the shape of the thing.

And the city four hundred miles off has not been asked a question by anybody in nine counties and is not in that street and is not being sent anybody, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the second time in about four months that a city four hundred miles off has not been in a market street, and about five have said that the first time was on the nineteenth of Fallowmonth and that nobody has asked it anything since and that nobody is going to.

Nobody thanked anybody and there was nothing at the top of it, and the reason there was nothing at the top of it is not that nobody wanted to put something there, and about four people in nine counties have said that it is that nobody in nine counties could call the next meeting, and about five have said that the thing that wanted a meeting was a man with a warrant and that he has stopped and that a thing which has stopped does not send for anybody.

Then, at about the twelfth hour, the last thing happened in that market street, and it was small, and it was local, and it was nobody's who had come up the made road that day, and it is the last thing that happened in that market street before the lamp was lit.

Nobody thanked her and the woman of about thirty-three who keeps a chandler's shop at the low end of the boards took a cloth off her own shelf and put it over the bench she has put out of that shop every market morning for eleven years with nothing on it and nothing on the bench, and nobody has ever been able to establish what she has been doing with that bench or who has been meant to see her doing it.

Nobody thanked her and about four people in an empty market street saw a woman put a cloth over a bench and about five did not see it at all, and she put the corners of it under and turned it once so that the fold faced the street, and then she went inside and shut the door of that shop behind its own door and the bench stood in the street under the cloth until the lamp at the high end of the boards was lit at the fourth hour.

Nobody thanked anybody and nine people in nine counties have said since that the first of Goatmonth was the day a woman who keeps a shop put a cloth over a bench, and that nobody in nine counties has any idea what is on it or is going to be on it, and about four people have said that this is the only thing that happened in nine counties this month that was not a matter of a road, and about five have said that it is a matter of a road, and that she sells rope.

**And the cost of that first of Goatmonth is a hearing with nothing at the top of it, about twenty-one counties left on a list that has not been read past the ninth, and a cloth over a bench in an empty market street, are not in it and none of them has a price.**

**a nail at 5, a mile of cord at 27, a hand adze at 31, a hurdle at 32, a gate stop at 34, a saw blade at 35, a length of chain at 36, a barrow wheel at 37, a shear at 39, and a barrow at 40.** Five, twenty-seven, thirty-one, thirty-two, thirty-four, thirty-five, thirty-six, thirty-seven, thirty-nine and forty is three hundred and sixteen pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and three hundred and sixteen pence is six marks and twenty-eight over.** The bench is still under the cloth at the low end of that market street and nobody has put anything on it and nobody has lifted the cloth and nobody in nine counties has asked her what is under it, and about four people in that market town have said that she has been putting that bench out for eleven years with nothing on it and that a woman who does that for eleven years is not doing it for nothing, and about five have said that she sells rope and that this has nothing to do with anything and that they would like it to go on for another eleven years.
