# Chapter 0682

**Wolfmonth the thirteenth, YR 321, a Thursday and a market morning, the second hour in the market street at the boards with the hearing in the street and four hundred people in it, four roads asked a different question, and the two that sent nothing being the two the column is standing on.**

The man of about thirty-four with a satchel in his coat had gone out on the Monday and had come back on the Wednesday and had not slept, and about nine people in that market street worked out where he had been in about nine seconds and five did not.

He had asked four roads this time and not the same four, and the question had not been the sixth clause.

“**I asked them what they would do about a boy,**” he said, at about the second hour, in a market street, to about nine people, and he said it the way he said everything, which was by putting the thing down in front of the question.

Nobody thanked him and four people at the boards had heard the whole of that and five had heard a man ask four roads about a boy.

Then he took four things out of the satchel and laid them on the boards, and two of them were sheets with writing on them and two of them were nothing at all, and the arrangement on the boards was the arrangement that everybody in that street had already worked out in nine seconds.

“**Two wrote,**” he said. “**Two sent nothing. And I am going to say the two that sent nothing out loud in this street before I say anything else, because about four people in this street have been waiting since Fallowmonth to hear it and five have not thought about it once.**”

Nobody said anything and two hundred people in that market street had stopped what they were doing.

“**The coast road sent nothing. The four miles at the ford sent nothing. Those are the two he is standing on. Those are the two with a chain and a forward party and about four men on them since the eighth of Embermonth, and neither of them has written to anybody in this county, and about four people in this street are about to say that that is a coincidence.**”

Nobody thanked him and four people at the boards had heard the whole of it and five had heard a man lay four pieces of paper out on a set of boards.

Then the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps a horse at the top of that market street said the sentence that about nine people in nine counties have been carrying since the seventeenth of Embermonth, and she said it out loud, and she is not the man with the stick and she is not a road and she has said so in a market street twice.

“**No it is not,**” the woman of about twenty-nine said.

Nobody thanked her and about four people in that market street had heard her say two words and five had heard a woman who keeps a horse contradict a man with a satchel.

“**Say the rest of it,**” the man of about thirty-four said, and he asked for it once and did not ask twice.

“**I said in this street on the seventeenth of the month before last that nothing had happened in two counties where nothing was asked of anybody, and something had happened in nine counties where about nine people have been asked things all year, and about four of you understood that at the time and five wrote it down.**” She had not moved from the top of the market street. “**That was true and I still say it. Here is the other half and I have been sitting on it for a month.**”

Nobody said anything and two hundred people in that market street had stopped what they were doing again.

“**Last time, the two that sent nothing were the waterworks country and the glass country, and he had not set foot in either of them and there was not a chain in either of them and nobody had asked anybody in either of them to stop anything. This time the two that send nothing are the two he is standing on.**”

Then she said the true thing and she said it slowly, and about four people in that market street understood every word of it and five heard a woman keep a horse.

“**So the shape has turned over. A month ago a road that would not answer was a road nobody had touched. This month a road that will not answer is a road with four men on it, and a road with four men on it is a road somebody is going to read off a list, and when it is read off a list there will be no moment and no name and nobody in nine counties can say a word about it, because the only people who could say a word about it are the two who are not writing.**”

Nobody thanked her and about four people at that market street had heard the whole of what a horse-keeper knows about lists, and five had heard a woman say a road was going to be read off a piece of paper.

“**I am not going to say what to do about it,**” she said. “**I am a woman who keeps a horse. I have got nine hundred words on a table in a room over a saddler's and I have not got a clause in it and I am not going to be handed one this month.**”

Nobody thanked her and four people at the boards said nothing and five said something at once and then stopped, and the man of about thirty-four with the satchel said that he had been going to say the other half of it and that she had said it better and that he wanted that noticed, and the copyist of about twenty-six said out loud, in the flat way, that she was not going to say it better than that and that the woman had not asked anybody's leave.

Nobody thanked either of them and about four people at the boards had heard a man concede a thing to a woman in a market street and five had heard a keeper of records decline to.

Nobody thanked him and about four people in that market street had understood that and five had heard a man lay paper out on a set of boards and count the holes in it.

There were four places on those boards and two of them had something on them. Boards do not have marks on them, and a person at a set of boards in a market street can see a gap in a row the way a person can see a gap in a row of teeth, and two hundred people in that market street saw the two empty places and nine of them had worked out before the man of about thirty-four finished speaking that the empty places were the two that had sent nothing.

“**You have laid them out in order,**” the copyist said, at about the second hour, in a market street, to about nine people. “**The one that wrote is first and the one that wrote is second and the two that sent nothing are third and fourth, and you have put them in that order on purpose, because you wanted two hundred people in this street to be able to see which two did not answer without being told.**”

“**I did,**” the man of about thirty-four said.

“**Then say out loud what you did that for,**” the copyist said, “**because a man who lays four things out in order in a market street is doing something, and I want nine people to know it is not a return and not a roll.**”

“**It is a notice,**” the man of about thirty-four said. “**It is not in a book and it is not addressed to anybody, and in nine days there will be a fourth piece of paper on a set of boards in a market town in a county that has not had one, because that is how I have been doing it for four years and it is the only trade I have.**”

Nobody thanked him and four people in that market street had heard a man say that he was the man a thing arrives for, in a second trade, without saying it.

Nobody said anything to that and the woman of about twenty-four who keeps a copy-book, who had put nine hundred words on a board in her own shop front that morning with no word at the top of it, said one thing out loud from four doors up and nobody thanked her for it.

“**Then I am not the only one keeping a copy-book for nothing,**” she said.

Then the copyist said the part that belongs to her, and she said it flatly and to nobody, and she has said some version of it in about nine places in four and a half years and she said it again because nobody in that market street had heard this version.

“**Two roads wrote. A road that has written is not a return. It is not a roll. It is not a sheet going out to a district. There is no series in nine counties into which the fact that a road answered goes, and I have said that in four markets and in a room over a saddler's and I am saying it here because a man has just laid four pieces of paper on a set of boards and about nine people in this street think that the pieces of paper are the answer.**”

Nobody thanked her and about four people at the boards had understood the whole of it and about five had heard a woman explain a book to a market street.

“**And the third series cannot carry it either, and I want that said by me and not worked out in a lane,**” she said. “**I have got about nine hundred sheets a month going out and about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month coming in and a band on a fixed day that I do not alter whatever is in the county. If a road answers me on a Tuesday then on Wednesday morning it is in nothing, and on Thursday it is still in nothing, and in about four years a man in a room is going to ask this county what the seven roads have said and the true answer is that this county does not know, and it will not be a lie.**”

Nobody thanked her and about four people in that market street had heard a keeper of records put a date on her own blindness and about five had heard the word Tuesday four times.

Then the man with the badge said one thing, and it was nine words, and he had not said it in that street for about four months, and about nine people heard it and four of them understood it.

“**A thing with no head is harder to get at, not smaller.**”

Nobody thanked him and nobody said anything and about four people at the boards looked at the four things on the boards and about five looked at the four hundred people in the market street.

Nobody said anything to that and about four people in that market street had heard a woman of about twenty-four put her shop and a man's satchel in the same sentence, and about five had not.

Then the man of about thirty-eight who puts boards on the inside of gates was at the low end of the boards, and he had not been there at the second hour and nobody had seen him come in, and about nine people in that market street had not known he was in the county.

“**Say the two that sent nothing again.**”

The man of about thirty-four said them again and he said them in the same words, because he had been asked once and he had the words.

Then the man of about thirty-eight stood at the low side of the boards for about as long as it takes to turn a cart and said nothing at all, and about four people in that market street understood why and about five thought he was sulking, and he had come from a county two days off with nine gates on a lane and about nine households on it and he had not come for this.

“**Are you going to ask me about the fourteenth of Rainmonth?**”

Nobody said anything and two hundred people in that market street had stopped what they were doing for the fifth time that morning.

“**No,**” the man of about thirty-four said. “**I asked once on the sixteenth of the month before last and you answered me and the answer was that you do not know and that you would not promise it twice, and I have not asked since, and I am not going to ask in a market street in front of four hundred people, because the second time I ask is the time a person has to have an answer ready.**”

Nobody thanked him and four people in that market street had heard a man say that he was not going to ask a thing twice, and the man of about thirty-eight said out loud that he had come four days for the market and not for that, and that was the whole of what he said in that street all morning.

Aren Kest was at the low side of the boards for the whole of it with his stick in his right hand and he had come on foot and had said nothing for about an hour, and about four people at the boards had been waiting for him to say something since the first of Wolfmonth and about five had stopped waiting somewhere in Fallowmonth.

“**Have you been up either of the two that sent nothing.**”

Nobody thanked him and the man of thirty-two said no, and he said it in the ordinary way, and he did not say it to make a point.

“**No. I have not been up the coast road and I have not been up the four miles, and I am not going to in this month, and I would like about nine people in this street to notice that the two roads that will not answer a letter are the two roads nobody in this county has stood on, and that is not a thing I have arranged.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a man of thirty-two own a fact about himself that four hundred people were standing in, and about five had heard a man say that he was not going to do a thing four and a half years.

Nobody thanked him and the copyist of about twenty-six said out loud, in the flat way, that a man who had not been stood on had just said so in a market street and that she was not going to write it down, and about four people at the boards heard that and about five did not.

Then a man of about forty-four who sells nails at the high end of the boards asked the question that about nine people in that street had come to ask, and he asked it out loud, and about four people in nine counties have said since that the whole of the rest of that morning turned on it.

“**Then why write to them at all.**”

Nobody said anything and two hundred people in that market street had stopped what they were doing for the fourth time that morning.

“**That is a good question and it has got a bad answer,**” the man of about thirty-four with the satchel said, “**and I am the man who has to give it. I write to them because a road that has written once will write again, and a road that writes twice has decided something, and a thing that has been decided twice by nine people is worth more in about nine counties than nine hundred words that nobody will sign. I write to them because I would rather be answered no than not asked. And I write to them because if I stop writing, then the only answers this county has are the ones a man in a livery gets at a gate, and I cannot stand what he writes down at a gate.**”

Nobody thanked him and about four people at that market street had heard a man say the real reason and about five had heard a man explain his own trade.

“**And I will tell you the cost of it, since we are all saying costs today,**” he said. “**I have walked nine days in a month. My own house gave nothing the last time and I said that out loud in this street in front of nine people and I am not going to say it twice. Two roads sent nothing. That is the whole of the return on nine days and it is a return in no series in nine counties.**”

Nobody thanked him and about four people at the boards had heard a man price nine days at nothing and about five had heard a man admit that two roads have not answered.

Then the two sheets were read out in the market street, and they were short, and the first of them was from the county where a list of names was burned and it was four lines long and it said what the woman of about thirty-six had said it would say, which is that a road has no clerk and a clerk has no road and the two have never met and are not going to.

Nobody thanked anybody and about four people in that street had heard the whole of it and about five had heard a county that burned a list answer a question about a boy.

The second was from the fruit valley and it was nine lines long and in two hands, and the copyist read it and stopped at the fourth line and said out loud that she was not going to read the other five in a market street, and nobody thanked her and about four people at the boards said nothing and five said something at once and then stopped.

Nobody thanked her and about nine people in that market street had heard a woman refuse to finish a letter out loud, and about four of them have said since that she was wrong and about five have said since that she was right.

What happened at about the fourth hour was ordinary and it went on for two hours and nobody ordered it.

The chain went up the lane to the ford at about the third hour with a boy of about nine walking beside the handcart, and it has gone up and come down every week since the thirteenth of Fallowmonth, and the woman of about thirty-nine who keeps the second gate from the top of a mile and a half was at her gate when it came past and did not shut it, which she has not done on a Thursday in about four years because a thing that comes up a lane at four hundred yards of a made road is a thing a person watches go by.

The list went up to the room over a saddler's in a handbasket and the man who carried it stood in the doorway for about as long as it takes to turn a cart, and the copyist came down and took it and did not thank him, and she has not thanked a person for carrying a sheet in four and a half years.

Nobody thanked anybody in that room and the nine hundred words were still on the table under the cloth, and the cloth had not been moved since the first of Wolfmonth, and about four people in nine counties have said since that the copyist moves it and about five have said the man with the stick moves it, and nobody has asked.

Nobody thanked her and the day-book on the table behind her had ten entries in it and the register above it had nine questions in it and no finding under any of them, and the drawer eleven inches deep was shut, and about nine leaves in it had no head over them, and nobody in that room took a paper out of it and nobody has ever.

**And the cost of that Thursday is two roads with four men on them that have not written to anybody in nine counties, nine days of a man's legs that went on no return in any series, and five lines of a letter that a copyist would not read out loud in a market street, are not in it and none of them has a price.**

**a tallow candle at 4, a leather strap at 6, a carter's tally at 23, a sheet of foolscap at 24, a hoop for a pail at 28, a seal box at 30, a bushel of lime at 31, a wicket latch at 32, a string of chalk at 34, and a printed notice at 40.** Four, six, twenty-three, twenty-four, twenty-eight, thirty, thirty-one, thirty-two, thirty-four and forty is two hundred and fifty-two pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and two hundred and fifty-two pence is five marks and twelve over.** The man of about thirty-four with the satchel took the two sheets that had come back off the boards and put them inside his coat and then took out a stub of pencil and wrote the day and the two roads on the back of a sheet, and did not write it in a book, and said out loud that this was the third time in about four years he had written something on the back of a sheet and that he was not going to be the man who keeps it in a book, and about nine people in that market street watched a man of about thirty-four write on the back of a piece of paper and about four of them understood why and about five did not.
