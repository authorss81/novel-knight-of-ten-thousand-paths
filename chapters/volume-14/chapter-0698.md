# Chapter 0698

**Hearthmonth the twenty-sixth, YR 321, a Sunday, no market, the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting, a man of thirty-two who has gone out on that made road every morning since the first of Longlight and did not go out on it at about the sixth hour this morning, and four people who have each heard a piece of that.**

A Sunday is not a market morning and the made road out of that market town was empty at about the sixth hour for the fourth Sunday running, and four people in nine counties have said that an empty made road on a Sunday is an ordinary thing and about five have said that it has been empty since the twenty-fifth of Hearthmonth and that a woman of about fifty-one who keeps a lodging house four streets up noticed it and that a woman who keeps four beds notices an empty road sooner than anybody in nine counties.

Nobody thanked anybody and the room over that weigh-house was shut behind its own door and had been shut since noon on the Saturday, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a hearing with no bench in it that stands in a street on the first of every month has now not sat for a fortnight on any day a person could have used, and that about four people have said that this is the cost of a month and about five have said that a hearing that can only be used on a market morning was never a hearing and only cost the days it was not there.

The walk out is four and a half hours on a good road and it has been that walk since the middle of Harvestmonth and before that it was four days of carting with a delegation on it, and the road itself is two miles and about nine hundred paces of hard sand with eleven houses on it and about nine hundred people living along it, and every one of those nine hundred people knows which of them goes out on it at six in the morning because there has been one man doing it since the first of Longlight and a road of eleven houses has a very short memory for anything else.

Nobody thanked anybody and four people in nine counties have said since that the whole of what a person notices about four and a half years of one man going out of a gate at six in the morning is that nothing happened, and about five have said that a road is the only place in nine counties where nothing happening for four and a half years is a thing a person can see, and that nobody has ever thought of that before and that a woman who keeps four beds thought of it on a Sunday.

Then the three times, and the third of them is the one this account gives in full, and the first two are reported because nine people in nine counties have said since that a thing a man did three times is a fact about a man and a thing a man did once is a thing that happened.

The first was on the nineteenth of Hearthmonth, a Sunday, at about the sixth hour, and he got to the sixth mile stone at about the eleventh hour and put his hand on the edging of stone on the high side of that road about nine paces past it and could not take his hand off, and stood there about as long as it takes to turn a cart, and then took it off and walked back and got into the market town at about the eighth hour on the Monday morning.

Nobody thanked him and four people in nine counties have said since that the first time is always the time a man tells people about and that the telling of it is the worst part of it, and about five have said that a man does not tell people about a thing like that and that the four people who heard about this one on a Sunday all heard it from somebody else and not from him.

The second was on the twenty-first of Hearthmonth, a Tuesday, at about the fifth hour, and he got to the seventh mile stone at about the eleventh hour, and at the seventh mile the high side of that made road stops being a bank and becomes a wall-foot, and the stone set in the line along the top of it goes up to about the height of a knee, and he got his hand on the knee-high stone and could not go past it, and stood there, and came back and got into the market town at about the eighth hour on the Wednesday morning.

Then the third was this morning, and this is the whole of it.

He went out at about the fourth hour on foot with his stick in his right hand and got to the ninth mile at about the eleventh hour, and nine people in nine counties have said that nine miles on foot is about four hours and a half in any weather and that he has done it since the middle of Harvestmonth, and that on this morning he had about as long as it takes to turn a cart on the ninth mile for a thing that has stopped him three times.

Nobody thanked him and the made road at nine miles runs on the shoulder of a hill and is a good road, and on the high side of it there is a wall about nine feet high with stone coping on it and a gate in it about four feet wide, and the gate was shut, and behind the wall there is a roof and a chimney and the chimney had smoke coming off it on that morning, and nine people in nine counties have said that it is four days in seven and that this was one of the four.

The coping on that wall is about nine inches wide and it is set in a line and there are about nine feet of it visible from the road, and the gate is a plank gate with iron straps and no latch on the outside, and four people in nine counties have said that a gate with no latch on the outside is a gate that is shut from the inside and about five have said that a gate with no latch on the outside is a gate that is not shut.

Nobody thanked him and the roof behind the wall is the roof of a building of two bays and it is higher than the wall by about four feet, and the chimney is at the west end of it and it is a stub, and on that morning there was a thin line of smoke off it going straight up in still air, and at two hundred yards a thin line of smoke going straight up in still air is a thing a person can see with the corner of an eye without turning the head, and that is the whole of the reason nine counties never looked at it.

Nobody thanked him and nobody in nine counties has ever been through that gate and five people in nine counties have said that a shut gate on a wall is a thing nobody looks at twice and about four have said that he looked at it four times in three days.

Then he got to about two hundred yards short of it.

Nobody said anything and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the only account anybody has of where it stops and that it is a distance of about two hundred yards off a gate nobody has ever been through, and about five have said that it is not a distance at all and that it is a thing about a man.

Here is the whole of what happened, and nothing in it is a story.

His left foot would not go forward. He put it forward once and it did not go. He put it forward a second time and it did not go. He was standing on nine feet of wet grass on the low side of that made road, with the made road between him and the wall-foot and the coping on the wall-foot at about the height of his knee, and the gate four foot wide and shut, and about two hundred yards off. He was not tired. His right leg is bad and it was not the right leg. He was not afraid and four people in nine counties have said that he has said so and that nobody has ever believed him and that nobody has ever asked him to say it again. He waited on that grass about as long as it takes to turn a cart and he tried it a third time and it did not go, and he did not try a fourth, and the reason he did not try a fourth is that he has been the man who goes at everything for four and a half years and a man who tries a thing nine times is a man who wants it to work.

Nobody thanked him and while he was standing on that grass he put his stick down in it, because a man holding a stick cannot put a foot forward as far, and four people in nine counties have said since that he put a stick down in a wet field in order to try to walk two hundred yards and that that is the only account of that morning anybody in nine counties believes.

Nobody thanked him and he did not shout and he did not call and there was nobody within four miles of him to shout to, and nine people in nine counties have said that a man who finds out that a thing is closed does not generally make a noise about it and about five have said that this is not general and that they do not know what he would have done because they were not there.

Then he turned round and walked back down the made road on his own and got into the market town at about the sixth hour and went into a house four streets up the market street and did not go out again that day.

Nobody thanked him and nobody was angry with him and nine people in nine counties have said since that nobody in nine counties was angry with him, and that about four people thought he had stopped and about five thought he was unwell, and that not one person in nine counties thought to ask him either of those two questions out loud, and that the not asking is the only thing anybody has done for him.

Then he got up off the bed and the bed had not been slept in since the Thursday, and he washed his boots and put them outside the door because that is what he has done for four and a half years, and he cut a loaf and did not eat it and put the loaf on the board, and he stood at his own window and looked down four hundred paces at a shut room with a door on a lane and did not go down the lane.

Nobody thanked him and four people in nine counties have said since that a man stood at a window for about nine minutes on a Sunday and looked at four hundred paces and did not go down it, and that this is the second time anybody in nine counties has seen him not do a thing he has done every day for four and a half years, and that the first time was in the rain outside a shut door in Wolfmonth, and about five have said that the two are not the same and that a man who stands at a window is a man who has decided something.

Nobody thanked anybody and a person in that lane asked him a question out loud through a window at about the second hour and nine people in nine counties heard about it, and the question was whether he was going up the road on the Monday.

“**Are you going up the road on the Monday?**”

Nobody said anything and he said six words, and four people in that lane heard them and five heard six words through a window.

“**I am going up the road.**”

Nobody thanked him and nobody argued with him and nine people in nine counties have said that this is the whole of what happened on the twenty-sixth of Hearthmonth in the mind of the man it happened to.

Then it got about, and it got about the way a thing gets about in a market town of about two thousand people on a Sunday, which is that four people each heard a piece of it and put the four pieces nowhere, and there was no market for the four pieces to meet in.

Nobody thanked anybody and the woman of about fifty-one who keeps a lodging house four streets up heard it at the second hour from the woman of about forty-nine who keeps a kitchen and four beds on the made road, who had heard it from the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up, who had heard it from a boy of about eleven who lives at the first house in the lane and who sees a man go out of a gate at six in the morning four days in seven because he is the only person in that lane who is awake at that hour.

Nobody thanked anybody and four people in nine counties have said since that a fact about nine counties has never once in four and a half years travelled four steps by four mouths, and about five have said that this one did and that nobody has ever been able to find out why.

Nobody thanked anybody and about nine people in that market town knew that the man of thirty-two had not gone out on the made road on the twenty-sixth of Hearthmonth, and about nine counties had not been told, and nobody in nine counties has been told since, and four people in nine counties have said that a fact that four people know and that nobody has been told is not a fact anybody can act on, and about five have said that it is the only kind this county has.

Then the woman of about fifty-one who keeps that lodging house came down the lane at about the fourth hour and stood in it and said the true thing about a man who cannot come, and she said it out loud, in the open, to about nine people who were out of doors on a Sunday for no reason anybody could establish, and nobody thanked her and nobody tried to make it better.

“**I have kept four beds for about nineteen years and I have had about nine hundred nights of people come in off that made road and go up it in the morning, and I have never once put a name to the four, and I was not going to start on a Sunday.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that lane had heard a woman who keeps four beds say that she has never named them and five had heard the number nine hundred.

“**So here is what I heard this morning, and I am going to say it in the open because there are nine people standing in this lane and nine people in nine counties have said since that a thing said in a lane is not said. I heard that the one person in nine counties who goes is not going. And I keep four beds. And there is nothing in my trade that fills a bed because a man does not go.**”

Nobody thanked her and four people in that lane had heard a woman say a thing that is not comfort, and five had heard a woman describe the shape of a business.

“**A bed is for a person who has come off a road. There is not a bed for a person who has not come off a road, and there is not a bed for a person who has not been able to come off a road, and those are two different people and I keep four of them and there is no fifth thing in this house. I have had them full since the middle of Harvestmonth and I am going to keep them full, and you are not in my trade, and there is nothing I can do about you, and I have not come down this lane to do anything about you.**”

Nobody said anything and nobody thanked her and about nine people in that lane had heard a lodging-house keeper say that she had come down a lane to say a thing that cannot be done anything with, and about four of them had understood all of it and five had heard a woman be cruel on purpose.

Nobody thanked anybody and she went back up the lane and shut her door and did not thank him and he did not ask her for anything, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the twenty-sixth of Hearthmonth was the day the man who goes stopped going and that nine people knew it on the evening and that nobody in nine counties was told and that nobody in nine counties has been told since, and that a county that knows a thing four times over and writes none of it down is the same county that has ten questions in a register and no finding under any of them.

Nobody thanked anybody and at about the seventh hour the made road out of that market town emptied in the ordinary way, and about nine people in that lane had come out of their doors because a lane at seven on a Sunday in Hearthmonth is where a market town goes, and nobody in that lane asked him anything, and nobody in that lane mentioned the made road, and about four people in nine counties have said that a lane that does not mention a thing is a lane that has decided not to mention a thing, and about five have said that a lane on a Sunday evening is nine people standing about eight feet apart and about nine hundred different things being not said.

Nobody thanked anybody and nothing was signed and nothing was written down anywhere, and that lane's room was still bolted when the daylight went, and on the Monday a man of thirty-two came down that lane at about the eighth hour with his stick in his right hand and went up the made road at about the ninth hour as he has gone every morning since the first of Longlight, and about nine people in that lane had seen him go out and five had not asked him where.

**And the cost of that Sunday is four and a half years of a man being the one who goes, and one morning when he did not, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
