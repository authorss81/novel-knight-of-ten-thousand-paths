# Chapter 0240

**The seventeenth day of Hearthmonth, YR 315, and about the sixth hour, and a room in a town of about four thousand people with a bench in it that is too short for him and a table that is the right height for a man of thirty-two and not for a man of thirty-two with a limp, and a notebook that has been in a coat and on a chair and in the hand of a boy of fifteen for seven days and is now in the inside pocket of a coat, and the sun is about four inches above a roof in Weir Street and is not a light that stands anywhere, and about four people in that town do not know that a man of thirty-two with a limp is going to try to write something down this morning and that he is going to get about half of it down and leave the other half.**

He got up on the ninth day, which is the seventeenth day of a month in a county that keeps two market days a week, and he was in a bed in Weir Street for seven days across a market morning and a market day, and neither of them is a thing anybody in that house mentioned to him. A woman of about sixty who has been the keeper of two rooms over a weigh-house for nineteen years has said that a house that works does not count its own days, and she said it once, to a carter of thirty, and he did not ask her what she meant and is not going to.

That is the whole of what the fever cost, and a week of Stage 3 is a week of somebody else holding a ferrier's stick and a notebook and a coat with four sheets of paper in it, and a boy of fifteen did all three of those for seven days without being asked and did not open the notebook, and a man of forty-seven said the word tools about it once in a kitchen and the word has not been used again in that county and about four hundred people in that town do not know what it meant.

**And the two unreconciled numbers are still on a page in that notebook and he has not written a figure on it.** A man of thirty-two with a limp has now been away from this town for a count of days that he did not keep, and there is no way at all of asking him what the count was, and the two numbers on the page in the notebook are about four hundred miles from any clock that either of them could be read against, and he has got to them five times without writing a figure and has now been away long enough that getting to it a sixth time would be about a different thing.

The notebook came out at about the sixth hour on the seventeenth day and he put it on the table and turned it to a page, and there are eleven lines on the page above it with no words in them, and the eleventh of them is a man of fifty-two on a shift nineteen years old, and he could not stop seeing it in a city of about nine thousand people and he cannot stop seeing it in a town of about four thousand. A man of thirty-two with a limp has come home from somewhere and can still see the eleventh of eleven lines that are not in a language, and the eleventh of them is a man he met once for about nine minutes in a yard.

He wrote one line. That is the whole of what he wrote on the seventeenth day of Hearthmonth, YR 315, and it took him about four minutes and he wrote it in his own hand, and it is the fourth page he has filled in in four years and it is not the page with no hour and no name in it, and it is about a frame and not about a figure.

**And that is the whole of it, and there is no figure on it, and there is not going to be one.** He wrote, *a city that keeps one hour standing has a frame that does not run away from this one, and that is a claim about the length of the interval and not about the length of the warning, and the length of the interval is a conversion somebody made and I cannot check it, and the claim is either the whole of what happened to me or it is nothing and there is no way at all to tell from here which.*

And then he put the pen down and did not write anything else. A man of thirty-two with a limp wrote one line in a notebook on the seventeenth day of Hearthmonth and the line is about a thing that stands in one place all the time in a place about four hundred miles off, and he has been asked about it once by a woman of about sixty who owns two rooms over a weigh-house, and he told her the same thing he has written down in about the same words, and he has not told her what it is about, and she did not ask twice.

**And there is a warning in a file in a county two hundred miles off that was printed in an office's hand two years ago and has said three weeks every week since and has not changed, and he has not got a copy of it and has never had a copy of it, and a man of thirty-two with a limp did not write a figure on the seventeenth day of Hearthmonth, YR 315 and is not going to.** A man who has been away for a count of days he cannot name came home and wrote down why and not how much, and about four hundred people in that town do not know that he was away at all, and the one person in that town who could have worked it out has not been asked and is not going to be.

**And a man of forty-seven came to Weir Street on the twentieth day of Hearthmonth, YR 315, and stood in the kitchen for about half an hour and said the four things, and did not ask one question.** He has now been in a kitchen in Weir Street twice in about a fortnight and has said the four things both times and has asked nothing either time, and a woman of about sixty has said that a man who does not ask is either the most careful man in a county or the only one in it who has nothing to ask, and that she has not worked out which and has not asked him.

**And the seventh line of a page of foolscap has said *a person* since a lane four hundred miles off, and he took the page out of the coat on the eighteenth day and looked at the seventh line and did not finish it, and that is the fourth time he has looked at that line and the fourth time he has not finished it.** A man of thirty-two with a limp has been back in his own county for a day and a night and has taken a page out of a coat twice and has not written under the same line once, and a man of thirty with a barrow on a hill road four hundred miles off said in about eleven words that a man who has not looked at the first six lines has stopped expecting to, and nobody in that county has ever met the man with the barrow and is not going to.

The four words he has had ready since the sixth day of a count of days he does not have are still ready, and they have been ready for a count of days he could give in one number and could not, and he did not say them, and nobody in that county knew he had them, and a man of forty-seven came to Weir Street on the seventh day of the fever and on the twentieth day of Hearthmonth and did not ask him one question either time, and a man of forty-seven has now been asked twice why he does not ask and has said the same four things both times, and about four hundred people in a town of about four thousand people have decided that a man of forty-seven is a careful man and that nobody has ever worked out what he is careful about.

**And the eleven lines are still eleven lines and there are no words in them and the eleventh of them is a man of fifty-two on a shift nineteen years old, and there is no column in four hundred years of anybody's book for what became of a woman and a boy who went out on one of eleven roads about a year between the going out and the coming back, and a man of sixty-one said so before he was asked, on the twenty-sixth day of a count of days that a city about four hundred miles off keeps and that this county does not, and he did not ask, and the not asking took four seconds, and he has been back in his own county for a day and a night and has not asked anybody anything about it and is not going to.**

**And the fourth bay of the reach was being pointed on the eighteenth day and the joint on its parapet is a foot and a half short, and about forty men were on the reach on the two market mornings and the road was open and the salt was coming over the first bay, and nobody measured the joint and nobody measured a line in the third course about nine inches long under about half an inch of lime that has not moved.** A man of thirty-two with a limp went up four hundred yards of road to the reach on the eighteenth day and stood on the gravel with a stick for about an hour and a half and did not get on it, and nobody said a word to him, and about nine men carried hod after hod past a man standing still, and a man of thirty-four with lime is not on that reach and has not been for a year and nobody has said why.

**And the four sheets of paper went into a press on Weir Street on the nineteenth day of Hearthmonth, YR 315.** A man of thirty-two with a limp carried a set of four sheets of printed paper four hundred yards up a road and put them in a press in a room with a bench-board in it, and a boy of fifteen was at the bench-board and did not ask what was in them, and a man of forty-seven has never been in that room and has never been asked to be.

**And a bench of nine in that county has not been asked anything since the crossing out and is not going to be asked anything in this block.** A man of forty-seven is on that bench because a man in a market asked who was going and he put his hand up, and he has not used his vote since the day he was elected and did not use it then, and he has been the witness at two ends of two crossings in a year and is now the witness at the end of a week in a kitchen in Weir Street, and he is paid fourpence a week out of a private box and not out of the chest and nobody in that hall has ever said anything about it.

**And a woman of about thirty with a pail came to Weir Street on the nineteenth day of Hearthmonth, YR 315 and said one sentence at a door and went on, and the sentence was that somebody had been asking after a man with a stick in two counties and that nobody had told her anything and that she was saying it because the asking had stopped, and a woman of about sixty heard it and did not repeat it, and about four people in that county have said since that the asking stopped on the nineteenth day of Hearthmonth, YR 315 and that nobody four hundred miles off has ever been told that it stopped.**

**And a man of thirty-four with lime is not on that reach and has not been for a year, and a carter of thirty has not been up a bank since, and a man of forty-seven has said the four things three times in about a year in three counties and has never once said where he got them, and a woman of about sixty in Weir Street has read them off her own wall four times in forty years, and nobody in any of those places has worked out that the same four sentences are standing in a kitchen in a fen county and in the mouth of a man in a hut and on a page in a city of about nine thousand people, and that they are not the same four sentences and that the three that are the same are the same by accident.**

**And about nine hundred feet of paving and a quay and about two hundred and forty strips of paper are four hundred miles off in a city of about nine thousand people, and about nine of the strips are in nine bundles of cord and reed on a shelf in a room off a lane that goes down to the quay, and one of the nine of them is a strip that came back into that city on the thirty-fifth day of a count of days that nobody in this county is keeping, with a mark in the corner of it that nobody in that city put there, and it is on the shelf with the other eight of its kind and it is not counted and it has not been looked at since the seventh hour of the thirty-fifth day, and about four hundred people in that city do not know that anything at all has come back into it.**

**And a man of about thirty-one with a satchel is about four days off a town two hundred miles off this county with a sheet in it, and the sheet has four places in it that are ruled for a figure and all four of them are empty, and the object column has a place name in it and a word in front of the place name, and the sixth column has no head on it and is the same width as the other five, and he has carried about two thousand sheets of that series in six years and has never once known what is in the four places and has not asked and has said so out loud twice in about a week, in a yard and in a room, in the tone of a man saying a price, and about four hundred doors on his list and about four hundred doors on another list in this county are the same four hundred doors, and nobody in either county has ever worked out how many.**

And a man of thirty-two with a limp put a notebook in the inside pocket of a coat at about the seventh hour of the eighteenth day of Hearthmonth, YR 315, and had eleven lines in it and one new line, and a page with a seventh line that says a person and is not finished, and a stick he cannot lean on, and he put a notebook in a coat and did not look at the first six lines of the page, and the road is open and the salt is coming over the first bay and the fourth bay is being pointed and the joint on its parapet is a foot and a half short, and a bell with forty-one rivets in it was rung at the fourth hour and a half that morning by a boy and a carpenter and not by anybody else, and nobody in that county has ever seen a mark in a child's hand, and one is about four hundred miles off on a shelf in a room with nine bundles of cord and reed on it, and it is still there.
