# Chapter 0244

**The fourth day of Goatmonth, YR 315, a Thursday, and a market day, and the second hour and the fourth hour and the seventh hour, and a room with a stove in it in a provincial town two hundred miles from a fen county, and a returns press, and a file on a shelf with the working papers on it, and about four people in that building know what the fourth of Goatmonth, YR 315 is for and about nine hundred in it do not, and one of the four is a woman of thirty-three who has been afraid of a column for eleven years and has not been asked about it and is not going to be.**

A return goes out to nine counties and about nine come back. The one that goes out on the fourth of Goatmonth, YR 315 is not one of the ordinary nine, and about four people in that building have said since that a return that is not one of the ordinary nine is a return that has been written on by a room and not by a press, and that there are about eleven of those in about nine years and that nobody has ever written a rule about them.

It is a sheet of the office's own printed series and it is ruled in six columns, five of them with heads, and the heads are *Object*, *Date*, *Person having knowledge*, *Keeper*, and *How often*, and the sixth has no head on it and is the same width as the other five, and about four of the six are ruled for a figure.

**The object column has a name in it that this office made up about a hundred years ago and has never sent a form to, and a county beside it that is in a table in a press in that same building, and nobody in that room has ever put the two of them together on one sheet before and about four people in that building have said since that a district is a set of market towns and that this office has now got a district with a market town in it that it did not put there and that the district is on the sheet because a sheet came in with it on.**

The name is not a market town and the county is a county of fens and the market town in that county is this one. A man of about fifty who is a clerk of the press said that in about nineteen words at about the third hour and it is the only time in about thirty-one years that he has said anything in that room that is about a word and not about a form, and about four people in that building have said that he said it to a sheet and not to a room and that a man who works in a press for thirty-one years talks about paper and that this was the first time one of them had heard him talk about a place.

---

**And in the second of the four places ruled for a figure there is a figure, and it is a figure of persons in respect of a district, and it is the figure this office has been keeping in a column with a head of its own since the year after the Sundering, and nobody in that room connected it to anything.**

It came out of a column. That is the whole of it. The column has a head on it and the head is the office's own construction and the head is not the head that a sheet of good paper about the size of a broadside is ruled with, and the two heads are nine hundred feet apart in this province and were in the same building for about a year and a half and nobody in that building has ever taken the two of them out together.

A county of fens is on the table in a press. There is a market town in it and the market town is a place where a thing is bought and sold, and the market town sends a roll once in the season on paper that is good and folded and soft at the folds, and the roll has a figure on it, and the office has been copying that figure into the same column against the same county every year since the year after the Sundering, and about four people in that building have said since that this is the only figure of persons in this province that has never been in an offer, and that an offer is a thing a room makes and a return is a thing a room can be held to, and that nobody in that building has ever been asked why and that about two of them have thought about it perhaps twice a year.

**And the number of persons this office would not print on an offer was asked for of a county in a return twice, about two years ago and again since, and it did not come, and an office that will not print a number of persons cannot show that any person has not been instructed.**

And on the fourth of Goatmonth, YR 315, at about the fourth hour, the Office of Provincial Registration printed it. On a return. Against a district that does not appear on any of its own earlier forms. In a column with a head on it. In a fair hand, and the hand was not the hand of the man of about fifty and was not the hand of the woman of about twenty-nine, and it was the hand of a woman of thirty-three who fills in that *Keeper* column about two hundred times a season and who did it in about a minute and a quarter and did not look up while she did it and read it back afterwards, and about four people in that building have said that she read it back and that the reading was slower and not better, and that a woman of twenty-nine who is a clerk of the returns has been in that room for about six years and has never once seen anybody else read anything back slower.

**And nobody in that room put the second of those two figures beside the first of them, and about four people in that building have said since that a man who made an offer in a sheet of good paper about two years ago and had it refused in four sheets in a bag in the ordinary post, and a woman who has been afraid of a column for eleven years, and a clerk of the press who has entered about eleven thousand forms in thirty-one years, all worked in the same room for about a year and a half, and that the offer is in a press in a county four hundred miles off and a copy of it is on a shelf in that county with a number of this office against it and nothing at all against a person, and that the figure went out of that room on the fourth of Goatmonth, YR 315 in a bag, and that the two of them are about two hundred miles apart and neither of the two people who put either of them there has ever heard of the other one.**

---

**And a woman of thirty-three said one thing in that room on that Thursday, and it is about nineteen words, and it is the only thing she has said in about eleven years that was not about a column, and about four people in that building have said since that she said it to the sheet and not to the room and that her want has not changed in eleven years and is not about the man the offer was made to and never was.**

“**A figure of persons in a column is the only kind of paper in this kingdom that cannot be refused, and I have been afraid of this column since I was twenty-two, and I would like it noticed that I said it and that the figure went out anyway and that noticing it is what I have instead of stopping it.**”

Nobody agreed with her and nobody refused her, and about four people in that building have said since that the not agreeing and the not refusing is the office entire and that it has never once had to decide anything because a thing left the building anyway, and a thing in a bag on the ordinary post does not need a room to agree to it.

And then she said the other half, and it is four or five words, and about four people in that building have said that a woman who has been afraid of a column for eleven years said these four or five words on a Thursday morning and then wrote a figure in the second of four places and did not put a name in anything, and that the four or five words are the whole of what she wants and have been the whole of what she wants since she was twenty-two.

“**Nobody enters a family by a name nobody entered.**”

And a man of about fifty who is a clerk of the press said that there is no column in that form for a name, and that there has not been one for two years, and that the fifth column has a head on it and the head has been the same for two years and nobody in that building has ever taken a decision in writing about changing it, and that a woman of thirty-three has been the person who fills in that column about two hundred times a season for eleven years and that she is right and that changing the head is not hers to do, and that he is not going to say anything else about it and has not been asked to.

Nobody said anything else about it. And about four people in that building have said since that a man of about fifty said the true thing about a column in about nine words in a room with a stove in it and that a woman of thirty-three did not thank him for it and did not tell him so, and that those are the two things that happened in that room on the fourth of Goatmonth, YR 315 and that about nine hundred people in that building did not know either of them had happened.

**And a note of a woman of thirty-three is in that building and has been since the sixth of Embermonth in the year before, and it is face down in a book on the second shelf, and about four people in that building have said since that it was written in about four minutes in a room with a stove in it and that nobody in that room asked what it was and that the not asking was in that room for about half a minute and that it was not entered anywhere, and that there is no book in that building in which a not asking could be entered.**

She wrote it in about four minutes and it is about nineteen words and it says that a column which asks for a name and is given a name is a column somebody has filled in, and that a column which asks for a name and is given nothing is not a hole in a document and that it is the finding of the sheet it is on, and that the reason it is under the rule and not in the column is that a rule exists to be ruled across.

Nobody in that building has asked her what became of the family. About four people in that room on the fourth of Goatmonth, YR 315 have said that a woman of thirty-three has been afraid of a column for eleven years and that the eleven years are the eleven years since a roll came down from a county in the west and she filled in a column on it out of a habit, and that a house went out of a record in about four seconds in a fair hand in a room with a stove in it, and that she found it out about a year afterwards and has never told anybody in that building and has not been asked and is not going to be.

And the note is face down in a book and about four people in that building have said that a woman who turns a leaf over is a woman who has made a decision about what comes next, and that she has not turned it over in about five months, and that nobody in that building has asked her to, and that a man of about fifty who entered about eleven thousand forms in thirty-one years has walked past that shelf about four times a day for about five months and has not once put his hand out to it.

---

**And there was a market in that town on that Thursday and about four hundred people were in it, and this office is about four streets from it, and about nine hundred people in that building have not been in that market in about four years and about four of them go.**

There was salt and fish and peat and one load of glass and four barrels of something out of the low country in that market on the fourth of Goatmonth, YR 315, and a man of about thirty-one with a satchel was two hundred miles from it and was not in it, and about four hundred doors on his list are about four hundred doors on a list in a county four hundred miles off, and a man of forty-six who has left about three thousand sheets in eight years wrote the same sentence on the back of his own list twice, about four inches apart, in the same hand, and has said he will not be able to explain them to anybody in about a year.

And about four people in that building have said since that this office has never once in about nine years sent a man to a market, and that about four hundred and nine parish rolls come down once in the season on paper that is good and folded and soft at the folds, and that a roll is a list of names and a district is a set of market towns and that this building has spent about a hundred years counting where people can be counted, and that a man of sixty-one with a satchel has been in it since the middle of last year and has been to a market about four times and has not bought anything and has not been asked and does not buy anything.

---

**And a hearing in that province has a companion file, and the hearing stands, and about four people in that building have said since that a thing which was entered for a date and had nobody in it is still entered for the date and that a file has been opened beside it and not against it.**

There was a hearing entered in this province for a day in Rainmonth for a delegation that cannot arrive, and it was heard with nobody in it, and it was not adjourned and it was not refused, and the delegation was not sent for and is not going to be sent for, and about four people in that building have said since that a thing that stands is a thing that is still waiting and that a waiting thing is the only kind of thing in that building that has a date on it, and that a file has been opened beside it and not against it, and that nobody in that room has read the delegation and that about four of them have read the file.

**And the file is a file of one sheet, and the sheet has a district on it, and the district is the one the return went out against, and about four people in that building have said since that a hearing entered for a delegation that cannot arrive now has a file with a district in it, and that the district came out of a return, and that the return was printed by a room and the file was opened by a room and neither room was told about the other one, and that this is the first time in about nine years that anything in that building has joined a thing to a thing by accident.**

And a man of about fifty said one thing about it and it was about eleven words and it was the last thing said in that room about a district on that Thursday, and about four people in that building have said since that he said it to himself and that nobody answered it and that it is the fifth thing in about a hundred years that anybody in that building has said about a district and that four of the five were said in that room and one of the five was said in a room over a cross in a valley about four hundred miles off by a man of fifty-eight who is a clerk and who does not know this office exists.

“**A district is a set of market towns and I do not know what a district is.**”

And the bag went out on the ordinary post at about the seventh hour of that Thursday, and a bag in the ordinary post is the only instrument in this kingdom that everybody trusts, and about four people in that building have said that this office is careful about what it prints and not careful about what it sends, and that those are two different dangers, and that the second one is nine hundred years older than the first, and that about four people in that building have been saying both of those things for years and that a woman of thirty-three said four or five words on the morning it went and then wrote a figure and did not put a name in anything.
