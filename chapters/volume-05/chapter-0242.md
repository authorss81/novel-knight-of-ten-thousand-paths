# Chapter 0242

**The twenty-sixth day of Hearthmonth, YR 315, a Wednesday, and the second hour, and a room with a stove in it in a provincial town two hundred miles from a fen county, and a press room off it with a drawer in it that has a word in it that the office printed two years ago and has never used, and a tray with five things in it that came out of a passage at about the first hour that morning, and four people in that building do not know that anything happened in that room on that Wednesday, and about nine of them will find out in about a year and not before, and one of the nine is not going to be told.**

It was a month end. That is the whole of why anything in that building ever gets read, and about four people in it have said that in about nine years, and the machine of it is that a form that is going somewhere is not read, because a form that is going somewhere has somebody else's name on the other side of it in a sense, and a form that has come back has nobody's name on either side of it, and a clerk of the press who has kept his own book for thirty-one years has been the man who reads what has come back for about eleven years and has never told anybody why he does it and has not been asked.

He had a tray out on the bench at about the second hour and there were five things in it and he did four of them in about nine minutes.

**A return with a figure in it went into the returns press. A return with a space empty and four lines beside it went into the returns press. A letter from a county about a boundary that has not moved in about nine years went on a spike that has had about four hundred things on it since the year before last. And then there were two left and one of them was a sheet with a place in its object column that is not a market town, and one of them was four sheets of the office's own printed series with nothing filled in on any of the five columns that have heads, and they had been in the tray since the sixth of Embermonth in the year before and nobody had ever read them.**

He read the four first. That is a thing about a man of about fifty and four people in that building have said it, and it is that he read the ones that had been in the tray longest, and that he has never in thirty-one years been able to give a reason for it in words that anybody has written down, and that in about nine years a woman of about twenty-nine asked him why and he said that a thing that has been in a tray five months is a thing somebody has decided not to look at, and that a thing somebody has decided not to look at is the only kind in that building that is interesting.

**And on the sixth line of each of the four, which is the line with no head on it and the same width as the other five, there was writing.**

A county. A name that was not a market town. A number. And a line of words, four or five of them, that say that a body may be sued and may sue, and that it has a door, and that the door is on a lane.

And four dates, and the four dates are five months apart, and the earliest of the four is about five months back and the latest of the four is about a fortnight back, and the four of them are in a fair hand and the hand is the same hand in all four, and four people in that building have said since that a fair hand is a thing anybody can be trusted to be able to recognise and that a man of about fifty recognised it in about a second and a half and that he then went and stood at a window for about two minutes and that nobody has asked him about the two minutes and that he has not offered.

---

**And what he did with it was not a thing anybody in that room was told about, and it is the whole of the twenty-sixth day of Hearthmonth, YR 315 in the Office of Provincial Registration, and it took about four minutes, and four people in that building have written it down afterwards in their own words and the four accounts do not agree and none of the four is a copy of the others.**

He did not enter it. That is the first thing and it is the thing he said out loud at about the third hour, once, to a woman of about twenty-nine who is a clerk of the returns, and four people in that building have said that he said it in the tone of a man reading out the head of a column.

“**A line with no head is not a column and is not entered and is not kept, and it has been that way since the form was made, and I have entered about eleven thousand of these forms in thirty-one years and I have never entered one of those lines and I am not going to start on a Wednesday with a man of sixty in the passage.**”

“Then where does it go.”

“**In a file.**”

And a woman of about twenty-nine said that a file is a place a thing goes when somebody has decided what it is, and that he had just decided that a thing in a line with no head on it is a file, and that four people in that building would think about that sentence for a long time and that one of them had already thought about it and had not said so.

He put the four sheets in a file. He wrote nothing on the wrapper. And four people in that building have said since that a man who has been a clerk of the press for thirty-one years and who has entered about eleven thousand forms in that time has never in thirty-one years put a thing in a file that he had not been told to put in a file, and that he did it on a Wednesday in about four minutes, and that the four minutes are the only reason anybody in that building knows it happened at all.

---

**And then the fifth thing came off the bench, and it was the sheet that came in two days before, and the fifth thing is a place that is not a market town, and a man of about fifty had a table out of a press in about two minutes and it is the only table in that building anybody has opened since the year it was made.**

The table is a sheet and it was made in about the year after the Sundering by a man in that building who had a table and a list of the market towns, and about forty districts are on it, and each of them is a set of market towns and a market town is a place where a thing is bought and sold, and every one of the about forty has a county against it and about nine counties in this province are in that column and there are about four hundred and nine parish rolls and the parish rolls are not on that table and were never on that table.

He put his finger down the column of counties and then down the column of market towns and he did it twice, and the first time he did it in about a minute and the second time in about four minutes, and the second time was slower and was not better, and that has been the practice of a few other rooms in this building for a long time and he has never heard of it in any of them.

**And the name in the object column of the fifth thing is not on that table, and the county on the sixth line of the four sheets is on that table, and the two of those are the whole of what he learned on the twenty-sixth day of Hearthmonth, YR 315.**

He said it out loud twice, and the second time he said it to nobody.

“**That is a place I have never sent a form to. And that is a county I have sent four hundred forms to, and the forms went out and about nine of every four hundred come back, and eight of the nine come back with a figure in them.**”

Nobody in that room said anything. A man of about fifty put the table back in the press in about a minute and a half and it is the only table in that building that has been opened since the year it was made, and four people in that building have said that it took him a minute and a half to put back a sheet of paper and that a man who works in a press for thirty-one years can put a sheet back in about four seconds and that he did not, and that nobody has asked him about the minute and a half and that he has not offered.

---

**And there were three sheets in that room from one place, and two of them had a figure in them and one of them had nothing, and the two that had a figure were in a pile together, and the man of about fifty looked at the two figures for about a minute and did not add them and did not ask why they were different.**

He did not add them. That is the fact of the twenty-sixth day of Hearthmonth, YR 315 in that room, and four people in that building have said since that a man who does an addition all his life looked at two numbers for a minute and did not put them together, and that the ordinary reason for a man not to add two numbers is that he has not been asked, and that about two or three of them think that is not the reason and one of them thinks it is.

**And he said one thing out loud about it, and it is about nine words, and it is the same shape of thing a man of about thirty-one said in a yard about four hundred miles off in about a fortnight and a man of forty-six said in a market in this province two years before that, and the three of them have never been in a room and two of them do not know the other's name.**

“**Two figures out of one place. I am not going to add them and I am not going to ask.**”

And a woman of about twenty-nine said that a return comes round twice and that the newer of two is the one that goes on, and that this is a rule and not a judgement and that four people in that building have said that a rule and not a judgement is the only kind of thing this office has ever had, and that the newer of those two figures went on and the older one went into a press and was not looked at again, and that neither of the two men who said the nine words has ever been told what the other one said, and that about four hundred doors on one man's list and about four hundred doors on another man's list are the same four hundred doors and nobody in either county or in this province has ever worked out how many.

**And nobody in that room multiplied the four either, and that is a thing about a man of about fifty and about a woman of about twenty-nine and neither of them has said it out loud.** The four places on each of the two sheets are ruled for a figure and each of them has the same figure in it, and a figure repeated four times on a printed sheet is a figure that has been entered four times by somebody who was asked four times, and four people in that building have said that a clerk who counted the four out loud and said the total would be doing the one thing in that room that nobody has ever done, and that a figure is a figure and four of the same is four of the same, and that nobody in this kingdom has ever printed thirty-six anything.

---

**And the other thing a man of about fifty said in that room that day was about counties, and it is the only sentence anybody in that building has said in about nine years about what a county is, and he said it to a woman of about twenty-nine and to a man of about sixty with a tray and neither of them was the person he meant.**

“**A county answers. That is the whole of what a county is to this office and I have never had it in writing and I have never needed it in writing, because about nine forms in four hundred come back and eight of the nine come back with a figure in them and a figure in one of those eight has been the same figure in about four counties for as long as I have been in this room.**”

He said it and then he stopped, and four people in that building have said since that a man who works in a press for thirty-one years and has entered about eleven thousand forms stopped in the middle of a sentence on a Wednesday in a room with a stove in it, and that the half of the sentence after the stop is the half that is about a place about four hundred miles off, and that he did not say it and that about two of them in that building have worked out what the half is and neither of them has said so.

Nobody improved on it. A woman of about twenty-nine wrote it on a leaf of her own hand and put the leaf in the book of working papers on the second shelf, and the leaf is the third leaf in that book in about six years that anybody in that building has read twice, and four people in that building have said that a man who says a thing about counties and then stops in the middle of it has told a room that a thing he has been doing for thirty-one years has a place in it he has never had a paper out of, and that he is not going to be asked, and that he will not ask either.

---

**And a man of sixty-one with a satchel came into that room at about the sixth hour and asked for nothing and was given nothing and did not ask, and he has been in that building since the middle of last year and can find a thing in it in about four minutes, and four people in that building have said that he stood in that room for about half an hour on the twenty-sixth day of Hearthmonth, YR 315 and that he was not doing his work and that his work is finding things, and that a man who can find a thing in four minutes and stands in a room for half an hour not finding anything has been told something and has not been asked about it.**

He said one thing on his way out, and it was to nobody, and it was about eleven words, and it was the only thing he said in that room in about a year.

“**I have been in this building eighteen months and a thing in a file is a thing somebody has decided about.**”

And a man of about fifty who is a clerk of the press said that was correct and that a file is the only place in that building where anybody has ever decided anything, and that four people in that building have said since that the two of them said the same shape of thing about a file and about a list about a minute apart and that neither of them said the other's and that a man of about fifty has not asked the man of sixty-one a question in eighteen months and that the man of sixty-one has not offered one, and that that is the arrangement of that room and it is the same arrangement as a room in a county four hundred miles off where a woman keeps a door on a lane, and that nobody in either room has ever been in the other one.

And the tray went out into the passage at about the seventh hour of that Wednesday evening with four things in it, and four people in that building do not know that a fifth thing came out of that tray at about the second hour and went into a file and that the file is on a shelf in a room with the working papers on it, and that nobody in that room has been told what is in the file, and that a man of about fifty wrote nothing on the wrapper.
