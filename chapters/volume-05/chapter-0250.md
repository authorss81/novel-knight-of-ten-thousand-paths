# Chapter 0250

**The twenty-fifth day of Goatmonth, YR 315, a Thursday, and a market day, and the second hour and the fourth hour and a half and the seventh hour, and a market four hundred yards long in a town of about four thousand people on a river, and a reach four hundred yards down a lane from it with about forty men on it and the road open over it and the salt coming over the first bay, and about four hundred people in this town who do not know that a man of thirty-two with a limp stood at a bench-board for about a quarter of an hour on the last day of a five-month span and did not write one word down.**

The market was on from the second hour until about the sixth. There was salt and fish and peat in it and there was no glass in it this week, which is a thing that happens about twice in a year and is not a thing anybody says anything about. The tide was under the wharf at about the fifth hour and a cart went down the lane four hundred yards with a barrow of nothing in it and came back with the same barrow, which is a thing carts do.

**And about four people in that market worked out, in the ordinary way, on that morning, that a man of thirty-two with a limp has been in this county for five months and has been called something in four counties, and that the four of those are four different somethings, and that one of the four is a word with a carrier in it and one of the four is a word with a road in it, and that he has never once asked what any of the four means.**

He heard the first of them at a gate in a valley nine days over a pass and the second of them at a gate in a valley that has no writ in it and the third of them in a market in this town from a clerk who had never seen him before and meant it kindly, and the fourth of them he has not been able to place and is not going to try, and four people in that market have said that a man who has been called something in four counties in five months and has not asked what one of the four means has decided something, and that what he has decided is to be whatever the room decides he is, and that this county has been deciding for him since he was seventeen and has not had to raise its voice.

There is no post of that name in this county and there is no post of any of the other three in the counties they were used in, and four people in that market have said that a word with no post behind it is a word that can be given to anybody, that a word that can be given to anybody is a word nobody has to be sorry for afterwards, and that a man of thirty-two with a limp has been handed all four of them and has kept all four and has not said one of them out loud in this county since the spring.

A man of about nineteen came down four hundred yards of lane with an empty hod at about the sixth hour and went through that market and bought a piece of bread and did not stop at anything else, and he has an elder sister of twenty-two in a house two streets off and has not said one word about her to anybody in that lane in a year, and four people in that market have said that a man of about nineteen who buys one thing and goes is saving for something, and that nobody in that market has asked him what for and is not going to.

A magistrate of this county came down four hundred yards of lane at about the seventh hour, and she is the elder sister of a reeve of this town and has been on a bench of nine in a long room over a wharf for nine years, and she does not vote on the guild's own matter, and she has not voted on it for nine years, and she has beaten two counts of persons with a form's own construction in that time and has said nothing about either of them in that room.

---

**And at about the fourth hour he went into a room on Weir Street with a bench-board in it and a press on the wall, and there were four sheets of printed paper in that press with four empty places in them and a place name in the object column and a word in front of the place name that nobody in this county can read the sense of, and they had been in that press since the nineteenth day of Hearthmonth, YR 315, and a boy of fifteen was at the bench-board and did not ask what was in them.**

Four sheets is a refusal in writing. It is the third refusal in a document in three volumes and the second time the same route has been refused in writing, and it is in the same register as the first and in a different sentence, and the whole of the difference is that the first was asked of him on a sheet in good paper and this one was asked of him in a room by two people, and none of that is in this county and all of it is in a press on Weir Street.

It has not been sent to anybody. There is nobody to send it to that has not already been told no in a room, and four people in that lane have said that a document in a press in a county of four thousand people is the safest kind of document there is, because a county of four thousand people cannot be served with anything and cannot be sued by anybody and has no writ and no seal and no office, a bench of nine has refused eight things in nineteen years and has not been asked to refuse a ninth, and the four sheets are not going to be asked.

A boy of fifteen at a bench-board did not ask what was in the press and has not asked and is not going to, and a man of thirty-two with a limp stood in that room for about four minutes with his hand on the outside of the press and did not open it and did not take anything out of it and did not put anything in it, and then he went down the lane to a house forty feet from a door on that lane, and the door was shut and it had been shut all day and he did not knock on it and did not go up the lane, and four hundred people in that town do not know that a man stood in a room with his hand on the outside of a press and do not know that a door on that lane was shut all day.

**And after that about forty men were on the reach and the road was open over it and the salt had been coming over the first bay since the spring, and the fourth bay was being pointed and the joint on its parapet was a foot and a half short and the man who began it had gone home at the fourth hour and had not come back.**

Nine inches of that pointing went into a third day. The mason of fifty and a half did the arches and did the pointing and did not do the laying, and the two men of twenty-six set a course, and a boy of fifteen was at the bench-board with a lime tub and a rule and a bill, copying the empty space under a number as well as the number. Nobody measured the joint and nobody measured the line in the third course about nine inches long under about half an inch of lime that has not moved, and a man of forty-seven was on the deck of the first bay with a hod and did not come off it.

**And at the fourth hour and a half the bell went at the Old Toll four hundred yards up the road, and it went by a boy and a carpenter and not by anybody else, and nobody on that reach heard it over the stone, and four people in that lane have said that a man on stone does not hear it and a man in a market does, and that the people of this county who hear that bell and the people who do not have never been counted apart and are not going to be.**

---

**And he stood at the bench-board from about the fourth hour and a half to the fifth hour and looked at the bill, and the bill had a number on it and a rule under the number and a space under the rule, and the space was the widest of the four and the boy of fifteen had copied it, and the man of thirty-two with a limp read all three of those things and did not put a figure in the space and did not write anything at all.**

He had a notebook. It was in the inside pocket of a coat over his arm and it has eleven lines in it and one new line, and a separate leaf in the same coat has a seventh line on it that says a person and has said it since a lane four hundred miles off, and he took the leaf out on the twenty-fifth day of Goatmonth, YR 315 and did not finish it, and that is the fifth time he has looked at that line and the fifth time he has not finished it.

**And the four words he has had ready since the sixth day of a count of days he does not have are still ready, and they have been ready for a count of days he could give in one number and could not, and he did not say them, and nobody on that reach knew he had them, and four people in that county have said that a man who has four words ready and does not say them is a man waiting for a person who can be asked a second time, and that there is nobody in this county to ask and nobody four hundred miles off who could be reached and asked.**

A man of thirty-four with lime has said out loud that a man who does not write a thing down is not a man who is keeping a thing out of a book, and that it is a man who has found out that nobody is going to read it, and that he said it once with a gather going and has not said it since. He said it once and a man of thirty-two with a limp has now not written three things down in three days, and four people in this county have said that the three of them were a man not asking a man, a man not asking a county, and a man not asking himself, and that none of the three is a decision and that the not-writing is not a decision and that a man who decides not to write a thing down has made a plan, and that he has not made one and is not going to.

---

**And the magistrate of this county stood four feet off the bench-board for about a minute and said one thing, and it is about nineteen words, and it is the only thing she has said in nine years that was not about a count of persons, and she said it to a man with a stick and not to a bench.**

“**I have had a construction ready since the second hour. I have had it since I read four empty places in a printed sheet in a room over a wharf on Saturday and I have not used it, and I would like somebody to write down that I did not use it, and there is nobody in this county I can ask.**”

Nobody asked her. A boy of fifteen at a bench-board did not look up, and a man of forty-seven on the deck of the first bay did not turn round, and a man of thirty-two with a limp did not offer to write it down, and about four people in that lane have said since that a woman who has had an instrument ready for five hours and has not used it and cannot have the not-using written down is in the same position as a keeper of records with five questions in a book, and that the two of them are in one county and neither of them has ever been in a room with the other one, and that each of the two of them has an instrument of her own and neither has used it, and that nobody in this county can say whether that is one thing happening twice or two things happening once each, and that nobody is going to say.

She put the four feet back to five and went up the lane to the wharf and did not say anything else, and she has not said anything else in that county, and the construction is still there, and four hundred people in a town of about four thousand people do not know that a magistrate of this county stood four feet off a bench-board on the last day of a five-month span and asked a man with a stick to write something down for her and did not ask him twice.

---

**And the market had gone in at about the sixth hour and the tide was under the wharf and the road four hundred yards down the lane was open with about forty men on it, and the salt was on the first bay and on the lane, and the fourth bay was being pointed, and the joint on its parapet was a foot and a half short, and a bell with forty-one rivets in it had gone at the fourth hour and a half by two people who are not paid for it and are not members of it, and a man of thirty-two with a limp went down four hundred yards of lane to a house in Weir Street with a stick and a coat and a notebook with nothing new in it, and a man of about thirty-one with a satchel is in a building two hundred miles off with about two thousand sheets of a printed series behind him, and about four hundred doors on his list and about four hundred doors on a list in this county are the same four hundred doors, and nobody in any of the four places has ever worked out how many, and a district is a set of about nine market towns and one of the nine is this one, and a figure of persons went out of a room in a provincial town two hundred miles off on the fourth of that month in a column with a head on it, and nobody in that room has ever seen the sheet on that bench and nobody in this county has ever seen the figure, and the sheet has four empty places in it and it is in a press in a room over a wharf and it is not going to be answered.**

And about four hundred miles off, on a shelf eleven feet long in a room off a lane that goes down to a quay, there is a strip of paper about a foot long lying with eight others of its kind, and in the corner of that strip there is a mark that is a line and a bar and a figure, and nobody in that room put it there, and nobody in that room has looked at it since the seventh hour of a morning in the spring, and nobody in this county has ever seen it, and it is a foot long and it is on the shelf and it is there.
