# Chapter 0743

**Wolfmonth the fifth, YR 322, a Saturday and a market morning, the second hour in the market street at the boards with about four hundred people in it and about two hundred of them stopped, the room over the weigh-house open, and nine hundred yards of a line four people have now seen once each at the same time.**

A Saturday in that market town of about two thousand people is the loudest thing in nine counties, and four hundred people were in that street by the second hour of the fifth of Wolfmonth and two hundred of them stopped what they were doing, and the frost came off the ruts about an hour after that, and four people in nine counties had worked out that a man of thirty-two had asked four people for one thing and five had not.

Nobody thanked anybody and that light has been on the top of that shoulder at about the fourth hour of every morning since the twenty-sixth of Embermonth, and that is ten mornings, and on the sixth of them four people were standing at the head of that made road and saw all nine hundred yards of it, and one of those four was the man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract for about nineteen years, and about four people in nine counties know that and five have never been up that road before the light.

Nobody said anything and a drove went up that lane four days off on the Thursday before, which was the third of Wolfmonth, at about the sixth hour, and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back down at about the fifth hour, and nobody went with it, and that is about thirty-one drove to go up that lane since the second of Mudmonth and about four hundred people in that market street have never watched one.

Eight of the nine yards of wall at the head of that made road are back in and the gap is about a yard long and about four feet high, and the stone is quarried two miles off on the low side and one man has been carrying it two miles in his own hands for two hundred and thirteen days, and the last yard is about nine feet of a course and he has not started it, and nobody has asked him when he will and nobody is going to.

The brood in a walled yard half a mile below that wall is five frames where there were nine on the night of the eighth of Thawmonth, and that night is two hundred and thirty-seven days back, and the fifth frame came in on the twentieth of Embermonth and is not all brood yet, and about four people in nine counties have said since that five frames is a figure and not a finding and that the ninth of Wolfmonth is the ninth day of a month this county has not had since Mudmonth and five have said nothing about it at all.

On that east rise the broom has been off for about nine days and about nine hundred yards of a line nine feet wide is left standing, and a line in grass that is not cut closes over in about three years, and about four people in nine counties have said since that the nine hundred yards a young man of about nineteen did not cut are the only nine hundred yards on that hill anybody in this county can point at and say was kept and five have said that a young man keeping nine hundred yards is not keeping them and is cutting round them.

The nine stones at the end of that line are about the height of a man's knee and about as wide as two hands and the tops of them are all at the same height, and nobody in nine counties has found out why, and about nine households keep beasts on the flat past those stones and seven of them had stock on it in the week of the twenty-eighth of Longlight and two of them did not, and nobody has ever asked any of the nine about the rota and nobody is going to, and about four people in nine counties know that the rota is older than any question anybody has asked about it and five have never worked out that there is a question.

Nobody said anything and the bar at the head of that made road is in on the lane side and has been in since Mudmonth and it is four feet of ash with two stones on the end of it, and the drove goes up through the gap at the low end of about nine hundred yards of wall and not through the gate, and no beast has gone through that gate in eleven years, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a gate a drove does not use is not a shut gate and that it is a gate with a bar on it and five have said that a man of forty-one who bars it on a Thursday morning after the water has gone out of a lane is doing a thing that has no name in this county.

Then the weigh-house, which is the only floor in nine counties where about four hundred and nine sheets a month are handled by one man, and which on that Saturday weighed two loads of lime and a load of wool.

Nobody thanked him and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that Wolfmonth is five days old and about fifty sheets have come past that floor in it, and that Frostmonth is the month they come past clean, and that nobody has ever asked him what a sheet weighs, and four people in nine counties have said since that about ten a day is about three hundred in a month of thirty and five have said that three hundred is not four hundred and nine and that nobody in this county has worked out what month is.

The inn at the high end had four beds full that morning and put no price out on any board, and the woman of about fifty-one who keeps that house came out into that street at about the fifth hour and stopped nine feet short of those boards and would not come nearer to them, and a chandler nine doors down put a cloth over a bench rather than write anything at all on it.

Nobody said anything and a man with a barrow of lime went the length of that street nine times and unloaded at four doors and loaded at two, and nobody stopped walking at any part of it, and the near wheel of a cart came off at the low end and the carter got it up again with a bar in about nine minutes, and two women at the third door argued about a bolt of cloth for about four minutes and then went into the shop and came out with it wrapped.

The room over that weigh-house was open because it is a Saturday, and the copyist of about twenty-six came down the thirteen stairs at the seventh hour and went back up, and four people in that lane saw her come down and five did not, and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled.

The man of about thirty-four with a satchel was at the high end of those boards on that Saturday and said nothing at all in that street, and he has said nine things out loud in this county in about four and a half years and said the ninth of them on the thirteenth of Rainmonth and has refused since that day to say anything twice, and nobody asked him and nobody sent for him, and four people in that market street had worked out that a man who has said nothing for a hundred and twelve days is a man waiting and five had worked out that he has run out.

Then the part of that Saturday that was said out loud, and only two things in all of it were, and neither of the two was about a road.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about fifty-two came into that market street at about the fourth hour with about nine feet of a new kerb standing against a shop wall and about nine hundred feet of it on a barrow behind him, and he put the back of his board up on the low end of the weigh-house step so that four hundred people in that street could see it, and about nine of them came and looked at it, and he said one thing out loud, in the open, in that street, in his own trade, and it was 35 words, and nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him.

“**I have drawn nine hundred yards of it on the back of my board in charcoal and I am not going to draw it again and I am not going to give the board away.**”

Nobody said anything and four hundred people in that market street had seen nine hundred yards of a line drawn in charcoal on the back of a roadkeeper's board and about nine of them had come and looked at it and about five had asked him what the thick end of it was, and he told them twice it was the top and neither of them asked twice, and about four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first map of anything anybody in this county has ever drawn and five have said that it is the back of a board a man carries about a road and not a map.

Nobody said anything and the board itself is a plan of four miles of made road that the man of about fifty-two has been under contract for about nineteen years, and it is about nine feet long and about nine inches wide and the coping of nine hundred yards of wall stands on it at the head of those four miles with the gate in it and the drove road above it, and the nine hundred yards of the line that four people saw once at the fourth hour is drawn above that wall and above the drove road and above the east rise, and nobody in nine counties has ever asked him why a roadkeeper's board goes nine hundred yards above his own four miles.

About nine people in nine counties have stood in that market street and looked at the back of that board, and about nine hundred have walked past it, and four people in nine counties have said since that a county of two thousand people has just looked at the first drawing of anything it has ever had and that nine of them went and looked at it deliberately and five have said that this is nine and not four hundred and about four have said that nine is how many ever look at anything and five have said that the other three hundred and ninety-one were in that street and were not looking at anything, which is the ordinary condition of a market morning.

The fruit on nine hundred trees between two miles of wall came down into four hundred yards of grass every year and was still in it in the spring, and nobody in nine counties has ever picked one up, and about four people in nine counties have said since that the owners of those two miles are nine households and not one of them has ever asked anybody anything about anybody who walks past its gate and five have said that nine households on a made road in this county have never in four and a half years asked anybody anything about a road and that nobody has ever asked them for a reason.

Nobody thanked him and then a question came into that market street at about the ninth hour from a person at the third door who wanted to know one thing, and nobody stopped it, and it was not about a road and not about a wall and it was asked kindly.

“**Is that the hill you said you would not go up?**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a question asked about a hill a man had said he would not go up and five had heard it asked kindly, and nobody stopped it and the man of thirty-two with a stick answered it out loud, in the open, in his own trade, and it was 51 words, and nobody thanked him.

“**That is nine hundred yards of a line four of us have seen once at the same time and one of us has seen it ten times, and I am going up it on the twenty-fifth of Wolfmonth with three people and not because anybody has seen anything on it twice.**”

Nobody said anything and nobody said anything back to him and nobody thanked him, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man who is going up a hill he has said he is not going up is a man going up it in company and that this is the first time in about four and a half years that he has done anything in company and five have said that he is going up it with three people he asked on the first of Wolfmonth and that they said yes in their own trades.

The two refusals in this county were given on the fourteenth and the twenty-sixth of Fallowmonth and neither has been argued with, and that is fifty-one days and thirty-nine days, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man who has been refused twice out loud is now standing in a market street saying that a thing four people have seen is not a thing anybody has seen twice and five have said that a refusal cannot be got round and that this is not getting round one.

The one sheet of paper with one line on it has been in an office four days off for thirty-nine days and the line on it is that a man said a thing out loud in a market street, and about four people in nine counties have said since that there is now a second thing in this county that four people have each seen once and that is nine hundred yards of light on a hilltop, and five have said that the sheet of paper has one line on it and that the line is not about the hill and about four have said that a thing seen four times is not a line on a sheet and five have said that a line on a sheet is not a thing seen four times and that this county has one of each and can move neither.

Nobody said anything and the sea is four days south of that market town and about four hundred people live on a far bank across nine hundred yards of water at the top of a tide and about four hundred at the bottom, and nobody in that market street has ever asked a person at a gate at the near end of that water anything at all, and four people in nine counties have said since that this county has two far sides and one of them is nine hundred yards of water and the other is nine hundred yards of grass on a hilltop and five have said that the water one is the easier of the two to look at from a distance.

Nobody thanked anybody and nothing about any of it is in a document, and there is no page in any room in nine counties with the fifth of Wolfmonth on it, and the register in the room over that weigh-house holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled, and about four people in nine counties have said since that nine hundred yards of a line that four people have seen is on the back of a board in a market street and not in any of them and five have said that a board is not a room and that a roadkeeper is not a keeper.

**And the cost of that Saturday is one board with nine hundred yards of charcoal on the back of it that nobody may take away, and about nine people who have stood in a street and looked at it, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
