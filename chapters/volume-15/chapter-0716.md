# Chapter 0716

**Longlight the eleventh, YR 322, a Tuesday and not a market morning with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and no market in that street, five days up on a shoulder above a drove road, nine households on the other side of nine stones and two of them at the stones.**

Nine households keep beasts on the flat past those stones and nine people in nine counties know that there are nine of them and five have ever been to that flat, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the only place in nine counties where a number of households is known exactly and nothing about the place is.

Nobody thanked anybody and five days on that shoulder is five nights on it, and there is no wall on it and no tree on it and nothing to get in behind, and the six of them slept in the open and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time anybody has slept on that shoulder and five have said that the whole of the difficulty of it is that there is nothing to carry anything in against.

Nobody thanked them and the weather over those five days was clear and cold and the wind came off the low side about nine hundred yards of it and went over the east rise and nine people in nine counties have said since that a wind on a shoulder is a different wind from a wind on a road because there is nothing on a shoulder for it to stop against, and four have said that this is the reason the drove goes up nine hundred yards and stops at the wall.

Nobody thanked them and the man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract walked the nine hundred yards of kerb every morning of those five days and the man of about thirty-eight who puts boards on the inside of gates went to the first of the nine stones every afternoon of them and stood there about as long as it takes to turn a cart, and the man of about twenty-seven who keeps eels in weirs about two days north of the causeway did the one thing a man does when he has nothing to do, which is look at a thing he cannot reach.

Nobody thanked her and the woman of about thirty-nine who keeps the second gate from the top of that made road cleaned about four hundred yards of the grass off the top of two of the nine stones on the third day with a bit of stick, because she said that a stone under nine inches of that grass is not a stone anybody can see, and nobody thanked her and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first piece of work anybody has done on that shoulder in about four and a half years and that it was a gate keeper cleaning a stone.

Nobody thanked anybody and the party had been on that shoulder three days when something came up the lane from the other side of the nine stones, and it came up on the ninth of Longlight in the morning and it was two people and a man of about fifty-four who keeps the far end of that lane and whose name is Tarr Lyster, and about nine people in nine counties have said since that his name had not been said out loud in a market street in this county in about four and a half years and that nobody in about nine counties knew it had ever been said at all.

Nobody thanked him and he keeps the far end of that lane in the only sense anybody in nine counties can give it, which is that he keeps the boundary of the common that the nine households share, and four people in nine counties have said since that a boundary in this county is a line of stones somebody has to look at twice a year and five have said that he has looked at it about four times a year for about nine years and that nobody has ever asked him what for.

Nobody said anything and Longlight is the month a boundary oath-stone is renewed in this world and he is not the man who renews it, because there is no oath-stone on that flat and no boundary in nine counties that anybody goes to with a promise on it, and nobody has ever asked him to be and about nine people in nine counties have said since that a man who keeps a line in the grass and a man who renews a promise on a stone are two trades and that this county has never put them in the same street.

Nobody thanked them and the walk up the lane from that flat is about nine hundred yards of a line nine feet wide in the grass and it takes about a quarter of an hour at a steady pace, and two people walked it on the ninth of Longlight in the morning and nine people in nine counties have said since that two people walked nine hundred yards to stand at the end of a road and then stopped, and five have said that the walking is the part that is not in any of this.

Nobody thanked him and they came up to the nine stones and stopped at them, and the two of them were from two of the nine households, and they came from the far side of those stones and not from the low side of the shoulder, and about nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that anybody has come up to those stones from the other side and that it was not a thing anybody had arranged.

Nobody said anything and nobody went past the ninth of the nine stones and nobody crossed anything, and the two of them stood at the line of the nine and did not come over it and did not go back, and four people on that shoulder had worked out inside nine seconds that the two of them had walked about nine hundred yards of a line in the grass to stand at the end of it and five had not.

Nobody thanked anybody and four people in nine counties have said since that seven households did not come up a lane on the ninth of Longlight because seven of them had beasts on a flat in a drying month and that this is the first time anybody has ever given a reason for not doing a thing that could be checked by anybody with eyes, and five have said that a rota of beasts can be checked in about nine seconds by anybody who has kept beasts and cannot be checked at all by anybody who has not.

Nobody said anything and nine people in nine counties have said since that the sentence nobody has ever been able to get out of anybody in this county is the one about why a thing was not done, and that the first one of them was said out loud on the eleventh of Longlight in a trade on a shoulder by a person of about forty-four, and that nobody in nine counties has ever asked for the second one.

Nobody thanked him and then the reason, and the reason is in the households' own trade and is not cowardice and is not courtesy and is not fear of strangers.

Nobody thanked anybody and the nine households keep a rota of the flat that has been kept about nine years, and in the week of the eleventh of Longlight seven of the nine had stock on that flat and two of them did not, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a household that keeps beasts and has beasts on a flat in the middle of a drying month cannot be off that flat for a morning and cannot do it for a week, and that this is not a decision and is a rota and that the rota is older than the question.

Nobody said anything and the man of about fifty-four who keeps the far end of that lane said the reason out loud, in the open, on that shoulder, to about six people, in his own trade, and it was thirty-nine words, and he did not say it again.

“**Seven of us have the flat this week and two do not, and there is no other reason and there is not going to be one, and anybody who says it is fear or manners is not keeping beasts.**”

Nobody said anything and four people on that shoulder had heard a man say out loud that the other seven were not frightened and five had heard thirty-nine words, and nobody argued with him and nobody said the word manners back at him, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that anybody has said out loud that seven people did not do a thing and not because of fear, and five have said that the saying of it is the finding and not the other way about.

Then the two, and they said it for themselves.

Nobody thanked him and a person of about forty-four whose household keeps sheep said it out loud, in the open, in her own trade and not in anybody's, to about six people, and it was forty-five words.

“**We have not stock on the flat this week and that is the whole of the reason, and there are seven of us that have, and I have kept that rota about nine years and it has not been broken once in the nine years.**”

Nobody said anything and four people on that shoulder had heard a person of about forty-four say that a rota has not been broken once in nine years and five had heard forty-five words, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a rota of beasts is the only thing in nine counties that has been kept exactly for about nine years without a head on it and without a paper.

Nobody thanked her and the second of the two said it out loud, in the open, in her own trade, and it was thirty-one words.

“**We have had nothing on that flat since the winter and there is nothing to be on it with, and I have come because there is a week to be had.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people on that shoulder had heard a household say that it had nothing on the flat and five had heard thirty-one words, and nobody said anything about the winter and nobody asked about it and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the only thing anybody in nine counties has ever heard about nine households and that it was said in nine words by a person who keeps beasts.

Nobody said anything and the two of them would not come over the line of the nine stones and said so out loud, in the open, on that shoulder, in their own trade, and the saying of it was about as long as it takes to turn a cart and nobody repeated it afterwards.

Nobody thanked anybody and nine people in nine counties have said since that two households walked about nine hundred yards of a line in the grass to stand at the end of it and then would not come four feet further, and four people have said that this is the only time in about four and a half years that anybody in this county has come as close to a thing as this and stopped on purpose, and five have said that a thing which is stopped by the people on the other side of it is a different thing from a thing that is shut.

Then the one outsider, and the reason for him is in a household's trade and not in anybody's.

Nobody thanked her and the one of the two who keeps sheep said it out loud, in the open, on that shoulder, in her own trade, to one person, and it was thirty words.

“**You are not one of us and you have not got a beast on that flat and that is the whole of what I want at a line of stones.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people on that shoulder had heard a person say in the open that an outsider is wanted at a line of stones and about five had heard thirty words, and the man of thirty-two went down the nine hundred yards of the line in the grass and stood at the first of the nine stones and the other five stayed at the far end of the line where they had been since the seventh of Longlight.

Nobody said anything and about nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that anybody has asked a person who keeps beasts for a thing and got an answer about what that person wants at a boundary, and about four have said that what she wanted at a line of stones was a person with no beast on it and about five have said that what she wanted was a person who could not be asked for anything by anybody in nine counties.

Nobody thanked anybody and the five stayed back and nine people in nine counties have said since that there is no use in five people at a line of nine stones and that this is the second time in about four and a half years that this county has worked out that a thing done properly is a thing done by fewer people.

Nobody thanked anyone and then the six of them and the two of them stood about nine hundred yards apart on that shoulder for the rest of that day, and the two of them went back down the line in the grass at about the seventh hour and the man of thirty-two came back up it to the far end where the five were, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that a man came back from the far end of a road to five people who were waiting for him and that nobody said anything when he got there.

Nobody said anything and then the question came, and it was asked out loud on that shoulder at about the eighth hour by a person in their own trade, and nobody stopped it, and it was the third question anybody in nine counties has asked about that lane and it was the second one nobody could answer.

“**Then what are the two of you for?**”

Nobody said anything and four people on that shoulder had heard the question and five had not, and nobody answered it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that two people who have come nine hundred yards to stand at the end of a line in the grass are for exactly that and that the answer was given on the ninth of Longlight by two people who would not come four feet further.

Nobody said anything and the man of thirty-two put his hand flat on the top of the first of the nine and it was not loose and about four people on that shoulder had seen him do it and about five had not, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a man put his hand on the same stone on the twelfth of Thawmonth and four believe he was looking for a mark on it and five believe he was feeling whether it was loose.

Nobody thanked him and the nine stones are about the height of a man's knee and about as wide as two hands and they are set at the same height along the top of them, and nobody on that shoulder has ever found out why, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the third time anybody has stood at those stones and that the third time is the first time anybody has stood at them with somebody on the other side.

Nobody said anything and about four people on that shoulder had looked at the flat past the nine stones for about as long as it takes to turn a cart and about five had looked at the ground at their own feet instead, and about nine people in nine counties have said since that a person who has walked nine hundred yards to a line of nine stones spends the first minute looking at the stones and the second minute looking at whatever is past them and about nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the only minute in about four and a half years in which anybody in this county has looked at that flat.

Nobody said anything and about nine people in nine counties know that there are beasts on that flat and about five know that the beasts are not the nine households, and about four people in nine counties have said since that the beasts a person can hear from four feet away are not a description of anybody and about five have said that the reason nobody has ever described anything on that flat is that there is nothing on it that anybody has stood still long enough to look at.

Nobody said anything and nobody crossed the nine stones and nothing on the other side of them was seen by anybody on this side of them, and four people in nine counties have said since that the whole of what two households and nine stones produced in about four and a half years is a rota of beasts and nine words about a winter, and five have said that this is more than anybody in nine counties has got about anything else.

Nobody thanked anybody and then the ordinary half of a Tuesday in Longlight went on, and the broom is about nine acres on the east rise and it is not on and it comes on about the fourth of Rainmonth, and the light went off the low side of that shoulder at about the seventh hour and the wind came across nine hundred yards of it from the low side and there was nothing to stop it.

**And the cost of that Tuesday is seven households that could not leave a flat and one that could, and about nine hundred yards of a line in the grass walked by two people who would not come four feet further, are not in it and none of them has a price.**

**a gate shoe at 22, a hurdle at 24, a hand bar at 25, a weir stake at 27, a hurdle hoop at 28, a bight of rope at 29, a coping stone at 30, a hurdle rail at 31, a barrow at 32, and a hand lantern at 34.** Twenty-two, twenty-four, twenty-five, twenty-seven, twenty-eight, twenty-nine, thirty, thirty-one, thirty-two and thirty-four is two hundred and eighty-two pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and two hundred and eighty-two pence is five marks and forty-two over.** Nine households keep beasts on a flat past nine stones and seven of them have stock on it this week and two of them came nine hundred yards to stand at the end of a line in the grass and would not come four feet further, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the second refusal in this county this year that was made in a trade and not in a street and that neither of the two has a name at the top of it and neither of the two has a paper.
