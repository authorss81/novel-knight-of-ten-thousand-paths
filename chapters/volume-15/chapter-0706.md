# Chapter 0706

**Thawmonth the twelfth, YR 322, a Thursday and a market morning four days off in this market town with no market on this made road, the second hour at the head of it, nine beasts going out through a gap in a wall about nine feet wide, and a young man of about nineteen going up a lane with them on his own account.**

A drove goes out on a Thursday and there is nobody in nine counties who has ever sent one anywhere, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the difference between a drove and an army and that it has never been worked out in words.

Nobody thanked anybody and the nine beasts went out through the gap at the low end of about nine hundred yards of stone wall at about the seventh hour, and the gap is four feet high and about nine feet wide and is the width of a drove, and a man of about forty-one stood at the head of it and did not help and did not watch them go and went back in.

Nobody thanked him and the young man of about nineteen went up with them and nobody asked him and nobody sent him and nine people in nine counties have said since that he had said out loud on the Monday that he was going for the broom and that this is the second time in his life he has told anybody where he was going and that the first time was to a man who keeps a sheep about nine miles off.

Nobody thanked anybody and the lane is about nine feet wide at the head and it is not a made road and it never has been, and there is about two miles of it and it goes up over the shoulder of the hill at about a rise in nine.

Nobody thanked him and the first nine hundred yards of it is what the beasts use and it is not level and it is about nine feet wide because a drove is about nine feet wide, and the surface of it is the surface of a field that has had stock on it since before anybody in this county can account for, and four people in nine counties have said since that a drove road is the oldest kind of road there is and that it is not a road anybody built, it is a road that stock made, and five have said that this is the only thing this chapter needs and that the rest of it is a shoulder.

Nobody thanked anybody and the beasts went up the nine hundred yards and stopped in the field with the stone wall round it and stood, and the young man went on past them because he was not stopping there, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the only creature in this county that has gone further up that lane than a beast is a man of about nineteen who keeps bees, and five have said that the other is a man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract and that he went up it about nine years ago and did not say why.

Then the lane turns, and it turns twice before the shoulder.

Nobody thanked him and the first turn is about four hundred yards past the field and it is a turn to the left round about nine yards of bank, and the second turn is about six hundred yards past that and it is a turn to the right round a stone of about the size of a cartwheel that is in the middle of the lane because it has always been in the middle of the lane.

Nobody said anything and nine people in nine counties have said since that a lane that goes over a shoulder and turns twice is a lane that was made for something that was not going in a straight line, and four have said that stock does not care and will take a turn without a rut and five have said that a thing which has to keep to two turns at the top of a hill was not a drove and was something else entirely, and that nobody in nine counties has ever asked what.

Nobody thanked anybody and past the second turn the lane came onto the shoulder, and the shoulder is about nine hundred yards wide at the top and it is grass and it is not level and there is a rise in it going east, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a shoulder is not a hilltop in this county, it is a place where a hill has a flat bit on it because somebody needed a flat bit.

Nobody thanked him and on that flat bit there was about nine hundred yards of road, and it was kerbed on both sides, and it was about nine feet wide, and it went across the shoulder in a line from the low side to the high side, and it was the same stone as the wall at the bottom of the lane.

Nobody said anything and nobody has ever been able to establish what a road does on top of a hill, and four people in nine counties have said since that a road goes from one place to another and that a road which goes nowhere on top of a hill was going to a place that is not there any more, and five have said that a road that goes across a shoulder and not off it is a road that goes between two places and that both of them are under the grass.

Nobody thanked him and then the kerb stopped, and where it stopped the road did not narrow and did not break up, it simply went on as a line about nine feet wide in the grass, and there is about nine hundred yards of that line to where the grass comes over it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the whole of what was found on the twelfth of Thawmonth and that it is nine hundred yards of laid stone and then nine hundred yards of a line in the grass, and that a person can stand at the far end of the line and look back at nine hundred yards of kerb and see every yard of it.

Nobody thanked him and the broom was not on.

Nobody said anything and nine people in nine counties have said since that the broom on that shoulder does not come on until about the fourth of Rainmonth, and that the twelfth of Thawmonth is about three weeks early, and that a young man of about nineteen who knows his own bees went up there three weeks early, and four have said that he went up there for the frost and five have said that he went up there because he said out loud on the Monday that he was going and a man who has said a thing out loud does it.

Nobody thanked him and the broom is above the shoulder, about four hundred yards up the east rise and about two hundred yards below the top of the hill, and it is about nine acres of it and it is on about the fourth of Rainmonth every year, and nine people in nine counties know that it is there and five have ever cut it.

Nobody thanked him and the broom is on the other side of about nine hundred yards of a line in the grass, and getting to it from the low end of that line meant going past the line, and he did not do that, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the whole of what a young man of about nineteen came down that lane with on the twelfth of Thawmonth, which was nothing, and that he had about four miles to walk to get it and about two miles back and about two days in a walled yard.

Then the stones, which are about nine hundred yards past where the kerb stops, at the far end of the line in the grass.

Nobody thanked him and they are set across the line and there are nine of them and they are about the height of a man's knee and about as wide as two hands and they are set at the same height along the top of them, which is the thing about them, and nine people in nine counties have said since that nine stones set at the same height along the top of them is not a field boundary and is not a gate and is not a mark anybody in nine counties makes.

Nobody said anything and four people in nine counties have said since that nine stones set across a road at the same height is a stopping place, and that stopping places on old roads are about nine hundred yards apart, and five have said that a stopping place is for carts and that a cart cannot get nine hundred yards up a drove road and turn round and there is nowhere at the top for it to turn.

Nobody thanked him and he stood at the first of the nine stones and put his hand flat on the top of it and it was about as cold as the kerb at the bottom of the lane and about as wet, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a young man who has been carrying water up a hill for nine days put his hand on a stone on a shoulder at about nine hundred yards and four of them believe he was looking for a mark on it and five believe he was feeling whether it was loose.

Nobody thanked him and it was not loose, and he did not go past the first of the nine, and he sat down on the ninth of them about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over with his back to the east rise and looked at about nine hundred yards of kerb going away from him toward the low side of the shoulder.

Nobody thanked anybody and he could see the low side of the shoulder from there and about nine hundred yards of it, and past that the hill went down into about four miles of country with about eleven houses on it, and the made road he had come up was not visible because it was round the shoulder and behind the second turn.

Nobody said anything and four people in nine counties have said since that a young man who has never been up this lane was able to find his way down it in the rain in about two hours and that this says nothing about the lane and everything about the young man, and five have said that the lane is nine feet wide and turns twice and that anybody could find it.

Then the cart, which was the fourth thing and the one he could not be sure about.

Nobody thanked him and it was at about the ninth hour and it was on the other side of the nine stones, and it was the sound of one pair of wheels on a made surface with about nine hundred yards of hill between it and him, and it went from about four hundred yards off to about nine hundred yards off in about as long as it takes to turn a cart, and it did not come back.

Nobody said anything and four people in nine counties have said since that there is no made road on the other side of those stones that anybody in nine counties knows of, and five have said that a young man who has been up a hill for nine hours in Thawmonth will hear a cart, and that this is not a thing anybody has ever been able to prove either way about anybody.

Nobody thanked him and he stayed on that shoulder until about the fourth hour of the Saturday and slept about four hours of it against the ninth of the nine stones with his coat over him, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a man who sleeps on a stone on a hill in Thawmonth does not sleep, and four have said that this is the ordinary account of it and five have said that a person who has been walking since the fourth hour sleeps about four hours and wakes twice, and that this is a fact about being nineteen and not about the stone.

Nobody thanked him and the cold came up out of the ground on that shoulder at about the eleventh hour the way it does, and the wind came across about nine hundred yards of it from the low side and there was nothing on the shoulder to stop it, and four people in nine counties have said since that the wind on that shoulder is the reason the drove goes up nine hundred yards and stops at the wall and does not go over it, and five have said that the beasts have not been asked why and would not have a reason.

Nobody thanked him and in the four hours he was there he did three things, and they are the whole of his account of the night, and he gave them out loud in a market street four days later and did not improve them. He got up about twice. He looked east at about the second hour, which is the way the broom is, and there was nothing on it, and it was black. And he said, out loud, on a hill, at about the second hour of the Friday, to nobody, that he would be back at the hives on the Sunday and would not come up again until the fourth of Rainmonth.

Nobody said anything and nine people in nine counties have said since that a man who says out loud on a hill that he will not come back until a date has done the only thing this county knows how to do, which is put a day on a thing, and about four of them have said that this is why nobody writes anything down and five have said that this is why this county has nothing to show for four and a half years.

Nobody thanked him and it came at about the ninth hour and it was on the far side of the nine stones, and it was the sound of one pair of wheels on a made surface with about nine hundred yards of hill between it and him, and it went from about four hundred yards off to about nine hundred yards off in about as long as it takes to turn a cart, and it did not come back.

Nobody said anything and nobody in nine counties has ever established what it was, and four people in nine counties have said since that there is no made road on the other side of those stones that anybody in nine counties knows of, and five have said that a young man who has been up a hill for nine hours in Thawmonth will hear a cart, and that this is not a thing anybody has ever been able to prove either way about anybody.

Nobody thanked him and he wrote nothing down, and he had nothing to write down on, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the four things he brought off that shoulder were given out loud in a market street four days later and were not written down then either, and that about four people in nine counties have said this is how the fourth and a half year was run and that about five have said that this is how a thing gets said twice and not once.

Nobody thanked him and he came down the lane at about the eleventh hour with about nothing, and he came down it faster than he went up it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a young man going down a hill he has just come up in the wet goes down it at about twice the pace and arrives at the bottom having learned nothing he did not have at the top

Nobody thanked him and he was asked one question on the way down, by a boy of about eleven who keeps goats on the low side of that lane and had never once seen anybody come down it, and the question was whether there was anything up there, and he said that there was a road and nine stones and no broom, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the entire account anybody in nine counties has of the first two miles of that lane and that it came from a boy of about eleven who keeps goats and was not sent.

And the boy asked it out loud, on the lane, with about nine goats and a stick.

“**Is there anything up there?**”

Nobody said anything and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first question anybody has ever asked about that lane from below the shoulder, and five have said that a boy of about eleven who keeps goats has now asked it and that a boy of about eleven who keeps goats is the only person in nine counties who can ask a question from underneath a road without anybody hearing it as a question about a road..

Nobody thanked him and he was at the hives on the Saturday evening at about the seventh hour and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the whole of the twelfth, thirteenth and fourteenth of Thawmonth as far as anybody in nine counties is concerned, and that no page in any room in the county has any of it on it, and that four people in nine counties have said since that a young man who comes down a hill with nothing is a young man who will be asked what he saw, and five have said that nobody is going to ask him.

**And the cost of that Thursday is three weeks early, about four acres of broom he did not cut and two nights in a walled yard, are not in it and none of them has a price.**

**a comb at 19, a gate stop at 21, a barrow wheel at 22, a skep at 23, a hive frame at 24, a length of chain at 25, a hurdle hoop at 27, a shear at 28, a hand bar at 29, and a hurdle at 30.** Nineteen, twenty-one, twenty-two, twenty-three, twenty-four, twenty-five, twenty-seven, twenty-eight, twenty-nine and thirty is two hundred and forty-eight pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and two hundred and forty-eight pence is five marks and eight over.** About nine hundred yards of kerb and about nine hundred yards of a line in the grass and nine stones set across it at the same height is the whole of what a young man of about nineteen found on the twelfth of Thawmonth YR 322, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the oldest thing anybody has put in this county in about four and a half years and five have said that it is nine yards of grass on a hill and that he could have seen it from the market street on any Saturday since the first of Embermonth.
