# Chapter 0708

**Thawmonth the twenty-sixth, YR 322, a Thursday and a market morning, the second hour in the market street at the boards with about four hundred people in it and about two hundred of them stopped, the room over the weigh-house open, and a leaf of a roll from the fen county road office being read out loud in the open by a copyist of about twenty-six.**

Nobody thanked anybody and a Crown party's cart went up this made road in the middle of Hearthmonth with about nine feet of paper under a tarpaulin and went on south and did not stop in this county, and about four people in that market town had seen the tarpaulin go past and five had not, and nobody in nine counties knows what was under it and about four people have said since that there is no reason in nine counties for anybody to want to know.

Nobody thanked anybody and the paper went into this county at some point between the fourteenth of Hearthmonth and the twenty-second, and four people in nine counties have said since that the room over that weigh-house is where paper goes in this county and that this has been true for about four and a half years and five have said that it is because it is a room with a door and a deed of thirteen words and about nine people have decided so far apart that four of them have never met.

Nobody thanked anyone and the roll was not loose in that cart, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the pages in it were in a handbasket with a cloth on top, and that the same handbasket came up this made road in the middle of Hearthmonth and went into the hired room over a saddler's, and that about four of them have said that a roll of hands and a list of counties were in one basket for about nine days and that whoever packed it did not look at either.

Nobody said anything and four people in that lane have said since that the page reached that room about a fortnight ago and was not entered in the day-book, and that the copyist read it and put it in a drawer about eleven inches deep and did nothing else with it for about nine days, and that nine days is about how long it takes a keeper of records to decide that a piece of paper has nothing to do with her three series and then to carry it down thirteen stairs anyway.

Nobody thanked him and the copyist of about twenty-six came down the thirteen stairs at about the ninth hour with a leaf in her hand and something on it, and she came down once and did not go back up, and about four people in that lane saw her come down once and five had been expecting her to come down twice, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first first of a market morning since the first of Hearthmonth on which that copyist has come down the stairs once.

Nobody thanked anyone and she stood in that market street about nine feet off the boards and read it out, in the open, to about four hundred people and to about nine of them, and she read it as it is written and did not improve it and did not hurry.

“**Fen county road office, roll of hands, YR 312, being the twelfth year of that reckoning. Entered, Aren Kest, of the Old Toll crossing, of the age of twenty-nine years, having served seven years before he was entered. Signed at the entering, Iowan Petch, master.**”

Nobody thanked her and four hundred people in that market street had heard a roll read out and four of them had done the sum and five had not.

Nobody said anything and the sum is the whole of this chapter, and it takes about nine seconds, and nine people in nine counties have said since that it is the fastest that anybody in this county has ever understood a thing and that it was done by four people in a market street about four hundred of whom were looking at a young woman with a leaf.

Nobody thanked anybody and the four of them were four trades and they did not speak to each other afterwards, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is now the fifth time in about four and a half years that four people in a market street have each understood something alone, and that the first four were about the wall on the made road at nine miles, and that nobody in nine counties has ever held a meeting about it.

Nobody said anything and the four who did the sum were the cloth seller three doors up, who is about thirty-three and has been in this market street for about eleven years, and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel, who is the mouth that says the finding of things in this county and who has never once been wrong about a sum, and a woman of about twenty-seven who keeps a shop two streets off and who is a different trade from the chandler at the low end of those boards, and the man of about twenty-six who tried to make a list of four things a week before and who stopped at the second number.

Nobody thanked him and four people in nine counties have said since that the twenty-six-year-old who stopped at the second number is the only person in nine counties who has been wrong in public about this in two consecutive weeks and has not been asked to be wrong again, and five have said that this is not a coincidence and that a man who is stopped once in a market street knows exactly what he is for.

The sum is this. It is the twenty-sixth of Thawmonth YR 322. The roll is of YR 312. It says he was twenty-nine years old when he was entered. Nine years have gone. Twenty-nine and nine is thirty-eight.

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had worked out that the roll says he is thirty-eight and five had worked out that the roll says he is something and about four had not worked out anything at all, and he is thirty-two, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that a figure and a man have been in the same street and have not agreed.

Then he said the true thing about it, out loud, in the open, in a market street, at about the tenth hour, and nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him.

“**Then there is a paper in this county that is wrong about me, and it is wrong in the direction that makes me more use to somebody. A man of thirty-eight with a master's entry behind him is a man somebody can put at a gate. A man of thirty-two is not. That is the whole of it and I am not going to say anything else about it in this street.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a man of thirty-two describe, correctly, in nine seconds, what a paper like that is for, and five had heard a man be modest, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that anybody in nine counties has said out loud that a thing about themselves was a use to somebody, and that it took a piece of paper about a road to make him say it.

Nobody thanked him and nine people in that market street had asked, in four pairs of words and about nine seconds, whether thirty-eight was the right figure, and about four of them asked him and about five asked each other

And one of the four asked him out loud, in the open, in front of about four hundred people, and nobody stopped him.

“**Then which is it?**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a man ask a question that has no answer in the room it was asked in, and five had heard a man ask the only question anybody in this county has been asking since the eighth of Embermonth. and he said it was not and did not say what the right figure was, and about four people in that market street heard that and about five did not.

Nobody said anything and another person asked him who had asked the fen county road office for that roll, and he said he did not know and had not been asked, and four people in nine counties have said since that a man who says he does not know in a market street about four hundred people is a man who has thought about it and not found out, and five have said that he does not know and has not thought about it and that those two are nine years apart.

Nobody thanked him and nobody was angry with him and nobody argued with him and nobody defended him, and nine people in that market street had wanted to say that thirty-eight was not right and four of them had the standing to and did not and five had neither and did not say it either, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the second time in about four and a half years that about nine people in nine counties wanted to say something about him and did not, and five have said that the first time was a bridge on the eighteenth of Hearthmonth.

Then the copyist said the one thing she had come down to say, and it was eighteen words, and she said it to the leaf and not to anybody.

“**That is a roll, it is nine years old, and nobody wrote it who has ever seen me.**”

Nobody thanked her and four people in that market street had heard a keeper of records say what a roll is in eighteen words and five had heard eighteen words, and nobody thanked her, and she went back up the thirteen stairs and shut the door of that room behind its own door and four people in that lane have said since that she did not ask anybody to come up and five have said that she did not send for anybody at all.

Nobody said anything and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the whole of what happened on the twenty-sixth of Thawmonth at the top of that lane, and that it is the fourth time in about four and a half years that a piece of paper has been read out in that market street, and that the first three were about a form with a fourth head on it, and about four of them have said that the fourth one is the first one about a man.

Nobody thanked him and the room over that weigh-house has a register in it and three series and a day-book and a drawer about eleven inches deep and a fourth ruled head that has been empty for about twelve months, and the roll that came up that made road is not in any of them and was not entered in the day-book and four people in nine counties have said since that a room that takes in about nine hundred sheets a month in three series has nothing at all for a paper about one person, and five have said that this is the correct practice and that she knows it is correct practice and that this is not the same as her not knowing what is in it.

Nobody said anything and the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps that room was not at the top of the stairs and was not in the lane and nobody sent for her and nobody sent anybody to her, and about four people in that market town had noticed that and five had not, and he was at the sea end of that street at about the tenth hour and did not go up the lane and nobody asked him to.

Nobody thanked him and nothing about it is in any document, and the roll is in a room that is shut behind its own door five days in seven, and nine people in nine counties have said since that there is now a piece of paper in nine counties that is wrong about him and that it is the first piece of paper about him that anybody in nine counties has ever seen, and that it came up a made road in the ordinary way in the middle of Hearthmonth and nobody has ever found out who asked for it, and four people in nine counties have said that the not finding out is the part of it that matters and five have said that a paper nobody asked for is a paper somebody wanted.

Nobody thanked anybody and the ordinary half of that Thursday went on after it, and the ordinary half is nine tenths of every day anybody in nine counties has ever had, and about two hundred people went back to about nine trades and the light came up the side of the weigh-house and found the upper edge of the boards and the boards were the colour of a wet slate.

Nobody thanked anyone and the weigh-house weighed two loads of lime and nothing else, and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that about nine hundred sheets had come past his floor in the last month across three series and that a roll of hands is not a sheet and does not come past his floor, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time anybody in nine counties has noticed that the room's practice has a blind spot in the shape of a person.

Nobody thanked him and the inn at the high end had four beds full and put a board out with a price on it in chalk, and a Crown party's cart did not come up that made road at all that day, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a market street at about the eleventh hour on a Thursday in Thawmonth is the loudest thing in nine counties and that nothing that has ever happened in it has been found out from the noise.
Nobody thanked anyone and the market went on, and the two hundred went back to about nine things, and about nine people stayed at the boards, and nobody wrote any of it down.

**And the cost of that Thursday is six years that a piece of paper has added to him and about nine hundred yards of hill that a young man described in a market street a week before it was printed on paper, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
