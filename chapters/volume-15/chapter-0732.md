# Chapter 0732

**Fallowmonth the sixth, YR 322, a Wednesday and not a market morning with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and no market in that street, four days out at the head of that made road where eight of nine yards of a wall are back in, and a man working out what one thing is.**

A Wednesday in that market town of about two thousand people is not a market morning, and nine people were in that street by the ninth hour of the sixth of Fallowmonth and four of them were at the boards with nothing on them and five were not, and the bolt on that room over the weigh-house went on from the Saturday and nobody went up the lane four hundred paces long that holds it.

Nobody thanked anybody and the first of Fallowmonth came and went on Friday and nothing was at the boards on it, and that is the ninth first of a month in a row on which nothing has happened at them, and four people in nine counties stand at those boards at about the ninth hour on a first of a month and five do not, and nobody has sent for the man who built it on any of the nine and the badge-man has not been at two of them on purpose.

Nobody said anything and the boards at the low end of that street have been standing since the middle of Longlight the year before and have been repainted twice and are the colour of a wet slate, and the cloth over the bench at the low end of them has been on it since the middle of Hearthmonth and nobody has lifted it and nobody has asked what is under it, and the lamp at the high end of them is lit at the fourth hour on every day of the week on which the light has gone off the wet slate of them and has been for about four and a half years.

Then four days off, and the ordinary half of that Wednesday is nine tenths of it, and about nine hundred people along that made road did the ordinary part of it.

Nobody thanked him and eight of the nine yards of wall at the head of that made road are back in and the gap is about a yard long and about four feet high, and the stone is quarried about two miles off on the low side and one man has been carrying it two miles on his own for a hundred and fifty-four days, and nobody in nine counties has asked him where it came from and nobody in nine counties is going to.

Nobody said anything and the drove went up that lane on the Thursday before, which was the thirtieth of Harvestmonth, at about the sixth hour, and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back down at about the fifth hour, and nobody went with it, and that is about twenty-two drove to go up that lane since the second of Mudmonth and about four hundred people in a market town have never watched one.

Nobody thanked anybody and a broom made off a hill about two miles above that man's house is still standing in that house in Fallowmonth, and he has not swept with it and nobody has asked him whether he sweeps with it, and four people in nine counties have said since that a broom standing in a house for two months is a thing a man has decided to keep and about five have said that it is a thing a man has not decided about.

Nobody said anything and the bar at the head of that made road is in on the lane side and has been in since Mudmonth, and the drove goes up through the gap at the low end of about nine hundred yards of wall and not through the gate, and nine people in nine counties know that and about five have ever watched which of the two a drove takes.

Nobody thanked him and there is about nine hundred yards of laid kerb on a far bank four days south of that market town with the water in a channel about nine hundred yards wide at the top of a tide, and about nine hundred yards of laid kerb on a shoulder two miles above the head of that made road, and four people in nine counties have said since that nobody has ever put those two figures in one sentence and five have said that the one on the far bank is about four hundred yards past a shut door and the one on the shoulder is about two miles up a lane and that nobody in nine counties has walked either of them twice.

Nobody said anything and the stone that goes into that wall is quarried about two miles off on the low side and there is no cart on it and there is no barrow and one man carries it in two trips a day in his own hands because a barrow with a wheel in it cannot go down nine hundred yards of that lane and come back, and nobody has ever asked him how he gets it up to the top of the gap and nobody is going to.

Then the hives, which are half a mile below that wall off a lane of their own that is not made, and where the frames have begun to come back.

Nobody thanked him and the young man of about nineteen who keeps about nine hives in a walled yard counted the brood on the sixth of Fallowmonth and it was three frames where there were nine on the night of the eighth of Thawmonth, and that is a hundred and seventy-eight days, and a brood frame comes back in about four weeks in a year like this one, and four people in nine counties have said since that a fourth frame is drawn and about five have said that a drawn frame is not brood.

Nobody said anything and the fourth frame in that yard was drawn on the second of Fallowmonth and about a third of it is brood and about two thirds of it is bare comb, and the young man put it in on the Sunday because it was drawn and it was in his own yard and he is not going to have more bees than the bees he has got, and nobody thanked him and nobody asked him about it.

Nobody thanked anybody and the bees in that yard had been flying about four feet off the ground all morning and going out and coming back, and about nine people in nine counties know that and about five have ever watched it, and four people have said since that a yard that was flying low for a hundred and seventy-eight days has been flying higher since the second of Fallowmonth and five have said that nobody in nine counties has measured it.

Nobody said anything and about nine brooms and about nine bee brushes were made out of four bundles of that broom in a walled yard in the third week of Rainmonth, and nine people took one off a board at the low end of that market street for nothing and about five did not, and about nine people in nine counties now own a thing off that shoulder and nobody will know in a year where it came from.

Then the east rise, and it is nine hundred yards off and about two hundred yards above that wall, and about nine acres of it are standing dead where they stand.

Nobody thanked him and the broom on that east rise was cut on the fourth of Rainmonth and nine days of it were left to be cut that year and it is standing dead where it stands, and a line in grass that is not cut closes over in about three years and nine acres of broom that is not cut goes down in about two, and four people in nine counties have said since that a man who cuts a thing once a year keeps it and about five have said that a man who cuts it once has been there once.

Nobody said anything and nine hundred yards of a line nine feet wide in the grass runs along that shoulder and nine households past nine stones at the end of it keep a rota of that flat that has been kept about nine years and has not been broken once in the nine years, and nobody crossed the stones in Rainmonth and nobody has crossed them since and nothing on the other side of them was seen by anybody on this side of them in Fallowmonth either, and the reason is said here rather than left out, and it is that there is no crossing at those stones from this side and none has been earned, and a county that cannot show what is past a line of stones is a county that has decided not to show it, and about four people in nine counties have said since that saying so out loud is the difference between a decision and an oversight and five have said that nobody out here is going to be able to tell which it was.

Nobody thanked anybody and then a question was asked out loud on that made road at about the second hour, and nobody stopped it, and it was not asked about a road and it was not asked about the stones, and it was asked by a person who wanted to know one thing and there was nobody near enough to hear the answer.

“**Are you going up on Thursday?**”

Nobody said anything and four people at the head of that made road had heard a question asked about whether a man is going up a lane on a Thursday and five had heard it asked kindly by a person who wanted to know, and nobody stopped it and the man of thirty-two with a stick answered it out loud, in the open, in his own trade, and it was 24 words, and nobody thanked him.

“**I go up on Thursdays because that is the day the beasts go and I am not going up on Thursday for anything else.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in nine counties have said since that this is the third time in about four and a half years a man of thirty-two has said out loud in this county that he is going somewhere on his own account and five have said that the third of them is the first one where he has not said what he is going up for, and nobody asked him again and nobody waited.

Nobody thanked him and a drove went up that lane on that Thursday at about the sixth hour with the man of thirty-two behind it, and the man of thirty-two did not come down with it, and the man of about fifty-four who keeps the far end of that lane came up behind the drove at about the seventh hour with a stick in his hand and about nine feet of new cord over his shoulder, and nobody arranged that and nobody asked him to.

Nobody said anything and that is the fourth time in about four and a half years that anybody has come up that lane on a Thursday behind nine beasts, and about four people in nine counties know that the man who keeps the far end of it has looked at that boundary about four times a year for about nine years and about five have ever asked him what for, and nobody in nine counties has ever asked him and nobody in nine counties is going to.

Nobody thanked anybody and the walked nine hundred yards of that line in the grass takes about a quarter of an hour at a steady pace, and the man of about fifty-four came up it at the speed of a man carrying a stick, and about four people in nine counties have said since that two people walked nine hundred yards of a line in the grass on the same morning from opposite ends and did not meet, and five have said that the walking is the part that is not in any of this.

Nobody thanked him and they did not light a fire, and there is nothing on that shoulder to burn that is not somebody's, and four people in nine counties have said since that two people who have been on a hilltop for a day in Fallowmonth and have not lit a fire is the ordinary condition of a hilltop and five have said that the reason is that nobody wanted to be the first one to put smoke on it.

Nobody said anything and they carried water up from the field with the stone wall round it, which is about nine hundred yards below that shoulder and about two hundred yards to the low side of it, and one of them went for it every morning and came back with two pails, and about four people in nine counties have said since that carrying water nine hundred yards up a hill is the only labour anybody in nine counties has ever recorded about that shoulder and five have said that it went in no document at all.

Nobody thanked anybody and the wind came off the low side at about the eighth hour and went over nine hundred yards of that shoulder and had nothing on it to stop against, and there is no wall on that shoulder and no tree on it and about nine hundred yards of it goes out flat and blue to the low side with nothing on it, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man who stands on that ground long enough stops seeing any of it and five have said that that is the ordinary condition of a hilltop.

Nobody said anything and nobody said anything else about it, and the drove came back down at about the fifth hour with nine beasts and no man, and the man of about fifty-four was still on that shoulder at the seventh hour with a stick in his hand, and the man of thirty-two was still on that shoulder at the seventh hour with his left hand in the side of his coat, and neither of them said one word to the other and about four people in nine counties have said since that two men were on a hilltop at the same time on purpose and five have said that neither of them knew it.

Nobody thanked him and the light came off the low side of that shoulder at about the seventh hour and there was nothing to see from the top of it except nine acres of a broom standing dead and about nine hundred yards of a road going away toward the low side, and a broom frame and a bee brush frame stand in a walled yard half a mile below and to the low side of it, and about four hundred people in a market town four days off do not know that either of those two things exists.

Nobody thanked him and the two miles of wall and orchard between the eleventh mile stone and the top of that hill were green and going over, and there were about nine hundred trees of them and the owners are nine households, and not one of those households has ever asked anybody anything about anybody who walks past its gate, and four people in nine counties have said since that nine households on a made road is the largest number of households anybody in nine counties can name in one place and five have said that there are more than nine on the coast and nobody here counts those.

Nobody said anything and about four hundred and nine parish rolls come past the floor of that weigh-house in a good month, and Harvestmonth is the month they come past in their best state and that month ended on Saturday, and the man who keeps that floor said out loud, to four people in and five out, that the first of Fallowmonth is the first day in four and a half years that floor has gone quiet after its best month and that nobody has ever asked him what a sheet weighs.

Nobody said anything and nothing about any of it is in a document, and there is no page in any room in nine counties with the sixth of Fallowmonth on it, and the register in the room over that weigh-house holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none and the day-book stands at eleven entries and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled.

Nobody thanked anybody and about nine hundred yards of an old road is now in about four hundred mouths and in nobody's hand, and four people in nine counties have said since that a thing in four hundred mouths is worth more than a thing in one hand because a hand can be lost in about nine days and five have said that four hundred mouths have never been known to lose one, and about four people have said that a county which has nine clerks and no way of being asked anything by anybody in it has neither a hand nor four hundred mouths of its own.

**And the cost of that Wednesday is a fourth frame drawn and not brood, eight of nine yards of a wall and a hundred and fifty-four days of carrying it, and two men on a hilltop at the same time saying nothing, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
