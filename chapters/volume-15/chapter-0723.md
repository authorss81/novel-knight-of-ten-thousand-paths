# Chapter 0723

**Longlight the thirtieth, YR 322, a Sunday and not a market morning with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and no market in that street, the last day of a month, four days out at the head of that made road where six of nine yards of a wall are back in.**

A Sunday is not a market morning and nine people are in that market street by the ninth hour and four of them are at the boards with nothing on them and five are not, and the inn at the high end puts no board out on a Sunday because it does that on Fridays and Thursdays and Saturdays, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a market town's beds are full on the two days it has a market and empty on the five it does not.

Nobody thanked anybody and Longlight ends on that Sunday and Rainmonth begins on the day after, and four people in nine counties have said since that the last day of a month is the day a county finds out what it did in the month and five have said that this county has not found out in four and a half years.

Nobody said anything and the room over that weigh-house was shut behind its own door, which it is on every day that is not a market morning, and it shuts at noon on a Saturday until the seventh hour on a Monday, and nobody went up the lane four hundred paces long that holds it and nobody sent for anybody and nothing about any of that is in a document.

Nobody thanked him and the light on that Sunday came up off the west bank and off the wet slate of those boards about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over and went down again about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over, and the lamp at the high end of them was lit at the fourth hour and stood lit until the light was gone, and four people in nine counties have said since that the lamp is the only thing in nine counties that has happened every day for four and a half years without anybody having decided that it should.

Then four days off at the head of that made road, and it is the ordinary half of a Sunday there, and it is nine tenths of every day anybody in nine counties has ever had.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about forty-one who keeps that wall has been asked one question about a road in about eleven years and has answered it once in ten words, and four people in nine counties have said since that a man who has answered once and will not answer twice is a man who has worked out what his one answer is worth, and five have said that it is worth about eleven years and nobody has offered him anything for it.

Nobody said anything and a man with nine yards to rebuild and eighty-eight days gone has to walk two miles for about nine loads a yard and carry them back on his own, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is what he has been doing every day since the second of Mudmonth and five have said that this is what he will be doing until the end of Harvestmonth, and nine people in nine counties have said since that nobody has ever offered him a barrow and that a barrow would be a thing in nine counties somebody could be thanked for.

Nobody thanked anybody and the gate at the head of that made road is a five-bar gate and the bar goes in on the lane side, and it went in on the Thursday morning after the water went out of that lane and it has been in since, and four people in nine counties have said since that it is the fifth time that gate has been barred on the lane side since it was hung and five have said that it has been barred about nine hundred times.

Nobody said anything and a woman of about thirty-nine who keeps the second gate from the top of that made road shut her own gate on the nineteenth of Longlight and put her own bar in it and stood in her own gateway, and four people in nine counties have said since that a gate shut by its keeper and a gate left are two gates and five have said that she had said so herself in a market street on a made road in Longlight.

Nobody thanked him and six of the nine yards of wall that came down on the twenty-eighth of Thawmonth are back in and the gap is three yards long and four feet high, and the man of forty-one who keeps that wall and a drove and a byre has been putting stone in it alone since the second of Mudmonth and has carried sixty loads of that stone two miles on his own in eighty-eight days, and four people in nine counties have said since that eighty-eight days is about half of one man's summer and five have said that nobody has ever asked him what his summer is.

Nobody said anything and four people in nine counties have said that nobody has ever worked out how long nine yards of wall takes one man on his own and five have said that nobody has ever asked him, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the only thing anybody in nine counties is waiting for that nobody in nine counties can put a date to and that the date is not the county's to keep.

Nobody thanked him and the drove went up nine hundred yards of that lane on the Thursday before and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back at about the fifth hour, and it does not go out on a Sunday and has not gone out on a Sunday in eleven years, and four people in nine counties have said since that the beasts know the day and five have said that the man who keeps them knows the day.

Nobody said anything and a drove of about nine beasts that knows the day has gone up that lane and come back down it about thirteen times since the second of Mudmonth, and four people in nine counties have said since that about thirteen is a Thursday every week for about three months and that it is the figure nobody has ever asked about and five have said that a figure nobody asks about is the only kind there is.

Nobody said anything and the causeway is four days south of that market town and about nine hundred paces long and about nine feet wide at the crown, with a low parapet on the seaward side that a cart cannot get over and a man can lean on, and a hand lamp is put on the coping at the low side of it every Monday afternoon in the winter and was not put there on a Sunday, and the hand bell on the gate post above it is about the size of a fist and is rung when the water is over the causeway and the water was not over it.

Nobody thanked anybody and the coat the man of thirty-two is in has about four inches of salt in the seams of the right sleeve from a causeway he crossed on foot in Goatmonth, and the seams have been washed twice since and the salt is in the cloth and not on it, and nobody in nine counties has asked him about the coat, and about four people in that market town had worked out that the coat is the coat he crossed that causeway in and about five had not.

Nobody said anything and nine yards of boards went on the inside of nine gates in the last week of Longlight this year, and about four hundred people in nine counties found out about those nine yards, and four people in nine counties have said since that a man who puts boards on the inside of gates is the only man in this county whose work is counted by the people it is for and five have said that nobody has ever counted it with him.

Nobody thanked anybody and the hives stand in a walled yard about four rods by six behind a stone wall about four feet high, and the young man of about nineteen who keeps them was in that yard on the Sunday doing a thing with nine frames of wood and about nine feet of wax and about nine strands of waxed twine, which is the winter job and the only winter job there is, and nobody in nine counties has ever asked him what he does with his hands in that month.

Nobody said anything and the brood in that yard is three frames where there were nine on the night before the eighth of Thawmonth, and it is a hundred and twelve days since that night, and a brood frame comes back in about four weeks in a year like this one, and four people in nine counties have said since that a frame a month would be four frames by now and that it is three and that nobody has worked out why.

Nobody thanked him and then the broom, and it is four days off, and the young man has said out loud in a market street four days off that he will not go up that lane until there is one to cut, and about nine people in nine counties know that and about five have ever cut one, and four have said since that this is the fourth month a man of nineteen has waited for a thing on a hill and five have said that a man of nineteen waited four months for nine frames once and got three of them back.

Then the ninth question, and it is the first one anybody in nine counties has asked about that lane that anybody could answer.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel came off that made road on the Sunday morning at about the fourth hour with his hand on a stick, and he came into that market town and went into the cloth shop three doors up and came out again, and then somebody in that street asked him out loud, kindly, at about the sixth hour, whether anybody was going up that lane before the broom was on, and about nine people in that street heard the question.

“**Is anybody going up before the broom is on?**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard the ninth question anybody in nine counties has asked about that lane and five had heard it as a question about a broom, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first question anybody in nine counties has asked about that lane that anybody in nine counties could answer, and about four people in that street had worked that out inside about nine seconds and about five had not.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel said out loud, in the open, in his own trade, that he did not know and that he was the man who says the finding of things in that market town and that he did not know, and about four people in that street had heard a man say out loud that he does not know and five had heard it and had not stopped what they were doing, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the first use anybody in nine counties has made of that man's mouth is for him to say out loud that there is nothing in it.

Nobody said anything and the young man of about nineteen was in that market town on the Sunday morning buying twine at the chandler's nine doors down, and somebody asked him out loud, in the open, kindly, at about the seventh hour, whether he was going up on the thirty-first or on the first of Rainmonth, and he said no, and then he said the rest of it out loud, in the open, to about four people in that street, in his own trade, and it was 42 words, and he did not give a reason and nobody asked him for one.

“**I am not going up on the thirty first or on the first and I am not going up on the second, and there is a broom on the fourth and it is the only broom within a day of my yard.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a young man give a date in the open and five had heard 42 words, and nobody asked him why the fourth and about nine people in nine counties have said since that the reason is a broom and that about four people have said that the reason is that a broom comes on the fourth every year and about five have said that he has never said the reason out loud and neither has anybody else.

Nobody thanked anybody and about four people in that market street had heard a young man say no to a question about a hill before anybody had said yes to a question about that hill and five had heard forty-two words, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a man who says no to four things in a market street gets a reputation for it and that a young man who keeps bees and has said no twice in about four and a half years has not.

Nobody said anything and about four people in that street had worked out inside about nine seconds that the young man said no to two questions in about an hour and about five had worked out that he said no to one question twice, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this county can now tell the difference between a man who will not go and a man who does not know and that it took nine seconds and about four people to do it.

Nobody thanked anybody and the weigh-house on that Sunday weighed one load of lime and nothing else, and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that a day with no market on it is a day on which about four hundred and nine sheets do not come past his floor, and that nobody has ever asked him what a sheet weighs.

Nobody said anything and about two hundred yards of orchard and about two miles of stone wall between the eleventh mile stone and the top of that hill were in blossom and about nine hundred trees of them, and the owners are about nine households and not one of them has ever asked anybody anything about the people who walk past its gate, and four people in nine counties have said since that the ninth mile stone on that road is cleaned by one man who nobody has ever watched do it and five have said that he cleans it about nine hundred times a year.

Nobody thanked him and then the ordinary half of that Sunday in that market town went on, which has about nine people in it, and there is no market in that street and nobody sold anything except the four things in that chandler's shop.

Nobody said anything and a chandler nine doors down put a cloth over that bench on the Sunday as he does on every day of the week, and the cloth over the bench at the low end of those boards was on it and nothing lifted it and nobody asked what was under it, and about four people in that market town had worked out that two cloths in one street have been on two benches since the middle of Hearthmonth and about five had not.

Nobody thanked anybody and about four of the nine people in that street stood at those boards and five did not, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the fourth Sunday running that four people have stood at a set of boards with nothing on them, and five have said that four people is more than the boards were built for.

Nobody said anything and then the light went off the west bank first and off those boards about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over, and about nine people went out of that market street into about nine trades and nobody stayed at the boards, and nothing about any of it is in a document and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none and the day-book stands at eleven entries.

Nobody said anything and a broom comes on that hill about the fourth of Rainmonth every year and it has been coming on about that day since anybody in nine counties can remember, and four people in nine counties have said since that a thing that comes on the same day every year for longer than a man's working life is the only calendar anybody in this county keeps, and about five have said that the day-book in the room over that weigh-house keeps a worse one and has eleven entries in it.

Nobody thanked him and the day-book in that room stands at eleven entries and the entries are not numbered by anybody and about four people in nine counties have said since that a book with eleven entries in four and a half years is a book a person can keep without deciding anything, and about five have said that it is a book nine people could each keep and none of them does.

Nobody said anything and the broom is four days off and Rainmonth is a day off and four people in nine counties have said since that there is nothing in this county that will make anybody go up a lane on the thirty-first or on the first of a month except a broom that comes on the fourth, and five have said that a man who waits four days for a thing that is coming has been waiting a great deal longer for other things.

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market town had counted how many days it is since the eighteenth of Longlight, which is when six people came down two miles of unmade road off a shoulder, and the count is twelve days, and about five had not counted and nine people in nine counties have said since that twelve days is shorter than anybody in nine counties has ever waited for a reason and four have said that it is shorter.
