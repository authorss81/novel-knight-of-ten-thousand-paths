# Chapter 0721

**Longlight the twenty-seventh, YR 322, a Thursday and a market morning, the second hour in the market street at the boards with four hundred people in it and two hundred of them stopped, the room over the weigh-house open, and two accounts of one road put in front of each other for the first time in four and a half years.**

A Thursday in that market town of two thousand people is a market morning, and four hundred people are in that street by the second hour and two hundred of them stop what they are doing, and the proportion has not moved since the first of Embermonth, and nine people in nine counties have said since that it is the only figure anybody in this county can check twice a week and has never once been written down.

Nobody thanked anybody and Longlight has three days left in it and Rainmonth is four days off and the fourth of Rainmonth is seven days off, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a month in this county is a thing that finishes and that nobody has ever worked out what a month is for.

Nobody said anything and the boards at the low end of that street have been standing since the middle of Longlight the year before and have been repainted twice and are the colour of a wet slate, and the cloth over the bench at the low end of them has been on it since the middle of Hearthmonth and nobody has lifted it and nobody has asked what is under it, and the lamp at the high end of them was not lit at the fourth hour because the light had not gone off the wet slate of them yet.

Nobody thanked him and the room over that weigh-house was open because it is a Thursday and a room over a weigh-house is open five days in seven, and the copyist of twenty-six came down the thirteen stairs at the seventh hour and went back up, and four people in that lane saw her come down and five did not, and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and the day-book stands at eleven entries and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled and none of that has moved in a month.

Nobody said anything and the first of the next month is four days off and four people in nine counties stand at those boards at about the ninth hour on a first of a month and five do not, and the hearing has not been called since the first of Goatmonth and does not sit, and nobody has sent for the man who built it on any of the five firsts that have come and gone.

Then the two accounts, and they were put in front of each other in the open for the first time in four and a half years, and neither of the two men who said them had ever heard the other one.

Nobody thanked him and the man of thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up had a piece of blue wool over his forearm and he took it off before he answered, which nobody in that street has seen him do twice, and then he said it out loud, in the open, at the low end of those boards, in his own trade, and it was 55 words.

“**A road that goes between two places has both of the places under the grass, and I have sold cloth into nine counties for nine years and nobody has ever come into my shop and asked me where either of them is. That is what a road is. It is a thing between two things.**”

Nobody said anything and about two hundred people in that market street had heard a cloth seller say out loud what a road is for and five of them did not stop what they were doing while he said it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that anybody has put that account of that road in that street, and four have said that the road is not a road and five have said that a man who sells cloth has no standing to say what a road is and said it anyway.

Nobody said anything and the second account came out of a different trade an hour after the first one, and it did not answer the first one, and the man who said it had not been in that street when the first one was said.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract for about nineteen years came in at about the fifth hour with nine feet of a new kerb standing against the wall of a shop at the low end of that street, and he put his hand flat on it the way a man does with a thing he has made, and he said it out loud, in the open, to about nine people in four trades, in his own trade, and it was 66 words.

“**A road that goes across a shoulder and does not go off it is a road that goes to a place that is not there any more, and I have laid stone on the low side of a hill for about nineteen years and I can tell you the two ends of a road that goes to a place, and both of mine are still standing.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a roadkeeper say out loud that a road across a hilltop goes to a place that has stopped existing and five had heard 66 words, and about four people in that street had worked out inside about nine seconds that the two accounts of that road do not agree with each other and about five had not, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that two trades have said two things about one road in one street and that nobody has asked either of them which of the two is right.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up answered it out loud, in the open, in his own trade, and it was 44 words, and he did not raise his voice and nobody thanked him.

“**Then it is not a road, it is a lane, and a lane that goes nowhere is the oldest thing in nine counties and I have been selling cloth beside it for about nine years and it has never once asked me for anything.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a cloth seller turn a road into a lane in the open and five had heard 44 words and had not heard the difference, and the man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract said one word back and the word was no, and nobody argued with him about it and nobody said the word back at him.

Nobody said anything and neither of those two men said where the kerb stops, and nine people in nine counties have said since that that is the whole of what two trades have for the oldest road in this county after about four and a half years in which four things were said out loud, and four have said that the man who has never once laid stone on a flat could put his hand on the stone where it stops in about nine seconds if anybody had asked him to go and stand at it, and five have said that nobody has asked him and nobody is going to.

Then the thing that neither of those two said, and it is the whole of what anybody in nine counties has.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel had been standing at the high end of those boards for about as long as it takes to turn a cart and had said nothing at all, and then he said one thing out loud, in the open, to nine people, in his own trade, and it was 47 words.

“**Nobody in nine counties can say where the kerb stops. One person has stood where it stops and has not written it down and has not said it, and the rest of us have been about four and a half years getting used to not having it.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a man say out loud that nine counties cannot say where a road stops and five had heard 47 words, and nobody asked him which road he meant because there is only one anybody in nine counties is talking about, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a man who says a thing out loud in a market street and names what is missing is doing a different trade from a man who says a thing out loud and names what is there, and about four people have said that the second is worth more and five have said that the first is the only one anybody can act on.

Nobody thanked anybody and a question about a broom came into that street at about the sixth hour and it was asked kindly and it was not asked about the lane.

“**Is the broom on?**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a question asked about a broom and five had heard it asked kindly by a person who wanted to know, and nobody stopped it and nobody answered it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first question anybody in nine counties has asked in about four and a half years that is about a thing growing on a hill and not about a road, and about four people have said that a question about a broom is a question that can be answered and five have said that this county does not know what to do with a question that can be answered.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up said one thing back about it and it was in his own trade and it was 41 words, and he put the blue wool back over his forearm before he finished it.

“**I sell cloth. I do not know what a broom is and I am not going to be asked about one twice in the same street, and there are two trades in this street today and neither of them is mine.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a cloth seller refuse to be asked about a broom and five had heard 41 words, and the man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract said nothing about a broom at all, and four people in nine counties have said since that the man who said on the first of Longlight that he would go up that lane has not said a word about a broom in three weeks and five have said that a man keeps his word in a trade and not in a street.

Nobody said anything and the drove at the head of that made road went out at about the sixth hour on that Thursday, as it goes out every Thursday, and went up about nine hundred yards of that lane through the gap at the low end of about nine hundred yards of stone wall and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back down at about the fifth hour, and nobody went with it, and about nine people in nine counties know that and about five have ever watched it happen.

Nobody thanked him and about six of the nine yards of wall at the head of that made road came down on the twenty-eighth of Thawmonth and have been going back in since the second of Mudmonth, and the gap is about three yards long and about four feet high, and the stone is quarried about two miles off on the low side and one man has been carrying it two miles on his own for eighty-five days, and nine people in nine counties know that and about five have ever asked him where it came from.

Nobody said anything and the hives stand in a walled yard four rods by six behind a stone wall four feet high, half a mile below that wall, off a lane of their own that is not made, and the young man of about nineteen who keeps them counted the brood on the twenty-seventh of Longlight and it was three frames in two hives where there were nine on the night before the eighth of Thawmonth, and a brood frame comes back in about four weeks in a year like this one, and it is a hundred and nine days since that night.

Nobody thanked him and the bees in that yard had been flying four feet off the ground all morning and going out and coming back with nothing, and four people in nine counties have said since that bees fly low when there is nothing out and that the same yard has been flying low since the eighth of Thawmonth and that nobody has ever asked him about it twice.

Nobody said anything and then the middle of that morning happened the way the middle of every morning in that street happens, and about two hundred people went back to about nine trades, and the near wheel of a cart came off at the low end and the carter got it up again with a bar in about nine minutes and nobody stopped walking.

Nobody thanked anybody and two women at the third door argued about a bolt of cloth for about four minutes and then went into the shop and came out with it wrapped, which is how that argument ends nine times in ten, and the inn at the high end put a board out with the price of a bed on it in chalk and had four beds full, and the woman of about fifty-one who keeps that house came out into that street at about the fifth hour and stood nine feet off the boards and did not come closer.

Nobody said anything and the weigh-house weighed two loads of lime and a load of wool and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that about four hundred and nine parish rolls come past his floor in a good month and that the last month was the best month that weigh-house has had since the middle of Harvestmonth, and four people in nine counties have said since that the only good news anybody in this county has had since the second of Mudmonth is about sheets and that nobody has ever asked him what a sheet weighs.

Nobody thanked him and a chandler nine doors down put a cloth over a bench rather than write anything at all on it, and nobody has lifted that cloth since the middle of Hearthmonth and nobody has asked what is under it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that asking would be the only way to find out and that nobody in nine counties has asked.

Nobody said anything and the man of about thirty-two with a stick stood at the sea end of that street for the whole morning with his right hand on it and nobody looked at him and nobody asked him anything, and about four people in that street had worked out that he has been in this county about four and a half years and has never once been in a trade here and about five had worked out nothing.

Nobody thanked them and about four people in that market street had heard four things said out loud about that lane since the twelfth of Thawmonth and five reasons on the first of Longlight and two accounts of a road this morning and a question about a broom, and about four of them believe the four things and about five believe the accounts, and four people in nine counties have said since that a person who believes one of two accounts of a road and not the other is worse off than a person who believes neither.

Nobody said anything and the hearing has not been called since the first of Goatmonth and cannot be called and does not sit, and its first of a month is four days off and will come on a Monday, and six firsts have now come and gone in a row with nothing on those boards and nobody has sent for anybody and nine people in nine counties have said since that the seventh first of a month in this volume's counting is the first one anybody in nine counties has been able to predict with any confidence.

Nobody said anything and then the light went off the west bank and off those boards about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over, and the lamp at the high end of them was lit and stood lit until the market was gone, and about four hundred people went out of that market street into about nine trades and about nine people stayed at the boards.

Nobody thanked anybody and nothing about any of it is in a document, and there is no page in any room in nine counties with two accounts of one road on it, and the register in the room over that weigh-house holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none, and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is now the second time in about four and a half years that two trades in one county have disagreed in the open about a road and that the first time nobody wrote it down either.

**And the cost of that Thursday is two accounts of one road that do not agree with each other, nine feet of a new kerb standing against a shop wall, and three frames of brood where there were nine, are not in it and none of them has a price.**

**a sheep peg at 1, a hurdle hoop at 2, a tallow dip at 3, a gate pin at 4, a hurdle at 5, a hand bar at 6, a bight of rope at 7, a hand adze at 8, a barrow wheel at 9, and a hurdle rail at 21.** One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine and twenty-one is sixty-six pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and sixty-six pence is one mark and eighteen over.** Two trades in one market street said two things out loud about the oldest road in this county and neither of them said where the kerb stops, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a road about which two trades disagree in the open in about four and a half years has still got nobody in nine counties who can say where it stops, and five have said that the man who has stood where it stops did not say it then and has not said it since.
