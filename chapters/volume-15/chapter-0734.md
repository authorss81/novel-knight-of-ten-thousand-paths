# Chapter 0734

**Fallowmonth the twenty-second, YR 322, a Friday and not a market morning with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and no market in that street, four days out at the head of that made road where eight of nine yards of a wall are back in, and a man who has been refused once and is not going to be refused twice for asking.**

A Friday in that market town of about two thousand people is not a market morning, and nine people were in that street by the ninth hour of the twenty-second of Fallowmonth and four of them were at the boards with nothing on them and five were not, and nobody went up the lane four hundred paces long that holds the room over that weigh-house.

Nobody thanked anybody and that is the eighth day since a man asked the man who keeps the far end of that lane for one thing out loud on a shoulder above a drove road, and about nine people in nine counties know that and about five have ever heard it said, and nobody has asked either of the two of them about it since and nobody is going to.

Nobody said anything and the boards at the low end of that street have been standing since the middle of Longlight the year before and are the colour of a wet slate, and the cloth over the bench at the low end of them has been on it since the middle of Hearthmonth and nobody has lifted it and nobody has asked what is under it, and the lamp at the high end of them is lit at the fourth hour on every day of the week on which the light has gone off the wet slate of them.

Then four days off, and the ordinary half of that Friday is nine tenths of it, and the drove went up that lane on the Thursday before.

Nobody thanked him and the wind was off the low side all day on the twenty-second of Fallowmonth and it came across nine hundred yards of that shoulder with about nine acres of a broom standing dead on it and about nine hundred yards of a drove road under nine beasts' feet on it, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a wind on a shoulder is a different wind from a wind on a road because there is nothing on a shoulder for it to stop against and five have said that the reason the drove goes up nine hundred yards and stops at the wall is that a beast will not go past a wall it did not go past last week.

Nobody said anything and about nine hundred yards of a line nine feet wide in the grass runs along that shoulder and nine households past nine stones at the end of it keep a rota of that flat that has been kept about nine years and has not been broken once in the nine years, and nobody has walked past the ninth of those stones since the seventeenth of Longlight and nobody is going to and about four people in nine counties have said since that a boundary which is kept without a head on it is the only thing in this county that has been kept exactly and five have said that it is kept because nobody has ever asked it for anything.

Nobody thanked anybody and the hearing has not been called since the first of Goatmonth and cannot be called and does not sit, and its first of a month is nine days off and will come on a Sunday, and about four people in nine counties stand at those boards at about the ninth hour on a first of a month and five do not, and nobody has sent for the man who built it on any of the nine firsts that have come and gone since and about nine people in nine counties have said since that a first of a month in this county is a day the calendar turns and not a day anybody attends.

Nobody said anything and the room over that weigh-house is open five days in seven and was shut behind its own door on that Friday with the bolt on from the Saturday, and the copyist of about twenty-six did not come down the thirteen stairs and did not go up them, and about four people in that lane had worked out that she was not in and five had not worked it out and never will, and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and the day-book stands at eleven entries and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled and none of that has moved in about four and a half years.

Nobody thanked him and then the weigh-house on that Friday weighed one load of lime and a quarter of a load of wool and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that about four hundred and nine parish rolls come past his floor in a good month and that Fallowmonth is a month with a wet half in it, and about four people in nine counties have said since that this is the only good news anybody in this county has had since the first of Harvestmonth and five have said that it is about sheets and about a quarter of a load of wool.

Nobody said anything and the inn at the high end put no board out on a Friday and had four beds empty for the ninth time since the middle of Harvestmonth, and the woman of about fifty-one who keeps that house came out into that street at about the fifth hour and stood about nine feet off the boards and did not come closer, and a chandler nine doors down put a cloth over a bench rather than write anything at all on it.

Nobody thanked anybody and the near wheel of a cart came off at the low end of that street and the carter got it up again with a bar in about nine minutes and nobody stopped walking, and about four people in that street had worked out that a cart went up the made road on the Wednesday with paper in it and that nobody in nine counties has ever sent anything up that made road out of a market town and five had worked out nothing at all.

Nobody said anything and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel was in that market town on the twenty-second of Fallowmonth and said nothing in that street for about as long as it takes to turn a cart, and he has said nine things out loud in that street in about four and a half years and said the ninth of them on the thirteenth of Rainmonth and has refused to say anything twice since, and about four people in that street had worked out that a man who said nine things and then said nothing for about four months is a man waiting to see whether anybody acts on the ninth and five had worked out that he has run out and about four have said that both of those are the same thing.

Nobody thanked him and then the light went off the west bank first and off those boards about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over, and about nine people went out of that market street into about nine trades and four stayed at the boards, and the lamp at the high end of them was lit at the fourth hour and stood lit until the light was gone, and nobody in that street has ever turned it out early.

Nobody thanked him and nine beasts went up about nine hundred yards of that lane at about the sixth hour on the sixteenth of Fallowmonth and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back down at about the fifth hour, and nobody went with it, and that is about twenty-four drove to go up that lane since the second of Mudmonth and nobody has ever written any of them down.

Nobody said anything and eight of the nine yards of wall at the head of that made road are back in and the gap is about a yard long and about four feet high, and one man has been carrying stone about two miles on his own for a hundred and seventy days, and nobody in nine counties has asked him where it came from and nobody asked him on Friday and nobody is going to.

Nobody thanked anybody and the bar at the head of that made road is in on the lane side and has been in since Mudmonth and it is four feet of ash with two stones on the end of it and it weighs sixty pounds with the stones, and he is forty-one and keeps a drove and a byre and nine hundred yards of wall and has been asked one question about a road in eleven years, and nobody asked him a second one on the twenty-second of Fallowmonth and about nine people in nine counties have said since that nobody is going to either.

Nobody said anything and the brood in a walled yard about half a mile below that wall is four frames where there were nine on the night of the eighth of Thawmonth, and that is a hundred and ninety-four days, and a brood frame comes back in about four weeks in a year like this one, and about four people in nine counties have said since that three frames on the sixth of Fallowmonth and four frames on the twenty-second is a thing that happens in a trade and five have said that it is the first time in about four and a half years that a figure in this county has gone up and been checked by anybody.

Nobody thanked him and the young man of about nineteen who keeps them said out loud, in the open, in his own walled yard, to about four people, in his own trade, that the fourth frame was drawn on the second of Fallowmonth and that he put it in because it was drawn and he is not going to make more bees than the bees he has got, and nobody thanked him and nobody stopped him.

Nobody said anything and about four people in that yard had heard a young man give a reason for putting a frame in and five had heard it, and nobody argued with him about it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a young man who has said out loud that he is not going to make more bees than he has got has then had one go up and nobody has worked out whether that was an accident or a trade.

Then the cart, which came up on the Wednesday, and it has been in that market town for three days.

Nobody thanked him and the cart that came up the made road with paper in it reached the market town at the low end of that street on the fifteenth of Fallowmonth and stood outside the Crown's road office for two days and went on up the made road on the twentieth, and about four people in that street had worked out that a cart with paper in it came up four days of made road and stopped outside an office and went on again and five had worked out nothing.

Nobody said anything and there are nine clerks in that road office and about twenty-one counties are still on a list in it that nobody in nine counties has read past the ninth, and about four people in that market town know that and about five have read the ninth, and about nine people in nine counties have said since that a county which has nine clerks in it and no way of being asked anything by anybody in it is a county that is on a list and nothing else, and five have said that this county is not on the list and that nobody in nine counties has checked.

Nobody thanked anybody and then a question was asked out loud on that made road four days off at about the third hour and nobody stopped it, and it was not asked about the stones and not asked about the shoulder, and it was asked by a person who wanted to know one thing and there was one other person near enough to hear the answer.

“**Did he say why?**”

Nobody said anything and four people at the head of that made road had heard a question asked about whether a man gave a reason when he said no and five had heard it asked kindly by a person who wanted to know, and nobody stopped it and the man of thirty-two with a stick answered it out loud, in the open, in his own trade, and it was 39 words, and nobody thanked him.

“**He said no and he said why and the why was that he is not a head and has never been asked to be one. That is not a reason for the road. That is a reason for him.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market town had heard a man of thirty-two say out loud that a refusal was about the person who gave it and five had heard 39 words, and nobody asked him a second question and nobody asked him who he would ask next, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years a man in this county has said out loud that a refusal belongs to the person who gave it and five have said that nobody in nine counties has ever said that out loud.

Nobody thanked anybody and then he said the second part of it, out loud, in the open, in his own trade, to about four people, and it was 42 words, and nobody stopped it.

“**I have got one thing left to ask for and I am going to ask the road office for it, and I am going to ask them out loud in a market street and I am not going to write to them.**”

Nobody said anything and four people at the head of that made road had heard a man of thirty-two say out loud that he was going to ask the road office for something and five had heard 42 words, and nobody said the road office could not be asked anything and nobody said it could, and about four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years anybody in this county has said out loud that he was going to put a thing in front of one of nine clerks and five have said that the man who keeps four miles of made road said the same thing in the opposite direction in the middle of Harvestmonth and nobody put the two together.

Nobody said anything and the man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract for about nineteen years was on that made road on the twenty-second of Fallowmonth and did not say anything at all, and about four people in nine counties have said since that he was not there and five have said that a man who has said out loud that he is not going to ask is not the man anybody looks for.

Nobody thanked him and the light came up off the coping on that wall about nine hundred yards below and to the east and the man of forty-one was in his own place and was not on that shoulder and was not at the gate, and the broom made off a hill two miles above his house was standing in his house and nobody has asked him what it is for and nobody has asked him this year.

Nobody said anything and the two miles of wall and orchard between the eleventh mile stone and the top of that hill were green and going over and about nine hundred trees of them, and the fourth days of that made road are hard at the two ends and soft in the middle, and about nine hundred people live along it and every one of them knows which house the man of thirty-two comes out of, and four of them have offered him a bed since the middle of Frostmonth and about five have not, and nobody in nine counties has ever asked him why he walks it.

Nobody thanked anybody and then the last of that Friday, and it is four days on from a shoulder and it is in a market town, and about nine hundred yards of an old road is in about four hundred mouths and in nobody's hand and one refusal is in two mouths, and about four people in nine counties have said since that this county has never had two refusals in it and five have said that it has had about nine and has never called them anything.

Nobody said anything and nothing about any of it is in a document, and there is no page in any room in nine counties with the twenty-second of Fallowmonth on it, and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled.

**And the cost of that Friday is one refusal that is in two mouths, eight of nine yards of a wall and a hundred and seventy days of carrying it, and four frames of brood where there were nine on the night of the eighth of Thawmonth, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
