# Chapter 0733

**Fallowmonth the fourteenth, YR 322, a Thursday and a market morning in that market town four days off with no market on this made road, the sixth hour at the head of the made road where nine beasts go out through a gap in a wall, a day on a shoulder above a drove road, and the first of two things a man of thirty-two goes out and asks for.**

Nobody thanked anybody and nine beasts went out through the gap at the low end of about nine hundred yards of stone wall at about the sixth hour on a Thursday in Fallowmonth, as they have gone out on every Thursday since the second of Mudmonth, and they went up about nine hundred yards of that lane and stood in the field with the stone wall round it, and nobody went with them except a man of thirty-two with a stick, and about five hundred people in a market town four days off do not know that this happens.

Nobody said anything and the man of about fifty-four who keeps the far end of that lane came up behind the drove at about the seventh hour with a stick in his hand and about nine feet of new cord over his shoulder, and his name is Tarr Lyster and about nine people in nine counties have said since that his name had not been said out loud in a market street in this county in about four and a half years and that nobody in about nine counties knew it had ever been said at all.

Nobody thanked him and he has looked at the boundary of that common about four times a year for about nine years and nobody has ever asked him what for and nobody in nine counties is going to, and he is not a head of anything and there is no head on that flat and there never has been and nobody has ever asked him to be one.

Nobody said anything and the man of about fifty-four who keeps the far end of that lane had walked up that line in the grass from the far side of it and had about two hundred yards of grass between the drove's track and the line, and he did not come up to the drove and he did not come up to the man, and four people in nine counties have said since that a man who keeps a boundary does not walk a boundary in front of anybody and five have said that he came up it in front of nine beasts and one man and that is the company he keeps.

Nobody thanked him and about nine households keep beasts on that flat past those stones and seven of them had stock on it in the week of the twenty-eighth of Longlight and two of them did not, and nobody has ever asked any of the nine about the rota and nobody is going to, and about four people in nine counties know that the rota is older than the question and five have never worked out that there is a question.

Nobody said anything and the wind came off the low side at about the seventh hour and went across about nine hundred yards of that shoulder and had nothing on it to stop against, and nine acres of a broom that was cut on the fourth of Rainmonth is standing dead where it stands on an east rise about two hundred yards above the gap in that wall, and about four people in nine counties know that and about five have ever been on it.

Then the ask, and it is the first of two, and it was made out loud and in the open and nobody arranged it.

Nobody thanked him and the man of thirty-two with a stick said it out loud, in the open, on that shoulder, at about the eighth hour, to one person, in his own trade, and it was 26 words, and nobody stopped it and nobody had told anybody it was going to be said.

“**I want to go past the ninth of the nine stones and stand on that flat and come back down and say what is on it.**”

Nobody said anything and about one person on that shoulder had heard a man ask out loud for the thing he has wanted in about four and a half years and five had heard 26 words, and nobody repeated the question back to him and nobody asked him why he wanted it, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years a man in this county has asked anybody for anything and five have said that the reason nobody asked him why is that nobody in this county has ever been asked a why.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about fifty-four who keeps the far end of that lane did not answer it at once, and he looked at the ground at his own feet for about as long as it takes to turn a cart, and then he said it out loud, in the open, on that shoulder, to one person, in his own trade, and it was 85 words, and he did not say it again.

“**I have got four yards of line in this grass and nine houses behind me on that flat and not one of them has ever been asked anything by anybody. I cannot answer for nine people and I am not going to be asked to be a head, and you are not going past the ninth of those stones on my account, and you are not going past them on anybody's, and that is the whole of what I have got to say about it.**”

Nobody said anything and about one person on that shoulder had heard the man who keeps the far end of that lane refuse something out loud and five had heard 85 words, and nobody argued with him and nobody thanked him and nobody said the word manners back at him, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years a person in this county has been refused out loud by somebody who keeps a boundary and five have said that the reason he was not asked a question about it first is that nobody in nine counties had thought of asking.

Nobody thanked him and then the man of thirty-two said one thing back, and it was short, and it was in his own trade, and nobody stopped it, and it was 31 words, and nobody repeated it afterwards.

“**Then that is the answer and I am not going to ask you it twice. That is the end of it and I will not come up here about it again.**”

Nobody said anything and about one person on that shoulder had heard a man accept a refusal out loud in about as long as it takes to turn a cart and five had heard 31 words, and nobody said anything back to him and nobody thanked him, and about four people in nine counties have said since that accepting a refusal out loud on a hilltop is a thing this county has no word for and five have said that there is no word for it here because it has never happened.

Nobody thanked anybody and then the ordinary half of that Thursday went on, and it is nine tenths of every day anybody in nine counties has ever had, and there is no wall on that shoulder and no tree on it and about nine hundred yards of it is not level with a rise in it going east.

Nobody said anything and the drove came back down that lane at about the fifth hour with nine beasts and no man, and the two of them were still on that shoulder at the seventh hour, and about four people in nine counties have said since that two men stood nine hundred yards apart on that shoulder for the best part of a day and said a total of about a hundred words and five have said that the hundred words are the most that has ever been said on that ground in one day.

Nobody thanked him and the frost on the tops of the nine stones that morning was about a sixteenth of an inch and about an inch on the grass either side of them, and the man of thirty-two cleaned about four hundred yards of the grass off the top of two of the nine with a bit of stick because a stone under nine inches of grass is not a stone anybody can see, and nobody thanked him and about nine people in nine counties have said since that the second time anybody has cleaned one of those stones it was the same man and five have said that nobody has ever asked him why he does it.

Nobody said anything and nine yards of a drove road above a made road is not a place where anybody spends a day and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man who spends a day there is a man who is looking for something and five have said that he is not looking for anything and he is waiting for a man to come up behind a drove, and nobody in nine counties has ever asked him what he is waiting for.

Nobody thanked him and nobody put a foot past the ninth of the nine stones and nothing on the other side of them was seen by anybody on this side of them, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man who has been refused at the ninth of nine stones has no reason to go past them and five have said that a man who has not been refused has no reason either and that is the whole of what four and a half years has produced on that flat.

Nobody said anything and a rope of about nine feet of new cord went over his shoulder and the other end of it was in his hand, and he said nothing about it and nobody asked him about it, and four people in nine counties have said since that nine feet of cord is the length of the ninth of nine stones with nothing left over and five have said that a man who counts a thing in his own trade counts it in lengths and not in yards.

Nobody thanked anybody and the nine stones are about the height of a man's knee and about as wide as two hands and they are set at the same height along the top of them, and nobody on that shoulder has ever found out why, and four people on that shoulder had looked at the flat past them for about as long as it takes to turn a cart and five had looked at the ground at their own feet instead.

Nobody said anything and the man of about fifty-four packed his cord and his stick and went down the nine hundred yards of the line in the grass at about the ninth hour, and the man of thirty-two stayed, and about four people in nine counties have said since that one man went back down that line in the grass because he had been asked a question and one stayed up because he had asked it and five have said that neither of those reasons would survive anybody who had not been on that shoulder.

Nobody thanked anybody and they carried water up from the field with the stone wall round it, which is about nine hundred yards below them and about two hundred yards to the low side, and one of them went for it every morning and came back with two pails, and nine people in nine counties have said since that about nine hundred yards of a made road and about nine hundred yards of a drove road and about nine hundred yards of grass is the ordinary account of one day in this county and five have said that the grass is the only one of the three anybody counts.

Nobody said anything and the light came off the low side of that shoulder at about the seventh hour and the wind came across about nine hundred yards of it from the low side, and there is nothing to see from the top of a shoulder at the seventh hour in Fallowmonth except nine acres of a broom standing dead and about nine hundred yards of a road going away toward the low side and a stone wall nine hundred yards below it with a lamp on it that is not lit.

Nobody thanked him and from the top of that shoulder a person can see the coping on a wall nine hundred yards below with a lamp on it that is not lit, and a yard with nine hives in it half a mile below that and to the low side, and the sea four days south of that, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man who has been refused at a line of stones will go and look at a sea four days off and five have said that he will not and that he will go and look at the nine acres.

Nobody said anything and a question came into that shoulder at about the ninth hour from the man who had been refused and nobody stopped it and nobody answered it, and it was the tenth question anybody in nine counties has asked about that lane and the fourth one nobody could answer.

“**Then what is nine hundred yards of kerbed road for, if nobody may walk past the end of it?**”

Nobody said anything and about one person on that shoulder had heard a man ask out loud what nine hundred yards of an old kerbed road is for after being refused at the end of it and five had not heard it, and nobody answered it and the man of thirty-two answered it himself about as long as it takes to turn a cart later and said 11 words, and four people in nine counties have said since that the tenth question anybody in nine counties has asked about that lane was asked by the man who had just been refused at it and five have said that this is the first of the ten that was asked by somebody with a reason that is his own.

Nobody thanked him and he said it out loud, in the open, to one person, in his own trade, and it was 13 words, and nobody stopped it.

“**It is for the nine households to know where their own boundary is.**”


Nobody said anything and about nine hundred yards of an old road is now in about four hundred mouths and in nobody's hand, and a refusal is now in two mouths, and four people in nine counties have said since that a county which cannot hold a finding cannot hold a refusal either and five have said that a refusal is easier to hold than a finding because everybody heard the same one at the same time.

Nobody said anything and the drove had gone up about nine hundred yards of that lane at about the sixth hour and had come back down at about the fifth hour of the same day, and the track it makes is about nine feet wide and it is not kerbed and nobody has ever kerbed it, and the grass on either side of that track has been killed by nine beasts going over it twice a week since the second of Mudmonth and it will not come back this year, and four people in nine counties have said since that the only road on that hill anybody in this county drives beasts over is nine feet wide because beasts make it and five have said that a kerb there would be under nine inches of dead grass by Fallowmonth.

Nobody thanked him and then there was a long stretch of that afternoon in which nothing happened at all, and the wind dropped at about the eleventh hour and came up again at about the second, and the two of them did not eat and there was nothing on that shoulder to eat that either of them had brought, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a day on a hilltop in Fallowmonth is a day of about fourteen hours of walking and about four hours of standing and five have said that the four hours of standing are the ones anybody remembers.

Nobody said anything and the road a man can see from the top of that shoulder goes away toward the low side and about nine hundred yards of it is kerbed and then about nine hundred yards of it is a line nine feet wide in the grass, and the kerb is older than the cutting and nobody in nine counties can say by how much, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man standing at the wrong end of a road can see the whole of it and cannot tell you where it stops and five have said that the one person who can tell you where it stops has not been asked.


Nobody thanked anybody and nothing about any of it is in a document, and there is no page in any room in nine counties with the fourteenth of Fallowmonth on it, and the register in the room over that weigh-house holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and about four people in nine counties have said since that the first refusal ever made out loud on that shoulder is not in anything and five have said that there is nothing to put it in.

**And the cost of that Thursday is about nine hundred yards of a grass line walked in both directions, one refusal given out loud by a man who keeps a boundary and did not explain himself, and about a hundred words said on a hilltop, are not in it and none of them has a price.**

**a pot of tallow at 1, a wall peg at 2, a straw skirt at 3, a gate shoe at 4, a wicket at 5, a sheet of parchment at 6, a hand spike at 7, a mooring ring at 8, a hod at 9, and a hurdle hoop at 12.** One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine and twelve is fifty-seven pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and fifty-seven pence is one mark and nine over.** A man of thirty-two with a stick asked the man who keeps the far end of that lane for one thing on a shoulder above a drove road, out loud, in the open, with nobody arranged, and the man who keeps the far end of that lane said no out loud in his own trade and did not explain himself, and the man of thirty-two accepted it out loud in about as long as it takes to turn a cart, and nobody argued and nobody thanked either of them.
