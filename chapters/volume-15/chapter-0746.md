# Chapter 0746

**Wolfmonth the twenty-fifth, YR 322, a Friday and not a market morning with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door, the fourth hour at the head of that made road with three people standing at the low end of nine hundred yards of light, and ten roads held at once by a man standing on the grass below them.**

Nobody thanked anybody and that light has been on the top of that shoulder at about the fourth hour of every morning since the twenty-sixth of Embermonth, and that is thirty mornings, and four people in nine counties had worked out that the whole of this county is going to stand on a hill on the twenty-fifth of Wolfmonth and five had not.

Nobody said anything and a drove went up that lane four days off on the Thursday before, which was the twenty-fourth of Wolfmonth, at about the sixth hour, and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back down at about the fifth hour, and nobody went with it, and that is thirty-four drove to go up that lane since the second of Mudmonth and nobody has ever written any of them down.

Eight of the nine yards of wall at the head of that made road are back in and the gap is about a yard long and about four feet high, and the stone is quarried two miles off on the low side and one man has been carrying it two miles in his own hands for two hundred and thirty-three days, and the last yard is about nine feet of a course and he has not started it, and nobody in nine counties has asked him when he will and nobody is going to.

The brood in a walled yard half a mile below that wall is five frames where there were nine on the night of the eighth of Thawmonth, and that night is two hundred and fifty-seven days back, and the fifth frame came in on the twentieth of Embermonth and has been thirty-five days coming and is not all brood yet and is not going to be hurried, and about four people in nine counties have said since that five frames is the figure that has stood for about five weeks and five have said that a figure that stands still while nine of something else have gone is a trade and not a finding and about four have said that a young man of about nineteen who has five frames of brood and nine hives has not put a sixth in and five have said that nobody has asked him.

On that east rise the broom has been off for about nine days and about nine hundred yards of a line nine feet wide is left standing where it stands, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a line in grass that is not cut closes over in about three years and five have said that this one has been standing thirty mornings in the dark and that no grass has been cut on it and about four have said that no grass has grown on it either and five have said that nobody has been up there to look.

The weigh-house weighed one load of lime and nothing else that morning, and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that Wolfmonth is twenty-five days old and about two hundred and fifty sheets have come past that floor in it, and that a sheet is about four hundred yards of it if a man wants the whole of one and about four inches of it if he wants the top of it, and that nobody has ever asked him what a sheet weighs.

The far bank is four days south of that market town and about four hundred people live across nine hundred yards of water at the top of a tide and about four hundred at the bottom, and a hand lamp goes on the coping at the low side of that causeway every Monday afternoon in the winter and the twenty-fifth of Wolfmonth was a Friday and the lamp was not put there, and the hand bell on the gate post above that coping is about the size of a fist and is rung when the water is over the causeway, and the water was not over it, and nobody in that market street has ever asked a person at a gate at the near end of that water anything at all.

The two miles of wall and orchard between the eleventh mile stone and the top of that hill are a mile of stone on the high side and a mile of orchard on the low side, and about nine hundred trees on that low side went out of blossom in the second week of Fallowmonth and the fruit came down into four hundred yards of grass and was still in it in the spring, and the owners are nine households and not one of them has ever asked anybody anything about anybody who walks past its gate, and about four people in nine counties have said since that nine households own two miles of a made road in this county and that on the twenty-sixth of Wolfmonth one of them has a neighbour nine hundred yards above him with a mile of stone on the high side and that nobody has ever asked either of them about it.

About nine households keep beasts on the flat past those nine stones and the rota of that flat has been kept about nine years and no third household came up between the eighth of Rainmonth and the seventeenth and nobody asked it for a reason, and the reason would be the rota, and about four people in nine counties know that the reason a household does not come up a lane is that nine other households have been keeping a boundary in that grass for about nine years and five have said that nine years is not old and about four have said that it is older than nine and five have said that nobody in nine counties has ever found out how old it is.

Nobody thanked him and then a question came into that shoulder at about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over from a person who wanted to know one thing, and nobody stopped it, and it was asked kindly, and it was not about a road.

“**What will it look like in the morning?**”

Nobody said anything and the man of thirty-two with a stick answered it out loud, in the open, on that grass, to three people, in his own trade, and it was 39 words, and nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him.

“**Nine feet wide and about nine hundred yards long and it will look the same in about three years, and nothing about it is going to close over, and it is not going to be the end of it.**”

Then the fourth hour, and it was before the light, and nine people in nine counties knew that three of them were going to be on that hill and eight did not.

Nobody thanked him and the man of thirty-two with a stick stood at about four hundred paces off at the low end of that shoulder with the roadkeeper of about fifty-two on his right and the young man of about nineteen on his left and the woman of about fifty-one nine feet behind him with a lamp she had not lit, and the man of forty-one was at the gate below with his hand flat on the ash of it and had said nothing at all and had not come up, and nobody had asked him anything since the first of Wolfmonth and nobody was going to.

Nobody said anything and he said it out loud, in the open, on that grass, at about the fourth hour, to three people, in his own trade, and it was 28 words, and nobody stopped it.

“**There are ten roads open tonight and I cannot hold ten and keep this one lit, and I am asking the three of you to hold one each.**”

Nobody said anything and one thing came into him at about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over and it was not a voice and nobody else on that shoulder heard anything, and he has never told anybody what it was and nobody in nine counties has asked him.

**What is held by one hand is taken by one hand.**

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about fifty-two said it back out loud, in the open, on that grass, to two people, in his own trade, and it was 39 words, and nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him.

“**I have held a wall up with a wedge and a man on the other side of it for about nineteen years and I will hold one of yours and I am not going to be thanked for it.**”

Nobody said anything and the young man of about nineteen said it out loud, in the open, on that grass, to two people, in his own trade, and it was 41 words, and nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him.

“**My bees go out when the sun is on that east rise and not before, and I will hold one of yours from the fourth hour until the sun is on the rise and I am not going to be thanked.**”

Nobody thanked him and the woman of about fifty-one said it out loud, in the open, on that grass, to two people, in her own trade, and it was 44 words, and nobody stopped her and nobody thanked her.

“**I have kept a house open at the fourth hour every morning of this year and I will keep one of yours open too and I will not be thanked for it, and I want a fire laid for four people and not nine.**”

Nobody said anything and that is ten, and seven of them have been there about four and a half years and he knows the weather on each of them the way a man knows the weather on four days of made road, and one of them has a bell going on it and one of them has a bell that has stopped and he does not know whose, and one of them has nothing on it at all, and four people in nine counties have said since that a man holding ten things on a hilltop in the dark is a man with ten things on him and five have said that eight of the ten have somebody awake at the other end and two have not and four have said that the two are the ones he is holding harder.

Nobody thanked anybody and that light went out from the low end of it upward about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over, and it did not go out the way a fire goes out, and when it was gone there was nine hundred yards of the nine-foot line in the grass with the grass dead inside it, and it is nine feet wide and about nine hundred yards long and it is not a fire and there is nothing burnt on it and nothing on it, and nobody has put it back and nobody in nine counties can.

Nobody said anything and he could not get up off that grass on the first attempt, and he put the flat of his stick down and got up on the second, and he was no quicker the third time he tried it that morning and nobody on that shoulder saw him try the second time, and four people in nine counties have said since that a man of thirty-two with a bad leg has carried a bar about four and a half years and that on the twenty-fifth of Wolfmonth he could not get off the ground with it and five have said that a leg that has been bad for four and a half years got worse in about nine seconds and that nobody in nine counties was told about it until the ninth hour.

Nobody thanked him and then the man of about fifty-two said one thing to him from his right hand side at about as long as it takes to turn a cart and he did not hear one word of it, and he said it out loud, in the open, on that grass, to one person, in his own trade, and it was 6 words, and nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him.

“**Say that again on the left.**”

Nobody said anything and the man of about fifty-two said it again on his left and he heard it, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man of thirty-two with a stick has asked one thing to be said twice in about four and a half years and five have said that the thing was not said twice and that it was not heard and about four have said that nobody in nine counties has ever been told that a man on that shoulder could not hear from the side his stick is on and five have said that nobody asked.

The nine stones at the end of that line are about the height of a man's knee and about as wide as two hands and the tops of them are all at the same height, and the dead grass stops at the ninth of the nine stones and does not go past them, and it has not gone past them and nobody has been past them since the seventeenth of Longlight, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a thing that stops at the ninth of nine stones is a thing that somebody decided and five have said that it is nine hundred yards long and that nine hundred yards is not a decision anybody in nine counties remembers.

Nobody thanked anybody and then the light came off the west bank first and off those boards about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over, and five of the nine people in that street went out of it into about five trades and four stayed at the boards, and the lamp at the high end of them was lit at the fourth hour and stood lit until the light was gone, and nobody in that street has ever turned it out early.

Nobody said anything and nobody thanked anybody and nobody in nine counties has consoled him and nobody has said one word to him about it, and four people in nine counties have said since that a man has crossed nine hundred yards of light on a hilltop and has lost the use of one ear and cannot get off the ground without a bar and five have said that nobody in that market street heard about either of those two things and about four have said that about nine people knew about one of them and about five have said that this county has never had anybody in it do a thing at four in the morning and then stand in a market street at nine and be asked nothing about it.

Nobody said anything and the ordinary part of that Friday is that nine people in that street each put a thing down and pick it up again, and a man with a barrow of lime went the length of it four times and put about two hundred pounds of it down at one door and took it up again at two others, and a carter at the low end got a wheel off a cart and put it on again with a bar in about nine minutes without anybody stopping on his account, and the two women at the third door had the same argument about a bolt of cloth that they have had on about nine mornings in four and a half years and finished it inside a shop.

The inn at the high end had four beds empty that morning and put no board out on the made street, and the woman of about fifty-one who keeps that house came out into it at about the fifth hour and stood nine feet off the boards and would not come nearer, and a chandler nine doors down put a cloth over a bench rather than write anything at all on it.

Nobody thanked him and the room over that weigh-house stood bolted from noon on the Saturday before last and the copyist of about twenty-six did not come down those thirteen stairs at all that day, and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled, and four people in nine counties have said since that a man who held ten things on a hilltop at about the fourth hour of that morning went down four days of made road and then came into a market street and that no room in nine counties has any of it and five have said that is what a room is for and four have said that the room in this county is shut behind its own door on every morning but two.

**And the cost of that Friday is nine hundred yards of grass that is dead and will not come back, the use of one ear, the use of one leg, and about ten roads that a man in nine counties can no longer be near without somebody standing with him, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
