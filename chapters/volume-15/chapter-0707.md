# Chapter 0707

**Thawmonth the nineteenth, YR 322, a Thursday and a market morning, the second hour in the market street at the boards with about four hundred people in it and about two hundred of them stopped, the room over the weigh-house open, and a young man of about nineteen who has been up a lane and is being asked about it in the open.**

Nobody thanked anybody and this was the largest crowd in that market town since the twenty-fifth of Hearthmonth, and the four hundred people in it and the two hundred who stopped were not the same people who stood at those boards on the twenty-fifth of Hearthmonth, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the market town of about two thousand people has now got two crowds in it and about four hundred people who have never been in either of them.

Nobody thanked anybody and he had walked in on the Wednesday at about the fourth hour and had been in about nine houses overnight, and his coat was stiff and there was about nine days of a hill on the knees of it, and four people in that market street had looked at the knees and about five had looked at his face.

Nobody thanked him and nobody in nine counties had sent for him, and nobody had asked him, and nobody knew he had been anywhere except that he was in the street and had been, as the saying goes in this county, on his own account.

Nobody thanked him and he had slept in about nine houses in that market town in three nights, and a young man of about nineteen who sells honey at a market and is in it four days out of nine sleeps where he is put, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the only trade in nine counties where a person's address is a list of about nine people who have each met him once, and five have said that this is better than the alternative.

Nobody thanked him and his hands were the other thing about him that about nine people in that market street looked at, and they were cracked at the knuckle of the right one and stained about half an inch up the inside of the left forearm with something brown, and four people in nine counties have said since that a stain like that on a forearm is a bee and five have said that a stain like that on a forearm is a stone.

Then the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up asked him about it, out loud, in the open, at the low end of the boards, and he asked it kindly and about two hundred people heard it and nobody stopped him.

“**Where did you go on Thursday?**”

Nobody said anything and four hundred people in that market street had heard a man ask a young man where he had been and about five had understood that the question was about a lane and four had understood that it was about the lane and about the county.

Nobody thanked him and the young man of about nineteen answered in his own order and not in anybody's order, and four people in that market street have said since that this is the whole of what makes an account worth hearing, and five have said that it is because he was not thinking about the order.

Nobody said anything and before he said anything at all somebody in that street asked him whether he was sure, and he said that he was not, and about nine people in that market street heard a young man be asked a question and answer it before he started, and then he said the first of the four things and he said it in about nine seconds.

“**I went up the lane behind the beasts. It is about two miles and it is not made and it turns twice, and past the turns there is about nine hundred yards of road with kerbs on both sides, and then the kerbs stop.**”

Nobody thanked him and four people in that market street had heard a young man describe nine hundred yards of kerb on a hilltop and five had heard nine hundred yards of something.

“**And after the kerbs stop it goes on in the grass as a line about nine feet wide for about nine hundred yards, and you can stand at the end of it and look back and see every yard.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a young man say that a road goes on after the road stops and five had heard nine hundred yards twice.
Nobody thanked him and he had said the kerb part twice before he got to the stones, and nine people in that market street had noticed that he had gone back over it, and about five had not, and four people in nine counties have said since that a young man repeating a thing in a market street is not a mistake and is a man making sure the two ends of it agree.

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had understood inside the first nine seconds that the road did not stop where the kerbs stopped, and about five had understood inside the first nine seconds that there were nine hundred yards of kerb on a hilltop, and that those two sets are nine people who have never spoken to each other and never will.

“**And there are nine stones set across it at the end, about the height of your knee, and the tops of them are all at the same height.**”

Nobody thanked him and two hundred people in that market street had heard nine stones set across a road at the same height, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the one of the four things that anybody in that market street could have got wrong and did not, and five have said that a young man who has been in a walled yard for eight years can count a thing he is looking at, and that this is the least interesting of the nine reasons.

“**And at about the ninth hour there was a cart on the other side of the nine stones and I heard it once and it went away and it did not come back.**”

Nobody thanked him and four hundred people in that market street had heard a young man say that he heard a cart and five had heard a young man be careful.

Nobody thanked him and then about nine people in that market street asked him something else at the same moment, and he said one thing about it that has not been repeated anywhere since, and it was that he was not going to be asked about it again that day, and four people in that market street had worked out what that meant and about five had not.

Nobody said anything and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the ordinary contract in this county between a person with something to say and about four hundred people who want it said, and that it lasts one day, and that the nine words said on the second of Hearthmonth had the same contract and the same limit, and that a thing said on a Thursday in this market street gets said once and that is the whole of its life unless somebody stands in the road and says it is a head.

Nobody thanked him and he stood there about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over after that and said nothing further, and nine people in nine counties have said since that he was right to, and that four people have said that a young man who says he will not be asked twice about a thing he has said once is a young man who has worked out the shape of this county faster than a man of thirty-two did.

Nobody said anything and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the fourth time in about four and a half years that a person has stood in that market street and said out loud that they heard or saw a thing that nobody else in nine counties can check, and that the other three times were all about the wall on the made road at nine miles, and five have said that a wall is nine feet high and can be pointed at from a street and that nine stones on a hill cannot be pointed at from anywhere.

Then somebody in that street tried to make a list of it, and it is worth putting down because about nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the moment the block nearly went wrong.

Nobody thanked him and a man in that market street, whose trade nobody in the county knows and who is about twenty-six, started to say the four things back in the order they had been said and began to number them, and he got as far as the second number and about nine people in that market street said the same word at the same moment, and four people in nine counties have said since that the word was somebody else's and five have said that nobody in nine counties has ever been able to establish whose it was.

Nobody said anything and the man of about twenty-six stopped, and nine people in that market street had heard a thing stopped in the open and four of them had worked out what had stopped it and five had not, and nobody thanked anybody and nobody argued with anybody and the man of about twenty-six went back to the middle of the street and did not say anything else that morning.

Nobody thanked him and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the fourth and a half year this county has had and it is the reason there is anything in it, and that four hundred people in a market street will not keep a set of four numbers for anybody, and that a thing in four hundred mouths is not a list, and that this is not a policy and is not anybody's doing and would stop the day a person decided to hold it up.

Then the man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract for about nineteen years said the other half of it, out loud, in the open, in front of about four hundred people, and nobody stopped him and he had come in from the made road and had not been asked.

“**I am not going up that lane, and I will say why in my trade and not in anybody's. Four miles of made road under my hand is coming up out of about nine days of frost and there is water in the low side of it at this hour that is not going to be gone before Mudmonth, and I have about nine men on that four miles and about nine I cannot spare, and if I go up a hill for two nights the four miles is about nine hundred yards of mud and nobody has told me it is nobody's business.**”

Nobody thanked him and four people in that market street had heard a man refuse a thing in his own trade and given a figure that could be checked, and five had heard a man who keeps a road say that a road is the whole of what he has.

Nobody said anything and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the fifth time in this block that the cost has come out of somebody's trade, and that the four of them before it were a causeway, a drove, a wall and a yard of bees, and that the fifth is a man who cannot leave four miles of made road, and four people in nine counties have said that this is why nothing has ever gone up that lane in nine counties and five have said that it is why nothing ever will.

Then the badge-man said the one thing, and he said it in thirty-two words at about the eleventh hour to about nine people out of four hundred.

“**A thing said in this street is worth less than a thing said in a trade, and I have been trying to tell this county that for four and a half years.**”

Nobody thanked him and four people in that market street had heard a man agree with himself in public for the first time in about eleven years, and five had heard thirty-two words, and nobody argued with him and nobody asked him what he meant.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up asked a second question and asked it out loud, and it was the question about nine people in that market street had been carrying since the twenty-fifth of Hearthmonth.

“**Then how does anybody find out if it is true?**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had thought of an answer and did not say it and five had not thought of one, and the badge-man did not answer it either, and nine people in nine counties have said since that on the second of Hearthmonth he answered a question of that shape in fifty words about nine gates or four, and that this time he did not answer it, and that the reason he did not is that the answer would have been about four hundred people and not about nine.

Nobody thanked him and nine people in nine counties have said since that four hundred people in that market street now know about nine stones on a hill and about nine hundred yards of kerb and one cart, and five of the four hundred have said that it is true, and that this is the first time in about four and a half years that five people in nine counties have said out loud that they believe something, and that nobody knows who they are and they have not spoken to each other.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of thirty-two was at the sea end of that street for the whole of it with his stick in his right hand and said nothing, and nobody looked at him, and four people in that market street had worked out that the young man of about nineteen went up that lane off a drove and that a drove goes out on Thursdays and that the man of thirty-two has been walking that made road every morning since the first of Longlight, and about five had worked out nothing at all.

Nobody thanked anybody and nobody wrote any of it down, and nine people in nine counties have said since that on the nineteenth of Thawmonth a thing was said in about four hundred mouths in this market street and there is no page in any room in this county with any of it on, and that this is the second time in about four and a half years that this has happened and that the first time was about four hundred people walking past a thing they cannot name.

**And the cost of that Thursday is four things said once in four hundred mouths and four miles of made road that nobody can leave in Mudmonth, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
