# Chapter 0745

**Wolfmonth the seventeenth, YR 322, a Thursday and a market morning, the second hour in the market street at the boards with about four hundred people in it and about two hundred of them stopped, the room over the weigh-house open, a Crown's cart come up four days of made road and stopped outside an office, and a sheet read out loud at the boards that nobody stopped.**

A Thursday in that market town of about two thousand people is a market morning, and four hundred people were in that street by the second hour of the seventeenth of Wolfmonth and two hundred of them stopped what they were doing, and the frost came off the ruts about an hour after that, and about four people in nine counties had worked out that a cart went up the made road on the Monday before and five had not.

Nobody thanked anybody and that cart came up four days of made road on the Monday before with paper in it and did not stop at the weigh-house and did not stop at the inn and stood outside the Crown's road office at the low end of that street for two days and went on up the made road on the Wednesday at about the sixth hour with about four hundred pounds of lime in it, and four people in that market street had worked out that this is the second cart in a month that has come up with paper in it and five had worked out nothing at all.

That light has been on the top of that shoulder at about the fourth hour of every morning since the twenty-sixth of Embermonth, and that is twenty-two mornings, and four people have seen it once each and nobody else has seen it at all, and four people in nine counties have said since that a thing four people have each seen once has been on that hill twenty-two mornings running and has got no further and five have said that nobody has been up there but the four and about four have said that one of the four has been up there twenty-two times and has said it out loud twice in the open, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man who says a thing out loud in a market street has told four hundred people already and five have said that telling four hundred people is the same as telling nobody.

Then four days off and about nine hundred people along the made road did the ordinary part of a Thursday.

Nobody said anything and nine beasts went out through the gap at the low end of about nine hundred yards of stone wall at about the sixth hour and went up nine hundred yards of that lane and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back down at about the fifth hour, and nobody went with them, and that is about thirty-three drove to go up that lane since the second of Mudmonth.

Eight of the nine yards of wall at the head of that made road are back in and the gap is about a yard long and about four feet high, and the stone is quarried two miles off on the low side and one man has been carrying it two miles in his own hands for two hundred and twenty-five days, and the last yard is about nine feet of a course and he has not started it, and nobody has asked him when he will and nobody is going to.

The brood in a walled yard half a mile below that wall is five frames where there were nine on the night of the eighth of Thawmonth, and that night is two hundred and forty-nine days back, and the fifth frame came in on the twentieth of Embermonth and is not all brood yet, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a brood frame comes back in about four weeks in a year like this one and that the fifth has been twenty-seven days coming and five have said that twenty-seven days is about right and four have said that a figure nobody in nine counties has looked at does not need to be right.

On that east rise the broom has been off for about nine days and about nine hundred yards of a line nine feet wide is left standing where it stands, and four people in nine counties have said since that a line in grass that is not cut closes over in about three years and that the nine hundred yards a young man of about nineteen did not cut are now the only nine hundred yards on that hill anybody has ever looked at twice and five have said that looking at a thing twice is not keeping it.

Nobody thanked him and the weigh-house weighed two loads of lime and a load of wool that morning, and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that Wolfmonth is seventeen days old and about a hundred and seventy sheets have come past that floor in it, and that about nine of them every month come past with a corner torn off, and that nobody has ever asked him what a sheet weighs.

Nobody thanked him and the inn at the high end was full that morning from about the fourth hour, four beds and four more on the floor above them, and no price went out on any board in that street because nothing has moved a price since the middle of Harvestmonth, and the woman of about fifty-one who keeps that house came out into it at the fifth hour and stopped nine feet short of those boards and would not come nearer, and a chandler nine doors down put a cloth over a bench rather than write anything at all on it.

Nobody said anything and the near wheel of a cart came off at the low end and the carter got it up again with a bar in about nine minutes and nobody stopped walking, and two women at the third door argued about a bolt of cloth for about four minutes and then went into the shop and came out with it wrapped, and the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up had a piece of blue wool over his forearm and he took it off and looked at the four people who were at those boards and put it back.

The room over that weigh-house was open because it is a Thursday, and the copyist of about twenty-six came down the thirteen stairs at the seventh hour with a sheet of paper under her arm that is not this county's paper and went back up with it, and four people in that lane saw her come down and five did not, and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled.

Then the part of that Thursday that was said out loud, and three things in it were, and the first two of them were a sheet of paper and a leaf and neither of them was about a road in nine counties.

Nobody thanked anybody and the copyist of about twenty-six came down those thirteen stairs again at about the ninth hour with the sheet in her hand and stood at the high end of those boards and read it out from the top to the bottom in the open, to about four hundred people, and nobody stopped her, and about four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first thing anybody in this county has read out loud in a market street in about four and a half years and five have said that it is the first thing anybody has read out loud that was not written by somebody in this county.

Nobody said anything and about two hundred people in that market street heard a sheet of paper about a road on the coast of returning roads read out loud to about four hundred people, and nobody stopped it, and she read out one line of it and it was 31 words, and nobody stopped her.

“**The returning road at the sixth post is shut from the twentieth of Frostmonth and no cart shall cross it and no keeper shall be paid for it after that day.**”

Nobody said anything and nobody wrote it down and about four people in nine counties have said since that a road on a coast four hundred miles off is shut on the twentieth of Frostmonth by a piece of paper and that nobody in nine counties is going to be told about it before then and five have said that the twentieth of Frostmonth is about thirty-three days off and about four have said that a road four hundred miles off is not a road in nine counties and five have said that it is still a road somebody keeps.

Nobody thanked him and then she read the last line of that sheet out loud in the same place, at about the same hour, to about four hundred people, and it was 21 words, and nobody stopped her, and four people in nine counties have said since that a printed sheet from four hundred miles off has come down one made road in a cart in the ordinary way and has been read out at the boards in this county and five have said that the man who put it in the cart did not know what was in it.

“**Given under the Marshal's hand, Edric Hunn, at the office four hundred miles off and sent down by the ordinary post.**”

Nobody said anything and about two hundred people in that market street heard a name read out at the boards at about the ninth hour of the seventeenth of Wolfmonth and about nine of them had heard it before and five had not, and nobody stopped her and nobody thanked her, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a name read out in a market street in this county is a thing that goes into four hundred mouths in about nine days and five have said that this one has been printed twice in about four and a half years and that a printed name does not need to be heard twice to be in four hundred mouths and about four have said that nobody in that street wrote any of it down and five have said that nobody ever writes anything in that street down.

Nobody thanked anybody and then the clerk of about forty-nine who is a clerk of that road office and who came down a lane on foot on the twenty-sixth of Fallowmonth and said no in four sentences in that street brought out a leaf, and it is not this county's leaf and it is not the leaf that is carried in the inside of a coat and has not been read out anywhere and is not addressed to anybody, and nobody in that market street had seen it before and about nine of them came and looked at it.

Nobody said anything and the clerk of about forty-nine read out the whole of that leaf in the open at the low end of those boards at about the ninth hour to about four hundred people, and it was 17 words, and nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him, and the leaf carries one thumb on it and about nine people in nine counties have said since that a thumb is not a signature and five have said that a thumb on a leaf is a thing nine people in this county have never seen and about four have said that it is the only mark anybody in nine counties has ever made on a piece of paper about a road.

“**We shut our own end before Hearthmonth and we are not asking anybody for anything about it.**”

Nobody said anything and about four hundred people in that market street had heard a leaf read out loud at the boards on a Thursday morning and four of them understood what it was about and five had understood the first line and not the second, and nobody stopped him and nobody argued with him and nobody thanked him, and about four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first thing anybody has read out at those boards that is not an order and five have said that a thing that is not an order cannot be answered and about four have said that a thing that cannot be answered cannot be refused either and that is why nobody in that street said a word.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about fifty-eight with a badge and no head was at the high end of those boards on that Thursday and said nothing at all, and the badge-man has asked one question in this county in about four and a half years and has not asked it since, and four people in that market street had worked out that he was there on a morning when two things were read out and five had not, and nobody sent for him and nobody asked him anything.

The two refusals in this county were given on the fourteenth and the twenty-sixth of Fallowmonth and neither has been argued with, and that is sixty-three days and fifty-one days, and nobody in nine counties has asked either of those two men anything since and nobody is going to, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a third thing was read out in that street on the seventeenth of Wolfmonth and that it was not a refusal and five have said that a thing nobody asked for cannot be a refusal.

Two hundred people went back to nine trades inside an hour of the last of it being read, and one of those nine trades is a man unloading lime out of a barrow and another of them is a woman selling cloth and another of them is the man who keeps that weigh-house, and four people in nine counties have said since that a street of nine hundred paces with four hundred people on it in the second hour has nine trades in it in the ninth, and five have said that the other two hundred went home and that nine of the four hundred stayed at those boards.

At the head of that made road four days off the bar is in on the lane side and has been in since Mudmonth, and the man of forty-one who bars it came out at the sixth hour that Thursday and did not go up the lane and did not go down it and stood at the gap in his own wall for about as long as it takes to turn a cart and went in, and nobody in nine counties has asked him why he stands at the gap and five have said that a man who built nine hundred yards of wall out of one hill has got nine hundred yards of it lying about in the weather and that standing at the gap is how a man looks at nine hundred yards of stone without saying one word about it.

Nobody said anything and the sheet of paper with one line on it has been in that office for fifty-one days and the leaf that came up in a cart on the Monday of the first of Wolfmonth has been in it for seventeen days and the returning road at the sixth post is shut on the twentieth of Frostmonth, and about four people in nine counties have said since that three pieces of paper have gone into one office in about two months and that one of them is a line about a man saying a thing in a street and five have said that none of the three can be sent on to anybody and about four have said that there is nowhere in nine counties to send them to and five have said that there is nowhere four hundred miles off either.

Nobody said anything and then a question came into that market street at about the tenth hour from a person at the third door who wanted to know one thing, and nobody stopped it, and it was asked kindly, and it was not about a road in nine counties.

“**Was that our road?**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a question asked about whether a printed road is a road in this county and five had heard it asked kindly, and nobody stopped it and the man of thirty-two with a stick answered it out loud, in the open, in his own trade, and it was 40 words, and nobody thanked him.

“**No. That road is on a coast four hundred miles off and nobody in this county has ever been on it and nobody in this county is going to be told a thing about it before the twentieth of Frostmonth.**”

Nobody said anything and nobody said anything back to him and nobody thanked him, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man who has been asked a question about a printed sheet in a market street has answered it about a road four hundred miles off and five have said that he answered it by saying that nobody is going to be told, and about four have said that a man cannot stop a sheet being read out and that he can only say so afterwards and five have said that this is the whole of what a man can do about a thing that is already in the air.

The far bank is four days south of that market town and about four hundred people live across nine hundred yards of water at the top of a tide and about four hundred at the bottom, and a hand lamp goes on the coping at the low side of that causeway every Monday afternoon in the winter and the seventeenth of Wolfmonth was a Thursday and the lamp was not put there, and nobody in that market street has ever asked a person at a gate at the near end of that water anything at all, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a gate in this county has three different jobs and that nobody has asked the man at any of the three for a second one and five have said that there are four gates and the fourth is four hundred miles off.

Nobody thanked anybody and nothing about any of it is in a document, and there is no page in any room in nine counties with the seventeenth of Wolfmonth on it, and the register in the room over that weigh-house holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a sheet was read out at the boards and that the boards had nothing on them before it and nothing on them after it and five have said that a board with nothing on it can hold a reading and cannot hold a finding.

**And the cost of that Thursday is one printed sheet read out loud to about four hundred people, one leaf with a thumb on it, and about nine hundred yards of a line that four people have each seen once, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
