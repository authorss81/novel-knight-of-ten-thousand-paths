# Chapter 0724

**Rainmonth the first, YR 322, a Monday and not a market morning and the first of a month, with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the bolt still on from the Saturday and the hearing not sitting and four people at the boards, and three days to a broom.**

The first of a month in this county comes whether or not anybody is in the street for it, and the first of Rainmonth came on a Monday, and a Monday is not a market morning, and nine people were in that market street by the ninth hour and four of them were at the boards with nothing on them and five were not.

Nobody thanked anybody and that is now the seventh first of a month in a row on which nothing has happened at those boards, and four people in nine counties stand at them at about the ninth hour on a first of a month and five do not, and nobody has sent for the man who built it on any of the seven and the badge-man has not been at two of them on purpose.

Nobody said anything and the room over that weigh-house was shut behind its own door and the bolt was still on from the Saturday, and nobody went up the lane four hundred paces long that holds it, and nobody sent for anybody, and four people in that market town had worked out that a thing which happens on the first of a month and cannot be called is a thing that has stopped being a thing anybody expects, and five had not.

Nobody said anything and the press in that room stands open with no date on it and has stood open since about the middle of Longlight the year before, and the keeper of that room is paid fourpence a year and holds a deed of thirteen words for it, and four people in nine counties have said since that a press with no date on it is a thing that has been opened and put down four hundred times and five have said that nobody has ever closed it.

Nobody thanked her and that lane is four hundred paces long and runs from a door on it to that market street, and a man has walked past that door nine hundred times since the first of Longlight and gone up those stairs once, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the most careful piece of silence anybody in this county has produced in about four and a half years and five have said that it is not a silence because nobody has noticed anything.

Nobody said anything and Rainmonth is the month about half of four hundred and nine parish rolls come past the floor of that weigh-house wet, and Rainmonth is the month the broom comes on about the fourth, and a month has four weeks in it in every year anybody in nine counties can remember, and nine people in nine counties have said since that two of those facts are about a broom and one of them is about sheets.

Nobody said anything and the boards at the low end of that street have been standing since the middle of Longlight the year before and are the colour of a wet slate and the cloth over the bench at the low end of them has been on it since the middle of Hearthmonth and nobody has lifted it and nobody has asked what is under it.

Nobody thanked anybody and a market town of about two thousand people has a market on two days of the week and about four hundred and nine parish rolls come past the floor of a weigh-house in a good month, and the days it does not have a market are the days on which about four hundred people walk nine hundred paces of street and go into about nine shops and five of them buy nothing, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the second of those figures is the truer one and four have said that nobody has ever counted it.

Nobody said anything and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel said nothing at all in that street on the first of Rainmonth and has said nine things out loud in about four and a half years, and four people in nine counties have said since that a man who has said nine things and then says nothing for about nine days is a man who has heard something and five have said that a man who has said nine things and then says nothing for about nine days is a man who has run out.

Then the one thing said out loud on that morning, and it was about the broom and not about the lane.

Nobody thanked him and the man of fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract for nineteen years came in at about the seventh hour and stood at the low end of those boards and said it out loud, in the open, to nine people, in his own trade, and it was 44 words, and nobody stopped him.

“**I am not going up before the broom is on and nobody is going to send me and there is nothing in this county that would make me, and I said on the first of Longlight that I would go and I am going.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a roadkeeper put a date on a hill four days off and five had heard 44 words, and nobody said anything back and nobody thanked him, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the third date anybody in this county has been given since the second of Mudmonth and that the other two were a date for the ground coming up and a date for the lane going out of it.

Nobody thanked anybody and four people in that market street had worked out that the man who keeps four miles of made road under contract has said no to going up that lane twice, once before anybody knew there was a broom and once after, and five had worked out that he said it twice in a trade and not in a street, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the two refusals in this county this year that were made in a trade and not in a street both had no head at the top of them and neither has a paper.

Nobody said anything and the young man of about nineteen was not in that market street on the first of Rainmonth at the seventh hour and had been at his own trade four days off since the Friday before, and about nine people in nine counties know that he gets a day on a Friday and about five have ever asked him what a day on a Friday is for.

Nobody thanked anybody and then somebody on that made road four hundred yards from the town asked him out loud, kindly, on the Monday, whether the young man was going to have any help with the broom, and four people on that made road heard the question and five did not.

“**Is he going to have any help with it?**”

Nobody said anything and four people on that made road had heard a question asked about a young man's account rather than about a young man and five had heard a question asked about a broom, and nobody stopped it and nobody answered it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first question in four and a half years anybody in this county has asked about what another person's work is going to cost and four people have said that a question about a person's cost is a question about a person's standing and five have said that it is a question about a broom.

Nobody thanked him and the question went out of that street with the afternoon and four people in nine counties have said since that nobody in nine counties will ask it again because it was asked kindly by a stranger and a man who keeps bees can hear a stranger, and five have said that a man who keeps bees cannot hear a stranger and that he has to.

Then the answer, given out loud in a market town on the Monday before the broom, by the man who is going to cut it.

Nobody thanked him and the young man of about nineteen came into that market town at about the eleventh hour on the first of Rainmonth and did not come to that street and went to the chandler's nine doors down and bought nine yards of tarred twine and nine feet of new waxed cord for his frames and paid for them and left, and he said one thing out loud on the made road to four people, in his own trade, and it was 48 words.

“**I am going up on my own account on Thursday and I will cut what four bundles of twine will hold and I will carry four bundles down two miles of lane and I am not asking anybody for anything and I have not got anybody to ask.**”

Nobody said anything and four people on that made road had heard a young man say out loud that he has nobody to ask about a hill and five had heard 48 words, and nobody said anything back and nobody thanked him, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in four and a half years that anybody in this county has said out loud that he has got nobody to ask, and about four people have said that a man who says that in a street is asking everybody in it and five have said that he did not say it in a street.

Nobody thanked anyone and four people in nine counties have said since that a bundle of broom four feet long is about nine hands across and that four of them is thirty-six hands of broom, and that a broom is cut with a sickle and not with a scythe because a scythe takes about nine paces to turn, and five have said that nobody in nine counties has ever cut nine acres with a scythe and four have said that nobody has ever cut anything with one.

Nobody said anything and a man with four bundles on his shoulder can walk down two miles of an unmade road about nine feet wide that turns twice, and a stone the size of a cartwheel is in the middle of it because it has always been in the middle of it, and four people in nine counties have said since that a bad leg is worse on a rise and better on a flat and that a lane with two turns in it is nine hundred yards of flat and one thousand one hundred of rise and five have said that the rise is the easy part because you can stop.

Nobody thanked him and the drove went up about nine hundred yards of that lane on the Thursday before at about the sixth hour and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back at about the fifth hour, and nobody went with it, and the bar went in on the lane side on the Thursday morning after the water went out of the lane and it has been in ever since and it has not been asked about.

Nobody said anything and about six of the nine yards of wall at the head of that made road are back in and the gap is about three yards long and about four feet high and the stone is quarried about two miles off on the low side and one man has been carrying it two miles on his own for about eighty-nine days, and four people in nine counties have said that a gap in a wall is shut the day it is filled and five have said that nobody in nine counties is going to be told the day it is.

Nobody thanked anybody and the hives stand in a walled yard about four rods by six behind a stone wall about four feet high half a mile below that wall, and the young man of about nineteen counted the brood in them on the twenty-seventh of Longlight and it was three frames where there were nine on the night before the eighth of Thawmonth, and a brood frame comes back in about four weeks in a year like this one, and it is a hundred and thirteen days since that night.

Nobody said anything and he had nine frames of wood and about nine feet of wax and about nine strands of waxed twine in that yard on the Friday before and he made nine frames in about two days and did not make ten, and four people in nine counties have said since that a man who makes nine and stops has counted and five have said that a man who makes nine and stops is a man who has nine and knows it.

Nobody thanked him and the bees in that yard had been flying about four feet off the ground all week and going out and coming back with nothing, and four people in nine counties have said since that bees fly low when there is nothing out and that the east rise is nine hundred yards off and about two hundred yards above, and five have said that a bee four hundred yards off a field of nine acres will find it in a year and not in a week.

Then the ordinary half of that Monday four days off, which is nine tenths of every day anybody in nine counties has ever had.

Nobody thanked anybody and about nine hundred people along that made road did the ordinary part of it, and about nine hives in a walled yard were doing the one thing they do in a month with no bloom in it, and a man of about forty-one was carrying stone two miles on his own with a bar about four feet of ash long and about sixty pounds with the stones on the end of it.

Nobody said anything and about two hundred yards of orchard and about two miles of stone wall between the eleventh mile stone and the top of that hill were in blossom and about nine hundred trees of them, and the owners are about nine households and not one of them has ever asked anybody anything about the people who walk past its gate.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about thirty-two with a stick came in off that made road at about the eighth hour on the Monday and did not go up the lane that holds that room, and the room was shut behind its own door, and about four people in that market town had noticed that he was back and about five had not, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the twelfth time he has come off that road and not gone up a lane and that nobody has ever asked him to.

Nobody said anything and the man of about thirty-eight who puts boards on the inside of gates was at the low end of that market street on the first of Rainmonth and said nothing about the broom and four people in nine counties have said since that a man who says nothing about a thing in a street is a man who has already said it somewhere that matters and five have said that he had said it in a trade on the eighteenth of Longlight on a shoulder, which is fourteen days ago.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about fifty-eight with a badge and no head was not in that market street on the first of Rainmonth and nobody sent for him, and he said one thing about the month of Longlight out loud in that street on the first of Longlight in eight words and has said nothing about a month since, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first first of a month in about four and a half years that has had nothing said about the month in it at all.

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market town think the whole of this county's business is finished and about five think it is not, and neither of those two sets has ever spoken to the other, and four people in nine counties have said since that the broom is the first thing in about four and a half years that has put a sixth one on the side of the county's business not being finished, and five have said that a broom is a thing a man cuts for himself.

Nobody said anything and the weigh-house on that Monday weighed one load of lime and one load of wool and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that a day with no market on it is a day on which about four hundred and nine sheets do not come past his floor and that a Wednesday and a Monday are the two days of the week on which this county's paperwork does not move.

Nobody thanked anybody and then the light went off the west bank first and off those boards about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over, and the lamp at the high end of them was lit and stood lit until the light was gone, and about nine people went out of that market street into about nine trades and about four stayed at the boards, and nothing about any of it is in a document and the register in that room gains nothing and the day-book stands at eleven entries.
