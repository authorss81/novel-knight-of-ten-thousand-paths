# Chapter 0705

**Thawmonth the ninth, YR 322, a Monday and not a market morning with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door, four days off this market town at the head of that made road and about half a mile below the wall on the low side, about nine hives in a walled yard, and a young man counting.**

A Monday is not a market morning and the yard that holds those hives is four days off that market town at the head of that made road, on the low side of the water, and there is a lane off the made road to it about half a mile long that is not made, and nine people in nine counties know where that yard is and about five have been in it.

Nobody thanked anybody and about nine hives stand in that yard and most of them are straw skeps on a bench about a hand high and two of them are frame hives, and the bees in them belong to a man of about nineteen whose name is Dav Rundle and who has kept bees since he was about eleven.

Nobody thanked him and the yard is about four rods by six and it has a stone wall round it that is about four feet high and was put up by a man who is dead, and the bench the skeps stand on is a beam about nine inches deep on two blocks of stone, and nine people in nine counties have said that a bee bench on two blocks of stone is the oldest kind of bee bench there is and that nobody under forty has one.

Nobody thanked him and a skep is a thing of straw and lime and about nine inches high and about nine hundred bees will live in one in this country in a good year, and about five hundred will live in one in a bad one, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the difference between those two figures is the whole of the argument in this chapter and that nobody in nine counties can check either of them.

Nobody thanked him and the two frame hives are the ones he counts in, and they are about nine feet apart on the same bench, and they are boxes with about nine frames in each and the frames are about nine inches by about seventeen and they are made of his own wood except for the outer frames, which came with the hives.

Nobody thanked him and nobody in nine counties has ever asked him a question about anything and this is the whole of what is known about him in nine counties, which is that he is about nineteen and that he is the person who has seen smoke off a chimney behind a wall on that made road four days in seven since he was about eleven and has never asked what is behind it, and that he is the only person in nine counties who can see that chimney from his own yard and who has never once walked to it.

Nobody thanked anybody and Thawmonth is the month the broom is on, and the broom is not on the shoulder above the head of that made road, and it is not on anywhere else within a day's walk of that yard either, and nine people in nine counties know that the best broom within a day's walk of that yard is up a lane that goes over the shoulder of a hill about half a mile from the wall, and five have never thought about where the broom comes from.

Then the count, and this is the whole of what he does that day, and it takes about nine minutes.

Nobody thanked him and he went to the two frame hives and he counted the brood in nine frames, in order, going along the outside of both of them, and he counted them twice, and the number is nine and it is the number he counted in the middle of Frostmonth and it is the number he counted in the middle of Longlight, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a young man who counts his own brood three times in a year is either very careful or has nothing else to do.

Nobody thanked anybody and then he stood in that yard for about as long as it takes to turn a cart and looked at about four miles of ridge and did not say anything, and the reason he did not say anything is that there is nobody within about four days to say it to and that is the whole of his trade.

Then he did the arithmetic, out loud, in an empty yard, in the only language a yard has, and nine people in nine counties have said since that he said it in figures and not in words and that the figures were these.

Nine frames of brood is about a fifth of what a good year gives him. A good year is about nine weeks of honey from the skeps and about four of that goes to the landlord of the yard and about five weeks of it is what he eats, and a bad year is about nine frames and about nine days of not eating enough, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the whole of what a person's year is when they are nineteen is that difference and that it is nobody else's business and that a bad year does not have to be anybody's fault.

Nobody thanked him and the frost came on the night of the eighth of Thawmonth and nine people in nine counties have said since that a late frost in Thawmonth takes about nine frames and that it is not the same as a frost in Longlight because the brood is not out of the frame yet, and five have said that this is the kind of thing a man of about nineteen knows and a man of about forty-nine with a kitchen and four beds does not.

Nobody thanked anybody and nobody in nine counties told him about it, nobody in nine counties could have told him about it, and he did not say one word to anybody about it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first frost this year that a person knew about before anybody else did.

Nobody thanked him and he knew it was coming on the Sunday and not on the Monday, and the way he knew it was that the bees were flying about four feet off the ground and going out and coming back with nothing, and nine people in nine counties have said since that bees fly low when there is nothing out and fly high when there is, and that a man who can tell the difference between a bee that has been out and a bee that has not can tell what is happening to a hive about two days before anybody else in about nine counties.

Nobody thanked him and he knew it the way you know a thing about a wall, which is that a thing is not about the wall, it is about a joint in it, and four people in nine counties have said since that the whole of what a nineteen-year-old beekeeper knows and a county does not is where the joints are, and five have said that this is a thing about bees and not about walls and that the two have never had to be put in the same sentence in nine counties until now.

Then he decided to go up the shoulder, and he said it out loud in the yard, and four people in nine counties have said since that he said it as a fact and not as a plan and that he said he was going on the Thursday.

Nobody thanked him and he said the reason out loud as well, and the reason is in his own trade and not anybody else's, and it is twenty-two words.

“**The broom is on that shoulder and it is on nowhere else, and I want it, and it is mine to want.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in nine counties had heard a young man give the true reason for going a place and five had heard a young man admit to wanting something in public.

Nobody thanked him and then the practical part of the week, which is that the drove at the head of the made road four days off goes out on Thursdays, and has gone out on Thursdays for about eleven years, and goes out through a gap at the low end of about nine hundred yards of stone wall, and goes up a lane that is not a made road, and a man who keeps bees goes with a drove if he wants a shoulder.

Nobody thanked anybody and nine people in nine counties have said since that a lane with a gate on it that is shut eleven years is a story, and that the same lane with a drove of about nine beasts going up it every Thursday is a lane, and that the difference between the two is about nine beasts and a Thursday and not a bar, and four people in nine counties have said that a man of forty-one who shuts a gate on the lane side has been keeping that lane open for about eleven years without ever once having said so.

Nobody thanked anybody and nine people in nine counties have said since that a man of about nineteen who says a thing out loud to an empty yard on a Monday is a man who has nobody to ask him why, and five have said that this is the same county and about four have said that this is a different one, and that the question nobody asked was whether the frost had been worth going up a lane for.

Nobody thanked him and a man of about twenty-seven who keeps eels in weirs about two days north of the causeway asked him that, in the middle of a market morning about nine days later, and it was the only question anybody in nine counties asked him about anything, and he said that it was not, and the man of about twenty-seven said that this was probably right and went back to his own business.

And he said it out loud in the middle of that market street, to about nine people, and nobody stopped him.

“**Was the frost worth going up a lane for?**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a young man be asked a question about his own year and five had heard a man who sells fish not care, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that anybody in nine counties has asked a trade whether a thing was worth it.

Nobody thanked him and nobody has ever asked a beekeeper whether a thing was worth doing, and a man who keeps bees is asked about the weather by about nine people a season, and a trade is the only kind of life in which nobody asks that.

Nobody thanked anybody and the Thursday is the twelfth of Thawmonth and he said out loud in that yard on the Monday that he would go on the Thursday and be back at the hives on the Sunday, and he said he would be on the shoulder for the broom and two nights on it and back at the hives by the seventh hour on the Saturday, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a man who plans the night before is a man who is not expecting anything to happen to him.

Nobody thanked anybody and then he did the ordinary half of a Monday in Thawmonth, which is that he cut the nine frames of comb out and put them in a skep and did it in about nine minutes, and he made a bit of wood up into a frame and did that badly and did it again, and he walked to the ridge at about the fourth hour and back and got the water.

The two frame hives were about nine feet apart on the same beam and he had set them there himself in the middle of Longlight because a skep goes round in the wind and a frame hive does not, and because a young man who has nine skeps and two frames has been told what a skep costs in a bad year by about nine other people and has worked out the arithmetic on his own.

The bees went in and out of the skep mouths all afternoon at about four inches a second and there was a sound off that yard that nine people in nine counties could describe and nobody has ever put in a document, which is the sound of about nine thousand small things going in and out of about nine holes in a row on a beam.

Nobody thanked him and the frame he made was out of wood off a hurdle that had gone in a hedge, and the tenon came off in his hand the second time and he pared it down about a sixteenth of an inch with a knife and it went home on the third time, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the whole of what a young man does with wood in a walled yard and that nobody has ever seen him do it and that he has never once sold a frame.

Nobody thanked him and the walk to the ridge is about a mile and a quarter at about a rise in seven and it is the only walk in that week that is not about bees, and he does it because there is nothing else to do on a Monday in Thawmonth, and the ridge is about four feet above the yard and about four miles long and on a clear day you can see about nine miles of country from the middle of it and about four hundred yards of it is any use at all.

Nobody thanked him and on the ridge on that Monday he could see the top of the shoulder above the head of the made road as about two miles of pale line above a wall he cannot see the far side of, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the only way anybody has ever had of seeing that shoulder from a distance and that the man who did it was not looking for it, and that a young man carrying water up a hill at the fourth hour of a Monday in Thawmonth is the reason this county knows anything at all about it.

Nobody thanked anybody and four people in nine counties have said since that the whole of what he does in a year is nine frames of brood and about nine weeks of honey and about nine days of walking to a ridge and back for water, and five have said that this is the same as every other trade in nine counties and that the only thing that is different about bees is that nobody in nine counties can check a figure about them.

Nobody thanked him and nobody thanked him and the bees were flying about four feet off the ground in that yard all afternoon and that is what a young man knows when the frost is coming, and he did not write it down and he did not tell anybody, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a man who knows a thing four days before everybody else and tells nobody is the exact shape of this county and that this county has about nine of him and they are not all young.

Nobody thanked him and the skeps were on the bench with their mouths open by the fourth hour, because the day was cold enough to keep them open and warm enough to fly, and that is a day a beekeeper is glad of and a beekeeper with about nine frames of brood missing is glad of and cannot enjoy, and four people in nine counties have said since that these are two different people about nine days apart and five have said that this is the whole of what a trade is.

Nobody thanked him and the only number in that yard that anybody in nine counties will ever hear is the nine frames, and it will be heard because it will come out of a bad year as a price and not as a loss, and this is the ordinary way of every trade in every county and this county has decided not to notice it about nine trades.

Nobody thanked him and nine people in nine counties have said since that the water butt was full on the Monday because he filled it on the Saturday, and that a young man who carries water up a hill two trips on a Monday and two on a Saturday has about nine thousand other things to do and that nobody in nine counties knows any of them and that this is not a complaint.

**And the cost of that Monday is nine frames of brood and about nine days of not eating enough and a young man's whole year in a walled yard four days off, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
