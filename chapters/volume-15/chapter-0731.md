# Chapter 0731

**Harvestmonth the thirtieth, YR 322, a Thursday and a market morning, the second hour in the market street at the boards with about four hundred people in it and about two hundred of them stopped, the room over the weigh-house open, and the last day of a month with one thing in it that a man has decided to ask two people about.**

A Thursday in that market town of about two thousand people is a market morning, and four hundred people were in that street by the second hour of the thirtieth of Harvestmonth and two hundred of them stopped what they were doing, and the frost came off the ruts about an hour after that, and four people in nine counties had worked out that a month ends and five had not.

Nobody thanked anybody and this is the last day of Harvestmonth and Fallowmonth is tomorrow, and about nine hundred yards of an old road has been in about four hundred mouths since the thirteenth of Rainmonth, and that is forty-six days, and nine people in nine counties know that and five have ever asked to hear any of it again.

Nobody said anything and there is a set of boards at the low end of that street and they have been standing there since the middle of Longlight the year before and they are the colour of a wet slate and they have been repainted twice, and the second of the two was in Hearthmonth, and the hearing they were put up for has not sat since the first of Goatmonth and cannot be called, and a cloth has been on the bench at their low end since the middle of Hearthmonth and nobody has asked what is under it.

Nobody thanked him and the room over that weigh-house was open because it is a Thursday and a room over a weigh-house is open five days in seven, and the copyist of about twenty-six came down the thirteen stairs at the seventh hour and went back up, and four people in that lane saw her come down and five did not, and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and the day-book stands at eleven entries and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled.

Then the ordinary half of that Thursday, which is nine tenths of it, and the drove went out four days off at about the sixth hour as it goes out on every Thursday.

Nobody said anything and the drove at the head of that made road went up nine hundred yards of that lane through the gap at the low end of about nine hundred yards of stone wall and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back down at about the fifth hour, and nobody went with it, and that is about twenty-two drove to go up that lane since the second of Mudmonth and about four hundred people in that market street have never once watched one.

Nobody thanked him and eight of the nine yards of wall at the head of that made road are back in and the gap is about a yard long and about four feet high, and the stone is quarried about two miles off on the low side and one man has been carrying it two miles on his own for a hundred and forty-eight days, and nobody in nine counties has ever asked him where it came from and nobody is going to.

Nobody said anything and a broom made off a hill about two miles above that man's house is standing in that house, and it came down two miles of unmade road on the eighth of Rainmonth, and he did not buy it and nobody has said that he did and nobody has said that he did not, and four people in nine counties have said since that a broom in a house by a fire is a thing a man of forty-one does not put there for himself.

Nobody thanked him and the brood in a walled yard about half a mile below that wall is three frames where there were nine on the night of the eighth of Thawmonth, and that is a hundred and seventy-two days, and a brood frame comes back in about four weeks in a year like this one, and four people in nine counties have said since that three frames is a third of nine and about five have said that three frames is going up and not down and that nobody in nine counties has checked it in a month.

Nobody said anything and the east rise above that wall is nine hundred yards off and about two hundred yards up and the broom on it is nine acres and it was cut on the fourth of Rainmonth and about nine days of it were left to be cut and it is standing dead where it stands, and four people in nine counties have said since that a broom that is cut is finished and about five have said that a broom that is standing dead is nine acres of nothing anybody in this county can use.

Nobody said anything and the two miles of wall and orchard between the eleventh mile stone and the top of that hill went out of blossom in the second week of Fallowmonth, or will, and the owners are nine households and not one of them has ever asked anybody anything about anybody who walks past its gate.

Nobody thanked anybody and the ninth mile stone on that made road had been cleaned on the Tuesday before by the one man who cleans it, and he does it with a bit of stick on a Tuesday and a Saturday and nobody in nine counties has ever seen him do it, and the stone is about the height of a knee and it is the ninth and it is not the eleventh and the man who cleans it is not the man who cleans the eleventh.

Nobody said anything and the bar at the head of that made road went in on the lane side on a Thursday morning in Mudmonth after the water went out of the lane, and it is four feet of ash with two stones on the end of it, and it is in, and nobody has asked about it, and the drove goes through a gap at the low end of the wall and not through the gate, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a gate that a drove does not use is not a shut gate, it is a gate with a bar on it.

Nobody thanked him and Fallowmonth is tomorrow and the bar is in the same place it was in and the drove will go up on Thursday as it goes up on Thursday, and that is the ordinary half of a month in this county, and about nine hundred people along four days of made road did the ordinary part of that Thursday and the ordinary part is that nothing about a Thursday is visible on a road.

Then the weigh-house, which is the only floor in nine counties where about four hundred and nine sheets a month are handled by one man.

Nobody thanked him and the weigh-house weighed two loads of lime and a load of wool and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that four hundred and nine parish rolls come past that floor in a good month and that Harvestmonth is the month they come past in their best state and that this has been the best month that floor has had since the middle of a Harvestmonth and that nobody has ever asked him what a sheet weighs.

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market town have worked out inside about nine seconds that the best month that floor has had in a year is the month this county put nine hundred yards of a road into four hundred mouths and five have not, and nobody said it out loud and nobody stopped the other one doing the sum.

Nobody thanked anybody and the inn at the high end had four beds full that morning and put nothing out on a board, because a price in that street has not moved since the middle of Harvestmonth, and the woman of about fifty-one who keeps that house came out into it at about the fifth hour and stopped nine feet short of those boards and would not come nearer to them, and nine doors down a chandler put a cloth over a bench rather than write a thing on it.

Nobody said anything and the near wheel of a cart came off at the low end and the carter got it up again with a bar in about nine minutes and nobody stopped walking, and two women at the third door argued about a bolt of cloth for about four minutes and then went into the shop and came out with it wrapped.

Then the thing that is in the middle of that morning, and nobody in that street had anything to do with it.

Nobody thanked him and a cart with paper in it went up the made road on the Wednesday, and it came through the gate at the low end of that street at about the sixth hour with a man in it who did not get down at the weigh-house and did not stop at the inn and went on up the made road four days, and about four people in that street had worked out that a cart going up a made road with paper in it is not a thing anybody in nine counties has seen in about four and a half years and about five had worked out nothing.

Nobody said anything and the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up had a piece of blue wool over his forearm and he took it off and put it back and said nothing about the cart at all, and four people in that street had worked out that he noticed it and five had not, and nobody has ever established that a cloth seller noticing a cart means anything.

Nobody thanked anybody and the lane that holds that room is four hundred paces long and runs off the made street at the back of that weigh-house, and it is not made, and it is about nine feet wide, and nobody goes up it on a day the room is shut, and nobody has ever gone up it to see whether the room is shut, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the only room in nine counties that people are careful about and five have said that they are careful about it because of the stairs.

Nobody said anything and the wind came off the low side at about the ninth hour and went along that street to the east and took the leaves off nine hundred yards of orchard between the eleventh mile stone and the top of that hill, and nobody on that made road picked a leaf up and nobody has ever picked one up, and about four people in nine counties have said since that the reason there is fruit on those trees is that nobody in nine counties has ever stood under one.

Nobody thanked him and then the man of thirty-two with a stick stood at the sea end of that market street with his right hand on it, and his left hand was in the side of his coat, and nobody looked at that and nobody asked him anything, and four people in that street had worked out that a man who walks four days of made road every morning has not come here for the market and five had worked out that he comes here for the room over the weigh-house being open, which it is, on a Saturday and a Thursday.

Nobody said anything and there was a thing he had worked out at about the fourth hour of that morning and he had not said any of it out loud, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the fourth time in about four and a half years a man of thirty-two has worked a thing out in that street and not said it, and five have said that the first was the first day of a year and the second was the twenty-second of Longlight and the third was the twenty-fifth of Harvestmonth.

Nobody thanked anybody and then he said something out loud, and it was the first thing he has said out loud in that street in about four and a half years that was not the finding of another person, and it was said to about nine people in four trades and it was in his own trade, and it was 42 words, and nobody stopped him.

“**I have got a thing I want and I am going to ask two people for it out loud in the open, and I have not asked either of them, and I am not going to send anybody to ask for me.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a man say out loud that he is going to ask two people for something and five had heard 42 words, and nobody asked him what the thing was and nobody asked him who the two people were, and about four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years a man of that age has said out loud in a market street that he is going to ask for something and five have said that the not being asked what it was is the whole of what happened.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel was at the high end of those boards and said nothing at all that morning, and he has said nine things out loud in that street in about four and a half years and said the ninth of them on the thirteenth of Rainmonth and has refused on that day to say anything twice and has not said anything since, and four people in that street had worked out that a man who has said nine things and then said nothing for forty-six days is a man waiting and five had worked out that he has run out and four had said that both of those are the same thing.

Nobody said anything and the two people he is going to ask are not anybody who has refused him before, and one of them keeps a road that has been shut about eleven years and the other one is nine clerks in an office that nothing in nine counties can be put in front of, and four people in nine counties have said since that a man who chooses the two people he is going to ask out of everybody in nine counties has already done the hardest part of it and five have said that he has done none of it.

Nobody thanked anybody and then the middle of that morning happened the way the middle of every morning in that street happens, and about two hundred people went back to about nine trades, and the light came up the side of that weigh-house and found the upper edge of those boards, and the lamp at the high end of them was lit at the fourth hour and stood lit until the market was gone and nobody in that street has ever turned it out early.

Nobody said anything and nothing about any of it is in a document, and there is no page in any room in nine counties with the thirtieth of Harvestmonth on it, and the register in the room over that weigh-house holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a county which cannot write down that a man is going to ask two people for something is a county that has not decided whether it wants him to.

Nobody thanked him and then a person at the fifth door asked him one thing out loud, kindly, at about the ninth hour, and nobody stopped it, and it was not about the two people and not about the road and it was asked by somebody who wanted to know one thing.

“**Is it about a road?**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a question asked about whether a thing is about a road and about five had heard a person at the fifth door ask it, and the man of thirty-two with a stick answered it out loud, in the open, in his own trade, and it was 33 words, and nobody thanked him.

“**It is not about a road and it is not about the finding and it is about me and it is the first thing I have wanted in four and a half years.**”

Nobody said anything and two hundred people in that market street had heard a man of thirty-two say out loud that he wants something and five had heard 33 words and had not understood one of them, and nobody asked him a second question and nobody asked him a third, and four people in nine counties have said since that the first question nobody in nine counties has asked him in about four and a half years was about him and not about a road and five have said that a question about a man is a question nobody in this county has a way of answering.

Nobody thanked anybody and the frost was back in the ruts by the eleventh hour and the light went off the west bank first and off those boards about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over, and about four hundred people went out of that market street into about nine trades and about nine people stayed at the boards, and about nine hundred yards of an old road is in about four hundred mouths and in nobody's hand.

**And the cost of that Thursday is one thing a man of thirty-two wants, four days of a drove going up a lane nobody walks, and about nine hundred yards of an old road in about four hundred mouths, are not in it and none of them has a price.**

**a sheep peg at 1, a hurdle hoop at 2, a tallow dip at 3, a gate pin at 4, a hurdle at 5, a hand bar at 6, a wicket at 7, a wall peg at 8, a barrow wheel at 9, and a peg at 10.** One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine and ten is fifty-five pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and fifty-five pence is one mark and seven over.** The last day of Harvestmonth came on a Thursday with about four hundred people in a market street, and a cart with paper in it went up the made road the day before and did not stop at the weigh-house, and a man of thirty-two with a stick said out loud that he is going to ask two people for one thing and nobody asked him what it was and nobody asked him who they were.
