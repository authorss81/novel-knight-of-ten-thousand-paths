# Chapter 0719

**Longlight the nineteenth, YR 322, a Wednesday and not a market morning with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and no market in that street, one day after six people came down two miles of unmade road, four days off at the head of that made road where about nine yards of a wall is down.**

Two days back is the whole of what there is between that shoulder and that market town and there is no third way, and nine people in nine counties have said since that eleven days up a hill and two days down it is a rate of about five and a half to one and that a man who has never done it has no way of judging it until he has done the down part.

Nobody thanked anybody and they came down on the eighteenth of Longlight at about the second hour and got to the head of that made road at about the eighth hour, and the first mile of it took them about as long as the two miles of it and the second mile took them the rest of the day, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the first mile of a road going down is always the slower one and that nobody has ever worked out why.

Nobody thanked anybody and the lane turns twice before the shoulder and both turns are on the way down as well as on the way up, and the first is a turn to the left round about nine yards of bank about four hundred yards past the field, and the second is a turn to the right round a stone of about the size of a cartwheel that is in the middle of the lane because it has always been in the middle of the lane.

Nobody thanked anybody and the field with the stone wall round it was at the end of the first nine hundred yards and it was empty on the eighteenth of Longlight because the drove goes out on Thursdays and had been up on the Thursday and come back at about the fifth hour, and nine people in nine counties have said since that six people came down that lane behind about nine beasts and about four feet of water in the ruts had been in it a month earlier and five have said that nobody has ever put those two facts in one sentence.

Nobody thanked anybody and the nine hundred yards of kerbed road on that shoulder and the nine hundred yards of a line in the grass are still there and nothing on either of them has been moved, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that anybody has gone over that road and left it exactly as it was, and four people have said that this is the difference between a party and a column and five have said that it is the difference between a man with a broom and a man without one.

Nobody thanked him and the ground at the head of that made road in Longlight is not mud and is not dry either, and about nine yards of the nine hundred yards of wall is still down where it came down on the twenty-eighth of Thawmonth, and the gap in it is about nine yards long and about four feet high, and the bar went in on the lane side on the Thursday morning after the water went out of the lane and it is in.

Nobody said anything and a man of about forty-one stood at the head of the gap and did not help and did not watch them come through and went back in, and nobody said anything to him and he said nothing, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the fifth time in about four and a half years that a man has stood at the head of that gap while somebody came through it and that four of the five were six people and one was a drove.

Nobody thanked him and the woman of about thirty-nine who keeps the second gate from the top of that made road went down the made road four miles on foot because she has not kept a horse in about nine years, and she stopped at her own gate and shut it and put a bar in it and stood in her own gateway and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that she has come back from anywhere at all and put her own bar in herself, and four people have said that a gate shut by its keeper and a gate left are two gates and five have said that she had said so herself on the seventeenth.

Nobody said anything and the man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract for about nineteen years went back down the made road toward the town and nine people in nine counties have said since that a man who has been off his own four miles for twelve days goes to it the day he gets down and not the day after, and five have said that this is true of every trade in nine counties and four have said that it is not true of bees.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about thirty-eight who puts boards on the inside of gates went the other way, toward the low side of that made road and the causeway and the coast road, and nine people in nine counties have said since that about four hundred people in nine counties will find out about nine yards of boards in a fortnight and that he said out loud on that shoulder that nobody had asked him for them.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about twenty-seven who keeps eels in weirs about two days north of that causeway went north along that made road with his hands still split at the second joint of two fingers, and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel went south into the market town, and nobody thanked anybody and nobody gave an account of anything to anybody.

Nobody said anything and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that five people have come back down a road from somewhere and not one of them has said one word about it, and that four of them had trades four days off and one of them had no trade in nine counties at all, and that four people have said this is a party and five have said this is six people who went to the same hill.

Then the two of the nine, which is the whole of what they came up for.

Nobody thanked anybody and two of the nine households at the far end of that lane had come up to a line of nine stones twice and had not been past them and had come down again, and nine people in nine counties know that and about five have ever seen it, and four people in nine counties have said since that nine households have been kept going for longer than anything in nine counties has been kept and that five have said that two of them came nine hundred yards twice and seven of them did not come once.

Nobody said anything and the reason seven and not nine is a rota of beasts that has been kept about nine years and has not been broken once in the nine years, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time anybody in this county has given a reason for not doing a thing that anybody with a beast could check in about nine seconds.

Nobody thanked him and the man of thirty-two came in at about the eighth hour on the nineteenth with his stick in his right hand and did not go up the lane that holds the room over that weigh-house, and the room was shut behind its own door because a Wednesday is not a market morning, and nobody sent for anybody and nobody was sent for and nothing about any of it is in a document.

Nobody said anything and four people in that market town had noticed that he was back and five had not, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a man who comes off a hill at the eighth hour on a Wednesday and does not go up a lane four hundred paces long is a man who has been on that hill, and five have said that nobody has ever asked him to go up a lane and that this is the eleventh time he has not.

Nobody thanked anybody and the ordinary half of a Wednesday four days off went on, and about nine hundred people along that made road did the ordinary part of it, and the two miles of wall and orchard between the eleventh mile stone and the top of the hill were in blossom and about nine hundred trees of them and the owners are about nine households and not one of them has ever asked anybody anything about the six people who went past it.

Nobody thanked anybody and then the ordinary half of a Wednesday in that market town happened, and it is nine tenths of every day anybody in nine counties has ever had, and there was no market in that street and nine people were in it, and four of them stood at those boards with nothing on them and about five did not.

Nobody thanked anyone and the inn at the high end put no board out on a Wednesday because it does that on Fridays and Thursdays and Saturdays, and the woman of about fifty-one who keeps that house had four beds empty for the first time since the middle of Harvestmonth and nine people in nine counties have said since that a market town's beds are full on the two days it has a market and empty on the five it does not, and that this is the only figure anybody in nine counties has about how many people come into a market town on a Wednesday.

Nobody thanked anyone and a chandler nine doors down put a cloth over a bench rather than write anything at all on it, and nine people in nine counties have said that the whole of the difference between that trade and the trade of the inn two doors up is whether a person can be found afterwards, and five have said that a cloth on a bench has been on it since the middle of Hearthmonth and that nobody has ever asked about it and that asking would be the only way to find out.

Nobody said anything and about nine hives stand in a walled yard about four rods by six behind a stone wall about four feet high half a mile below the wall, and the young man of about nineteen who keeps them had about three frames of brood in two hives where there were nine on the night of the eighth of Thawmonth, and a brood frame comes back in about four weeks in a year like this one and it is a hundred and one days since that night, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a figure a person gives out loud about his own year is the only kind of figure that has ever come back better than it went.

Nobody thanked anybody and the causeway is four days south of that market town and about nine hundred paces long and about nine feet wide at the crown, and six people went across it on the nineteenth at about the third hour with the water four hundred yards wide and the ebb at that hour takes about an hour and a half, and a man with a bad leg took about nine minutes and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the second crossing of that causeway on foot in about four and a half years and that the first one was on a Tuesday in Goatmonth with salt on the man's coat.

Nobody thanked him and there is about nine hundred yards of laid kerb on that far bank and then grass, and four people in nine counties have said since that six people were on the far side of that channel on the nineteenth of Longlight and that nobody has ever been able to establish what any of them thought about it, and five have said that nobody thought about it at all because nobody was looking for anything and were about nine hundred yards past it before the light went.

Nobody thanked anybody and a hand lamp is put on the coping at the low side of that causeway every Monday afternoon in the winter, and the nineteenth was a Wednesday and the lamp was not put there, and the hand bell on the gate post above it is about the size of a fist and is rung when the water is over the causeway, and the water was not over it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a bell that is not rung is the ordinary condition of a bell and that nobody in nine counties has ever asked whether anybody on that far bank knows it is there.

Nobody said anything and the copyist of about twenty-six did not come down the thirteen stairs on that Wednesday, and the register behind that door holds ten questions with no finding under any of them, and the fourth ruled head is empty, and no fifth head is ruled, and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and none of that was touched on the nineteenth of Longlight and none of it was touched on any day in the last three weeks.

Nobody thanked anyone and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel went into that market town on that Wednesday and stood at the high end of those boards with nothing on them and said nothing at all, and four people in that street had worked out that a man with a satchel had been on a hill that week and five had not, and nobody asked him anything, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a man with a satchel went into a town on the day after he came off a hill and that this is the whole of what that day is.

Nobody thanked anybody and the weigh-house on that Wednesday weighed a load of lime and nothing else, and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that a day with no market on it is a day on which about four hundred and nine sheets do not come past his floor, and that nobody has ever asked him what a sheet weighs.

Nobody said anything and about four hundred people in that market town have heard four things said out loud since the twelfth of Thawmonth and four of them believe the four things and five have never spoken to any of the other four, and nine people in nine counties have said since that about four hundred people hearing a thing and not acting on it is a thing this county can now do at about four hundred to a market morning and that it has been doing it since the middle of Harvestmonth.

Nobody thanked anybody and four people in nine counties have said since that the whole of this county's business is finished, and five have said it is not, and neither of those two sets has ever spoken to the other and nobody in nine counties has asked either of them why.

Nobody said anything and a person in that market town asked him out loud, kindly, on the made road, on the Wednesday, about four hundred yards from the town, whether the road went anywhere, and he said that it went about nine hundred yards of kerb and about nine hundred yards of a line in the grass and stopped at nine stones, and nobody said anything else and nobody asked him a second question.

“**Does it go any further than that?**”

Nobody said anything and four people on that made road had heard the question and five had not, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the fifth question anybody in nine counties has asked about that lane and that the answer has not changed since the twelfth of Thawmonth and that nobody has yet asked the sixth question, which is what a person does with an answer that has not changed.

**And the cost of that Wednesday is twelve days off four trades, nine yards of boards a fortnight late, a gate shut by its own keeper, and about nine hundred yards of a line in the grass that five people walked down and nobody has written a word of, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
