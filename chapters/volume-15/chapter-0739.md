# Chapter 0739

**Embermonth the twenty-first, YR 322, a Saturday and a market morning and the last one before the twenty-fifth of that month, the second hour in the market street at the boards with about four hundred people in it and about two hundred of them stopped, the room over the weigh-house open, and a county with two refusals in it and no way of holding them.**

A Saturday in that market town of about two thousand people is the loudest thing in nine counties, and four hundred people were in that street by the second hour of the twenty-first of Embermonth and two hundred of them stopped what they were doing, and the frost came off the ruts about an hour after that, and about four people in nine counties had worked out that the fifth frame came in yesterday and five had not.

Nobody thanked anybody and four hundred people in that street have now heard two men say no out loud in two places, and about nine of them can say where each one was said and about five cannot, and four people in nine counties have said since that a finding gets into four hundred mouths in about nine days and a refusal gets into the same four hundred mouths in about as long, and five have said that a refusal is the easier of the two because everybody heard the same one at the same time and nobody had to be told about it twice.

The cloth over the bench at the low end of those boards has been on it since the middle of Hearthmonth and nobody has lifted it and nobody has asked, and the lamp at the high end of them is lit at the fourth hour on every day of the week on which the light has gone off the wet slate of them, and about nine people in that market street have said since that a lamp and a cloth and a set of boards with nothing on them are the whole of what a county of two thousand people can look at from its own market street.

Then the ordinary half of that Saturday, which is nine tenths of it, and nothing about the two refusals is in any of it.

Nobody said anything and a drove went up that lane four days off on the Thursday before, which was the nineteenth of Embermonth, at about the sixth hour, and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back down at about the fifth hour, and nobody went with it, and that is about twenty-nine drove to go up that lane since the second of Mudmonth and nobody has ever written any of them down.

Eight of the nine yards of wall at the head of that made road are back in and the gap is about a yard long and about four feet high, and one man has been carrying stone two miles in his own hands for a hundred and ninety-nine days, and the last yard is about nine feet of a course and he has not started it, and nobody in nine counties has asked him when he will and nobody is going to.

The brood in a walled yard half a mile below that wall is five frames where there were nine on the night of the eighth of Thawmonth, and that night is two hundred and twenty-three days back, and the fifth frame came in on the twentieth of Embermonth, and a brood frame comes back in about four weeks in a year like this one, and four people in nine counties have said since that five frames is the first figure anybody in this county has given this year that is half of what it was and five have said that half is a figure and not a finding and that nobody has been asked to make anything of it.

Nobody thanked him and the young man of about nineteen who keeps those nine hives said out loud, in the open, in his own walled yard, to about four people, in his own trade, that the fifth frame came in on the twentieth and that he had not put it there for anybody's sake and that it was a frame and a half mile below a wall and he was not going to make more bees than the bees he has got, and nobody thanked him and nobody argued with him about it, and nobody in nine counties has asked him whether he meant any of it.

The yard itself is about four rods by six behind a stone wall about four feet high and nine hives stand in it, and the ground inside it is beaten flat by nine pairs of boots going over the same twenty feet every morning since the first of Fallowmonth, and the man who cleans the ninth mile stone on that made road with a bit of stick has never been inside it, and the young man who keeps those hives has never been to the ninth mile stone, and neither of those two men has ever heard the other one mentioned, and about four people in nine counties have said since that this is the second time a young man has said that sentence out loud and five have said that the first time was about a month ago and that nothing has happened since the first time and that this is not a criticism of the young man.

The broom on that east rise is nine acres of standing dead about nine hundred yards off and about two hundred yards above the gap in that wall, and two miles of wall and orchard between the eleventh mile stone and the top of that hill went out of blossom in the second week of Fallowmonth, and the owners of those two miles are nine households and not one of them has ever asked anybody anything about anybody who walks past its gate.

The sea is four days south of that market town, and a man can walk those four days of made road to it in six days with a bad leg and a stick, and about four hundred people live on a far bank across nine hundred yards of water at the top of a tide and about four hundred at the bottom, and nobody in that market street has ever asked a person at a gate at the near end of that water anything at all, and four people in nine counties have said since that this county has two far sides and one of them is nine hundred yards of water and the other is nine hundred yards of grass on a hilltop, and five have said that the water one is the easier of the two to look at from a distance.

Nobody said anything and the weigh-house weighed two loads of lime and a load of wool and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that about four hundred and nine parish rolls come past his floor in a good month, that Embermonth is twenty-one days old and about two hundred and ten sheets have come past that floor in it, and that the twenty-first of a month has brought nothing to that floor that the twentieth did not, and that nobody has ever asked him what a sheet weighs.

The inn at the high end had four beds full and put no price out, because a price in that street has not moved since the middle of Harvestmonth, and the woman of about fifty-one who keeps that house stood nine feet off those boards at the fifth hour and would not come nearer to them, and a chandler nine doors down put a cloth over a bench rather than write a thing on it.

The ordinary part of that market morning is that four hundred people each put a thing down and pick it up again.

The fruit on nine hundred trees between two miles of wall came down into four hundred yards of grass every year and was still in it in the spring, and nobody in nine counties has ever picked one up, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the only harvest in nine counties that nobody has ever had to gather, and five have said that there is a ninth household on that made road whose gate nobody has ever asked anybody anything about either and that nobody has ever counted it, and a man with a barrow of lime went the length of that street nine times and unloaded at four doors and loaded at two, and nobody stopped walking at any part of it, and the near wheel of a cart came off at the low end and the carter got it up again with a bar in about nine minutes, and two women at the third door argued about a bolt of cloth for about four minutes and then went into the shop and came out with it wrapped.

Nobody said anything and the room over that weigh-house was open because it is a Saturday and the copyist of about twenty-six came down the thirteen stairs at the seventh hour and went back up, and four people in that lane saw her come down and five did not, and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and the day-book stands at eleven entries and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled.

Then the part of that Saturday that was said out loud, and only two things in it were, and neither of them was about a road.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract for about nineteen years came in at about the fourth hour with about nine feet of a new kerb standing against a shop wall and he said nothing about the two refusals and one thing about the winter, and it was in his own trade, and it was 53 words, and nobody stopped him.

“**Two men saying no in two places and about nine hundred yards of a road and there is not one of the three I can put on a contract, and I have got about nine hundred feet of kerb to lay before the weather turns and a winter to get through after it.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a roadkeeper put a refusal and a road and a winter in one sentence in the open and five had heard fifty-three words, and nobody argued with him and nobody thanked him, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years a man in this county has priced a refusal against nine hundred feet of kerb and five have said that a roadkeeper who has found out he cannot lay anything is still the only man in nine counties who knows what a thing costs.

Nobody said anything and then the man who keeps that weigh-house asked a question out loud at the low end of that street at about the ninth hour, and nobody stopped it, and it was not about a road and not about a wall and it was asked kindly.

“**Is any of it going in a book?**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a question asked about whether two refusals and nine hundred yards of a road are going in a book anywhere and five had heard it asked kindly, and nobody stopped it and the man of thirty-two with a stick answered it out loud, in the open, in his own trade, and it was 46 words, and nobody thanked him.

“**No. There is nowhere in nine counties to put it and I have looked, and the one room that could take it takes nothing, and its register has ten questions on it and no finding under any of them, and that is not mine to fill.**”

Nobody said anything and nobody said anything back to him and nobody thanked him, and four people in that market street had worked out inside about nine seconds that a man who has been refused twice and has said the sentence about not arguing has also just said out loud that the only room in nine counties that could take a finding has nothing on it, and five had not, and four people in nine counties have said since that a man who says out loud that he has looked is a man who has been looking for about four and a half years and five have said that he said it in a market street on a Saturday and that is the first place anybody in this county says a true thing that is also about himself.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about fifty-eight with a badge and no head was at the high end of those boards on that Saturday and said nothing at all, and four people in that market street had worked out that he did not ask the man of thirty-two a second question after the first of them, and five had worked out nothing, and nobody sent for him and nobody asked him anything, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a badge-man who has asked one question in about four and a half years and has not asked it twice is the only man in this county who has understood something that happened on the seventeenth of Rainmonth, and five have said that this is not a compliment to him and that it is a fact about him.

Nobody said anything and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel was at the high end of those boards and said nothing in that street for about as long as it takes to turn a cart, and he has said nine things out loud in this county in about four and a half years and said the ninth of them on the thirteenth of Rainmonth and has refused since that day to say anything twice, and nobody asked him and nobody sent for him, and four people in that market street had worked out that ninety-eight days of not saying anything is longer than the nine days it took the last finding to get nine hundred yards into four hundred mouths, and five had not worked that out and will not.

Then the whole of what that Saturday is, and it is short, and it is not about a road.

Nobody thanked anybody and the part of that man of thirty-two which was going to come home to him on the fourth of Rainmonth is not going to come home, and he said out loud in this street on the twenty-eighth of Fallowmonth that he is not going to ask either of them again and that he is not going to argue with either of them, and that sentence is the whole of what he has done about it, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man who says out loud that he is not going to ask again has given the whole of it away in one morning and five have said that he had nothing to give and that the giving is what costs.

Nobody said anything and nobody in nine counties has consoled him and nobody in nine counties has thanked him and nobody has said one word to him about the night of the ninth hour that a thing at his left hand went back to about four and a half years ago, and about four people in nine counties have said since that this county has never had a thing go wrong that nobody in it had to be told about and five have said that a thing that goes wrong and is not told about is a thing this county has four of now.

Nobody thanked him and that thing at that left hand is still there and was still warm at about the fourth hour of the morning of the twentieth of Embermonth and it will be warm again in about four days, and what he said out loud on the nineteenth of that month is not that he is never going to use it, and nobody in nine counties has asked him to say that and nobody is going to.

Nobody said anything and about nine hundred yards of an old road is in about four hundred mouths and in nobody's hand, and two refusals are in about four hundred mouths and in nobody's hand either, and one sheet of paper is in an office four days off and one thing at a left hand is in one man's head, and four people in nine counties have said since that a county can hold a thing in four hundred mouths or in one hand or on one sheet of paper and that this county has one of each of the three and has never once been able to move one of them into another.

Nobody thanked anybody and nothing about any of it is in a document, and there is no page in any room in nine counties with the twenty-first of Embermonth on it, and about four people in nine counties have said since that the last market morning of a month in this year is a morning with two refusals in it and a fifth frame of brood in it and nine hundred yards of a road in it and that a person who could put any two of those three in the same book would have this county's whole business on one page and five have said that the person who could do that is the man who has been refused twice.

Nobody said anything and then the light came off the west bank first and off those boards about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over.

Four hundred people went out of that market street into about nine trades inside an hour, and one of those nine trades is a man unloading lime out of a barrow and another of them is a woman selling cloth and another of them is the man who keeps that weigh-house, and four people in nine counties have said since that a street of nine hundred paces with four hundred people on it in the second hour has nine trades in it in the ninth, and five have said that the other three hundred and ninety people went home and that about nine of the four hundred stayed at the boards. The lamp at the high end of them was lit at the fourth hour and stood lit until the market was gone, and nobody in that street has ever turned it out early.

**And the cost of that Saturday is a fifth frame of brood where there were nine, about nine hundred feet of kerb that a roadkeeper has laid against two refusals and has not been able to put on a contract, and about four hundred yards of a street that cannot hold what it has heard, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
