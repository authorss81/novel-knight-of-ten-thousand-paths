# Chapter 0729

**Harvestmonth the first, YR 322, a Wednesday and not a market morning and the first of a month, with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and the boards standing in a market street with four people at them, and a finding nine hundred yards of an old road that is in nobody's hand.**

The first of a month in this county comes whether or not anybody is in the street for it, and the first of Harvestmonth came on a Wednesday, and a Wednesday is not a market morning, and nine people were in that market street by the ninth hour and four of them were at the boards with nothing on them and five were not.

Nobody thanked anybody and that is the eighth first of a month in a row on which nothing has happened at those boards and the eighth of them is the first that came on a Wednesday, and four people in nine counties stand at them at about the ninth hour on a first of a month and five do not, and nobody has sent for the man who built it on any of the eight and the badge-man has not been at two of them on purpose.

Nobody said anything and the room over that weigh-house was shut behind its own door and the bolt was on from the Saturday, and nobody went up the lane four hundred paces long that holds it, and four people in that market town had worked out that the room keeps a register at ten questions with no finding under any of them and has kept it since the middle of Longlight the year before, and five had not worked it out and never will.

Nobody thanked him and Harvestmonth is the month that four hundred and nine parish rolls come past the floor of that weigh-house in their best state, and nine people in nine counties know that and about five have ever asked the man who keeps it, and the four weeks before this one were the middle of Rainmonth and about half of that month's sheets came past that floor wet.

Nobody said anything and the boards at the low end of that street have been standing since the middle of Longlight the year before and are the colour of a wet slate and the cloth over the bench at the low end of them has been on it since the middle of Hearthmonth and nobody has lifted it and nobody has asked what is under it, and the lamp at the high end of them was lit at the fourth hour and stood lit until the light was gone.

Nobody said anything and a hearing in this county stands in a street on the first of every month and has done since the middle of Longlight the year before, and since the first of Goatmonth it cannot be called and does not sit, and its firsts have now come and gone seven times running, and four people in nine counties have said since that a thing that has happened seven times and done nothing seven times is not a thing anybody in nine counties has stopped expecting, and five have said that it is a thing everybody in nine counties has stopped expecting and four have said that those are different.

Nobody said anything and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel has said nothing in that market street since the thirteenth of Rainmonth and four people in nine counties have said since that a man who said nine things in about four and a half years and then stopped on the day after the ninth of them has either been told something or has worked something out, and five have said that he has been told something and four have said that he has worked it out and that neither of them will say what.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up has nine names in his head from the middle of Fallowmonth and has still not written them down, and four people in that market street asked him for the list in the middle of Frostmonth and about five did not, and he has said the same thing on this first of Harvestmonth that he said on the first of Mudmonth, which is that he is not going to write nine men down in a book and hand it to anybody.

Then the badge-man, and he said one thing, and it was in his own trade.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about fifty-eight with a badge and no head came down that lane from the weigh-house at a slow walk with the bar against his leg and the strap of it over his shoulder and did not hurry, and he stopped four feet short of those boards and put his hand flat on the top rail, and the rail had a skin of white in it at about the seventh hour, and about nine people in that market street have said since that a man who touches a thing he is about to speak at is about to speak carefully.

Nobody said anything and he said it out loud, in the open, to nine people, in his own trade, and it was 78 words, and he did not say it again that day.

“**I have three clauses in nine hands with no copy of them and they are about a man doing a thing to a road, and a paper does not do a thing to a road, and there is no clause in nine hands about a road that nobody has done a thing to and about nine hundred yards of that has just been said out loud in this county by a young man with brooms on a board.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a badge-man put the shape of his own arrangement into a market street on the first of a month and five had heard 78 words, and nobody argued with him and nobody thanked him, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the second time in about four and a half years that a person has said out loud that the arrangement does not cover a thing and that the first time it was said he said it in a trade and this time he said it on a first of a month in a street with nine people in it.

Nobody thanked anybody and about four people in that market street had worked out inside about nine seconds that what the badge-man said is the same thing he said on the twenty-second of Longlight in about nine more words and about five had not, and four people in nine counties have said since that the same arrangement described twice in two streets by the man who wrote it is the only repetition anybody in nine counties has got and five have said that it is a repetition and not an answer.

Then a question asked out loud in that street on the first of a month, and it was asked kindly.

“**Then what do you call it?**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a question asked about what a badge-man calls a road nobody has done a thing to and five had heard a question asked kindly by a person at the weigh-house who has never asked the badge-man anything, and nobody stopped it and the badge-man answered it out loud, in the open, in his own trade, and it was 46 words, and he put his hand back on the rail.

“**I call it a road and I have called it a road in nine hands and nobody has ever heard me use the word. That is the whole of what I have got and I have had it since the middle of Longlight the year before.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a badge-man say out loud that he has a word in nine hands that nobody has ever heard him use and five had heard 46 words, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that a person in this county has admitted out loud that there is something in their own hand they have never used and four have said that this is a standing defect and five have said that it is not a defect because nobody has ever needed it.

Nobody thanked him and then the road office, which is nine clerks, and it was said in a trade.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract for about nineteen years came in at about the fifth hour with about nine feet of a new kerb standing against a shop wall and he said it out loud, in the open, to nine people, in his own trade, and it was 58 words, and nobody stopped him.

“**There are nine clerks in the road office in this county and about nine hundred yards of that old road has been in this county's mouth for eighteen days now and nobody in nine counties has any way of putting it in front of a clerk and I have not asked one and I am not going to.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a roadkeeper say out loud that there is a way of putting a thing in front of a clerk and that he is not going to use it and five had heard 58 words, and nine people in nine counties have said since that about nine hundred yards of a road older than this county has now been described three times in the open and cannot be given to anybody, and about four people have said that this is the third finding in this county that is not a finding and five have said that it is the first one that has been handed to a person who could carry it and put it down.

Nobody said anything and about nine people in nine counties know that about twenty-one counties are still on a list in that office and that nobody in nine counties has read past the ninth of them, and about five have ever read the ninth, and four people in nine counties have said since that a county that cannot be asked a question about nine hundred yards of its own road is a county that is on a list and nothing else, and five have said that this county is not on the list and that nobody in nine counties has checked.

Nobody thanked anybody and then the thing that everybody in that street did not do, and it is the whole of the second half of that month.

Nobody said anything and nobody in that market street, or on that made road, or in that market town, or in nine counties, has asked the man of thirty-two with a stick one word about the fourth of Rainmonth or about the fourth hour of the fourth day, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the second time in about four and a half years that a man of thirty-two has done a thing on his own account and told nobody in this county and that the first time was the first day of a year, and about four people have said that a man who tells nobody gets asked nothing and five have said that a man who tells nobody cannot be asked anything.

Nobody thanked him and four people in nine counties have said since that nobody in this county is going to find out what was offered to him on that shoulder and five have said that nobody is going to ask him because asking him would be asking him to produce something, and four have said that a man who has asked nine hundred girders the same question twice and has not asked a man who keeps beasts twice is a man who has a rule, and five have said that a man who has a rule about what he asks is the reason nine counties has four and a half years of anything at all.

Nobody said anything and about four hundred people in nine counties have heard nine things about one hill and about nine of them have heard the ninth thing and about five have not heard any of the nine, and nobody has asked either of those two groups a question, and about nine people in nine counties have said since that a county that can find four hundred people who have heard a thing and cannot find five who have not is a county with a shortage of five and about four have said that the shortage of five is not in this county.

Nobody thanked him and a chandler nine doors down put a cloth over a bench rather than write anything at all on it, and the cloth over the bench at the low end of those boards has been on it since the middle of Hearthmonth, and about four people in nine counties have said since that this county has three things it can look at from a market street and that a wall four days off is one of them and a bench under a cloth is one and a set of boards with nothing on them is one, and about five have said that the wall is the only one of the three anybody has done anything about.

Nobody said anything and the ordinary half of that Wednesday in that market town happened, and it is nine tenths of every day anybody in nine counties has ever had, and there is no market in that street and nine people were in it.

Nobody said anything and the near wheel of a cart came off at the low end and the carter got it up again with a bar in about nine minutes and nobody stopped walking, and two women at the third door argued about a bolt of cloth for about four minutes and then went into the shop and came out with it wrapped, and the inn at the high end put no board out on a Wednesday and had four beds empty.

Nobody thanked anybody and the weigh-house weighed one load of lime and a load of wool and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that about four hundred and nine parish rolls come past his floor in a good month and that Harvestmonth is the month they come past in their best state, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the only good news anybody in this county has had since the second of Mudmonth and that it is about sheets and that nobody has ever asked him what a sheet weighs.

Nobody said anything and about four days off at the head of that made road the drove went out on the Thursday before, which was the twenty-fifth of Rainmonth, at about the sixth hour and went up about nine hundred yards and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back at about the fifth hour, and nobody went with it, and about seven of the nine yards of wall at the head of that road are back in and the gap is about two yards long and about four feet high, and one man has been carrying stone two miles on his own for about a hundred and nineteen days.

Nobody thanked anybody and the brood in a walled yard half a mile below that wall is three frames where there were nine on the night before the eighth of Thawmonth, and it is a hundred and forty-three days since that night, and four people in nine counties have said since that three frames where there were nine has stopped going up and five have said that it has not stopped and that nobody has counted it in a month.

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market town think the whole of this county's business is finished and about five think it is not, and neither of those two sets has ever spoken to the other, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a broom and about nine hundred yards of an old road have not changed the size of either set and about four people have said that a broom is not a business and five have said that neither is a road nobody is responsible for.

Nobody said anything and about nine hundred yards of an old road is now in about four hundred mouths and in no hand at all, and four people in nine counties have said since that this county has no way of holding a finding once it is out of somebody's mouth and that it has had four and a half years to build one and has not built one, and five have said that a county does not hold a finding, a person does, and about four people have said that there are about four hundred people and five have said that about four hundred people is not a hand.

Nobody thanked anybody and then the light went off the west bank first and off those boards about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over, and about nine people went out of that market street into about nine trades and about four stayed at the boards, and nothing about any of it is in a document, and the register in that room gains nothing and the day-book stands at eleven entries and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled.

Nobody said anything and there is no hearing in nine counties that cannot be called and does not sit and makes nothing, and about nine hundred yards of an old road two miles above a made road is in about four hundred mouths, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this county has found nine things and can hold none of them and that the eleven days in Longlight were the best of it and five have said that the eighteen days since the thirteenth of Rainmonth are worse and four have said that they are better and that nobody has asked the difference.

**And the cost of that Wednesday is about nine hundred yards of an old road in about four hundred mouths and in no hand, a road office with nine clerks and no way of being asked anything by anybody in this county, and a silence about a man of thirty-two that nobody in nine counties will break, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
