# Chapter 0715

**Longlight the eighth, YR 322, a Saturday and a market morning, the second hour in the market street at the boards with about four hundred people in it and about two hundred of them stopped, the room over the weigh-house open, and eight days in which nothing has happened.**

Eight days is not a long time and it is the whole of what this county has done with five people saying out loud in a market street that they were going up a lane, and about nine people in nine counties have said since that a fact said out loud on the first of a month has one life and that the life is a week and that nobody in nine counties has ever found out what a thing does at the end of its life.

Nobody thanked anybody and four hundred people were in that market street by the second hour and two hundred of them stopped what they were doing, and the two hundred are not the same two hundred every Saturday, and the proportion has not moved since the first of Embermonth.

Nobody thanked anybody and that market street runs east and west for about nine hundred paces from the gate at the low end of it to the inn at the high end, and the bank to the west of that town is higher than the street, and the light goes out of that street before the light goes out of the sky.

Nobody thanked anyone and the inn at the high end put a board out with the price of a bed on it in chalk, which it does on Fridays and Thursdays and Saturdays, and it had four beds full and had had them full since the middle of Harvestmonth, and the woman of about fifty-one who keeps that house came out into that street at about the fifth hour and stood about nine feet off the boards and did not come closer.

Nobody said anything and about nine people in that market street have said since that a woman who keeps four beds came out into that street on a Saturday morning and stood nine feet off is the whole of her part in it, and four have said that she does it on every market morning and five have said that she does it because a market morning is the only morning of the week anybody comes into that street off a road.

Nobody thanked anyone and a chandler nine doors down would put a cloth over a bench rather than write anything at all on it, and nine people in nine counties have said that the whole of the difference between that trade and the trade of the inn two doors up is whether a person can be found afterwards, and five have said that this county has been working that difference out for about four and a half years.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about twenty-six whose trade nobody in that market town knows was in that street on the eighth of Longlight and did not try to make a list of anything, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the third market morning on which he has been in that street and not done it, and that a man who is stopped once in a market street knows exactly what he is for.

Nobody thanked him and the badge-man was not in that street on the eighth of Longlight and nobody sent for him, and four people in that market town had noticed that this was the first market morning since the first of Longlight on which he had not come and five had not, and nobody has ever established which of the two it was.

Nobody said anything and the boards were standing at the low end with nothing on them and the rail along the top of them had a skin of white in it at about the seventh hour because the cold comes up out of the ground in about nine days in Mudmonth and about nine days in Longlight and nobody in nine counties has ever worked out whether that is the same nine days.

Nobody thanked anybody and that room is over a weigh-house and the press in it stands open with no date on it and has stood open since about the middle of Longlight the year before, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a press standing open is the ordinary state of a press and that this one is the only press in nine counties anybody has ever looked at without being asked to.

Nobody said anything and the three series in that room stand and are not derived from one another, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the only arrangement in this county that nobody has ever taken anything out of and five have said that nobody has ever taken anything out of it because nothing has ever gone in.

Nobody thanked anybody and the room over that weigh-house was open because it is a Saturday and the copyist of about twenty-six came down the thirteen stairs at the seventh hour and stood in the lane while the room let the cold off it and went back up, and four people in that lane saw her come down and five did not, and nobody sent for anybody and nobody was sent for.

Nobody said anything and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them, and the fourth ruled head over a place where a finding goes is empty, and no fifth head is ruled, and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and none of that has moved in eight days and none of it is going to.

Nobody thanked her and the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps that room was not at the top of the stairs and was not in that lane, and nobody sent for her and nobody sent anybody to her, and four people in that market town had noticed the bolt was not on for the fourth market morning running and five had not.

Nobody thanked anybody and the hearing has not been called since the first of Goatmonth and does not sit, and its first of a month comes whether or not anybody is in the street for it, and the first of Longlight came on a Saturday with four hundred people in that street and not one of them at the boards, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is now the fourth first of a month on which nothing has happened at it and that nobody has sent for the man who built it on any of the four.

Nobody said anything and nobody in nine counties can call that hearing and nobody has tried, and four people in nine counties have said since that a thing which cannot be called is a thing which cannot be said to have gone wrong and five have said that a thing which cannot be said to have gone wrong is a thing which cannot be put right either.

Nobody said anything and then the middle of that morning in that street happened in about nine trades at about nine hundred people doing nine things at once.

The light came up the side of the weigh-house at about the ninth hour and found the upper edge of the boards, and the boards were the colour of a wet slate and had been since the middle of Harvestmonth.

Nobody thanked anybody and two hundred people went back to nine trades and nine stayed at the boards, and nobody wrote any of it down.

Then the thing that is not in that street, which is six people about four miles off on a drove road and over a shoulder of a hill, and it is worth putting in because about nine people in nine counties have said since that a county cannot see the shape of a thing that is not there.

Nobody thanked anybody and six people went up that lane on the fifth of Longlight and arrived at about the fourth hour on the seventh and stood at the far end of about nine hundred yards of a line in the grass and nine stones set across it at the same height, and nobody went past the ninth of the nine, and nobody has come down, and about nine people in nine counties know that and about five have worked out what it means.

Nobody said anything and four people in nine counties have said since that six people standing at the end of a road is not a thing anybody can do one thing about, and about five have said that a man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract is one of the six and that he is the only one of the six that nine counties can ask a question of, and that he has not been asked.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about fifty-two has not been in that market street since the first of Longlight, and four people in that market town had noticed that and five had not, and nobody sent for him and nobody has asked him anything and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the second time in about four and a half years that a man has said out loud that he is going somewhere and has then not been in a street, and that the first time was a young man of about nineteen on the twenty-second of Goatmonth.

Then the street, and a question asked out loud in it, kindly, at the low end of the boards.

Nobody thanked anybody and the road office in that market town is a room with nine counties of clerks in it and no clerk in this county, and four people in nine counties know that a clerk comes down that made road about nine times a year and five have ever seen him get out of a cart, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the office sent a cart up that road in the middle of Hearthmonth with about nine feet of paper under a tarpaulin and that the cart went on south and did not stop in this county.

Nobody said anything and nobody in nine counties has written to that office about a lane, a shoulder or nine stones, and four people in nine counties have said since that if somebody had written to it in the middle of Thawmonth the letter would have gone down that made road in the ordinary way with the next cart, and five have said that nobody has written to it and that the reason nobody has written to it is that a letter has a name on it and this lane does not have anybody's name on it.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up asked it at about the third hour in the open to about two hundred people and nobody stopped him.

“**Then when do they come back down?**”

Nobody said anything and about four hundred people in that market street had heard a man ask when six people will come back from a place and about five had understood that nobody in that street knows, and nobody answered it, and about nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the second question anybody in this county has asked about that lane and that the first was a boy of about eleven who keeps goats asking whether there was anything up there, and that neither question was answered and that about four people have said the answers are the same and about five have said they are not.

Nobody said anything and the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up still has nine names in his head from the middle of Fallowmonth and has still not written them down, and four people in that market street asked him for the list in the middle of Frostmonth and five did not, and he said the same thing on the eighth of Longlight that he said on the first of Mudmonth, which is that he is not going to write nine men down in a book and hand it to anybody.

Nobody thanked him and the nine names are not a list and there is no book and there is no head at the top of it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the third time in about four and a half years that a person has refused in a market street to write down nine names and that the first was the badge-man refusing to count nine gates and the second was a cloth seller refusing the list of nine gates in the middle of Fallowmonth, and that the two refusals have never been put together in the same street by anybody.

Nobody said anything and four hundred people in that market street have heard five reasons given out loud in five trades since the first of Longlight and four of them have said out loud since that they believe the reasons and five have not, and nobody has spoken to anybody about it and about nine people in nine counties have said since that this is now the sixth time in about four and a half years that about four hundred people in one street have carried a thing and put it nowhere.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about thirty-eight who puts boards on the inside of gates was four days off on a drove road and over a shoulder of a hill, and he puts about nine hundred yards of boards on gates in this county in a year and about nine yards of them this month, and nobody in nine counties has asked him for either figure and four people have said that he gives them to anybody who stands still for about nine seconds.

Nobody thanked anybody and the weigh-house weighed two loads of lime and a load of wool and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that about four hundred and nine parish rolls come past his floor in a good month and that in a bad one they come past it in sheets of nine and that he weighs them and they weigh nothing, and about nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the only sentence anybody in this county has ever spoken about a record that is not a lie.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about twenty-seven who keeps eels in weirs about two days north of that causeway was four days off and two days north of that, and nine people in that market street buy eels off him on a market morning and five have not bought one in a year, and four people in nine counties have said since that the weirs are in water and that Longlight is not eel season and that this is the fourth time in about four and a half years that a man has gone out of this county in the wrong month for his trade, and five have said that there is no wrong month when you are the only one who goes.

Nobody said anything and the cloth over the bench at the low end of those boards was on it and nothing lifted it and nobody asked what was under it, and the inn at the high end put a board out with the price of a bed on it in chalk and had four beds full, and about nine people in that market town have said since that the lamp at the high end of those boards is lit at the fourth hour every day of the year and that it is the only thing in nine counties that has happened on every day for about four and a half years without anybody having decided that it should.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of thirty-two was four days off on that made road on his own account and was not in that street and was not sent for and nobody in nine counties knew where he was, and four people in that market town had worked out that he went up with them on the fifth and five had worked out nothing, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a man who goes up a lane with five people he has not met is the sixth and that nobody has asked him whether he is.

Nobody thanked anybody and nothing about any of it is in a document, and there is no page in any room in nine counties with six trades and nine stones on it, and the united front is on a table under a cloth in another county and is not withdrawn and is not repaired, and the leaf of the eleventh of Hearthmonth that nine houses wrote at Nine Elms on the coast is in the inside of a coat and is not read out anywhere and is not addressed to anybody.

Nobody said anything and there are two sets of nine houses in nine counties and neither set has ever spoken to the other, and one of them is on a coast four days south and the other is on the far side of nine stones about two miles over a shoulder of a hill, and about nine people in nine counties have said since that nobody in this county has ever put the two sets together and that a coat with a leaf in it and a shoulder with nine stones on it have been about nine days apart all year.

**And the cost of that Saturday is six people about four miles off with a question nobody can answer, about nine names in one man's head, and eight days in which a market town heard five reasons and did nothing with one of them, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
