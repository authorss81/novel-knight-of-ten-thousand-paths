# Chapter 0722

**Longlight the twenty-ninth, YR 322, a Saturday and a market morning, the second hour in the market street at the boards with four hundred people in it and two hundred of them stopped, the room over the weigh-house open, and the eighth question anybody in nine counties has asked about that lane.**

A Saturday in that market town of about two thousand people is the loudest thing in nine counties, and on this one the four hundred and the two hundred did not want an about in front of them, because a man who stands at the low end of those boards can count the ones who stop and get the same number a second time, and the proportion has not moved since the first of Embermonth, and nine people in nine counties have said since that it is the only figure anybody in this county can check twice a week and has never once been written down.

Nobody thanked anybody and Longlight has one day left in it and Rainmonth is two days off and the fourth of Rainmonth is five days off, and four people in that market street had worked out inside nine seconds that a month in this county ends on a Sunday and five had not.

Nobody said anything and the boards at the low end of that street have been standing since the middle of Longlight the year before and are the colour of a wet slate, and nine people could have told you which of the two coats is on them last and five could not, and nobody took them down, and the hearing has not been called since the first of Goatmonth and does not sit and its first of a month is two days off and will come on a Monday.

Nobody thanked him and the room over that weigh-house was open because it is a Saturday and a room over a weigh-house is open five days in seven, and it shuts at noon on a Saturday until the seventh hour on a Monday, and the copyist of twenty-six came down the thirteen stairs at the seventh hour and went back up, and four people in that lane saw her come down and five did not, and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and the day-book stands at eleven entries and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled.

Then the ordinary half, which is nine tenths of every day anybody in nine counties has ever had, and which is the whole of that morning until about the fifth hour.

Nobody thanked anybody and the inn at the high end put a board out with the price of a bed on it in chalk and had four beds full for the ninth Saturday running, and the woman of fifty-one who keeps that house stood nine feet off the boards and did not come closer, and four people in that street had heard her say that a market town's beds are full on the two days it has a market and empty on the five it does not and five had never worked that out.

Nobody thanked him and the weigh-house weighed two loads of lime and a load of wool and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that four hundred and nine parish rolls come past his floor in a good month and that Rainmonth is the month about half of them come past wet, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the only figure anybody in nine counties has about Rainmonth and that it is about sheets.

Nobody said anything and a chandler nine doors down put a cloth over a bench rather than write anything at all on it, and nobody has lifted that cloth since the middle of Hearthmonth and nobody has asked what is under it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that asking would be the only way to find out and that nobody in nine counties has asked.

Nobody thanked anybody and the near wheel of a cart came off at the low end and the carter got it up again with a bar in nine minutes and nobody stopped walking, and two women at the third door argued about a bolt of cloth for four minutes and then went into the shop and came out with it wrapped, which is how that argument ends nine times in ten.

Nobody said anything and the frost came off the ruts at the low end of that street by the ninth hour and the light came up the side of that weigh-house and found the upper edge of those boards, and four people in that street had worked out that the boards are repainted in the one month of the year when nobody in this county can see them and five had not.

Nobody thanked anybody and the ninth mile stone on that made road is cleaned by one man and nobody has ever seen him do it and he does it nine hundred times in a year, and four people in nine counties know that he cleans that stone and five have ever watched him do it, and there was a ring of mud at the foot of it in the first week of Mudmonth and he has been getting at the ring with a bit of stick ever since.

Nobody said anything and the four days of that made road are hard at the two ends and soft in the middle, and nine hundred people live along it and every one of them knows which house he comes out of, and four of them have offered that man a bed since the middle of Frostmonth and five have not, and nine people in nine counties have said since that he has not taken one and has not said why.

Nobody thanked him and two miles of wall and orchard between the eleventh mile stone and the top of that hill were in blossom and nine hundred trees of them, and the owners are nine households and not one of them has ever asked anybody anything about anybody who walks past its gate, and four people in nine counties have said since that an orchard in blossom four hundred yards off a market street is the only thing anybody in this county has to look at and five have said that it is a fruit crop and not a view.

Nobody said anything and that Saturday was nine days after the first of Longlight, and four people in that street had worked out that five people went up that lane on the fifth of Longlight for five reasons in five trades and came down on the eighteenth and went five ways and have not said one word about it since, and five had not worked it out and four people in nine counties have said since that a party that gave no account of itself on the way down is the only kind of thing anybody in nine counties can put on a hill.

Nobody thanked him and four days off at the head of that made road six of the nine yards of the wall that came down on the twenty-eighth of Thawmonth are back in and the gap is three yards long and four feet high, and the stone is quarried two miles off on the low side, and one man has been carrying it two miles on his own for eighty-seven days, and four people in nine counties have said since that nobody in nine counties has ever worked out how long nine yards of wall takes one man on his own and about five have said that a gap in a wall is shut the day it is filled and not the day it is mended.

Nobody said anything and the bar he carries is four feet of ash with two stones on the end of it and it weighs sixty pounds with the stones, and the man who carries it is forty-one and keeps a drove and a byre and nine hundred yards of wall and has been asked one question about a road in about eleven years, and nobody asked him a second one on the twenty-ninth of Longlight and four people in nine counties have said since that that is because of what the man of thirty-two said out loud on that made road in Mudmonth and five have said it is because nobody thought of it.

Nobody thanked anybody and the hives stand in a walled yard about four rods by six behind a stone wall about four feet high half a mile below that wall off a lane of their own that is not made, and the young man of about nineteen counted the brood in them on the twenty-seventh and it was three frames where there were nine on the night before the eighth of Thawmonth, and it is a hundred and eleven days since that night, and a brood frame comes back in about four weeks in a year like this one.

Nobody said anything and about four people in nine counties have worked out that three is one third of nine and about five have not, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a figure a person gives out loud about his own year is the only kind of figure that has ever come back better than it went, and about four people have said that it went from nine to three and five have said that it is going up and that nobody in nine counties has checked.

Nobody thanked him and the drove at the head of that made road went out on the Thursday before at about the sixth hour as it goes out every Thursday, and went up about nine hundred yards of that lane and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back at about the fifth hour, and nobody went with it, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first drove to go up that lane since the second of Mudmonth that anybody has been on the made road to see go.

Then the question, and it is the eighth, and it is the fourth nobody could answer.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel had been standing at the high end of those boards for about as long as it takes to turn a cart, and he had heard both accounts of that road said out loud in that street on the Thursday and had said nothing about either of them, and then he said a question out loud, in the open, to about four hundred people and to about nine of them, in his own trade, and it was 6 words.

“**Then where does the kerb stop?**”

Nobody said anything and about four hundred people in that market street had heard the eighth question anybody in nine counties has asked about that lane and five had heard the word kerb said out loud in a market street by a man with a satchel, and nobody answered it and nobody tried, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the fourth question about that lane that nobody in nine counties could answer and that the first three were asked on a hilltop or on a made road and this one is the first that was asked where about four hundred people could hear it.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up said one thing back about it and it was in his own trade and it was 40 words, and he did not raise his voice.

“**A man who sells cloth is the last man in nine counties anybody should ask where a road stops, and I answered that question wrongly on Thursday and I am not going to answer it twice in the same street.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a cloth seller refuse a question in the open and five had heard 40 words, and the man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract for about nineteen years did not answer it either and said nothing, and four people in nine counties have said since that the man who could put his hand on the stone where it stops in about nine seconds did not say it on the Thursday and has not said it since, and five have said that a man who keeps four miles of road under contract does not answer a question in a market street about nine hundred yards of somebody else's.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about thirty-eight who puts boards on the inside of gates said one word about it and the word was nine, and he said out loud that he has been up nine ladders a week for about nineteen years and that nine was a number he had used all his working life and that nobody had ever asked him to use it about a road, and nobody stopped him and nobody asked him what he meant.

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had worked out inside about nine seconds that the ninth question about that lane has not been asked yet and about five had not, and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel said nothing about that and looked out at the low end of that street at about nine hundred paces of it.

Nobody thanked anybody and about four people in that street had asked out loud, kindly, and mostly of the man with the satchel, whether anybody in nine counties had been paid to keep that road and nobody answered that one either, and four people in nine counties have said since that it is the first question anybody in this county has asked about that road that anybody in nine counties could answer, and five have said that the answer is a rota of beasts kept about nine years and that nobody in nine counties is on a rota and that both of those sentences are true at once.

Then the thing the question was actually about, and it is that about four people repeated what was said on the Thursday and five did not.

Nobody thanked anybody and about four people in that market street had said out loud on the twenty-seventh of Longlight that a road that goes between two places has both of the places under the grass or that a road that goes across a shoulder goes to a place that is not there any more, and five of them had not repeated either, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a thing said twice in one market street by two people is the same as a thing said once and that a thing said twice by nine people is not a thing anybody in nine counties can do anything with.

Nobody said anything and four have said that the second of those is the reason nothing has happened since the twelfth of Thawmonth and five have said that the first is the reason nothing happened before it, and neither of those two sets has ever spoken to the other and nine people in nine counties have said since that a county that has never had a reason and a county that has had one and not used it are the same county four and a half years later.

Nobody said anything and about four hundred people in that street had heard four things about that lane since the twelfth of Thawmonth and about two accounts of it since the Thursday, and about four of them believe the four things and about five have never spoken to any of the other four, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is now the second time in a week that about four hundred people in this county have heard something and have not acted on it and that the first was the wall on that made road.

Nobody thanked him and the broom on that east rise is about nine acres and comes on about the fourth of Rainmonth and is five days off, and about nine people in nine counties know that it is the only broom within a day's walk of that yard and about five have ever cut it, and four people in nine counties have said since that a young man of about nineteen has said he will not go up that lane until there is one to cut and that this is the fifth day of a promise that has not been broken.

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had worked out that a broom is the only thing anybody in nine counties knows about that hill that anybody can do anything about before the first of a month and about five had worked out nothing, and the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up said one word about it and the word was cloth.

Nobody thanked anybody and then the middle of that morning went on, and about two hundred people went back to about nine trades, and the man of about thirty-two with a stick stood at the sea end of that market street with his hand on it and nobody looked at him and nobody asked him anything, and about four people in that street had worked out that a question nobody can answer is a question that is still in the county and about five had worked out that it is a question that is now in four hundred mouths.

Nobody said anything and the light went off the west bank and off those boards about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over, and the lamp at the high end of them was lit and stood lit until the market was gone, and the cloth over the bench at the low end of them was on it and nothing lifted it and nobody asked what was under it, and about four hundred people went out of that market street into about nine trades and about nine people stayed at the boards.

Nobody thanked anybody and nothing about any of it is in a document, and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none, and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the eighth question about that lane is the first one anybody in this county has asked in a place where the answer could be heard by about four hundred people, and five have said that it makes no difference at all because nobody in nine counties knows it.

**And the cost of that Saturday is a question asked in a market street that about four hundred people heard and nine could answer, six of the nine yards of a wall back in and three not, and a hundred and eleven days of a young man's year going one way, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
