# Chapter 0748

**Frostmonth the seventh, YR 322, a Wednesday and not a market morning with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and no market in that street, the ninth hour in that market street with nine people in it and four of them at the boards, and the whole of what has been found out about the oldest road in this county, which is a boundary and a refusal and no body.**

A Wednesday in that market town of about two thousand people is not a market morning, and nine people were in that street by the ninth hour of the seventh of Frostmonth and four of them were at the boards with nothing on them and five were not, and the bolt on that room over the weigh-house went on from the Saturday and nobody went up the lane four hundred paces long that holds it.

Nobody thanked anybody and that scar is nine feet wide and about nine hundred yards long and it is twelve days old, and it is on the top of that shoulder from the ninth of the nine stones down to the place where the nine-foot line in the grass begins and the kerbed road starts, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a scar is a word for a thing that healed once and five have said that this one has not and that nobody in nine counties is going to be told that it is going to.

Nobody said anything and he went up that shoulder on the Tuesday before at about the seventh hour and walked the whole length of it, and he walked it three times, and he counted it twice with a rod he had made out of the handle of a barrow, and it is nine hundred yards and about nine feet wide and it stops where the grass line stops and not one yard of it is in the kerb, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man who counts a thing in his own trade counts it in lengths and not in yards and five have said that he counted it three times and got nine hundred yards three times.

Then four days off, and about nine hundred people along that made road did the ordinary part of a Wednesday, and the ordinary part is a made road with about nine hundred people on it and about nine trades in the market town at the low end of it.

Nobody said anything and a drove went up that lane on the Thursday before, which was the first of Frostmonth, at about the sixth hour, and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back down at about the fifth hour, and nobody went with it, and that is about thirty-five drove to go up that lane since the second of Mudmonth and nobody has ever written any of them down.

Eight of the nine yards of wall at the head of that made road are back in and the gap is about a yard long and about four feet high, and the stone is quarried two miles off on the low side and one man has been carrying it two miles in his own hands for two hundred and forty-five days, and the last yard is about nine feet of a course and he has not started it, and nobody has asked him when he will and nobody is going to.

The brood in a walled yard half a mile below that wall is five frames where there were nine on the night of the eighth of Thawmonth, and that night is two hundred and sixty-nine days back, and the fifth frame has been all brood since the twenty-seventh of Wolfmonth and no sixth has gone in, and about four people in nine counties have said since that about seven weeks have gone by with the figure standing at five and five have said that a figure that stands still is a figure and about four have said that a young man of about nineteen has said out loud twice that he is not going to make more bees than the bees he has got and five have said that nobody has ever asked him whether he meant any of it.

The weigh-house on that Wednesday weighed one load of lime and nothing else, and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that Frostmonth is seven days old and about seventy sheets have come past that floor in it, and that about nine of them every month come past with a corner torn off, and that nobody has ever asked him what a sheet weighs.

Nobody thanked him and the inn at the high end had four beds empty that morning and put no board out on the made street, and the woman of about fifty-one who keeps that house came out into it at about the fifth hour and stood nine feet off the boards and would not come nearer, and a chandler nine doors down put a cloth over a bench rather than write anything at all on it.

The room over that weigh-house stood bolted from noon on the last Saturday and the copyist of about twenty-six did not come down those thirteen stairs at all that day, and about four people in that lane had worked out that she was not in it and five had not worked it out and never will, and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled.

Then the part of that Wednesday that was said out loud, and it was said once, in the open, on a shoulder, in his own trade, and it is the whole of what has been found out about the oldest road in this county.

Nobody thanked him and the young man of about nineteen was about nine hundred yards off up that east rise with a sickle in his hand on the Tuesday before and the man of thirty-two with a stick was on the grass at the ninth of the nine stones and did not go past them, and he said it out loud, in the open, there, at about the seventh hour, to one person, in his own trade, and it was 122 words, and nobody stopped him and nobody had been told it was going to be said.

“**A road is a thing somebody walks. That line is nine hundred yards of grass that nine households have cut and kept and never once walked, and the nine hundred yards of kerb below it is nine hundred yards of road with not one yard of this in it. The oldest road in this county is the whole thing on that hill and what is broken is the boundary. Nine stones at one height across the top of nine hundred yards is a thing a man on the made road at the bottom could read, and they put them there and they never walked up it, and that is the whole of what I have got and there is nobody to find.**”

Nobody said anything and one person on that shoulder had heard a man of thirty-two say out loud what the oldest road in this county is, and about four people in nine counties had worked out that the answer to where that road goes is that it does not go anywhere and five have not, and nobody argued with him and nobody thanked him and about four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first finding anybody in this county has got that is the size of a thing and five have said that it is the size of nine hundred yards and that nobody in nine counties has ever counted anything in this county before except a man who keeps a weigh-house.

Nobody said anything and there is no body and there was never a body and nobody came down any road in nine counties with one, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a county which has been told where the oldest road goes will ask next who was on it and five have said that there is nobody to ask about because nobody was found and about four have said that nobody was found because nobody was there, and five have said that the people who put nine stones across the top of a boundary and did not walk up it are nine hundred years gone and that is not anybody's business and about four have said that it is everybody's.

The nine stones at the end of that line are about the height of a man's knee and about as wide as two hands and the tops of them are all at the same height, and about nine households keep beasts on the flat past them and the rota of that flat has been kept about nine years, and nobody has ever asked any of the nine about it and nobody is going to, and about four people in nine counties have said since that the two people who walked that line on the Wednesday before said two different reasons for it and that neither of them said anything about the nine and five have said that a boundary nobody has ever asked about is the reason nobody has ever asked about it.

The two miles of wall and orchard between the eleventh mile stone and the top of that hill are a mile of stone on the high side and a mile of orchard on the low side, and about nine hundred trees on that low side are bare now and their fruit has been in four hundred yards of grass since the second week of Fallowmonth and nobody in nine counties has ever picked one up, and about four people in nine counties have said since that the owners of those two miles are nine households and five have said that one of the nine has about nine hundred yards of dead grass nine hundred yards above his own wall and that nobody has ever asked the two of them about it.

Nobody said anything and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel was at the high end of those boards on that Wednesday and said nothing in that street for about as long as it takes to turn a cart, and he has said nine things out loud in this county in about four and a half years and said the ninth of them on the thirteenth of Rainmonth and has refused since that day to say anything twice, and nobody asked him and nobody sent for him, and four people in that market street had worked out that a hundred and forty-four days is longer than the nine days it took the last finding to get nine hundred yards into four hundred mouths and five had not.

Nobody thanked him and then a question came into that shoulder from the young man of about nineteen at about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over, and nobody stopped it, and it was asked kindly, and it was not about a road.

“**Is that where it started?**”

Nobody said anything and the man of thirty-two with a stick answered it out loud, in the open, there, to one person, in his own trade, and it was 40 words, and nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him.

“**That is where it is now and I am not going to tell you where it started because I do not know and there is nobody in nine counties who does and nobody is coming up here with an answer.**”

Nobody said anything and nobody said anything back to him and nobody thanked him, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man who has just given the whole of what he has got away has then said out loud that he does not know where it began and five have said that this is the first time in about four and a half years that a man in this county has refused an answer he was never asked for and about four have said that refusing an answer nobody asked for is the whole of what four months of this has been.

Nobody said anything and he came down off that shoulder on the Tuesday before at about the ninth hour with his left hand at his side and his stick in his right hand and he could not get down the last nine hundred yards of grass in one go and sat down about two hundred yards from the bottom and got up again, and about four people in that market town had worked out that a man who walks four days of made road every day sat down two hundred yards from the bottom of a hill on the seventh of Frostmonth and five had not, and nobody asked him about it and nobody sent for him.

Nobody thanked anybody and then a question came into that market street at about the tenth hour from a person at the fifth door who wanted to know one thing, and nobody stopped it, and it was asked kindly, and it was not about a road.

“**Will you be able to go up it on your own?**”

Nobody said anything and the man of thirty-two with a stick answered it out loud, in the open, in his own trade, and it was 32 words, and nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him.

“**No. I have to have somebody with me and I do not have to be asked twice about that and nobody in nine counties is going to say that to me either.**”

Nobody said anything and nobody said anything back to him and nobody thanked him, and about four people in that market street had worked out inside about nine seconds that a man who has been refused twice in the open in this county has now said out loud that he cannot be alone on a hill, and five had worked out that it is not the same thing and about four have said since that the first time anybody in nine counties has said that out loud was about four and a half years ago on the day he came back and five have said that it was the same man saying the same kind of thing.

Nobody thanked him and that office four days off has now got three pieces of paper in it, being a sheet with one line on it that says a man said a thing in a market street, a leaf with one thumb on it that says a place is shutting its own end before Hearthmonth, and a printed notice with a name at the foot of it that says a road on a coast four hundred miles off is shut from the twentieth of Frostmonth, and nobody in nine counties has asked for any of the three and nobody in nine counties could read the third of them if they asked.

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had worked out inside about nine seconds that a leaf saying a place is shutting its own end came into that office in about five weeks and a printed notice saying a road is shut came into it about three weeks ago, and that none of the three can be sent on and five had worked out that one of the three came up a made road in a cart and about four have said since that the county has been unable to answer anything since the first of Goatmonth and five have said that the first of Goatmonth is when the thing that answers stopped being able to be called.

The two refusals in this county were given on the fourteenth and the twenty-sixth of Fallowmonth and neither has been argued with, and that is eighty-three days and seventy-one days, and nobody in nine counties has asked either of those two men anything since and nobody is going to, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man who has been refused twice out loud and has now answered a question nobody asked about a hill he is not going up alone is a man who has said three things out loud in about nine days and five have said that three is more than anybody in this county has said in about four and a half years.
