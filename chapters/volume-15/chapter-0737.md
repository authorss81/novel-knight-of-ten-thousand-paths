Then the thing he decided on that Friday, and it is the whole of the middle of the chapter, and nobody arranged it and nobody had been told about it.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of thirty-two with a stick was at the head of that made road at about the second hour on the sixth of Embermonth and said it out loud, in the open, to about four people, in his own trade, and it was 48 words, and nobody stopped him and nobody asked him why.

“**I am not going past the ninth of those stones and I am not going to look at that flat from a distance either, and the reason I am giving is that he said no to me and a no is not a thing you can go round.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people at the head of that made road had heard a man give a reason for not going somewhere and five had heard forty-eight words, and nobody repeated it back to him and nobody argued with him, and about four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years a man in this county has said out loud that he is not going to do a thing and given the reason in the same breath, and five have said that giving the reason in the same breath is what makes it permanent.

Nobody thanked anybody and the inn at the high end put no board out on a Friday and had four beds empty for the tenth time since the middle of Harvestmonth, and the woman of about fifty-one who keeps that house came out into that street at about the fifth hour and stood about nine feet off the boards and did not come closer, and a chandler nine doors down put a cloth over a bench rather than write anything at all on it.

Nobody said anything and then a question was asked out loud at the head of that made road at about the third hour by a person at that weigh-house who wanted to know one thing, and nobody stopped it, and it was not about a road and not about a wall and it was asked kindly.

“**Are you going to try them again?**”

Nobody said anything and about four people at that weigh-house had heard a question asked about whether a man is going to ask two people the same thing twice and five had heard it asked kindly, and nobody stopped it and the man of thirty-two with a stick answered it out loud, in the open, in his own trade, and it was 34 words, and nobody thanked him.

“**No. I said out loud in a market street that I am not going to ask either of them again and I am not going to be the one who says a thing twice.**”
