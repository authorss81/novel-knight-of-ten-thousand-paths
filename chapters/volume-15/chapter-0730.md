# Chapter 0730

**Harvestmonth the twenty-fifth, YR 322, a Saturday and a market morning, the second hour in the market street at the boards with four hundred people in it and two hundred of them stopped, the room over the weigh-house open, and two trades in one street saying out loud what four and a half years is worth.**

A Saturday in that market town of two thousand people is the loudest thing in nine counties, and four hundred people were in that street by the second hour of the twenty-fifth of Harvestmonth and two hundred of them stopped what they were doing, and the frost came off the ruts about an hour after that, and four people in nine counties had worked out that a month ends and five had not.

Nobody thanked anybody and Harvestmonth the twenty-fifth is twenty-five days after the ninth thing anybody in nine counties has said out loud about that lane was said out loud in a market street, and nine people in nine counties know that and five have ever asked to hear it again, and four people have said since that a finding is worth nine days in this county and five have said that it is worth more after nine days.

Nobody said anything and the boards at the low end of that street have been standing since the middle of Longlight the year before and have been repainted twice and are the colour of a wet slate, and the hearing has not been called since the first of Goatmonth and cannot be called and does not sit, and the cloth over the bench at the low end of them has been on it since the middle of Hearthmonth and nobody has lifted it and nobody has asked what is under it.

Nobody thanked him and the room over that weigh-house was open because it is a Saturday, and the copyist of twenty-six came down the thirteen stairs at the seventh hour and went back up, and four people in that lane saw her come down and five did not, and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and the day-book stands at eleven entries and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled, and nine hundred yards of an old road two miles above a made road is not in it.

Then the ordinary half of that Saturday, and it is nine tenths of it, and the two trades that are in it are the last thing it does.

Nobody thanked anybody and the inn at the high end had four beds full and put no price on a board that morning because the price had not moved since the middle of Harvestmonth, and the woman of fifty-one who keeps that house came out into that street at about the fifth hour and stood nine feet off the boards and did not come closer, and a chandler nine doors down put a cloth over a bench rather than write anything at all on it.

Nobody said anything and the weigh-house weighed two loads of lime and a load of wool and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that four hundred and nine parish rolls come past his floor in a good month, that Harvestmonth is the month they come past in their best state, and that it has been the best month that floor has had since the middle of a Harvestmonth and that nobody has ever asked him what a sheet weighs.

Nobody thanked him and the near wheel of a cart came off at the low end and the carter got it up again with a bar in nine minutes and nobody stopped walking, and two women at the third door argued about a bolt of cloth for four minutes and then went into the shop and came out with it wrapped, and the light came up the side of that weigh-house and found the upper edge of those boards.

Nobody said anything and four days off at the head of that made road the drove went out on the Thursday before, which was the twenty-third of Harvestmonth, at about the sixth hour and went up nine hundred yards and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back at about the fifth hour, and nobody went with it, and eight of the nine yards of wall at the head of that road are back in and the gap is about a yard long and four feet high, and one man has been carrying stone two miles on his own for a hundred and forty-three days.

Nobody thanked anybody and nobody in nine counties has been up that lane on foot since the eighth of Rainmonth, and the broom on that east rise is nine acres and it has been standing on it for thirty days with nine days of it left to be cut, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is now the first errand anybody in this county has with a date on it and five have said that it has had a date on it for about nine years and that nobody came.

Then a question about the rest of that broom, asked in a market street, and answered in a trade.

Nobody thanked anybody and the young man of about nineteen was in that street on the twenty-fifth of Harvestmonth for the first time since the thirteenth of Rainmonth, and somebody asked him out loud, kindly, at about the seventh hour, what happened to the nine bundles he said he would leave on the stones at the top, and about four people in that street heard the question and five had heard it as a question about a hill.

“**What happened to the nine bundles?**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a question about nine bundles of broom left on nine stones and five had heard a question asked kindly by a person who wanted to know, and nobody stopped it and the young man of about nineteen answered it out loud, in the open, in his own trade, and it was 54 words, and nobody thanked him.

“**Eight of them were gone on the ninth of Rainmonth and the ninth was on the stones with the twine cut off it in nine places and the stalk end split, and that is how you tell whether a man has cut a bundle or taken it, and nobody has ever left one whole.**”

Nobody said anything and about two hundred people in that market street had heard a young man say out loud how to tell whether a man has cut a bundle of broom or taken it and five had heard 54 words, and nobody asked him where the eight went and nobody asked him who had been up there, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first thing anybody in this county has ever been told about how nine households treat a boundary and five have said that it is the first thing anybody has ever been told about anything on that flat by anybody from this side of it.

Nobody said anything and that question went out of that market street with the afternoon and nobody wrote it down, and four people in nine counties have said since that a county which has worked out how nine households treat a boundary and cannot say where the boundary is has got the two halves of one thing and not the thing, and five have said that it has got the two halves and that the thing is on the other side of nine stones.

Then the last of the morning, and it is at the sea end of that street, and nobody asked him anything and he said nothing at all.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about thirty-two with a stick stood at the sea end of that market street with his hand on it for the whole morning and nobody looked at him and nobody asked him anything, and nobody in nine counties has asked him one word about the fourth hour of the fourth day of Rainmonth and nobody is going to, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a man of thirty-two has now had two things in about four and a half years that he has not said out loud and that both of them were on his own account and neither of them was about a road.

Nobody said anything and he worked a thing out at about the tenth hour of that morning, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the third time in about four and a half years a man of thirty-two has worked a thing out in that street and not said it, and five have said that the first was the first day of a year and the second was the twenty-second of Longlight, and that all three were on his own account and not one of them was about a road.

Nobody said anything and the thing he worked out was this. Nine households have cut the grass along nine hundred yards of a road for about nine years without knowing that the thing they were cutting was a road. Nobody in nine counties has asked them whether they knew. Nobody in nine counties knows whether anybody would have told them if they had been asked. And a man with no standing cannot put that in a market street on a Saturday morning, because putting it there would be putting it into about four hundred mouths, and about four hundred mouths is a thing, and a thing that nine households have been inside for about nine years without knowing it is not a thing anybody in nine counties has a name for.

Nobody said anything and asking it would be making a claim, and a man with no standing in this county who makes a claim about nine people who have never been asked anything about anything has made nine people into a body, and those nine households are not a body and have never been asked to be one and keep a rota about beasts between them and not a head and not a paper, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the third thing he has not asked, and that the first two were about four hundred people and the third is about nine households, and that all three of them were about a number.

Nobody thanked him and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the whole of what he has learned in about four and a half years and that it is the third thing he has not asked and four have said that a man who has asked nine hundred girders the same question twice is a man with a rule and five have said that a man who has not asked nine households anything at all is a man with a rule, and about nine people have said since that he does not know the difference either.

Then the two trades, and they were put in front of each other in the open, and they disagree, and nobody agreed with either of them.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract for about nineteen years came in at about the fourth hour with about nine feet of a new kerb standing against a shop wall and he said it out loud, in the open, to about four hundred people and to about nine of them, in his own trade, and it was 78 words, and he did not say it again that day.

“**About four and a half years of this county's business has produced about nine hundred yards of a road on a hilltop and a hearing that has made nothing in four and a half years, and I mend nine yards of wall in about a hundred and forty-three days because a wall has to be mended, and I have got no trade at all for nine hundred yards of a road and that is not a roadkeeper's job.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a roadkeeper price about four and a half years of this county in the open and five had heard 78 words, and nobody argued with him and nobody thanked him, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time anybody in this county has put a figure on what four and a half years produced and that the figure is a length of road and not a document.

Nobody said anything and the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up had a piece of blue wool over his forearm and he took it off before he answered, which nobody in that street has seen him do three times, and he said it out loud, in the open, in his own trade, and it was 81 words, and nobody stopped him.

“**About four and a half years of this county's business has produced about four hundred people who have heard nine things and nobody anybody could find about any of them, and I can find a person who has heard a thing in about nine seconds and I have never once found one who has not heard it, and a finding in four hundred mouths is worth four hundred shops and three clauses in nine hands with no copy are worth nothing.**”

Nobody said anything and about two hundred people in that market street had heard a cloth seller say out loud that about four hundred people who have heard a thing are worth something and five had heard 81 words, and the man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract said one word back and the word was nine, and the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up said one word back and the word was hedges, and nobody argued with either of them about it.

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had worked out inside about nine seconds that the two of them were arguing about about four hundred people and not about a road and about five had worked out that it was about both, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the second time in about two months that two trades in one market street have said out loud what the same people are worth and got two answers and that the first of the two was on the twenty-second of Longlight and about four people have said that this county now does this twice a month and five have said that this county has never done it twice at all.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about fifty-eight with a badge and no head had been standing at the high end of those boards for about as long as it takes to turn a cart and had said nothing at all, and then he said one thing out loud, in the open, to nine people, in his own trade, and it was 59 words, and he put his hand flat on the top rail and the rail had no white in it at that hour.

“**Three clauses in nine hands and no copy of them, and not one of them is about a road nobody has done a thing to, and I said that on the first of Harvestmonth in this street and I am not going to say it twice, and there are two trades in it today and I am the third.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a badge-man count himself as the third thing in a street and five had heard 59 words, and nobody thanked him and nobody agreed with him and four people in nine counties have said since that a man who has said the same true thing three times in two streets has learned the way this county works and five have said that he has learned the way this county works and that he does not like it.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel stood at the high end of those boards and said nothing at all that morning, and he has said nine things out loud in that street in about four and a half years and said the ninth of them on the thirteenth of Rainmonth and refused on that day to say anything twice, and about four people in that market street had worked out that the man who says the finding of things in this county is not going to say the tenth and about five had not.

Then the last of it, and it is not the shoulder and not the stones and not the broom.

Nobody thanked anybody and the lamp at the high end of those boards was lit at the fourth hour and stood lit until the market was gone and nobody in that street has ever turned it out early, and the cloth over the bench at the low end of them was on it and nothing lifted it and nobody asked what was under it, and about four hundred people went out of that market street into about nine trades and about nine people stayed at the boards.

Nobody said anything and about nine hundred yards of an old road is in about four hundred mouths and in nobody's hand, and four people in nine counties have said since that this county has no way of holding a finding once it is out of somebody's mouth and that it has had four and a half years to build one and has not built one, and five have said that a county does not hold a finding, a person does, and four have said that there are about four hundred people and five have said that about four hundred people is not a hand.

Nobody thanked anybody and nothing about any of it is in a document, and there is no page in any room in nine counties with the twenty-fifth of Harvestmonth on it, and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and nine people in nine counties have said since that about four and a half years of this county has now produced nine things said out loud and no way of holding any one of them, and about four people have said that the two trades this morning are the first two people in nine counties who have said out loud what that is worth and five have said that a thing said twice in a market is not a thing being held.

**And the cost of that Saturday is about nine hundred yards of an old road in about four hundred mouths and in nobody's hand, about four and a half years priced twice in one market street and got two answers, and one question about nine households that was not asked because asking it would be making a body of nine people, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
