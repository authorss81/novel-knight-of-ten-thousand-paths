# Chapter 0728

**Rainmonth the seventeenth, YR 322, a Wednesday and not a market morning with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and no market in that street, four days out at the head of that made road where three yards of a wall is down and a broom made off a hill is standing in a house.**

Nobody thanked anybody and the drove at the head of that made road went out on the Thursday before at about the sixth hour as it goes out every Thursday, and went up nine hundred yards of that lane through the gap at the low end of nine hundred yards of stone wall and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back down at about the fifth hour, and nobody went with it, and the bar went in on the lane side and it has been in since Mudmonth.

Nobody said anything and four people in nine counties have said since that this is about the fifteenth drove to go up that lane since the second of Mudmonth and about five have said that the beasts know the way and that this is the second year anybody has been able to check that they do, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a drove that goes up a lane and comes back down it is the only regular thing anybody in nine counties has ever had on that hill.

Nobody thanked him and six of the nine yards of wall at the head of that made road are back in and the gap is three yards long and four feet high, and the stone is quarried two miles off on the low side and one man has been carrying it two miles on his own for a hundred and five days, and four people in nine counties have said that a gap in a wall is shut the day it is filled and five have said that nobody in nine counties is going to be told the day it is.

Nobody said anything and the bar that man carries is four feet of ash with two stones on the end of it and it weighs sixty pounds with the stones, and he is forty-one and keeps a drove and a byre and nine hundred yards of wall and has been asked one question about a road in eleven years, and nobody asked him a second one on the seventeenth of Rainmonth and nine people in nine counties have said since that nobody has and that the reason given out loud on a made road in Mudmonth was that a first answer is the whole of what a man has got.

Nobody said anything and there are nine hundred yards of laid kerb on a far bank four days south of that market town with the water in a channel about nine hundred yards wide at the top of a tide, and about nine hundred yards of laid kerb on a shoulder two miles above that made road with the wind coming across it, and four people in nine counties have said since that nobody has ever put those two figures in one sentence and about five have said that the one on the far bank is about four hundred yards past a shut door and that the one on the shoulder is about two miles up a lane and that nobody in nine counties has walked either of them twice.

Nobody thanked him and a cart can get onto the kerb on that far bank and a cart cannot get within four hundred yards of the kerb on that shoulder, and four people in nine counties have said since that the difference between the two is about nine hundred yards of hill and five have said that the difference between the two is that one of them goes to a place and the other one does not.

Nobody said anything and the woman of about thirty-nine who keeps the second gate from the top of that made road came off that made road on foot on the seventeenth because she has not kept a horse in about nine years and stood at her own gate and did not shut it, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the second time since the nineteenth of Longlight that she has come back from anywhere and stood in her own gateway and five have said that she did not shut it and that a gate shut by its keeper and a gate left are two gates.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract for about nineteen years was on that made road on the seventeenth with about nine feet of a new kerb on a barrow that is not his, and four people in nine counties have said since that a kerb on a barrow that is not the roadkeeper's is a figure about four hundred yards long that nobody will ever be able to add up and five have said that a man who lays stone will not have his own barrow and that this is how anybody knows.

Nobody said anything and the man of about twenty-seven who keeps eels in weirs about two days north of that causeway was two days north of that made road on the seventeenth and not in it, and his weirs are in water and Rainmonth is not eel season and four people in nine counties have said since that a man whose trade loses nothing in a month has nothing to be in a market street about and five have said that he has nine days of not standing in a stream in the cold.

Then the broom, which is a thing that is used, and this is the half of it that the finding was not.

Nobody thanked him and there is a broom standing in the house of the man of about forty-one at the head of that made road, and it is about four feet of stalk on a handle of about three feet, and it came down two miles of unmade road on the eighth of Rainmonth off a hill about two miles above him, and he did not buy it and nobody has said that he bought it and nobody has said that he did not, and about nine people in nine counties know that he has one and about five have ever been in that house.

Nobody said anything and a man who keeps nine hundred yards of wall and a drove and a byre has a broom in his house and nobody in nine counties has ever asked him what for, and four people in nine counties have said since that a broom is the only thing in this county that is used and not asked about and five have said that a broom in a house by the fire is not a thing a man of forty-one puts there for himself.

Nobody thanked anybody and about nine people took a broom or a bee brush off a board at the low end of that market street on the thirteenth of Rainmonth and five did not, and nine people in nine counties have said since that about nine people in nine counties now own a thing off that shoulder and five have said that about nine is not a number anybody can do anything with.

Nobody said anything and a question about the broom in that house came up on a made road four days off on the seventeenth of Rainmonth and it was asked out loud, kindly, by a person who has not been in that house, and nobody stopped it.

“**Where did that broom come from?**”

Nobody said anything and about four people on that made road had heard a question asked about a broom in a house and five had heard a question asked about a thing in a man's house by somebody outside it, and nobody in that house answered it and nobody outside it waited, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time anybody in nine counties has asked about a thing in that man's house and that nobody asked him.

Nobody thanked him and then the hives, and the young man of about nineteen said out loud, in the open, in his own trade, to about four people in his own yard, and it was 57 words, and nobody stopped him.

“**I have nine frames of wood and about nine feet of wax and I make nine frames in about two days and I do not make ten, and I am not going to make more bees than the bees I have got, and the three frames I have are the three frames I am going to have.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that yard had heard a young man say out loud that he is not going to have more bees than he has got and five had heard 57 words, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the only figure anybody in this county has heard from a beekeeper this year that is about what he is going to do and five have said that a figure about what a person is not going to do is the hardest kind to be held to.

Nobody said anything and the brood in that yard is three frames where there were nine on the night before the eighth of Thawmonth, and it is a hundred and twenty-nine days since that night, and a brood frame comes back in about four weeks in a year like this one, and four people in nine counties have said since that three frames is a third of nine and five have said that three frames is going up and not down, which nobody in nine counties has checked.

Nobody thanked him and that yard is about four rods by six behind a stone wall about four feet high half a mile below that wall off a lane of their own that is not made, and the bees in it had been flying about four feet off the ground all day and going out and coming back with nothing, and four people in nine counties have said since that bees fly low when there is nothing out and that the east rise is nine hundred yards off and about two hundred yards above.

Then nobody came up that lane at all, and the reason is a rota and it is not a new one.

Nobody thanked anybody and nobody came up that lane on foot between the eighth of Rainmonth and the seventeenth, and the drove went up about nine hundred yards of it on the eleventh of Rainmonth and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back at about the fifth hour, and the nine households past those nine stones keep a rota of beasts that has been kept about nine years and has not been broken once in the nine years, and four people in nine counties have said since that the rota is the only reason anybody can give for anything about that flat and that it is a reason anybody with a beast could check in about nine seconds, and five have said that no third household came up and nobody asked it for a reason and that the reason would have been the same one.

Nobody said anything and a third household did not come up that lane and nobody in nine counties asked it anything, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that a household on that flat was not asked anything and that asking would have been a thing nobody could refuse, and about four people have said that asking a household at a boundary makes a boundary a gate and five have said that nobody in nine counties is going to find out.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about thirty-two with a stick came in at about the fourth hour on the seventeenth and stood about nine feet off that gap for about as long as it takes to turn a cart and did not help and did not ask and did not go up the lane, and a man of about forty-one came to the head of that gap and did not watch him and went back in, and nobody said anything to either of them and neither said anything.

Nobody said anything and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the second time in about four and a half years that two men have stood about nine feet off that gap at the same time and said nothing to each other, and five have said that the first time was in Mudmonth and that about sixteen words were said then about where to take hold of a bar and that nothing has been said since.

Nobody thanked anybody and then the ordinary half of a Wednesday four days off went on, and it is nine tenths of every day anybody in nine counties has ever had, and about nine hundred people along that made road did the ordinary part of it.

Nobody said anything and the two miles of wall and orchard between the eleventh mile stone and the top of that hill were in blossom and about nine hundred trees of them and the owners are about nine households and not one of them has ever asked anybody anything about anybody who walks past its gate, and the ordinary half of that Wednesday was that.

Nobody said anything and the fourth days of that made road are hard at the two ends and soft in the middle, and about nine hundred people live along it and every one of them knows which house the man of thirty-two comes out of, and four of them have offered him a bed since the middle of Frostmonth and about five have not, and nobody in nine counties has ever asked him why he walks it.

Nobody thanked him and in that market town there is no market on a Wednesday and about nine people were in that street and about four of them were at those boards with nothing on them and about five were not, and the room over that weigh-house was shut behind its own door and nobody went up the lane four hundred paces long that holds it.

Nobody said anything and the weigh-house on that Wednesday weighed one load of lime and nothing else, and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that a day with no market on it is a day on which about four hundred and nine sheets do not come past his floor, and that Rainmonth is about half of them, and that nobody has ever asked him what a sheet weighs.

Nobody thanked anybody and the inn at the high end put no board out on a Wednesday and had four beds empty for the third time since the middle of Harvestmonth, and a chandler nine doors down put a cloth over a bench rather than write anything at all on it, and the cloth over the bench at the low end of those boards was on it and nothing lifted it and nobody asked what was under it.

Nobody said anything and about nine hundred yards of an old road is now in about four hundred mouths and in nobody's hand, and four people in nine counties have said since that a thing in four hundred mouths is worth more than a thing in one hand because a hand can be lost in about nine days and five have said that a hand can be lost in about nine days and that four hundred mouths have never been known to lose one.

Nobody said anything and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel was in that market town on the seventeenth of Rainmonth and said nothing in that street for the fourth day running, and he has said nine things out loud in that street in about four and a half years and said the ninth of them on the thirteenth of Rainmonth and refused on that day to say anything twice, and four people in nine counties have said since that a man who has said nine things and has said nothing for four days is a man waiting to see whether anybody acts on the ninth and five have said that he is a man who has run out and about four people have said that both of those are the same thing.

Nobody thanked anybody and the hearing has not been called since the first of Goatmonth and cannot be called and does not sit, and its first of a month is fourteen days off and will come on a Wednesday, and about four people in nine counties stand at those boards at about the ninth hour on a first of a month and five do not, and nobody has sent for the man who built it on any of the seven firsts that have come and gone.

Nobody said anything and the lamp at the high end of those boards is lit at the fourth hour on every day of the week on which the light has gone off the wet slate of them, and has been for about four and a half years, and about nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the only thing in that market town that has happened every day for four and a half years without anybody having decided that it should, and about four people have said since that a lamp is a decision somebody made once and then forgot and about five have said that this is why nobody has ever taken it down.

Nobody thanked anybody and the light came up off the coping on that wall about nine hundred yards below and to the east, and the man of about forty-one was in his own place and was not on that shoulder and was not at the gate, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the drove went out on the Thursday and went up about nine hundred yards and stood in a field and came back and the man who keeps it did not go with it.

Nobody said anything and nothing about any of it is in a document, and there is no page in any room in nine counties with the seventeenth of Rainmonth on it, and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled, and four people in nine counties have said since that a room with ten questions and no answers under any of them is the only piece of furniture in nine counties that has not changed since the middle of Longlight the year before.

**And the cost of that Wednesday is about three yards of a wall still down and a hundred and five days of one man's account not asked about, a broom in a house nobody has asked the owner what for, and a third household on that flat not asked anything, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
