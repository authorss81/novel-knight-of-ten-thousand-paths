# Chapter 0744

**Wolfmonth the ninth, YR 322, a Wednesday and not a market morning with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and no market in that street, the ninth hour in that market street with nine people in it and four of them at the boards, and two men measuring one wall two ways.**

A Wednesday in that market town of about two thousand people is not a market morning, and nine people were in that street by the ninth hour of the ninth of Wolfmonth and four of them were at the boards with nothing on them and five were not, and the bolt on that room over the weigh-house went on from the Saturday and nobody went up the lane four hundred paces long that holds it.

Nobody thanked anybody and that light has been on the top of that shoulder at about the fourth hour of every morning since the twenty-sixth of Embermonth, and that is fourteen mornings, and four people have seen it once each and nobody else has seen it at all, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a thing four people have each seen once is not one thing four times and five have said that it is four things and that four people who have each seen a thing once at the same time have still not seen it between them.

Then four days off, and about nine hundred people along that made road did the ordinary part of a Wednesday, and the ordinary part is that nothing about a Wednesday is visible on a road.

Nobody said anything and a drove went up that lane four days off on the Thursday before, which was the third of Wolfmonth, at about the sixth hour, and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back down at about the fifth hour, and nobody went with it, and that is about thirty-one drove to go up that lane since the second of Mudmonth and about four hundred people in a market town have never watched one.

Eight of the nine yards of wall at the head of that made road are back in and the gap is about a yard long and about four feet high, and the stone is quarried two miles off on the low side and one man has been carrying it two miles in his own hands for two hundred and seventeen days, and the last yard is about nine feet of a course and he has not started it, and nobody in nine counties has asked him when he will and nobody is going to.

The brood in a walled yard half a mile below that wall is five frames where there were nine on the night of the eighth of Thawmonth, and that night is two hundred and forty-one days back, and the fifth frame came in on the twentieth of Embermonth and is not all brood yet, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a young man of about nineteen who has cut nine days of nine acres of broom and left nine hundred yards of it standing has done more to that hill in nine days than anybody four days off has done in about four and a half years and five have said that cutting broom is not doing anything to a hill.

The weigh-house on that Wednesday weighed one load of lime and nothing else, and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that Wolfmonth is nine days old and about ninety sheets have come past that floor in it, and that Frostmonth is the month they come past clean and that about nine of them every month come past with a corner torn off and that nobody has ever asked him what a sheet weighs.

Nobody thanked him and the inn at the high end had four beds empty that morning and put no board out, and the woman of about fifty-one who keeps that house came out into that street at about the fifth hour and stood about nine feet off those boards and did not come closer, and a chandler nine doors down put a cloth over a bench rather than write anything at all on it.

Nobody said anything and the near wheel of a cart came off at the low end of that street and the carter got it up again with a bar in about nine minutes and nobody stopped walking, and two women at the third door argued about a bolt of cloth for about four minutes and then went into the shop and came out with it wrapped, and about four people in nine counties have said since that those two women have been at that third door on about nine market mornings in four and a half years and that the fourth of those nine was a Tuesday with no market on it and five have said nothing about it.

The room over that weigh-house was shut behind its own door with the bolt on from the Saturday, and the copyist of about twenty-six did not come down the thirteen stairs and did not go up them, and about four people in that lane had worked out that she was not in and five had not worked it out and never will, and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled.

That room stands over a weigh-house and a weigh-house is open on the two mornings of a week the market is in that street and shut behind its own door on the other five, and the bolt goes on at noon on a Saturday and comes off at the seventh hour on a Thursday, and about four people in nine counties have said since that the five days a room in this county is shut are the five days nobody in nine counties has ever gone up a four hundred pace lane to see whether it is shut and five have said that thirteen stairs is the reason people are careful about that door and about four have said that it is a stair and not a door that they are careful about.

On that east rise the broom has been off for about nine days and about nine hundred yards of a line nine feet wide is left standing where it stands, and the nine hundred yards of that line is the same nine hundred yards four people have seen lit up at the fourth hour, and about four people in nine counties have worked that out inside about nine seconds and five have not, and nobody has said it out loud in that market street and nobody has said it out loud four days off either, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a young man of about nineteen who left nine hundred yards of a nine-foot line standing in the fourth week of Frostmonth did not know what he was standing in and that nobody in this county has told him and five have said that nobody is going to.

Then the part of that Wednesday that was said out loud, and it was three things, and two of them were about a wall and both of them were said by a man with a load on a cart.

Nobody thanked anybody and the first carter of about forty came into that street at about the sixth hour from four days off and said it out loud, in the open, to nine people, in his own trade, and it was 46 words, and nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him.

“**I came over that wall on the Monday with nine hundredweight of wool on a cart and the near end of it is nine feet and the far end of it is about knee high on a cart, and I have been both ways over it.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a carter describe about nine hundred yards of wall two different heights in one breath and five had heard 46 words, and nobody argued with him and nobody asked him which end was which, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man who has been both ways over a thing cannot be got to say which way he saw it the first time and five have said that a carter who is wrong about a wall gets his cart turned over and a carter who is right about a wall gets his cart over it, and that nobody in nine counties has worked out how a wall does that.

Nobody thanked him and the second carter of about thirty-one came into that street at about the seventh hour from four days off and said it out loud, in the open, to nine people, in his own trade, and it was 43 words, and nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him.

“**I came over that wall on the Monday with eleven sacks of wool on a cart and every yard of it is nine feet from end to end and I have laid my hand on the coping at the far end of it.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard two men say out loud on the same morning that about nine hundred yards of the same wall is two different heights and five had heard nothing, and nobody argued with either of them and nobody thanked either of them, and about four people in nine counties have said since that two men who have both been over a thing and disagree about it in front of nine people is the oldest thing in this county and five have said that it is the only thing anybody has ever done in public here with a disagreement and about four have said that a county which cannot call a meeting can still get two men in a street on a Wednesday.

Nobody said anything and then a question came into that street at about the ninth hour from a person at the fifth door who wanted to know one thing, and nobody stopped it, and it was asked kindly, and it was not about a road and not about a hill.

“**Which of you is right?**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a question asked about which of two men is right and five had heard it asked kindly, and nobody stopped it and the man of thirty-two with a stick answered it out loud, in the open, in his own trade, and it was 47 words, and nobody thanked him.

“**Both of you. I have put my hand on that coping at both ends of it and I am not going to say one of you is wrong, and a thing two men can measure two ways is not a thing anybody in this county can hold.**”

Nobody said anything and nobody said anything back to him and nobody thanked him, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man who has been refused twice out loud in this county has now stood in a market street and told two carters that both of them are right and five have said that this is the first time anybody in this county has said that out loud and that nobody has ever said it and about four have said that a man cannot hold a thing two people measure two ways and five have said that is why the thing is on the back of a board.

The man of about thirty-four with a satchel was at the high end of those boards on that Wednesday and said nothing at all in that street, and he has said nine things out loud in this county in about four and a half years and said the ninth of them on the thirteenth of Rainmonth, and nobody asked him and nobody sent for him, and four people in that market street had worked out that a hundred and sixteen days of not saying anything is a hundred and sixteen days and five had not.

At about the ninth hour of that Wednesday a man of about fifty-two stood at the low end of that street with his board under his arm and about nine hundred feet of kerb on a barrow behind him, and he did not put the board up and he did not turn it over and he did not say one word about it, and about four people in that market street had worked out that a man who drew nine hundred yards on the back of a board on Saturday and put it up on the step of that weigh-house has taken it down again and five had not, and nobody asked him about it and nobody sent for him and about four people in nine counties have said since that the first thing anybody in this county did with a map of anything was to take it out of the rain.

The two refusals in this county were given on the fourteenth and the twenty-sixth of Fallowmonth and neither has been argued with, and that is fifty-five days and forty-three days, and about four people in nine counties have said since that the second of those two was given by a man in an office four days off and that the first was given by a man on a shoulder above a drove road and that a third thing has now been said in that office four days off by two carters and nobody has refused anything and five have said that a thing said by two men who are not refusing anybody is not a refusal and is not a finding either.

Nobody thanked him and the sheet of paper with one line on it has been in that office for forty-three days, and the leaf that came up the made road in a cart on the Monday of the first of Wolfmonth has been in the same office for nine days, and nobody in nine counties has asked for either of them, and about four people in nine counties have said since that an office of nine clerks has now got three things in it and that the third one has been in it nine days and that nobody has opened it twice and five have said that a thing that is not opened twice is not a thing anybody has decided about.

The sea is four days south of that market town and about four hundred people live on a far bank across nine hundred yards of water at the top of a tide and about four hundred at the bottom, and a hand lamp goes on the coping at the low side of that causeway every Monday afternoon in the winter and the ninth of Wolfmonth was not a Monday and the lamp was not put there, and nobody in that market street has ever asked a person at a gate at the near end of that water anything at all.

Nobody thanked anybody and nothing about any of it is in a document, and there is no page in any room in nine counties with the ninth of Wolfmonth on it, and the register in the room over that weigh-house holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled, and about four people in nine counties have said since that two men have now measured one wall two ways in a market street and that nothing in this county has a place to put that and five have said that the place is the back of a board and that the board is not a room.
