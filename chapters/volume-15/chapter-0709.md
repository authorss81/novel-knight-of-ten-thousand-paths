# Chapter 0709

**Mudmonth the first, YR 322, a Tuesday and not a market morning and the first of a month, with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and the boards standing in an empty market street, about four people in it, and Mudmonth.**

A first of a month in this county comes whether or not anybody is in the street for it, and the first of Mudmonth came on a Tuesday, and Mudmonth is the worst month for roads in this county and there is no market on a Tuesday, and this is the third of the twelves since the first of Goatmonth on which nothing at all has happened at it.

Nobody thanked anybody and about four people came to those boards at about the ninth hour and about five did not, and the badge-man was not there, and nobody sent for him, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a man who has been absent from two firsts of a month running has now made a habit of it and that this is the first thing in about four and a half years that anybody in nine counties has watched a person do on purpose.

Nobody thanked him and the boards were standing in that market street with nothing on them and they had been there since the middle of Longlight, and the rail along the top of them had frost in it at about the seventh hour because the cold had come back up out of the ground over the weekend, and about nine people in that market street have said since that Mudmonth and Thawmonth are the same cold and that the difference between them is what is under it.

Then the fact that the whole of this month turns on, which is not in that street and is not going to be.

Nobody thanked anybody and the water came up in the lane at the head of that made road on the twenty-fourth of Thawmonth and it has been under since, which is seven days, and about nine people in nine counties know that the lane goes under about twice a year and about five have ever seen it do it, and about four people in that market street on the first of Mudmonth said out loud that they had not known that either.

And the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up asked the boards, out loud, in the open, at about the tenth hour, and nobody stopped him.

“**Then when?**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a man ask one word and five had heard a man ask the second question anybody in nine counties has asked since the first of Longlight.

Nobody thanked him and the shoulder is above the lane and the shoulder does not go under, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the whole of what a young man of about nineteen found on the twelfth of Thawmonth and that nine days later it is the whole of what nobody in nine counties can do about it, and that both of those sentences are about a lane and about nothing else and that this is the arrangement working and is not a compromise.

Nobody said anything and nine people in nine counties have said since that the finding of the last week has turned out to be a thing four hundred people can carry and not a thing anybody can act on, and that this is the first time in about four and a half years that this county has found out something about itself and been unable to do one single thing with it, and four of them have said that the last time was the second of Hearthmonth and five have said that the last time was in Longlight and that nobody has ever worked out which.

Nobody thanked him and then the ordinary half of the first of Mudmonth happened, and it is nine tenths of every day anybody in nine counties has ever had, and it began about nine days ago and has not stopped.

Nobody thanked anybody and there was mud on every road in that market town by the sixth hour and there had been mud on them every morning since the twenty-fourth of Thawmonth, and a made road in Mudmonth carries about a quarter of what it carries in Frostmonth and about a fifth of what it carries in Harvestmonth, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the figure of nine hundred people living along four days of made road is not a figure at all in this month and that about four hundred of them have not been out of their own front doors since the water came up.

Nobody thanked anyone and the mud was not the mud of a wet winter, which is a thing that goes into the ground and stays there, and this was the mud of a thaw, which is a thing that comes up out of the ground in about nine days and goes away in about nine more, and four people in nine counties have said since that a road in the first week of Mudmonth is worse than a road in the worst of Frostmonth and about five have said that this is because the ground under a road in Frostmonth is frozen and a road on frozen ground is a road that is standing on itself.

Nobody thanked him and the man of thirty-two went out on that made road at about the sixth hour every morning and walked the four days, and it took him about nine hours longer in the last week of Thawmonth than it had at the middle of Harvestmonth, and about nine people in nine counties have said since that he has walked that road in Mudmonth before and has never once walked it in the first week, and about four have said that he should have and about five have said that nobody who goes out every morning goes out the same way in every month, which is the whole of what about four and a half years of it has been for.

Nobody thanked anyone and on the first of Mudmonth he got to the ninth mile stone at about the eleventh hour and the stone had about four inches of mud round the foot of it, and he cleaned it, because he has cleaned it about nine hundred times since the middle of Harvestmonth and about nine people in nine counties know that he cleans it and about five have ever watched him and about nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the only thing in nine counties that is done for nothing on a road by anybody who is not paid to do it.

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market town had heard a wain driver of about forty-four say on the Friday that his wain had been in the low side of a made road up to the axle at about the fourth hour and that he had got it out again by about the ninth and that this is the third time in nine years and five had heard him say that the fourth time will be the one that finishes the road.

Nobody thanked anybody and the weigh-house weighed a load of lime and nothing else, and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that a cart that has been in the low side of a road carries about nine times its own weight of road out of the road in about four loads, and that nobody in nine counties has ever asked him for that figure and that about four hundred and nine parish rolls a month come past his floor in a good month and that in Mudmonth they come past it wet.

Nobody thanked anyone and the lamp at the high end of the boards was lit at the fourth hour and stood lit until the daylight went, and nine people in that market town have said since that the lamp is the only thing in nine counties that has happened on every day for about four and a half years without anybody having decided that it should.

Then the one decision, and it was not the protagonist's, and it was made in a trade and not in a street.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract for about nineteen years came down that market street on the Monday and told about nine people in four trades, out loud and in the open, that he is not going up the lane before Hearthmonth and that he will say the reason again in his own trade whenever anybody asks him and that he has said it once already in that street on the nineteenth of Thawmonth.

Nobody said anything and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first date anybody in nine counties has given for anything since the first of Longlight, and that it was given by a man who keeps four miles of made road and not by a man who goes out on it every morning, and that about four of them have said that a date from a contractor is worth more than a date from a person with no contract, and five have said that a date is a date.

Nobody thanked him and the cloth seller three doors up was asked whether the ninth of that morning was going to be in Hearthmonth and he said that about nine people in nine counties have known that since the middle of Thawmonth and that about five of them have said that Hearthmonth is eleven weeks away, and nine people in that market street said the same word at the same moment, and four people in nine counties have said since that the word was somebody else's and five have said that nobody in nine counties has ever been able to establish whose it was.

Nobody said anything and nine people in nine counties have said since that the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up still has nine names in his head from the middle of Fallowmonth and has still not written them down, and that about four people in that market street asked him for the list in the middle of Frostmonth and about five did not, and that he said the same thing on the first of Mudmonth that he said in Fallowmonth, which is that he is not going to write nine men down in a book and hand it to anybody.

Nobody thanked him and the nine names are not a list and there is no book and there is no head at the top of it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the second time in about four and a half years that a person has refused in a market street to write down nine names and that the first time was the badge-man refusing to count nine gates, and that the two refusals have never been put together in the same street by anybody.

Nobody thanked anybody and then the people who are not in that street are four and they are named here, because a first of a month that only lists what was present is not a first of a month.

The woman of about thirty-nine who keeps the second gate from the top of the made road is at her gate, and her gate is open, and she is not in that street and nobody sent for her and nobody sent anybody to her.

The man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles under contract has water in the low side of his four miles and is not in that street and has given a date.

The young man of about nineteen who keeps bees is four days off this market town at the head of that made road and half a mile below the wall and is not in that street and nobody sent for him and nobody knows he said a date to himself on a hill at about the second hour of a Friday.

And the city four hundred miles off has not been asked a question by anybody in nine counties and is not in that street and is not being sent anybody.

Nobody thanked anybody and nothing at the top of it, and the reason there is nothing at the top of it is not that nobody wanted to put something there, and four people in nine counties have said that it is that nobody in nine counties could call the next meeting, and five have said that the thing that wanted a meeting was a man with a warrant and that he has stopped and that a thing which has stopped does not send for anybody, and that this is now the fourth first of a month on which that has been the whole of the answer.

Nobody thanked him and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them, and the fourth ruled head over a place where a finding goes is empty and has been empty for about thirteen months, and no fifth head is ruled, and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and the three series stand and are not derived from one another, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the fourth and a half year this county has spent not finding out, and that about four of them have said that this is a record and five have said that a record is a thing anybody can look at.

Nobody thanked anyone and about nine people in nine counties have said since that the fourth ruled head has been empty for about thirteen months and that the copyist of about twenty-six has said out loud, in three months and in two rooms, that she is not going to rule a fifth head and that neither is anybody else, and that nobody has ever asked her what the fourth head is for, and about four of them have said that this is because the answer is in the room and about five have said that this is because nobody in nine counties wants to hear it.

Nobody thanked him and the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps that room was not in her lane and not at the top of the stairs and nobody sent for her, and about four people in that market town had noticed the bolt still on the door since the Saturday and about five had not, and the room was shut behind its own door on a Tuesday because a Tuesday is not a market morning and has not been a market morning in nine counties for about four and a half years.

Nobody thanked him and nobody wrote anything down on the first of Mudmonth, and four people in that market street had each heard a piece of the last week and put the four pieces nowhere, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the whole of what happened between the nineteenth of Thawmonth and the first of Mudmonth and that about four hundred people heard nine stones described on a Thursday and about five of them believe it.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about thirty-eight who puts boards on the inside of gates was in that street at about the sixth hour on the first of Mudmonth and said one thing out loud, to four people in and five out, about a lane two days off that is under about nine inches of water from end to end and has been since the twenty-fourth of Thawmonth, and the thing he said was that a gate at the end of a lane that is under is not shut and is not open and is the third thing, and that nobody has a name for the third thing, and that about nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that anybody has said out loud that there is a third state for a road, and that it is not the badge-man's doing and the badge-man was not there.

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a man put a third state on a road and five had heard a man describe a puddle, and about nine people in nine counties have said since that both of those readings are correct and that this county has been standing on a puddle for about four and a half years and has been calling it something else.

Nobody thanked anybody and the room over that weigh-house was still bolted at about the seventh hour and the boards stood in an empty market street with nothing on them, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the first of Mudmonth YR 322 is the first first of a month in this county on which the whole of what was decided was a date eleven weeks away, and that this is a decision, and that nobody in nine counties has any standing to say so.

**And the cost of the first of Mudmonth is seven days of water in a lane and about four hundred people who have not been out of their own front doors since the twenty-fourth of Thawmonth, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
