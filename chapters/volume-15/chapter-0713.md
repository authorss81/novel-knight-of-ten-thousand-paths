# Chapter 0713

**Longlight the first, YR 322, a Saturday and a market morning and the first of a month, the second hour in the market street at the boards with about four hundred people in it and about two hundred of them stopped, the room over the weigh-house open, and a shoulder that has been firm for forty-two days.**

The first of a month in this county comes whether or not anybody is in the street for it, and the first of Longlight came on a Saturday, and a Saturday is the loudest morning of the week in nine counties, and about four hundred people were in that market street by the second hour and about two hundred of them had stopped what they were doing, and not one of them was at the boards.

Nobody thanked anybody and four people in that market street had worked out that the first of a month had arrived in the middle of the loudest morning of the week and five had not, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first first of a month in about four and a half years that arrived with four hundred people standing about nine feet off it, and that nobody has ever worked out what a first of a month is for.

Nobody said anything and the boards were standing at the low end with nothing on them and they had been repainted twice since the middle of Longlight the year before and are the colour of a wet slate, and nobody took them down, and nine people in that street could have told you which of the two coats was on them last and five could not.

Nobody thanked anybody and the lamp at the high end of those boards is lit at the fourth hour on every day of the year including the days with no market in the street, and the light in that street goes off the west bank first and off the boards about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over, and four people in nine counties have said since that the boards are repainted in the one month of the year when nobody in this county can see them and five have said that the second coat went on in Harvestmonth.

Nobody thanked anybody and Longlight is the month a boundary oath-stone is renewed in this world, and about nine people in nine counties know that, and it is not relevant, and it was said out loud in that market street on the morning of the first of Longlight by a man of about fifty-eight with a badge and no head, in the open, to about nine people, and he said it in eight words.

“**Longlight is the month the oath-stone is renewed.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a badge-man put a fact about a month into a market street on a Saturday and five had heard eight words, and about four people in nine counties have said since that he has not said a thing out loud in that street since the twenty-eighth of Goatmonth and that this was the shortest of them.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up said the other half of it out loud, in the open, at the low end of those boards, at about the third hour, and nobody stopped him, and it was twenty-four words and it was in his trade.

“**There is no oath-stone in this county and there is no boundary on that made road to renew, and a month is a month.**”

Nobody said anything and four hundred people in that market street had heard a cloth seller answer a badge-man about a stone and five had heard a cloth seller say the true thing about his own trade, and nobody argued with him and nobody agreed with him and nine people in nine counties have said since that about nine people knew the fact and that this was the first time anybody had put it in that street and that the answer was that it did not matter.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of thirty-two stood at the sea end of that street with his stick in his right hand for the whole of it and said nothing at all and was not asked anything at all, and nobody looked at him, and four people in that market street had worked out inside about nine seconds that the sixth man going up a lane had come in off the made road the night before and five had worked out nothing.

Then the shoulder, which is the gift, and it is said in a trade and not in a street.

Nobody thanked anybody and the shoulder is above the lane at the head of that made road and about two miles above the wall, and it is about nine hundred yards wide at the top, and the ground on it came up on the nineteenth of Mudmonth, which is forty-two days ago, and about nine people in nine counties know that and five have ever been on it.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract for about nineteen years came in at about the fourth hour and said his reason out loud, in the open, in that market street, to about nine people, in his own trade, and it was thirty words.

“**Four miles of made road come off that shoulder and I have had them under contract about nineteen years and I have never once been on the top of it.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a man who keeps a road say that he is going up a hill to look at the top of a road he has kept for nineteen years, and five had heard thirty words, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that the man who said out loud on the nineteenth of Thawmonth that he would not go up that lane before Hearthmonth has said out loud that he is going.

Nobody thanked him and he had said the other half of it in that street on the nineteenth of Thawmonth in his own trade, which was that four miles of made road under his hand was coming up out of about nine days of frost with water in the low side of it that was not going to be gone before Mudmonth, and that he had about nine men on that four miles and about nine he could not spare, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the water in the low side of that road went out of it on about the twenty-eighth of Mudmonth and that this is the whole of the reason and that nobody in nine counties has written it down.

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had worked out that a roadkeeper's reason is a date and about five had not, and the date was the twenty-eighth of Mudmonth, and about nine people in nine counties have said since that this county has now had two dates given to it in five weeks by two men in trades and that neither of them gave the second one to anybody.

Then the other four, and they were not a list, and nobody made one.

Nobody thanked anybody and a man of about thirty-eight who puts boards on the inside of gates said his reason out loud, in the open, at the low end of those boards, in his own trade, and it was fifty words, and nobody stopped him.

“**Every board I put on the inside of a gate goes on a gate somebody can stand in front of, and I have said out loud that a gate at the end of a lane under water is a third thing and nobody has asked me what it is called.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a man who puts boards on gates say that he wanted to see a gate nobody can stand in front of, and five had heard fifty words, and nobody asked him what the third thing is called and nine people in nine counties have said since that he has been waiting about a month to be asked and that the shoulder was firm on the nineteenth of Mudmonth and the lane came out on the twenty-eighth and that nobody in nine counties thought to go and ask him.

Nobody thanked him and a man of about twenty-seven who keeps eels in weirs about two days north of that causeway said his reason out loud, in the open, in that market street, in his own trade, and it was forty-four words, and nobody stopped him.

“**There is about nine hundred yards of kerb on that far bank and then grass, and I keep eels in weirs two days north of it and I have never once been across on foot, and there is nobody I could ask who has.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a man who keeps eels say that he wants to stand on a far bank he has never stood on and five had heard forty-four words, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the man who keeps that gate has crossed that causeway on foot about nine hundred times in about eleven years and that the man who keeps eels two days north of it has not crossed it at all, and that neither of those two facts has ever been put in the same sentence.

Nobody thanked anybody and a woman of about thirty-nine who keeps the second gate from the top of that made road said her reason out loud, in the open, in that market street, in her own trade, and nobody put a number on it afterwards and four people in that street have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that she has said a reason for anything instead of a no.

Nobody thanked her and she said that she has kept a gate for about nine years and that the lane above the top gate has been shut for about eleven and that she has said no four times in a market street this month to four things that were not about the lane, and that she would like one time to be asked a question about the lane by somebody who keeps something, and nine people in nine counties have said since that she has not been asked one and that she came four miles on foot to say it in a street where nobody asked her.

Nobody thanked him and a man of about thirty-four with a satchel said his reason out loud, in the open, at the high end of those boards, in his own trade, and nobody stopped him, and it was that he is the man in that market town who says the finding of things out loud and has said four of them since the twelfth of Thawmonth and that nobody has checked one of them and that he is going to see whether the ninth hundred yards of kerb is where a young man said it was.

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a man admit in the open that he is the mouth of the finding and five had heard a man say he is going to check his own work, and about nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the only one of the five whose reason is about the four things and that about four people have said that it is the reason that is not a reason and about five have said that it is the reason that took the longest.

Then what the party is, and it is said once, out loud, in the open, in a market street, by a person in his own trade.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about fifty-eight with a badge and no head had been standing at the high end of those boards for about as long as it takes to turn a cart and had not said anything at all, and then he said it out loud, in the open, to about four hundred people and to about nine of them, in his own trade, and it was fifty-six words, and he did not say it again.

“**There are five people going up a lane in this county on their own business and there is no head of it and there is no paper of it, and I have been saying since the first of Goatmonth that sending one of them would make a head, and nobody in this street has sent anybody.**”

Nobody said anything and then somebody in that street asked him out loud, kindly, at about the ninth hour, whether one of the five would be asked to come back down and say what was up there, and he said that no, and that he had not sent anybody and would not, and that if anybody in that street wanted to ask one of the five then that was his own account and not this county's business.

“**Will one of them be asked to come back down?**”

Nobody said anything and four hundred people in that market street had heard the same question asked twice on the same morning by two people in two trades and five had heard it, and nobody asked it a third time and nobody stopped either of them asking it.

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had counted five and five had counted six, and the sixth was a man of thirty-two with a stick who stands at the sea end of that street on about nine market mornings since the first of Embermonth and who had come in off that made road the night before and who is going up that lane on his own account and not as part of anything.

Nobody said anything and nobody said so out loud, and the badge-man did not say it again, and about nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that two sets of people standing in one street have counted one thing and got two answers and that about four of them have said that the man of thirty-two is not one of the five and about five have said that he is the sixth and that neither of those two sets has ever spoken to the other.

Nobody thanked anybody and there is no paper of it and there is no list and there is no head of it, and about nine people in nine counties have said since that the first time in about four and a half years that a person in this county has refused to write down nine names in a book and hand it to anybody was on the nineteenth of Thawmonth, and that it was the badge-man refusing to count nine gates.

Nobody said anything and nobody thanked him and the broom on that shoulder is about nine acres on the east rise and it is on about the fourth of Rainmonth every year, and nobody has gone up for it, and nine people in nine counties know that it is the only broom within a day's walk of that yard and five have ever cut it, and four people in nine counties have said since that a young man of about nineteen who went up there on the twelfth of Thawmonth for the broom came down with nothing and has said he will not go up again until the fourth of Rainmonth.

Nobody thanked anybody and the broom will be on about the fourth of Rainmonth, which is thirty-three days off, and the five are going up on the fifth of Longlight, which is four days off, and about nine people in nine counties have said since that no person in nine counties has ever gone up a hill for one thing and come back with another and five have said that this is not going to be one of them.

Nobody thanked anybody and the inn at the high end put a board out with the price of a bed on it in chalk and the woman of about fifty-one who keeps it had four beds full and had had them full since the middle of Harvestmonth, and four people in that street had heard her say that a man who walks four days of made road in Mudmonth and comes in at the eighth hour wants a floor and not a bed and five had not thought about it.

Nobody said anything and then the market went on and the middle of that morning happened the way the middle of every morning in that street happens, and two hundred people went back to nine trades, and the weigh-house weighed two loads of lime and nothing else, and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that about four hundred and nine parish rolls come past his floor in a good month and that a roll of hands is not a sheet.

Nobody thanked anybody and the four hundred people in that street have heard four things said out loud since the twelfth of Thawmonth and have heard five reasons this morning, and four of them believe the four things and five believe the reasons, and nobody asked the man with a satchel a single question, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is now the second time in about four and a half years that about four hundred people in a market street have heard a thing and not acted on it, and that the first was the wall on that made road.

Nobody said anything and the lamp at the high end of the boards was lit at the fourth hour and stood lit until the market was gone, and the cloth over the bench at the low end of them was on it and nothing lifted it and nobody asked what was under it.

Nobody thanked anybody and the broom is on about the fourth of Rainmonth and the shoulder has been firm for forty-two days and the five are going up on the fifth of Longlight, and nine people in nine counties have said since that there is nothing in this county that will make five people turn up at the top of a lane on the fifth of a month except five reasons in five trades, and about four people in nine counties have said that this is the first time in about four and a half years that this county has been able to say what a thing is for.

Nobody said anything and the man of about fifty-eight with a badge and no head went down the lane from the weigh-house at a slow walk with the bar against his leg and the strap of it over his shoulder and did not hurry, and nine people in nine counties have said since that he has never once walked into that market street fast and that the only thing that had changed about him on the first of Longlight was that he had said something.

**And the cost of that Saturday is forty-two days of a shoulder that nine counties could walk on and did not, five reasons said out loud in a market street that nobody wrote down, and about seven days last month when a man could have stood up there and not found out, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
