# Chapter 0702

**Goatmonth the twenty-eighth, YR 321, a Thursday and a market morning, the second hour in the market street at the boards with about four hundred people in it and about two hundred of them stopped, the room over the weigh-house open for the fourth day running, and one question that was not in the county on Tuesday.**

A Thursday is a market morning and the boards were standing by the seventh hour and about four hundred people were in that market street by the second hour and about two hundred of them stopped what they were doing, which is the proportion on every market morning since the first of Embermonth and has not moved since the first of Embermonth.

The cold that morning came off the land and not off any water, and it had been in the ground since the middle of the night, and the ruts at the low end of that street had a skin of white in them that went to powder under a heel. Two men were unloading boards off a cart and breathing out of their mouths and not talking, because talking at that hour on a Thursday costs a man about nine seconds of work he does not get back.

Nobody thanked anybody and that market street is about nine hundred paces from the gate at the low end of it to the inn at the high end, and it runs east and west, and the bank to the west of that town is higher than the street, which is the only thing anybody has ever been able to say about the light in it, and that the light goes out of that street before the light goes out of the sky.

Nobody thanked him and about four hundred people were in it by the second hour and about two hundred of them stopped what they were doing, and the two hundred are not the same two hundred every Thursday, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a market street is the only place in this county where the same four hundred people meet twice a week and have never once arranged it.

Nobody thanked anyone and the boards were standing at the low end and they have been repainted twice since the middle of Longlight and are the colour of a wet slate, and about nine people in that street could have told you which of the two coats was on them last, and about five could not, and the cloth seller says that the second coat went on in Harvestmonth and that a coat of paint on a board is the only thing in this town that anybody has voted on and nobody can remember the vote.

Nobody thanked anybody and the room over that weigh-house was open and the copyist of about twenty-six came down the thirteen stairs at the seventh hour and stood in the lane while the room let the cold off it, and she went back up and shut the door and came down a second time with a leaf in her hand and nothing on it, and about four people in that lane saw her come down twice and about five saw her come down once.

Then the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up asked the question, and he asked it out loud, in the open, at the low end of the boards, and nobody stopped him.

He had a piece of blue wool over his forearm and he took it off before he asked, which nobody in that street has ever seen him do, and then he stood with his hands behind him and waited, and the waiting took longer than the asking did.

“**Then what is on the far side of the water?**”

Nobody said anything and about two hundred people in that market street had heard a man ask about a thing nine hundred yards off, and four of them had understood that he meant the coast and about five had understood that he meant nothing in particular and had asked it because it was in his head.

Nobody thanked him and the answer came in about as long as it takes to turn a cart, which is to say it did not, and the silence after the question was about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over.

Nobody said anything and nine people in that market street had worked out within about nine seconds that nobody in nine counties knows what is on the far side of that water, and five of them had worked out that the person standing four feet away from the man who asked it had been on it on Tuesday morning and had not said so.

Nobody thanked anybody and then the man of about fifty-eight with a badge and no head came down the lane from the weigh-house at a slow walk with the bar against his leg and the strap of it over his shoulder, and he did not hurry, and about four people in that street have said since that he came down that lane in about nine minutes that everybody in it could see, and five have said that he has never once walked into that market street fast.

Nobody thanked him and he came to the high end of the boards and stopped four feet short of them and put his hand flat on the top rail, and the rail has frost in it from the middle of the night, and his hand stayed there, and about nine people in that market street have said since that the badge-man has not put his hand on those boards in about eleven years and that a man who touches a thing he is about to speak at is about to speak carefully.

Nobody thanked him and he looked at the sea end of that street for as long as it takes to turn a cart, which is the end the water is at when there is water there and is not there when there is not, and about four people in that street understood what he was looking at and five understood that he was looking at the end of a street.

And he said the true thing about it, out loud, in the open, to about four hundred people and to about nine of them, and it was fifty words, and he did not say it again that day.

“**Nobody in nine counties has been across that water and come back on foot in about eleven years except the man who keeps that gate, and I am not going to send anybody, and the reason I am not going to send anybody is that sending is making a head.**”

Nobody thanked him and four people in that market street had heard the whole of what nine counties has said in fifty words and five had heard a man refuse to do a thing.

Nobody thanked him and he said the half of it that about four people had not understood, and he said it because he had thought about it going up the lane, and about nine people in that market street heard both halves and about five heard the second half first.

“**And I will say why the second half is the one that matters. A man who goes is not a head. A man who sends is a head. There has been one man crossing that causeway on foot in about eleven years and he is not here and he is not going to be asked about it, and if anybody in this street wants to go then that is his own account and not this county's business.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a man of fifty-eight take the arrangement off a thing and put it on a person, and five had heard a man say a thing that sounds like permission and is not.

Nobody thanked him and nine people in nine counties have said since that the twenty-eighth of Goatmonth was the first time in about four and a half years that the badge-man had said in a market street that a thing done by somebody on their own account is not the county's business, and that about four people in nine counties have said that he said it because the first of Goatmonth came and went and nothing happened and a man of fifty-eight does not enjoy a month that does nothing.

Nobody thanked anybody and the woman of about thirty-nine who keeps the second gate from the top of this made road came in on foot, and it is four miles, and she came in on foot because she has not kept a horse in about nine years and has not wanted one, and she has said in two market streets since the first of Hearthmonth that a gate keeper who keeps a horse is a gate keeper who can be asked to go somewhere.

Nobody thanked her and she stood at the high end of the boards through the whole of the question and said nothing while it was being asked and while it was not being answered, and about nine people in that market street have said since that she had the answer before the question was finished, and about four of them have said that she had it before the man who asked it stood there, and five have said that a person who lets a question stand empty in the open is doing the thing this county does on its best mornings.

Nobody thanked her and her gate is the second one from the top of that made road and it has been shut four times since the second of Hearthmonth, and she is the woman who said no before anybody asked her, and nine people in nine counties have said since that she came in on the Thursday because the first of a month had been and gone and nothing had happened at it, and about four of them have said that she came in because she had a gate to keep and the keeping of it was going to be asked about and five have said that she came in because she had four noes of her own and no market to put them in.

Then she said the part of it that was about the coast, and she said it standing up, which she has now done four times since the second of Hearthmonth.

“**The coast said no to being visited and the coast said it in writing on the eleventh of Hearthmonth and nine houses wrote it and two signed and seven put a thumb on it, and I have read it and I am not going to pretend I have not.**”

Nobody thanked her and four people in that market street had heard a gate keeper put a leaf on a market street and five had heard a woman name a date.

“**And I will say the thing about it that is my own and not the houses', because I keep a gate and not a road. A house that writes no down has said it once and it stands. A gate that is shut has to be shut again by somebody, and it is shut by me, and I have said no four times this month and the fifth time will be the same as the first. That is the whole difference between a leaf and a gate and nobody in this street has ever asked me for it.**”

Nobody said anything and nine people in that market street had heard a woman say that a thing written down says itself and a thing shut has to be shut again every morning by a person, and four of them had heard that she is asking for the leaf to be read out again and five had not.

Nobody thanked her and the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up was asked by somebody in that street whether the coast had said no to him personally, and he said that it had not and that it had not needed to, and nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him.

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a man give up the only ground he had in nine seconds and five had heard a man be accurate.

Then the middle of that morning happened the way the middle of every morning in that street happens, and there is no account of it in any book and there could not be, because it is about nine trades and about nine hundred people and nobody watching.

The near wheel of a cart came off at the low end and the carter got it up again with a bar in about nine minutes and nobody stopped walking. A boy of about eleven came down the lane from the weigh-house with a pail and went back up it with the pail full, and nobody said anything to him at either end of it. Two women at the third door argued about a bolt of cloth for about four minutes and then went into the shop and came out with it wrapped, which is how that argument ends nine times in ten. The frost came off the ruts by the ninth hour and the light came up the side of the weigh-house and found the upper edge of the boards, and the boards were the colour of a wet slate and had been since the middle of Harvestmonth. Nobody thanked anybody. The inn at the high end put a board out with the price of a bed on it in chalk, which it does on Fridays and Thursdays, and a chandler nine doors down would put a cloth over a bench rather than write anything at all on it, and nine people in nine counties have said that the whole of the difference between those two trades is whether a person can be found afterwards.

Then the ordinary half of that Thursday happened, and about two hundred people went back to about nine things, and about nine stayed at the boards.

Nobody thanked anybody and the weigh-house weighed two loads of lime and a load of wool, and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that the parish rolls came past his floor at the rate of about four hundred and nine a month in a good month, and that nobody had ever asked him to make a mark on one of them, and that on Tuesday morning there had been no water in this county's business at all, which was true, and nine people in nine counties have said since that it was the first true thing anybody said in that street all morning that had nothing to do with anything.

Nobody thanked anyone and the cloth seller three doors up did not go back to his cloth for about nine minutes after he had asked the question, and then he did, and about four people in that market street have said since that a man who asks a thing in the open and then stands still for nine minutes is a man who has found out that nobody will answer it, and five have said that he found that out before he asked it, and that asking it in the open was the price of the finding out.

Nobody thanked anyone and the inn at the high end had four beds full and had had them full since the middle of Harvestmonth, and the woman of about fifty-one who keeps that house came out into that street at about the fifth hour for the first time on a Thursday since Frostmonth and stood about nine feet off the boards and did not come closer, and nobody said anything to her and she said nothing and nine people in nine counties have said since that a woman who keeps four beds came out into that street on a Thursday morning and stood nine feet off is the whole of her part in it.

Nobody thanked anybody and about nine people in that market street went on doing the ordinary half of a Thursday, and the ordinary half is nine tenths of every day anybody in nine counties has ever had, and two hundred people went back to about nine trades and about nine of those trades are the same trades they were at the sixth hour.

Nobody thanked anybody and a Crown party's cart went past on the made road above that street at about the fifth hour with about nine feet of paper under a tarpaulin, and it was going south and it was not stopping, and about four people in that market town had counted the paper and five had not, and nobody in that street asked the driver anything.

Nobody thanked him and nobody has been able to establish who was in that cart, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the clerk on the near wheel was a man of about thirty-one in a livery and had been in this county in the middle of Hearthmonth and had not been in it since, and that a man who asks a question at a gate goes away and comes back on another road.

Nobody thanked anybody and then the thing that is not in that market street is worth putting down in order, because about nine people in nine counties have said since that a county cannot see the shape of a thing that is not there.

There was no paper on those boards. There was no list. The list of thirty counties in order is in the hired room over a saddler's in this market town under a cloth on the same table as nine hundred words that nobody has withdrawn, and about nine of the thirty are struck through with a single line each and about twenty-one are still on it with nothing against them, and nine of those twenty-one have not been read since the eighth of Embermonth.

Nobody thanked anybody and nine people in nine counties have said that a question asked in a market street on a Thursday is in the county for good, and five have said that is exactly what makes a market street dangerous, and that a market street is the only place in nine counties where about four hundred people hear the same sentence on the same morning.

Nobody thanked anybody and about four people in that market town knew that the man of thirty-two was standing at the sea end of that street with his stick in his right hand and salt on the front of his coat, and about five had seen him walk in and about nine had not noticed him at all, and nobody asked him a single question all morning and he said nothing at all.

Nobody thanked him and he had come in at the fourth hour off the made road and had not come from the sea end of that street, and four people in that market town have said since that a man who walks in at the fourth hour from the made road and stands at the sea end with his hands empty is a man who has come a long way and is standing where the thing he has come from would be if it were at the end of a street, and five have said that a man of thirty-two has stood at that end of that street on about nine market mornings since the first of Embermonth and that salt on a coat is not why.

Nobody thanked him and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the second time in about four and a half years that a fact has been in a market street and not been asked about in a market street, and that the first time was the twenty-fifth of Hearthmonth and about four people in nine counties remember it and about five remember the other one.

Nobody thanked anybody and the lamp at the high end of the boards was lit at the fourth hour and stood lit until the market was gone, and nine people in that street had been in daylight for six hours and five had noticed the lamp, and nobody went up the lane with a lamp and nobody went down it either.

**And the cost of that Thursday is a question asked out loud at the low end of the boards about nine hundred yards of water, and about two hundred people who heard it, are not in it and none of them has a price.**

**a quill at 11, a bristle brush at 12, a pot of tallow at 13, a hand spike at 15, a gate shoe at 16, a boat's hook at 17, a wicket at 19, a cresset at 23, a straw skirt at 24, and a bight of rope at 25.** Eleven, twelve, thirteen, fifteen, sixteen, seventeen, nineteen, twenty-three, twenty-four and twenty-five is one hundred and seventy-five pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and seventy-five pence is three marks and thirty-one over.** About four hundred people walked out of that market street that afternoon and about nine of them knew that a question about the far side of that water had been asked in the open and not answered, and about three hundred and ninety of the rest are going to walk past it, and four people in nine counties have said since that a chandler would call that the arrangement working and five have said that it is not that arrangement and has nobody's name at the top of it.
