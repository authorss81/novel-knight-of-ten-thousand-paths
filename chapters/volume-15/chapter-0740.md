# Chapter 0740

**Embermonth the twenty-fifth, YR 322, a Wednesday and not a market morning with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and no market in that street, four days out at the head of that made road where eight of nine yards of a wall are back in, and a county with about four hundred people who have heard nine things and about nine who have said them and no way of holding the first thing anybody in it has been refused out loud twice for.**

A Wednesday in that market town of about two thousand people is not a market morning, and nine people were in that street by the ninth hour of the twenty-fifth of Embermonth and four of them were at the boards with nothing on them and five were not, and the bolt on that room over the weigh-house went on from the Saturday and nobody went up the lane four hundred paces long that holds it.

Nobody thanked anybody and that is the twenty-fifth of Embermonth and five days of it are left, and the first of the next month is six days off and comes on a Tuesday, and four people in nine counties stand at those boards at about the ninth hour on a first of a month and five do not, and nobody has sent for the man who built it on any of the ten that have come and gone since the first of Goatmonth.

Nobody asked what is under the cloth that has been on the bench at the low end of those boards since the middle of Hearthmonth, and the lamp at the high end of them is lit at the fourth hour on every day of the week and has not been out early in about four and a half years, and about four people in nine counties have said since that this county can look at three things from a market street and can do nothing with any of them.

Then four days off, and the ordinary half of that Wednesday is nine tenths of it, and about nine hundred people along that made road did the ordinary part of it, and the ordinary part is that nothing about a Wednesday is visible on a road.

Nobody said anything and a drove went up that lane on the last Thursday before, which was the nineteenth of Embermonth, at about the sixth hour, and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back down at about the fifth hour, and nobody went with it, and that is about twenty-nine drove to go up that lane since the second of Mudmonth and the next one goes on Thursday and four hundred people in a market town have never watched one.

Nobody said anything and the nine beasts stood in that field for about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over and did not go past the stone wall round it, and the field has a gate on the far side of it that has never been opened, and about four hundred people in a market town have never seen any of this and about five have never worked out why not.

Eight of the nine yards of wall at the head of that made road are back in and the gap is about a yard long and about four feet high, and the stone is quarried two miles off on the low side and one man has been carrying it two miles in his own hands for two hundred and three days, and the last yard is about nine feet of a course and he has not started it, and nobody in nine counties has asked him when he will and nobody is going to.

The brood in a walled yard half a mile below that wall is five frames where there were nine on the night of the eighth of Thawmonth, and that night is two hundred and twenty-seven days back, and a brood frame comes back in about four weeks in a year like this one, and the fifth frame came in on the twentieth of Embermonth and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first month this year in which a figure in this county went up and nobody said anything about it, and five have said that this is because the figure is about bees and not about a road.

Nobody said anything and the broom on that east rise is nine acres of standing dead about nine hundred yards off and about two hundred yards above the gap in that wall, and it has been standing for about a hundred and twenty days with about nine days of it left to be cut, and a line in grass that is not cut closes over in about three years and about nine hundred yards of a line nine feet wide along that shoulder has not closed over, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a thing that does not close over is being kept and about five have said that nobody who keeps it has been asked for what.

The nine stones at the end of that line are about the height of a man's knee and about as wide as two hands and the tops of them are all at the same height, and nobody in nine counties has found out why, and the man of thirty-two cleaned about four hundred yards of the grass off the top of two of the nine with a bit of stick on the fourteenth of Fallowmonth and has not been back up that lane since, and nobody has asked him why he does it.

The bar at the head of that made road is in on the lane side and has been in since Mudmonth and it is four feet of ash with two stones on the end of it, and the drove goes up through the gap at the low end of about nine hundred yards of wall and not through the gate, and he is forty-one and keeps a drove and a byre and nine hundred yards of wall and has been asked one question about a road in eleven years, and nobody asked him a second one on the twenty-fifth of Embermonth and about nine people in nine counties have said since that nobody is going to either.

Nobody thanked him and the weigh-house weighed one load of lime and nothing else on that Wednesday and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that about four hundred and nine parish rolls come past his floor in a good month, that Embermonth is twenty-five days old and about two hundred and forty sheets have come past that floor in it, and that five days of it are left and that nobody has ever asked him what a sheet weighs.

A sheet of a parish roll is about four hundred yards of it if a man wants to look at the whole of one and about four inches of it if he wants the top of it, and about four hundred and nine of them come past that floor in a good month and about two hundred and forty have come past it in twenty-five days of this one, and that man has said out loud on about nine market mornings in about four and a half years that nobody has ever asked him what a sheet weighs, and about four people in nine counties have said since that this is the only figure anybody in this county has repeated to himself nine times and five have said that it is the only figure nobody has ever asked him for.

Nobody thanked anybody and the inn at the high end put no board out on a Wednesday and had four beds empty and the woman of about fifty-one who keeps that house came out into that street at about the fifth hour and stood about nine feet off the boards and did not come closer, and a chandler nine doors down put a cloth over a bench rather than write anything at all on it.

A man with a barrow of lime went the length of that street four times and unloaded at one door and loaded at two, and nobody stopped walking on any account, and the near wheel of a cart came off at the low end and the carter got it up again with a bar in about nine minutes, and two women at the third door argued about a bolt of cloth for about four minutes and then went into the shop and came out with it wrapped.

The fruit on nine hundred trees between two miles of wall came down into four hundred yards of grass every year and was still in it in the spring, and nobody in nine counties has ever picked one up, and the sea is four days south of that market town and about four hundred people live on a far bank across nine hundred yards of water at the top of a tide and nobody in that market street has ever asked a person at a gate at the near end of that water anything at all.

Then the last of it, and it is not the two communities and it is not the road, and it takes about as long as it takes to turn a cart to say.

Nobody said anything and here is the whole of what a man of thirty-two has in this county on the twenty-fifth of Embermonth. He has been refused twice in the open by two people with standing, once on a shoulder above a drove road by a man who keeps the far end of that lane and once in a market street four days off by a clerk of an office of nine clerks, and he has taken both, and he said out loud in a market street on the twenty-eighth of Fallowmonth that he is not going to ask either of them again and that he is not going to argue with either of them, and nobody argued with him and nobody thanked him.

Nobody said anything and he has told nobody about either of them except that market street, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man who has been refused twice in the open by two people with standing has nobody in nine counties to tell and five have said that he has told about four hundred people in one market street and that telling four hundred people is the same as telling nobody and that this is the first time that has been true of him.

Nobody thanked anybody and he has given one thing away in the open as well, on the nineteenth of Embermonth, to a man who has three clauses in nine hands with no copy of them, and about four people in nine counties have said since that the thing he gave away was the fact of a thing at his left hand and five have said that what he gave away was not the thing but the fact, and that the thing is still there and is still warm about four days in seven and that he has not said out loud that he is never going to use it.

Nobody said anything and about nine hundred yards of an old road is in about four hundred mouths and in nobody's hand, and two refusals are in about four hundred mouths and in nobody's hand either, and one sheet of paper with one line on it is in an office four days off that cannot do anything with a sheet, and one thing at a left hand is in one man's head, and four people in nine counties have said since that a county with four things in it and four places to put them has never once been able to move one thing from one place into another and five have said that this county has now acquired the first thing anybody in nine counties has ever been refused out loud twice and has no way of holding that either.

Nobody thanked him and nobody in nine counties has consoled him and nobody in nine counties has thanked him and nobody has asked him one word about any of it since the nineteenth of Embermonth, and four people in nine counties have said since that a man who has been refused twice and has lost the one thing that was going to come home has not had one single person in nine counties say one single word to him about any of it and five have said that this county has never had anybody in it lose a thing and say nothing about it, and that this is the first of that and not the first of losing a thing.

Nobody said anything and there is no page in any room in nine counties with the twenty-fifth of Embermonth on it, and the register in the room over that weigh-house holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled, and the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps that room has not said one word to him about any of it and he has not said one word to her, and four people in nine counties have said since that two people in this county have been in the same building on about forty days of this year and have not said a word to each other on any one of them and five have said that this is not a thing anybody has ever asked about and that is the reason it has lasted.

Nobody said anything and then a question came into that market street at about the ninth hour from a person at the third door who wanted to know one thing, and nobody stopped it, and it was not about a road and not about a wall and it was asked kindly.

“**Are you going back up the lane?**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a question asked about whether a man is going back up a lane that goes to nine stones and five had heard it asked kindly, and nobody stopped it and the man of thirty-two with a stick answered it out loud, in the open, in his own trade, and it was 14 words, and nobody thanked him.

“**Nobody is going up that lane about this and I am not going either.**”

Nobody said anything and the drove will go up it on Thursday as it has gone up it on every Thursday since the second of Mudmonth and about nine hundred yards of a line nine feet wide in the grass goes along the top of that lane and about nine households past nine stones keep a rota of that flat, and four people in nine counties have said since that the only thing in this county that goes up that lane on a fixed day without anybody arranging it is nine beasts and about five have said that the drove has been asked nothing and has refused nothing and that a thing which is asked nothing is the only refusal in nine counties that has never once been argued with.

Nobody thanked him and the light came off the west bank first and off those boards about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over, and the lamp at the high end of them was lit at the fourth hour and stood lit until the light was gone, and nobody in that street has ever turned it out early, and nine people went out of that market street into about nine trades and four stayed at the boards.

Nobody said anything and here is the last of it and there is nothing after this page about it. About nine hundred yards of an old road is in about four hundred mouths and in nobody's hand. Two refusals are in about four hundred mouths and in nobody's hand. One sheet of paper is in an office four days off. One thing at a left hand is in one man's head and he has not said out loud that he is never going to use it. A fifth frame of brood is in a walled yard half a mile below a wall. Eight of nine yards of that wall are back in. Nobody in nine counties has been thanked for any of the six of those and nobody has been consoled.
