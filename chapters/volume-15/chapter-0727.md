# Chapter 0727

**Rainmonth the thirteenth, YR 322, a Saturday and a market morning, the second hour in the market street at the boards with four hundred people in it and two hundred of them stopped, the room over the weigh-house open, and the ninth thing anybody in nine counties has said out loud about that lane.**

A Saturday in that market town of about two thousand people is the loudest thing in nine counties, and about four hundred people were in that street by the second hour of the thirteenth of Rainmonth and about two hundred of them stopped what they were doing, and the frost had not come off the ruts at that hour, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the loudest morning of the week in this county and the muddiest one are the same morning for about nine months of the year.

Nobody thanked anybody and Rainmonth the thirteenth is nine days after the broom came on, and nine people in nine counties know that and five have ever cut any, and four bundles of it came down two miles of unmade road on the eighth of Rainmonth behind a young man of nineteen and a man of thirty-two with a stick, and nobody asked either of them anything about it.

Nobody said anything and the boards at the low end of that street have been standing since the middle of Longlight the year before and are the colour of a wet slate, and the hearing has not been called since the first of Goatmonth and does not sit and will not sit on the first of the next month, and the cloth over the bench at the low end of them has been on it since the middle of Hearthmonth and nobody has lifted it and nobody has asked what is under it.

Then the thing on the board, and it is the first thing anybody in nine counties has had off that shoulder.

Nobody thanked him and the young man of nineteen made nine brooms and nine bee brushes out of four bundles of that broom on the Friday and the Saturday in his own walled yard, and put them on a board at the low end of that market street at about the seventh hour with nothing written on the board at all, and nine people took one and five did not, and nobody thanked him and four people in nine counties have said since that nine people took a broom off a board at four hundred paces for nothing and five have said that they did not take one because they have one.

Nobody said anything and a broom out of that hill is four feet of stalk and a handle of three feet, and a bee brush is about the length of a hand and is not for sweeping anything and is for putting bees back into a frame without stinging yourself, and four people in nine counties have said since that nine brooms and nine brushes came down two miles of unmade road on a young man's back and that this is the first object anybody in nine counties has had off that shoulder in four and a half years.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up looked at that board for about as long as it takes to turn a cart and said one thing out loud about it and it was in his own trade and it was 42 words, and he did not raise his voice.

“**I sell cloth for money and that board has nothing on it and there is a man in this street who will not sell a yard of anything to anybody who has not got the money and I am one of them.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a cloth seller say out loud that a broom board has no money on it and five had heard 42 words, and the man of thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up had said nothing at all about where the broom came from and nobody in that street asked him.

Nobody thanked him and a question about that board came into that street at about the seventh hour and it was asked kindly by a person at the fifth door who wanted to know one thing.

“**Is that broom off the hill at the top of the lane?**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a question asked about where a broom came from and five had heard a person at the fifth door ask it, and nobody stopped it and the young man of nineteen answered it out loud, in the open, in his own trade, and it was 47 words.

“**It is off the east rise above the shoulder and I cut it on Thursday with a sickle and I carried it down in four bundles and a man with a bad leg carried two of them, and that is the whole of where it came from.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a young man give the whole of where a broom came from in one answer and five had heard 47 words, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in four and a half years that anybody in this county has said where a thing came from and said all of it, and four people have said that saying all of it is the same as saying nothing and five have said that it is not and that nobody has ever heard all of it.

Then the ninth thing, and it was said out loud in a market street for the first time in four and a half years by the man who said it on a shoulder.

Nobody thanked him and the young man of nineteen stood at nine feet off those boards at about the eighth hour and said the first of three things out loud, in the open, to two hundred people, in his own trade, and it was 49 words.

“**There is nine hundred yards of kerbed road on that shoulder and the kerb stops where the grass comes over it, and I can put my hand on the stone where it stops and nine people in nine counties can check it in two days if anybody wants to.**”

Nobody said anything and two hundred people in that market street had heard a young man give a place where a kerb stops and five of them did not stop what they were doing while he said it, and four people in that street had worked out inside nine seconds that a question asked out loud in this street on the twenty-ninth of Longlight has just been answered and five had not, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in four and a half years that anything anybody said out loud in a market street has been answered in the same street.

Nobody said anything and the man of thirty-four with a satchel had been standing at the high end of those boards and he said one word back and the word was how, and the young man of about nineteen said the second of three things out loud, in the open, in his own trade, and it was 57 words.

“**The line is nine feet wide because the east rise is cut along it once a year and a line in grass that is not cut closes over in about three years, and I have seen two other lines like it in this county and one was gone in two years and one was gone in five.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard the width of a road in the grass explained by the man who cuts it and five had heard 57 words and had not worked out that it is the width of the road because somebody cuts it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that about four hundred people have heard in one morning that a road is nine feet wide because a man cuts the grass on it once a year.

Nobody said anything and the young man of about nineteen said the third of three things out loud, in the open, in his own trade, and it was 76 words, and nobody stopped him, and he did not say it the way he said the other two, and he said it slower.

“**I have watched nine households cut that flat for nine years from the top of it and I am the only one who has ever watched it from the top and not from a gate, and the cutting is not for the road. It is for beasts, and the kerb was under the grass before the first of them ever cut, and that is the whole reason nobody has ever put a hand on that road.**”

Nobody said anything and about two hundred people in that market street had heard the ninth thing anybody in nine counties has said out loud about that lane and about four of them believe it and about five have never spoken to any of the other four, and nobody asked him a second question and nobody asked him a third, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that nobody in a market street asked a man who had just said something true a second question, and five have said that nobody asked because there was nothing to ask.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel then said one thing out loud, in the open, to nine people, in his own trade, and it was 57 words, and nobody thanked him.

“**That is a young man's account and not mine and I am not going to say it again in this street. I have said nine things out loud in this county in about four and a half years and I am not going to say the tenth and I am not going to say this one twice.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard the man who says the finding of things in that county refuse out loud to say a finding twice and five had heard 57 words, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time that man has refused a finding he had been given, and about four people have said that refusing a finding he had been given is a different thing from refusing to find one and five have said that it is the same thing with a better trade behind it.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract for about nineteen years came in at about the ninth hour and stood at the low end of those boards and said nothing about the broom and one thing about the road and it was in his own trade and it was 65 words, and nobody stopped him.

“**About nine hundred yards of kerbed road is a road and about nine hundred yards of a line in the grass is a road a man mows, and there is no road office in nine counties that has ever heard of either and I have kept four miles of made road for about nineteen years and I could not tell you who keeps the other.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a roadkeeper say out loud that he could not tell anybody who keeps nine hundred yards of an old road and five had heard 65 words, and the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up said one word back and the word was nobody, and nobody argued with him about it.

Then the ordinary half of that Saturday, which is nine tenths of it, and the ninth thing is not in any of it.

Nobody thanked anybody and the inn at the high end had four beds full and put no price on a board that morning because the price had not moved since the middle of Harvestmonth, and the woman of about fifty-one who keeps that house came out into that street at about the fifth hour and stood about nine feet off the boards and did not come closer, and a chandler nine doors down put a cloth over a bench rather than write anything at all on it.

Nobody said anything and the weigh-house weighed two loads of lime and a load of wool and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that about four hundred and nine parish rolls come past his floor in a good month and that Rainmonth is the month about half of them come past wet, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the only good news anybody in this county has had since the second of Mudmonth and that it is about sheets.

Nobody thanked anybody and the near wheel of a cart came off at the low end and the carter got it up again with a bar in about nine minutes and nobody stopped walking, and two women at the third door argued about a bolt of cloth for about four minutes and then went into the shop and came out with it wrapped, and the frost came off the ruts by the ninth hour.

Nobody said anything and the ninth mile stone on that made road was cleaned that morning by the one man who cleans it and nobody in nine counties has ever seen him do it, and two miles of wall and orchard between the eleventh mile stone and the top of that hill were in blossom and about nine hundred trees of them, and the owners are about nine households and not one of them has ever asked anybody anything about anybody who walks past its gate.

Nobody said anything and the drove at the head of that made road went out on the Thursday before, which was the eleventh of Rainmonth, at about the sixth hour, and went up about nine hundred yards of that lane and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back at about the fifth hour, and nobody went with it, and four people in nine counties have said since that about six of the nine yards of wall at the head of that made road are back in and the gap is about three yards and about four feet high and five have said that a drove going up through a gap in a wall that is being mended is a thing nobody has ever put in one sentence.

Nobody thanked him and the hearing has not been called since the first of Goatmonth and cannot be called and does not sit, and its first of a month is eighteen days off and will come on a Wednesday, and about four people in nine counties stand at those boards at about the ninth hour on a first of a month and five do not, and seven firsts have now come and gone in a row with nothing on them.

Nobody said anything and about nine people took a broom or a bee brush off that board and went out of that market street carrying it in about nine directions, and four people in nine counties have said since that a broom is not a thing anybody in this county is asked about and five have said that this is the first thing anybody in nine counties has carried home off a hill in about four and a half years and that nobody will know in a year where it came from.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about thirty-two with a stick stood at the sea end of that market street with his hand on it for the whole morning and nobody looked at him and nobody asked him anything, and about four people in that street had worked out that he came down off that shoulder on the ninth of Rainmonth and about five had worked out nothing, and nine people in nine counties have said since that four days on a shoulder with a man of about nineteen is the longest time anybody has been up there since the twelfth of Thawmonth and that nobody has asked either of them one word about it.

Nobody thanked anybody and nothing about any of it is in a document, and there is no page in any room in nine counties with the thirteenth of Rainmonth on it, and the register in the room over that weigh-house holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is now the second time in a fortnight that about four hundred people in this county have heard something true and have not acted on it, and four have said that the first one was a question and this one is an answer and five have said that a county can hear an answer and do exactly as much with it as with a question.

Nobody said anything and about four hundred people in that market street have now heard nine things about one hill and about nine hundred yards of an old road is in about four hundred mouths and in nobody's hand, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this county has not known in about four and a half years what to do with a thing it cannot write down and has never once been asked, and about four people have said that a thing nobody can write down cannot be handed to the next county and five have said that there is no next county.

Nobody thanked anybody and the light went off the west bank first and off those boards about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over, and the lamp at the high end of them was lit and stood lit until the market was gone, and about four hundred people went out of that market street into about nine trades and about nine people stayed at the boards.

**And the cost of that Saturday is about nine hundred yards of an old road in about four hundred mouths and in nobody's hand, nine brooms and about nine bee brushes taken off a board for nothing, and a mouth that has said it will not say it again, are not in it and none of them has a price.**

**a sheep peg at 1, a hurdle hoop at 2, a tallow dip at 3, a gate pin at 4, a hurdle at 5, a hand bar at 6, a hurdle rail at 27, a coping stone at 36, a barrow wheel at 39, and a cresset at 40.** One, two, three, four, five, six, twenty-seven, thirty-six, thirty-nine and forty is one hundred and sixty-three pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and one hundred and sixty-three pence is three marks and nineteen over.** Three things were said out loud in a market street on the thirteenth of Rainmonth by a young man who keeps bees and had cut about nine acres of broom with a sickle, and the first of them was a place where a kerb stops, and about four people in that street had worked out inside about nine seconds that it answered a question asked in that street on the twenty-ninth of Longlight and about five had not, and nobody wrote any of it down.
