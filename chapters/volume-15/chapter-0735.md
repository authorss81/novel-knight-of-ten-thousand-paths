# Chapter 0735

**Fallowmonth the twenty-sixth, YR 322, a Tuesday and not a market morning with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and no market in that street, the ninth hour in the market street at the boards with nine people in it and four of them at the boards, and the second of two things a man of thirty-two goes out and asks for.**

A Tuesday in that market town of about two thousand people is not a market morning, and nine people were in that street by the ninth hour of the twenty-sixth of Fallowmonth and four of them were at the boards with nothing on them and five were not, and nobody has been in that street since the Friday to hear anything from anybody.

Nobody thanked anybody and that is four days after a man said out loud on a made road four days off that he was going to ask the road office for one thing out loud in a market street and was not going to write to them, and about nine people in nine counties know that and about five have ever been told it, and nobody asked him whether he was going to and nobody sent anybody with him.

Nobody said anything and a hearing in this county stands in a street on the first of every month and has done since the middle of Longlight the year before, and it cannot be called and does not sit, and the boards it stands in are the colour of a wet slate and have been repainted twice, and nobody has lifted the cloth off the bench at the low end of them and nobody has asked what is under it.

Then the man from the office, and he came down that lane from the weigh-house at about the seventh hour with a satchel and a stick and no hat.

Nobody thanked him and the man who came down that lane was about forty-nine and has been a clerk of that road office for about eleven years and his name is Corve Sallow, and about nine people in that market town have worked out that a man from that office came out into the street on foot and about five have worked out that he came out at the seventh hour and not at the ninth, and nobody has ever asked him why he came out at all.

Nobody said anything and nobody thanked him and he put no courtesy at anything, and about four people in that street had heard a Crown officer come out of a lane without saying good morning to anybody and five had not, and about nine people in nine counties have said since that the first time in about four and a half years that anybody from that office has stood in that street is a Tuesday with no market on it, and five have said that a thing that happens on a day with no market on it is a thing that was not arranged for the market.

Nobody thanked him and he stood about nine feet off the boards with his satchel against his leg and looked at the wet slate of them for about as long as it takes to turn a cart, and nobody stopped him and nobody told him anything and about four people in that street had worked out that a clerk looking at some boards for that long is a man waiting to be asked something and five had worked out that he is a man waiting for the street to be quiet.

Nobody said anything and then the ask was made, and it was the same thing that had been asked on a shoulder twelve days before, out loud, in the open, to one person, in his own trade, and it was 53 words, and nobody stopped it.

“**I want to go past nine stones at the top of a lane four days off and stand on the flat beyond them and come back down and say what is on it. I have asked the man who keeps the far end of that lane. I am asking you the same thing.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a man of thirty-two ask a clerk of the road office for the same thing he asked a man who keeps a boundary and five had heard 53 words, and nobody stopped it and nobody repeated it back to him, and about four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that one thing has been asked out loud in this county twice in two places and five have said that the two people it was asked of have never been asked anything by anybody in this county before today.

Nobody thanked anybody and the clerk of about forty-nine looked at the man of thirty-two for about as long as it takes to turn a cart and then said it out loud, in the open, in the street, in his own trade, and it was 34 words in four sentences, and he did not say it again and he did not explain any part of it.

“**No. That is not a thing this office has an entry for. I will write down that a man said it out loud in a market street. Nothing will be done with the paper.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a clerk of the road office say no to a man out loud in four sentences and five had heard 34 words, and nobody stopped him and nobody argued with him and nobody thanked him, and about four people in nine counties have said since that the first time in about four and a half years that anything has come back out of that office it was four sentences and no reason in them and five have said that a refusal with no reason in it cannot be argued with and about four people have said that a refusal with no reason in it cannot be complained of either.

Nobody thanked him and then the man of thirty-two said one thing back, and it was short, and it was in his own trade, and nobody stopped it, and it was 28 words, and nobody repeated it afterwards.

“**Then that is the answer and I am not going to ask you it a second time and I am not going to write to you again either.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a man accept a refusal out loud in one sentence and five had heard 28 words, and nobody said anything back to him and nobody thanked him, and about four people in nine counties have said since that two men have now refused one man of thirty-two out loud in two places in twelve days and five have said that neither of them refused him and both of them refused the thing and that this is the first time in this county that those two things have come apart.

Nobody said anything and then nothing happened for about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice, and the clerk of about forty-nine put his hand on the top rail of those boards and the rail had a skin of white in it at about the ninth hour, and he said one more thing out loud, in the open, in his own trade, to nine people, and it was 33 words, and nobody thanked him.

“**A man can say a thing out loud in a street and I can put the saying of it on a sheet of paper and that is the whole of what there is.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a clerk describe the whole of what there is and five had heard 33 words, and about four people in nine counties have said since that the whole of what there is is a sheet of paper in an office four days from a hill and five have said that a sheet of paper in an office is a hand and about four have said that a hand that cannot do anything with what it holds is not a hand.

Nobody thanked him and the light came off the west bank first and off those boards about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over on a day with no market on it, which is the same as it does on a market morning except that there is nobody in it to see it, and about four people in nine counties have said since that the only difference between a Tuesday and a Saturday in that street is the number of people who watch the boards go dark and five have said that it is the boards.

Nobody said anything and a question came into that street at about the tenth hour from a person at the fifth door who wanted to know one thing and nobody stopped it, and it was the eleventh question anybody in nine counties has asked about that lane and the fifth one nobody could answer.

“**Did he put it in a book?**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a question asked about whether a clerk writes things down and five had heard it asked by a person who wanted to know, and nobody stopped it and the man of thirty-two with a stick answered it out loud, in the open, in his own trade, and it was 36 words, and nobody thanked him.

“**He put one line on a sheet of paper and the line is that a man said it in a street, and that sheet is in an office and nobody in this county can read it.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market street had heard a man describe what a clerk wrote down about him and five had heard 36 words, and nobody asked to see the sheet and nobody asked whether a copy could be had, and about four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that anybody in this county has been written down by anybody and five have said that being written down is not the same as being heard.

Nobody thanked him and the second of Fallowmonth is three days off and the hearing's first of a month is that day and will come on a Sunday, and about four people in nine counties stand at those boards at about the ninth hour on a first of a month and five do not, and the badge-man has not been at two of the nine that have come and gone on purpose and nobody has sent for him on any of them.

Nobody said anything and the room over that weigh-house was shut behind its own door with the bolt on from the Saturday and the clerk of about forty-nine went up the lane four hundred paces long that holds it and about four people in that market town had worked out that a man from a Crown office went into a room that a woman of about twenty-nine keeps and five had worked out that he did not and nobody has ever asked either of those two people anything about the other.

Nobody thanked anybody and a drove went up that lane four days off on the Thursday before, which was the twenty-first of Fallowmonth, at about the sixth hour, and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back down at about the fifth hour, and nobody went with it, and eight of the nine yards of wall at the head of that road are back in with a gap about a yard long and one man has been carrying stone two miles on his own for a hundred and seventy-four days.

Nobody said anything and the bees in a walled yard half a mile below that wall are at four frames of brood where there were nine on the night of the eighth of Thawmonth, and that night is a hundred and ninety-eight days back, and a brood frame comes back in about four weeks in a year like this one, and about four people in nine counties have said since that the hives are the only thing four days off that has gone up in a month and five have said that the wall has not.

Nobody thanked him and the clerk of about forty-nine went back up the lane four hundred paces long that holds that room over the weigh-house and did not stop at the boards on his way past them, and about four people in that street had worked out that a man from that office went into a room that belongs to a county and not to an office and five had worked out nothing, and about nine people in nine counties have said since that nobody in nine counties knows which of those two things that room is and five have said that there is a keeper of it and fourpence a year.

Then the ordinary half of that Tuesday, which is nine tenths of it.

Nobody thanked him and the weigh-house weighed one load of lime and nothing else and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that a day with no market on it is a day on which about four hundred and nine sheets do not come past his floor and that nobody has ever asked him what a sheet weighs, and the inn at the high end had four beds empty and put no board out.

Nobody said anything and the near wheel of a cart came off at the low end of that street and the carter got it up again with a bar in about nine minutes and nobody stopped walking, and two women at the third door argued about a bolt of cloth for about four minutes and then went into the shop and came out with it wrapped.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up had a piece of blue wool over his forearm and he took it off and looked at the four people who were at those boards and put it back, and nobody has ever established that a cloth seller looking at boards means anything and about four people in nine counties have said since that he has nine names in his head from the middle of Fallowmonth and has still not written them down and five have said that he will not.

Nobody said anything and nobody stopped walking on that account, and the light came up the side of that weigh-house and found the upper edge of those boards about as long as it takes to turn a cart, and the lamp at the high end of them went out with the light because there was not enough light off the wet slate of them to need it.

Nobody thanked him and then he stood at the sea end of that market street with his stick in his right hand and his left hand in the side of his coat for about as long as it takes to turn a cart, and about four people in that street had worked out that a man who has been refused twice in eight days is a man who is going to be refused a third time and about five had worked out nothing, and nobody asked him anything and nobody looked at that coat.

Nobody said anything and about nine hundred yards of an old road is in about four hundred mouths and in nobody's hand, and one refusal is in two mouths and the other one is in the mouths of about nine people in one street, and about four people in nine counties have said since that this county has now two refusals in it in two places and five have said that the second of the two is the harder one and about four have said that it is harder because nothing came of it at all and not because a man said it better.

Nobody thanked anybody and a sheet of paper went into an office four days off from that hill with a line on it about a man saying a thing in a street, and that is the first paper anybody in this county has written about any of it in about four and a half years, and about four people in nine counties have said since that the county has waited about four and a half years for a hand and got one that cannot use it and five have said that a hand that cannot use what it holds is worse than no hand because it can be looked at.

Nobody said anything and nothing about any of it is in a document, and there is no page in any room in nine counties with the twenty-sixth of Fallowmonth on it, and the register in the room over that weigh-house holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled.

**And the cost of that Tuesday is one refusal in four sentences with no reason in it, one sheet of paper in an office four days off that cannot do anything with what is on it, and about nine people who heard a man say no twice in eight days, are not in it and none of them has a price.**

**a sheep peg at 1, a hurdle hoop at 2, a wall peg at 3, a wicket at 4, a hand bar at 5, a hurdle rail at 6, a bight of rope at 7, a sheet of foolscap at 8, a waxed thread at 10, and a gate pin at 13.** One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, ten and thirteen is fifty-nine pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and fifty-nine pence is one mark and eleven over.** A man of thirty-two with a stick asked a clerk of the road office in a market street on a Tuesday with no market on it for the same thing he had asked the man who keeps the far end of a lane twelve days before, and the clerk said no in four sentences and explained none of them and was not thanked, and about four people in that street had worked out that neither of the two men refused him.
