# Chapter 0718

**Longlight the seventeenth, YR 322, a Monday and not a market morning with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and no market in that street, eleven days on a shoulder above a drove road, nine people on it, and a thing being said out loud that nobody has standing to say.**

A Monday is not a market morning and about four people in that market town four days off stand at those boards at about the ninth hour and about five do not, and the badge-man is not there, and nobody sent for him, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the fifth first of a month in a row on which nothing has happened at the boards and that the badge-man has not been at two of them on purpose.

Nobody thanked anybody and then the ordinary half of a Monday four days off happened, and it is nine tenths of every day anybody in nine counties has ever had, and about nine hundred people along that made road did the ordinary part of it and the ordinary part is that nothing about a Monday is visible on a road.

Nobody thanked anybody and about nine hives stand in a walled yard about four rods by six behind a stone wall about four feet high, half a mile below that wall on the low side of the made road, off a lane of their own that is not made, and the young man of about nineteen who keeps them counted the brood in three frames on the seventeenth of Longlight, and a brood frame comes back in about four weeks in a year like this one, and it is ninety-nine days since the night of the eighth of Thawmonth, and nine people in nine counties have said since that three frames is the third figure anybody in nine counties can do this year and five have said that this is the first figure a person in nine counties has ever given that got better.

Nobody thanked him and the bees in that yard had been flying about four feet off the ground all morning and going out and coming back with nothing, and nine people in nine counties have said since that bees fly low when there is nothing out and fly high when there is, and that this is the same yard and the same beam and the same nine thousand little things going in and out of nine holes in a row that a young man described in a market street in about nine minutes and that nobody has ever asked him to describe it again.

Nobody thanked anybody and the drove at the head of that made road went out on the Thursday as it goes out every Thursday, which was the sixth of Longlight, and went up about nine hundred yards of that lane and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back down at about the fifth hour, and nobody went with it, and nine people in nine counties know that and five have ever watched it happen, and nobody has ever written down that a drove goes up a lane on a Thursday.

Nobody said anything and about nine yards of the nine hundred yards of wall at the head of that made road is still down, and the gap is about nine yards long and about four feet high, and the man of about forty-one who keeps that wall and a drove and a byre has been putting stone in it alone since the second of Mudmonth, and nine people in nine counties know that and five have ever asked him where the stone came from, and the bar went in on the lane side on the Thursday morning after the water went out of the lane and it is in and it has not been asked about.

Nobody thanked anybody and it is the eleventh day a shoulder has had people on it, and nine people in nine counties know that and about five have ever known, and four people in nine counties have said since that eleven days is a long time to be off your own trade and about five have said that eleven days is nothing and that a man who says it is nothing is a man who has never left a trade for eleven days.

Nobody thanked him and the broom is not on and it comes on about the fourth of Rainmonth, which is about fourteen days off, and nine people in nine counties know that and about five have ever cut it, and four people in nine counties have said since that a young man of about nineteen said he would not come up again until the fourth of Rainmonth and that this is the eleventh day of a promise that has not been broken.

Nobody said anything and on the seventeenth of Longlight nine people were on that shoulder, and that is five who came up on the fifth of Longlight for five reasons in five trades, a man of thirty-two with a stick who is not one of the five, two households that have nothing on the flat, and a man of about fifty-four who keeps the far end of that lane, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the fourth time in about four and a half years that nine people have been in one place in this county and that none of the four times was arranged.

Nobody thanked anybody and nine people in nine counties have said since that a party is not an army and has no head and is not going to get one, and that a party is five people who each arrived at the top of a lane on their own business and then did the same thing for eleven days, and about four people have said that this is the whole of what the word means and about five have said that a word which means five people and eleven days is not a word anybody needs to keep.

Then the cost, and it is said by the person with no standing to say it.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of thirty-two said it out loud, in the open, on that shoulder, at about the eleventh hour, to about eight people, and he said it once and he did not say it again that day, and it was fifty-four words.

“**There is no crossing at these nine stones and there is not going to be one from this side, and the whole of what this party came up here to do cannot be done from this side, and I have got no standing in this county to say so and I am saying it.**”

Nobody said anything and four people on that shoulder had heard a man say out loud that there is no crossing at nine stones and about five had heard fifty-four words, and nobody argued with him and nobody thanked him, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that a man has said out loud in this county that a thing cannot be done and that the whole of the county did not argue with him and did not thank him, and about four have said that the not arguing with him is the finding and about five have said that the not thanking him is because there is nothing to thank.

Nobody said anything and four people in nine counties have said since that a man who has got no standing in this county has said the truest thing anybody has said on that shoulder in about four and a half years and that about five have said that a man with no standing who says a true thing out loud is the reason nobody with standing in this county has ever said one.

Nobody thanked anybody and nobody crossed the nine stones and nothing on the other side of them was seen by anybody on this side of them and the man of thirty-two did not put a foot past the ninth of them, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the third time that the crossing has been said out loud on that shoulder and that the third time is the first time it was said by a person who had been asked nothing.

Nobody said anything and the person of about forty-four whose household keeps sheep said the other half of it out loud, in the open, in her own trade, and she said that the crossing is not what nine households are for and that there is no rota for it and that nobody in nine counties would ever agree to it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time anybody has said out loud that nine households will not agree to a crossing and that the saying of it is worth more than the crossing would be.

Nobody thanked her and the man of about fifty-four who keeps the far end of that lane said nothing at all, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a man who keeps a boundary did not say one word in the open about a thing that cannot be done across a line of stones, and about four people have said that a man who keeps a boundary has said the true thing by not saying anything and about five have said that a man who keeps a boundary said nothing because a boundary is not a thing anybody can carry.

Nobody thanked anybody and about nine people in nine counties have said since that the eleventh day of a shoulder is the day a party stops being five reasons in five trades and becomes eleven days of five trades, and that nobody in nine counties has ever had to find out when that happens in anybody's case, and about four people have said it happened on the seventh and about five have said it happened on the eleventh.

Nobody thanked anybody and the grass on that shoulder is about nine hundred yards wide and it is not level and there is a rise in it going east, and about nine people in nine counties have said since that about nine hundred yards of grass with a rise in it is the hardest ground in nine counties to leave in the dark and about five have said that it is the easiest because there is nothing on it to get lost in.

Nobody thanked anybody and eleven days on a shoulder is not nothing to the body, and there is no wall on it and no tree on it and nine people in nine counties have said since that about nine hundred yards of grass on a hilltop takes the wet off a man's boots by the second morning and keeps them off it for about nine days, and five have said that the ground takes it back.

Nobody thanked anybody and they carried water up from the field with the stone wall round it, which is nine hundred yards below them and about two hundred yards to the low side, and one of them went for it every morning of the eleven days and came back with two pails, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the only labour anybody in nine counties has ever recorded about that shoulder and that it went in no document at all.

Nobody thanked anybody and they did not light a fire, and nine people in nine counties have said since that six people who have been on a hilltop for eleven nights in Longlight and have not lit a fire is the strangest thing this county has produced in about four and a half years, and four people have said that the reason is that there is nothing on that shoulder to burn that is not somebody's, and five have said that the reason is that nobody wanted to be the first one to put smoke on it.

Nobody thanked anybody and then the eleven days were counted out in five trades, because that is the only way anybody on that shoulder would say what they had spent.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract for about nineteen years said that four miles have been in the hands of about nine men for eleven days and that eleven days is one month and that a month of a road under contract is taken out of his winter and not out of his summer, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first figure anybody has given for the cost of going up a hill and that about five have said that a figure is better than a speech.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about thirty-eight who puts boards on the inside of gates said that nine yards of boards are not going on the inside of any gate in nine counties for about a fortnight and that nobody has asked him for them and that about four hundred people will be finding out about it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the second figure anybody has given for the cost of going up a hill and that both of the figures are for work that is not in anybody's trade as a road.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about twenty-seven who keeps eels in weirs about two days north of the causeway said that his weirs are in water and that Longlight is not eel season and that he would not have been at them anyway and that about eleven days is eleven days of not standing in a stream in the cold, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the only one of the five whose trade does not lose anything and that about five have said that a man whose trade loses nothing has nothing to be spent.

Nobody thanked her and the woman of about thirty-nine who keeps the second gate from the top of that made road said that her gate has been shut for about eleven days with nobody in her gateway and that a gate with nobody in it is a gate that has not been shut, it has only been left, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the third time in about four and a half years that she has made a distinction out loud that nobody had made and that none of the three has been written down.

Nobody said anything and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel said that he has said nothing in a market street for thirteen days and that he has about nine reasons in him that he gave out loud on the first of Longlight and that nobody has asked him for one of them since, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time a person who says things for a living has not said anything for thirteen days and about five have said that this county has never had to find out what that looks like.

Nobody thanked anybody and the fifth trade was not one of the five, and it was his, and he did not say it, and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel said out loud that he was not going to say it for him either, and nobody asked him for it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that a person has refused in the open to give his own cost and that about four people have said it is the only one of the six that is anybody's business.

Nobody said anything and then a question was asked out loud on that shoulder at about the fifth hour by the woman of about thirty-nine who keeps the second gate from the top of that made road, and nobody stopped her, and nobody answered it, and it is the fourth question anybody in nine counties has asked about that lane and the third one nobody could answer.

“**Then what do we do with eleven days of it?**”

Nobody said anything and four people on that shoulder had heard a gate keeper ask what a person does with a thing she has paid for in her own trade, and five had not, and the man of thirty-eight who puts boards on the inside of gates said one word back and the word was nothing, and nobody argued with him about it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a man who puts boards on gates is the only one of the six who had anything to do with his eleven days.

Nobody said anything and the light went off the low side of that shoulder at about the seventh hour and the wind came across about nine hundred yards of it off the low side and there was nothing to stop it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a wind on a shoulder in the dark is the reason nobody has ever stayed on one for eleven days and that about five people stayed on it for eleven days.

Nobody thanked anybody and nothing on the other side of those stones was seen by anybody on this side of them on the seventeenth of Longlight, and nine people in nine counties have said since that eleven days on a shoulder and a crossing that is not there is the whole of what this party has produced, and about four people have said that it is more than anybody in nine counties has produced with nine counties in it for about four and a half years.

**And the cost of that Monday is four miles of made road a month short, nine yards of boards not going on gates, a gate left and not shut, thirteen days of a mouth that says things not saying them, and a crossing that is not there, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
