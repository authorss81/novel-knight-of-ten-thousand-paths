# Chapter 0725

**Rainmonth the fourth, YR 322, a Thursday and a market morning, the second hour in the market street at the boards with four hundred people in it and two hundred of them stopped, the room over the weigh-house open, and the broom.**

A Thursday in that market town of about two thousand people is a market morning, and this Thursday was the fourth of Rainmonth and the broom was on the hill about two miles above the head of that made road, and about four hundred people were in that street by the second hour and about two hundred of them stopped what they were doing, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the proportion has not moved since the first of Embermonth and that it is the only figure in this county anybody can check twice a week.

Nobody thanked anybody and Rainmonth the fourth is the day the broom comes on, and it comes on about that day every year and not on any other, and nine people in nine counties know that and five have ever cut it, and the light in that street goes off the west bank first and off the boards about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over.

Nobody said anything and the boards at the low end of that street have been standing since the middle of Longlight the year before and are the colour of a wet slate and the cloth over the bench at the low end of them has been on it since the middle of Hearthmonth and nobody has lifted it and nobody has asked what is under it, and the hearing has not been called since the first of Goatmonth and does not sit.

Nobody thanked him and a woman of about thirty-nine who keeps the second gate from the top of that made road was in that street at about the fourth hour and said nothing about the broom, and four people in nine counties have said since that she has said no five times in a market street in Longlight and Rainmonth to five things that were not about the lane, and five have said that a gate keeper saying no to a broom is not a trade opinion but a trade decision, and that it is the only decision anybody in that street made that morning.

Nobody said anything and six of the nine yards of wall at the head of that made road are back in and the gap is three yards long and about four feet high and the stone is quarried about two miles off on the low side and one man has been carrying it two miles on his own for ninety-two days, and about four people in nine counties have said that nobody in nine counties has ever worked out how long nine yards of wall takes one man on his own and about five have said that nobody in nine counties is going to be told the day it is.

Nobody thanked anybody and the hives stand in a walled yard about four rods by six behind a stone wall about four feet high half a mile below that wall off a lane of their own that is not made, and the young man of about nineteen had counted the brood in them on the twenty-seventh of Longlight and it was three frames where there were nine on the night before the eighth of Thawmonth, and it is a hundred and sixteen days since that night, and four people in nine counties have said since that he cut his own account of the broom instead of staying to count anything and five have said that a beekeeper counts in an afternoon and has the rest of the day.

Then the broom, and it is said out loud in a market street before anybody has started it.

Nobody thanked him and the young man of nineteen was in that street at about the fifth hour with nine yards of tarred twine over one shoulder and a sickle in his hand and nine feet of new waxed cord in the crook of his arm, and four people in that street had worked out inside nine seconds that the broom is on this morning and five had not, and he said it out loud, in the open, to nine people, in his own trade, and it was 39 words.

“**The broom is on the east rise this morning and I am going up at six for it and I am going on my own account and I am not waiting for anybody and there is nothing to arrange.**”

Nobody said anything and two hundred people in that market street had heard a young man say out loud that he is going up a lane at six in the morning and five had heard 39 words, and nobody said anything back and nobody thanked him and nobody sent anybody with him, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in four and a half years that anybody in this county has said out loud in a market street that they are going up that lane for the thing the lane is actually for, and four people have said that it is a broom and five have said that it is the first sentence anybody in nine counties has heard about that lane that is not about a road.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up said one thing back about it and it was in his own trade and it was 40 words, and he did not raise his voice.

“**A man who sells cloth cannot tell you what a broom is for and I have been asked about a broom twice in a week and I am not going to be asked a third time in the same street.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a cloth seller refuse to be asked twice about a broom and five had heard 40 words, and the man of fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract for nineteen years said nothing about the broom at all, and four people in nine counties have said since that the man who said on the first of Longlight that he would go up that lane on the fourth of Rainmonth did not say it again this morning and five have said that a man keeps his word in a trade and not in a street.

Nobody thanked him and then a question came into that street at about the sixth hour and it was asked kindly by a person who had heard nothing else about the broom and wanted to know one thing about it.

“**And what will you do with the rest of it?**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a question asked about the rest of a broom and five had heard a question asked kindly by a person standing at the third door, and the young man of nineteen answered it out loud, in the open, in his own trade, and it was 56 words, and he did not stop cutting his own account of it to do it.

“**The same as any year. I will cut what four bundles of twine will hold and tie the rest in nine bundles and leave them on the stones at the top for whoever wants them, and there has been one bundle left there every year since I was eleven and it is never the same man.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a young man say out loud that a bundle of broom has been left on nine stones for eight years and five had heard 56 words, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first thing anybody in this county has ever been told about the top of that shoulder that is not about a road, and four have said that it is about a bundle and not about the nine stones.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of thirty-eight who puts boards on the inside of gates said out loud, in the open, in his own trade, that he is going up nine hundred yards with the drove that morning and no further, because nine yards of boards that were late in Longlight went on the inside of nine gates in Longlight's last week and four hundred people have found out and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the only figure in this county in four and a half years that has gone down and five have said that a figure going down is not a finding.

Then the ordinary half of that Thursday, which is nine tenths of it.

Nobody thanked anybody and the inn at the high end put a board out with the price of a bed on it in chalk and had four beds full, and the woman of fifty-one who keeps that house came out into that street at about the fifth hour and stood nine feet off the boards and did not come closer, and a chandler nine doors down put a cloth over a bench rather than write anything at all on it.

Nobody said anything and the weigh-house weighed two loads of lime and a load of wool and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that four hundred and nine parish rolls come past his floor in a good month and that this month has been the best month that floor has had since the middle of Harvestmonth, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the only good news anybody in this county has had since the second of Mudmonth and that it is about sheets.

Nobody thanked him and the room over that weigh-house was open because it is a Thursday, and the copyist of twenty-six came down the thirteen stairs at the seventh hour and went back up, and four people in that lane saw her come down and five did not, and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and the day-book stands at eleven entries and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled.

Nobody said anything and the near wheel of a cart came off at the low end and the carter got it up again with a bar in nine minutes and nobody stopped walking, and two women at the third door argued about a bolt of cloth for four minutes and then went into the shop and came out with it wrapped, and the frost came off the ruts by the ninth hour.

Nobody thanked him and the ninth mile stone on that made road was cleaned that morning by the one man who cleans it and nobody in nine counties has ever seen him do it, and he does it about nine hundred times in a year, and there was a ring of mud at the foot of it from the first week of Mudmonth and he has been getting at the ring with a bit of stick ever since.

Then the sixth hour, and two people went up that lane, and neither of them was sent.

Nobody thanked him and the drove at the head of that made road went out at about the sixth hour on that Thursday as it goes out every Thursday, and went through the gap at the low end of about nine hundred yards of stone wall that is four feet high at its lowest point and about nine feet wide, and about nine beasts went up about nine hundred yards of a drove road that has never been a made road in the life of this county, and stood in the field with the stone wall round it, and the young man of about nineteen went up with them.

Nobody said anything and about four people in that market town had worked out that the young man went up that lane on the drove and not on the lane and about five had not, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a drove is a thing that goes up a lane about four days a week whether or not anybody in nine counties is interested in it, and about four people have said that this is the first errand anybody in nine counties has ever been able to hide a second errand inside and five have said that it is not an errand and he is going up for a broom.

Nobody thanked anybody and a man of about forty-one stood at the head of that gap and did not help and did not watch them go and went back in, and nobody said anything to him and he said nothing, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the sixth time in about four and a half years that a man has stood at the head of that gap while somebody went through it and that five of the six were a drove.

Nobody said anything and the man of about thirty-eight who puts boards on the inside of gates went up about nine hundred yards with that drove and stopped at the field and did not go past it and came back down at about the ninth hour, and nobody thanked him and about nine people in nine counties know that and about five have ever watched it happen from the top of a shoulder about two miles above.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about twenty-seven who keeps eels in weirs about two days north of that causeway was in that market street at about the eighth hour and said nothing about a broom, and four people in nine counties have said since that a man whose trade loses nothing in Rainmonth has nothing to be in a market street about and five have said that he came in anyway and stood about nine feet off the boards for about as long as it takes to turn a cart.

Nobody said anything and the man of about thirty-two with a stick went out of that market town at about the sixth hour on that Thursday with his hand on it, up that made road four days to the head of it, the way he does every morning of the year, and about four people in that street had noticed which way he went and about five had not, and nine people in nine counties have said since that he did not say that he was going up the lane and was not asked.

Nobody said anything and five people went up that lane on the fifth of Longlight for five reasons in five trades and eleven days and came down on the eighteenth and went five ways and gave no account of anything to anybody, and four people in nine counties have said since that the whole of what that party cost was counted out in five trades by the five themselves and was four miles of made road a month short of a contractor, nine yards of boards a fortnight late, a gate left and not shut, and thirteen days of a mouth that says things not saying them.

Nobody thanked him and one of those five has not said one word in a market street since the eighteenth of Longlight and about nine people in nine counties know that and about five have ever asked him why, and four people in nine counties have said since that a man who goes quiet for about a fortnight after eleven days of saying things out loud has found out what it is to have said them and five have said that he was tired.

Nobody said anything and about nine hundred people live along the four days of that made road and every one of them knows which house the man of thirty-two comes out of, and four of them have offered him a bed since the middle of Frostmonth and about five have not, and nobody in nine counties has ever asked him where he goes on a Thursday at the sixth hour.

Nobody thanked anybody and about four hundred people in that street had heard four things about that lane since the twelfth of Thawmonth, and two accounts of a road on the twenty-seventh of Longlight, and a question about where the kerb stops, and a young man say he is going up for a broom at six in the morning, and about four of them believe about four of those and about five have never spoken to any of the other four.

Nobody said anything and about two hundred people in that market street went back to about nine trades by the eleventh hour, and about nine people stayed at the boards, and nobody wrote any of it down, and the register in the room over that weigh-house holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none and the day-book stands at eleven entries.

Nobody thanked anybody and the light went off the west bank first and off those boards about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over, and the lamp at the high end of them was lit and stood lit until the market was gone, and the cloth over the bench at the low end of them was on it and nothing lifted it and nobody asked what was under it.

Nobody said anything and a broom is about nine acres on the east rise, about four hundred yards up from the shoulder and about two hundred yards below the top of that hill, and it is about nine hundred yards short of the ninth of the nine stones, and nobody in nine counties has ever gone past those stones for a broom, and four people in nine counties have said since that a broom on that rise is the only thing in this county that is on the far side of a hill and not on the far side of nine stones, and five have said that a broom is not a reason to cross nine stones and they are the same sentence.

Nobody thanked anybody and there were about four hundred people in that market street and about four hundred of them have now heard the word broom said out loud in a trade on a Thursday in Rainmonth, and nothing about that is in a document, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first finding anybody in nine counties has ever printed on a thing that grows on a hill, and five have said that nobody printed it and four have said that it would not have mattered.

**And the cost of that Thursday is about nine acres of a broom on the east rise that nobody is going to get paid for, about nine hundred yards of a drove road walked behind nine beasts, and one young man's account of going up not being in anybody else's mouth, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
