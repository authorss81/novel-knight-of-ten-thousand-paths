# Chapter 0747

**Frostmonth the first, YR 322, a Thursday and a market morning, the second hour in the market street at the boards with about four hundred people in it and about two hundred of them stopped, the room over the weigh-house open, a twelfth first of a month come and gone with nothing at those boards, and two people from the flat past the ninth of the nine stones walking nine hundred yards of grass in their own trade.**

A Thursday in that market town of about two thousand people is a market morning, and four hundred people were in that street by the second hour of the first of Frostmonth and two hundred of them stopped what they were doing, and the frost came off the ruts about an hour after that, and about four people in nine counties had worked out that a month has turned and five had not.

The first of Wolfmonth came and went on a Tuesday a month ago at about the ninth hour with nothing at those boards at all, and this one is today and nothing is at them either, and that is the twelfth first of a month in a row on which nothing has happened at them, and four people in nine counties stand at those boards at about the ninth hour on a first of a month and five do not, and nobody has sent for the man who built it on any of the twelve and the badge-man has not been at two of them on purpose.

Those boards have been standing since the middle of Longlight the year before and have been repainted twice and are the colour of a wet slate, and the cloth on the bench at their low end has been on it since the middle of Hearthmonth and nobody has lifted it and nobody has asked what is under it, and the lamp at the high end of them is lit at the fourth hour on every day of the week on which the light has gone off the wet slate of them.

Nobody thanked anybody and that scar is nine feet wide and about nine hundred yards long and it is six days old, and it is on the top of that shoulder from the ninth of the nine stones down to the place where the nine-foot line in the grass begins and the kerbed road starts, and it is not a fire and there is nothing burnt on it, and nobody has put it back and nobody in nine counties can, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a scar that does not close over in about three years is a scar and that about five have said that it is not going to close over at all.

Then four days off, and about nine hundred people along that made road did the ordinary part of a Thursday, and the ordinary part is a made road that goes up and comes down and is four inches of mud scrape at the ninth mile stone.

Nobody said anything and nine beasts went out through the gap at the low end of about nine hundred yards of stone wall at about the sixth hour and went up nine hundred yards of that lane and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back down at about the fifth hour, and nobody went with them, and that is about thirty-five drove to go up that lane since the second of Mudmonth, and about four hundred people in that market street have never watched one.

Eight of the nine yards of wall at the head of that made road are back in and the gap is about a yard long and about four feet high, and the stone is quarried two miles off on the low side and one man has been carrying it two miles in his own hands for two hundred and thirty-nine days, and the last yard is about nine feet of a course and he has not started it, and nobody in nine counties has asked him when he will and nobody is going to.

The brood in a walled yard half a mile below that wall is five frames where there were nine on the night of the eighth of Thawmonth, and that night is two hundred and sixty-three days back, and the fifth frame has been all brood since the twenty-seventh of Wolfmonth and no sixth has gone in, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a young man of about nineteen with five frames of brood in nine hives has about four weeks before he needs a sixth and five have said that he has had that about six weeks since the twentieth of Embermonth and about four have said that nobody in nine counties has asked him what he is going to do about the four weeks.

Nobody thanked him and the weigh-house weighed two loads of lime and a load of wool that morning, and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that Frostmonth is one day old and about ten sheets have come past that floor in it, and that Frostmonth is the month they come past clean, and that nobody has ever asked him what a sheet weighs, and four people in nine counties have said since that ten a day is about three hundred in a month of thirty and that three hundred is not four hundred and nine and five have said that four hundred and nine is a month this floor has not had since the middle of a Harvestmonth and nobody has ever asked him which month it was.

The inn at the high end was full that morning from about the fourth hour, four beds and four more on the floor above them, and no price went out on any board in that street because nothing has moved a price since the middle of Harvestmonth, and the woman of about fifty-one who keeps that house came out into it at about the fifth hour and stopped nine feet short of those boards and would not come nearer, and a chandler nine doors down put a cloth over a bench rather than write anything at all on it.

Nobody said anything and a man with a barrow of lime went that street nine times over that morning and put about two hundred pounds of it down at four doors and took it up again at two of them, and nobody stopped walking at any part of it, and the near wheel of a cart came off at the low end and the carter got it up again with a bar in about nine minutes, and two women at the third door argued about a bolt of cloth for about four minutes and then went into the shop and came out with it wrapped.

The room over that weigh-house was open because it is a Thursday, and the copyist of about twenty-six came down the thirteen stairs at the seventh hour and went back up, and four people in that lane saw her come down and five did not, and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled.

The nine stones at the end of that line are about the height of a man's knee and about as wide as two hands and the tops of them are all at the same height, and nobody in nine counties has found out why, and the man of thirty-two cleaned about four hundred yards of the grass off the top of two of the nine with a bit of stick on the fourteenth of Fallowmonth and has been up that shoulder twice since, both times with somebody standing on it, and nobody has asked him why he does it and nobody is going to.

About nine hundred yards of the nine-foot line in the grass is dead inside its width and standing outside it, and a line in grass that is not cut closes over in about three years and a line in grass that is cut dead does not close at all, and about four people in nine counties have said since that nine hundred yards of a line is the only thing in this county that has been made worse by being looked at and five have said that it has not been made worse and that nobody has been up there to see.

That gate has had four feet of ash and two stones across it since Mudmonth, and nine beasts a week have gone up through the gap at the low end of about nine hundred yards of wall and not one of them has gone through the gate in eleven years, and about four people in nine counties have said since that the gate a drove does not use is not a shut gate and five have said that a man of forty-one who lays nine hundred yards of wall and bars a gate nobody uses is a man who has built two things and joined them with nothing.

Nobody said anything and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel was at the high end of those boards on that Thursday and said nothing at all in that street, and he has said nine things out loud in this county in about four and a half years and said the ninth of them on the thirteenth of Rainmonth, and nobody asked him and nobody sent for him, and four people in that market street had worked out that a hundred and thirty-eight days of not saying anything is longer than the nine days it took the last finding to get nine hundred yards into four hundred mouths and five had not worked that out and will not.

Then the part of that Thursday that happened two miles above the head of that made road, and two people went up it in their own trade and neither of them was asked and neither of them was thanked.

Nobody thanked anybody and a man of about fifty-one and a woman of about forty-four came up that lane on the Wednesday before at about the sixth hour behind the drove and walked nine hundred yards of the nine-foot line in the grass from the low end to the ninth of the nine stones, and they stopped there, and they did not go past them, and nobody had sent for them and nobody had told them about anything, and about four people in nine counties have said since that they came up because the grass has gone off nine hundred yards of their line and five have said that they came up because they cut that grass and nobody else does.

Nobody said anything and the man of about fifty-one said it out loud, in the open, on that grass, to one person, in his own trade, and it was 55 words, and nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him.

“**We two cut that grass and the other seven have stock on the flat and stock does not need a boundary that is showing, and we came up because the grass has gone off nine hundred yards of our line and a boundary that is not showing is a boundary somebody will be asked about.**”

Nobody said anything and the woman of about forty-four said it out loud, in the open, on that grass, to one person, in her own trade, and it was 54 words, and nobody stopped her and nobody thanked her.

“**We have not been past the ninth of those stones and we are not going to and nobody has asked us to and we are two and not nine because nine would be a thing agreed and there has not been a thing agreed on that flat since before anybody living was on it.**”

Nobody said anything and about four people on that shoulder had heard two people give two different reasons for walking nine hundred yards of their own boundary and five had heard one of them, and nobody argued with either of them and nobody thanked either of them, and about four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time anybody has walked that line in about four and a half years except one man with a stick who cleaned the tops of two stones on the fourteenth of Fallowmonth, and five have said that the two of them have not asked anybody for anything and that nobody in nine counties has asked them for anything.

Nobody thanked him and then a question came into that shoulder at about the ninth hour from one person, and nobody stopped it, and it was asked kindly, and it was not about a road.

“**Is it on a map?**”

Nobody said anything and the man of thirty-two with a stick answered it out loud, in the open, on that grass, to two people, in his own trade, and it was 49 words, and nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him.

“**It is on the back of a roadkeeper's board in a cart shed and about nine people have seen it and it is nine hundred yards long and it is not on anything else in nine counties, and I am not going to ask that man for his board.**”

Nobody said anything and nobody said anything back to him and nobody thanked him, and about four people in nine counties have said since that this county has now got nine hundred yards of grass on the back of a board in a cart shed and about four hundred people in a market street who can go and look at it and five have said that a cart shed is about nine hundred paces from that hill and about four have said that the nine people who have seen the board are the same nine who saw the light and five have said that nine people can point at a thing on a map and that is the whole of what nine counties can do with it.

Nobody said anything and two carters of this county have measured the same wall two ways in one morning, and one of them said the far end of it is about knee high on a cart and the other said every yard of it is nine feet and he has put his hand on the coping at the far end, and nobody in nine counties has settled it and nobody is going to, and about four people in that market street have said since that a wall cannot be two heights and five have said that a wall can be anything at all to a man on a cart and that both of those carters have been paid for the same loads.

The two refusals in this county were given on the fourteenth and the twenty-sixth of Fallowmonth and neither has been argued with, and that is seventy-seven days and sixty-five days, and nobody in nine counties has asked either of those two men anything since and nobody is going to, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man has now been up that shoulder four times in about nine days and five have said that three of those four times he was in company with somebody and about four have said that the fourth time he was alone and that nobody sent for him.

Nobody thanked him and nothing about any of it is in a document, and there is no page in any room in nine counties with the first of Frostmonth on it, and the register in the room over that weigh-house holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled, and about four people in nine counties have said since that two people walked nine hundred yards of a boundary and said two reasons out loud and that neither reason went on any paper and five have said that a boundary is not a document and that nobody in nine counties has ever treated one as a document.

**And the cost of that Thursday is nine hundred yards of a boundary walked by the two people who cut it and not by anybody else, and about six days of a scar that is not going to close over, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
