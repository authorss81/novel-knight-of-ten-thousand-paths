# Chapter 0717

**Longlight the thirteenth, YR 322, a Thursday and a market morning four days off in that market town with no market on this made road, the ninth hour on a clear day on a shoulder with nine people on it, and a sound on the far side of nine stones.**

A Thursday is a market morning and about four hundred people are in that market street by the second hour and about two hundred of them stop what they are doing, and there is no market on that made road and there has never been one, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a drove goes out on a Thursday about four miles from a market that nobody on the drove will be in.

Nobody thanked anybody and the drove at the head of that made road went out at about the sixth hour on the Thursday as it goes out every Thursday, and went up about nine hundred yards of that lane through the gap at the low end of about nine hundred yards of stone wall and stood in the field with the stone wall round it, and nobody went with it, and nine people in nine counties know that and about five have ever watched it happen from the top of a shoulder about two miles above.

Nobody said anything and four people on that shoulder had heard the beasts arrive and about five had not, and the sound of about nine beasts coming up nine hundred yards of a drove road carries further than about nine beasts standing in a field do, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the only thing anybody at the top of that shoulder has heard from the bottom of it in about four and a half years and that it is a drove.

Then the ninth hour, on a clear day, and this is the whole of what happened at it.

Nobody thanked anybody and the weather over the seven days they had been on that shoulder had been clear and cold with a wind off the low side, and on the thirteenth of Longlight the wind had dropped at about the eighth hour and the sky over about nine hundred yards of flat ground beyond those nine stones was the colour of a wet slate, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a clear day is the only day on which a sound carries nine hundred yards of hill and that about five of the last seven have been clear.

Nobody said anything and there were nine people on that shoulder, which is five who had come up on the fifth of Longlight for five reasons in five trades, a man of thirty-two with a stick who is not one of the five, and three who had come up from the other side of nine stones on the ninth, and nine people in nine counties have said since that nine is the largest number of people that has ever been on that shoulder and that five have said that nine is the least number that could have been called a group.

Nobody said anything and the three from the other side were two households and a man of about fifty-four who keeps the far end of that lane, and they had come up again on the eleventh and had come up a third time on the thirteenth, and nine people in nine counties have said since that two households and a boundary keeper came nine hundred yards twice in four days on their own account and that nobody sent for them and five have said that a boundary keeper who has been asked nothing has come twice.

Nobody thanked him and the cold came up out of the ground on that shoulder at about the eleventh hour on the twelfth of Longlight the way it does, and every one of the nine was awake twice in the night and nobody said anything about it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a man who sleeps on a stone on a hill does not sleep and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the ordinary account of it and five have said that a person who has been on a hill for nine hours in Longlight wakes at the third hour and at the ninth and that this is a fact about the ground and not about the stones.

Nobody thanked anybody and the frost on the morning of the thirteenth was about a sixteenth of an inch on the tops of the nine stones and about an inch on the grass either side of them, and the woman of about thirty-nine who keeps the second gate from the top of that made road got up before the light and found that the tops of the nine stones were the only nine things on that shoulder with a white line on them, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the only mark the nine stones have ever made that anybody has been able to see.

Nobody thanked him and the man of thirty-eight who puts boards on the inside of gates has been up nine ladders a week for about nineteen years and his hands are the hands of a man who works in cold, and he was the one who noticed the frost on the tops and said nothing about it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a man who puts boards on gates in nine counties and a line of nine stones with a white line on them are about nine days apart in this county and that nobody has ever put them in the same sentence.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about twenty-seven who keeps eels in weirs about two days north of the causeway had the backs of his hands split at the second joint of two fingers and had been putting oakum and tar into the splits for about eleven days, and four people in nine counties have said since that a man who works in water all winter gets that in Thawmonth and not in Mudmonth and that it is the only figure about that man anybody in nine counties has.

Nobody thanked him and the man of thirty-two is about thirty-two and has a bad leg that is worse on a rise and better on a flat and worst of all on a wet flat, and there is no wet flat on that shoulder in Longlight, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the seven days a man of thirty-two spent on that shoulder is the longest continuous stretch of level ground anybody in nine counties has ever given him and five have said that the whole of what he came up here for can be said in one line and that the line has fifty-four words in it.

Nobody thanked anybody and the nine stones are about the height of a man's knee and about as wide as two hands and they are set across a line about nine feet wide in grass, and the tops of them are all at the same height along the top, which is the thing about them, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a man can put a straightedge across the tops of nine of them and have it lie flat and that nobody in nine counties has ever wanted to.

Nobody said anything and the man of about fifty-four who keeps the far end of that lane stood at about nine feet off the line of them with his back to about nine hundred yards of flat ground and looked at the tops of them for about as long as it takes to turn a cart, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a man who keeps a boundary looks at a boundary twice a year and that this was the second time this year and five have said that a man who keeps a boundary does not look at nine stones and that he has.

Nobody thanked anybody and then the sound came, and it was at about the ninth hour, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the ninth time a sound has been put on that shoulder and that the eighth of them was on the twelfth of Thawmonth at about the ninth hour and was the sound of one pair of wheels on a made surface.

Nobody said anything and this one was not that, and it was three strokes of a thing being struck on something hard, and it came from about four hundred yards off and went to about nine hundred yards off in about as long as it takes to turn a cart, and it did not come again.

Nobody said anything and four of the nine people on that shoulder had heard all three strokes and about five had heard the first and not the rest, and nobody said anything for about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over, and nine people in nine counties have said since that nobody has ever established what was struck against what and that this is now the second time in about four and a half years that a sound has come out of that direction and that the two of them are not the same sound.

Nobody thanked anybody and one of the five said it out loud, in the open, on that shoulder, to eight people, in his own trade, and it was fifty-five words, and he did not say it again that day.

“**A thing heard once is not a road and it is not a place and it is not a fact anybody can build on, and I say things out loud in a market street and I have said them for four and a half years and nobody has ever built anything on one of them.**”

Nobody said anything and four people on that shoulder had heard a man say out loud that a sound is not a road and about five had heard fifty-five words, and nobody agreed with him and nobody argued with him, and the man of thirty-four with a satchel stood at the fourth of the nine stones and looked out at about nine hundred yards of flat ground and said nothing else at all that day.

Nobody said anything and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that a person has said out loud in this county that a thing heard is not a thing known and that nobody agreed with him and nobody argued with him and five have said that the reason nobody argued with him is that a person who says a thing like that in the open has nothing anybody could correct him about.

Nobody said anything and then a person of about forty-four whose household keeps sheep asked a question out loud, in the open, on that shoulder, in her own trade, at about the tenth hour, and nobody stopped her.

“**And the three strokes on Thursday? Where did they come from?**”

Nobody said anything and four people on that shoulder had heard the question and five had not, and nobody answered it, and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel said one thing about it, out loud, in the open, and it was in his own trade and it was thirteen words.

“**It came from four hundred yards off and it went to nine hundred.**”

Nobody said anything and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the only fact anybody has about the sound on the other side of nine stones and that it is a fact about distance, and that four people on that shoulder had worked out that a man who says a thing is not a road is also the only man who will say how far away a sound was.

Nobody thanked anybody and then the five who had come up on the fifth of Longlight did what they had done for seven days, which was that the man of about fifty-two who keeps four miles of made road under contract walked the nine hundred yards of kerb and the man of about thirty-eight who puts boards on the inside of gates went to the first of the nine stones and stood there and the man of about twenty-seven who keeps eels in weirs about two days north of the causeway looked east at the broom that is not on yet and said nothing.

Nobody thanked her and the woman of about thirty-nine who keeps the second gate from the top of that made road sat on the ninth of the nine stones and looked back down the line at nine hundred yards of kerb going away toward the low side, and nine people in nine counties have said since that she has done that on nine of the last nine days and that nobody has asked her why and about four people have said that she is looking at the road she keeps from the other end.

Nobody thanked anybody and the broom is about nine acres on the east rise about four hundred yards up and about two hundred yards below the top of the hill, and it is not on, and it comes on about the fourth of Rainmonth, and there are about twenty-one days between the thirteenth of Longlight and the fourth of Rainmonth, and nine people in nine counties know that and about five have ever cut it.

Nobody said anything and four people on that shoulder had looked east at the broom every day for nine days and about five had not looked east at all, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a young man of about nineteen went up this hill on the twelfth of Thawmonth three weeks early for that broom and got nothing and wrote nothing down and said he would not come up again until the fourth of Rainmonth, and five have said that this is the first promise anybody in this county has ever kept about a hill.

Nobody thanked anybody and about nine yards of the nine hundred yards of wall at the head of that made road was still down where it came down on the twenty-eighth of Thawmonth, and the gap in it is about nine yards and about four feet high, and nine people in nine counties know that a man has been putting stone in it alone since the second of Mudmonth and five have ever asked him where the stone came from, and four people in nine counties have said since that a wall rebuilt at about two days a yard is shut before Hearthmonth and five have said that a gap in a wall is shut the day it is filled and not the day it is mended.

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street four days off had counted six people into it by the second hour and about five had not, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the second market morning on which six people have been somewhere in this county at the same time and that on the first of Longlight it was five reasons in a street and on the thirteenth of Longlight it is six people about four miles off.

Nobody said anything and nobody in that market street knew on the thirteenth of Longlight that three strokes of anything had been heard on a shoulder about two miles above a drove road, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the whole of what a sound is worth in this county and five have said that it is worth that one piece of weather carried nine hundred yards on a clear day and that nobody was in a position to hear it twice.

Nobody thanked anybody and the light came up off the coping on the wall at the head of that made road about nine hundred yards below and to the east of them, and the man of about forty-one who keeps a drove, a byre and about nine hundred yards of wall was not on that shoulder and was not at the gate and was in his own place, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a drove went out on the Thursday and went up nine hundred yards and stood in a field and came back and the man who keeps it did not go with it and that this has happened about four times since the second of Mudmonth.

Nobody said anything and the two households went back down the line in the grass at about the seventh hour and the man of about fifty-four who keeps the far end of that lane went with them as far as the stones and no further, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a man who keeps a boundary went back to it on the thirteenth of Longlight and that nobody in nine counties has ever heard him say what the boundary is for.

Nobody thanked anybody and the five slept on that shoulder again and the wind came off the low side about nine hundred yards of it after the light went, and nine people in nine counties have said since that there is no tree on that shoulder and that the wind on it in the dark is the reason nobody has ever slept on it and five have said that it is the reason anybody who has slept on it once has come back.

Nobody said anything and nobody crossed the nine stones and nothing on the other side of them was seen by anybody on this side of them, and a sound was heard out of that direction once in about four and a half years and once in about nine days, and four people in nine counties have said since that two sounds and thirty-one words and a rota of beasts is the whole of what nine counties has about the far side of nine stones, and five have said that this is more than this county had about anything at the start of Longlight.

Nobody thanked anybody and nobody wrote any of it down, and there is no page in any room in nine counties with the thirteenth of Longlight on it, and the register in the room over that weigh-house holds ten questions with no finding under any of them, and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the longest document anybody in this county possesses about that shoulder is a roll of hands dated YR 312 that is wrong about one man, and that five have said that this is the whole of what a record is for.
