# Chapter 0720

**Longlight the twenty-second, YR 322, a Saturday and a market morning, the second hour in the market street at the boards with about four hundred people in it and about two hundred of them stopped, the room over the weigh-house open, and two trades disagreeing in the open.**

A Saturday in that market town of about two thousand people is the loudest thing in nine counties and about four hundred people are in that street by the second hour and about two hundred of them stop what they are doing, and the proportion has not moved since the first of Embermonth, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the only figure in this county that anybody can check twice a week and has never once been written down.

Nobody thanked anybody and that market street runs east and west for about nine hundred paces from the gate at the low end of it to the inn at the high end, and the bank to the west of that town is higher than the street, and the light goes out of that street before the light goes out of the sky, which is the only thing anybody has ever been able to say about the light in it.

Nobody thanked him and the boards at the low end of it have been standing since the middle of Longlight the year before and have been repainted twice and are the colour of a wet slate, and the cloth over the bench at the low end of them has been on it since the middle of Hearthmonth and nobody has lifted it and nobody has asked what is under it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this county has three things it can look at from a market street, and that a wall four days off is one of them and that a bench under a cloth is one and that a set of boards with nothing on them is one, and five have said that the wall is the only one of the three anybody has done anything about.

Nobody said anything and four hundred people in that street had heard four things said out loud since the twelfth of Thawmonth, which is that about two miles of lane above a drove road is not made and turns twice, and that past the turns there is about nine hundred yards of road with kerbs on both sides and then the kerbs stop, and that after the kerbs stop it goes on in the grass as a line about nine feet wide for about nine hundred yards, and that there are nine stones set across it at the end about the height of a knee with the tops of them all at the same height, and nobody has written any of it down.

Nobody thanked anybody and three days ago six people came down two miles of unmade road off a shoulder of a hill and went five ways and said nothing to anybody about where they had been, and four hundred people in that street know that six people went up on the fifth of Longlight for five reasons in five trades and about five have worked out that four of them were trades they could name.

Nobody thanked anybody and four people in nine counties think the whole of this county's business is finished and about five think it is not, and neither of those two sets has ever spoken to the other and nobody in nine counties has asked either of them why, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the two sets have been the same size since the twenty-eighth of Thawmonth and that one of them is always in a market street.

Nobody thanked anybody and the badge-man's three clauses in nine hands have no copy and they govern closing a road and not opening one, and they are about a man doing a thing to a road and a paper does not do a thing to a road, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the third of them about people and roads says nothing about a number, and five have said that this has been said out loud in that street four times since the middle of Longlight the year before and that nobody has put it in a document.

Nobody thanked him and the room over that weigh-house was open because it is a Saturday and the copyist of about twenty-six came down the thirteen stairs at the seventh hour and went back up, and about four people in that lane saw her come down and about five did not, and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them, and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and the fourth ruled head is empty, and no fifth head is ruled, and none of that has moved in three weeks.

Nobody said anything and the hearing has not been called since the first of Goatmonth and does not sit, and its first of a month came and went on a Saturday with four hundred people in that street and not one of them at the boards, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is now the fifth first of a month in a row on which nothing has happened at it and that nobody has sent for the man who built it on any of the five.

Then the true thing about what a hearing is worth, and it is said in a trade.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about fifty-eight with a badge and no head came down the lane from the weigh-house at a slow walk with the bar against his leg and the strap of it over his shoulder and did not hurry, and he stopped four feet short of those boards and put his hand flat on the top rail, and the rail had a skin of white in it at about the seventh hour, and nine people in that market street have said since that a man who touches a thing he is about to speak at is about to speak carefully.

Nobody said anything and he said it out loud, in the open, to about four hundred people and to about nine of them, in his own trade, and it was forty-seven words, and he did not say it again that day.

“**A hearing is worth the paper it makes and this one has made nothing in four and a half years, and what it made is worth what the Marshal's hand makes of it, which is nothing, and that is the whole of what a hearing is worth.**”

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had heard a badge-man price a hearing in public and about five had heard forty-seven words, and nobody stopped him and nobody asked him what the paper was, and nine people in nine counties have said since that four people in that street have worked out that what he said about the paper and about a hand is the same sentence and about five have not.

Then the answer, in another trade, and they disagreed in the open.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up had a piece of blue wool over his forearm and he took it off before he answered, which nobody in that street has ever seen him do, and then he said it out loud, in the open, at the low end of those boards, in his own trade, and it was sixty-six words, and nobody stopped him.

“**Four hundred who have heard are not in the same place as four hundred who have not, and I sell cloth and I will say it in my trade. A person who has heard a thing can be found, and a person who has not heard a thing cannot be found, and there is no other difference between the two of them and there never was.**”

Nobody said anything and about two hundred people in that market street had heard a cloth seller say out loud that four hundred people are in a different position from four hundred other people and about nine of them had stopped what they were doing while he said it and about five had not.

Nobody said anything and the badge-man answered it out loud, in the open, in his own trade, and it was forty-seven words, and he did not raise his voice and nobody thanked him.

“**Four hundred who have heard cannot be asked, and the reason has not changed since the first of Goatmonth and it is that asking is making a head, and I have said it in this street and nobody has ever asked me what a head is for.**”

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about thirty-three who sells cloth three doors up still has nine names in his head from the middle of Fallowmonth and has still not written them down, and four people in that market street asked him for the list in the middle of Frostmonth and five did not, and he said the same thing on the twenty-second of Longlight that he said on the first of Mudmonth, which is that he is not going to write nine men down in a book and hand it to anybody, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the third refusal of nine names in a market street in this county and that all three were made by two people in two trades.

Nobody said anything and the two of them disagreed in the open for about as long as it takes to turn a cart, and nobody agreed with either of them and nobody took a side and nobody thanked anybody, and four people in that market street had worked out inside about nine seconds that the badge-man and the cloth seller were arguing about four hundred people and not about a hearing and about five had worked out that it was about both.

Nobody said anything and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that two trades in one market street have said out loud what the same four hundred people are worth and got two answers, and about four people have said that the badge-man is right and about five have said that the cloth seller is right, and that neither of those two sets has ever spoken to the other.

Nobody thanked anybody and then the middle of that morning happened the way the middle of every morning in that street happens, and about two hundred people went back to about nine trades, and the near wheel of a cart came off at the low end and the carter got it up again with a bar in about nine minutes and nobody stopped walking.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel asked a question out loud, in the open, at the high end of those boards, at about the fourth hour, and nobody stopped him.

“**Then what would the four hundred have to hear?**”

Nobody said anything and about four hundred people in that market street had heard the same question for the first time and about five had heard the word four hundred four times in one morning, and nobody answered it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the seventh question anybody in nine counties has asked about that lane and that it is the first one anybody has asked that is about four hundred people and not about a road.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel has said nothing in that market street for seventeen days and came back on the eighteenth and stood at the high end of those boards and said nothing, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the man who says the finding of things in this county has not said a finding for seventeen days and four people have said that this is what a finding costs and five have said that this is what a finding is.

Nobody said anything and four people in that market street had worked out that six people came off that shoulder and five had worked out that nothing came off that shoulder because nothing was ever put on it, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the first time in about four and a half years that a market street has been asked to work out which of two accounts of a thing is the account, and that nobody in that street is going to.

Nobody thanked anybody and the weigh-house weighed two loads of lime and a load of wool and the man who keeps it said out loud, to four people in and five out, that about four hundred and nine parish rolls come past his floor in a good month and that the last month was the best month that weigh-house has had since the middle of Harvestmonth, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the only good news anybody in nine counties has had in about a month and that it is about sheets.

Nobody said anything and the inn at the high end put a board out with the price of a bed on it in chalk and had four beds full, and the woman of about fifty-one who keeps that house came out into that street at about the fifth hour and stood about nine feet off the boards and did not come closer, and a chandler nine doors down put a cloth over a bench rather than write anything at all on it.

Nobody thanked anybody and the woman of about twenty-seven who keeps a shop two streets off was in that street and did the sum on the thirteenth of Thawmonth about a man and a roll of hands dated YR 312 and has not said anything about it since, and nine people in nine counties have said since that a paper which makes a man older makes him more use to somebody and that four people in nine counties have worked that out and five have not, and that the sentence has been said out loud twice in this county and both times by the man it is about.

Nobody thanked anybody and four hundred people in that street have heard four things said out loud since the twelfth of Thawmonth, and about four of them believe the four things and about five have never spoken to any of the other four, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is now the ninth first of a month in a row on which about four hundred people have heard something and acted on none of it, and about five have said that the hearing is not the thing and that this is the ninth month of it.

Nobody said anything and then the last of it, and it is at the sea end of that street, and nobody asked him anything and he said nothing at all.

Nobody thanked anybody and the man of thirty-two stood at the sea end of that market street with his stick in his right hand for the whole morning and nobody looked at him and four people in that street had worked out that he had been on that shoulder and about five had worked out nothing, and the coat he was in had about four inches of salt in the seams of the right sleeve from a causeway he crossed on the Wednesday, and nobody in that street has ever asked him about the coat.

Nobody said anything and there is a question, and it is the one he has, and he did not ask it, and four people in nine counties have said since that this is the second time in about four and a half years that a man of thirty-two has had a question in that street and said nothing, and about five have said that the first time was the first day of a year and that both of the times were on his own account.

Nobody said anything and the question is whether four hundred people who have all heard the same four things are in a different position from four hundred people who have not, and he worked that out at about the tenth hour of that morning standing at the sea end of that street with a stick in his right hand, and he did not say it out loud to anybody, and nine people in nine counties have said since that the reason he did not say it is that asking it would be sending, and that a man who asks a market street whether four hundred people are a thing would be making four hundred people into a thing and that this is the reason the badge-man said what he said at the fourth hour.

Nobody said anything and he did not ask it and nobody asked it for him and the boards were standing at the low end of that street with nothing on them, and nobody thanked anybody, and nine people in nine counties have said since that this is the sixth time in about four and a half years that this county has had a question it could put in a room and that this is the first time it has had one it could put in a street and that nobody in nine counties can call a street.

Nobody thanked anybody and the lamp at the high end of those boards was lit at the fourth hour and stood lit until the market was gone, and the cloth over the bench at the low end of them was on it and nothing lifted it and nobody asked what was under it, and about four hundred people went out of that market street into about nine trades and about nine people stayed at the boards.

**And the cost of that Saturday is four hundred people who have heard four things and are in a different position from four hundred who have not, and one question about them that was not asked because asking it would be sending, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
