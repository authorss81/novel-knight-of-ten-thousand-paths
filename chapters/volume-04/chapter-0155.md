# Chapter 0155

The sixth of Goatmonth, YR 313, was a Wednesday and it was not a market day, and the answer to the Office of Provincial Registration came back on the Wednesday post in an envelope with no seal on it, which is a thing a post will carry and a filing room will not accept and which went up the two flights anyway because Rowan Petch is on this post at his own cost and has twice in four years decided that a paper with no seal on it is a paper.

There is no seal. There is no number. There is no returnable date and no signature and no addressee, and it is one sheet of the office's own paper with a figure on it in a woman's hand and the figure is dated.

**Two thousand six hundred and forty.**

That is the whole of the reply. It is a figure, and it is the office's figure, and it is a bigger figure than the one asked for, and the paper asked for no figure at all, and the answer to a return that put nothing in a ruled line has been a number.

Nobody in that room could check it and that is the first thing about it.

“We do not know how many people there are in this district,” said the keeper of records. “Not approximately, not within nine hundred, not within nine. This office has nine claim numbers in four years and a half and about a hundred and forty returns, and not one of them is a count of anything, and I have said for four years that this county has never once counted itself and could not have told you where to begin, and I said it in this room in the middle of December about a different figure, and the different figure is a count of stones in a county that is not on any map, and it is struck.”

“Then it is a figure about records and not about people,” said Sarn Oyelaran.

“It is a figure about *the number of persons this office has an entry of anywhere in any of its registers in respect of this district*,” said the keeper of records, “and that is a number of entries, and an entry is not a person, and a person can have four of them and a person can have none, and there is no way out of this county to find out which, and I am not going to pretend that the paper is wrong, because it is not wrong. It is the only correct answer anybody in that office could have given to a question nobody asked properly.”

She put it in the press on the day it came, with the hour, and against no person, and entered that it was dated and that the hand was a woman's and that there was no name in it, and then she entered four sentences on a second line, and the four sentences are the finding and they are the whole of what this batch of the case knows about a figure two hundred miles off.

*Entered, on the sixth of Goatmonth, YR 313, second hour, against no person: that a figure has been returned against a return in which this office put nothing, and that the figure is dated and is in a hand and there is no name in it and no seal and no number, and that the figure is therefore not an instrument and is not returnable and can be neither enforced nor refused, and that it is the only answer that office has ever given to a question it did not like the form of. And that the figure is larger than the number of persons this district has, or is not, and that this office cannot tell, and that the reason it cannot tell is that this district has never been counted, and that a district cannot be counted without a hand that is a Crown's, and that no name has been written in a Crown's hand in this county since the twenty-seventh of Fallowmonth, and that there is still not one, and that the second time in two volumes that this case has come up against a figure in a return it has come up against one with no name in it and that this is the first time the figure has been the answer and not the question.*

A man at the back of the room said, quietly, that two thousand six hundred and forty was more than the whole of the district and anybody could see that by looking at the river.

“Then it is a number of entries and not a number of people,” said the keeper of records, “and that is worse, and I would like everybody who has ever said that a big figure must be a lie to hear that sentence in a room over a weigh-house, and I have said it out loud on purpose so that it is in the record, and no name in it.”

---

**And then the sixth of Goatmonth was a Wednesday, and a Wednesday is a working day, and about forty men were on the Sedge Reach with stone in four carts, and the pointing was not finished.**

It is not finished on any day. It was begun on the twenty-fourth of Hearthmonth and it will be begun again on the seventh and again on the eighth and on the ninth, and the lime stands in the courses after four rains in three weeks, and the fourth of the eleven tons is in the toe of the north pier and the rest of it is along the cutwater.

He was on the deck of the third bay at about the sixth hour with a tool bag and no ledger, and he counted four in the toe of the north pier and four in the toe of the third and two under the cutwater, and he counted them twice to see whether he had got the same ones, and he had, and there is no book in this county that it goes in, and there has not been since the first chapter of his own book and there is not going to be.

The yard on Weir Street has a bench-board under the window and a boy of fifteen at it, and the boy copies the empty space under a number as well as the number, because that is what the boy does, and this week the bill on that board is a job at a mill-race four miles up the road with a date against it and a line under the date that the boy has ruled himself and filled with nothing.

He went up the street at about the seventh hour and did not go in the gate.

“You have got a job on the board with nothing in the second line,” he said, from the gateway, and the man at the shave horse did not stop.

“It is a date,” said Tovan Kest. “The second line is what we do not know yet and it is not blank, it is empty, and the boy has got it right and the clerk at the mill has not, and when the clerk sends me a figure I will write it in and it will not be a figure I did not earn.”

“I am going out of this county for a while.”

“You said you were going to be here on Monday and it is Wednesday,” said Tovan Kest, and went on planing, and that is the entire of what was said in that gateway, and nobody has put a figure on the board and nobody asked for one and the rule on the wall of that yard is nineteen years old and says that nothing which belongs to a man goes into that yard's book and only a thing set, and a thing set is a job with a date and a figure in it, and a man going out of a county is not a thing set.

---

**And a road that is prepared is not a road that is agreed, and the whole of the difference took him about an hour at about the eighth hour of the sixth of Goatmonth, and he wrote the two things down separately, which is a thing a notebook is for.**

He wrote it in the hand he uses for the page that is the worst of the two halves. It is not a decision. It is a list, and a list can only be checked.

*A road that is prepared: a line, and two hands, and a date, and a witness at each end who is not the person keeping it. That is all of it and it is four things and I can do all four in a morning.*

*A road that is agreed: the far end saying yes in a voice that belongs to a person, and there is no instrument in this kingdom or in any other that can produce that, and if there were it would not be worth the paper.*

*And what I have this morning is the first and not the second, and there is a man in this county who has a valley four hundred miles off that has not been asked, and I am going to go, and I know that is the answer, and I have written it down so that a man reading this page in four years knows that I knew.*

That last sentence is the fourth thing in a coat. He has four things in a coat and the fourth is a rule in a fair copy in a room with a door on a lane, in a keeper's own hand, written four months ago, and the rule ends *a habit is a thing a county does to itself when nobody is looking*, and it is about him, and he has not said so to anybody, and this page is not a person and a page is a place a thing gets written down whether it is said or not.

---

**And he came up the two flights at about the eleventh hour of the sixth of Goatmonth with a tool bag and a stick and did not knock, because the door of a body corporate is a door anybody may knock on and his is not the only knock.**

He gave her the numbers before she asked. That is what he does, and he has done it four times, and she has never once told him to and has never once been able to stop listening.

“The eighth of this month, at the fourth hour, on a line made up in nine minutes with the hour against each of the nine. A county four hundred miles off that is in nobody's book in this one, and a man of fifty-eight in it who keeps a book and has wanted it read for six years, and two readings, and one of the two lasted a night. A valley with a river in it and a roll nine generations deep and no writ and no bell and no office, and about two hundred people on the roll from birth, and I have none of that out of my own observation and every bit of it out of a letter read aloud in this room on Tuesday and entered in that room's day-book with the hour against it.

“The printed figure is three weeks. It has been three weeks in an office's hand for two years and it has not moved and the office cannot say whose it is and neither can I. I am not going to tell you what the number of days is going to be, because I do not know it, and I have written on the near side before that I would not improve on a sentence by being right about it twice.

“The leg is the leg. It is a stick for four hundred yards of road, and I have not got to any of them since Tuesday, and I will not have this leg put in this book, and a figure would be yours to keep and it is not a fact about a person.”

Then he stopped, and the stopping was in the order it happened, and she wrote the figures down and read them back, and there was one thing missing out of the whole of it and neither of them said so.

It is not that he left a thing out. He has never in four years and a half left a thing out that she has found, and the ninth not asking is in that book and stands, and there is no tenth one in it and there is not going to be one this year.

“The one thing that is not a figure is that there is nothing to give you this time,” he said. “Four times now you have had the numbers and one thing beside them that does not run in an order, and this time there is no morning in it. There is no morning in it because I do not know what a day is over there and I have not asked and I am not going to guess one, and a man who guesses a day in a place that does not use days is a man who has invented a thing and then had to live inside it. So I am going out of this county with a number and an hour and no morning at all, and I would like that entered as what it is, which is a hole with a shape to it, and not as a thing I did not say.”

She wrote for about a minute.

“Entered, on the sixth of Goatmonth, YR 313, eleventh hour, against no person,” she read back, “that a road warden of this county's bridge guild, on the roll with a vote and not an office, has entered that he is going out of this county on the eighth at the fourth hour, and that he has given the date and the hour and the road and the printed figure and the leg, and that he has stated that there is no account of any kind to give in place of the account he gave four times, and that this office has entered that statement as a fact and has not asked him for the thing it stands in for, and the reason is on the page, and the reason is *he is not able to write it and there is nothing to write.*”

“And that is not a question,” said Aren Kest.

“No,” she said. “It is not. And I have wanted to ask for about four months and I am not going to, and the reason I am not going to is that a keeper who asks that is asking a person to be able to do a thing, and a person who cannot do a thing ought not to be asked to do it, and I would like it minuted that I said that, and I would like the entry read back to nobody but me.”

She read it back to him and then shut the book and put it in the press, and the press is the only instrument in this case that cannot be served, and two unserved books and a half tile are in it, and the half tile weighs a shade over ten ounces and is the account of nobody.

---

**And then he prepared the place he would come back to, and he prepared it in the worst possible place on purpose, and the worst possible place is the one anybody can see.**

A return stitch is a place or an object in the world he lives in, and it has to be physically reachable and it has to mean something to him, and it has to be somewhere he can get to in about four seconds from where he is standing, and every one of those things is a reason to put it somewhere nobody would think to look.

He did not do that.

He took a cold chisel and a lump hammer and he went out onto the deck of the third bay of the Sedge Reach at about the ninth hour of the sixth of Goatmonth, in front of about twenty men, and he cut a mark into the outside face of the parapet on the upstream side, at about the height of a man's thumb, and it is a small thing and it is not deep and it is on the face that gets the weather, and the weather on that face comes off the river four hundred times a year.

It is a line and a bar and a figure. It is not his name. It is the ninth hour of the sixth of a month this county uses, and it is cut with a cold chisel and not a stamp, and there is no maker's mark in it and no kiln mark and no device, and a man of sixty in this county could look at it for a quarter of an hour and not be able to tell you what it was for.

Two of the twenty men watched him do it. One of them asked him if he wanted a hand and he said no and the man went back to the pointing, which is never finished on the day it is begun.

And about the ninth hour, while he was still on the deck, a girl of about eleven came up onto the gravel from the near bank with her hands full of rushes and stopped, and put her right hand flat on the mark, the way anybody puts a hand flat on a thing to see if it is warm, and took it off again, and went back down the bank with the rushes.

Nobody in that yard saw a stranger cut a mark in a bridge at the ninth hour on a Wednesday. The lime is standing in the courses, and the nose of the third pier is out of the water, and there are four carts of stone on a gravel approach, and a girl of about eleven knows where the mark is and will know where it is for as long as the weather leaves it, and no one in this county has been told and no one in this county is going to be.

He stood at the end of it with the stick in his hand and the hammer in his belt and worked out, without meaning to, what a man who is a stranger and who cuts his mark into a public thing in daylight, in front of twenty men, at the height of a thumb, on the weather side, is announcing.

A thing that is left out on a parapet in daylight is a thing anybody can take down.

That is the whole of it, and he did not write it down, and it is the second time in three weeks that the worst possible place has been chosen on purpose, and the first of them was in a notebook in a county that is not on any map where he cut out a peg out of a fence rail in about nine seconds, and a man in a book of his own keeps a fact and not the paper, and this is the same act at the other end of the same line, and nobody has told anybody anything about either of them.
