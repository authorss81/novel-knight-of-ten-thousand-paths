# Chapter 0199

**The twentieth of Harvestmonth, YR 314, a Monday, and not a market day, and the fourth hour, and a man of thirty-two came up in the third course from the low end of the fourth bay of the Sedge Reach with a limp in his right leg and a mark in the coping of the first bay four yards behind him that he had cut in June, and the stone under his left hand had a line in it, nine inches long, that had not been there in the spring.**

There were forty men on that reach and the road was open and the tide was under the wharf four hundred yards downstream, and a man of forty-seven on the roll of the bridge guild with a vote and not an office was standing in the fourth bay with his back to the tide and he was the only person in that yard who had ever watched anybody come up out of a stone, and what he saw, and what he said afterwards, was this.

“He came up out of the joint between the third and the fourth course, and he came up out of it the way a man comes up out of a river, which is not a thing a person does, and his hand was on the stone before the rest of him was out of it, and he put his hand flat on the stone on either side of the line, the way you put a hand flat on a thing to see whether it is warm, and the stone was the same temperature on both sides of it, and he took his hand off.

“**And he looked at it once. And he did not put a rule on it, and I have been in this trade nineteen years and I know what a man with a rule does, and he did not do it, and I have not asked him why and I am not going to.**”

The witness at that end was the man of forty-seven and he was not the person keeping the reach, and there is no roll in this county that says he was, and nobody asked him to stand there and he was standing there because about forty men were on a reach at the fourth hour of a Monday and that is where a man of forty-seven who is on the roll stands.

And then he went up the bank. He gets up onto a deck off the gravel in three moves instead of two and it is a thing nobody in that yard can see, and on that Monday about eleven men saw it and about four of them have said that the third move is not a get-up, it is a put-down, and one of those four is a mason of fifty and a half who has been cutting stone for thirty-one years.

**And at about the sixth hour he went back along the reach to the first bay and put his hand flat on the mark in the coping on the upstream side, at the height of a thumb, and it was cold, and it was the same mark, cut with the same chisel, and he did not chip it and he did not say one word about it to anybody, and there is no way in this county to find out what day is in it, and the fourth yard of a stone course with a line in it is forty yards from it and the two of them are the only two things on that reach that nobody in that town can account for.**

---

**The fever took him that night and the six days after it and it broke on the seventh, and it was a week, and it is a week because the stage he came back at is a stage that takes a week, and nobody in this county knew that except the man himself and a woman of twenty-nine in a room over a weigh-house with a door on a lane, and neither of them said so out loud.**

There are three days of it in a book in Volume 01 and there is a stage that takes seven and there is a stage further on that takes a month of visible illness, and the seven is what he got, and the seven broke on the seventh morning, and he was not out of the house again until the second day after that.

And three things happened in the seven days that were not the fever, and all three of them are the price, and he wrote none of them down that week and all three of them are on the page.

**The first is that a man of twenty-five who owns the crews and the yard on Weir Street, four hundred yards up a road, held his tools for seven days, and nobody asked him to, and he has not said that he held them.**

He came down at about the seventh hour on the first day with a barrow and took the two boxes out of the room over the cooper's and carried them up to the yard and put them on the bench-board, and a boy of fifteen copied them, and the man's rule is that nothing that belongs to a man goes in that yard's book and only a thing set, and he wrote nothing down and put two boxes on a bench-board and left them there for seven days, and on the eighth day he carried them back down and put them in the room and said, “Your barrow is out of true and I have not looked at it because it is not mine to look at,” and went up the road, and he has not come down the road since, and that is everything that happened between them in seven days, and the word was not used and the word has not been used in this county since the first of the year before last.

**The second is that a man of twenty-five ran his errands for seven days, and the errands were nine of them and about four of them were for somebody else, and one of them was for a woman of about seventy in Weir Street who has been in Merefen since before there was a bell foundry in it, and she is the aunt, and he told her where he was on the second day and did not tell her anything else, and she asked him on the fourth day what the place was called and he said it was called a valley and he did not know the name of it and had not asked.**

That is everything a family in this county was told in seven days, and about four people in Weir Street have said since that a man of twenty-five went into a house on the second day and told a woman of about seventy where his brother was and that he did not say one word about how long for, and one of those four is the cooper whose rooms those are and who has not said anything about it and is not going to.

**And the third is that a carrier of about fifty with a waggon of his own and a firm of two came down two flights of a stair in a hall over a wharf on the fourth of the seven and put a page of a waggon-book on a table and said he was going to have to tell the bench that the man he had described to them in the fourth hour of the fourth of Harvestmonth was in the town.**

Nobody in that hall had asked. He came on his own. And the reason he came is in about four sentences and it is the reason the last six weeks of this case is the shape it is, and it is not a moral.

“I have been up that pass twice in eleven weeks and I told this bench on a Saturday that there is no road and no gate and no cart and about four hundred people who have never once wanted to be anywhere, and I put a figure on a page and I asked this bench for thirty-one miles of road and the bench said no in about a minute and a half over a chest with two marks in it, and the bench was right and I said so before I said anything else.

“**And a man came down this morning who was in that valley, and he is in this county, and he has been in it about six months, and nobody in this hall knew, and that is not anybody's fault, and it is also the reason the sixth thing that was asked in this hall was not the fifth, and if I had been told on that Saturday I would have said the name of the county and I did not, because I did not know it.**”

That is a document road of paper carrying nobody and a man on a deck and a man in a valley four hundred miles off, and the two ends of it are four hundred miles apart and neither of them is holding the other, and the reader who wants the two of them in one sentence is a reader who is going to be disappointed in a few years.

---

**And on the second of the seven there was a whole day in a room over a weigh-house with a door on a lane in which a man of thirty-two with a fever was not, and the second copyist of that office was in it from the second hour to the fourth and then again from the sixth to the ninth, and the ordinary post of a Tuesday came up the two flights and went down the two flights and a thing that had been on that table since the eighth of Harvestmonth was opened at about the third hour.**

It is a sheet of good paper and it is about the size of a broadside and it came in the ordinary post with nine other things from a county four hundred miles off, and this office has nine such things on a second shelf with a piece of tarred hemp through it, and it has never opened one of them and has never written to a person in that county, and there is no instrument in this kingdom that can make a county answer a question that is not one.

“You open it,” said the keeper of records.

“No,” said the second copyist, who is thirty and is on a warrant that is not in the press and has wanted a post with her name in the day-book since the summer before last. “**It is the eleventh and it goes in a bundle and the bundle has a piece of tarred hemp through it, and the day I open the eleventh one is the day this office is a body corporate that goes through a county's post, and I have not got a post with my name in the day-book and I am not going to be the woman who did that.**”

The keeper of records opened it herself, at the third hour, and read it out in a room of two, and it is a set of terms, and no part of the terms describes what the thing is, and that is in the document and not in a reader's imagination.

*That a hand be given to a man in this county, being a road warden with a vote and not an office, and a room, and a house, and an allowance of four shillings the week, and a clerk.*

*That the eastern paths be closed by the man so appointed, and by nobody else, and for so long as he holds the appointment, and that no other office is to be given to the man so appointed for the term of the appointment.*

*That the county of nine hundred and forty-one hearths be entered in the register of the office making the appointment, and that the entry be made by number and not by name, and that the number be not given to the man so appointed.*

*That no question be asked of the man so appointed about the object of the appointment, in the county of nine hundred and forty-one hearths or in this one, at any time, by anybody.*

**“There is nothing in it about the thing,” said the second copyist. “Four things in it are given, and one thing is not to be done, and the thing that is not to be done is written in nine words, and it is the only sentence in the whole instrument that anybody could act on, and everything else in this is a room and a house and four shillings.”**

“And nobody has asked us a question,” said the keeper of records, “in four years and a half. That is the fourth instrument in this case in four years and a half that has arrived with no question attached to it, and the other three are a count, a strip of cut paper, and a resolution nineteen lines long that this office agreed eighteen months ago to hear a delegation it then resolved not to send for.”

“And we are going to answer it,” said the second copyist.

“**We are going to do what this office has done four years and a half with every one of them, which is to hold it. The county of nine hundred and forty-one hearths is already in this office's own register and has been for four years and a half, and nobody in this county was told it was there, and it is in there as a company with a door and a number and the capacity to sue and to be sued, and the company is nine jars and a ditch.**”

And she said, and it is the fourth thing she has entered in this office in nine weeks and she has entered nothing in nine years, “I am not going to guess at a document. A document that does not say what a thing is for cannot be produced against anybody, and this one does not say, and the not saying is the most careful thing in it, and I have had a hand in about four hundred documents in four years and a half and I have not met one before that was careful in the other direction.”

---

**And he wrote the refusal on the fourth of the seven, in his own room, in his own hand, on four sheets, and it is the second refusal in a document in this case in two volumes and neither party knows the other exists.**

*That the offer of a hand, a room, a house, an allowance of four shillings the week, and a clerk, made in a sheet of good paper in the ordinary post of this county, is refused.*

*That the eastern paths are not to be closed by the man to whom it was made, and that the closing of them is not his to do, and that he has no standing in this county or in any other to do it, and that the office which has made the offer is not told here in what it has got wrong, because this office has not been asked.*

*That the number of persons the appointment has been made out to is not on the sheet, has not been asked for in a return, and is not going to be supplied by this county, and that a document which makes a person out of a class of persons without giving the number of them is a document this office cannot hold and is not going to hold.*

*That there is no thanks in it, and that the offer did not require any, and that a thing which does not require thanks does not get any.*

**And the day and the hour are two things and go in as two things with the rule between them, and the reason is the second rule on the wall of that room in a fair copy in a keeper's own hand and it is nineteen years old, and he did not ask her to break it and she did not offer to keep it for him.**

She copied it in about twenty minutes and it is slower than the writing and not better, and that has been the practice of that office since the first year. And when she had done it the two of them read all of it back, and the day and the hour were read back as two things, and she said the third of the four things out loud twice because it is the load-bearing clause and there is no number in it and there is no name in it and it is a sheet in an ordinary county's own form in a county where no name has been written in any Crown's hand since a day at the end of a month it keeps, and this volume has never heard of that month.

**And then she made him say three things, and she did it in a room of two with a lamp on and the door on the lane shut, and it took nine minutes, and none of the three was an apology and neither of them said that either of them had been wrong, and the nine not askings in the day-book of that office stand at nine and there was not going to be a tenth and there was not one.**

“Your leg,” she said.

“It is the same leg it was in June and it does not get up a bank any better than it did and it is not going to.”

“That is not what I asked and you know it is not what I asked.” She had the finding of the eighth of Harvestmonth in front of her and she had the third sheet of it, and the fourth line of the third sheet was in no column and against no person. “**Say the three things. A leg, a seam, and about two hundred people who are going to be somewhere else in a year. I have spent nine weeks writing down what a body corporate owes for a hearing against nobody and I cannot have one line of it that is about a man who has been in a valley for seven months and came back on Monday morning and went to work in the afternoon.**”

He said the leg. He said that a line in a stone course in the fourth bay of the reach was nine inches long and had not been there in the spring and he had looked at it once and had not put a rule on it and did not know what it was, and that he had been told by a man with a level in a hall four years ago in in nine seconds on a Friday what a figure turns into, and that he was not going to be a man who put a figure in a book about a thing he had looked at twice.

He said the two hundred, and he said it in about four sentences: that about two hundred people of the ninth generation were in a book from the day they were born and had been asked once and had said yes once and had said it in four words on a morning in the ninth week of eleven, and that the first three families out were the three that had said no, and that about two hundred people were going to be somewhere else in a year, and that he had not asked any of them to be anywhere and that two hundred yards of the best ground that valley ever had went to a slope in nine days and four roofs came in and a mill went.

**And the leg and the seam and the two hundred people are the price of the thing that is in the room over the weigh-house, and they are said in a room by the two of them and neither of them has said one word about the morning and neither of them is going to, and the breach is open and it is not repaired and it is not repaired in a document and the not asking is not entered, and the third of the three rules on the wall of that room ends in a sentence about a habit that a place does to itself when nobody is looking and it is about him and she wrote it and he knows it is about him and neither of them has ever said so and neither of them said it in the nine minutes.**

“That is three,” she said.

“It is three.”

“And it is not an apology and I did not ask for one and there is not one in this room to be had, and if anybody in this county including you thinks a cost said out loud in a room of two is a thing that has been paid, then I have made a mistake of about four years and a half and I would like to know about it now and not in nine years.”

“Not that I know of.”

“Right,” said Mara Vey. “Then it is not a cure and it is not a settlement and it is not the end of anything, and I am going back to the room over the weigh-house at the eighth hour and I am going to be there tomorrow and the day after, and you are going to be on that reach in the morning because that is where your hands are and they are the only two things you have got that anybody in this county can check.

“**And I am not going to walk up that road with you, and it is not a punishment and it is not a test, and the reason is that a road warden with a vote and not an office who walks up four hundred yards with a keeper of records in front of a town of four thousand people is a thing this county will have an opinion about inside a week, and the opinion will be about me, and I have four years and a half of a body corporate that answers to nobody and I am not going to lose it to an opinion about a man with a limp.**”

**And the refusal went out in the ordinary post on the fifth of the seven, in a bag, and it went up two flights and down two flights and out of a door on a lane, and a copy went onto a shelf with the number of the office against it and nothing against a person, and the word eastern is in a document in a bag and in a file in a county four hundred miles from the county it was made in, and neither party has ever heard of the other one, and the office that made the offer does not know yet that a body corporate in this county exists.**
