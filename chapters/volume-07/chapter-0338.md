# Chapter 0338

**The thirtieth day of Thawmonth, YR 317, a Sunday, and not a market day, and the second hour and the fourth hour and the seventh hour, in a town of about four thousand people on a river, where the market is shut and about two hundred of the four thousand are out of their houses, and a lane off the north end of that market has nine doors in it and a wagon arch over it and a weigh-house at the end of it with a numbered door at the top of the stair, and about four thousand people in that town do not know that a sheet of that office's own printed series went up that lane in a bag at the second hour of that Sunday and went back down it in the same bag at the seventh.**

The tide was under the wharf and had been since the tenth hour of the day before and it went back at about the third. A mill turned at the low end of the town the way a mill turns. Nine people went across the four hundred yards of that shut market and nine more went across at the fourth hour, and about nine men were in a field a mile off hedging a ditch and came in at the wet hour.

**And a bundle came up that lane in the ordinary post at about the second hour, in a bag with a mill and some oil and a good deal else, and a sheet of that office's own printed series was in it, and about nine hundred people in a town two hundred miles off know that that series goes out four hundred times a month and nobody in the county it went to was told that it had gone to it.**

Six ruled spaces. Five heads standing over five of them in that office's own setting, and the sixth the same width as the other five with nothing at all over it. About four of the six are ruled for a figure. The third takes a person by his trade and not by that person's name, and the fourth is headed *Keeper* and takes a person the same way, and the fifth says *How often*. **And nowhere on the whole of one of them, in any of the six spaces, at the head or the foot or between two lines, is the name of a person.**

The first column had a place in it, written the way that office writes a place, which is a set of market towns. And this county is the place. The second column had a figure in it, and the head over the second column says *Date*, and the figure is a figure of persons and it is the figure that has been standing against this county on that sheet since the autumn, and nobody in this county can check it and nobody in this county has been asked to show cause against it.

**And the fourth column had a keeper against it, and the keeper was a trade, and it was the same trade that is standing in the fourth column of the copy that is at the bottom of a press in the corner of that room, and a man in that room in that lane have known that there are two copies of a figure of persons standing against this county in one room and neither of them can be carried out by the office and one of them can be carried out by anybody who walks up that lane.**

---

**And the register is on that table at seven entries, all seven of them questions, not one of them a finding, the fifth of them about four months old and the last two of them both dated in the same month, and she looked down the seven of them for a while and did not read them out, and nobody in that room has ever asked her to read them out and nobody is going to.**

The seven are all of them opening with a set of market towns put down the way that office puts a place, and that is the whole of what a set of market towns is when it is the first four words of a line in a book: a way of naming a place so that a clerk two hundred miles off and a woman at the end of a lane are writing about the same ground. A body corporate with a door and a number can be sued over a finding and cannot be sued over a question, and she has entered seven questions in four years and a half and answered none of them, and about two people in that lane have known that the seventh of the seven is the only one she has ever acted on and that acting on it put a sheet in a bag.

**And the day-book beside that register is a book about nine inches by six and it stands at nine entries and every one of the nine is a thing that was not asked, and nothing in this kingdom can be served out of that book, and it was not opened on that Sunday, and it is not going to get a tenth, and a tenth would not be a gift if one came, and nine people on that floor in that lane have known that the only book in that county which cannot be produced against anybody is a book nobody can be served out of, and that the reason it cannot be served out of is the same reason it cannot be copied, and that nobody in that county has ever worked out that those are the same reason.**

**And the shelf beside those two books holds four papers and the press holds a fifth, and a woman of twenty-nine and the woman of about thirty with her have both counted those four more than once and neither of them has ever counted them out loud. The first is the paper this county was entered on, with a head over the figure of persons that says a day. The second is a true copy of the register in a second hand, signed at the foot with a trade. The third came back up that lane with this county's set of market towns in the first space and the second space empty. The fourth left that room two months ago with a place in the first, a trade in the third, and four spaces carrying nothing at all, and it is in the ordinary post and has been for about two months and nobody in this county has asked how long that takes.**

She read it in about four seconds. It took her a further minute and a half to decide what to do with it, and what she decided was to put it back in the bag, and about four people in that lane have known that a woman of twenty-nine looked at a sheet with a figure of persons standing against her county for about a minute and a half and then put it in a bag and tied the bag, and that the minute and a half is the only part of that Sunday anybody in that county would have called a decision.

“**It came up in the post,**” the second copyist said.

“**It came up in the post,**” the keeper of records said.

“**It is a return.**”

“**It is a return and it is the second one of that figure anybody has sent this county and the first one is on a shelf six feet away,**” the keeper of records said, and about two people in that lane have known that a woman of twenty-nine said the true thing about a document in about eleven seconds and that the true thing was that there are two of them, and that the county cannot refuse either and cannot check either and has been asked to show cause against neither.

“**Then what are you doing with it.**”

“**I am putting it back in the bag,**” the keeper of records said, and two people in that town know that a body corporate which has spent four years and a half refusing to be a body corporate has decided that a document it cannot answer is a document it should not hold, and that a county which hands a paper back is a place a paper goes through, and that about four thousand people live in that town and not one of them has been told that the county has become that.

**And the post is not a market and goes on days the market does not go on, and that is the whole of the reason it went out that evening, and the bag went out of that lane at about the seventh hour of a Sunday in a hand that is not the office's.**

She tied it herself. About four people in that lane have known that a woman of twenty-nine tied a bag with a figure of persons standing against her county in it and carried it out to the end of the lane and gave it to whoever was in the lane at the time, and that she did not look at the man she gave it to, and that about four thousand people live in that town and she is one of about two of them who know what was in that bag and the other one is the woman who made the fair copy of the register and does not know there was a bag.

“**You could leave it,**” the second copyist said, at the seventh hour, from the bottom of four steps.

“**I could,**” the keeper of records said.

“**Then it stays in the county.**”

“**Then it stays in the county in a room with a press in it that anybody at all can open,**” the keeper of records said, and about two people in that lane have known that a woman of twenty-nine gave a second reason for a thing she had already done in about eleven words and that the second reason is the true one and that the first one she gave was not, and that a man at that stone in that building have known that a body corporate which has answered a figure with nothing has now worked out that the only safe place for a document is a bag somebody else is carrying, and that a county which has arrived at that has stopped being a county and become a road, and that nobody in that county has said the word road about this office in four years and a half.

---

**And the ground is a set of market towns, and the sheet had this county's set of market towns at one end of it, and about nine people in that lane have known that a place in the first column of a printed series is a set of market towns and a set of market towns is ground, and that the ground is nine feet under the floor of a market four hundred yards long and a lane off the north end of it and a reach four hundred yards down another lane, and that the sheet went back down that lane in a bag without any of it being asked anything.**

The four hundred yards of that market are stone set in clay and there are about nine hundred stones in them that a person can name and nobody has ever tried, and the ground under them is a fen and about four feet down in it there is a thing nobody in that county knows the shape of. About forty men were on that reach that day with the road open over it and the salt gone off the first span about a month before, and about two hundred people were standing on nine hundred stones in a town of about four thousand and none of them were standing on the six squares of ground that the first column of a printed series names, and about two people in that lane have known that a sheet which cannot name a person can name four squares of a market and about nine feet of a lane and a river, and that what it cannot name is the one thing the sheet is for, and that this is not a thing anybody in that county has worked out and is not going to.

**And the man who writes down what goes up the channel road and what comes down it wrote one line that day, and the tide went back at about the third hour and drew the stones of that market clean for about two feet on each side, and about nine people walked across four hundred yards of it between the second hour and the fourth and the count of how many stones they each put a foot on is not a figure anybody keeps, and there is no form in that county that a count of stones could be entered on.**

On that Sunday the whole of that town went through it alone. Two hundred people came out of their houses and nine hundred stayed in them. Nine men walked in off a mile of fields at the wet hour. A mill turned where a mill turns. At the seventh hour a bag was carried out of a lane by somebody whose hand is not the office's, and there was nobody at the end of the lane to see it go and nobody behind it.

**And on a sheet about nine inches by six under the fourth step of an outside stair at the north end of that market there are nine marks under a figure, and they were nine marks before that Sunday and they are nine marks now, and what they count is the number of times that room has been asked something, and a number like that is not money and is not people and is added to nothing anybody keeps, and in the second space of a printed sheet there is a figure of persons standing against this county that went back down that lane in a bag five hours ago and is on its way to somebody now, and this county cannot refuse that figure and cannot check it and was never asked to show cause against it, and about four thousand people live in that town and not one of them stood in that lane.**
