# Chapter 0322

**The twenty-first day of Hearthmonth, YR 316, a Monday, and not a market day in a county with a market, and the second hour and the fourth hour and the seventh hour, at the top of a lane in a valley four hundred miles from any county that is on a map, in a room over a cross with twenty-nine benches bolted down in four rows and a number cut in the end of every one of them and a hundred and forty cut in the end of the one at the back of the right-hand row, and about four hundred people in that valley of whom about nine had wanted a thing since before a season turned and had said so out loud in that room for the first time that day.**

The twenty-nine benches are bolted to boards and the boards are bolted to the boards of that floor, and a number is cut in the end of every one of them and the numbers run from one to twenty-nine in the order they were set out, and the one at the back of the right-hand row has a hundred and forty in the end of it instead of a number. Nobody in that valley has ever worked out who cut that figure or what it is a count of, and both of those questions have been asked in that valley and neither of them has been asked in that room, and a room that has never been asked anything is not a room anybody brings a question to.

Against the far wall there is a press with a door and a bar and no shelf in it that anybody looks at, and a man of thirty-four put a thing into it on the fourth morning of four days and barred it and it is still barred, and it stands about six feet from where nine people were sitting. The thing in it is a copy of a book of nine spreads, and the book it was copied from is under a trestle six feet the other way, and there are two of that book in that room and only one of them is a thing anybody in that valley can name, and a man of fifty-eight has carried the one that can be named up that lane himself every year since he was seventeen.

---

Nothing has ever been put to that room. Not once, and not twice, and not in a hundred and forty years, and the whole of what is known about it inside that valley is the number of benches and the width of the aisle. About four people in that valley have worked out since that the only way to tell a room from a bench is to wait for it to be asked the same thing a second time, and that a room which has never been asked the same thing twice is a bench that nobody has looked at, and that about four hundred people have walked up that lane for a hundred and forty years and have never once put anything to it.

**And it was asked something for the first time at about the second hour, and the asking was done in the ordinary way by a man who had waited about a month, and nobody in that room had put it to a vote and no vote was taken and there is no line in that room for a vote.**

“**I am asking it because I have to stand here and say it,**” the man of thirty-four said. “**Four of my sixteen have nowhere to work this season and a trough with a lid on it has been at the low end of this valley for about four months, and nine of us in this room have known about it for about four months, and I have found out who the nine are by asking one question in a yard, and I am not going to sit on it. I am asking this room whether a valley of about four hundred people will let nine men in a shed pour something into a trough and four hundred people drink it, and if the answer is no then the nine men will be told no by somebody who is not me.**”

About two hundred people were at the back of that room and about thirty were standing in it and about nine were on the benches, and about two hundred of the two hundred had come up that lane for the asking and about nine of them had come for a covered vessel, and the four feet of aisle between the two middle rows was empty, and the covered vessel was standing on the boards at the mouth of it where a boy with a barrow had set it down five days before and nobody had moved it.

---

**And a man of forty stood up off the third bench on the left, and his name is Hoben Ryle, and he has lived at a steading up the water for about as long as anybody can remember, and he is the man who said at the top of that lane five days ago that he had had some, and about nine people in that room had known for about four months that he had, and about two hundred people at the back of that room found it out that morning.**

“**I have forgotten my wife twice this year, I took what they offered, and I would take it again,**” he said.

It is nineteen words, and it foots at nineteen, and it is the whole of what he said in that room for the first time in two years, and nobody improved on it and nobody has said it since in that valley.

And after those nineteen words he said that he is not the one who will stand up in that room and say a valley of about four hundred people ought not to be allowed a thing that works, and that was not in the nineteen words and he said it after them and about nine of the thirty people in that room heard both halves and about two hundred at the back heard neither.

**And nobody thanked him for saying it and nobody said he was right and nobody asked him a second question, and a man of forty who has been the one person in that valley to say a thing in that room for two years was not asked a single thing about it, and about four people in that room have said since that a man who had wanted a thing for about four months and had not said so in a room got nine sentences of silence in about nine seconds and went back to the third bench on the left, and that the silence is the ordinary outcome of a person who has done a thing other people have not done yet.**

---

**And then a woman of thirty-four stood up, and she is the one who goes.**

She has gone up about four hundred miles of lane and down it again about nine times a season since the fever came into that valley, and she has gone about nine times for a man of fifty-eight who wanted a thing read out loud and about nine times for a woman who forgot a name and got it back, and she has been asked for a clear about nine times in about four months by people who could not come down to the low gate themselves, and she has not given any of them any, and about four people in that valley have been saying since that a woman who is sent for when something has been forgotten has been asked nine times for the thing that stops it and has said no nine times, and that not one of the nine asked her why.

Her want is her own and it is not printed and it is not about the man of forty and it has nothing to do with the trough.

“**It takes the reasons and it does not bring them back,**” she said.

It is eleven words and it foots at eleven, and it is the answer of the nine, and the reason in it is about the reasons and not about the man who had had some, and about two people in that room have said since that a room of nine people in a valley of about four hundred people refused a thing that works in eleven words and that the reason it gave was a reason and not a figure, and that a reason can be argued with on the spot by about four hundred people and a figure cannot, and that nobody in that room argued with it.

Nobody improved on it. The man of thirty-four did not say that four of his sixteen had nowhere to work. Marek Ollen, who keeps the roll of who is in that valley, did not say that the nine ruled days in it are a thing no copy has ever shown anybody. The man of forty did not say that he had forgotten his wife twice and would do it again. The woman of thirty-four did not say it a second time in a different key, and nobody asked her what else she had thought about it, and about two people in that room have been saying since that the shortest answer anybody has ever given in that room is the one that has been asked anywhere else in that valley most often.

---

**And in the fourth hour one of the nine who had wanted it asked a question, and it was about the thing and not about the room, and it is the last question that room was asked that day and nobody answered it well.**

A woman of twenty-eight at a steading up the water had a mother in that valley who has forgotten her twice since the water turned and had her back once.

“**If it is me you are all talking about,**” she said, “**then what happens to the ones it does not reach.**”

“**Nothing has been said about that,**” the man of forty said, from the third bench on the left.

“**You have had some.**”

“**I have had some and I will have some again and I do not know what it did to a person it did not reach, and I have not asked, and I am not going to ask in front of two hundred people who did not ask.**”

“**Then you are asking nine people to let nine men pour a thing on the word of a man who will not say what it is.**”

“**I am asking nine people to take a thing or not to take it, and I have not said what it is, and I have said what it did to me, and those are the two things I have got, and one of them is mine.**”

And about two hundred people at the back of that room heard two people say out loud that neither of them knew, and about two people in that room have said since that a room of nine refused a thing in eleven words and was then asked a harder question than the one it refused, and that the harder question was put by a person who had not wanted the thing at all, and that it was answered by the man who had already had some, and that nothing came of either answer and the vessel stood on the boards at the mouth of the aisle for the rest of it.

---

**And that is the whole of what happened in that room on that day, and the room sat for four hours afterwards, and about two hundred people at the back of it came and went up and down the four feet of floor for those four hours and did not go out into the lane.**

The covered vessel stood on the boards at the mouth of the aisle the whole time. It was not carried out and it was not carried in and nobody emptied it and nobody spilled it and about four people in that valley have said since that a thing with a board across it stood in a room for four hours while a room said no to it and nobody in the room did anything at all of it, and that the only instruction anybody anywhere in that valley gave about it was given by a man with sixteen men and no work, who said in a yard five days ago that the rest of it was for the room and a room gets it when it asks.

Nobody walked a part of that day with anybody. The mill went on at the low end of the water and the man at that wheel never got down off it, the wharf did nine things, the market stood down for a second day running, the oven went through two batches, a boy of about nine went up a made road with a barrow and carried on past the standing finish, and about four hundred people in that valley went through their day not knowing that nine people at the top of a lane had said no to a thing in a trade word, and about nine of them had wanted it and had said so in a room for the first time in about four months, and about two hundred had heard that.

**And the eleven words are standing in that valley, and the two people who said the nineteen and the eleven have not spoken to each other since and are not going to, and the nineteen words are not going to be said in that valley again by anybody including the man who said them, and the trough is still behind a shed with a lid on it and nine men in that shed and about four people in that valley know that the nine men have not been told no by anybody except one woman standing up off a bench, and a thing that has not been told no by the man who made it does not get emptied and does not get carried away and is not going to.**
