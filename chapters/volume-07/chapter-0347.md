# Chapter 0347

**The twenty-sixth day of Greenmonth, YR 317, a Sunday, and not a market day, and the second hour and the fourth hour and the seventh hour, in a county that has four market towns in a district and about nine hundred people, in a room over a byre across that street from where a shop stood, and in that room there is a long table, and a clerk of about sixty keeps the minute of it, and a woman of about fifty has about ninety sheets, and about nine hundred people in that county do not know that a man of thirty-two with a limp went to the far end of a thing at the fourth hour of that Sunday and that it was not there, and that nobody in that county asked him about it and that he did not say.**

Two hundred people were out of their houses that Sunday and seven hundred were not, the market was shut and so were four streets of stalls behind it, a mill at the low end of that water turned, a yard nine hundred yards off that market had nine men in it, and a man turned three bars on the road above that market and went back to his hut.

**And a bench of nine in that room did not sit, and under the fourth step of the outside stair of it there is a slip of paper about nine inches by six, and it said nine before that Sunday and it said nine after it, and about nine hundred people in that county do not know that it is nine.**

---

**And at the fourth hour he went to the far end of it, and that is a thing he has done four times, and it took about four seconds, and he came back to the long table and sat down on the stool the clerk of about sixty had offered him ten days ago.**

Nobody in that room saw him do it and nobody in that room has ever seen him do it, and two people in that county have been saying since that a man of thirty-two with a limp went to the far end of something in a room over a byre and came back and sat down, that neither of them has worked out what it was, and that about four people in four counties have worked out that it was the same thing he has gone to four times and that nobody in any of the four counties has ever been in the room it goes to, and that there is no form in any of them on which a person can enter the fact that a thing was not there.

He has not explained it. Nobody has asked him and he has not said. Two people in that county have been saying since that the whole of what the man of thirty-two said in that room on that Sunday was two sentences, that one of them was to a woman of about fifty and was *then what is at the other end*, and that the other one was to nobody and was *not today*, and that nine people in four counties have said since that a man asked the question he has carried for two years and got the same answer the man of about forty gave him fifteen days ago, and that a trade is not a name and that a trade is not a place, and that about two hundred people in four counties have said since that the answer he has now is that the question has two parts and the man of about forty answered one of them and the second part is about four hundred people four hundred miles off and that nobody in that county has ever been there.

---

**And this is the other half of it, and it is four hundred miles off, and it is not in a county that is on any map, and nothing in it has a month in it and nothing in it has a day of the week in it, because nothing in it keeps either.**

There are about four hundred people in it in eleven steadings and four hamlets, a mill at the low end of the water with a man on its wheel who does not get down, nine men in a glass works, and a room over a cross at the top of the lane with twenty-nine benches bolted down in four rows and a figure cut in the end of every one of them, and a hundred and forty in place of a number on the one at the back of the right-hand row.

**And the copies that were going to hold those people are about forty sheets of a printed series with a place in the first column and a figure in the second and a trade in the third and a trade in the fourth and a figure in the fifth, and about four of the six of those columns take a figure, and nowhere on the whole of one of them is the name of a person, and the record those forty sheets are copies of was made by an office two hundred miles from here and has never been near that place and has never been seen by anybody in it.**

**And nobody in that place has been told anything and nobody is going to tell them, and the roll that is the only thing in that valley anybody can be shown has a gap of nine ruled days in it that one man ruled and left empty and a second man has since filled with nine names in a hand that is not the first man's, and no line in four hundred miles of country says which of those two men was writing about a thing that happened.**

**And the woman of about fifty said one more thing in that room at about the sixth hour and it was the only thing she said all afternoon that was not about paper, and it was about a woman of thirty-four who keeps a bridge two hundred and forty feet of stone on and who goes when a name goes, and nobody asked her why she said it and she did not say twice.**

“**I have been up that road once,**” she said, “**nine days each way, in an earlier year, and I was in a room over a cross at the top of a lane with nine people on benches and about two hundred at the back, and somebody read a book out loud. I have not been asked me what I did and I have not told anybody, and I have had about two years to work out whether saying it in a room is a thing a person does, and I have worked out that it is, and I have said it in this room and not in any other and you may do what you like with it.**”

**And the man of thirty-two did not say anything about that either, and the clerk of about sixty put his pen down about two seconds after it and picked it up about two seconds after that, and about nine people in four counties have worked out since that a woman of about fifty went up a road nine days once in an earlier year and sat in a room over a cross and said so in a room over a byre in a county four hundred miles away, eleven years later, to two men and to a man with a stick, and that nobody asked her why and that she was not thanked, and that about four hundred people in that valley are not going to be told why they cannot be reached and that the one person who has been in both rooms is in neither of them now.**

Nobody walked a part of that day with anybody. Two hundred people came out of their houses and seven hundred stayed in, the mill two days up the water turned, nine men went out of a yard at the wet hour and came back in, three bars on the road above the market were put down and put up and a man counted them as he did it, and a man of forty-seven put a day in on a third bay with a barrow and nobody on that reach asked him anything at all.

**And in a room over a byre a woman of about fifty said a thing at the seventh hour that is the whole of what a county of about nine hundred people has to say about a place four hundred miles off, and it is a sentence, and nobody in that room has written it down, and there is no form in that county on which it could be entered.**

“**Then what is holding them,**” she said.

“**A roll. About nine hundred lines and about four hundred people in it, folded in four, in one pair of hands, and it is the only thing in that place anybody can be shown,**” the man of thirty-two said, “**and there is a second book six feet away under a trestle that nobody in that valley knows is there, made out of one half of the first, and the man who made it will not sign it. And nobody in the four counties has ever seen either, and about two hundred people in four counties have worked out that if that place is not reachable by anything then the only copy of those people that exists is a roll one man keeps under a trestle, and that is the copy, and the sheets are not the copy, and about nine hundred people in this county have never heard of any of it.**”

“**And you cannot get there.**”

“**No.**”

“**And you are not going to say why.**”

“**No.**”

**And the clerk of about sixty put his pen down about two seconds before that and picked it up about two seconds after it and wrote nothing, and he has an answer ready for a question nobody in that county has ever wanted, and about four people in four counties have been saying since that a man of thirty-two with a limp went to the far end of a thing in a room over a byre and came back and said four words to a woman of about fifty, and that the four words were *no* and *no*, and that a place that can be reached by nobody is a place that can be reached by nobody and is not called anything by anybody in this county, and that about four hundred people are in it and about four hundred people live in a county of about nine hundred people and the two of those are not added to each other anywhere in this kingdom and have never been.**
