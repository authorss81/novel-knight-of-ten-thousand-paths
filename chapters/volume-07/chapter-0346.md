# Chapter 0346

**The sixteenth day of Greenmonth, YR 317, a Thursday, and a market day, and the second hour and the fourth hour and the sixth hour and the seventh hour, in a county that has four market towns in a district and about nine hundred people, in a room over a byre on that market's north side, some way along the street from where a shop stood, and in that room there is a long table and a clerk of about sixty keeps the minute of it, and about ninety sheets of that office's own printed series came out of a drawer under a counter ten days before a Sunday, and about nine hundred people in that county do not know that ninety pieces of paper changed hands in that room at the fourth hour in nine minutes, and that a man of thirty-two with a limp was in it and did not touch one of them.**

A market was standing in that county at the second hour with about four hundred people in two hundred yards of it and five hundred more indoors, the mill two days up the water went round, a bench-board four feet off the ground had nine men at it, and three bars on a road above that street were turned down and up nine times by a man who has been in the hut nineteen years and has a lamp in it that is not lit at that hour.

**And a bench of nine in that room sits on Saturdays and on nothing else in the week, and that was a Thursday, and it did not sit, and under the fourth step of the outside stair of that room there is a slip of paper about nine inches by six and it said nine before that Thursday and it said nine after it, and nobody in that hall has ever entered that count on a form.**

---

**And the ninety sheets were on that table at the fourth hour, and there were three people in that room who could have picked one of them up, and all three of them picked one of them up, and that is a transfer, and about two people in that county have said since that a transfer in that county happened in a room over a byre at the fourth hour of a Thursday in nine minutes, and that it was conducted by the two people giving it and receiving it and by nobody else, and that a man of thirty-two stood nine feet off that table for the whole of it with a stick in his hand that he does not need and has never once put down.**

The man of about forty had the ninety. He had them in a bundle under his arm and he has not been a holder of anything before and has not been asked to be one and does not know how he came by it, because a drawer under a counter came out of a shop and the shop was his and nobody in that county has ever asked him what was in it.

The woman of about fifty took them. She has a plank of four feet with a figure in the corner of it in her own hand and nothing to put the figure on, and she has said out loud in that room that she will hold them, and nobody thanked her for saying it and nobody asked her why.

**And the clerk of about sixty read the minute of that room for nineteen years and has never entered anything that was not asked for, and on that Thursday he was asked for nothing, and he wrote nothing, and about two people in that county have said since that a transfer of ninety sheets happened in his room and the man who keeps the minute of that room has not entered it, and that he has an answer ready for a question nobody has ever wanted, and that the answer is about a page under the fourth step of the outside stair and not about this.**

---

**And the man of thirty-two was asked to stand in it, and he said the true thing about what he was, and then he was in it and did not conduct any part of it.**

“**You are not keeping any of it,**” the woman of about fifty said.

“**I am not keeping any of it. I have never kept any of it. I have a page in a coat with nine lines on it and there is no line on that page for this and there is nowhere in this county to enter it and no one has asked me to enter it.**”

“**Then you can stand there.**”

“**I can stand there and I will, and I will not open my mouth unless one of you asks me a question, and if you ask me one and I answer it then what I say is a thing said by a man who was in the room and not a thing kept by the man who keeps it, and that is a different kind of thing and there is no line anywhere for the other kind either.**”

And about four people in four counties have said since that a man of thirty-two with a limp said out loud in a room that he was the wrong person for the thing in it, and that being the wrong person for a thing is the whole of what he is for, and that a person who says that in a room before anybody has asked him is doing a thing that about two hundred people in four counties have never once seen done in a room.

**And the nine minutes were nine minutes of two people, and the giving was done by the man of about forty and the taking was done by the woman of about fifty, and nobody else spoke for nine minutes except the man of about forty twice, and the first time he said that the sheets were wet along one edge and the second time he said that he did not know whether that mattered and that nobody had ever told him.**

There is no table in that county for the nine minutes. A county that has four market towns in a district and about nine hundred people does not have a form for the movement of ninety pieces of paper from one pair of hands to another, and the man of about forty did not write it down and the woman of about fifty did not write it down and the clerk of about sixty, who has kept a minute of that room for nineteen years and who writes down things that happen in it, wrote nothing at all, because nobody asked him to and because the line he has for a thing that was asked for does not take a thing that was not.

The bundle was under the man's arm and it came up onto that table at the fourth hour and lay on the boards for four minutes, and two hundred people in that county have been saying since that a bundle of ninety sheets of another county's paper lay on a table in a room over a byre for four minutes and that nobody in that room looked at one of them, and that a clerk of about sixty was sitting nine feet off it with a pen in his hand the whole time and put the pen down two seconds after it went on the table and picked it up two seconds before it came off, and that nobody in that room has ever worked out what he was waiting for and he has not said.

Then it went into her hands and she counted them, and she counted them twice, and about nine people in that county have worked out since that a woman of fifty counted ninety pieces of paper twice in a room over a byre and that the second count was not to check the first one, and that she has counted four hundred and fifty sheets into nine piles twice a week for nineteen years and that a person who counts for nineteen years does not count once, and that two of the nine have worked out that the second count was for herself and not for the room, and that she has not said so and is not going to.

---

**And at the sixth hour a man who had come up the lane two days before with a message in his hand put another message in the room, and it is the second one, and the first one has not come back and is not coming back, and the second one is a sheet with a place in the first column and a question in the third, and it is going nine days up the water to a place with a mill in it and about two hundred people in it.**

The place has not answered anything for a year. Four people have gone up that water in that year and two came back and two did not, and the two that came back said the same thing, which is that a man there writes the answers and that nobody in this county can say what his trade is, because he is a man at the low end of a mill in a place nine days off and there is no office in it and no clerk and no seat.

**And the message went out and a message came back at the fourth hour of the Thursday after, and it is one line in a hand nobody in that county has ever seen before, and the one line is that there is nobody there to hold anything.**

“**That is a no,**” the man of about forty said.

“**That is a no,**” the woman of about fifty said.

“**It is the first thing anybody in that place has sent us in about a year and it is a no and it is the correct one and it goes in nothing.**”

**And nobody went again. That is the whole of what was done about it, and two people in that county have worked out since that a place of two hundred people nine days up that water was asked to hold forty sheets of another county's paper and said no in one line in a hand nobody here has seen before, and that the no was honoured, and that honouring it is the only correct thing anybody in that county has done in nine years with anybody nine days away, and that two hundred people in that place are not in any of the nine sets of forty sheets and are not going to be, and nobody has worked out which of the nine they would have been in and nobody is going to.**

---

**And the answer to a question nobody in that county knew was being asked came out of that room at the seventh hour, and it was not asked, and it was given anyway, and about two people in that county have worked out since that a man of thirty-two with a limp asked a man of about forty who was at the other end of about ninety sheets, and that the answer was a trade and not a name, and that a trade is not a person and that a person is not a trade, and that the page in the coat has a seventh line on it and the seventh line has not been finished for about two years and a job title is not a name and does not finish it.**

“**Who is at the other end,**” the man of thirty-two said.

“**A woman of thirty-three in a building of four floors, two hundred miles off, who is an aid to a register,**” the man of about forty said, “**and I have never seen her and I have told you what she is for a living because I know it off a strip of paper that came round with them and there is no name on the strip and there is no name on the sheets either.**”

“**That is what I asked and that is what I got.**”

“**It is four floors and a trade and a county you have not been in and it is the whole of what a man in this county could tell you, and about nine hundred people in that county do not know what her trade is and I know it because a strip of paper came round nine counties with a place on it and no head on it.**”

**And the man of thirty-two did not write it down and did not say that he was going to and did not take anything out of that coat, and about nine people in four counties have been saying since that a man got an answer to the only question he has carried since a day he cannot count and it was a trade and not a name and that he did not write it down, and that two hundred people in four counties have said since that an aid to a register in a building of four floors is what is at the other end of ninety sheets, and that two hundred people in four counties have also worked out that a line is not a person and that a sheet is not a person, and that nobody in four counties has said either of those out loud in the same room.**

---

Nobody walked a part of that day with anybody. About four hundred people were in that market and five hundred stayed in their houses, the mill two days up the water went round, nine men were in a yard, three bars above the market went down and up nine times, and in a room over a wharf four hundred yards off a man of forty-seven was in four feet of floor for about two hours and was asked nothing, as he is asked nothing, and what he says about twice a year is written down nowhere in this county.

**And a man of forty gave away the only paper he will ever be shown, in a room over a byre, on a Thursday, and got nothing for it, and he did it because two other people in that room were carrying it and he did not want to be the one carrying it, and that is not a reason anybody can enter anywhere and there is no form in that county for it and no line in the minute the clerk of about sixty has kept for nineteen years, and two people in that county have been saying since that a man gave a thing away and lost the standing of it in the same nine minutes, and that a holder of a thing who gives it away is not a holder of anything, and that he is not a holder now and did not want to be one and about two hundred people in that county have worked out that he did not want to be one, and that nobody in that county has asked him and nobody is going to.**
