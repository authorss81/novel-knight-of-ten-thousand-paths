# Chapter 0330

**The twenty-fifth day of Goatmonth, YR 316, a Sunday, and not a market day, and the second hour and the fourth hour and the seventh hour, in a town of about four thousand people on a river, where a market four hundred yards long is shut and about nine people cross it, and a mill turns at the far end of the town and about two hundred of the four thousand are out of their houses, and a lane off the north end of that market has nine doors in it and a wagon arch and a door at the end of it over a weigh-house, and about nine hundred people in a town two hundred miles off do not know that a copy of a roll came out of a drawer in a building of four floors and seven presses three days ago and was used, and that the woman who used it went home at the seventh hour and was not thanked.**

Water had been standing under the boards of the lower of those two rooms since about the tenth hour of the day before and drew off the stone at about the third, and a waggon of salt came up the channel road and stood at the end of it about an hour with nobody under it and went back down it, and the man who keeps the book of that road's up-and-down put one line in it, and that is the twelfth line in that book this year, and there is no form anywhere that a book of twelve lines in a county of about four thousand people could be produced out of.

**And a press in the corner of that room has been standing open for six days and nobody has shut it, and at the bottom of it there is a copy of a contested sheet, and in the second column of that copy there is a figure of persons against this county standing under a head that says a day, and the day by which cause might be shown went between the ninth and the thirteenth of a month and about four of it were left on the fifth and not one person in this county was asked to show cause and not one did, and the copy in that press can be carried out of that room tonight by any person at all without saying one word to anybody.**

**And copies are in rooms this month that they were not in the month before. One is in a drawer in a building two hundred miles off that nobody empties on any day anybody keeps. One is on a shelf in a room in this county. One is going round nine counties in a bag in the ordinary post with four of its six columns empty. One is in a press six feet from a book it was copied from, in a valley four hundred miles from any county that is on a map, and the man who made it will not sign it and the man who keeps the book he copied it from does not know it is there. And nobody in four counties has worked out how many there are and nobody is going to and no two of them have been added to each other, and a book of about nine hundred lines that a man of about fifty keeps in that valley has nine ruled days in it that one man ruled and left empty and a second man has since filled in with nine names in a hand that is not the first man's, and there is no line anywhere in four hundred miles of country that says which of the two of those men was writing about a thing that happened.**

Nobody walked a part of that Sunday with anybody. Nine people went across the four hundred yards of that shut market and nine more went across at the fourth hour, the reach was going with nine men on it instead of the forty a working day puts there, and a man of fifty and a half was on the second bay laying one finish over another with a bar in his hand he is not using, and a man of thirty-two with a limp was not in that county, and has not been in it for three weeks, and nobody in that county knows that or knows the other thing.

---

**And a woman of twenty-nine opened that room on that Sunday at about the seventh hour because a press in the corner of it has been open for six days, and the second copyist of about thirty came down about four steps and stood at the bottom of them, and neither of them said the thing they had both come for until the second copyist said it in about nine seconds.**

“**You are not going to shut it,**” the second copyist said.

“**I have not shut it,**” the keeper of records said.

“**That is not the same as saying you are not going to.**”

“**It is the same and it is not,**” the keeper of records said. “**I have not shut it, and I am not going to shut it, and the reason I am not going to shut it is that a press in this room can be opened by anybody who walks up this lane and there is one thing in it that came from that office and nobody in this county can say whose hand put it there, and if I shut it then in a month somebody will open it and find it shut and the finding will be that I shut it.**”

“**And if you leave it open,**” the second copyist said.

“**Then it is open,**” the keeper of records said, “**and an open press is a thing a person can look in, and a shut one is a thing a person can only break, and there is a difference and I have known it since the nineteenth of that month and I have not got a better reason than that one.**”

And about two people in that lane have worked out since that a woman of twenty-nine has left a press open in her own office for six days over a holiday because a shut thing can only be broken and an open thing can be looked in, and that this is the first instrument in that room that anybody in that county has ever left in a state on purpose, and that four things are on that shelf and a fifth is in the press and about nine hundred people in a town two hundred miles off do not know that there are five.

“**The register,**” the second copyist said, at the end.

“**Is seven,**” the keeper of records said. “**All questions and no finding, and it is seven on a Sunday the same as it was on a Monday, and I am not entering anything today, and there is no line in that register for a woman having opened a press.**”

“**There is a line for everything you have ever wanted,**” the second copyist said, and then stopped, and about four seconds went by.

“**There is not,**” the keeper of records said, and did not say anything else, and the day-book on that shelf was not opened and stands at nine and does not get a tenth, and a tenth would not be a gift if it came.

---

**A woman of about thirty went up that lane in the fourth hour with a pail in her hand and put one thing in front of each of those nine doors, and at all nine of them she was off down the lane again before the last of the knocking had died in the frame, and what she put in front of them that day was a man who went into the water at the third span on the Friday.**

“**He went in on the Friday,**” she said, at the first door.

“**Did he,**” a woman said, from behind a door that was not opened.

“**At the third span,**” the woman with the pail said, and moved on.

She said it at the second door, and at the third, and at the fourth, and at the fifth, and at the sixth, and at the seventh, and at the eighth, on the same two-sentence frame she has used at those nine doors for about a year with a different thing standing in front of it. About two people in that lane have worked out since that a drowning is the first thing she has ever said at any of those doors that is not about this county or the market or the salt, and that about nine people in that lane know a man with a stick came up that road in the middle of that week and stood in a yard and was in a press room in a town two hundred miles off and has not been in this county since, and that nobody in that lane is ever going to say any of that out loud.

**And the keeper of records in the room forty feet from that wagon arch did not hear her, and has not asked her where she gets the nine things, and did not ask her on that Sunday and has not asked her in four years and a half, and a tenth not-asking would not be a gift if it came, and the day-book on that shelf stands at nine entries of a thing nobody asked and was not opened on that Sunday.**

---

**And the cost of those forty days is a person and she is two hundred miles off and she is not in that lane, and nobody in that lane has ever heard her name.**

A woman of thirty-three in a building of four floors and seven presses filled the fourth column off a roll of about four hundred people in about a minute and a quarter on the Friday without looking up, and the fourth column is headed *Keeper*, and the roll was a copy of a roll of a place four hundred miles off, and the person she was writing down was on a list she had never seen, and nobody thanked her and nobody asked her why she did it, and her want is nine words and has not changed since she was twenty-two, and a leaf in a book on the second shelf behind her is turned face down and was not turned over, and she went home at the seventh hour and will be at that column on Monday.

**And that is the whole of the cost of forty days in four counties and in a valley four hundred miles off, and it is one woman who was not asked about a column she has filled in about two hundred times a season for eleven years, and about four hundred forms a month go out of that building and not one of them ever carries a line for what a person means to do next.**

---

**And the room over that wharf at the north end of that market stood open all day with nobody in it, and the one bench in that room sits on Saturdays and on nothing else in the week and did not sit that day, and under the fourth step of that outside stair there is a sheet about nine inches by six with a figure at the head of it and nine marks under that figure, and it is nine, and it is a count of askings and not a price and not a count of persons and it is added to nothing, and a hundred and forty is cut into the end of a bench in a room over a cross four hundred miles off and is a first count of the times anybody has ever stood in an aisle to be asked, and the two of those are not the same figure and have never been added to one another and are not going to be.**

**And the crossing four hundred yards up that road carried nobody that day, as it has carried nobody for four years, and nobody in that county has ever called it anything, and the tide went back at about the third hour and a salt waggon that came up the channel road and stood at the end of it with nobody under it went back down it, and about nine people went down a lane and about four of the nine were somebody else's business, and a press in a room over a weigh-house has been open for six days with a copy of a figure of persons in it, and a day-book in that room stands at nine and does not get a tenth, and about four thousand people live in that town and about nine of them were in that lane on the second day of that week and about nine hundred people in a town two hundred miles off were at that column on Friday and about two hundred people at the back of a room in a valley four hundred miles off went home having watched nine people take a thing that works, and nobody stopped them, and none of the three of those rooms is in the same county as any of the other two, and nobody in any of them has been told about the other two.**
