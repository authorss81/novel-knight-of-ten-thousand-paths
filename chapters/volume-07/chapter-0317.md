# Chapter 0317

**The sixth day of Hearthmonth, YR 316, a Sunday, and not a market day, and the second hour and the fourth hour and the seventh hour, and a Sunday in a town of about four thousand people on a river, when the four hundred yards of that market is shut, a mill turns at the far end of the town, and about two hundred people are out of their houses, and in a lane off that market a door with a number cut into it stands at the end over a weigh-house, and nobody in that town knows that a sheet of a printed series came back up that lane on that Sunday and went onto a shelf behind that door.**

A town of about four thousand people on a river does not stop on a Sunday. It goes to about nine doors, to a field a mile off, and to a mill, and about two hundred of them were out of the house and nobody in that county knew where. The market was shut and the four hundred yards of it were empty at the second hour and about nine people crossed it.

**And a room over a weigh-house with a door on a lane is shut on a Sunday, and on that Sunday it was open, because a man with a satchel came up the lane and put his hand on the door.**

Thirty-one, and six years of carrying that printed series to doors, and the number of sheets is about two thousand and the number of doors is about two thousand, and there is a firm behind him and a rate in his head that nobody in this county could ask him for. Three times in six years, in three towns, in a voice a man uses when he is quoting a price, he has said that he does not know what goes in the four places on one of them, and about nine hundred people in the town where that series is printed have heard none of the three. Nobody put it to him on that Sunday and he did not offer it and he is not going to.

**A carrier is a man with a round and a firm and a rate that is not printed anywhere in the county he is standing in, and about two thousand doors are on that round, and no office in this kingdom knows how many doors that series goes to in a year, and nobody has ever added one county's number of doors to another county's, and nobody in that lane has ever asked him which counties the other lists are in and he has never said.**

“**Is it for you,**” he said, at the door.

“**It is for whoever keeps that room,**” the keeper of records said.

“**That is me saying it is for you and you saying it back,**” he said, and gave her the sheet and went out again, and it took about four seconds and he read none of it and reads none of anything.

---

**And what he gave her was a sheet of that office's own printed series with a place in the first column and nothing at all in the second, and the first column had not come back empty, and it was not empty with a word and it was not empty with a hand.**

Six ruled spaces. Five heads, set in that office's own fount. Four of the six are ruled for a figure. The fifth is for a man and takes him by what he earns his bread by. The sixth is as wide as any of them and has nothing at all over it. **Take that sheet apart and there is no name of a person in it, at either end or in any line between.**

The first of the six is the column a place goes into, and a place is in it, and this county is the place, written the way that office writes one, which is a set of market towns, and it is the same set of market towns that this county has been entered under twice before on two other sheets of the same printing.

**And the second column has nothing in it, and the head over the second column says *Date*, and a column that takes a figure of persons has come back with no figure of persons in it.**

The second copyist of about thirty came down about four steps at about the second hour and a half and read the first column over in the ordinary way, in about two seconds, and then stopped, and neither of the two women said anything for about four seconds.

“**It has come back,**” the second copyist said.

“**It has,**” the keeper of records said.

“**With the place in it.**”

“**With the place in it and nothing in the second,**” the keeper of records said. “**And there is no column on the whole of that series that will take a place by itself. The first column takes a place and the second takes a figure and about four hundred of them a month go out of that building into nine counties with a place in the first and a figure in the second, and the one that came back up this lane on a Sunday morning has the first and not the second, and it went round, and it came back, and it is a return.**”

“**Then somebody did not fill it in.**”

“**Somebody did not fill it in, or somebody filled it in and the figure did not go on,**” the keeper of records said, “**and there is no line on the whole of that sheet for the person whose hand it was either way. About four hundred of that series come back to that building a month and every one of them goes into the returns press, whatever is on it or not, and there is no column in that building for what is in a column.**”

**And nobody in that county can be shown a hand. The county has been entered on that series three times in about four months and cannot produce the first two of the three, and the third of them has come back with a place in it and no figure against it, and the person who would have written the figure is in a town two hundred miles off in a building with four floors and seven presses in it, and there is no line in that building for the name of a person, and about nine hundred people in that town do not know that the sheet came back.**

---

**And about two people in that lane have said since that a place with nothing against it went round nine counties and came back, and that a place with nothing against it is the only thing that office has ever sent anybody that a county could carry without answering anything, and that nobody in that county has ever found out that it exists.**

That is the whole of what an empty second column is, and it is the only time in four years and a half that anything from that office has come up that lane with nothing on it that anybody had to answer, and four people in that lane have worked out that had there been a figure in that column then this county would have had to do the thing it has done twice already, which is to put the paper on a shelf and say nothing, and paper on a shelf with nothing on it does not need anything said about it.

**And that is the difference between a place and a figure, and neither the keeper of records nor the second copyist said it out loud on that Sunday, and about two people in that lane have said since that the two of them stood in that room for about a minute and a half with a sheet in front of them and neither of them said the sentence, and that the same sentence is standing in about four other rooms in four hundred miles of country and it has not been said in either of those rooms either.**

---

The keeper of records put it on the shelf beside the register, and two other things in that office had come down from the same building and this was the third of them, and she chose the shelf and not the press, because anybody who walks up that lane may put anything into that press and may take anything out of it again, and four people in that lane noticed a third thing going onto that shelf that Sunday and about nine hundred people in that town did not.

A day-book on that shelf carries nine entries, every one of them a thing that was not asked, and it was not opened that day and is not going to get a tenth, and a tenth would not be a gift if it came, and the register beside it holds six entries, all six of them questions and none of them a finding, and neither of those two books has a line in it that would take a return.

**And a fair copy of that register made by a woman of thirty in that room about three weeks ago, which carries her trade at the foot of it and not her name, was on that shelf as well, and about two people in that lane have said since that a county which cannot be sued for a question can now be produced a question out of twice, and that there is a third sheet on that shelf that nobody can answer and nobody has to, and that nobody in that county has ever worked out which of the three of them is the oldest, and about four people have, and the oldest is the contested one and it is on its own and it has been there since the autumn.**

---

Nobody walked any part of that Sunday with anybody. About two hundred people went out of their houses and about nine hundred of that town did not leave them, and about nine men were in a field about a mile off hedging a ditch and came in at the wet hour, and a mill went at the low end of a town on a Sunday the way it goes, and the tide came in at about the tenth hour and had been under the boards of that lower room since about the eleventh, and a man of thirty-two with a limp was not in that county that day and has not been in it for about three weeks and nobody in that county knows either of those things.

**And a sheet of a printed series came up a lane on a Sunday morning, and it had come round nine counties, and the place in its first column is this county, and the second column is empty, and there is nowhere on the whole of it for the name of the person who did not fill it in, and about four people in that lane have said since that the county's answer to a figure it cannot refuse has turned out to be the same shape as the answer to nothing at all, and that the difference between a place with a figure against it and a place without one is about four seconds in a press room two hundred miles off where nobody has asked, and that nobody in that county was told that the two things are the same kind of thing.**
