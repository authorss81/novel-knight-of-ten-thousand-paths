# Chapter 0357

**The third day of Harvestmonth, YR 317, a Thursday, and a market day, and the second hour and the fourth hour and the sixth hour, in a provincial town about two hundred miles off a fen county, in one room over a weigh-house with a door on a lane, where a body corporate with a door, a number and the capacity to sue and to be sued has kept a register of seven questions for four years and a half, and a man of thirty-two with a limp was in that room at about the fourth hour with a list he had made himself, and about four hundred people in that town do not know that either of those two things is in it.**

A market day in a provincial town is a market day in a town that is not the town. About two hundred people came into a street of about nine hundred yards and the seven presses of a building with four floors were working, and about forty people were in that building, and a woman of thirty-three was at a column on the third floor at about the fourth hour with a stove in her room that has not been lit since the weather turned. **A press that can be served with stands in that room and has stood open since the twenty-fourth day of a month in Longlight two years back and is standing open now, and nobody has shut it, and a second press four hundred yards up a lane in another county belongs to a yard and cannot be served with anything at all, and the two prices of the same two rooms upstairs are a rate of a year from a body corporate with a door and a rate of a month from a salt merchant, and the two are not the same thing and are not added anywhere in this kingdom and are not added here.**

---

**And a keeper of records had about nine sheets of her own on the table that morning, because an account has to be written by the sixth and it is written on the backs of other people's returns, and about two hundred people in that street have worked out since that a document in this kingdom is very often the wrong side of something else, and that this is not a complaint and is a fact about a building in which seven presses run and about four hundred forms a month come in and about nine hundred a month go out.**

**And a man of thirty-two put a page on the table, and it was a list of nine crossings with no heads on it and no columns and no ruling, and it was the first schedule anybody had made in this kingdom in about nineteen years, and he had made it in about nine minutes in a yard with a stick against a bench-board, and he did not say where he had got it.**

“**That is a schedule,**” the keeper of records said, and did not touch it.

“**It is a list.**”

“**It is a list of nine things with a price against each of them, and you made it, and the making of it is the whole of it,**” she said. “**You did not get it from anybody. Nobody in this kingdom has issued it. That is what makes it a schedule and not a list: a list is what a person keeps and a schedule is what a person makes and then other people act on.**”

“**Nobody is going to act on it.**”

“**Four hundred and nine parish rolls, about nine hundred forms a month into nine counties in two rounds, a sheet with a price on it read into a guild hall in a market on a Saturday, and about eight hundred people in a field,**” she said, “**and about nine of those four hundred and nine rolls belong to people who will be asked whether they will put a man on a crossing for two shillings a cart. You do not have to act on it. It will act on itself and it will do it out of the same column that has been writing down trades since before you were in a yard.**”

---

**And he did not answer that for about a minute, and about two people in that room have said since that a man of thirty-two came into a room with a page and was told in about a minute that it was a schedule and not a list, and that he did not argue, and that a man who does not argue about the correct description of his own document is a man who has been told the correct description before and has not accepted it, and that the last person to tell him was a woman of about thirty-four in a glass works four hundred miles off who said in eleven words that a cure removes about four hundred reasons for not forgiving and nobody in that room was told.**

**And she refused him one part of it, and she refused it in a document, and about two hundred people in that street have said since that a keeper of records of twenty-nine refused a man of thirty-two a thing he had carried nine days to give her, and that she was right, and that being right about it is the cheapest thing available to a person in a room, and that she has known that since an earlier month than this one and has not improved on it.**

“**I will write the account,**” she said. “**It has to be written by the sixth and it has to survive whatever comes after the sixth, and it will survive if it is a thing a man in an assembly can be annoyed by, because annoyance is the only kind of feeling that is written down twice.**”

“**And the nine crossings.**”

“**The nine crossings go in as nine crossings,**” she said, “**and the price goes in, and the date goes in, and the fact that a yard of nine men was offered the same figure twice in one day goes in, because that is the fact I can make anybody angry with. And if a crew is written down as holding one of them, that crew is a keeper in a column, and a keeper is a trade, and I have spent a year learning what happens to a thing that is written down in one place with a trade against it, because there are about nine people in four counties who each hold a set of forty sheets and everybody in those four counties knows which person, and that rule is the only rule anybody has made in four counties in a year and it is the best thing anybody has made and it is going to be the thing that undoes all of it.**”

“**How many are there still in the four counties.**”

“**Eight,**” she said, and stopped.

**And about two people in that building have said since that a woman who keeps a register said the word *eight* about a thing that is in that register and is not in it, and that there is a register on that table with seven entries and not one of the seven is a finding and all seven of them are questions, and that the eighth is a question she has not entered and has said out loud once, and that she has entered it nowhere, and that the reason she has entered it nowhere is that a day-book in that room cannot be served with and stands at nine entries of a thing nobody asked, and that there is not going to be a tenth, and that a tenth would not be a gift if it came.**

“**I am not going to ask you how that happened,**” she said. “**There is a thing I could ask and it is the only one I have got and I am not going to ask it and I am not going to write it in the register and I am not going to write it in the day-book, and nobody is going to thank me for not asking, and I would like that understood, because the not asking is not a favour to you. It is a count. It is at nine and it is not going to be at ten.**”

---

**And he refused her one question, and it was the first thing he has kept from her that is not a cost to his own body, and about two people in that room have worked out a man of thirty-two was asked a question in a room over a weigh-house and did not answer it, and that a man who has concealed a fever and a limp and a verse for four years has a habit, and that this was the first time in about a year that the habit was spent on a thing that was not about him.**

“**Who signed it,**” she said. “**The covering letter. There is a covering letter. Every one of those sheets goes out under one and it is not the office that sends it, it is a person, and if the person is on the sheet then the account is a story about a person and a story about a person survives an election and a price does not.**”

“**I know who signed it.**”

“**Then put it in.**”

“**No,**” the man of thirty-two said, and did not say anything else for about eleven seconds, and the two shillings a month and the fourpence a year were on the same table on that afternoon in two different books and were not added.

“**You will not put a name in an account because you cannot prove what the name did,**” she said, “**and that is the first thing you have ever not given me that is not a fever,**” **and she wrote it down, on a sheet of her own, in a column of her own, with no head on it, and about two people in that building have worked out since that she entered a refusal in the only place in that building where a refusal can be entered and that the only place is a sheet of her own with no head on it, and that there is no register entry and no form and no office, and that the whole of the record of that afternoon is a line in a book nobody can be served with and a column with no name over it.**

“**I am not a traitor for writing that,**” she said. “**You are not a traitor for not answering. Those are two different sentences and this kingdom has one word for both of them and the word is wrong and I have been putting it on other people for about a year.**”

---

**And a woman of twenty-nine of that office has a want in it that has been in it unchanged since the month she was twenty-two, and it is about four or five words long, and it is not written down anywhere, and about two people in that building have worked out since that a person who has kept the same four or five words for seven years has not been failing to say something and has been refusing to say it, and that nobody in that building has ever asked her to say it out loud, and that a sheet with a price on it read into a hall in a fen county is the kind of thing that would make a person of twenty-nine say it, and she did not.**

Nobody walked any part of that day with anybody. **The man of thirty-two was in that room about two hours and went out into a street of about nine hundred yards alone and stood at the end of it for about a minute, and the keeper of records stayed in that room because there are seven presses and one of them stands open and does not get closed by being looked at, and about two hundred people in that street saw the two of them not walking together and about none of them worked out what had been said at a table, and the not-askings stand at nine, and a tenth is not a gift, and nobody in that building has thanked anybody for anything since the weather turned.**

**And a woman of thirty-three on the third floor of that building went on filling in a column at about the fourth hour and did not look up, and a set of forty sheets went into a drawer on that floor on the Friday after, and she is the only one of four floors who could put a hand on that drawer in the dark, and the eighth thing that happened in four counties is in a press in that building under a head, and nobody in that room on that Thursday entered a question about it, and the count is nine and the register is at seven and the two are not added to each other and are not added to anything.**
