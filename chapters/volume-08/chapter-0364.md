# Chapter 0364

**The seventeenth day of Fallowmonth, YR 317, a Saturday, and a market day, and the third hour and the fourth hour and the eighth hour, in a town of about two thousand people on the salt, in a room over a shop in a lane off that market, where a man of about fifty-eight put four drawings on a table that had been in a store under an abbey nine days of cart off for about nineteen years and had come down on a Tuesday and was going back on a Sunday, and two thousand people in that town do not know that four drawings came into it on a Tuesday and will go out of it again on a Sunday.**

The room was nine feet by seven and had a table in it and a window that did not shut and a fire that was not lit, and about four people in that lane have said since that a man who rents a room for two nights and a window that does not shut has stopped expecting the window, and it was over a shop that sold nothing on Saturdays because the market took the trade, and the man of about fifty-eight had the room for the two nights of a Saturday and a Sunday and paid for it out of his own money and did not put it on anything. **The four drawings came out of a bundle of about four hundred sheets that is being sold for the weight of it, and the four hundred are a hundred and forty years old and are nine inches by six and the drawing on them is better than the drawing anybody in that town has done this year.**

He put them down in the order they came, which was not the order they were folded, and he did not number them and he did not say what they were of, and he said one thing before he said anything else and it was about the floor of the room.

“**I have kept that store nineteen years,**” he said. “**I have had a paper since the sixth of a month in Fallowmonth that says the room is to be cleared by the first of Longlight, and four hundred sheets in it are going out in nine loads to be weighed and sold by weight, and I am not going to be the man who argues with a paper. I would like four of them to be of use to somebody before I clear the floor.**”

“**You would like them used.**”

“**I would like them used,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, “**and I have no way of finding out, because I am a man with a key and not a man anybody writes to, and I have been up nine days of a cart road with four sheets in a bag and I have got about two days of that road left in me and a Sunday to get back by.**”

---

**And a man of about fifty-eight slept in a lodging over a cooper's shop at the north end of that market and paid for it out of his own money and did not put it on anything, and a man who has kept a store for nineteen years pays for his own bed in a town he has never stayed in, and the reason is that a body corporate with a door and a number pays for a room and a man with a key does not have one, and the difference between the two is a piece of paper and the piece of paper is in a building two hundred miles off.**

**And he was up before the light because a man who has a key is up before the light, and he folded the four sheets the way they had been folded, and the second line on the lightest one has a kink in it a quarter of the way along where the hand that drew it changed, and a kink in a line a hundred and forty years old is the only mark on those four sheets of a person, and the person is not a person anybody will ever hear of, and a man with a limp put his finger on the kink without looking for it.**

**And the four drawings are of forty miles of that country. Each of them has nine places on it, and at each of the nine places there is a fixed thing, and beside the fixed thing there is a second line.** The fixed thing is drawn in the same ink as the rest. The second line is in a lighter hand and is not in the same ink and was not drawn at the same time, and it goes somewhere else.

**Nobody in that room asked a man of thirty-two with a limp what the second line was, and he did not say, and two people in that lane have worked out since that a man who is good at seeing a load path looked at four sheets of a country he has not walked in about four seconds and then put his hand flat on the sheet with the lightest hand on it, and that four seconds is how long it takes, and that neither of them said a word about it, and that a person who has been in this trade for eleven years does not need to.**

“**These are good,**” the man of thirty-two said, and then said nothing else about them for a minute.

“**They are a hundred and forty years old.**”

“**They are good,**” the man of thirty-two said again, “**and they are the drawings of nine places on forty miles, and there is a second line at every one of the nine, and the second lines do not go anywhere anybody is going this year.**”

---

**And the four hundred sheets are not a record of anything anybody has ever been asked to keep, and nine of them are about the works under that abbey, and the works under that abbey were done before anybody living was born and were written down again twice, and the writing down again is the reason about nine hundred forms a month go out of a room two hundred miles off on a series with six columns and five heads.** Two people in that lane have said since that the whole of what is under an abbey in that part of the country got into a form about two hundred years ago by being drawn, and that a thing nobody was ever meant to read is a thing that gets read by whoever finds the sheets, and that an office has wanted four of those four hundred since before a man with a key came to that store and has not once come itself.

**And emptying a store is not a dramatic thing.** It is nine loads going out on nine days and about four hundred sheets weighed against a beam by a man who is paid by the load, and a man of about fifty-eight has kept that floor nineteen years and on the sixth of a month in Fallowmonth he was told the floor is to be cleared, and he has not argued.

**And a second line on a sheet a hundred and forty years old is a drove, or a hard, or a ford, or sand, or a way behind a wall, and nine men in a yard in a fen county four hundred miles off would tell a stranger that in about nine seconds, and none of them would say where the ground is and all of them would know it.** A man of thirty-two with a limp did not say any of that. He put his hand flat on the sheet with the lightest hand on it.

---

**And a man of about thirty-one with a canvas bag came into that room at the fifth hour because he was in that lane and had been told there were drawings in it, and he looked at the four of them, and two people in that lane have worked out since that a man who has nine places to go to and a list of nine places in a bag and no way of telling which nine are the right nine has been handed the answer to the only question he has asked anybody since the first of Fallowmonth, and that he did not know it was the answer, because nobody drew a head on the sheets.**

He stood at the table for four minutes. He put his canvas bag on the floor, which he had not done in that lane before. He traced one of the second lines with a finger from one end to the other and did not put his hand on the sheet.

“**Where did these come from,**” he said.

“**Under the abbey,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, “**in a store, in a bundle, with about four hundred others. They are about a hundred and forty years old and they have been in a room the whole time and I have never seen anybody draw them.**”

“**And whose were they.**”

“**I could not tell you and I have not been told and I did not ask,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, “**and I will tell you the thing that I can tell you, and it is not a small thing. There is an office that has wanted these four sheets since before I came to that store, and the office is not the office I work for, and it has had a man ask a question about them twice in nineteen years and both times the answer was that there was a bundle.**”

“**And now there is not.**”

“**Now there is four,**” the man of about fifty-eight said, “**and I did not take four. I took four that were going to be sold by weight, and I have been carrying them for nine days, and I would like them used, and that is what I have come about and I am going back on a Sunday.**”

---

**And a man of about thirty-two with a limp said that he would take them, and then said the term out loud.**

“**Until the bars come off,**” he said. “**Not one day past it, and if I have not used them by the day the ninth of the nine is done then they go back into a bag and you take them home on the Sunday, and I will not have them. And you are not going to be asked what I did with them and I am not going to tell you, and if anybody asks you where they went you have said four sheets went down a cart road and that is true.**”

“**That is a strange thing to ask a man to hold,**” the man of about fifty-eight said.

“**It is not strange. It is ordinary.**”

**And about four people in that lane have worked out since that a man with a limp said the words *and that is true* in a room over a shop and that the man with the key heard them and did not look up, and that a person who has been asked to hold a thing that is not his and has said yes in a sentence and a half has bought himself about ninety days and not one hour of it is his own, and that nobody in that lane has ever heard an arrangement made that way and one of them said so and was not believed.**

---

**And the man of about thirty-one with a canvas bag had the four drawings, and the man of about fifty-eight went to find a bed for the night, and the man of about thirty-two with a limp sat at a table in a room over a shop with four sheets on it and did not look at them for a quarter of an hour.**

He decided it at about the seventh hour. **He gave the four drawings to the man of about thirty-one, and he did it in about nine seconds and without a sentence about why, and the man of about thirty-one took them and did not thank him and asked him one question, which was whether there was a copy of them anywhere.**

“**There is a bundle of about four hundred going out in nine loads to be weighed,**” the man of thirty-two said, “**and after that there is whatever anybody buys a hundred and forty years of drawings for, and I do not know, and neither does he with the key.**”

“**Then these are the only four.**”

“**Then these are the only four,**” the man of thirty-two said, “**and you are the only man in this district who is going to stand at all nine of the nine places, and I am not going to go to nine places, and I cannot get up a bank, and a drawing in a room two hundred miles off is a drawing that about forty people look at and none of them is a person who has a bar to set.**”

**And two people in that lane have said since that a man of thirty-two with a limp gave away the only four drawings in that country in about nine seconds, and that he did not say why, and that the reason was about a man with a canvas bag and a list of nine places in a bag, and that the two of them are the only two people in that district who knew before the seventh hour that the second line on those sheets was there, and that one of them is going to have to stand at all nine of them in about a fortnight and the other one is going to stand at the bottom of forty miles of made road and look at it, and that neither of them said any of that.**
