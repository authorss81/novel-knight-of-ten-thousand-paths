# Chapter 0389

**The twenty-first day of Goatmonth, YR 317, a Saturday, and a market day, and the first hour and the fourth hour and the eighth hour, in a market street of four hundred yards on a coast about two days west along a salt road from a town of about two thousand people on the salt, and in a room at the back of a salt warehouse off that street, where a market had been full since the first hour and a woman of about thirty-four had called eleven households into a room and had not taken advice from anybody, and nobody on that coast knows that a place of about four hundred people on that salt road has been put between two prices this month and that one of the two is a Crown price.**

The market was full at the first hour and the tide was making. **Eleven boats go out on the making and eleven come back on the making, and about half of them were gone by the second hour and the other half were not, and a market of four hundred yards on that coast is not a market of four hundred yards of stone two days east of it, because everything in it is either wet or in a net.**

The room at the back of the salt warehouse has a table in it and a bench on each side of the table and a door that opens on the slip. **The woman of about thirty-four called the eleven households herself, one at a time, on foot, over two days, and she wrote the order of them on the back of a bill because she has no book, and the back of that bill is the only list of eleven anywhere on that coast, and nobody on that coast knows that a list exists and two hundred of the nine hundred would not know what to do with it.**

The man of about thirty-four with a sheet was not in that room. **He had gone two days east with a form he had not filled in and a figure of eleven on it and nothing about four, and four people on that coast have worked out since that a Crown officer came, asked, and was not answered, and went away with the answer that a woman had not said yes, and that is the whole of what that office has about that coast, and the office is two hundred miles off the salt, and the yard the man had walked to is eleven days east of that slip, and nobody in that office has been to the coast.**

---

**And the third thing that was settled in a room on that coast on the twenty-first day of Goatmonth was settled by a woman of about thirty-four in about a quarter of an hour, and it is the third of three and the other two were said eleven days east in a yard and two days east in a room over a shop, and the three of them have still not met and were not meant to.**

“**The four you have been asked for are the four of my eleven that cannot run,**” she said. “**I am not giving you four and I am not refusing you four. Seven of eleven do not feed nine hundred people for four months, and that is the whole of what your asking costs, and it is not a price and it is not a rate and there is no column of mine for it. What I am doing instead is that nine of them run the eight months they can run, at the fare the eleven households agreed about nine years ago, to anybody who asks, and the four that run the other four months are yours if you can pay the going rate for a boat, and I will name that rate in a room and not on a sheet.**”

“**That is not an answer,**” a man of about fifty said, who is one of eleven and not a man of a household.

“**It is three answers,**” she said, “**and I have not said a fourth and I am not going to, and I have said them in the order they are useful, and the order is that the nine first because the nine are the ones that matter and the four are the ones that are easy.**”

**A man of about fifty is one of eleven and is not a man of a household and has not been one since his father died, and he runs one of the four boats that go out with fish and he is in that room because he is one of the eleven, and he had said nothing at all through the quarter of an hour, and four people in that room have said since that the one man in it who has run a boat for twenty-two years and walked eleven days to say a thing in a yard said nothing in the quarter of an hour in which it was settled, and that a man who has just been told what his eleven boats are going to do will find out what he has got in about eleven days on a road that is hard in the last week of it, and that about eleven people in that room did not think about that and about nine hundred people outside that room did.**

**And about four people in that room have worked out since that a woman of about thirty-four who has not said yes and has not said no gave a coast eleven boats in a quarter of an hour by putting nine of them in front of four of them, and that no man in that room knew she was going to do that, and that a man of about fifty who has run a boat for twenty-two years and walked eleven days to say one thing in a yard was in the room and had not been asked and had not offered.**

---

**And the trade war on that salt road opened in the same week and it did not open on that coast and it is the reason the coast has eleven boats and not four.**

An office that puts a price on nine crossings has begun to buy salt out of the flats. **It buys at a fixed price, delivered, on a day of the week, into a yard two hundred miles from that coast, and the figure is fourpence a bushel, and the office has a series and a head and about nine hundred sheets a month and a person can be found by what a person keeps, and the price is on a sheet and the sheet goes out about nine hundred times a month into nine counties in two rounds.**

**The four houses that agreed six pence nine years ago are not a company and have never claimed to be one. They are four houses with salt on their own ground, and they buy from a place of about four hundred people, and they have bought from that place every year since, and when they say six pence they are not setting a price against the Crown, they are saying what they have always paid and will not be told a different figure by an office with a series and a head and about nine hundred sheets a month. Two of the four are still there. One of them stopped in the third week of that month and has stopped for good and has not said why, and four people on that coast have worked out since that a buyer stopping is the only thing in a price that arrives without a cart, and that nobody wrote that one down either.**

The buyers who were there before the Crown came will not come under six pence a bushel. **Six pence is not a Crown price and is not anybody's rate and is not on anybody's series, and it is a figure four houses in a place on that salt road agreed in a room about nine years ago, and about four hundred people in that place have been living on the difference between four and six for about nine years without ever having to write either of them down.**

The cost of a season to a person in that place is this, and it is one price and not two, and the two prices of the same thing are not in a column together anywhere in this kingdom and are not added to each other in any book including a notebook.

**A bushel of salt is four pence at the Crown price. Eleven bushels is forty-four pence. The cart that takes them eleven miles is three pence and the man who rakes the salt is eleven pence. Three and eleven is fourteen pence. Forty-four and fourteen is fifty-eight pence, and fifty-eight pence is four shillings and tenpence.**

**And a bushel of salt that is bought at fourpence and carted and raked at fifty-eight pence for eleven bushels is a bushel of salt that a person in that place can sell at six pence and cannot sell at four, and the figure of three shillings a bushel that is on a sheet two days east of here is for salt that has been on a cart from somewhere, and this salt has not, and the two figures are two prices of two different things and neither of them is in a column with the other and neither of them is a toll and neither of them is a wage.**

---

**A place of about four hundred people on that salt road is nine miles from that coast and it is on the salt and it has been on the salt for about nine hundred years, and of those four hundred some rake and some cart and some do both in a season and cannot do both in a price war. It has one lane, a well, a chapel that is shut and a kiln that is not, and the man of about fifty-four who burns lime has his kiln two miles off that lane and has never in thirty years been asked by anybody in that place what he pays for his salt.**

A man of about fifty-four who burns lime at a kiln two miles off that place had come nine miles on the salt road and then two up a lane in the fourth week of that month and had a sheet. **He is not a Crown officer and he has not asked anybody anything in this kingdom and he keeps a day-book and has kept one for thirty years, and four people on that coast have worked out since that a man who keeps a day-book for thirty years came eleven miles to ask a question about two prices and that the question was not the one anybody expected, which was whether the people between the two prices are the people who make the salt or the people who buy it.**

“**You are both,**” a woman of about thirty in that room said. “**And that is the whole of what a trade war is. It is never the man at the top and it is never the man at the bottom. It is a place of about four hundred people who are in the middle of it because they salt their own and sell their own and cannot do both at four pence.**”

**And the middle of a price war is a place of about four hundred people and not a market, and a market is not a place anybody keeps a roll of, and a Crown price and a figure four houses agreed in a room nine years ago are not two rates and are two prices of the same thing, and nobody on that coast knows any of it and the four hundred people in that place know all of it and have nobody to say it to.**

---

**And the cost of the twenty-first day of that month is eleven households in a room at the back of a salt warehouse, a quarter of an hour, and a woman who said three answers and not a fourth, and a man of about fifty who walked eleven days to be in that room and was not asked and did not offer, and a man of about thirty-two with a limp was not in it and was two hundred miles off on a made road and was not sent anything.**

She said the last thing at about the eighth hour and she said it to nobody in particular and it was the fourth thing and it was not an answer.

“**Eleven days east there is a yard that has agreed to walk a load a week down this road in the four months my boats cannot run, and I did not ask them and they did not know I existed, and I am not going to tell you that I arranged it, and if you write that down I will know before you are home.**”

**And about four people in that coast have said since that a woman of about thirty-four said a fourth thing after saying she would not say a fourth thing, and that the fourth thing was a warning and not an answer, and that nobody in that room wrote it down, and that about nine hundred people on that coast went to bed that night with nine of eleven boats promised to eight months and four of eleven boats promised to a rate nobody has set, and about four hundred people in a place eleven miles up a salt road went to bed with a bushel of salt at four pence and a bushel of salt at six and no way of having both, and the one that is a Crown price arrived first and is not going away.**
