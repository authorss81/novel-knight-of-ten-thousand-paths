# Chapter 0381

**The twenty-sixth day of Frostmonth, YR 317, a Sunday, and not a market day, and the second hour and the fourth hour and the seventh hour, in an upper room over a chandler's shop in a lane off the north end of that market, in a town of about two thousand people on the salt, where the shop under that room was shut and about two hundred people were out of their houses, and a man of about thirty-four with a sheet said nine sentences to a man of about thirty-two with a limp and neither of the two of them read anything aloud, and about two thousand people in that town do not know what nine hundred returns written in a field four miles off it are being used for.**

He had been in that lane for eleven weeks and had not been in that room before. **A chandler's shop shuts on a Sunday because the market takes the trade on a Saturday and a shop that sells candles and salt to a market cannot sell them on a day the market is shut, and two hundred people out of two thousand is the ordinary figure of a Sunday in that town, and four hundred miles off in another county four men were mending a sill in a yard.**

A man of about thirty-four with a sheet was in that room with nine days of his own round in it. **He has a form and it is a good form and it has six columns and five heads and a sixth with no head on it, and the second column takes a figure and the third takes a person by that person's trade and the fourth is headed *Keeper* and takes a person the same way the third does, and that head has been the same head for two years, and nine days of parish work does not get lighter by being in a room with the man who brought it.**

**That round was not his own choosing that morning. He is a Crown officer of eleven years, he is not on a roll in any office he does not work for, he is not a member of anything, and nobody in that room had put a question about himself to him in eleven weeks and nobody had any reason to. A thing he has been counting since the eighth of a month in Fallowmonth is four hundred and nine rolls that leave a building two hundred miles off on a cart every month and are back a fortnight later, and the count is the count of what goes out and not of what comes in, and nobody in this kingdom has ever asked him to count the other end.**

He put a paper down on the table. **It was the first page of a list of nine places and there were four more, and every one of the five had a place in the first column and a figure in the second and four columns with no head on them, and the second column of one of the nine was empty and nothing on the paper said so.**

---

He asked for it in the ordinary way and he asked it without a form.

“**What are those for.**”

And the man of about thirty-four with a sheet said nine sentences. **The nine sentences foot at nine and the speech as printed is a hundred and thirty-seven words with the attribution excluded, and the word *about* does not stand in front of either figure.**

“**They came into the building two hundred miles off in a tray with a strap over it on the sixteenth of that month. Nobody told the field they were going to. They have been read against a list of places that is kept open. The places with a figure in the second column are being held and the places without one are being let go. There is nothing on any of it that says which. The round comes round again next month and nobody rings it. The man who wrote them does not know and nobody is going to tell him. You have about a week before anybody thinks to ask me. I am telling you because you asked me and I have nine days of my own round in that bag and none of it is this.**”

**And a man of about thirty-four with a sheet said nine sentences in about forty seconds in a room over a chandler's shop on a Sunday, and nobody in that town knows that nine hundred returns written in four days in a field by people in a field are being used to decide which places are held and which are let go, and the eight hundred people in that field are not told either.**

---

**And four people in that room two hundred miles off have said since that a sheet with writing on the back of it went into that building on the same cart as a tray of returns and was put in with them, and that nobody in that building has ever turned a sheet over, and that those two facts together are the whole of what happened.**

**And a man in a market in a town on the salt said on the twenty-fifth of that month that a sheet with the back of it written on had been in a tray in that building for nine days, which is the sixteenth, and had said on the eighteenth that the same sheet was on its way into that building in about nine days, which is the twenty-seventh, and one of those two men was wrong by about eleven days and the wrong one was the one who said it first, and the sheet has been in a tray since the sixteenth of that month and has not been looked at since.**

The man of about thirty-four with a sheet had said in that market that the back of a sheet is not part of it. **The man of thirty-two with a limp did not say a word about either of the two sentences then and has not said a word about either of them since, and the sheet is in a tray and the nine sentences are the only account anybody has of what the tray is for, and the officer was not asked to write them down and did not offer to.**

---

Then the man of about thirty-four with a sheet said the thing that was about his own work and not about anybody else's, and he said it before he was asked and he said it once.

“**I have four hundred and nine rolls to account for in a fortnight and they go out on a cart every month and I am the man who counts what goes out. I do not want this round. It has nine days of my round in it and it will have nine more by the second, and there is no column on any of it for the nine days.**”

“**There is no column on any of it for nine hundred returns either,**” the man of thirty-two said.

“**No,**” the man of about thirty-four said. “**And the six columns and the five heads have been the same for two years and I have never once had to explain one of them to anybody, and a man of about fifty at a press could tell you what each of the five is for and neither of us would be able to say what a list of nine places is for, and I am not complaining about that. I am telling you what I want. I want my round.**”

**And a person with a want of his own that has nothing to do with a register said it out loud in a room in about eleven seconds, and nobody in that town has ever heard a Crown officer use the word round, and it is the only thing in nine sentences and forty seconds that anybody asked for.**

---

**And a man of about thirty-four with a sheet asked a fair question and got a fair answer and neither of them was a lie, and the whole of what was wrong with the question was that the two hundred miles were in the same direction as the thing being asked about and the man had not noticed that, and about four people in that room have worked out since that a fair question asked by a man who is carrying his own work is still a fair question, and that a fair question is how nine hundred people a month come to say yes to a thing nobody has explained to them.**

“**Is the field going to be told.**”

“**No.**”

“**Has anybody in it been told.**”

“**No.**”

“**Then I will go and tell them,**” the man of about thirty-two said, and got as far as putting his hand flat on the table and then took it off again, and did not say the next thing, and two people in that lane have worked out since that a man who can be angry for about two minutes and then not be angry is a thing this country has only ever heard about, and that the thing he did not say was not a threat and would have sounded like one.

**And he did not walk nine miles to that field, and nobody in that room asked him why not, and the eight hundred people in that field have not been told what was done with the second column of a sheet, and there is no column in this kingdom for a thing a person was about to do, and that is the whole of what was in that room on that Sunday at about the fourth hour.**

---

**And a man of about thirty-one with a canvas bag was going to be in that room at the fourth hour and was not.** **He is the man who sealed nine places in the third week of Fallowmonth, and he is not the carrier who came into that district with a satchel in Longlight, and he is not the man who came up to a yard four hundred miles off with a handcart in Wolfmonth and counted nails, and four people in that district have said since that three men of about thirty-one have been on pages in that county in four months and that a man of about thirty-one with a bag is the one who put a seal on a place, and that a place with a seal on it is a place that does not get a why.**

He had a term of ninety days and it ran out on the eighth of that month. **He had been told on the ninth, in a lane, by a person with no stake in it, that a tray of returns had gone into a building two hundred miles off, and he had been on his way to find out what a tray of returns was for since the ninth, and four hundred miles of cart road is nine days and he had had seventeen.**

The man of about thirty-four with a sheet said the nine sentences to him as well. **He said them in the same order and in the same forty seconds and he did not change a word of them and he was not asked to, and about four people in that lane have worked out since that a man who says the same nine sentences to a second person in the same room is doing the one thing a form is for, which is to be said to whoever is in front of it.**

“**Then there is nothing for me to bring you,**” the man of about thirty-one with a canvas bag said.

“**There is nothing for me to give you,**” the man of about thirty-four with a sheet said. “**And I would rather you had it from me in forty seconds than off a list in a month.**”

That was four words and then a room in which nothing was said. **It ended there and it ended in the same hour it had begun in, and nobody made anything of it, and neither of the two men said a word about it afterwards, and nobody in that town knows that a cooperation between a man with a stick and a man with a bag ended in a room over a chandler's shop on a Sunday at about the fourth hour without one word of an argument.**

---

**And what a cooperation of about four months had bought, in that room on a Sunday, was four drawings about nine inches by six out of a store under an abbey nine days of cart off, a hundred and forty years old, out of a bundle of four hundred that was being cleared and sold by weight, and a man of about fifty-eight with a key who kept that store nineteen years and who has gone back and is not coming again, and a list of nine places with four crosses on it in a pocket book, and nine places that were sealed in the third week of Fallowmonth and are sealed still.**

**And the four drawings are in the canvas bag in a room in that town and nobody has looked inside it in about four months, and the two men who have looked at them have both said that they are the right drawings, and that is the whole of what either of them has said, and neither of them has said what a drawing is for.**

---

**And two people in that lane have said since that the whole of what came out of that room on the twenty-sixth of that month was nine sentences and a man of about thirty-four who wanted his round back, and that a man of about thirty-one with a bag went out of it with nothing, and that a man of about thirty-two with a limp went out of it with nine sentences and no place to put them, and about nine hundred sheets a month go out of a room over a weigh-house with a door on a lane into nine counties in two rounds and not one of those two series has a column that would take why nine hundred returns written in a field are being used to hold a place open or let it go.**

The round comes round again in a month and nobody rings. **And the two things that are true at the seventh hour of the twenty-sixth of that month are that a man of about thirty-one who helped write the register does not know what it is for, and that the office which has read it told nobody in the field that it was going to, and neither of those two is a speech and both of them are in a room over a chandler's shop in a lane in a town of about two thousand people on the salt, and nobody in that town has been told either.**
