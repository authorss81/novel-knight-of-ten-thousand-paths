# Chapter 0388

**The fifteenth day of Goatmonth, YR 317, a Sunday, and not a market day, and the third hour and the fifth hour and the sixth hour, in a room over a shop in a lane off the north end of that market in a town of about two thousand people on the salt, and the shop under it was shut and had been shut since the middle of the morning, and a woman of about forty who had come four hundred miles off a river and had been in that town for sixteen weeks was at a table with a man of about thirty-four with a sheet, and nobody in that town knows that a place with about nine hundred people in it has been findable for about five months.**

**Nobody sells candles on that lane on a Sunday, because the trade on them goes with the market and the market is shut. There are two hundred people out of two thousand in that town on a Sunday and four of the two hundred are in that lane. The room has one table, two chairs and a window that does not shut, and the table belongs to the shop and the shop has let it stand under that window for eleven weeks and has not charged for it and has not mentioned it.**

**A round is a cart, and a cart goes out on a fixed day and comes back a fortnight later, and the men who load it do not unload it, and about four hundred and nine rolls go out of that building in a month into nine counties in two rounds, and a roll is a place and a figure and about four heads, and a place that has emptied is still a place on the roll and the figure stays where it was because nobody has a column that could move it.**

**She walked that line and arrived on the twenty-third of a month in Embermonth, eight days behind the woman of about thirty-five who had the paper, and she has had a bed in another street since. About nine hundred people on that river sent one person out and will send nobody else inside four months, and a person who is sent out waits, and she has been waiting sixteen weeks and has not once been asked by anybody in this county what she is waiting for.**

**A lodging house in another street took her bed and her washing in the first week and did not ask her a question, and has asked her one in every week since, and the question is always whether she has any washing and the answer is always no. The woman behind that counter has not asked her where she is from in sixteen weeks and has not told anybody else in that town where she is from, and that is not kindness and is not a rule. Nobody in a lodging house asks, because a person who is not asked is a person who stays.**

The man of about thirty-four with a sheet was in that room on his own errand and not this one. **He has been the loading end of that cart since the eighth of a month in Fallowmonth, and eleven weeks of it is left to him, and the two days of this one are the whole of what he came for, and a man who has two days of a thing and nine days of another in the same week is a man who has been put on the second errand by somebody who does not go to the coast.**

A man of about fifty-four who burns lime at a kiln two miles off that town was in the room at about the fifth hour. **He has kept a day-book for thirty years and a day-book is not an account and an account is not a day-book, and he had been in that room on the twenty-ninth of a month in Embermonth and had listened to four pages of the worst winter in eighteen years being read out by the person who wrote them, and nobody copied a word and he had been the one who asked whether the pages had been kept dry.**

---

**And what he had come to say, he said at about the fifth hour in about nine seconds, and he said it to the man with a sheet and not to her, and four people in that lane have said since that this is the same man who stood in this room on the eighteenth of a month in Frostmonth and said four sentences in it in about four minutes, and that a man who puts the same thing into a room twice inside a month without being asked twice has decided that the room has not been told.**

“**A man from your place wrote it on the second morning of four, in a field, and he wrote it because a woman who counts faster than a man with a stick asked him to, and there are about nine hundred sheets of that round and yours is one of them, and the second column of yours has a figure in it, and the sheet is in a tray in a building two hundred miles off, and the roll that has always carried your place is four hundred and nine rolls behind the same building and has not been amended since.**”

Nobody in that room said anything. **And nobody in that town knows that the place of about nine hundred people on a river four hundred miles off is now in the first column of a sheet that four people in this kingdom can get at, and that a figure of about nine hundred is the second column, and that a figure of about nine hundred has never been anybody's business.**

“**Who wrote it,**” she said.

“**A man of about thirty-two, at the second hour, on the second morning, from a woman of about forty-two who counts faster than a man with a stick and had taught in the four places round where that field is.**”

“**One of the six people from my place was in that field.**”

“**I did not know that,**” the man with the sheet said. “**And I have not asked, because asking is a different errand and I have two days of this one.**”

---

**She has had four letters since the twenty-third of a month in Embermonth. A letter is a thing that takes eleven days in one direction and eleven in the other when the ground is hard and does not go at all when it is not, and four letters in sixteen weeks is four letters and a month apart, and none of the four has asked her anything, and the two that were written nearest to the third week of a month in Fallowmonth were written by a woman who does not know what a return is and would not have used the word.**

**And what the place says about being found is not said in that room, and is said by the one person in that room who is not allowed to say it and has said it once.**

She said it at about the sixth hour and it was four minutes long and it was not an account and it was not a want and a woman of about forty who has carried four pages for eleven years and could not make them say anything else spent four minutes of a Sunday in that room on a question of arithmetic and not on a winter, and she has never once been asked a question about arithmetic.

“**We have been here a long time,**” she said. “**Not long. Four hundred years. I do not mean that as a figure. I mean that a place on a river that is shut eight months in the year and open four has never had to be found by anybody, because the people who want to come here are the people who can stand on a bank in the four months, and the people who want to come here in the other eight are people who have already been in this county and do not want to come again.**”

“**That is not a want,**” the man with the sheet said.

“**It is not a want,**” she said, “**and I am not the person who can make it into one, and neither is the man of about fifty-four and neither is the man with a form, and the three of us in this room are three people with no standing in a place four hundred miles off and every one of the three of us has said a true thing about it this month.**”

---

**And the answer is shorter than a year, and it was given by the man with the sheet in eleven words, and it is the whole of what happened that Sunday and nobody improved on it.**

“**Four months. And going down. And it is not four months.**”

**The eleven words foot at eleven and the speech as printed is eleven words with the attribution excluded, and the word *about* does not stand in front of the figure. And a man of about fifty-four who burns lime wrote eleven words in a day-book that night and has not shown it to anybody, and a day-book is not an account.**

**And a man of about thirty-two with a limp was not in that room. He was four hundred miles off in another county on a lane with a crossing on it, and nobody in that lane told him any of it and nobody has told him any of it, and about two thousand people in a town on the salt do not know that he was not in that room, and the man with the sheet has said since that a man who is not in a room is a man who cannot be asked what was said in it.**

---

**A man of about fifty-four who burns lime kept a day-book that night and wrote down what he had been told and the day he heard it and the hour and the name of the man who told him, and a day-book is a thing a man does for himself at the end of a day, and he has kept one for thirty years and has shown it to nobody, and about two hundred people in that county have worked out since that a man who has kept a day-book for thirty years and has shown it to nobody has now written the first line in it he would not be willing to lose.**

**Four hundred miles is eleven days on a line that is not a made road for the first two hundred and thirty of it. A person travels that line in eleven days and a sheet of paper travels it in the same eleven days, and the two of them arrive about the same week, and one of the two can answer a question when it gets there and the other cannot, and nobody in that county has ever worked out which of the two of them is quicker and nobody in that county is going to.**

**And the cost of the fifteenth day of that month is a fact four hundred miles long that has to be carried by one person who is not the one who can decide anything about it.**

The woman of about forty is not the steward. **The steward is four hundred miles off and she does not know this week whether he is in his own house, and their council is a room and not a bench, and about nine hundred people on that river have given one person the power to say no and nobody can say how, and four people in that lane have worked out since that a community of about nine hundred people has one person who can say no and no room that can be shown, and that the two of those are the same fact and that nobody there has ever said so.**

**And there is no version of this in which it is not told. The place is in a sheet in a tray and the round comes round again in a month and nobody rings, and a place that is in a return is a place that can be asked about, and nobody in that town knows that a community of about nine hundred people has become a place that can be asked about, and about nine hundred people four hundred miles off do not know it either, and a woman of about forty has to go back and be the one who tells them, and nobody has told her what to say and she has not asked.**

She said one thing about that at about the sixth hour and she said it to the man of fifty-four who burns lime and not to the man with the form, and the ten words foot at ten and the word *about* does not stand in front of the figure, and it was not a want and it was not an account and nobody wrote it down.

“**I will have to be the one who says it.**”

**And about four people in that lane have said since that a woman of about forty has spent sixteen weeks in a town on the salt learning that she is the only person who can read out a thing to about nine hundred people, and that a second thing has now come up that she is the only person who can say at all, and that the two of those are the same job and it is not a job and it is not paid and there is no column in this kingdom for it, and that nobody in that town has been eating their breakfast for sixteen weeks within four hundred yards of the room where it was decided and not one of them knows.**
