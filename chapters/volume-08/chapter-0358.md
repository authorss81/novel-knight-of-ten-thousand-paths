# Chapter 0358

**The seventh day of Harvestmonth, YR 317, a Monday, and not a market day, and the second hour and the fourth hour and the sixth hour, in a field above a ditch four miles off a town of two thousand people on the salt, where eight hundred people were living who had been living somewhere else nine days ago, and two thousand people in that town do not know that there is a field, and four hundred people nine days off do not know there is a ditch, and nobody anywhere calls anybody in that field a refugee and about nine people in that field have used the word once between them and one of those nine was telling a child.**

Harvestmonth on the salt is the month the flats are hard enough to walk on without a rut and the month a person who has been moved off them can be moved again. About two hundred of about two thousand people in that town were out of their houses. **The crossing four miles south of that town ran, and a bar of oak and iron went down and came up four times a day, and a woman of forty-one was on her own side of it, and eleven people a day went through it in this month and four carts a day, and nobody in that town has ever asked her whether there is anybody to come.**

**And a field above a ditch four miles off had eight hundred people in it on that Monday, and they had been in four villages nine days before, and the four villages are still on the roll of a county that has four market towns in a district and about nine hundred people, and four hundred and nine parish rolls in a building two hundred miles off still carry them, and the county does not know that any of the four has been emptied and the roll does not know either and there is no column for a village that is not there.**

---

**And the four villages those eight hundred came from are about nine miles apart on a made road and about four apart on a track, and the track is the one a cart came in on and will go out on while the ground is hard, and the field is about two hundred yards off the end of the track, and the ditch that the field sits above runs for about a mile and then goes into a salt pan that is dry and white and has been dry since before anybody in that district was born, and two people in that town have worked out four hundred people in a field above a ditch are drinking out of a ditch that runs into a dry salt pan, and that the water in it is the only water for about nine miles and that it is not anybody's and has never been anybody's and is not on any sheet.**

**And a woman of about forty-two who was a schoolmistress in one of those four villages is in that field, and she is the only person in it who can count faster than a man with a stick, and she counted the eight hundred on the first morning from the high arc in about eleven minutes and got a figure that a man with a stick did not get, and two people in that town have said since that there are now two counts of eight hundred people in a field and that neither of them is in a register and that they are not added to each other and that a sheet with six columns and five heads has nowhere to put either.**

“**Eight hundred and a bit,**” she said, “**and the bit is the low arc, and the low arc is the village whose wheel went and the low arc is eleven families, and I have counted the high arc twice and the low arc once and I am not going to count it again in this light.**”

“**You are not the one counting,**” the man with the stick said.

“**I am not the one counting,**” she said, “**and there is nobody in this field who is, and if you would like the figure to be a figure I would have to be the one counting, and then it would be a figure that a woman counted and not a figure that a man counted, and in about four months there will be a sheet somewhere with a number in it and nobody will know whose count it was.**”

---

**About eight hundred people is not a figure of persons. It is about eight hundred people, counted by a man of about forty with a stick in his hand who walked a line twice on the first morning and gave up on the second morning because a line of carts is not a line of people, and two hundred of the eight hundred came from one village and four hundred from two and two hundred from a fourth, and the ninth figure in that field is the number of carts and the carts are not a figure of persons and are not added to them and are not in any register at all.**

The carts stand in two arcs because the field is not flat and the second arc is on the high side where the ground rises about four feet. **A tilt cart out of the low village has a wheel out of true and has been mended with a plank and a nail, and the mending is the work of a man of about thirty with a mallet and about nine nails, and it took him a day and a half, and the mend will not last the winter, and nobody has told him it will not last the winter and he has not asked.**

**A cart came off the track into the high arc on the second morning and could not get down again, because the low side is about four feet and the ground between is soft at the shoulder, and about nine men got it round in about an hour with a plank and a rope, and the man of about thirty whose cart it was said the low side was no good and the other eight did not want to hear it, and about two people in that town have said since that a man who has been off the ground for nine days can be the only person in a field of eight hundred who knows what the ground will not take.**

A boil came up on a boy of nine in the second arc on the third morning. **It was lanced on the fourth morning with a knife that was boiled first, and the woman who did it has done it before and to her own, and there is no person in that field who is a person anybody has sent for, and two hundred people in that field have said since that the single hardest thing about a field of eight hundred people is that four hundred of them are not speaking to one hundred of them, and that the two hundred are not the four hundred, and that the whole of the field is two fields standing next to each other with a ditch between them.**

---

**And nine people in that field have used the word refugee once between them, and one of the nine was telling a child about a house that is still standing about eleven miles off, and the child asked whether they were going back, and the person telling the child said that they did not know, and two people in that town have worked out the single most common sentence in a field of eight hundred people is short and is about a future, and that a sheet with a price on it has no column for a future and that a person can be moved off a crossing and a person cannot be moved off a question about when they are going back, and that nobody has yet asked any of them that on any paper.**

**And there is a woman in that field of about thirty-three with a pail who has been telling nine doors in a lane in a fen county about nine days off one thing at a time for about a year, and she is not in that field, and nobody in that field knows her, and about two people in a lane in that county have worked out since that a woman with a pail who has said one thing at each of nine doors for about a year was not among the people in a field and has not asked where she went, and that the lane is four hundred yards from a weigh-house and the field is four hundred miles off and the two are not joined by anybody in either place.**

A boy carrying water went the length of the high arc four times between the second hour and the sixth hour, and the water came off the ditch, and the ditch is four feet wide and about two feet deep and has been dry since Mudmonth and has about two inches of brown water in the bottom of it, and nine hundred people in that field drink from it and nine hundred people in that field would not drink from it in Harvestmonth if there were anything else, and there is nothing else, and there is no form on which a field's water can be entered and there has never been one.

**And a Crown officer of thirty-eight was not in that field and has not been in it, and a woman who keeps a list of nine crossings was not in that field and has not been in it, and a woman who takes a toll at a bar four miles south was not in that field, and about two people in that town have worked out since that four separate people who would each have something to say about eight hundred people in a field are four hundred miles off or nine days off, and that the one person who was in that field for an hour on that Monday was a man of forty with a stick who gave up counting on the second morning and came back to say that the ditch had a smell.**

---

**And nothing about that field was on any sheet on that Monday, and a sheet with a price on it went into nine counties that week in the ordinary round, about nine hundred times, on a series with six columns and five heads, and the first column takes a place, and about four of the six take a figure, and there is no head on the sixth, and nowhere on the whole of it is there a place for the name of a person.**

About eight hundred people in a field above a ditch, and nine carts and a tilt mended with a plank, and a boil lanced with a boiled knife, and two hundred people who are not speaking to four hundred, and nine hundred people drinking out of a four-foot ditch with two inches of brown water in the bottom of it, and not one of those figures is on a sheet, and not one of them can be put on a sheet, and there is no column in this kingdom for a field.

**And two thousand people in that town know there is a field and four hundred people nine days off know there is a ditch, and neither of those two facts is on anybody's paper, and about eight hundred people in that field are being counted twice a day by a man with a stick who has given up on the second counting and who will not be asked to stop.**
