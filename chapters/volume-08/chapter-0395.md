# Chapter 0395

**The eighth day of Thawmonth, YR 318, a Tuesday and not a market day, and the second hour and the fifth hour and the eighth hour, on about thirty miles of a salt road that runs out of a coast about two days west of a town of about two thousand people on the salt, where a yard eleven days east has agreed one load a week and a woman of about thirty-four has eleven boats and about nine small ferry cooperatives have between them about nine flat-bottomed boats and about nine places to land one, and about nine hundred people on that coast do not know that the nine of them were all in one room on the same day.**

## One

A cooperative is not a company and has never claimed to be one. **The one at the second stile is four households and a flat-bottomed boat twenty-two feet long with a pole on it and a man who can pole a flat-bottomed boat in nine inches of moving water and a boy of about fourteen who is learning to, and it does about nine trips a day in the four months and has done for about nine years, and it has a name, which is the name of the stile, and it has a bit of board with the fare scratched into it that one of the four households scratches out with a nail every time it changes, and the fare has been the same since before the man who poles it was born.**

There are about nine of them along about thirty miles of that road and they are not one another. **They have not met. They have not sent anybody to anybody. Two of them will not speak about the other seven in front of their own households, because a household that hears that there are eight other places to land a boat on thirty miles of salt road will ask what the boat is for, and there has never been an answer to that which anybody has wanted.**

That is what the corridor is made of, and it was made on the same day in nine rooms, and the man of about thirty-four with a sheet was in four of the nine and the woman of about thirty-four was in all nine because she called them and it took her about eleven days to call them on foot.

## Two

**The rate is the one she had said in a room in the third week of a month in Hearthmonth and had said she would name in a room and not on a sheet, and she named it in nine rooms in about eleven days, and the yard eleven days east has its own day rate, and the four of her eleven that run the other four months of the year out of the slip run on hers, and the seven of the nine places that are not her slip each have their own.**

**Nobody on thirty miles of that coast knows what any of the other places charges.** Not the woman of about thirty-four, who has said a figure in nine rooms and has been in nine rooms and has not asked a single one of the nine what the other eight are asking. Not the man of about thirty-four with a sheet, who has been in four of the nine and has a bag and would write it down if he were asked, and who has not been asked, and who has said in four rooms that he is not the man who decides it. Not the man of about fifty who has run a boat for twenty-two years and has been in two of the nine and came out of both of them with nothing in his hand.

**And that is why nothing on that coast can be undercut.** A thing you cannot price is a thing nobody can go under, and the only way to find out what any of it costs is to walk it, and the road is about thirty miles of salt with a stretch of it that goes under at a state of tide a cart does not want, and about nine feet of oak in a kerb at the near end of it, and a person on the wrong side of the nine feet of oak, and by the time a man has walked thirty miles and asked one question and got an answer, the tide has turned and he is either on the far side of it or he is standing in nine inches of moving water waiting for it.

**And there are seven figures that everybody on that coast knows and not one of them is in a column with any of the other six.** The first is the fare a yard of nine men eleven days east pays a man for a day, and the second is the going rate for one of four boats out of a slip for a season's hire. The third is the fare scratched on a bit of board with a nail, and the fourth is the six pence four houses have paid for nine years for a bushel of salt, and the fifth is the four pence on a Crown sheet, and the sixth is the two shillings a cart at nine places on the flats, and the seventh is a penny a person and four pence a cart at one of the nine, and nobody on thirty miles of that coast has ever had all seven on one piece of paper and there is no line in this kingdom that takes them.**

## Three

The rule was not made. It was found, and the man who found it was a man whose whole work is counting what goes out, and he found it on the eighth morning of that month in the second of the nine rooms he was in.

“**Three times since the third week of Hearthmonth,**” he said. “**The road under the water at the wrong tide has been shut at the near end three times since the third week of Hearthmonth, and the man who lives nearest the nine feet of oak has opened it three times. The first was a woman of about thirty-four at the third landing and the second was a man of about sixty-three at the fifth and the third was the man who poles the boat at the second stile, and the three of them are not one another and the three of them have not spoken about it. I have the three of them because I have a round and a round goes past all three.**”

**And he put it on the table in the middle of that room, and it was a count, and it was the only count anybody had ever made on that coast in about thirty miles of it, and it was three.**

“**How much did it come to,**” said the woman of about thirty-four.

“**Nothing,**” the man of about thirty-four said. “**Not to them, and not to anybody. I have the three of them and I can tell you what each of them lost and I have not put it down, and the two of them who are not the woman are not in a position to be paid because neither of them has anybody to pay.**”

“**Then it is not a rate,**” said the woman of about thirty-four, “**and a thing that is not a rate cannot be put in a column and cannot be undercut and cannot be argued about, and it is the only thing on thirty miles of that coast that anybody cannot take away from anybody.**”

“**It is a bad rule,**” the man of about thirty-four said. “**You know what it is. A road gets shut and the man who lives nearest is the one who opens it, and the man who lives nearest is the man who can least afford the nine hours it takes and has the least call on anybody, and in a year the man who lives nearest will be a man who cannot be nearest, and the road will be shut more often and not less, and that is what happens.**”

“**Yes,**” she said.

“**And I am not going to say it is not a bad rule,**” he said, “**because it is, and because I have counted three and three is not a season, and I am going to say the other thing instead.**”

“**Say it.**”

“**It is what everybody has been doing anyway,**” the man of about thirty-four said, “**and nobody had written it down, and a thing nobody has written down cannot be taken off anybody, and I have nine days of my own round in a bag and none of it is this and I am not writing this down and if it goes in a tray I will know.**”

**And the woman of about thirty-four said it, and she said it in about a quarter of a minute and it came out of her the way a thing comes out of a person who has said it in her head since the first of those three times, and it is this: whoever is nearest opens a shut road, and the person nearest is not paid for it.**

**And nobody in that room said the word rule. Nobody wrote it down. A yard's book takes a thing set and this is not a thing set. A man of about thirty-four with a sheet has a form in his bag with heads on it and the whole of what was said in that room goes in none of the columns, and the woman of about thirty-four has eleven boats and no book and keeps the order of eleven households on the back of a bill. The whole of it fits inside a wrapper with nothing on it, and there is no piece of stationery in this kingdom that has ever been in every room it needed to be in except that one.**

## Four

And the yard eleven days east of that coast is in it and is not on that coast, and that is the third end of it. **One load a week down that road for the four months the boats cannot run, for anybody who asks and for nobody who does not, at the yard's own day rate, in coin on a Friday and not on a sheet; it is never the man on a bar, it is a man the nine of them name on a Friday, and it is one week in three and not one week in one, and the man who goes knows before he goes which of the three weeks it is and the coast knows it before he starts.** That has not changed since the twelfth day of a month in Goatmonth and nobody has gone back to that room to change it and about two thousand people in a town of about four thousand on a river do not know that a yard in their county has agreed to run a road eleven days off in the middle of a year, and the yard's own reach four hundred yards down a lane off the north end of that market has forty men on it and has had all year.

**And the corridor has three ends and a middle, and the middle is about nine days of nothing.** Eleven days from the yard to the coast by a salt road, and four hundred miles from a county on a map to a bar on a rise at the top of a road, and the two of those are not the same eleven days and the two of them do not meet and there is no chain of anybody from the yard to that bar and there is not going to be one in this year. A load a week goes down about thirty miles of salt road between nine landings. And a place of about nine hundred people on a river four hundred miles off has a bar in a kerb at the top of a rise and about nine people on the wrong side of it, and about four hundred people on that flat country are on the far side of that bar and are not asked, and the corridor does not reach them and nobody on thirty miles of that coast has ever heard of the flat country and nobody there is going to.

## Five

And about nine hundred people on that coast did not know any of it, and about four hundred people nine miles up that salt road did not know any of it, and a man of thirty-two with a limp was a long way off in a county on a river and had not been in any of the nine rooms and had not been told about any of them and was not going to be, and a place of about nine hundred people four hundred miles off had a bar on its own road and was not in any of the nine rooms either, and about nine people were on the wrong side of it, and the two of those things are four hundred miles apart and there is no room on thirty miles of that coast in which the two of them could have been put next to each other, and nobody tried.

**And the man of about fifty, who has run a boat for twenty-two years, was in two of those nine rooms that day and in neither of them did anybody mention the four months, and he went out on the making of the tide and he has still not been told and he has asked twice now and about two hundred people on that coast have not noticed either asking.** And the third landing's woman of about thirty-four opened the road on the ninth of a month in Hearthmonth at about the fourth hour and the nine feet of oak was up for about four hours while she took a boat and a pole and a boy of about fourteen across it, and nobody paid her and nobody has asked her about it and she is one of about nine households and her own household is the first one.
