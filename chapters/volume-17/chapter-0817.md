# Chapter 0817

**Thawmonth the tenth, YR 324, a Monday and not a market morning, the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and no market in that street, the fourth hour on a floor four days off with seven people on it, a week after a person stood in a doorway and did not go in, and a man behind a counter at the low end of that street being asked what he does all day.**

There were nine people in that street at the ninth hour and the bolt had been on the room over the weigh-house since noon on the Saturday before, and the roll has been on those boards two hundred and eighteen days.

Four days off, on a floor above a byre with the cow door open on the cold, seven people stood up and said a number before the light. It was the three hundred and fifth morning.

“Twice.”

Four. Four. The man with the bad hip wrote nothing, because nothing is written, and the six mornings lost in the nine days of water in Mudmonth are still six.

“A hundred went up on Thursday,” the woman at the door said, “and I have thought about that all week and I have not worked out why I thought about it.”

“Because it is a figure you did not make.”

“That is it. That is the whole of it and it took me a week.”

The number has been four for three hundred and one mornings and it was eight before the water, and two of the ten things came back after the water and two have not.

In the room over that weigh-house the woman of about twenty-nine had the second lamp at the foot of those thirteen stairs lit at the fourth hour and it went out at about the sixth, and nothing about the four lines of chalk on the sill went in with it. The register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gained none in seventy-four days. The day-book stands at eleven entries and gained none in seventy-four days. The fourth ruled head of that page is empty and no fifth head is ruled anywhere in this county. The press stands open with no date on it and a leaf at the back of that day-book carries nine figures and a date and the names of nobody.

On that sill there are four lines of chalk and they mean four, and the hood over that door has kept the weather off them for about four and a half years. They have not been added to since the forty-ninth of Mudmonth. Nine people went past that door on the tenth of Thawmonth and about four of them looked down, and about four and a half years ago somebody put them there and nobody in nine counties has read them to anybody.

Then, at about the ninth hour, at the low end of that market street, the man of thirty-two with the stick asked the man behind that counter one question about his own trade, kindly, because he had worked out over the winter that the cheapest instrument an occupation has is a man behind a counter and that nobody in nine counties had ever asked him what he does all day.

There were about nine people near that counter and about four of them heard the whole of the answer and about five heard the end of it.

“**I sell a copy of the notice at a penny and a copy of anything else at a halfpenny and I write the day and the weather in a book, and about nine things a week is the whole of it, and I have stood behind this counter thirty-one years and nobody has ever asked me what a gate is for.**”

It was sixty-one words. About four people in that street heard the middle of that and about five heard the end of it, and nobody argued with him about any part of it.

He said the weather in the book about four times a day, whether or not anybody came in for it, being that about four people in nine counties have said that a man who writes down the weather in a book he keeps is doing something nobody in nine counties has done for money and about five have said that it is the only part of that trade which anybody will ever be asked to check.

A carter at the second door said that nine things a week was not a week's work, and the man behind the counter said it was not, and that a good week was about nine things and a bad one was about four, and that the count had not been above about fourteen in thirty-one years. About four people in nine counties have said since that a man who can say what his worst week was and what his best week was has a trade anybody could learn in about four and a half years, and about five have said that a trade nobody has asked about is a trade nobody has had to be asked to explain.

Then the man of thirty-two asked him what to call him, because he wanted to know who to ask a second time, and the man told him, and about four people in that street heard it and about five did not, and about four people in nine counties have said that a man who has been behind a counter at the low end of his own street for thirty-one years and has a name nobody four hundred yards away could produce is a man this county has been using without asking and that using is not the same as employing.

About four people in that street have also worked out that the counter had nine things done on it in the last week and that about four of those nine were copies of the notice at the top of the frame, and about five have said that a penny a copy is the cheapest instrument anybody in nine counties has ever had for telling nine counties what a county has been offered, and about four have said that nobody in nine counties knows what a person does when they have read that notice and have decided not to come and buy it.

At the back of that office there is a low room with a door in front of it and a clerk's table and a press with no date on it and a drawer eleven inches deep. That room was shut behind its own door on that Monday as it has been on every morning since the twenty-sixth of Frostmonth, and nobody in nine counties went into it, and the drawer in it was not opened once. The county's one seal is still in there with its ribbon round it and a set of standard weights beside it, and nobody in nine counties has asked what a seal is for and nobody is going to be asked this year.

About four people in nine counties have said since that the cheapest instrument an occupation has is a counter, a door and a man who was born in the street, and about five have said that the cheapest instrument is also the one nobody in nine counties has looked at, and about four have said that a man who has been asked one question in seven weeks and has answered it in sixty-one words has been asked more in seven weeks than in the four and a half years before them.

About four people in nine counties have said that about nine hundred names are in a book about four miles off with a trade against each of them, and about five have said that about nine trades are on a flap of a stall with no name against any of them, and about four have said that between those two books this county has everything it would need to answer a paper with one line on it and has not put them in the same room.

Then the man of thirty-two stood at those boards with nine people in that street and no market in it and touched nothing on that frame. The date at the foot of that sheet is eleven days past and nothing in nine counties is shut, and a man who came up eleven miles of made road with an empty cart a week ago has not been back, and nobody has asked where he went or why he came.

The cost of that Monday was in the leg, which took four hundred paces of a lane and about four hundred yards of a made road to reach a board and stand at it for about two hours and had a hot band across the knee by about the tenth hour, and in the ear on the side the stick is on, which is gone, and which is the side she spoke from, and which will always be the side she spoke from.

And there was, on the inside of one wrist, a bleed one hundred and twenty-five days old that had not faded by anything he had done to it, that nobody in nine counties had asked him about, that is in no document this county keeps, that has never been said out loud in a room in front of one other person, and that had no panel said about it and no explanation and no page of its own.

The rest of that Monday went on. There were nine people in that street and no more than nine, and about four of them went into that office for a halfpenny copy of something during the morning and about five did not, and about four hundred people along eleven miles of made road have not been inside that office in about four and a half years and about nine of them were in it last week and about four of those nine bought a copy of a notice at a penny.

About four people in nine counties have said since that a penny is the cheapest instrument anybody in nine counties has ever had for telling nine counties what a county has been offered, and about five have said that a penny is also the cheapest instrument anybody has ever had for telling a county what it has been offered by, and that those two things have not been said in the same sentence by anybody in nine counties.

The near wheel of a cart came off at the low end of that street and the carter got it up again with a bar in about nine minutes without anybody stopping on his account. The two women at the third door took a bolt of cloth apart in the doorway over about four minutes by the clock, counting the threads at the selvedge against each other, and went in after it and came out with it wrapped. The inn at the high end had two of its four beds full and marked the two occupied ones on its tally-stick by the door and put no board out on the made street, because there was nothing left to fill.

The light went off the west bank and found the upper edge of those boards, and the lamp at the high end of them was not lit, because the ninth hour of a Monday in Thawmonth is dark and there is nothing off the wet slate of them to need it. Five of those nine people went out into five trades and four stayed on at the boards.

At the causeway four days south the hand lamp goes on the coping at the low side every Monday afternoon in the winter, and the tenth of Thawmonth is a Monday, and the lamp was put there that afternoon.

The water was off the causeway and the hand bell on the gate post above that coping is the size of a fist and was not rung. About four hundred people live on the far bank of that water and have never once been across in about nine years, and the four miles above that lamp are the smoothest four miles of made road in nine counties, and a man of about thirty-eight runs a wain on them once a week and has said out loud once why they are smooth and about nine hundred people have not heard it.

Four days out, the ninth mile stone is the height of a knee and the ring somebody had cleaned round the foot of it on the Saturday before is a foot wide and half an inch deep and had taken grit into it again. Four hundred yards past it the eleventh mile stone has about nine hundred names cut into the side of it going back about two hundred years and nobody in nine counties can read more than nine of them, and about four hundred people a day go past a stone with nine hundred names on it and about nine of them stop.

Eight of the nine yards of wall at the head of that made road are back in, and the gap in that wall is about nine feet long and about four feet high, and one man has been carrying that stone two miles in his own hands for six hundred and ninety-eight days, and the ninth yard has not been started, and the mallet is on the bank two miles off with the line of stone and it was not picked up. The third course of sill at the culvert at this town's own gate is unlaid.

The five frames of brood in that walled yard are under about nine straw skirts and the board is still on the skirt stones at the gate, and the young man of about nineteen has been in and out of that yard about nine times this week with a honey pail and has not touched a frame and has not added a sixth, and nobody in nine counties has asked him for an account of the three things he has said out loud in that street in about four and a half years.

At the head of that cart track nine hurdles are still across it and the thumb of lime on the shoe of the top one has gone to powder. The last cart of lime came up that track four hundred and seventeen days ago and the road behind the hurdles has been shut three hundred and ninety-eight days, and nobody has been up that track since the second of Goatmonth, and nobody lifted a hurdle and nobody tried a bar and nobody is going to this year.

Behind the ninth of the nine stones there are about nine hundred yards of grass nine feet wide that has been dead inside its own width for four hundred and sixty-five days, and the dead grass stops at the ninth of them, and nobody has ever been past the ninth of them and the far side of them has never been shown to anybody.

Nine doors down the cloth was over the bench and nobody lifted it and nobody asked what was under it. The half leaf in the press in that room over the weigh-house is face down and was not turned over. The three series in that room are three series and none of them is derived from another and the band figure is not written down anywhere in this county.

He went out of that market street at about the eleventh hour with the leg grinding and did not go up the made road past the ninth mile stone, and he did not go in at the low end of that street, and the low room at the back of that office stayed shut behind its own door. Five things were on that board and eleven days past the date and the fourth line of the roll empty on all three of its copies, and nobody was thanked for any of it.

**And the cost of that Monday is sixty-one words said once out loud by a man who has stood behind a counter at the low end of his own street for thirty-one years about nine things a week and a penny a copy and nobody having ever asked him what a gate is for, a penny being the cheapest instrument anybody in nine counties has ever had for telling nine counties what a county has been offered, a man of about thirty-four who came up eleven miles of made road with an empty cart a week before and stood in a doorway and did not go in and has not come back and nobody has asked where he went, a low room shut behind its own door with a drawer eleven inches deep in it that was not opened once and the county's one seal and its ribbon and a set of standard weights still in it and nobody asked what a seal is for, nine hundred names against their trades in a book about four miles off and nine trades against no names on a flap of a stall and neither of them in the same room, seven people on a floor four days off saying four twice for the three hundred and fifth morning with the loss of six mornings still six, a sheet seventy-four days on a board and eleven days past the date on it and nothing shut anywhere in nine counties, three copies of a roll with nothing on any of them and the fourth line empty on all three and two hundred and eighteen days on that frame, one hundred and twenty-five days of a bleed on the inside of one wrist that has not faded by anything anybody has done to it and that nobody in nine counties knows about, one thing gone quiet four hundred and seventeen days ago and alive and shut and unnamed and unheld, about nine hundred yards of grass four days out that will not come back, the use of one ear, the use of one leg, and a year, none of which is in any of it and none of which has a price.**
