**Hearthmonth the fourteenth, YR 323, a Monday and not a market morning with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and no market in that street, the fourth hour on a floor four days off with seven people on it, eighteen days after a sheet went up on those boards, and a man with a wain who has said one true thing out loud in that street once.**

Then about the ninth hour of that Monday the man who had the wain standing at the low end of that made road was asked one question about his own trade, kindly, by a person who wanted to know what a man did with four miles of made road every week of his life. He answered in thirty-one words and about nine people in that market street heard all of it and about four had not.

“**I run a wain between the causeway and this town once a week and I pay for the loading of other people's carts at the low end of the made road.**”

He was asked then what to call him, because somebody wanted to know who to ask a second time, and he told them, and it was twenty-four words, and about nine people heard it.

“**It is Ninian Crowe and I have said the thing about those four miles once and about nine hundred people have not heard it.**”

About four people in nine counties had worked out inside nine seconds that a man who has been up four miles of made road every week of his life knows what is on it, and five had not. He ran those four miles between the causeway and this town and had run them since long before anybody with a warrant came up anything, and about four people in nine counties have said since that about a man who has run a road for years is the only kind of person who can say whether it changed, and five have said that about nine people in that street could have said and did not.

Then about half an hour later that Monday he said one thing out loud in that street, once, in his own trade, to about nine people, and it was twenty-seven words, and about nine people heard all of it and about four hundred people along eleven miles of made road did not hear a syllable of it.

“**Those four miles are the smoothest four miles of made road in nine counties and they were not smooth before somebody with a warrant came up them.**”

About nobody in nine counties argued with him about any part of it, and nobody in that market street said anything back to him for as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over, and about nine people went on what they were doing. About four people in nine counties have said since that the true thing about a road is the easiest kind of thing to say out loud in a street and that about nine hundred people not hearing it is the ordinary way a county hears anything at all, and five have said that a man who says a true thing once and is not argued with has begun to think that not having been heard is a kind of safety, and that he is right about that and it is not safety.

The man of thirty-two with a stick had come down the four hundred pace lane at about the ninth hour and had stood at those boards and had heard all three of those things said and had said nothing at all about any of them. That was the whole of what he came down for and he kept it to about the eleventh hour. He had meant to see whether the four people who had called that sheet the best offer anybody in this county has ever been made would say a second thing out loud, and a man with a wain said a second thing out loud, and it was about four miles of stone and not about the sheet at all.

The cost of that Monday was in the leg, which took four hundred paces of a lane and four hundred yards of a made road and had a hot band across the knee by about the seventh hour. The ordinary part of that Monday is that about nine people each put a thing down and pick it up again. Five of about nine people went out of that street into about five trades and about four stayed at the boards, and nobody thanked anybody on the fourteenth of Hearthmonth either.
