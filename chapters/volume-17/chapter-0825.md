# Chapter 0825

**Mudmonth the sixth, YR 324, a Saturday and a market morning, the room over the weigh-house open and the hearing not sitting, the ninth hour with about four hundred people in that street, a hundred days after a sheet went up on those boards, thirty-seven days past the date at the foot of that sheet, and a man of thirty-two refusing four copied pages in the room that keeps them.**

The market filled that street from about the eighth hour and by nine there were about four hundred people in it and the five things were up on the frame at the low end, and the bolt had come off the room over the weigh-house before the light and the copyist of about twenty-six was up those thirteen stairs with the register under her arm.

The roll has been on that frame two hundred and forty-four days with nine empty lines on each of three copies and the fourth of the nine this county's. The notice above them is three hundred and sixty-five days past its own date and has shut nothing. The date at the foot of the sheet went past thirty-seven days ago and nothing in nine counties is shut on it, and a hundred days of a sheet is about three and a half months and about nine hundred people along eleven miles of made road do not know there is one.

The four copied pages were on the top shelf of the press in that room, above the half leaf, which is face down and was not turned over, and they have been there since the Thursday of the twenty-seventh of Thawmonth and nobody in nine counties has asked to see them and about nine people in that market street know that they exist.

Then, at about the tenth hour, the man of thirty-two with the stick went up those thirteen stairs and the leg took the last four on the rail, and about nine people were on that floor and the market was going on four hundred strong in the street below.

He had been gone four days on a made road with the four of them not in his hands and about a week in this county since, and he came back to that room on the Saturday because he had said on the Monday that he would come back on the Saturday.

He stood in the middle of that floor about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over, and about nine people on that floor gave him room, and about four of them had worked out before he got to the ninth stair what he had come up for and about five had not.

He had it with him in that room for the length of a breath. It was not a voice and about eight other people on that floor heard nothing at all, and it is in no document this county keeps and he has never put it in one.

**Nine roads would go back to one. The tenth would keep the history it had and the other eight would lose the histories they had and would take theirs out of it, and after that there would be no way of showing anybody which of the two had been true first, because a road that takes its dates from another road has no dates of its own left to show anybody. The method says nothing whatever about the woman who wrote the four of them and nothing whatever about where any of them is.**

Then he said it out loud, once, in his own trade, asked to about nine people in a room over a weigh-house, and nobody in nine counties has ever heard him refuse anything in a market street.

“**I am not going to put nine of them on the tenth, and that is the whole of what I have to say about those four sheets, and I will not say it a second time in this room.**”

It was thirty-nine words. About four people on that floor heard all of it and about five heard the first half of it and went on looking at the boards, and nobody said anything back to him for about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over.

There was no argument with him about it and there was no question about why, and nobody in nine counties has asked him why since, and about four people in that market street have said since that a man who is not going to be asked why has had to have worked out what he is going to say next, and about five have said that a refusal in his own trade to about nine people who keep records is the only kind of refusal there is.

There was no thanks in that room for it and there has been none in nine counties, and about nine hundred people along eleven miles of made road have not heard that it happened, and about four people in that market street have said since that being the man who refused is not an office anybody in this county has agreed to give him and about five have said that nobody asked him for it.

The four copied pages stayed on the top shelf of that press, and they are in that room and not in his keeping, and about nine people in that market street have worked out that a thing a man has refused stays where the person who keeps it keeps it, and about five have worked out that nobody in nine counties has asked the person who keeps it whether she agrees with the refusal, and she did not ask them to.

There was nobody at the head of that cart track in this six weeks, and no hurdle was lifted and no bar was tried. The four pages do not name where anything is shut and nobody has asked the people at the end of a shut road anything, and they have said nothing and have not been asked and are not going to be, and about four people in nine counties have said that this month is the fourth month of not asking them and about five have said that a thing nobody has asked in four months has not gone anywhere and would not have gone anywhere if they had.

On the stone sill of that weigh-house door, under the hood where the rain does not reach, there are four lines of chalk. They mean four. They have been dry and sharp there since the forty-ninth of Mudmonth and about four hundred people went past that door that morning without looking down at it, and nobody has read them out loud to anybody in about four and a half years.

At the front of the Crown's road office at the low end of that market street there is a counter and behind it there is a man who was born in that street, and he was at that counter at about the tenth hour with a sheet of foolscap in front of him and a pen in his hand and nothing to write on it, and nobody in nine counties has asked him what a gate is for.

At the back of that office there is a low room with a door in front of it and a clerk's table and a press with no date on it and a drawer eleven inches deep. That room was shut behind its own door on that Saturday as it has been on every morning since the twenty-sixth of Frostmonth, and nobody in nine counties went into it, and the drawer in it was not opened once. The county's one seal is still in there with its ribbon round it and a set of standard weights beside it, and nobody has asked what a seal is for.

Four days out, a hundred and four drove have gone up that lane since the second of Mudmonth YR 322, and the last of them went up on the Thursday before, which was the fourth of Mudmonth, at about the sixth hour, and stood in the field inside the stone wall and came back down at about the fifth hour. Nobody in that street has ever watched one go up and about four hundred people in nine counties have never watched one go up.

One man has been carrying that stone about two miles in his own hands for seven hundred and twenty-four days, and the gap in that wall is about nine feet long and about four feet high, and the ninth yard has not been started, and the mallet is on the bank about two miles off with the line of stone and it was not picked up. The third course of sill at the culvert at this town's own gate is unlaid.

At the causeway four days south the hand lamp goes on the coping at the low side every Monday afternoon in the winter, and the sixth of Mudmonth is a Saturday, and the lamp was not put there. The water was off the causeway and the hand bell on the gate post above that coping is the size of a fist and was not rung, and about four hundred people live on the far bank of that water and have never once been across in about nine years, and the four miles above that lamp are the smoothest four miles of made road in nine counties and about nine hundred people have not been told why.

At the head of that cart track nine hurdles are still across it foot to foot and the thumb of lime on the shoe of the top one has gone to powder. The last cart of lime came up that track four hundred and forty-three days ago and the road behind the hurdles has been shut four hundred and twenty-four days, and nobody has been up that track since the second of Goatmonth, and nobody lifted a hurdle and nobody tried a bar and nobody is going to this year.

Behind the ninth of the nine stones there are about nine hundred yards of grass nine feet wide that has been dead inside its own width for four hundred and ninety-one days, and the dead grass stops at the ninth of them, and nobody has ever been past the ninth of them and the far side of them has never been shown to anybody.

A refusal that nobody argues with is the cheapest thing in this county and it is not free, being that it is paid for out of the mornings it is said on, and about four people in nine counties have said that and about five have said that a man who refuses in a room of about nine people on a Saturday is a man who has spent a market morning doing it and about four hundred people in that street went on buying string.

The five frames of brood in that walled yard are under about nine straw skirts and no sixth has gone in, and the young man of about nineteen has carried a pail in and out of that yard about nine times this week and has not opened a skirt and has not added a sixth frame, and about nine people in nine counties have seen him do it and about four have asked him nothing about it.

The rest of that Saturday went on, and it went on the way a market morning goes on, being that about four hundred people in that street did about four hundred things that have nothing to do with a press in a room over a weigh-house. The near wheel of a cart came off at the low end of that street and the carter got it up again with a bar in about nine minutes without anybody stopping on his account, and the two women at the third door took a bolt of cloth apart in the doorway over about four minutes by the clock and went in after it and came out with it wrapped and one of them carrying a second parcel she had not gone in for. The inn at the high end had four beds full and kept its board out on the made street from first light.

The light went off the west bank about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over and found the upper edge of those boards, and the lamp at the high end of them was lit, because the ninth hour of a Saturday in Mudmonth is dark and there is wet slate on that frame to need it. Five of about nine people went out of that street into about five trades and about four stayed at the boards.

Nine doors down the cloth was over the bench and nobody lifted it and nobody asked what was under it. The half leaf in the press in that room over the weigh-house is face down and was not turned over on the day of a refusal. The three series in that room are three series and none of them is derived from another and the band figure is not written down anywhere in this county.

The cost of that Saturday was the leg, which took four hundred paces of a lane and about four hundred yards of a made road to reach that frame and thirteen stairs after that, and it had a hot band across the knee by about the eleventh hour, and the ear on the side the stick is on, which is gone, and which is the side she spoke from.

And there was, on the inside of one wrist, a bleed one hundred and fifty-one days old that had not faded by anything he had done to it, that nobody in nine counties has asked him about, that is in no document this county keeps, that has never been said out loud in a room in front of one other person, and that a refusal in a room of about nine people does not touch. Four lines on a sill are not a cure for it. Neither is a page in a woman's hand on the back of a day-book. Neither is a sentence in an unclaimed hand on a folded sheet inside the covering paper of the second copy of that roll. Neither is a bleed that has not faded in one hundred and fifty-one days.

He went out of that market street at about the eleventh hour with the leg grinding and did not go up the made road past the ninth mile stone, and did not go in at the low end of that street, and the low room at the back of that office stayed shut behind its own door. Five things were on that board and thirty-seven days past the date and the fourth line of the roll empty on all three of its copies, and nobody was thanked for any of it.

**And the cost of this is a refusal honoured and not argued with, thirty-nine words said out loud once in his own trade in a room over a weigh-house to about nine people, and nobody asking him why and nobody in nine counties asking him why since. The four copied pages stayed on the top shelf of a press that has no date on it above a half leaf that stays face down, and they are in the keeping of the person who keeps them and not in his, and about four hundred places are writing a paper out by hand and no two of about four hundred copies agree because nobody has asked them to. He gave up the one history that could not have been shown to be wrong and kept nine that each can be. One hundred and fifty-one days of a bleed on the inside of one wrist that has not faded by anything anybody has done to it and that nobody in nine counties knows about, one thing gone quiet four hundred and forty-three days ago and alive and shut and unnamed and unheld, about nine hundred yards of grass four days out that will not come back, the use of one ear, the use of one leg, and a year, none of which is in any of it and none of which has a price.**
