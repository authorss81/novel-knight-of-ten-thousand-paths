# Chapter 0275

**The seventh day of Longlight, YR 316, a Monday, and not a market day, and the second hour to the sixth hour, and a crossing four hundred yards up a fen county's own road that carries nobody and has carried nobody for four years, and a sheet of the office's own printed series in the sill of it, and about four hundred people in a town of about four thousand people on a river who do not know that a man of thirty-two with a limp stood in a toll house at about the third hour of that Monday and read a thing about a crossing out of its own sill and found a person's name in a column with a head on it, and that the person is two hundred miles off at a register, and that she is not a keeper of anything and has never been asked.**

He went up the road at about the second hour and got to the toll house in about nine minutes at a rate that is a great deal better than the nine days of the made road out of this county in Mudmonth, because a lane is short and a made road is not, and about four people in this county have said since that the only thing that made those nine days long was that there was a valley at the end of them and a lane is four hundred yards.

The sheet was where a bag leaves things, in the sill, weighted with a stone off the bank. The weather had got at one corner of it and the corner had gone soft and the words on it had run about a ninth of an inch down the sheet, so that a name in the fourth column is a name with a leg on it.

He read it in about a minute and a half and he read it once.

Six columns and five heads. The object column is the first and it has a place in it and a word in front of the place name, and the word is the office's own and he has seen that word in a drawer and on a sheet and in a bag and he has never once known what it is for and is not going to ask.

The fourth column is headed *Keeper* and there is a name in it.

And a name in a column with a head on it is a claim about a person, and a claim about a person is the only kind of paper in this kingdom that can be produced in a room a long way off, and nobody in that room is going to be asked, and the room has a table of three in it and a form of oath printed on the back of a return.

He knew the name. He had never seen it before and he knew it, because a name in a fourth column is a loop and nine shapes and a particular way of leaving a stroke short at the top, and he has been looking at that hand for four years in one room and two hundred and forty rooms, and it is a hand he could pick out of a tray of four hundred in about a minute and a quarter, and he picked it out of a sill in a minute and a half.

He did not know who she was. He did not know that she was two hundred miles off or that she filled that column about two hundred times in a season or that she had been afraid of it for eleven years. He knew a hand. There is a difference between the two and it is the whole of what he had at about the third hour of that Monday.

---

And a man of forty-seven was at the low end of that sill at about the third hour with a hod he was not carrying anything in, and he is the man who puts his hand up in a market when a man asks who is going, and he is not on a trade and does not hold the reach or this crossing or anything else in this county.

He had been asked one question. That is the whole of the conversation between two men at that crossing on that Monday, and it took about eleven seconds.

“**Have you seen that before,**” the man of thirty-two with a limp said.

**“Not me,” the man of forty-seven said. “I do not know whose it is. I have been in that house about nine times in two years and it is the same in it as far as I have looked, and I have not looked at it, and I am not going to start.”**

That was the whole of the answer and none of it was the four things.

The man of forty-seven has said the same four things five times in a year, to a yard wall, to a bench-board, to a man with a stick, and to nobody besides, and he was not asked for a sixth occasion on that Monday and he did not offer one, and a man of forty-seven who is not asked a sixth occasion in a year is a man whose list is the same length it was in Harvestmonth and is going to be the same length at the end of this year.

“**You are on the roll,**” the man of thirty-two said.

**“I am on the roll,” the man of forty-seven said, “and I have not used my vote since the day they put me on it, and I did not use it then, and I have never told anybody that in that room and I am not going to start now, and you will want to know why and I have not worked it out and it is not your business.”**

**“It is not my business,” the man of thirty-two with a limp said, and meant it, and did not improve on it, and about four people in that lane have said since that two men in a toll house said the word *business* twice in about a minute and neither of them asked the other one anything, and that a man who does not hold a thing and is on the roll of the thing is the only kind of man in this county that there is.**

---

A man of fifty-five sat in a hut at a barrier of three bars three hundred yards down that road, and he has been in that hut nineteen years and has turned round about four hundred people and let about nine thousand through, and the four words he gives at that barrier are the same four words three hundred yards from any two of them, and nobody in that county has ever worked out where a set of four words comes from.

“**Is somebody after the toll,**” he said.

“**There is no toll,**” the man of thirty-two with a limp said. “It has not had a toll since before you were asked to sit here and a court has said it has nothing further to say about it at any time.”

**“Then I have nothing to do,” the man of fifty-five said, “and I have said the same four things to about four hundred people in nineteen years and I have never once been asked where I got them, and you are not going to ask me, and I would rather you did not, because I have looked and I cannot tell you.”**

The man of fifty-five was correct. Nobody in that lane has ever asked a man of fifty-five where he got the four words and he has never said and he is not going to, and about four people in that lane have said since that a man who has been asked the same question four hundred times and has never once been asked it is a man the county has decided not to ask, and that is not the same as a man nobody has thought about.

---

**And he could not get up the bank, and that is the first fact about that Monday afternoon and it is the fact that the rest of it stands on.**

The bank is about four feet off the lane at the near end of the crossing and it is the side the hemp went in on, and a man with a permanent limp in the right leg and a stick that is a ferrier's stick, ash, four feet nine, cut out of a fence rail in a county that is not on any map, cannot get up four feet in one movement and cannot get up it in four either.

He got up about two feet of it and sat down on the stone with the mortar on and put his hands out in front of him and did not go the rest of the way, and he was sitting on the dressed block when he worked out that he had been sitting on it.

He did not put his left hand flat on it. His left hand was warm at about the fourth hour every day and has been since a lane in a county that is not on any map, and it was about the fourth hour, and it was on his own knee.

And nobody saw him sit down and nobody saw him get up off it, and about four people in that town have said since that a man who sits down on a thing four hundred yards up a road at about the fourth hour and gets up off it again without putting a hand on it has done something the most careful man in this county has never done, and that the most careful man in this county is not him, and that nobody has ever worked out who is.

He went over the sheet again at about the fifth hour, standing up in the lane with the wind on his back, and it gave him four things it had not given him at the third hour.

The first was that the line is a week late and every line round it is a week late, and a line that is late can be argued with, and a line that is complete cannot.

The second was that there is no figure in the second of four places ruled for a figure, and that a classification with a place in it and no figure in it has not been costed, and that a thing that has not been costed has not been decided about by anybody who pays for it.

The third was that the fourth column has a name in it and the name is not of anybody in this county, and that a keeper of a place is a person, and a person who is entered as a keeper of a place in a county she is not in is a person who cannot say no to it.

The fourth was that the last of the six is not a column at all, because it has no head on it, and that a column with a head on it is something a person can refuse and a space without one is something a person can only be entered into, and that nobody is going to be entered into it, and that he is not going to be either, and that the sheet has no way of knowing he came to a sill and read it.

---

He went down the lane at about the sixth hour past a man of about sixty packing lime at the end of a yard in the open with a lid on a heap, and the man of about sixty did not turn round, and about nine men in that yard did not look up, and the man of thirty-two with a limp went up to a room over a smithy that he pays for by the week and that nobody has ever asked him anything about, and he did not light anything and he did not eat, and he was not ill.

He had the sheet on the table in front of him and the four findings in it, and about the ninth hour he said one of them out loud to a room with nobody in it, and it was nine words, and nobody answered him, and he did not say it again.

**“A line that is late can be argued with.”**

That is not the marshal's office's sentence and it is not the county's and it is not in any form, and he had it in about four years and had never said it, and there are about nine men in this kingdom who have said the other half of it, and one of them is four hundred miles off in a room with a long table in it, and this man does not know that he has it and has not been told.

---

And the sixth day of Greenmonth is the day a man of thirty-two with a limp came back into this county after nine days, and the seventh day of Longlight is the day before the eighth, and the eighth is a Tuesday and is not a market day, and about four hundred people in a town of about four thousand people on a river do not know that the same man has been at that crossing twice in three days and has not put a hand on anything on either occasion, and that nobody has asked him why a man with a limp goes up a bank he cannot get up, and that he has not said, and that the day-book of an office four hundred yards down that lane has nine entries in it of a thing that was not asked and did not get a tenth on that morning, and that the not-asking is not a courtesy and is not a rule and is the ordinary habit of a room, and that a room hears a thing once.
