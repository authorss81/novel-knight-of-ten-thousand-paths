# Chapter 0263

**The fourteenth day of Mudmonth, YR 316, a Thursday, and a market day, and the fourth hour, and a room with a stove in it on the first floor of a building in a provincial town two hundred miles from a fen county, and about four hundred people in a town of about four thousand people on a river who do not know that a sheet went into a record in that room on that Thursday with a name in the fourth column of it that nobody entered, and that the name is the name of a woman who was at a bench on that floor at about the first hour, and that the second column of that record is the second column of a form and not a name, and that about four of the six columns on that sheet are ruled for a figure and there is nowhere on the whole of it for the name of a person.**

The clerk of the returns read the sheet up before he entered it. That is the way that room works and it is not a thing anybody in it has ever written down, and he read it in about a minute and a quarter and then asked one question, and the question was about the fifth column and not the fourth, and nobody in the room noticed that the question was about the fifth column and not the fourth except about four people who noticed it on a stair about two days later.

“**Is that the day it came in.**”

“It is the day it came in.”

He entered the place in the first column in about four seconds and the day in the fifth in about two and he entered the fourth column from the sheet without reading it out, and about four people in that building have said since that a man who has read a sheet up in a room in a minute and a quarter and then entered a name from it in about a second is a man who read the sheet up for the two columns that have figures in them, and that the two columns that have figures in them are the two columns he can check.

**And a form with four places ruled for a figure and nowhere at all for the name of a person is not a form that anybody in that room was taught to distrust, and about four people in that room have said since that the office has printed about nine hundred copies of it in nine counties and there are four hundred and nine parish rolls behind the nine counties, and that the whole of the arrangement works, and that the only part of the arrangement that is broken is the one part nobody has ever been shown, and that nobody has been shown it because there is nothing to show: a person was never asked to be on the paper.**

The tray went out at about the seventh hour with four things in it and came back at the first hour of the Friday morning, and the man of about sixty who carries it put it on the bench and said nothing, and the man of about twenty-nine who is a clerk of the returns was in the room and did not look at the tray and about four people in that room have said since that a man who has carried a thing for twenty-two years and has said once that nobody ever asked whether he would rather it went at the sixth has been asked nothing about it since and is not going to be, and that the hour has been wrong for twenty-two years and that a wrong hour is the only thing in that building that everybody agrees about and nobody has written down.

The woman of about thirty-three was at a press on that floor at about the fifth hour and the man of about twenty-nine was four feet off her and neither of them said anything to the other one, and about four people in that room have said since that two people in a room who have each said a true thing about a column in the last three days and are four feet apart at the fifth hour is the ordinary condition of that room and always has been, and that the office was not built for two people to be in it and there is no column in that building for the distance between them.

---

**And a man of about thirty came down at about the second hour of that Thursday with a form and went back up and about four people on that stair have said since that a man who carries a form down a stair and up a stair and does not stop on the landing has a thing he is not sure of and that the not stopping is the whole of the method, and that about four hundred forms a month go through that building and the great majority of them are carried by somebody up or down a stair by a person who is not sure of one thing in them, and that the office calls that a circulation and it is not a circulation, it is about forty people each carrying a thing they did not write to about nine people who will decide nothing about it.**

**And a woman of about twenty-nine who wanted the sixth column ruled for six years asked one question in that room on that Thursday and it was at about the sixth hour and it was to a man of about fifty who had come up from the ground floor with a sheet under his arm, and it was six words, and she has asked nothing about the sixth column in six years and is not going to ask about it again and the six words are not about the sixth column.**

“**Did you write that name yourself.**”

He said no and it was a true thing and about four people in that room have said since that a man who has entered about eleven thousand forms in thirty-one years said the truth about nine shapes in about a second and a half without hesitating, and that the not hesitating is the frightening part and that nobody in that room has ever asked him anything else about the nine shapes and is not going to.

She asked him nothing else and he went down and the woman of about thirty-three was still at the press and did not hear either question, and about four people in that room have said since that the two of them have now said one true thing each about the same column within about four hours of each other and neither of them knows the other one has, and that nobody in that building is going to tell them and that the sixth column is still unruled and still the same width as the fifth.

---

---

**And about nine hundred people in a town of about four thousand people live in that town and about nine hundred of them have been in that building at some point in the last year, and about nine of those nine hundred have been in the chamber at the second hour on a Wednesday, and about four of the nine have noticed that a line went in, and about two of the four have said out loud that a line went in, and neither of the two was in the room, and about four people in that building have said since that a thing two floors above a register is a thing a register cannot see, and that this is not because anybody has hidden it and is because the room with a stove in it is a floor above the floor with the bench in it and a room is a room and a floor is a floor and about four hundred people have never once asked what is directly above their own heads in a building they work in every day.**

A woman of about thirty-three came in at about the fifth hour and asked the man of about twenty-nine a question about a form and it was an ordinary question and it took about nine seconds and it was about the second column, and he answered it in about four seconds, and they were four feet apart and about nine other people were in the room, and about four people in that room have said since that two people who both work in the fourth column of a form in the same building asked each other about the second column at about the fifth hour of a Thursday and neither of them asked about the fourth one, and that this has been happening in that room for about eleven years and that the fourth column is the only one in that building that has never been the subject of a question between two people who both fill it in.

---

**And a sheet of the office's own printed series is a penny, and a second sheet of the same series for the same classification is a penny, and a clerk's fee for entering one classification is fourpence, and the tray goes out and comes back and is not charged to anybody because the man who carries it is paid at the year and a year is not a column in that office.**

*A penny and a penny is twopence, and twopence and fourpence is sixpence.*

**Total: six pence. And that is the whole of what it cost that building to enter one line about a length of made road in a county two hundred miles off, and about four hundred people in a town of about four thousand people on a river do not know that the figure is six pence, and the six pence is not a figure of persons and is added to nothing, and the figure of persons that is not printed is not printed here either, and nobody in that room has put the two anywhere near each other and is not going to.**

---

---

**And the file the sheet went into is a file of about nine sheets and the wrapper on it has nothing on it, and about four people in that building have said since that a wrapper with nothing on it is the only honest thing in the whole of that room and that the four sheets in a file in a press in a county two hundred miles off that a man of twenty-nine walked past about four times a day for about two years had nothing on its wrapper either, and that the two files have never been in the same room and that nobody in either county knows the other one exists.**

He put it in at the end and closed it and did not write on the wrapper, and about four people in that building have said since that a man who closes a file and does not write on the wrapper is a man who has decided that the wrapper is not a place for the truth, and that in about nine years of files in that room about four people have done that and about four of them are still there, and that nobody has ever asked any of the four why and the four have never said and about four people on that floor have said since that a person who has never been asked why a thing is not written down is a person who has an answer ready and does not want the answer used.

And a man of about fifty came up at about the seventh hour on that Thursday and asked one question at the top of the stair and it was about the tray and not about the name, and it was four words, and the man of about twenty-nine answered it in about two and neither of them said anything else and about four people on that stair have said since that a man who has just had a name put in a column in his own hand and has not been asked about it has gone downstairs and asked a clerk of the returns a question about a tray, and that the two questions are the same question and that neither of the two men has worked out that they are the same question and is not going to.

---

**And the schedule is a day later and it is still a week late and the line is complete, and a line with a place in it and a name in it is a thing that can be acted on, and about four people in that building have said since that a schedule is a count of places and a count of places is a form of words, and that a form of words with a name in the fourth column of it is not a count of anything at all any more and is an instruction, and that nobody in that room noticed the moment it stopped being one of the two and has been told that it happened on a Thursday at about the fifth hour by nobody.**

The name in the fourth column of that line is the name of a woman who is at a register on the first floor of that building at about the first hour every morning, and she has filled that column about two hundred times in a season for eleven years, and she is not a member of anything and has never been, and there is a leaf face down on the second shelf of a case behind her with nineteen words on it in her own hand that she wrote on the sixth of a month in a year before last, and she has not turned it over, and nobody in that building has asked her to, and she has said nothing about the fourth column this week and is not going to.
