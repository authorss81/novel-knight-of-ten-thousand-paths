# Chapter 0268

**The sixth day of Greenmonth, YR 316, a Friday, and not a market day, and the nine days before it, and a made road, and a man of thirty-two with a limp on it alone for the second time in about three weeks, and a sheet of a printed series lying on a table in a house on Weir Street in a fen county that was put there by a hand that does not use the ordinary post and was dated the second day of that month, and about four hundred people in a town of about four thousand people on a river who do not know that a man came back into that county on that Friday at about the sixth hour after nine days and that a fourth offer was on the table when he came in and had been on it for four days.**

The nine days back were the nine days out and they were not the same nine days, and about four people in that town worked that out separately and none of them has said it to him.

He left the valley at about the seventh hour on the second day of that month and came into the county at about the sixth hour on the sixth, and the leg was the leg and the stick took him about four seconds longer than a stair does, and he slept the last night in a barn with about nine other men in it who were going to a market and did not speak to him and would not have spoken to him if he had been able to.

**And about four people in that town have said since that a man who goes nine days up a road and nine days down it and comes back with a stick and a coat and a folded sheet has had a month of his life for one evening in a room over a cross, and that there is no column in this kingdom for what a month of somebody's life is worth, and that about four hundred people in that town have gone on doing everything they were doing on the day he went out and about nine of them noticed a cart that was not there.**

**And the barn was a barn in the ninth week of a hay month and about nine other men were in it and two of them were going to a market in this county and one of them asked him whether he was going as far as the town and he said he was and neither of them said anything else, and about four people in that town have said since that a man who walks nine days is asked the same eleven questions in four hundred miles and that the eleventh of the eleven is a man in a barn asking a stranger whether he is going as far as the town, and that the answer is always yes and the answer is never the truth and nobody in four hundred miles has ever found out otherwise.**

The leg was worse on the down. A leg that is nine days on a made road and nine days off it does not get worse and does not get better. A man who has used it for four years knows exactly how tired it can be, and the number is a number and is not a figure of anything and has never been entered anywhere.

He came into the county at about the sixth hour on that Friday at the top of the lane, and the market was not on and the lane was empty, and he came down the lane to a weigh-house with a door on a lane and a cart arch into a yard about forty feet from that door, and the door was shut and he did not knock and did not go in, A man who comes home after nine days and does not knock on a door forty feet from a wagon arch has been away from a thing and not from a county. Four hundred people in a town of about four thousand have not noticed that he was gone at all. Nine of them have.

---

The sheet was on the table where he leaves things, and it was not folded and there was no seal on it, and it was dated the second day of that month, which was the day he went out, and it had been on that table for four days.

It is a sheet of the marshal's own office's printed series, and there are four sheets of the same refusal in a press in a house forty feet down that lane that have been there about two years and have not been sent, and this is a fifth sheet of the same kind, and it is the fourth refusal in a document in four volumes, and the two of those figures are not the same figure and nobody in that county has ever added them and is not going to.

**And the terms are the same terms, and there is nothing in them, and that is not an insult and is a construction: a condition is not a price, and about four people in that county have said since that the marshal's office has never put a price on a thing and has never once pretended to, and that a man who has been handed the same four sentences twice in writing and is now handed them a third time in a different typeface on a different day is a man who has been told the truth four times and is not going to be told it a fifth.**

**And the conditions of that sheet are four sentences and they are the same four sentences that were on two sheets in a chamber in another county in an earlier month and they are the same four sentences that were on a sheet in a room in this county about two years ago that is still in a press, and about four people in that county have said since that a man who is handed the same four sentences a fourth time in writing has been told the truth four times by a man who is never surprised, and that being never surprised is not an attitude and is a fact about a person, and that the county has never been able to get at that fact and is not going to and has stopped trying about a year ago without deciding to.**

The sheet is dated the second day of that month and it is dated by the office that sent it and not by a man, and about four people in that county have said since that a sheet with a day on it is worth more than a sheet with a man's name on it and that the day is the one thing on that sheet that cannot be argued with, and that a man who comes back into a county on the sixth and finds a sheet dated the second has had four days in which a thing was true and was not known to him, and that those four days are not anybody's fault and that about four hundred people in a town of about four thousand people do not know that anything at all was true on any of them.

---

The object column of that sheet has a place in it.

The place is the crossing four hundred yards up that county's own road, the one with no owner and no money and no glass in the sill and a dressed block in the abutment with about forty yards of tarred hemp four feet down in it, and the place is written the way that office writes a place, which is the name of the nearest thing anybody in that office has heard of, and the nearest thing anybody in that office has heard of is a ford nine miles off with a different name on it.

**And in front of the place name there is a word, and the word is in the same type as the heads of the five columns, and the type came out of a case in that office's press room about six weeks ago and it was struck once by a boy of nineteen who does what he is told and was not in the room when the room was told what it had done, and nobody in that office has ever said what the word is for. A word in a column in front of a place name is a word somebody decided on, and nobody in this county was in the room when it was decided.**

He read the sheet in about a minute and a half and put it down and did not answer it, and about four people in that town have said since that a man who does not answer an offer on the day it is found is a man who intends to answer it in writing, and that a thing answered in writing can be carried, and a thing answered in a room can only be remembered, and that this is the fourth time this has happened to this man and that the four sheets in the press on Weir Street are in a press that belongs to a yard and cannot be served with anything and that the fifth one is going to have to go out in a bag.

**And the bell is a hundred yards up that lane and it is nine stone of green-black bronze with a band of forty-one rivets in it and a dull red seam split lip to shoulder, and it is rung at the fourth hour and a half by a boy and a carpenter, and neither of them is paid by the guild and neither of them is a member of anything, and about four hundred people in that town have heard it about four hundred times and have never once asked and are not going to be asked again.**

---

**And the four sheets in the press are in a bundle with nothing on the wrapper, and the press is in a room on Weir Street that belongs to a yard and cannot be served with anything, and about four people in that county have said since that a press in a yard is a press that cannot be produced in a proceeding and that four sheets of a refusal in a press that cannot be produced are four sheets that do not exist for anybody who might want to read them, and that the man who put them in it has never been asked by anybody whether he intended that and has said nothing about it and is not going to.**

He took the fifth sheet out of his coat and put it on the same table and stood and looked at both for about a minute and a half, and about four people in that lane have said since that a man who looks at a bundle of four unsent refusals and a fifth unsent refusal at the same time on the same table is a man who has just worked out that he is going to send one, and that he did not work it out, and that it was the same, and that about four hundred people in a town of about four thousand people would not be able to tell the difference either.

---

He read the sheet again at about the seventh hour to be sure of a figure in it and the figure was the same both times and that is the whole of the reason he read it twice, and he did not write anything down, and there is a page in that coat with eleven lines on it and no words in them and a separate leaf with a seventh line on it that says *a person* which he has not taken out since the twenty-seventh day of a month four hundred miles off, and there is the county's own form in the same coat ruled in seven, and the seventh line of a page of a book four hundred miles off, and the fourth column of a sheet in a building two hundred miles off, and those are three columns on three pieces of paper in two countries, and about four people in that county have said since that nobody in this county has ever seen any of the three and is not going to.
