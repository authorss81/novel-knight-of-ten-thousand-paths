# Chapter 0252

**The twenty-sixth day of Goatmonth, YR 315, a Friday, and not a market day, and the second hour and the fourth hour and the seventh hour, and a room with a stove in it in a provincial town two hundred miles from a fen county, and about four hundred forms a month out of a press across four floors into nine counties and four hundred and nine parish rolls, and about nine of the four hundred come back and eight of the nine come back with a figure in them and one comes back with a space empty and four lines beside it, and about nine hundred people in that building do not know that anything has happened in it.**

The proceeding was entered at about the second hour on the day after the return went out, and a proceeding is a thing that gets a date, and the date is the second thing anybody in that room has to decide and the first thing anybody in that room has to be wrong about.

There is no form for a proceeding in that office. There is a form for a return and a form for a form and a form for a letter that has come back from a county, and all three are of the same printed series and all three are ruled in six columns with five heads, and a proceeding is not any of them and about four people in that building had worked that out on the day before and had not said it to anybody.

**And a woman of about twenty-nine who is a clerk of the returns wrote it on the back of a form because there was nothing else to write it on, and the back of a form is where the form of oath is printed, and nobody in that room said what was on the sheet she was writing on.**

A woman of about twenty-nine has been in that room six years and has written three leaves in that time that anybody in that building has read twice, and one of the three is a leaf about a column with no head on it and the other two are about the same column, and she has wanted in that six years to be the person who signs a return, and there is no line on a return for a signature, and about nine returns go out of that building twice a year and not one of them has ever been signed by a person.

She said one thing about it and it was in her own voice and it was the only thing she said in that room that day, and she said it to the stove and not to the man of about sixty with a tray, and four people in that building have said since that a woman who says a thing about a signature to a stove in a room with nine people in it has decided not to be answered and has not decided anything else.

“**I have wanted six years to sign a return and there is no line for it, and this morning I have written a proceeding on the back of an oath, which is worse, and I would like somebody to notice that it is worse and nobody is going to.**”

---

**And what she wrote on the back of that form took about eleven minutes, and the fourth of the eleven is the whole of a date and the eighth of the eleven is the whole of a pigeonhole.**

The proceeding wanted a date. The date was the twenty-fifth of Goatmonth, YR 315, because a proceeding is convened on the day it is asked for and not on the day it is written, and the day it was asked for was the day the summons went out of a press in that building, and the summons had been in a bag in the ordinary post since the seventh hour of that day and had come up a lane in a fen county at about the sixth hour in the evening and been put on a sill.

A woman of about twenty-nine wrote the date and stopped with the pen on the paper, and asked a question out loud, and the question was to a man of about sixty with a tray who has carried that tray out of that room at the seventh hour of the evening and back at the first hour of the morning for twenty-two years and who has never once been asked anything about a date.

“**Can a thing be undecided.**”

“**I have carried the tray twenty-two years,**” the man said. “**I have never been asked that.**”

“**It is not a question about the tray.**”

“**It is a question about a date and I have never been asked one of those either.**”

**And she wrote the date anyway, and that is the whole of the fourth of the eleven minutes, and a date on a proceeding is a thing that cannot be taken back by anybody who is not the person who put it there, and a proceeding is a question that has been given a day, and a question with a day on it is a different animal, and a woman of about twenty-nine knew that at about the second hour and a half of the twenty-sixth day of Goatmonth, YR 315 and has not said it out loud since.**

She wrote the name of the body. The name of the body is four words and a number and the number is the number a body corporate has had for four years and a half because a body corporate is a door and a number before it is anything else, and she wrote the number and not the four words, and about four people in that building have said that a proceeding against a body corporate is the one kind of paper in that building that cannot be sent to a county, because a county has no office that can receive it and the door is a door and a door does not come to a room.

She wrote the date and she wrote the hour and she wrote the room, and the room is two floors above that room and it has a table of three in it and a clerk of the Crown, and a room with a table of three in it is a room that a person can be put in front of and that a room with a table of nine in it is a room that a person can be asked, and that that office has about four hundred forms a month and has never once in thirty-one years written down which of the two it was.

**And she wrote the fifth thing, and the fifth thing is the thing a proceeding cannot be written without, and she wrote it in about four seconds and it is the whole of a woman's want and it is four or five words long and it has not changed since she was twenty-two.**

The fifth thing is a line for a person having knowledge. The sheet is ruled in six columns, five of them with heads, and this is the third of the five, and it is ruled for a figure and there is nowhere on the sheet, in any column, in any line, at the head or the foot, for the name of a person.

She wrote a figure in it. That is a clerk of about twenty-nine putting a figure into a line with a head on it at about the second hour and a half of a Friday, and four people in that building have said since that a woman who has wanted six years to sign a return and has found that there is no line for a signature has found a building where there is a line for a person and no line for a person, and that she put a figure in it because a figure is the only thing that fits, and that she has not told anybody what the figure is and is not going to.

**And she did not tell the man of about sixty with a tray what the figure was, and the man of about sixty with a tray did not ask, and there is no column in that office for two people having agreed about a thing, and there has never been one, and four people in that building have worked that out in the ordinary way over about nine years and have never said it out loud because there is nothing to say it to.**

---

**And then she did the eighth of the eleven minutes, and the eighth of the eleven is a man of about sixty with a tray putting two files in one pigeonhole, and it took about four seconds, and nobody in that building has ever worked out how long it took.**

A hearing in that province stands. It was entered for the ninth of Rainmonth for a delegation that cannot arrive, and it was heard with nobody in it, and it was not adjourned and it was not refused, and the delegation was not sent for and is not going to be sent for. Beside that hearing, and not against it, there is a file of one sheet, and the sheet has a district on it, and the district came out of a return, and four people in that building worked that out on the fourth of Goatmonth and about nine hundred in that building have not.

The new file would not go on the returns shelf, because the returns shelf is four inches deep and has been four inches deep since the press was made. The working papers are on a second shelf and the hearing is in a pigeonhole on that second shelf with its companion file, and the pigeonhole is two inches deep, and a file of one sheet and a file of one sheet both go in a pigeonhole two inches deep.

So the man of about sixty with a tray put the two of them in together, and the second one went on top of the first one, and the label of the first one was underneath the label of the second one, and a man of about sixty with a tray has carried that tray out of that room at the seventh hour of the evening and back at the first hour of the morning for twenty-two years and has never in twenty-two years put two files in one pigeonhole, and he did it on the twenty-sixth day of Goatmonth, YR 315 because the second shelf was full and the returns shelf was full and his hands were full.

**And that is the first time in about nine years that anything in that building has joined a thing to a thing by accident, and the join is a proceeding against a body corporate in one county to a hearing in the same province for a delegation that cannot arrive, and the join is made of a file of one sheet with a district on it that was opened beside a hearing and has been beside it since the fourth of Goatmonth, YR 315, and nobody made the join and nobody was told about the join and nobody in that building is going to be told about the join.**

The district has about nine market towns in it. One of the nine is a market four hundred yards long on a Saturday and on a Thursday, twice a week, in a town of about four thousand people on a river, and a body corporate in that town has a door on a lane and fourpence a year and five questions in a register and not one finding, and the district is on the sheet because a sheet came in with it on, and the office made the district up out of a table about a hundred years ago and has never sent a form to it and does not know what a district is, and a man of about fifty who is a clerk of the press said so out loud to himself in that building on a Thursday in Goatmonth and nobody answered him, and about four people in that building have said since that the sentence he said about it is seventeen words and that the figure he gave for it is not seventeen.

Nobody in that room put the two together and nobody in that room read both files, and the man of about sixty with a tray has said since that a pigeonhole is a pigeonhole and that he put two things in it because there was nowhere else to put them and that if anybody wants to know what the sheet under his one is for they can come and get it, and four people in that building have said since that a man who has carried a tray for twenty-two years has just described a filing system and did not know it, and that about nine hundred people in that building do not know that anything has been joined to anything.

---

**And a man of about fifty who is a clerk of the press and has entered about eleven thousand forms in thirty-one years came into that room at about the fifth hour with a press cloth over his arm and was not asked anything, and he was in the room for about four minutes and he looked at the pigeonhole on the second shelf and did not ask what was in the bottom of it, and a man who has entered about eleven thousand of these forms knows what is under a form and what is not, and he did not ask, and nobody in that building is going to ask him either, because a man who is not asked does not answer, and because a man who is asked has to say which of the two things was under the sheet.**

He left at about the fifth hour and went down two flights and did not stop at the press room drawer, and there is a drawer in that press room that has a word in it that the office printed two years ago and has never used, and he has walked past that drawer about four times a day for five months and has not once put his hand out to it, and that is the reason, and the reason is that a man who puts his hand out to a drawer in a press room finds out what is in it and a man who does not can keep walking.

**And the room with the stove is the same room on the Saturday morning as it was on the Friday morning, and the difference between the two is a sheet of paper about nine inches by six that was not in that room on the Thursday, and the stove in that room has been let out at about the fourth hour every working day for about nine years and is let out by a man of about sixty with a tray and not by anybody else, and a fire is the only thing in that building with a time against it that is not written on anything.**

The woman of about twenty-nine left at about the sixth hour. She left the register on the table, spine toward her, and the summons is not a copy of anything, it is the only one, and it lay on a table in a room with a stove until she came in on the Saturday at the second hour and put it into a folder with a strap on it, and there is no line on the folder for what a folder is for, and a folder with a strap on it is the only kind of paper in that building that nobody can serve anybody with.

**And about four hundred forms went into the press that month across four floors, and eight of every nine of the about four hundred that go out of it into nine counties come back with a figure in them, and one of the four hundred comes back with a space empty and four lines beside it, and all nine go into the returns press because a return is a return, and the only difference between the eight and the one is what is in a column, and there is no column in that building for what is in a column.**

The tray went out into the passage at about the seventh hour of that Friday evening with four things in it, and the tray is not a file and a bag is not a tray, and the two of them are the two places in that building where a thing can be for a long time and not be anywhere, and the tray came back at the first hour of the morning on the Saturday and about nine of the four hundred forms in that building go into the returns press on a Friday and eight of the nine come back with a figure in them, and about four people in that building do not know that a woman of about thirty-three who is an aid to the register filled in the second of four places on a sheet on the fourth of that month in about a minute and a quarter without looking up, and that she was not in that room at any hour of that day, and that her name is not in the pigeonhole and is not in the file and is not in the proceeding and is not going to be in any of the three.
