# Chapter 0256

**The ninth day of Thawmonth, YR 316, a Thursday, and a market day, and the sixth hour, and a press room off the room with the stove in it in a provincial town two hundred miles from a fen county, and seven presses across four floors and about four hundred forms a month going out of them into nine counties, and a drawer that has been open and shut about four times a day for two years by about nine people in this building, and about four hundred people in a town of about four thousand people on a river who do not know that a word the office printed two years ago and has never used was set in metal at about the sixth hour on the ninth of Thawmonth, YR 316 by a boy of nineteen who does what he is told and is not in the building when anybody finds out what he has done.**

The drawer is in the press room and it is the fourth drawer down in a case against the north wall and the case is one of nine and the drawer does not lock and nobody has ever put a lock on it, because a lock on a drawer of type in a press room is a thing that says the type is not to be used and the type is not to be used by anybody in that building and there is no need of a lock to say a thing that nine people already know.

The word is in the line with the heads of the five columns. That is the whole of where it is. It is set on its own sort with a space on either side of it and it is the same size as *Object* and *Date* and *Person having knowledge* and *Keeper* and *How often*, and it is the sixth of the six and it is the only one of the six that has never been struck, and it has been in that drawer since the week the form was made, which was about two years ago and was in a Harvestmonth, and four people in that building have said since that a word is not put in a case at the same size as a head by accident, and that nobody in that building has ever asked whose idea it was, and that two or three of them have asked each other whether they think it was somebody's idea, and that neither of them has ever asked anybody.

**And the sheet that was wanted at about the sixth hour was a single sheet of the office's own printed series with one line filled in, and the one line is the object column, and the one thing wanted in the object column was a place with a word in front of it, and a woman of about twenty-nine who is a clerk of the returns read the line over a man's shoulder at about the fifth hour and said the word out loud in that room for the first time in about two years and then said nothing else, and a boy of nineteen at the composing stone heard her say it and did not ask her what it meant and that is the whole of his part in it.**

“I want that word,” the woman said.

“**Which word.**”

“**The one in the drawer. Fourth down. It wants a space on either side and it wants to come out at the top of the line, before the place, and not after it.**”

The boy of nineteen set it in about four minutes. There is no mystery in setting a sort that has been in a drawer, and the four minutes were four minutes, and the space on either side is a quad and a quad is a piece of lead the width of an em and there is a box of them under the stone.

---

**And a woman of about twenty-nine who is a clerk of the returns has wanted one thing in six years in that room and she has never asked for it in a form and she asked for something else on the ninth of Thawmonth, YR 316 at about the fifth hour and a half, and the two things are the same thing and only one of them is a decision.**

She wants the sixth column ruled. That is the whole of it. It has no head on it and it is the same width as the other five and about four of the six are ruled for a figure and the sixth is ruled for a figure too, and about four hundred forms a month go out of that building with something or nothing in it and nothing has ever come back into it, and she has worked that out in six years and has not asked.

She said it in fourteen words at about the fifth hour and a half to a man of about fifty who was standing at the stone with a sheet in his hand, and she said it in her own voice, and nobody answered it. A woman who has wanted a rule in a column for six years and has not asked for it asked for a word instead on a Thursday afternoon. Asking for a head is a decision and asking for a word is not, and she knows the difference and has known it for nine years and made the smaller ask on purpose.

“**I want a word before the place and not a rule for the sixth.**”

The man of about fifty put his hand flat on the stone for about a second and then picked up a sheet. A man who has entered about eleven thousand of these forms in thirty-one years and has not once put his hand out to a drawer put his hand flat on a stone for about a second, and nobody in that building has asked him what the second was, and he has not offered it, and the word went in at about the sixth hour because a man of about fifty said *set it*.

---

**And a printer's mate of nineteen has been a mate for two years and has eleven weeks to serve before the next sitting and wants to be a journeyman, and he said two things about the word in that room on that Thursday and one about the eleven weeks, and nobody answered him about any of the three.**

The first was at about the sixth hour and a half, when the woman of about twenty-nine had gone back to the room with the stove and the man of about fifty had gone down to the second floor and there was nobody in the press room he was working for.

“**Is it a real word.**”

“**It is a word.**”

“**That is not the same as a word anybody uses.**”

“**No.**”

He said that and went back to the stone, and four people in that building have said since that a boy of nineteen who asks whether a word is real and is told that it is a word has been told the correct answer and has heard something else, and that the something else is in the answer, and that a printer's mate is the only person in a press room whose whole trade is the difference between the two.

**And he wanted the eleven weeks, and four people in that building have said since that a boy of nineteen with eleven weeks to serve before a sitting does not say so in a press room, and that he said it once, at the sixth hour, to a man of about fifty in the doorway, and the man of about fifty said nothing at all and went down, and that the boy has not said it again and is not going to.**

What he said was this, and it is fourteen words, and it is the only thing he said in that room in about two years that was not about a sheet, and a man of about fifty heard all fourteen of them and gave back none of them.

“**I have eleven weeks and I would rather the week were not this one.**”

The man of about fifty did not answer it and went down two flights, and four people in that building have said since that a man who has entered about eleven thousand forms in thirty-one years and has not once put his hand out to a drawer is a man who knows what a boy's eleven weeks are and has not got one and has not had one for thirty-one years, and that nobody in that building has ever put those two facts in the same sentence and it is in this one.

---

**And the press room is the smallest room on that floor and it is the warmest, and there is one window and it is nine feet off a wall, and four floors of that building are working on forms and about four hundred of those forms go out in a month, and none of the four floors is the room the word is in, and the room the word is in is on the ground floor at the end of a passage with the stove.**

A press room at the sixth hour in Thawmonth is a room with the doors shut and the windows not opened and every voice about twice the size it is in a yard, and a man who is going to be heard properly in that building has to be in a room, and a man who is not going to be heard has to be in a yard, and four people in that province have worked that out in nine years and none of the two of them has ever put it in a building.

A printer's mate of two years stands at a stone and does about two hundred and thirty lines a day, and a boy of fifteen at a bench-board in a fen county two hundred miles off copies the empty space under a number as well as the number, and the two of them are the same age within four years and are two hundred miles apart and neither of them is named and neither of them has been asked what is in the press in front of him and neither of them is going to be.

---

**And he pulled the sheet at about the seventh hour, and it came off the press wet at the fourth corner, and he carried it up two flights on a board because a wet sheet tears if a man puts a thumb on it, and he was not in that room when the room was told what it had done.**

The room is the chamber two floors up and there were four people in it: the woman of thirty-eight, a clerk of the Crown, a man of about fifty who had come up with the sheet and had gone down again, and the man of forty-four with the schedule. The sheet went on the table face up because a wet sheet goes face up, and it lay there for about nine seconds before anybody turned it over, and four people in that room have said since that nine seconds is how long it takes four people to look at a thing that has been put in front of them, and that one of the four read it, and that one of the four said the word out loud.

“**It has the word on it.**”

“**Yes.**”

“**In the object column.**”

“**In the object column, before the place, which is what was asked for.**”

“**Then it is in.**”

That is the whole of the room being told. Nobody in that room asked what the word means, and nobody in that room said what it means, and the man of forty-four looked at it for about a second and a half and went back to his schedule, and four people in that building have said since that a man who has read about two hundred lines in a morning and has not turned his head once for two hours turned his head once for a second and a half for a word, and that a man who has not been asked anything all morning is a man who was not asked that either, and that a schedule is a count of places and a count of places is a form of words, and that there is no column in that building for the difference between a word that is used and a word that is set.

**And the word is *eastern*, and it is in a drawer, in a bag, in two documents and an object column and now on a wet sheet in a chamber two floors up, and it is on a stone in a fen county two hundred miles off in a press that belongs to a yard and cannot be served with anything, and nobody in this building and nobody in that county and nobody in that office has said what it is for, and it is not going to be said in this building, and there is no column in that office for what a word is for, and about four hundred people in a town of about four thousand people do not know that a boy of nineteen has eleven weeks to serve and has set a word he does not use and has gone down two flights to wash his hands and is not going to be told what it did.**
