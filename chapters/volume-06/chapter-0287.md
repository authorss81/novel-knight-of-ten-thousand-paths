# Chapter 0287

**The seventeenth day of Rainmonth, YR 316, a Saturday, and a market day, and the second hour and the fourth hour and the seventh hour, and a chamber two floors above a room with a stove in it in a provincial town two hundred miles from a fen county, and a long table in it that is not the table of three, and a schedule of about two hundred and forty lines on it with a head above it that carries a date that has gone by, and a press room on the ground floor at the end of a passage, and a second copyist of about thirty who is eight days in that town and has not been sent for and is not going to, and about nine hundred people in that town who do not know any of it.**

The head of the record on that table is in a smaller type than the schedule, and what it says is that classifications are to be complete before the first of Longlight, and the name of the office they are for is in a line with the other heads and is not the office that sent the return and is not the office that wrote the proceeding.

**And the first of Longlight was forty-six days before that Saturday, and the head has said the same thing at the top of that page every time for about six weeks, and about nine people in that room have read it every time, and about four of them have worked out that the date on it is a date that has been and gone, and none of them has said so to anybody, and there is no column in that building for a head that is out of date.**

---

The schedule is a month late and it is read on Saturdays and on Wednesdays by a man of forty-four who is a member of the Culling Order and a classifier, and he reads the object column first, and he has said out loud in that room that he is not going to write in it twice in a day, and nobody in that room has ever asked him what is in the four places on a form, and he has never asked, and nobody in that room is going to ask him in the next four weeks.

His want was said once, in thirty-four words, at about the fourth hour of the eighth of a month in the year before, to a clerk who was carrying a tray out of the room, and it was that a classification that is late is a classification that can be argued with, and he has argued with nine of them in a year, and it is spent, and he did not say it again that Saturday and is not going to say it again anywhere.

**And he read the first column of every line and the fifth column of about nine of them and the sixth column of none of them, and about two hundred and thirty lines went into a book in about two hours, and the sixth column of a form of that series is the same width as the other five and has nothing at the top of it and has never had anything in it in about two years, and about four hundred forms a month go out of that building with a blank one on them and not one of them has ever come back.**

He read about two hundred and thirty lines in about two hours and a clerk with a bad wrist copied them at the other end of that table, and the woman of thirty-eight was at that table with a sheet of her own and wrote down three things in the two hours and the three things set down were the time, the place and the number of lines, and she has been in nine of those rooms in nine years and has not once read a finding at the end of one of them that described a room.

**And a man who has read a count of places every Saturday and Wednesday for about a year has never once been asked what the count is for, and nobody in that room knows, and the not-knowing is not a gap in the record and is not going to be, and there is no line in that record for a reason a number was written down.**

---

And a woman of about thirty has been in that building for eight days and she is not in the chamber and she is not in the press room and she is in a room at the end of the second-floor passage with a bed in it that a clerk of the Crown had a made up on the Thursday because the building had nowhere to put a person who had come two hundred miles.

She came on a warrant to produce and there is no second sitting entered yet, and a clerk of the Crown entered none on the Friday and the office has not entered one, and a person who is producing a document in a proceeding that has not sat again is a person who is being held, and the word for that is not a word anybody in that building uses, and a warrant that names a room and a day and does not name a person is a permit to be in a building until somebody says otherwise, and nobody has said otherwise and nobody is going to.

**Nobody has written to her. The keeper of records of that office is two hundred miles off and has not written and has not been asked to, and the second keeper of twenty-six is in that county with a warrant for a third post in a day-book and it is not signed, and the two women have never spoken about the want or the warrant and are not going to, and a day-book is the only book in that office nobody can be served with and it has nine entries in it of a thing that was not asked.**

She went to the market on that Saturday because there was nothing else to do in a town of about nine hundred people on a Saturday, and about three hundred people were in it, and about four of them said something ordinary to her and got an ordinary answer, and she has not told anybody in that market that she is two hundred miles from the only room she has ever worked in, and she has not told them because they would not know what to do with it.

“**Are you working,**” a woman at a stall said, which is what a market says to anybody it has not seen before.

“**Not today,**” Alish Renn said.

“**Somebody from the office,**” the woman at the stall said, and looked at her hands, and asked her nothing else, and a woman at a stall in a provincial town looked at a second copyist's hands at about the fourth hour of a Saturday and worked out what she was in about four seconds and did not ask, and a market is the only room in a county where nobody asks a person anything and it is the reason markets work.

---

**And the woman of thirty-eight was at that table for the whole of the two hours and she read the first column of the first line of that schedule over the shoulder of a man of forty-four and did not say anything about it for about nine minutes, and then she said one thing, and it was about a sheet and not about a person.**

“**The line for your county has a name in the fourth column.**”

“**It has,**” the man of forty-four said, and read on.

“**I am not asking you about it,**” the woman of thirty-eight said, and did not ask him about it, and set down three things on her own sheet in those two hours and the three things set down were the time, the place and the number of lines, and a Crown officer who has asked in nineteen sentences for a record to show what was asked has been in a room with a name in a column in a county two hundred miles off and has written down the time of it, and about four people in that building have said since that a woman who notes a thing and does not ask about it has made a decision and that the decision is not a question, and that there is no column in that minute for a decision.

---

And at about the fourth hour a sheet went out of that building in the ordinary way with a list on it of what had been entered since the last reading, and the list is printed on that office's own series by a man of about fifty who has been a clerk of a press for thirty-one years, and about nine lines of it are object lines, and in one of the nine there is a word in front of a place name, and the word is in the same type as the heads of the five columns, and the place is a county two hundred miles off.

**The word is *eastern*, and it is in the same type as the heads of the five columns, and a man of about fifty at the press in the room below has looked at it every day for about a year and has not been asked what it is for and has not asked, and a word printed on a list going out to nine counties has stopped being a sort in a drawer and become a thing that travels, and that is the last stage of anything anybody in that building can watch happen to a word.**

The list went out at about the seventh hour and a man of about thirty-one with a satchel put nine of them in the satchel and carried them down to the cart and did not read any of them and does not know that a county of about four thousand people is in one, and about nine hundred copies of a form of that series go into nine counties in a year, and about nine hundred people in that town do not know that the word in a drawer in a press room on the ground floor is now in about nine counties and has never been in a book.

---

**And a man of about fifty put the list on the stone at about the fourth hour and put his hand flat on it for about a second, which is the third time anybody in that building has seen that, and the first time was about two years ago and the second was on the Thursday of the week before, and a boy of nineteen who has eleven weeks to serve before the next sitting and wants to be a journeyman saw the second one and has not said anything about either of them.**

“**Is it going out.**”

“**It is going out.**”

“**With the word on it.**”

“**With the word on it.**”

“**I set it,**” the boy of nineteen said.

“**I know you set it,**” the man of about fifty said.

“**I did not know what it was,**” the boy said, and about four people in that building have said since that a boy of nineteen with eleven weeks to serve before a sitting has said the true thing about a word in a press room and has been told that he set it, and that nobody in that room has said the other half of it, and that the other half is the only part of it anybody in that building wanted.

And that was the whole of that exchange and neither of them improved on the other and neither of them asked the other one anything, and two people in a press room agreeing with each other about a word is not a decision and is not a refusal and is the only thing that has ever happened in that room about that word, and it happened three times in two years and about nine hundred people in that town do not know that it is a thing anybody has ever said out loud.

---

And the room with the stove in it on the first floor had four people in it at about the fifth hour and a case behind one of them with a book in it and a leaf in that book turned face down, and nobody in that room asked about the leaf and nobody is going to.

And about four hundred forms a month went on going out of that building into nine counties and four hundred and nine parish rolls, and a woman of thirty-three filled in the fourth column of two of them on the Monday and on the Tuesday from a roll in about a minute and a quarter each without looking up, and a man of forty-four read about two hundred and thirty lines in that town and a word went out to nine counties in it, and about four hundred people in a town of about four thousand people on a river do not know that a woman is eight days away from the only room she has worked in for eleven years and has not been sent for.
