# Chapter 0280

**The twenty-seventh day of Longlight, YR 316, a Sunday, and not a market day, and the second hour and the fifth hour, and a crossing four hundred yards up a fen county's own road and a barrier of three bars three hundred yards down it and a yard on a street four hundred yards from a wharf, and about four hundred people in a town of about four thousand people on a river who do not know that a man of thirty-two with a limp said one claim out loud in a hut to a man of fifty-five on that Sunday morning, and that the claim was not against anybody and not for anybody, and that the verdict was not given and is not going to be given, and that a thing was refused at about the fifth hour of that Sunday that has been the price of a thing in this county for four years and a half and nobody in this county ever charged it.**

The fever was over on the seventeenth and he had been up ten days by the twenty-seventh and the leg was what it is and the stick was a stick, and he went up that lane on his own on a morning when there was nobody else on it and got to the toll house in about nine minutes and stood in it for a while.

The toll house was open at the low end and nobody was in it. The sill is a foot and a half wide and it has nothing in it and no glass in it and no third seat, and the dressed block is in the abutment with the mortar round it, and the forty yards of tarred hemp that were in it are not in it and are four feet down somewhere in a hole in a bank that nobody has dug and nobody is going to dig.

He did not go up the bank. He had not gone up the bank since the seventh day of the month and was not going to on that Sunday, and a man who has decided in advance that he cannot do a thing has got twenty days of thinking out of it for nothing, and he has not told anybody that he cannot do it and has not been asked.

A man of fifty-five sat in that hut on that Sunday and gave a man of thirty-two with a limp the same four words he gives everybody, and added nothing to them, and was not asked for a fifth thing, and is not going to be asked for one in this county by anybody.

Then the man with the stick said one more thing, and it was twenty-seven words, and it was said in the ordinary way to a man who was not going to repeat it.

**“The road she crossed has a claim on its own history, and it is not a claim against her and it is not a claim for her.”**

The man in that hut did not repeat it. Nobody has ever asked him where he gets the four words and he has never said, and about four people in that lane have said since that a man who is not asked has a much better chance of keeping a thing than a man who is asked, and that this is the only trade in this county where that is true and that nobody in it is paid.

“**You are the second person in a month who has said something to me in that hut that was not about the barrier,**” he said, after about a minute. “**The other one was in a lane and did not come in. I have thought about it since and I have not worked out whether that is a thing that happens to me or a thing that happens in this county, and I have been in this hut nineteen years and I have never been able to work out a thing of that size.**”

“**Nobody has ever said a thing like that to you.**”

“**It is not,**” the man of fifty-five said. “**It is a thing I have noticed and I have never mentioned it and I have never mentioned it to anybody in nineteen years, and I am mentioning it now because you have not asked me, and that is the only reason I would.**”

The hut is about six feet by four and there is a stool in it and a stove in the corner of it that is lit in the cold months and not in the warm ones, and the three bars go up across that lane and come down again, and the man of fifty-five has done the up and the down about four thousand times in nineteen years and has never done them at the same speed and has never once been asked to, and about four hundred people in that town drive past a barrier on a Saturday and about nine of them have ever wondered what the three bars are for, and the answer is that a toll was suspended by an instrument a court has found lawful and has said it has nothing further to say about it at any time, and nobody has taken the bars down, and nobody has ever asked the man in that hut which of those two things he would rather.

And the claim is stated and the verdict is not, and nobody is going to state the verdict, and there is nowhere to state it, because the document the verdict would have to come out of is a list in a drawer in a building two hundred miles off with a keeper in the first column and no column at all for what became of anybody, and there is no finding about this anywhere and there is not going to be one, and a woman of thirty-three two hundred miles off who filled that column in is at a register this morning and is not going to be told by this county or by any other.

The observation that a book can have no column for what became of anybody was made to him before he was asked, in a yard, by a man at a mill wheel who cannot leave it, and the not asking took about four seconds, and that man is four hundred miles off in a county that is not on any map, and he is not asked about this by anybody in this county and is not going to be, and he is not asked because there is no road that gets there and not because anybody in a fen county decided that he would not be asked, and those two things are the same thing and only one of them is a decision and it was not made here.

---

**And the price of the thing that was done at the second hour of the eighth of Longlight is another crossing, and a crossing is a thing a man makes with two hands and a day and a witness at each end, and the man of thirty-two with a limp did that arithmetic at about the fifth hour of that Sunday in a yard on Weir Street and did it out loud to about nine men who were not listening.**

He did it to nobody in particular. He had it out and he had it out in the middle of a yard with nine men in it and a bench-board four feet off the ground and about nine feet long, and a man of about nineteen with an empty hod went past the end of it and a boy of fifteen at the bench-board copied a number under a number and did not look up.

**“The price of the thing I did on Tuesday is a crossing,” he said, “and I am not going to make it, and I want that said in a yard where nine men can hear it, because I have never said a thing like it anywhere else and I am not going to start saying it in a room.”**

Nobody answered him. About nine men in that yard have heard a man of thirty-two with a limp say two things out loud in about two months and both of them were to nobody, and a yard is the only room in this county where a man can say a thing like that and be told nothing about it, and about four of the nine heard it and about four did not, and neither of those four has ever mentioned it.

And a crossing is a claim. A road that has a crossing put on it by somebody is a road that has been claimed, and the claim is not a description and it is not a question, and a thing that has been claimed can be claimed again by somebody else with a different column and a different hand, and the sheet in a coat has four places ruled for a figure and a name in the fourth column and a word in front of the place name, and there is no head on the sixth column at all, and a man who makes a second crossing on a road is a man who has put a second name on it in a column nobody is keeping.

So the road had one claim on it and the claim had an owner and the owner was standing in a yard with a stick, and he was not going to put a second one there, and that is the whole of the refusal and it is not a speech and it was said in a yard on a Sunday to nine men who were not listening, and a man of about sixty packing lime was at the end of that yard with his back to all of them and heard every word of it in the wrong order and has never said a word about it and is not going to.

---

The owner of the yard is twenty-five and he was in it and he said one thing.

**“There is a press in that room and there is a bundle in it with nothing on the wrapper and it is not mine and it is not on my book and it is not going to be on my book, and you have never asked me to put it there and I have never asked you to take it out, and that is the only arrangement either of us has ever had with the other one and I would like to keep it until one of us dies.”**

“**I am not going to take anything out of it,**” the man of thirty-two with a limp said.

**“Then it is a thing that stays in a press in a room in a yard,” the man of twenty-five said, “and about four hundred yards down that lane a room with a door on it can be served with anything, and yours cannot, and I have never worked out which of those two things is the better one and I have had four years to work it out and I have not.”**

There is a woman of twenty-nine who keeps a room forty feet from that wagon arch and a woman of about thirty who keeps a book in it, and a woman of about thirty with a pail who goes up that lane nine doors a day, and a man of about thirty with a pail who came up a flight of stairs in the week before this one and put something on a table and went out again without asking a question. None of them is in that yard and none of them was asked anything on that Sunday, and not one of them has been asked anything by this man in about a year and a half, and the not-asking is not a courtesy and is not a rule and is the ordinary habit of a room, and a room hears a thing once and a person can be asked twice and this county has never worked out which of the two it is doing.

Nobody walked any part of that Sunday with him. He came down the lane on his own and went up it on his own and about four hundred people in that town did not notice a man going either way, and the tide was under the wharf and the salt was over the first bay and the fourth bay was finished and pointed, and a bag had gone out of that yard in the ordinary post three weeks before with a sheet in it and a word in front of a place name on it that nobody in that county has ever said out loud, and a yard that sends nothing can be ignored and a yard that has sent one thing cannot be, and this yard has sent one thing, and nobody in it is going to be asked about it, and nobody in this county is going to be able to take it back.
