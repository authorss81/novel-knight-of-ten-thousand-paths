# Chapter 0294

**The twenty-second day of Harvestmonth, YR 316, a Saturday, and a market day, and the first hour and the third hour and the fifth hour, and a room with a stove in it on the first floor of a building of four floors and seven presses in a provincial town two hundred miles from a fen county, and a returns book in it, and a stove in it that is not lit, and a case behind one desk with a shelf on it and a book on the shelf and one leaf of that book face down, and about nine hundred people in that town of whom about three hundred are in a market, and about four hundred of the rest do not know that a set of market towns is being counted for a fourth time.**

There is one window in that room and it looks at a wall nine feet off, and the four hundred forms a month are read under it at waist height by light off the floor, and the stove in that room was cold and had been cold since the weather turned.

The book is ruled, nine inches by six, and every form that comes into that building goes into it as a line. A line carries a place, a day, and three figures, and of the five things a line will hold, four are ruled for a figure, and the whole of a line has nowhere in it at all for a person's name.

**And a quarter ends in that book and a quarter begins in it, and the returning of a district from one quarter to the next is done by a woman of about twenty-nine who is a clerk of the returns and has been in that room six years, and about eleven returns in about nine years have been written on by a room and not by a press, and nobody in that room has ever asked which of them was which.**

---

The schedule has gone out of the ordinary way and that is not a thing anybody in that room has to know in order to do the work of that morning. The head on the long table two floors above carried a date in the spring and the date is in the second month now and the date has gone, and a man of forty-four who read about two hundred and thirty lines into a record on Saturdays and on Wednesdays for about a year is not in that building and his room is two doors down it and the stove in it is cold, and nobody in that building has asked him where he is.

What is on the desk in that room is the returns for the quarter, and they came up from four counties and one of them is a county on a river with a market four hundred yards long in it, and about four hundred people stood in that market that morning and did not know that a set of market towns had been written into a ruled book about nine inches by six on the first floor of a building two hundred miles off, twice in about a year, and a third time that week.

“**Bring me the last quarter,**” the woman of about twenty-nine said.

“**It is the one under the top one,**” a man of about thirty who is a clerk of that room said, “**and the district is on the same line as it was on.**”

“**Of course it is on the same line,**” the woman of about twenty-nine said. “**There is a line for it. Lines do not have quarters on them.**”

And she carried it forward, and it took her about four seconds. The thing that has gone out of the ordinary way in this building is a schedule, and a schedule is a count of places, and a district is a place, and a place does not stop being counted because the counting of it has been given up by somebody two floors up, and nobody in that room has worked out that those are two different offices' habits, and there is no column in that book for which office's habit it is, and the head of the book says nothing at all except the name of the book.

---

Through that building at about the second hour went a man of about thirty-one with a satchel and about nine sheets in it, and on the Thursday before he had been up a lane in a county two hundred miles off and had lifted nine sheets off nine sills in the rain, one of them from under a stone, and he read none of them then and reads none of anything, and there are four places on a form of that series and he has never found out what is in them, and he has said so out loud three times in six years in three different places, and about nine hundred people in that town have heard none of the three.

“**Is the line for that county still on the book,**” he said, to nobody in particular. It is the first question anybody has asked in that room about what a line in that book is for, and it was not asked of anybody and got an answer from nobody, and about four people in that room have said since that a man with a satchel is the only person who comes into that building who has never once seen the thing he carries.

“**It is on the book,**” the woman of about twenty-nine said, and did not ask him what he wanted to know for, and he went out again and did not come back up.

---

A man of about thirty who is a clerk of that room came in with a pigeonhole under his arm about the fourth hour, set it down on the table, and carried it out again inside a minute. About nine hundred sheets of that series go round twice a year into nine counties in it. Nobody in that room has counted them and neither has the man who carried them in.

“**Is the schedule still going out,**” he said, to the woman of about twenty-nine.

“**I do not know,**” she said, and she said it in the ordinary way, and then she said something else, which she has not said in that room in about six years.

“**Nobody has told this room. If it stops, it stops in the room it stops in, and this room will go on turning the page.**”

“**And if somebody tells you,**” the man of about thirty said, “**what will you do with it.**”

“**I will put it in the book,**” the woman of about twenty-nine said, “**and the book has four places ruled for a figure and nowhere at all for a name, and a thing somebody tells me is not a figure, and I have been in this room six years and I have never once put a name in this book and I am not going to start on a Saturday.**”

And a clerk of six years worked out on a Saturday morning that her room's habit and another room's habit are two things, and she worked it out about a month after a Crown officer in a chamber two floors up worked out that a schedule that cannot be completed before a date that has been is not a schedule that is late, and the two of those are about four hundred miles of country apart and neither of them was in the room where the other one was said, and there is no column in that book for what a room knows.

The man of about thirty put the pigeonhole down again and did not say anything else about it and went back to his own bench, and nobody in that room has asked him since whether the schedule is still going out, and he has not asked again.

---

A woman of thirty-three is an aid to the register and is not a member of anything and has never been, and she has filled the fourth column of that office's own printed series about two hundred times in a season for eleven years, and the fourth column is headed *Keeper*, and she came to that column off a roll in about a minute and a quarter without looking up on two forms that morning and about a minute and a quarter on a third, and the four other people in that room did not look up either, and nobody in that room has asked her to look up.

On the second shelf of that case stands a book, and in the book one leaf is lying face down with nineteen words on it in her own writing, put there on the sixth day of a month two years back, and at about the fifth hour she laid her hand flat on the cover of that book and lifted it again without opening the book at all, and about nine hundred people in that town do not know that a woman stood at a case on a Saturday morning and touched a book and left it as it was.

**Nobody in that room thanked her for anything and nobody in that room said anything to her at all between the first hour and the sixth, and that is the third Saturday in a row on which a kind thing has not been offered at that table and it is not going to be offered at it again.**

A man of about sixty came in with a tray at about the third hour, put down enough tea for four, said that there was tea in that room if anybody wanted it, and went out again, and he has been doing that about nine times a week for twenty-two years and has never once been asked whether anybody wanted it, and nobody in that room said one word to him on that Saturday, and in twenty-two years he has not become a person to anybody in it and he is the only one in it who is paid to be there.

---

---

On the line for that district, under the date and the figures, there is one more entry, and it is the only entry in that book that is not a return, and it went in in the second month of this year as a sheet because a sheet is what it is and there was no line for anything else, and a man of thirty-four is a second clerk of the returns and has been in that building eleven years.

The woman of about twenty-nine carried the quarter forward over the top of it. The pen went across the line above and the line above that and did not stop on it and did not go round it, and about four seconds is not long enough to decide to do that and long enough to do it, and she has not asked him about it and he has not come up and explained it, and there is no column in that book for an entry that was made correctly and is still not a return.

---

The market in that town was on and about three hundred people were in it, and a building of four floors and seven presses went on working at about four hundred forms a month into nine counties and four hundred and nine parish rolls on a day two thirds of that town are not at work on, and nobody in that building has ever gone out to it, and the returns came up from four counties that week and one of the four is a county on a river and the other three are counties that have not been named in that room this year.

Nobody walked any part of that Saturday with anybody. About three hundred people were in a market in that town and about four hundred people were in a market in a county two hundred miles off, and the tray went up two flights and came back down, and a district was carried from one quarter to the next in about four seconds by a woman who has been in that room six years, and a woman of thirty-three filled in a column with a head on it three times from a roll without looking up, and about nine hundred people in that town do not know that a county of about four thousand people on a river is being counted for a fourth time and that the counting is not the thing that stopped.
