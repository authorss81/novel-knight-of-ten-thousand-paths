# Chapter 0259

**The fourteenth day of Thawmonth, YR 316, a Tuesday, and not a market day, and the second hour, and a room on the third floor of the building in a provincial town two hundred miles from a fen county, and a man in that room who had a sheet with a name on it before anybody in that building had been asked for the name, and about four hundred people in a town of about four thousand people on a river who do not know that a man of thirty-two with a limp was sent for on the twelfth day of Thawmonth by a hand that does not use the ordinary post, and that he was in the room for about an hour and a half and said seventy words to a man who did not ask him a question and was answered in ten, and that nobody in that room was surprised.**

He was sent for by a sheet with his own name on it and the sheet had been written before he was in the building on the second of Thawmonth, YR 316, and he has not worked out how and has not tried, and four people in that building have said since that a man who says *I have not worked out how* about a thing that was done for him has understood that the not-working-out is the correct position and has chosen it.

**The marshal did not stand up. That is the first thing anybody in that room has ever said about the marshal and it is a thing about a man not standing up, and four people in that building have said since that a man who does not stand up for a person who has been in a passage for eleven hours is a man who has been expecting him, and that expecting is not surprise and that the difference between the two is the whole of what that man is.**

There is no window in that room either, and there is a candle in a tin holder on the wall, and there is a press in the corner with nothing in it, and there is a sheet on the table with a name on it and the name is his and there is nothing else on the table.

“**You have a mother.**”

He said it in the plain way and he did not raise his voice and four people in that building have said since that a man who says a thing like that in a room with no window in it has said it the way a man says a thing he has known for four months, and that the man said it at about the second hour and did not sit down afterwards either.

“I do not have a mother in a record. I have a woman who went out of a valley in the year the Sundering happened with a boy of about one-and-twenty and about a year between the going out and the coming back, and there is no name in it.”

“**There is no name in it. There is a mark. There are two marks on the eleventh line and the eleventh line is the only one with two on it and I have had a copy of the list in a drawer in this building for about nine years and I have never once had a reason to open it until you sat down.**”

---

**And what is in that room is a press with nothing in it and a jug of water and a candle in a tin holder and about nine feet of table, and the table has one thing on it, and the one thing is the sheet with his name on it, and four people in that building have said since that a man with nine hundred sheets in four buildings has a table with one sheet on it and that the one sheet is the only one in that room that is not in a drawer, and that it is there because a man who is going to be looked at wants the person looking at him to have something to look at.**

The jug was poured and the water was cold and four people in that building have said since that a man who offers water and does not ask why a man of thirty-two with a limp is sitting down sideways in a chair is a man who has noticed the leg and has decided that the leg is not the thing he is buying, and that a man who has decided that has bought something else and has not said what.

The man of thirty-two put his hand flat on the table for about two seconds and the table is deal and it is not warm, and nothing came back, and about four hundred people in a town of about four thousand people on a river do not know that a man of thirty-two with a limp put his hand on a table in a room with no window in it on the fourteenth of Thawmonth, YR 316 and that the table is deal and is not warm and that his left hand is warm at about the fourth hour and a half every day and has been since a lane in a county that is not on any map, and that a hand does not know the difference between a table and a sill, and that a person does, and that he did not put his hand on anything in that room a second time.

---

**And he took a sheet out of a drawer and put it on the table and did not let it out of the room, and that is the whole of the offer and the whole of what is in the sealed record that anybody in that building will see, and the rest of the sealed record is in ten other drawers in three other buildings and is not in this room.**

It is a list of about eleven lines. It is ruled in two columns and the two columns are in two parts, and the first part of the first column is a hand and the second part is a road, and there are eleven hands and eleven roads and they are the same size and the same shape and they are not the same thing, and four people in that building have said since that two columns of the same width with the same kind of thing in them is a list of a journey and that a list of a journey is the oldest paper in this kingdom and that it is the one kind of paper that cannot be checked by anybody who was not there.

**And there is no third column. There is no column for what became of anybody. The eleven lines are a hand and a road and eleven going out and eleven coming back and about a year between the first of them going out and the eleventh coming back, and the eleventh has two marks on it, and a man's notebook in a coat on a slope in a city four hundred miles off has eleven lines in it with no words in them, and the two pages are the same length, and neither of them knows about the other one.**

A man of thirty-two with a limp read the eleven lines in about a minute and a half and did not say anything, and then he read them again, and four people in that building have said since that a man who reads a list twice has decided something and does not know what, and that a keeper of records in a county two hundred miles off has entered the same question four times in five months and has not answered it and would have said, if she had been in that room, that a list is a list and a list is a thing that can be read twice, and that she is not in that room and has not been told about it.

**And a copy of that list went back into the drawer at about the second hour and a quarter, and the drawer was shut, and there is no second copy of that list in that room, and about four hundred people in a town of about four thousand people on a river do not know that a list of eleven hands and eleven roads is in a drawer in a building two hundred miles off, and that a woman and a boy went out on one of them in a year this kingdom counts in months and has not got a name for, and that the two marks on the eleventh line are the only marks on the sheet and that neither of them is a name.**

“**The rest of it is what you have come for and I will give you the whole of it. It is eleven drawers in four buildings and about nine hundred sheets and I have never read the eight hundred of them and I have read the eleven lines. It is yours on the terms.**”

“The terms are that I give evidence.”

“**The terms are that you give evidence in a proceeding that I do not sit in, before a person who does not want you there, on a day in a month you have not been told.**”

He said it the way a man reads out four items off a sheet, and he did not look up off it.

“**That is a condition and it is not a price and you know the difference and I know you know.**”

“**I know the difference. I have been offering things to people for a long time and I have never once had to say that.**”

---

**And he did not say yes and he did not say no, and that is the whole of the hour and a half, and four people in that building have said since that a man who has been offered nine hundred sheets in four buildings and has not said yes and has not said no has done the one thing in that room that nobody in it expected, and that a man who is not surprised is a man who has a second drawer and the second drawer is the reason.**

*Nobody has said out loud what a list of what went out is for.*
*It has been in a drawer since before you were born and no man has ever been kept from it.*

And he did not write it down. A man of thirty-two with a limp has had four of those in four years and has written one down, and the one he wrote down has no hour against it and no name in it, and this one has the day on it in his head and the day is not written anywhere and is not going to be, and about four people in that building do not know that a man said two lines in a room with no window in it and kept them.

**And then he said one thing, and it was in his own voice, and it was seventy words, and it is the only thing he said in that room that was about himself, and the marshal did not improve on it and did not write it down.**

“**I have four words ready that I have had since a day I cannot count and I did not say them in that room and I am not going to say them in this one, and you are the first man in about two years that I have been in a room with who did not ask me a question, and that is not a compliment, it is a trade.**”

The marshal said one thing back and it was ten words and four people in that building have said since that it is the only thing the man has said in that building in about nine years that is not an offer, and that nobody in that room has said anything after it and that the room went on for about eleven minutes after that without anybody saying anything at all.

“**That is the correct way to be in a room.**”

---

**And about four people in that building have said since that the terms are the only part of that offer that the marshal made himself, and that a condition is not a price and that about four people in that province have said the two are the same thing in about nine years and that about four of them have said they are not, and that nobody in that room said either thing twice and that the room went on for about eleven minutes after the man said his seventy words without anybody saying anything at all, and that eleven minutes is the longest silence in that room in about nine years and that there is no column in that building for a silence and that a man who keeps one is a man who has to keep it himself.**

He stood up at about the third hour and a half, which is about an hour and a half after he went in, and the stick took him about four seconds longer than the chair did, and about four people on a stair in that building have said since that a man who is given nine hundred sheets in four buildings and a condition that is not a price gets up when he wants to and not when he is asked, and that he did not say he would think about it, and that saying he would think about it is the ordinary way a man keeps a thing alive, and that he did not do it, and that about four people on that stair have worked out between them what that means and have not written it down and are not going to.

---

**And the room he wanted to be in is two floors down and he was not sent for there and was not on the list and had not asked to be, and the woman of thirty-eight is in that room on the ninth of Rainmonth, YR 316, which is a Friday and is not a market day, and a bench of nine in a county of about four thousand people does not sit on it, and the date has a day in it and the day makes it a finding waiting to happen, and about four hundred people in a town of about four thousand people do not know any of that, and one of them keeps a door on a lane and has five questions in a register and not one finding, and she was not told, and she is not going to be.**
