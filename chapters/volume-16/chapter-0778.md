**Rainmonth the fifth, YR 323, a Monday and not a market morning with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and no market in that street, the fourth hour on a floor four days off with seven people on it, and a woman of about twenty-nine at the back of a day-book writing a figure down in her own hand for the first time in four and a half years.**

A Monday in that market town of about two thousand people is not a market morning, and the street was quiet at the ninth hour of the fifth of Rainmonth with about nine people in it and rain coming in from the west on the slant it comes in. Her name is Mara Vey, and it had been printed once in this county for years, and four people in that lane had known it all along without saying it.

The man of thirty-two with a stick had come to the foot of those thirteen stairs at about the ninth hour of that Monday with about nine minutes of walking in him from the four hundred paces, and that was his goal for the morning and he kept it past the tenth hour. He had been wrong about the walking in, and the wrongness was that he had believed a keeper of records had a place to put a figure, and there is no such place in this county, and a keeper of records has a book and a book has heads and every head in that one is empty or ruled against, and he had come four hundred paces to ask the only person in nine counties who has ever written anything down anything whether there was anywhere at all. The cost of it was in the leg, which had been grinding since the second of the four hundred paces and had nine feet of ache in it by the time he stood under that lamp, and in the ear on the side the stick is on, which had the drip off that hood in it and would not take the drip out.

She said it first, from the doorway, short, in her own trade, to one person, and it was twelve words.

“**Where would you have me put it so that somebody reads it?**”

He said it back, short, in his own trade, to one person, and it was thirty-seven words.

“**There is no place. A roll wants a name against a line and I have no name and no line, and a register wants a finding and there is no finding under any of the ten questions.**”

Four people in that lane had heard a man tell a keeper of records that there is nowhere in nine counties to put a figure, and five had not, and nobody said anything back to either of them for about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over. Then the woman of about twenty-nine went up those thirteen stairs and shut the door at the top of them and was inside that room for about nine minutes, and she said one more thing over her shoulder before the door was shut, and it was thirty words, and it was to one person.

“**Then I will write it where a keeper writes the thing that is not an entry, and you will not read it and you will not tell me you have.**”

Four people in that lane had heard a keeper of records say that a figure would go on the back of a book and not in it, and five had not, and nobody said anything back to her, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a person who says you will not tell me you have read it has put the reading outside the room as well as the writing, and five have said that about nine minutes behind a shut door is the whole of the time a hand takes to write nine figures and a date.

The man of thirty-two with a stick stood in the lane at the foot of those thirteen stairs for about nine minutes in the rain and did not go up and did not knock, and then the door at the top of them opened and she came down four steps and said one thing, and it was thirteen words.

“**It is on the back leaf and it is not in the book.**”

There was chalk on the sill of that weigh-house door four days off, and it had been on a stone sill where the rain does not reach since the forty-ninth of Mudmonth, and the woman of about twenty-nine took the stick of it off that sill on the Monday after he made it and put it in the press drawer, and the man of about fifty-four has not asked her for it and is not going to.
