**Wolfmonth the twenty-fifth, YR 323, a Monday and not a market morning with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and no market in that street, the fourth hour on a floor four days off with nine people on it and the man of thirty-two one of the nine again, and twenty days of blood on the inside of one wrist that has not faded, and the last clear thing he will ever have of his mother.**
