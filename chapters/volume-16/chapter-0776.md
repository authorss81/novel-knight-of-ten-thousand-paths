**Longlight the eighteenth, YR 323, a Friday and not a market morning with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and no market in that street, the fourth hour on a floor four days off with seven people on it, and a man of thirty-two with a stick on that floor for the one hundred and eighteenth morning not being one of the two.**

A Friday in that market town of about two thousand people is not a market morning, and the street was quiet at the ninth hour of the eighteenth of Longlight with about nine people in it and heat standing in the middle of it. Four people in nine counties have said since that two papers asking for one name is an office that files, and five have said that filing is not holding, and four have said since that two offices about one name is not two demands, it is one demand in two shapes.

The man of thirty-two with a stick had come up that made road at about the fourth hour of that Friday to stand on that floor once and find out what a practice feels like from outside it, and that was his goal and he kept it until about the eighth hour. He had meant to ask one thing while he was there, in somebody's trade, and the thing he had meant to ask was why nobody had ever written a figure down, and he had meant to get a second name for it to go beside the first in whatever place it was going. One thing came into him there at about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over and it was not a voice and nobody else on that floor heard anything, and he has never told anybody what it was and nobody in nine counties has asked him.

**Holding is said by two and not heard by nine, and the nine are not the holding.**

Then he went on up that made road to the head of it at about the ninth hour, where eight of the nine yards of wall are back in and the gap in the wall is about nine feet long and about four feet high, and the man of forty-one was at the foot of it with a spade in his hands turning water off the bottom course, and he looked up once and said one thing in his own trade, which was sixteen words, and went back to the spade.

“**I hold the wall. The gap is nine feet and I am not starting the ninth.**”

Four people in nine counties heard a man of forty-one speak on a shoulder in Longlight, and five did not, and nobody had asked him anything before it and nobody has asked him anything since. Four people in nine counties have said since that about nine yards of wall is a man's trade and that the man who holds it says sixteen words and puts the spade back in, and five have said that a man of forty-one who has been asked nothing for eleven years and has said sixteen words in a wet year is not a man anybody in nine counties is going to ask anything else.

The man of thirty-two stood in that gap for about nine minutes and put his hand on the near end of the eight finished yards and then took it off, and there is a stone on the bank two miles off in a line with the mallet lying on top of it, and he did not go for it and nobody has asked him to go for it and nobody is going to. Four people in nine counties have said since that about four hundred and thirty-six days of carrying is a thing a man ends or does not end, and five have said that a man who has stopped carrying for a morning has not stopped and about four have said since that the ninth yard of a wall is about nine feet and about four feet high and about one day, and that about one day is not a reason and is a length.
