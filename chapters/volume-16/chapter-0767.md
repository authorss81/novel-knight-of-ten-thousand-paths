# Chapter 0767

**Mudmonth the twenty-seventh, YR 323, a Wednesday and not a market morning with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and no market in that street, the fourth hour on a floor four days off with five people on it, and nine days of the shoulder under water come to an end.**

A Wednesday in that market town of about two thousand people is not a market morning, and the street was quiet at the ninth hour of the twenty-seventh of Mudmonth with about nine people in it and mist on the boards. The bolt on the room over the weigh-house had gone on from the Saturday before and nobody went up the four hundred pace lane that holds it, and the boards at the low end stood with the notice on them gone soft with wet. The cloth nine doors down was still over the bench, and about four people in that street had stopped seeing it.

Then four days off along that made road, and about nine hundred people along it did the ordinary part of a Wednesday, and the ordinary part is that the shoulder takes the road for nine days and gives it back changed.

A drove went up that lane four days off on no Thursday of the nine days, because the shoulder was under water from the nineteenth to the twenty-seventh and no drove went up at all. That is fifty-five drove to go up that lane since the second of Mudmonth, and about four hundred people in that market street have never once watched one go up, and about four have said since that two Thursdays with no drove is a field resting.

Eight of the nine yards of wall at the head of that made road are back in, and the gap is about five feet long and about four feet high, and the stone is quarried two miles off on the low side. One man has been carrying it two miles in his own hands for three hundred and eighty-five days, and the ninth yard has not been started, and nobody in nine counties has asked him when he will and nobody is going to, and about four people in nine counties have said since that three hundred and eighty-five days is past a year of carrying.

The practice has run eighty-one mornings and the number said out loud at the fourth hour of the twenty-seventh of Mudmonth is four. It was eight before the water, and four of the nine stopped when the shoulder went under, because the floor is four days off and the two people who say the number are on that floor, and four people could not get there through nine days of water. About four people in nine counties have said since that four out of nine is less than half, and five have said that four saying a number twice is still a practice, and about four have said since that nobody knows which until the road hardens.

Two of the ten things are not held, and four more went unheld for nine days and two of the four came back. One of them is the Wethergill end of a cart track with nine hurdles across it and nine houses about four hundred yards back, and the last cart of lime came up that track on the thirteenth of Hearthmonth, which is a hundred and seventeen days ago, and nobody has been up that track since the second of Goatmonth. About four people in nine counties have said since that a hundred and seventeen days is a third of a year, and five have said that the tenth thing has never been held by anybody at all.

That tenth thing is nine feet wide and about nine hundred yards long and it is a hundred and fifty-two days old. It is dead inside its own width and standing outside it, and it is on the back of a roadkeeper's board in a cart shed four days off. About four people in nine counties have said since that a thing a hundred and fifty-two days old is the one thing nobody tried to hold through the water, and five have said that nobody was asked to.

A printed notice is on those boards in two sentences. It asks for the name of the person who keeps every road in nine counties, and says a road with no name against it is to be shut before the first of Mudmonth, and it is under the Marshal's hand and there is no name at the foot of it. The first of Mudmonth is twenty-six days past and is a Friday gone, and about four people in that market town had worked out inside nine seconds that a date gone twenty-six days with nothing shut is a paper county, and five had not.

The printed request of the fifteenth of Frostmonth is in that same office, and it asks for the name of the person who holds that wall and a copy of any drawing anybody in that county has made of what is above it. About four people in nine counties have said since that two papers asking for one name is an office that keeps asking, and five have said that water answers louder.

Two refusals are in about four hundred mouths and in nobody's hand, and they were given on the fourteenth and the twenty-sixth of Fallowmonth. Nobody in nine counties has asked either of those two men anything since and nobody is going to, and about four people in nine counties have said since that nine days of water is no time to ask anybody anything either.

The ninth mile stone on that made road is about the height of a knee and was cleaned with a bit of stick on the Tuesday before the water, and the clean ring at the foot of it is about a foot wide and about half an inch deep, and the water had softened the ring so that it had to be cut again. The eleventh mile stone about four hundred yards past it has about nine hundred names cut into the side of it going back about two hundred years, and nobody in nine counties can read more than about nine of them.

The five frames of brood in that walled yard are under about nine straw skirts, and the yard had stood in water to the second course for nine days. The young man of about nineteen went in at about the eighth hour of the twenty-seventh and found two skirts shifted and set them straight with his hands, and about four people in nine counties have said since that two skirts shifted in nine days is a light cost, and five have said that he has said twice he will make no more bees than the bees he has got, and about four have said since that the water tested the saying and the saying held.

The broom on that east rise has been off the hill since the twenty-ninth of Embermonth, and about nine hundred yards of a line nine feet wide was left standing, and the water had laid nearly all of it flat. About four people in nine counties have said since that flat broom after nine days of water is a field cleared by weather, and five have said that nobody will cut it any sooner.

The room over that weigh-house stood bolted from noon on the Saturday before, and the copyist of about twenty-six did not come down those thirteen stairs at all that day. The woman of about twenty-nine who keeps that room had the second lamp at the foot of them lit at the fourth hour, and it went out at about the sixth hour, and the register in that room holds ten questions with no finding under any of them and gains none, and the day-book stands at eleven entries, and the fourth ruled head is empty and no fifth head is ruled.

The man of thirty-two with a stick had come to that floor to count who was left, and that was his goal for the morning and he carried it through the mist with the stick sinking. He had meant to find nine people and restart eight, and to say the number himself if nobody else would. He had been wrong about the morning before the fourth hour was out, and the wrongness was that he thought the practice was his to restart, and the practice has a growth rate and the growth rate is not his to set, and five people on a floor saying four is a floor telling him so. The cost of it was in the leg from four days of waiting and then walking a soft road, which gave under the stick so that every step took twice, and in the ear on the side the stick is on, which had gone strange with damp until his own breathing sounded loud in it and other voices thin.

At about the fourth hour of that Wednesday five people stood on that floor four days off in mist, and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel said it first and the woman of about fifty-one said it again, and the number was four, and three of the nine were not there and one had sent word by a neighbour that the track to him was knee deep. About four people in nine counties have said since that four is half of eight, and five have said that half a practice saying its number twice is still said out loud in the same hour, and about four have said since that the rule holds at four the way it held at eight, and that this is the first thing about the notice that this county can feel in its own hands, because a practice which needs a made road is a practice that a person can switch off by ruining the road.

The man of forty-one was on that shoulder at about the fifth hour with a spade in his hands, turning water off the wall foot, and he looked up once when the man of thirty-two came past and said one thing in his own trade and went back to the spade.

“**Water takes the shoulder nine days and gives it back, and I hold the wall.**”

About four people in nine counties heard a man of forty-one speak on a shoulder in Mudmonth, and five did not, and nobody said anything back to him, and about four have said since that speaking in Mudmonth is a second speaking, and that nobody asked him anything before it and nobody has asked him anything since.

The man of thirty-two stood on the soft road and understood all of it there in the mist with water in his boots, and the other half of it a mile back when the stick sank to the head and he had to drag it out with both hands. A practice that stops when a road goes is not a room practice, because a room is shut five mornings by its own bolt and a road is shut by water, and the bolt can be drawn and the water cannot. He had thought writing nothing down was honesty, and honesty on a wet road is four people remembering four, and four people remembering is a different thing from nine. He had been wrong about what the floor needed, and the wrongness stayed with him past the ninth mile stone, and the cost of the morning was a leg gone from ache to dead weight and an ear gone from thin hearing to a roar of damp, and he sat on the dry side of the wall for about nine minutes with his head on his knees until the roar thinned to rain.

At about the ninth hour he said it out loud at the third door of that market street, to four people in and five out, standing in his own trade with mist on his coat.

“**Nine days of water took four of the nine, and the number is four, and a practice that needs a made road can be switched off by ruining the road.**”

About four people in that market street had heard a finding said out loud about a practice, and five had not, and nobody said anything back to him for about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over. About four people in nine counties have said since that a finding said in Mudmonth is a winter lesson learned in spring water, and five have said that lessons are not writs and nobody is bound by one.

The ordinary part of that Wednesday is that about nine people each put a thing down and pick it up again. The near wheel of a cart came off at the low end of that street and the carter got it up again with a bar in about nine minutes without anybody stopping on his account. The two women at the third door argued about a bolt of cloth for about four minutes by the clock and then went into the shop and came out with it wrapped. The inn at the high end had three of its four beds full and put no board out on the made street.

The light went off the west bank about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over and found the upper edge of those boards, and the lamp at the high end of them was not lit, because the fourth hour of a Wednesday in Mudmonth is dark and there is nothing off the wet slate of them to need it. Five of about nine people went out of that street into about five trades and four stayed at the boards, and nobody thanked anybody on that Wednesday either.

The hand lamp goes on the coping at the low side of that causeway four days south every Monday afternoon in the winter, and the twenty-seventh of Mudmonth is a Wednesday and the lamp was not put there. The water had been over the causeway for nine days and was off it that morning, and the hand bell on the gate post above that coping is about the size of a fist and was rung twice at about the eighth hour for the going off. About four hundred people live on the far bank of that water and have never been across in about nine years, and about four people in that market street had worked out inside nine seconds that nine days of water is the shoulder's own notice.

The two miles of wall and orchard between the eleventh mile stone and the top of that hill are a mile of stone on the high side and a mile of orchard on the low side, and the owners are nine households. About four people in nine counties have said since that nine households with nine days of water behind them is a county washed and not written, and five have said that washed is not shut.

The ninth of the nine stones at the end of that nine-foot line in the grass is about the height of a man's knee and the tops of them are all at the same height. The dead grass in the scar stops at the ninth of them and nobody has been past them, and about nine households keep beasts on the flat beyond them and the rota of that flat has been kept about nine years.

Then the fourth hour of that Wednesday morning, which is before the light, and the number said out loud on that floor was four and it was said twice by five people standing in mist. The day-book in the room over the weigh-house stands at eleven entries and did not gain one, and the fourth ruled head is still empty and no fifth head is ruled, and nothing about the practice is written down anywhere still.

He was not thanked for any of it, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man who counts five where nine stood is a man nobody thanks, because counting is not mending. He had come into this county four and a half years ago to stop being asked where a figure came from, and the practice he had started on a floor in Frostmonth had been run for eighty-one mornings by nine people saying numbers twice in the dark, and there was a printed notice twenty-six days past its date that had shut nothing and taught one thing.

He went out of that market street at about noon with the leg bad and the stick in his right hand caked with mud to the head after about nine miles of soft road, and did not go up the made road past the ninth mile stone. The shoulder steamed in the first sun of nine days, and he had told a market street once that he had not been to the place at the head of the cart track and was not going to be sent, and nobody had sent anybody, and the hurdles were still across the top of that cart track, and there was a thumb of lime on the top one of them.

**And the cost of that Wednesday is nine days of water on a shoulder, four of nine stopped by it, a number of four said twice by five in mist, a practice found to need a made road, a finding said out loud at a third door, one thing that went quiet a hundred and seventeen days ago and has not been looked at, one thing that has never been held by anybody, nine hundred yards of grass four days off that will not come back, the use of one ear, the use of one leg, and a year, are not in it and none of them has a price.**
