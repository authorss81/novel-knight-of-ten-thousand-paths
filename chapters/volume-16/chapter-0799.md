**Frostmonth the eighteenth, YR 323, a Wednesday and not a market morning with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and no market in that street, the fourth hour on a floor four days off with nine people on it, and thirteen days to the first of Hearthmonth, and nothing on any of the boards in that street with a name on it.**
