**Hearthmonth the twenty-ninth, YR 322, a Saturday and a market morning with the room over the weigh-house open, the second hour in the market street at the boards with about four hundred people in it and about two hundred of them stopped, a figure said out loud on a weigh-house floor that is one short of what it should be, and a cart of lime that was due on the Thursday before and did not come.**

A Saturday in that market town of about two thousand people is the loudest thing in nine counties, and four hundred people were in that street by the second hour of the twenty-ninth of Hearthmonth and two hundred of them stopped what they were doing, and the frost came off the ruts about an hour after that, and four people in nine counties had worked out that a cart of lime was due two days ago and five had not.

Nobody said anything and the cart of lime that comes up about nine hundred yards of a cart track and eleven miles of made road on every second Thursday was due on the twenty-seventh of Hearthmonth at about the sixth hour, and it did not come, and the twenty-ninth is the second day after, and the one after that is due on the eleventh of Goatmonth, and four people in nine counties have said since that a cart is not late until the day after the day it was due and five have said that this county has no way of knowing what a day after a cart that was due is.

Nobody thanked him and the man of about fifty-four who keeps that floor said the number of loads out loud at about the ninth hour to four people in and five out, and nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him, and it was 53 words.

“**Two hundred and twenty-nine carts have been on this floor since I came to it and I have had every one of them to a pound, and there is one missing and the one missing is the twenty-seventh, and I have never once been one out in about four and a half years.**”

Nobody said anything and nobody said anything back to him and nobody thanked him, and four people in that market street had heard a man say that he has never once been one out and five had not, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man who has never been one out in about four and a half years is a man who is now one out and that the one out is a figure and not a feeling and five have said that about four hundred people in a market street did not stop what they were doing when they heard it and about four people in nine counties have said since that a figure that is wrong by one is the hardest kind of figure there is, because a figure that is wrong by a hundred can be a bad year.

Nobody said anything and the practice has run twenty-four mornings and the number said out loud at the fourth hour of the twenty-ninth of Hearthmonth is five, and it has been five since the twentieth, and the sixth ask was made on the twenty-fourth of Hearthmonth, five days ago, to the man of about fifty-four who keeps the far end of that shut lane, and he said one sentence and the count has not moved and nobody has been on that shoulder since the seventeenth of Longlight.

Nobody said anything and that one sentence is on the page and it is eleven words, and it explains no part of itself and it was thanked by nobody, and it is the third time in this county that a man has been asked for one thing each and said a thing nobody can use, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man of about fifty-four who refused a knight out loud in a market street in Fallowmonth has now been asked for a smaller thing and has answered it in eleven words and five have said that the eleven words are not a yes and about four have said that the count on that floor is said by two people who are on it and that he is not on it and that is the whole of the arithmetic.

“**How long is a cart late?**”

Nobody said anything and the man of thirty-two with a stick answered it out loud, in the open, in his own trade, and it was 47 words, and nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him.

“**Nobody in this county can tell you, and I have been on that made road in both directions and there is nobody at the far end of it to ask, and the man who keeps that floor will know on the second of Goatmonth and not before.**”

Nobody said anything and nobody said anything back to him and nobody thanked him, and about four people in that market street had worked out inside nine seconds that a man has said that nobody can tell and five had not, and about four people in nine counties have said since that the answer to that question is the whole of what this county has learned in twenty-nine days of Hearthmonth and five have said that about four hundred people in a market street have heard a cart is missing and about four hundred people have gone on arguing about a bolt of cloth.

Nobody said anything and the light went off the west bank on that Saturday and found the upper edge of those boards about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over, and the lamp at the high end of them had been standing lit since the fourth hour and went out with the light, and about two hundred people went out of that street into about five trades and about two hundred stayed at the boards.
