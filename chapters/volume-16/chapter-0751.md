# Chapter 0751

**Frostmonth the twenty-sixth, YR 322, a Monday and not a market morning with the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door and the hearing not sitting and no market in that street, the fourth hour in that market street with nobody in it at all, and three people on a floor four days off being asked for one thing each.**

A Monday in that market town of about two thousand people is not a market morning, and nobody at all was in that market street at the fourth hour of the twenty-sixth of Frostmonth, and the bolt on the room over the weigh-house went on from the Saturday before and nobody went up the four hundred pace lane that holds it, and the boards at the low end of that street stood in the dark with nothing on them and the cloth was still over the bench nine doors down.

Then four days off, and about nine hundred people along that made road did the ordinary part of a Monday, and the ordinary part is that nothing about a Monday is visible on a road.

Nobody said anything and a drove went up that lane on the Thursday before, which was the twenty-second of Frostmonth, at about the sixth hour, and stood in the field with the stone wall round it and came back down at about the fifth hour, and nobody went with it, and that is thirty-eight drove to go up that lane since the second of Mudmonth, and four hundred people in that market street have never once watched one.

Eight of the nine yards of wall at the head of that made road are back in and the gap is about nine feet long and about four feet high, and the stone is quarried two miles off on the low side and one man has been carrying it two miles in his own hands for two hundred and sixty-four days, and nobody in nine counties has asked him when he will and nobody is going to.

Nobody thanked him and the five frames of brood in a walled yard half a mile below that wall were under about nine straw skirts for the winter, and the skirts went on about the ninth of Fallowmonth and the young man of about nineteen who keeps those nine hives has not been inside one of them since, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a skep under a skirt in Frostmonth is a thing nine people in nine counties can describe and five have said that nobody has asked him what is under the skirt.

Nobody said anything and the broom on that east rise has been off the hill since the twenty-ninth of Embermonth and about nine hundred yards of a line nine feet wide is left standing where it stands, and a line in grass that is not cut closes over in about three years and that one is dead in its own width in a winter that is not over, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a standing line of dead broom is harder in a hard winter than a standing line of dead grass and five have said that nobody in nine counties has asked him which of the two he meant.

Nobody thanked anybody and that scar is nine feet wide and about nine hundred yards long and it is thirty-one days old, and it is on the top of that shoulder from the ninth of the nine stones down to the place where the nine-foot line in the grass begins and the kerbed road starts, and it is on the back of a roadkeeper's board in a cart shed four days off that is about nine feet long and about nine inches wide, and about nine people in nine counties have stood in front of it and nobody in nine counties has asked the roadkeeper for it.

Nobody said anything and two refusals are in about four hundred mouths and in nobody's hand, and they were given on the fourteenth and the twenty-sixth of Fallowmonth, and that is a hundred and two days and ninety days, and nobody in nine counties has asked either of those two men anything since and nobody is going to, and four people in nine counties have said since that three months of not arguing with a thing is longer than anybody in this county has gone without asking twice about anything.

Nobody thanked him and the ninth mile stone on that made road was cleaned with a bit of stick on the Saturday before and the clean ring at the foot of it is about a foot wide and about half an inch deep, and he cleans that stone on a Tuesday and a Saturday and the twenty-sixth of Frostmonth is a Monday and he was two days short of it, and nobody has ever watched him do it and nine people in nine counties know that he does it and five have ever seen it.

Then the floor, which is the only floor in nine counties where a figure has been kept out loud every morning for about four and a half years, and it is about four paces long and it is the floor of a weigh-house with a board on the wall outside it and about four hundred and nine parish rolls come past it in a good month and Frostmonth is the month they come past clean.

Nobody said anything and Aren Kest was on that floor at about the fourth hour of that Monday with a woman of about fifty-one who keeps the inn at the high end of that market street and a man of about thirty-four with a satchel he has not opened, and he had come four days up the made road on the Sunday with the stick in his right hand and his left hand hanging at his side where anybody could see it, and he had asked none of them anything on the way and about four people in nine counties had worked out inside about nine seconds that a man who walks four days and asks nothing is a man who is going to ask something, and five had not.

Nobody thanked anybody and then he asked, and he asked out loud, and in the open, and in the market street at the sea end where a man with a barrow had just put about two hundred pounds of lime down at one door and taken it up again at two others.

“**I hold about ten things and I cannot hold ten on my own and I am asking the three of you to hold one each between you and to tell me the number out loud at this hour on the morning you meant to, and I am not going to say what the ten things are and nobody is going to be thanked for any of it.**”

Nobody said anything and the woman of about fifty-one said it back out loud, in the open, on that floor, to two people, in her own trade, and it was 43 words, and nobody stopped her and nobody thanked her.

“**I have a house open at the fourth hour every morning of this year and I will have one of yours open too, and I am not going to be thanked, and I want a fire laid for four people and not nine.**”

Nobody said anything and the man of about thirty-four with a satchel said it out loud, in the open, on that floor, to two people, in his own trade, and it was 58 words, and nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him.

“**I have been four days up a made road with nothing in the satchel and I will hold one of yours and put it in the satchel, and a man who puts a thing in a satchel is a man carrying it, and I am not going to be thanked for carrying a thing I asked to carry.**”

Nobody said anything and the man of about fifty-four who keeps that floor did not answer for about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over, and then he said it out loud, in the open, on his own floor, to two people, in his own trade, and it was 76 words, and nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him.

“**I have had a figure out loud on this floor every morning for about four and a half years and nobody has ever asked me where I get it, and I will hold one of yours and say the number at this hour, and I am not going to be thanked, and there is one thing I am not going to do and I will tell you what it is when I know what it is.**”

Nobody said anything and then he said it out loud, in the open, on that floor, to three people, in his own trade, and it was 52 words, and nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him.

“**A thing is held when two people have said out loud in the same hour that they are holding it, and both of them are still holding it on the next morning they said they would be, and that is all of it and I am not going to improve on it.**”

Nobody thanked anybody and the number was said out loud on that floor at about the fourth hour of the twenty-sixth of Frostmonth by one person and then said again by a second person, and both of them said three, and about four people in nine counties had worked out inside about nine seconds that a man of thirty-two with a stick had got three people to say a number out loud in the same hour and five had not, and about four have said since that a number said twice in the same hour is not a record and five have said that it is the only kind of count this county has ever had.

Nobody said anything and about four hundred and nine sheets came past that floor in Frostmonth and the man of about fifty-four said that out loud at about the ninth hour to four people in and five out and nobody stopped him and nobody thanked him, and about four people in that market town had worked out inside about nine seconds that a man who has said a number out loud every morning for about four and a half years has about four hundred and nine of them in the month that just went and five had not.

Nobody thanked him and the two miles of wall and orchard between the eleventh mile stone and the top of that hill are a mile of stone on the high side and a mile of orchard on the low side, and about nine hundred trees on that low side went out of blossom in the second week of Fallowmonth and the fruit came down into four hundred yards of grass and was still in it under the first frost, and the owners are nine households and not one of them has ever asked anybody anything about anybody who walks past its gate.

Nobody said anything and the hand lamp goes on the coping at the low side of that causeway four days south every Monday afternoon in the winter, and the twenty-sixth of Frostmonth was a Monday and the lamp went on at about the fourth hour of the afternoon, and about four people in nine counties had worked out inside about nine seconds that a Monday in this county is the one day of the week the lamp is lit and five had not, and nobody in that market street has ever asked a person at a gate at the near end of that water anything at all.

Nobody said anything and the ordinary part of that Monday is that nothing in that street is visible, and the near wheel of a cart came off at the low end and the carter got it up again with a bar in about nine minutes without anybody stopping on his account, and two women at the third door argued about a bolt of cloth for about four minutes and then went into the shop and came out with it wrapped, and the inn at the high end had four beds full and put no price out on any board.

Nobody thanked anybody and the room over that weigh-house stood bolted from noon on the Saturday before and the copyist of about twenty-six did not come down those thirteen stairs at all that day, and the woman of about twenty-nine who keeps that room had put a second lamp at the foot of them on the Friday of the week before and had it lit at the fourth hour as she has lit it every fourth hour since, and it is the second lamp in that room and it is on thirteen stairs and there was no lamp on them before, and about four people in that lane had worked out that she paid for it out of fourpence a year and five had not, and the four of them on the floor four days off did not see that lamp and were not told about it.

Nobody said anything and here is the whole of what this county has on the twenty-sixth of Frostmonth, and nothing about any of it is in a document. The eleventh mile stone about four hundred yards past the ninth has about nine hundred names cut into the side of it going back about two hundred years and nobody in nine counties can read more than about nine of them, and the two mile stones between the seventh mile stone and the ninth that had come out of line are in line where a man of thirty-two put them about the middle of Harvestmonth with a bar and a lever and no mortar, and the second of the two has not walked out of line since, and nobody in nine counties has asked him and nobody has been told.

The four feet of ash with two stones on the end of it is still across that lane gate at the head of the made road, and the drove has gone up past it through the gap at the low end of about nine hundred yards of wall on every Thursday since the second of Mudmonth and has never once gone through that gate, and about four people in nine counties have said since that the gate is not the road and five have said that nobody has ever asked the man who bars it what the gate is for.

About nine hundred yards of a cart track goes north out of that market town and up the made road for about eleven miles and then stops being made, and about a mile of it after that is a cart track with a stone in the middle of it that a man put there about nine years ago. A cart of lime has come up that track and onto that made road on every second Thursday for about nine years and about two hundred and thirty times, and the last one came on the twenty-ninth of Frostmonth, which was the Thursday before, and it stood on that weigh-house floor for about as long as it takes to turn a cart twice over and went on up the made road with about four hundred pounds of it in it, and it is the only thing in nine counties that arrives on a day anybody here can name in advance.

Then the ninth hour came, and the market street was still not a market, and about nine people were in it and four of them were at the boards with nothing on them and five were not, and a person at the third door asked him one thing out loud at about the ninth hour, kindly, and nobody stopped it, and it was not about a road and not about a wall.

Four people in that market street had heard a man ask three people for one thing each and five had heard a rule about two people saying a number out loud in the same hour, and nobody argued with him and nobody thanked him, and about four people in nine counties have said since that a man who asks three people for one thing each in the fourth hour of a Monday has started a thing and five have said that a man who asks three people for one thing each in the fourth hour of a Monday has been on four days of made road to do it and has not said what the ten things are.

“**Is it going to be all right?**”

Nobody said anything back to him and nobody thanked him, and about four people in that market street had worked out inside about nine seconds that a man with a stick has told a market street that a thing is not a count unless two people say it and five had not, and about four people in nine counties have said since that nobody in nine counties has consoled him and nobody has thanked him and five have said that this county has never had anybody in it ask three people for one thing each and be given it before.
