# Chapter 0428

**The ninth, tenth, eleventh and twelfth days of Harvestmonth, YR 318, a Saturday, a Sunday, a Monday and a Tuesday, and one of the four is a market day, and the four places are an office over a shop, a gate in a field, a yard, and a single arch over a beck nine miles off the kilns, and the four are not in a line with each other and about nine hundred sheets a month go out of the first of them into nine counties while a mason of about sixty-eight stands about nine feet off the fourth of them and is not sent for.**

## One

**On the Saturday a man of about thirty-four with a sheet asked him for a person.**

The form was on the counter with the three lines on it and a second form behind it with a head on it that the first form did not have, and the head took a trade, and the trade was how a man is found.

“**It wants somebody's trade against it,**” the man of about thirty-four with a sheet said. “**Not yours particularly. Anybody's. If there is a trade against that place then a man in this county can go and stand on it and see whether it is what the sheet says.**”

**And that is a good argument and it is in his interest and the whole of it is in his interest, and it had not occurred to the man of about thirty-four that a trade against a place is also a thing that makes a place the kind of place a man can be sent to.**

Aren did not do it. He did not say he would not and he did not say why, and he said that a top course of dressed stone wants a man who is good at a ring and that the man in that room is nineteen, and the man of about thirty-four said that was not what he had asked, and Aren said he knew, and the clerk of about nineteen did not look up.

## Two

**On the Sunday a man of about forty-four who farms a field asked him to say that a figure was right.**

He had come into Merefen with eleven dead sheep off the salt bank and he was in a yard in a street four hundred yards long and he had about two hours before the cart went.

“**They have put four hundred and seventy on it,**” he said. “**If that stands they will make the lane up and the carts will stop going round the quarter and they will go up my gate instead, and I have been four years with a barrow in the soft at the bottom of my own field.**”

He was not lying. **The count was honest, the nine were honest, the man of about thirty-four was honest, and about thirty-four people live in that lane, and all four of those are true and the man of about forty-four is asking a stranger to put his own need in front of all four of them.**

Aren did not do it. He did not tell him the figure was wrong, because it was not wrong, and he did not tell him it was right, and he said that he had asked the man what the place was called and the man had told him, and the man of about forty-four said that was not what he had asked either, and then the cart came and he went.

## Three

**On the Monday a man of about thirty-three with a barrow asked him to read a sheet out of a coat.**

He did it in a yard about two miles from the bar he stands at on four days in seven, and he had the sheet in his hand and not out of the coat, which is a different thing, and he had carried it thirty-seven days and had a barrow-load of glass in the other hand.

“**You can read.**”

“**I can.**”

“**Then read that.**”

**And the man of about thirty-three with a barrow has been told not to say what a thing is, and has decided some while ago that not looking is the same as not saying, and has been right about that for thirty-seven days, and he was not asking about the sheet and he was asking whether a man with a stick would do a thing for him that nobody will do for him and cannot be asked to do twice.**

Aren did not do it. He did not take it and he did not open it and he said that a man who has been carrying a thing for five weeks and two days and has not opened it has a reason for that, and the man of about thirty-three with a barrow put it back in the inside pocket of a coat that is not his and said that he had, and went off up a road with a load of glass in a barrow.

## Four

**On the Tuesday a mason of about sixty-eight asked him to carry one sentence to a man four miles off, and the sentence took him a day and a half to walk there and a day and a half to walk back and ten words to say.**

The arch is nine miles off the kilns at the end of an unclassified road and it is one span with no parapet on the ring and the beck is about eleven inches over the sill. **The top course of it is out of level by about a hand's width and it wants two men and a barrow and four days and a new stone, and the man four miles off only does drains, and the man standing nine feet off that arch has laid about nine hundred rings and has not been asked for one of them in about eleven years.**

“**Will you ask him whether he would take it on.**”

That is ten words and it is the whole of what a mason of about sixty-eight said to him on the Tuesday, and it is the first time in about forty years that he has asked anybody for anything at all, and he is not sending anybody and nobody has sent anybody.

Aren did not do it. He said he would, and he did not, and the mason of about sixty-eight went down to the beck and put a hand on the ring and said nothing else about it, and about nine feet of dressed top course on the north approach is a hand's width out of level and will be a hand's width out of level next spring.

**A man of about fifty-one is laying a drain arch four miles off and he is going to be free on the first of a month, and nobody has told him that a top course is going to be free in the same week, and nobody has told the mason of about sixty-eight that the drain man is free, and about two men who would both be in the same field inside a fortnight of each other are about four miles apart and neither of them has the other's name.**

That is the whole of the fourth of the four and it is about nine feet of stone and about four days of a man who is sixty-eight, and there is no form in this kingdom on which a man is entered as somebody who is available.

## Five

**Nobody said one word to him about any of the four, in any of the four places, on any of the four days, and that is the whole of what happened and it is the reason the four are four days and not a scene.**

A man of about thirty-four with a sheet wanted a trade against a place and got a remark about a ring. A man of about forty-four wanted a number to stand and got the name of his own field back. A man of about thirty-three with a barrow wanted a sheet read and got the reason he had not opened it. A mason of about sixty-eight wanted a sentence carried four miles and got a promise.

**And each of the four wanted the thing that was in their own interest, and two of the four would have been damaged by getting it and did not know that, and one of the four would have been damaged by not getting it and knew it, and the four of them are not in a line with each other and were not in a line with each other and will not be, and there is no column in this kingdom that has ever held the four of them and there is going to be one inside four days that holds three of them.**

The cost of the four days is a column and it is the smallest one anybody has kept about four days in about nine counties and it does not contain the four.

**A loaf at one penny on each of the four days is four pence. A bed at two pence on each of the four days is eight pence. A quart of small beer at two pence on each of the four days is eight pence. A horse's feed at nine pence on the two days he took a horse is eighteen pence. A cart's fare at four pence on the two days he did not walk is eight pence. A sheet of paper at one penny. Four and eight and eight and eighteen and eight and one is forty-seven pence.** A mark is forty-eight pence and forty-seven pence is a penny under one, and he came to it inside a penny on the fourth of the four days, and there is no form in this kingdom on which a man enters four things he did not do.
