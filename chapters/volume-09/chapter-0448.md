# Chapter 0448

**The fifteenth day of Wolfmonth, YR 318, a Thursday and a market day, and the first hour and the fifth hour and the ninth hour, in a market town of about two thousand people on the salt, where the market runs on a Thursday and a Saturday and this is the first of the two, and nine feet of oak across nine feet of a kerb at the head of a made road four miles west of the town is down this day because it comes up on a Monday, a Wednesday, a Friday and a Sunday, and about two hundred carts a week come up that road whether it is up or down, and the boy who stands at one end of it when it is up is at the market with a basket on his arm and is four miles from his own bar.**

## One

**The bar is down on a Thursday and has been down on a Thursday every Thursday since the twenty-fourth of a month in Wolfmonth of the year before, and the man of about nineteen has not once been at his bar on a day it was down, and he is not at it now, and he is about nine feet off a stall with his hands in the front of his coat.**

He came in at about the fifth hour with a hand-basket of glass that nobody had ordered, which is about four hundred times in four years, and he stood there. About two hundred people are in that street on a market morning and the market trades on two days in seven.

**A man of about thirty-three with a barrow came up that street at about the sixth hour with a load of glass in the barrow and stopped about four feet off the boy of nineteen and put the barrow down, and the two of them had not spoken in about three months, and the man of about thirty-three with a barrow is the man who asked the boy one question in a month in Harvestmonth on that same road and was believed.**

## Two

**And the boy of nineteen worked his own figure out in about nine minutes with a pencil on the back of a hand, standing at a stall, on a market morning, and nobody asked him for it and nobody in that street knows that he counts days and not twists.**

It is three hundred and fifty-one days. That is fifty weeks and one day.

**Fifty weeks of four up-days in seven is two hundred. The Sundays in fifty weeks are fifty. Two hundred less fifty is a hundred and fifty.** And the one day over is a Wednesday, because the bar went up on a Wednesday and fifty weeks lands on a Wednesday, and a Wednesday is up and a Wednesday is paid, and the Sunday that is not paid is two days off. A hundred and fifty and one is a hundred and fifty-one.

**And it cross-checks. Three hundred and one days was on the twenty-fifth of a month in Fallowmonth and that is a hundred and twenty-nine paid days. Fifty days is seven weeks and one day. Seven weeks is twenty-one paid days. A hundred and twenty-nine and twenty-one is a hundred and fifty, and then a Wednesday again is a hundred and fifty-one.**

He is paid nine pence a day for three of the four up-days and the one he is not paid for is the Sunday, and the start is the twenty-fourth of a month in Wolfmonth of the year before, which was a Wednesday, and that is the start.

**A hundred and fifty-one twists of paper at nine pence each is one thousand three hundred and fifty-nine pence, and a mark is forty-eight pence, and forty-eight into that is twenty-eight with fifteen over, and that is twenty-eight marks and fifteen pence. And the two figures are not in one column and are not in any column anybody keeps and the boy has counted the twists and not the days, as he has done every week for about a year, and about two hundred carts a week went up that road while he was doing the sum.**

There is a sheet under a stone on a bench in a shed nine feet by seven, four miles up that made road, with about nine figures on it and no head on it, and it went under that stone on the fourteenth of a month in Greenmonth. **That is a hundred and eighty-one days.** It has not been read and it is not going to be read and he is not going to be asked about it again.

**And there is a third piece of paper in the inside of this boy's coat that he has carried since the second of a month in Fallowmonth, which is seventy-three days, and it is a receipt in a fair hand and he cannot read it and nobody has ever read it for him. A man of about thirty-three with a barrow read every word of a copy of it in a yard in that same month and could do nothing with it and put it back in the inside pocket of a coat that is not his.**

## Three

**The morning went the way a market morning goes, and about two hundred people were in that street and none of them was there for any reason that anybody could afterwards have put in a column.**

A carter at a stall said to another carter that there would be no salt up the made road that week because a man who carts it had been three days on a job, and the other one said that was the fourth week of it, and a third one said something about the price at a port, and the trade of salt carting was said out loud in that street in the ordinary way at about the seventh hour by three men who were talking about hay.

A woman at a fish stall said that about four hundred and seventy went down past the stile to the nine-mile road in a day and a half, and the man she said it to laughed at her and said there was no counting it now anybody, and she said her father counted it twice and gave up, and they went on to a thing about eels.

**The man of about thirty-three with a barrow stood with his load a foot off that stall for about nine minutes with his back to it, and the paper in the inside pocket of a coat that is not his has been in that coat for a hundred and thirty-one days, and it is about nine figures and a date at the foot and there is no head on it, and nobody in four counties can make anything of it, and he was not asked about it and did not offer it and has not offered it to anybody since a man with a stick gave him a reason for not opening it in a yard in a month in Harvestmonth.**

Two counties off, a man of about fifty-one finished a drain arch in a wall in a village in the first week of a month in Wolfmonth and took his money on the ninth, and he was at a market in a different county on that Thursday and drank a quart of small beer and told a man about a course of stones that was out of true. Four miles from that village a man of about sixty-eight has been out of work eleven years and did not come to that market and does not know there was a drain man in his county in the first week of a month, and the drain man does not know there is a mason of sixty-eight four miles off who has laid about nine hundred rings and has not been asked for one of them in about eleven years.

**And the two of them are about four miles apart and neither has the other's name, and that is not a gap in anything, because there is no form in this kingdom on which a man is entered as somebody who is free in a particular week.**

## Four

**And then the man of about thirty-three with a barrow said the thing he had walked four miles to say, and he said it in nine words, and it is the only sentence anybody in that street said that morning that cost either of them anything.**

"**Your four months came out the twenty-fourth of Thawmonth.**"

**The twenty-fourth of a month in Thawmonth was two hundred and thirty-one days back, and two hundred and thirty-one is thirty-three weeks exactly, and about eight people in that town of about two thousand have worked that out, and about four of the eight are wrong by a day, and not one of the eight had ever said one word about it, and the man who said it does not know whether he is one of the four who are right or one of the four who are wrong, and there is nothing in that town and nothing in that county that would ever settle it, and he found that out about four seconds after he had said it and has not mentioned it since.**

The boy of nineteen did not do anything.

**He looked at the man of about thirty-three with a barrow for about four seconds and then he looked at the middle of that street, which is what a man does who has been at one end of nine feet of oak for a year and has about a second and a half to decide a thing, and then he picked his basket up off the stall and went and did his day's work, and about two hundred carts came up a made road four miles west of that street that day and the bar was down and the boy was not at it and the boy was about two miles east of it.**

The man of about thirty-three with a barrow put his load back in the barrow and went and did his day's work. **A man of about thirty-three on a cart is paid four pence a day and he gave up about a quarter of that day to walk four miles and say nine words, and that is about a penny, and it is not in any column and he is not going to tell anybody he gave it up and nobody is going to tell him that they noticed.**

## Five

The cost of that Thursday is a column and it is a market morning and about two hundred people were in that street and the four things in it cost nothing and were not paid for and were not remarked on by anybody.

**a second sheet of paper at one penny, a third bed at two, a lamp at two, a cart's fare at four, a handcart hired for the day at four, a tin of nails at five, a third room at six, a wheel iron at eight, a ream of paper at eight, a second ream of paper at eight, a horse's feed at nine, a second horse's feed at nine and a load of sand at nine.** One, two, two, four, four, five, six, eight, eight, eight, nine, nine and nine is seventy-five pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and seventy-five pence is a mark and twenty-seven over**, and a hundred and fifty-one twists of nine pence is printed outside that column and added to nothing, and the two are not in one column, and about two hundred carts a week come up that road whether it is up or down and about two hundred people in that town know exactly why and not one of them has ever started it.

**And a place, a figure and a trade were all said out loud in one market in about four minutes by three people who were talking about something else, and about four hundred and seventy went down past a stile, and a trade is in four counties, and a sheet nine inches by six with three lines and no head on it is in the inside pocket of a coat about four feet off a boy of nineteen and is the only record in the world of when those three things were entered and by whom, and he did not take it out, and nobody in nine counties knows that he is the only man who could say.**
