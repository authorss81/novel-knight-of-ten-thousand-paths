# Chapter 0440

**The thirteenth day of Embermonth, YR 318, a Sunday and not a market day, and the fourth hour and the eighth hour and the eleventh hour, in a room over a shop in a street four hundred yards long in a market town of about two thousand people on the salt, where the market trades on a Thursday and a Saturday and this is neither of them, and the room is taken by the day and a man of thirty-two with a limp has it from the fourth hour and gives it up at dusk.**

## One

**A fortnight after the twenty-ninth of Fallowmonth, and a fortnight after he stood in that same yard and did not see a boy of seventeen count a shelf board with his thumb, the taking is in nine counties, and that is the whole of it.**

It is in a kilns country, in a gate, in a door, in about four mouths, none of which is anybody's mouth in particular. It is in a fold thirty miles south, in a man of about forty-one's answer, in twenty-one words. It is in a roadhouse with a plate on a bracket over its door, in a till box under a pound of silver, shown to four people in a month. It is in a kitchen nine miles on, in a woman's hand and in four figures with no head on them. It is on a made road at a shed nine feet by seven, in the inside of a coat that is not his, where a man of about nineteen cannot read it. It is in a carter's mouth, in a handcart man's mouth, and in the mouth of a woman of about fifty-eight who sweeps a yard and counts the men in it. It is under a sill stone in a yard, in a line a mason of about sixty-eight has not told anybody about. It is in the inside pocket of a coat that is not his, on top of another one, in a county on the salt. And it is in a book of about four hundred entries a boy of seventeen made himself, and he has shown it to nobody.

**Nine counties and eleven places, and about four weeks for it to get to the last of them, and a fortnight of it standing in the ninth, and not one of the eleven has ever been in a room with another of the eleven, and the one possible exception is a woman who has put a plate in front of a boy of seventeen about four hundred times in four years and has never once asked him a question, and a plate in front of a person is not a conversation, and she has had about four hundred of those and he was in about four hundred of them and neither of them knows the other is on this list.**

**And the only person alive who knows how many there are is a man of thirty-two with a limp, and he is on no list, and there is no list.**

## Two

**Nobody in nine counties can say who sent four men with a paper and a crowbar to a door that was not locked, and that is not a failure of enquiry, and it is worth having exactly why.**

A boy of seventeen counted nine worn places on a shelf board and worked out that the four men did not know what was on the shelf, and a man who does not know what is on a shelf is not a man who has been told what is in it. **The paper had a seal on it and a name on it, and the four of them read it and did what it said and asked nobody anything, and a piece of paper with a name on it has a name on it, and the name is an office, and an office is a room, and that room is four hundred miles off.**

So there is no man in nine counties to bring to anything. **There is a seal, and a form of words, and four men who did what a paper said on a Monday in a yard, and about three hundred and sixty sheets that went out of the same door three weeks later because a yard has to be empty, and a second line under a receipt that says the building was cleared and does not say what went out of it.**

**And the one man in nine counties who could be asked about any part of it is a man of about thirty-four with a sheet, and he was sent out three times in four days in a month in Rainmonth and found a place and a figure and a trade for a road that was none of the three, and he answered the right question and wrote down the true answer every time, and he is not a villain and he does not know what is in his own coat and he is not going to be told.**

## Three

**And the receipt is in nine counties, in a fair hand, and it is accurate, and a man who reads it gets a place and a figure of men and a trade and a name at the foot of it, in that order, and a receipt is a record, and a record with a place and a figure and a trade on it is the thing that nine counties have spent a year arguing against.**

He has had that in his head since the eighth hour of the second of Fallowmonth and he has not said one word of it to anybody in forty-one days, and he is not going to, and there is nobody he could say it to. **The same hand wrote four figures at the bottom of a column with no head on it in a kitchen nine miles on, and the two of those are the same kind of thing, and a column with a head on it and four figures under it is the fourth of the four bad things a number can do, and the only person in nine counties who can see both of them at once is a man on no list in a room taken by the day.**

**He did not write it down. He had the notebook out on the table at about the ninth hour with the eleven lines on it and no words on any of them, and he turned it over once, and he put it back in the coat.**

## Four

**And at about the eighth hour a man of about twenty came up those stairs with a fire he had not paid for and asked whether there was anything in the room he wanted, in the way a man asks that in a room over a shop that is let by the day, and the man of thirty-two with a limp told him there was not and gave him the fire anyway.**

That is the practice and he has never held it and he is not going to, and it is held in nine places in nine counties and this room is a room that is let by the day and its keeper goes home at dusk and there is nobody to hold anything.

**And the fire is the only thing he gave anybody in nine counties in about four months that was not information, and the reason is that a plate in front of a person is not information.**

Then he sat and did the thing that costs him the most and is the whole of the decision.

**He is the connection. He is the only person alive who knows that nine counties have heard about a door with no lock on it, and that about three hundred and sixty sheets went out of a building nobody was told to clear, and that the four men who took the first forty-one of them were not told what was on the shelf, and that a figure with no head on it cannot be refused and cannot be handed back, and that a boundary of two miles and nine stones is the only record any county keeps of whether a person has been there twice, and that a mark cut with a flat chisel in the bed of a sill stone is a count and not a name, and that a receipt is a record, and all seven of those are his, and every one of the seven is in a different county, and nobody else has more than one.**

**And he is not going to tell one of them about another of them, and he is not going to make a list, and the reason is not a reason anybody would call decent.**

A list of eleven people in nine places, each of whom has heard about a door with no lock on it, is a column. **A column is a thing that can be picked up by the next man with a form, and a man with a form is not a bad man and is doing his work, and about four hundred and seventy people went down a road in a day and a half and the county put it in a column, and the whole of the harm of that was done inside four days by people who were each doing exactly what they were asked.**

**So the connection goes back to being eleven people who have not met, and the only person carrying it is a man of thirty-two with a limp who is on no list in nine counties and who cannot leave it, and that is the cost, and it is a cost he has chosen, and he chose it at about the eleventh hour of a Sunday in a room that is let by the day over a shop in a street four hundred yards long.**

## Five

The cost of that Sunday is a column and it is three days on a road and it is the last one he keeps before the winter, and the three days bought him about nine inches by six of paper in the inside pocket of a coat, three lines and no head, and about nine counties.

**a loaf on the road, a cart's fare, a loaf, a third horse's feed, a room, a bed, a fire, a second horse's feed, a bed on the road, a quart of small beer, a candle on the road, a tallow candle, a fire on the road, a horse's feed, a sheet of paper, a quill and ink, a room on the road: one, four, one, nine, six, two, three, nine, two, two, two, two, three, nine, one, seven, six is sixty-nine pence.** A mark is forty-eight pence and sixty-nine pence is a mark and twenty-one pence over, and a fire given to a man of about twenty at the eighth hour of a Sunday is not in that column and is not going to be in any column anybody keeps.

**And about forty-one sheets went out of a building with a door that was not locked, and about three hundred and sixty went out of it on two handcarts on the twenty-third, and a receipt with a place and a figure of men and a trade on it is in nine counties in a fair hand, and a man of thirty-four with a sheet wrote it and does not know what is in it, and a woman of about fifty has four of his figures on a board with no head on it and does not know he is the same man, and a mason of about sixty-eight has a mark under a sill stone and has not told anybody, and a boy of seventeen who carries glass has about three hundred and sixty in a book he made himself and has shown to nobody, and a man of thirty-two with a limp is on no list, and eleven people in eleven places have been connected by a man who is not on any of it and who has not told anybody that he did it, and not one of the eleven has been given back to anybody, and about four hundred and seventy people have got a number on a place and about nine counties have got a door.**
