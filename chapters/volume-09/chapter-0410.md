# Chapter 0410

**The seventh day of Longlight, YR 318, a Sunday and not a market day, and the second hour and the fifth hour and the eighth hour, in a town of about four thousand people in a fen county where about two hundred people were out of their houses and about a hundred and forty of the two hundred went to a chapel about nine miles off and about forty of them went nowhere in particular and stood about, and a cart went out of a room over a weigh-house with a door on a lane about a hundred and four sheets on a Saturday, and eight of the hundred and four are the same eight words, and the room is two hundred miles off and its keeper was in this county until the fifth morning and went back to work on the sixth.**

## One

**Eight places. Four of them are places a man of thirty-two with a limp has been in inside about forty-one days and four of them are places he has never been and does not know and is not going to be told.**

The four he has been in are the kilns, the arch, the fold thirty miles south, and the roadhouse nine miles on from the kilns. **The other four were named on the tenth of Greenmonth in about nine seconds by a woman of twenty-nine who was standing at a bench-board with a sheet on it, and she named them off a list of nine rooms that she keeps and has never shown to anybody and could produce inside a quarter of an hour, and she would not say where they are and he did not ask twice.**

**That is the whole of what she gave and it is the largest thing anybody has given anybody in about nine days, and it is eight words in eight places, and four of the eight are in four counties and the keeper of records has never been in any of the four and rode up on the cart on the fifth morning and went back to work on the sixth.**

## Two

The paper is a column and it is short.

**A quire of paper at two shillings and sixpence, which is thirty pence. Eight copies take two sheets between them, and a quire is about twenty-four sheets, and a room that sends about nine hundred sheets a month into nine counties has about nine hundred quires a year somewhere behind it and does not pay for them out of a column of its own.**

That is the whole cost. **Thirty pence for a quire, and eight words, and two sheets, and the four feet of a board in a kitchen nine miles on from the kilns that has been kept for thirty-one years cost nothing and is worth more.**

**And a rate is not a wage and a wage is not a toll and a bar is not a rate and a return is not a toll, and there is a roadhouse nine miles on from the kilns that puts a plate in front of about nine people a week for thirty-one years and has never had a column with a head on it, and the column with no head on it is four feet of board, and the two of those facts have never been in the same sentence in this kingdom.**

## Three

**The cart was loaded on the Saturday and it is loaded on about a hundred and four sheets and the sheets are banded in dozens, and a band is a hundred and eight, and about a hundred and four is a band with four taken out of it, and the band is the same band and it does not fit and it is tied with a cord.**

**And eight of the hundred and four are the same eight words, and they are not in a bundle, and they are in the middle of the load, and a man of about thirty-three with a barrow loaded that cart and looked at the ninth sheet down and said, without any particular expression at all, that he had seen those eight lines before, in a market, in a road.**

Nobody asked him where. **He said, in about nine words, that he had not got to the bottom of what he said in that market and that he was not going to start on it in a yard, and he put the sheet in and tied the band and went on loading, and about a hundred and four sheets went out of a room with a door on a lane on the fixed day, and the room has no list and the man who loaded the cart does not know what he said.**

**And that is the whole of the distribution, and there was no plan in it, and about four of the eight are in four counties inside a day's ride of that town by the eighth hour on that Sunday, and the other four are in a cart on a road with a fortnight in front of it, and nobody on that cart is a keeper of anything, and two of the eight are already on their way back and three of them never went anywhere at all.**

**The man of about twenty was not in that building and does not know any of this and will not find out for about eleven years, and he is the only person alive who has seen the whole of it twice.** He wrote the eight words on the back of a form in a back room on the first of Greenmonth, and he put the pen in his pocket, and about nine days later a hundred and four sheets went out of a room two hundred miles off with the same eight words on eight of them, and both of those things happened because of a bench-board and a piece of chalk and a millimetre of dust, and he is a man of about twenty and he is doing another job on that Thursday and nobody has told him.

## Four

**Four of the eight have already sent word, and none of the four sent it in a hurry, and none of the four was angry, and three of the four said no to the paper and did the thing anyway.**

**The woman of about forty-four at the kilns said that she would not have a sheet, and that she will say the first eight in a draw the way she has said them for nineteen years, and that a piece of paper with eight words on it in a kiln yard is a thing that can be picked up, and she is right about that and nobody in that yard has yet thought of the answer to it.**

**The man of about forty with a boundary of two miles said the thing already said, at length, and it is not improved on, and a boundary will be walked on the first of Longmonth by one man and not by a boy of nine, and that is the end of that.**

**The woman of about fifty at the roadhouse said that she read seven of them out loud to an empty room and did not read the eighth, and that she will keep doing the eighth, and that the paper can go back where it came from, and the paper did go back, on the same cart, in the same band, and the man of about thirty-three with a barrow tied it and did not ask what it was.**

**The fourth is a man of about thirty-one with a canvas bag and he is not one of the four places and he sent word anyway, about nine days' ride away, and his whole of it was that a man of about twenty had written the eight words on the back of a form because there was nothing else, and that he had a form in a bag that a man of about twenty-two filled in about four years ago with somebody else's arithmetic on the front, and that if anybody ever wanted the same eight words again there was a better place to put them and he would know where it was and he would not say.**

**And a fifth word came back from a road, and it is the only one of the five that asked a question.** A man of about thirty-four on the coast about two days off wrote nine words and asked whether the first of the eight was still being done anywhere, and he did not say who he was and he did not say where he was and the nine words came in on the same cart as the eight sheets with no letter and no seal and a place in the county written on the back in a hand nobody in that room recognised, and the keeper of records read it standing up in about four seconds and then sat down and did not answer it for about a minute and a half, and the minute and a half is the only time in about nine days that anybody in that building was not doing what they were doing.

**And the four places she named off the list she will not produce are not on any of this, and a man of about thirty-three with a barrow is going to have to ride that cart in about a fortnight to find out whether a sheet with eight words on it got to a man in a byre, and he has not been asked to and has not offered, and there is no form in this kingdom on which a man volunteers for a delivery.**

## Five

**The ninth copy is on a bench in a shed nine feet by seven at the head of a made road four miles west of Marl, under a stone, with nine figures on it and no head and a date at the bottom, and a man of about nineteen cannot read any of it and has asked nobody to read it and is not going to, and about eighty and three twists of paper with nine pence in each of them are in the left pocket of a coat that is not his, and a bar that is up four days in seven for a hundred and ninety-three days gives about a hundred and ten, and he is paid for three of the four, and that is about eighty and three, and he has counted the twists and not the days.**

**And the eight are in eight places, and where they are is this, and three of the eight are nowhere, and that is the finding and not a disappointment.**

The first is in nobody's hand. It is in a woman's mouth in a yard of four kilns two days west, on a beat of about seventy times a round, and it will be said at the next draw, and the draw is in about four days, and the fourth kiln at that yard is still cold and is still the first thing a buyer sees from the road.

The second is nowhere, because the woman who would have held it refused it and said why in a hundred and eight words.

The third is nowhere, because a man of forty-one in a fold of ground thirty miles south put it in a coat and gave it back, and a coat is not a place to keep a thing in and there is no form in this kingdom that says it is.

The fourth is nowhere, because a mason of about sixty-eight was asked to take it and said that a stone is not a place to keep a piece of paper and that he would lose it, and a mason who has lost one job in about eleven years is not going to be handed a piece of paper to lose.

The fifth is in a drawer in a house in a market town, taken off a bench-board on the fifteenth by a man who did not open it and has not opened it and is not going to.

The sixth is in a cart, which is the best of the eight.

The seventh is about nine days of a cart ride from the man of about thirty-three with a barrow, who has not been asked and who has offered, and about whom there is no form in this kingdom on which a man volunteers for a delivery.

And the eighth is on a front-room table at a roadhouse, with a piece of chalk dust on it, because a woman of about fifty read seven of the eight out loud to an empty room and did not read the eighth, and the paper went back on the same cart in the same band, and the chalk is still on the table.

**And a man of about thirty-two with a limp is not holding any of the eight and is not holding the ninth, and has not held any of them since the fourth of Greenmonth, and is on no list that any of the nine places is on, and there is no form anywhere in nine counties that has his trade against any of the eight, and it is the first thing he has wanted in about four years that he was not also the person to pay for.**

**About four hundred people went to a chapel about nine miles off that Sunday, and about a hundred and forty of the about two hundred who were out of their houses in that town of about four thousand were among them, and about forty went nowhere in particular and stood about, and about a hundred and four sheets went out of a room two hundred miles off on the Saturday, and eight of them were the same, and two of them are already on their way back, and about nine counties do not know that any of it happened and one of them is going to find out from a man with a barrow who does not know he is carrying it.**
