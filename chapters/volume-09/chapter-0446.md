# Chapter 0446

**The thirtieth day of Embermonth, YR 318, a Wednesday and not a market day, and the eleventh hour, and the second hour and the fourth hour of the second day of Wolfmonth, YR 318, which is a Friday and not a market day, in a meeting room over a store in a township of about nine hundred people at the eleventh hour of the first of the two days, and on a street of working yards in a fen town of about four thousand people on the second of them, four hundred yards below a yard where nine men keep the tools, and the room is given up at dusk and the man of thirty-two with a limp walks out of it and does not come back.**

## One

**He left at about the eleventh hour and he did not make a speech and nobody made one at him, and what happened is that nine people in a room of eleven paces by nineteen carried on for about nine minutes after the door shut and then stopped, and then started again, and the stopping and starting took about four seconds and no one in that room has mentioned it since.**

He had asked for the room until dusk on the Monday and the woman of about sixty-three who keeps the book had not been in it and had sent a boy of about nine with a note, and the note said what it always says, which is that the room is let by the day and the bolt is on the inside and has not been drawn since she decided against it in about nine seconds.

**He left the sheet of nine inches by six in the inside pocket of his coat, where it has been since the eleventh day of Harvestmonth, and it is the only record in the world of when three things were entered on a form and by whom, and he took it with him, and he was not asked for it, and no clerk of about nineteen in any county will ever ask him for it.**

**The room had a shilling in it that was not his and a bench-board under the window with four heads on it and a ninth line ruled and nothing on it, and he saw both of them from the doorway and he did not go back up the stairs.**

He walked out of that township at about the second hour of the first of the two days and he was on a made road by the fourth hour and he slept in a barn about nine miles on with about eleven other men in it, and one of the eleven asked him whether he was going as far as the fen and he said he was, and that was the whole of the conversation and the man did not ask again.

## Two

The fen town is about four thousand people and it is four hundred yards of street of working yards between his door and the yard, and he came into it at about the second hour of the second of the two days and went to the cooper's and had the room over the shop that he has had for about three years.

**Since the seventh day of a month in Longlight he has not gone up a bank and he is not going to. Coming out of a chair takes him about four seconds longer than going into one, he has counted it perhaps nine hundred times, and it has not once come out at three.**

There was a market in that town on the Thursday of Wolfmonth and he walked through the middle of it and about nine hundred people were in a street four hundred yards long and about forty of them had heard a song sung in a market in another county in a month in Greenmonth, and that market was about five months back, and he looked at the middle of the street and not at the ends of it, and there is no such thing as looking at four hundred people.

## Three

**And here are the two men, and it is the whole of the second of the two days, and it took about an hour and a half and it is not worth a paragraph of anybody being pleased about.**

The yard is four hundred yards up that street and the gate is open and the gate has been open all day and has been open for about nine years, and nine men keep the tools in it and a man of about twenty-five owns it, and the yard's rule is that nothing which belongs to a man goes in that yard's book and only a thing set.

Aren went up the street at about the third hour with a loaf in his coat and did not go in the gate.

**Tovan Kest was in the yard with a barrow that had a wheel out of it.** He was on the off side of the barrow with the pin in his hand and the pin was not going back in, and he had been at it about twenty minutes, and the axle was soft and the hole had gone out of true with about a season of frost in it, and a man who is good at loads can see that from about nine feet without going near it, and a man of about thirty-two with a limp is that man and he saw it and he kept walking.

Nobody said anything to anybody. **There is no word for what the two of them are, and there has not been one for about a year, and about four hundred people in that town of about four thousand have one, and neither of them has used it and neither of them said anything on that street.**

He went to his room over the cooper's. He sat on a stool. He had the notebook in his coat and did not take it out, and the eleven lines he carried had no words on any of them, and there is no twelfth line in it, and there has not been one since Thawmonth.

At about the fourth hour a barrow came past his door going up the street with about nine feet of dressed stone in it, and it was the same barrow and the wheel was on it and the pin was in it, and it went up the road and did not stop.

**And that is the whole of it. It is a barrow and a wheel and about a hundred and forty yards of walking in one direction and a door, and the two of them did not say one word, and neither of them said anything about the year, and one of them got the pin in and the other one kept his hands in his coat, and about nine men in that yard saw a barrow go out and did not know anything about it and would not have believed it either way.**

## Four

**And the leaving was not a defeat and it is not written as one, and the page does not explain it and does not admire it, and what happened to the room is this.**

Nine people said a correct thing on the Monday and eight people let it stand and the tenth went out of the door on the Wednesday, and the nine went on meeting, and there is a book about a hundred and forty years old on a shelf behind the door that has nothing to do with them, and the room was let on the Thursday to a woman of about forty-one who wanted a room for a thing she had not explained, and she was in it for about two hours and left a coin on the table by the door, and the coin went in a box on the shelf and not in the book.

**He is not on a list in that township and he is not on a list in the nine counties round it, and he did not lose a thing that can be put in a column, and he is the only person in nine counties who knows where nine of the eleven places are, and he is going to keep that, and the reason he is going to keep it is a column and the reason he said nothing about it on the Monday is that the two of them were right.**

The span of nineteen mornings that two men built four miles off is a hand's width out of level and will be a hand's width out of level next spring, and it is not a repair and it is not about this, and the two of them have not walked it together and are not going to and the man of about thirty-two with a limp was not at the oath-stone in Longlight and did not send anybody and the man of about twenty-five sent a barrow and two men and a length of board because a barrow going four miles for a day is not a job anybody has to think about.

**And nobody walked a road with anybody for pleasure in either of those two days and nobody will, and the closest two men in that county to being in a room together are about nine feet apart and a barrow is between them, and that is a distance and not a thing anybody has a word for that they would both use.**

## Five

The cost of those two days is a column and it is two days and one night in a barn with about eleven men in it, and it is a horse for the second of them and not for the first.

**a loaf at one penny, a sheet of paper at one penny, a bed at two, a second bed at two, a tallow candle at two, a quart of small beer at two, a second quart of small beer at two, a cart's fare at four, a second cart's fare at four, a third cart's fare at four, a horse's feed at nine, a second horse's feed at nine, a third horse's feed at nine, a wheel iron at eight, a pin at three, a second sheet of paper at one, a third sheet of paper at one and a quill and ink at seven.** One, one, two, two, two, two, two, four, four, four, nine, nine, nine, eight, three, one, one, seven is seventy-one pence. **A mark is forty-eight pence and seventy-one pence is a mark and twenty-three over**, and a room over a cooper that he has had for about three years is a room he is paying for out of what a yard pays nine men with, and the difference is not in that column and is not in any column.

**And a barrow with a wheel in it went up a street four hundred yards long at about the fourth hour of a Friday in Wolfmonth and the man who sent it up the street did not know that the man who walked past the gate at the third hour was the man who has seen every load path in nine counties in about four months, and the man who walked past the gate did not stop, and a bench-board with four heads on it and a ninth line ruled and empty is in a room over a cooper's shop in that town and was not looked at.**
