# Chapter 0421

**The twenty-first day of Rainmonth, YR 318, a Tuesday and not a market day, and the second hour and the fifth hour and the ninth hour, in an office over a shop in a street four hundred yards long in a fen town of about four thousand people, where about nine hundred sheets a month go out into nine counties and about a hundred and four of them go out on a fixed Saturday in a band with four taken out of it, and the two figures are counts of two different series and are not added, and a clerk of about nineteen is the only person in that room who can say what the form wants.**

## One

The office is over a shop and it has a counter and a rail and a stove and about nine feet of floor in front of the counter, and a man who has come off a made road with a load stands in the nine feet because there is nowhere else to stand, and a clerk of about nineteen sits behind that counter with a sheet of his own in front of him and has been sitting there two years.

**The form is the only thing on the counter. It has three lines ruled on it and it has three heads, and the three heads are a place, a figure and a trade, and there is no line on it for a name and there is no line on it for anything else, and the heads were cut about forty years ago in a room two counties off by a man who is not alive.**

The man of about thirty-four with a sheet came in at the second hour and put the sheet on the counter and then put a second thing beside it. The second thing was a slip of paper about two inches by one with a name written on it in a hand nobody in that room had seen, and there was no head on the slip and no letter and no seal, and on the back of it there was a place in the county and a street and nothing else.

“**I want this entered.**”

The clerk of about nineteen read the slip and read it a second time, and the second time was slower.

## Two

“**What is it.**”

“**It is a road.**”

“**Where does it go.**”

“**It goes nowhere the county trades. It is nine miles.**”

The clerk put his finger on the three heads and did not move it for about four seconds, and he did not look up while he did it, and a clerk of about nineteen doing that in a room over a shop is doing the ordinary thing and not a thing.

“**A place, a figure and a trade, and a road is not one of the three,**” he said.

“**Then I will find the three,**” the man of about thirty-four with a sheet said.

“**You will,**” the clerk said, and he turned the form round so that the three heads were the right way up for a man standing in nine feet of floor, and he put a quill on the counter beside it, and **nobody in that room said one word to him about finding them, and there was no line on the form that a word could have gone on, and the clerk was nineteen and the form was older than the building.**

**That is the whole of what happened in that room on that morning, and it is worth having exactly. A man of about thirty-four said a true thing to a room in the nine feet in front of a rail on a Saturday in the previous month, and he said he would not say it a second time to anybody at all, and he did not say it again, and the room did not stop him, and the room was not wrong to let him go on.**

The man of about thirty-four with a sheet picked up the quill and put it down again and said that he would come back with the three and would not want to be asked for them twice, and the clerk of about nineteen said that nobody asks twice, and meant it, and it is true, and it is the whole of what an office in this kingdom is on the subject of.

## Three

**The man of about thirty-three with a barrow was in that room the whole time and was not the subject of one line of it.**

He had come in off the salt road with a load of glass and had been standing at the rail since the second hour with a docket in his hand that nobody had looked at, and he is a man who has come into that office about nine times in about four years and has been fed nothing and asked nothing and has a coat that is not his.

He said the name out loud at about the fourth hour, in the ordinary way, to the clerk, because the clerk asked him which road he had come in by.

“**Which road did you come in by.**”

“**Coldharbour road,**” the man of about thirty-three with a barrow said, “**and then the made one, and it is about nine miles, and there is a bar on the made one and I came up to it.**”

**And the man of about thirty-four with a sheet had a slip of paper two inches by one on the counter in front of him with that name on it, and he had not written it, and he had been given it, and it had come to that office off a cart out of a room with a door on a lane two hundred miles off on the fixed day, and the name on it had come out of a man's mouth in a market about a month before that with about forty people in earshot, and the man of about thirty-three with a barrow did not know what any of it was worth and has not worked it out and is not going to.**

“**You will not want to write that,**” the clerk of about nineteen said, and he said it to the man of about thirty-four and not to the man with the barrow, and it is the only thing a clerk of about nineteen said on that morning that was not a question about a form.

“**I am not writing it,**” the man of about thirty-four with a sheet said. “**I am writing a place and a figure and a trade.**”

**And he was right. That is the finding and it is a finding and not an error, and it took one morning and about four seconds, and nobody in that room has ever been shown anything that would make it a different finding, and about nine hundred sheets a month go out of that room and about a hundred and four of them go out on a fixed Saturday in a band with a cord round it and the cord is because a band is a hundred and eight.**

## Four

He came back to that office three times in the four days after that one. **The first line of the form was written on the twenty-second and the second on the twenty-fourth and the third on the twenty-fifth, on three different days, by three different sets of people, and no two of the three were in the same place, and nobody in that office compared the three with each other because there was nothing on the form that asked anybody to.**

**That is the ordinary way and it is not anybody being careless. A man of about thirty-four was sent out to find a place, and he found one in a day. He was sent out again for a figure, and he found one in two. He was sent out a third time for a trade, and he found one in a day. Three errands, three days of walking, and a form with three lines on it, and the three lines were filled in the order they were on the form and not the order they were found in, because a form is filled in the order it is ruled.**

A man of about thirty-four who is sent out three times comes back three times. Nobody in an office over a shop in a street four hundred yards long is going to say to him on the second day, you have got a place now, and you have a week to decide whether you want the figure to be a figure of the same place, and he would not have known what to do with the question.

**And the whole of the damage was done inside four days by people who were each doing exactly what they were asked, and the place was found by a man who farms it, and the figure by nine people at a stile one of whom is a boy of seventeen who cannot read, and the trade by two men in a yard, and not one of them was asked what a figure of persons is and not one of them is going to be.**

## Five

The cost of that Tuesday is a column and it is the officer's own and he paid it out of a day he does not get paid for, and the clerk of about nineteen was not in it and does not know it exists.

**A horse's feed at nine pence, a bed at two pence, a sheet of paper at one penny, a second sheet at one penny, a tallow candle at two pence, a quart of small beer at two pence, a loaf at one penny, a man of about twenty hired to copy the heads out fair at four pence, a room over a shop at six pence, a fire at three pence, and a second horse's feed at nine pence on the road out: nine, two, one, one, two, two, one, four, six, three, nine is forty pence.** A mark is forty-eight pence and forty pence is under one, and that is the whole of what it costs to be told a thing you already knew by a man of nineteen in a room over a shop.

**And a form with three heads and a quill on the counter beside it and a slip of paper two inches by one with a name on it that is the name of a road, and about nine hundred sheets a month out of that room in nine counties, and a road is not a place, has nobody in it, and cannot be put on a form at all, and the first thing anybody did about it was a clerk of about nineteen turning the form round so that a man standing in nine feet of floor could read the three heads, and he was right, and it was the ordinary thing, and it is the whole of the first day.**
