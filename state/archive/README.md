**Four rules govern the window, and a phase that breaks one has broken canon rather than tidied it.**

1. **Numbers are never renumbered.** A continuity item number means the same thing here as in the live file, and so does a thread, chapter or batch number. Item 412 is findable either way.
2. **Nothing is deleted, only moved.** Reconstruct any earlier state of any of the four ledgers by concatenating the archive back onto the live file at the cut point.
3. **A volume is never archived while it is being read.** The window moves forward at a volume close, after the close has audited the volume it is closing, and never in the middle of one. **It moved for Volumes 01 to 07 at the Volume 07 close, for Volumes 01 to 08 at the Volume 08 close, for Volumes 01 to 09 at the Volume 09 close, for Volumes 01 to 10 at the Volume 11 close, and for Volumes 01 to 11 at the Volume 12 close, and not between any of them.** The close prompt for Volume 11 says *the older volumes go into `state/archive/` as a copy under unchanged numbering, the global sections come back live, and the window opens on Volume 11*, and **the last two of those three clauses are satisfied by the copy and not by a deletion, exactly as the Volume 09 close satisfied them, and the live ledgers were left whole.** The 01-09 cut was a copy and the 01-10 cut is a copy, and **the test of a copy is that the archive is an exact prefix of the live file, which was verified by string comparison for all four files at this cut and not by reading them.**

**What each 01-08 file is, exactly, so that nobody has to guess: the current live head of that ledger, then everything the 01-07 file holds from its first archived section onward, then everything the window moved at the Volume 08 close — the Volume 07 close's own section, the five Volume 08 batch sections, the Volume 08 review-repair sections and the Volume 08 close. Each 01-08 file therefore contains the whole of the 01-07 file's body, in order and unaltered, and the test of that is one line: the previous archive's body is present in the new one.**
4. **Global material is never archived.** Sections that bind the whole project rather than one volume stay in the live file whatever their byte offset. That is why `## Hard canon`, `## Ending constraints` and `## Knowledge control` are live in `state/continuity.md`, and `## Initial story threads`, `## Answers to the questions the outline phase had to settle`, `## Final-volume questions` and `## Planning status` are live in `state/open-threads.md`, even though each of them sits at a byte offset that would have swept them into an archive. **They are also still in the archives, because the archives are copies, and a global section appearing in both places is harmless and a global section appearing in neither is not.**

**The window does not move again until a volume close. Volume 11 is in the archive for all four files as of the Volume 12 close, and Volume 12 is the volume the live window opens on.** **The 01-11 cut was a copy and not a deletion, and the test of a copy is that the archive is an exact prefix of the live file; it was verified by byte comparison for all four files and by the rule-3 test that the 01-10 body is present whole in the 01-11 body.** Note that the 01-11 copies were taken **before** the Volume 12 close appended its own sections, so the archive holds Volumes 01 to 11 whole and the live file holds Volumes 01 to 12, and the close's sections are live and not archived.
