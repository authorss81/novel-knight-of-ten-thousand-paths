## How to read this file

**This file is a rolling window. It holds the whole of the volume currently being written, one section per batch, and nothing older, and everything older is in `state/archive/chapter-summaries-volumes-01-08.md`, verbatim, with every chapter section exactly as it was here.** The window moved for Volumes 01 to 07 at the Volume 07 close and for Volumes 01 to 08 at the Volume 08 close, **and after that cut this file holds the guide and nothing else.** As of the review of Batch 0004 this file was **1.19 MB across 340 chapters**; it was about 140 KB through Volume 07 and **is now about 4 KB, which is the size a rolling summary is supposed to be.** **Nothing is deleted, only moved, and nothing is renumbered, and a volume is never archived while a close is reading it, so the window moved for Volumes 01 to 07 at the Volume 07 close and not before.**

**Read this guide, then read the section for the volume you are writing, and go back further only when a thread demands it. For the Volume 09 first prompt the useful reading is this guide, `state/current.md`, and `outline/volumes/volume-08-close.md` §§3 and 4, which carry the volume in two tables; the five Volume 08 batch sections and the Volume 08 close section are in `state/archive/chapter-summaries-volumes-01-08.md`.** **The live window is empty and opens on Volume 09; the Volume 01 index and every batch section for Volumes 01 to 08 are in `state/archive/chapter-summaries-volumes-01-07.md` and are reachable by grep, not by reading.** A Volume 08 writer who needs a per-chapter summary of a Volume 07 chapter greps the archive; the summaries are all there under the numbers they always had.

**The same warning as in `state/continuity.md` applies. Do not trust a line-number pointer into a chapter file. Refer to a passage by its chapter and its opening words.**
