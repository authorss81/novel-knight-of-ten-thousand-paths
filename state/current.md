## The finding the review of Batch 0002 made, and what was done about it

**The batch's prose had stopped being prose.** Across Chapters 261 to 270 the narrator formula *about four people in that building have said since that* appeared seventy-four times in twenty-three thousand words, against seventy times in the larger and much more various Batch 0001, and total quoted dialogue had fallen to 170 words in ten chapters, with two chapters carrying none at all. The formula was doing the work of dramatising meaning, which is the failure AGENTS.md names as replacing a scene with a list of states. **The block's own self-audit had recorded this as a finding that "is not a defect." That judgement was wrong and has been reversed.**

**The repair pass kept every event, figure, date, name and printed speech in the block and changed no plan.** It cut the formula from seventy-four to twenty-five and `about four people` from eighty-four to thirty-eight, and it spent the recovered space on things a room actually does: the clerk's thumb wiping a blot into the rule instead of onto a cloth, a boy's hand coming off a quoin, the straighter-edge slipping twice off the back of a trestle, a lime-heap lid going on in the rain, a woman with a pail saying one sentence at a door and not waiting for an answer. Dialogue rose from 170 to 224 words and no chapter is now empty of it. **The block went up by 1,326 words, from 23,184 to 24,510, and the added words are scene.**

**The repair also fixed six malformed section breaks, an intra-chapter statement of the bell's full specification that was written out twice, and the same specification restated across two chapters.** It consolidated the bell to one full statement in Chapter 264 and a reference in Chapter 268.

**Two things the repair found about the state files themselves and that the next phase needs to know.** First, **line-number pointers into chapter files are unreliable.** Continuity item 697 pointed the four lines at `chapter-0266.md:39` and the actual positions were 47, and after the repair they are 45, 47, 49 and 51. Item 702 pointed the two-line statement at `265:24` and `265:26` and the actual positions were 24 and 26 at the time and are 27 and 29 now. **The repair has rewritten the live pointers to name the chapter and the text, and a later phase should do the same rather than chase numbers.** Second, **the paragraph-level duplicate and near-repeat sweeps the project has been running cannot see this class of defect at all.** They are paragraph-scoped, and the defect was two sentences in one chapter. The Batch 0003 prompt now carries a clause-level basis, and it also carries the finding that an eight-word shingle against the prior two hundred and sixty chapters is too loose a basis for this house voice, because the house register is built from stock phrases and a shingle of that length fires on almost every sentence. **A longer shingle, or a clause-level comparison of the specific pair, is the basis to use.**
