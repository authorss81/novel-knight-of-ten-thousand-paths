## What `outline/volume-17.md` established, and what Volume 17 inherits out of it, in one paragraph

An outline on disk for a volume of fifty chapters that has written none of them, running day 2971 to day 3180 in five blocks of forty-two days because seven months on the carried thirty-day rule is two hundred and ten days and the divisor is exact; a volume whose own question is that the thing which came up the made road in the ordinary post is not a threat and not an order and not a third copy of a roll but an offer, and that about four people in nine counties find out inside nine seconds that it is the best offer anybody in this county has ever been made, and that the reason it cannot be answered here is that answering it needs a name and the only man who could be put against nine roads said out loud in a market street that the fourth line is not his to fill; a woman of about sixty-one who keeps the county's one seal, its ribbon and a set of standard weights in a drawer eleven inches deep in the low room at the back of the office at the low end of that market street, and a man of about thirty-eight who runs a weekly wain on the four smoothest miles of made road in nine counties and has said so once to about nine people; nine lines on this county's copy of a paper nobody can seal with a trade on each and no name on any, and the finding that a line with a trade on it can be argued with and a line with a name on it cannot; four copied pages authenticated in the room over the weigh-house in her own trade while the man of thirty-two was four days off on a made road, and the re-anchoring refused, and the sealed community never asked and saying nothing; a sheet saying that people with a warrant and about nine others went up about nine hundred yards of a stair and that the gate at the top of that stair is held, and a second sheet eleven days later saying the upper terraces are out of the hands of the men who held them and the place is still held from below and the central engine is damaged and one man has gone into the lower archive and has not come out, both read out in a room by a carter and neither of them seen in this county; a place still occupied at the volume's last page and a roll still on a board at three hundred and fifty-three days with nine empty lines on all three copies and a fourth line empty on all three; a scar six hundred days old and a bleed two hundred and sixty days old and one ear and one leg and a standing capacity of ten roads that cannot be spent and cannot be given up and cannot be transferred; a name budget of six, two of them spent in Block 0001 and four held; a money ceiling declined on the page with three reasons and the third of them deciding; three promises written out so that a block which breaks one is caught; and a prohibition list of eighteen traps in which the fourth ruled head stays empty, the half leaf stays face down, the ninth of the nine stones is not crossed, the roll is not filled in and not destroyed, the ninth yard does not start, the cloth stays over the bench, the register holds ten questions with no finding under any of them, the day-book stands at eleven entries on all fifty datelines, the three series are three series and the band figure is not printed, the ninth thing is alive, unnamed, unheld and shut, nothing explains how a roadmark works and no character says what one is, and none of it is a cure.
