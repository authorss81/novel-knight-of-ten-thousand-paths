# Batch Summaries

One entry per completed batch, newest last. This is the durable home the quality gate needs: `logs/` is gitignored, so a batch summary written only into a phase log leaves no record for the next writer.

Rules for an entry:

- Write it after the chapters are finished, not before, and do not pad it to a length. Six to ten lines is usually right.
- Say what the batch **changed**, not what it was about: the new situation at the end, the cost paid, the promise made or broken, and the one question the next batch inherits.
- Record any canon the batch invented that later batches must obey, and put the durable part in `state/continuity.md` rather than leaving it only here.
- The one-line `Last batch summary` field in `state/current.md` names the most recent entry in this file.

## How to read this file, and the rolling window it now sits in

**This file is a rolling window. It holds the whole of the volume currently being written, one entry per completed batch, newest last, and nothing older. **The live window begins at *Volume 07 — Close — Chapters 301 to 350*. The entries for Volumes 01 to 07, including all seven volume closes and every review-repair entry, are in the archive under the same headings.**

**A batch summary is the durable record of a batch, and `logs/` is gitignored, so a summary written only into a phase log leaves no record at all for the next writer.** That is why this file exists and why it is not to be trimmed for length. What may be trimmed is the *age* of an entry, and only by moving it to the archive, never by deleting it.

## Volume 07 — Close — Chapters 301 to 350, "The Memory Quarantine"

**The volume is finished, audited whole, and closed. The close record is `outline/volumes/volume-07-close.md` and it is the authority for everything below; this entry is the durable summary and the numbers are re-derived there on the page.**

- **Words: 121,993 in fifty chapters and 1,168,586 in three hundred and fifty**, in batches of 23,954 / 23,809 / 23,273 / 25,128 / 25,829, each file counted on its own. **The close's own instruction printed 23,958, 23,270, 25,867, 122,032 and 1,168,625 and five of those did not reproduce; the corrected figures stand above and the first printings stand beside them in the close record.** 926,261 + 120,332 = 1,046,593 + 121,993 = 1,168,586.
- **The situation at the end: about nine sets of about forty sheets in four counties, none complete, each held by one person and everybody in four counties knowing which person, and that is the one new rule in the volume and nobody made it, and it was said out loud once in sixty-nine words in a room over a byre and there is no form for it in this kingdom.** A month went by in four counties in which nothing at all was entered. The bench was asked nothing on the one Saturday it could have been asked.
- **The cost paid: about four hundred sheets burned in a room behind a shop on the twenty-first of Mudmonth with a lamp standing unlit at one end of a bench nine feet long, and about four hundred people in a valley four hundred miles off who are not told why and are not going to be told.** The word *failed* is at zero across the fifty chapters and a crossing that carries nobody, a building that burned and a place that cannot be reached are none of them a failure.
- **The promise kept and the one not.** Kept: the bleed asked in Block 1 and corrected in Block 3, one corrector refusing, and the breach open through all fifty chapters with the not-askings at nine and no tenth entered. Not kept: the plan's second loss — the illusion a record can be made to hold by filling it in — is broken only structurally, by objects and counties, and **no person on the page loses it**, so the reader is shown a burnt room and told about an argument. And the Stage 4 qualification is not earned and not claimed and the ledger does not move.
- **Ten words were repaired in eight chapters and nothing else on three hundred and fifty pages was touched:** a barred age removed from two chapters, four narrator self-references re-voiced, a speech-length figure corrected in Chapter 340, a wrong figure and a wrong sentence-length in Chapter 349, and a protected bay in Chapter 350. The first printing of every one stands in the close record.
- **A review pass over the close record found seventeen defects in it and corrected all seventeen without touching a chapter, and nine of them were the close misremembering the volume rather than misreading it, and one of the nine was a true sentence it decided was the only true sentence.** A correction that reads like a correction is the one thing nothing in this project's instrument set is built to catch.
- **The question the next phase inherits:** `outline/series.md`'s Volume 8 row asks *which communities first wrote the original Gatehouse consent rule*, and the volume's answer is on the page in the volume's own words and not in a document's — about nine sets of paper, four counties, nine people, and a rule nobody made. **Volume 08's own question is what a room becomes when it is copied nine times and named by nine people who were not in it, and the volume's second question is what a county of about four thousand people does in the second year, when nothing has worked once and the nothing is in a book nobody can be served with.**
