## How to read this file, and why the guide is at the top

**This file is a rolling window. It holds the global canon, the whole of the volume currently being written, and nothing older, and everything older is in `state/archive/continuity-volumes-01-12.md`, verbatim, with every item number exactly as it was here.** The window moved for Volumes 01 to 07 at the Volume 07 close and for Volumes 01 to 08 at the Volume 08 close, **and after that cut this file holds the global canon and nothing else, because Volume 09 has not been written yet.** The reason is arithmetic and it is not a preference. As of the review of Batch 0004 this file was **1.94 MB and about 373,000 words, growing about 1,100 words a chapter, which is roughly a third of the manuscript**, and it was being carried whole into every phase, which is the opposite of what AGENTS.md asks a rolling summary to be. It is now about 170,000 characters, and it stays about that size for as long as the window is maintained.

**Three rules make this safe, and a phase that breaks one of them has broken canon rather than tidied it.**

1. **Items are never renumbered.** 1 to 1000 are fixed and are what they were, and the highest of them, item 1000, is the last item of the Volume 08 close. 646 onward were appended by volume, by batch and by repair pass in the order they were established, and that is unchanged. **An item number means the same thing whether you find it here or in the archive**, and the archive carries the same numbers, so *item 412* is findable either way.
2. **Nothing is deleted, only moved.** The archive is a byte-for-byte copy of what was cut, and the four files under `state/archive/` reconstruct any earlier state of any of the four ledgers exactly.
3. **A volume is never archived while it is being read.** The window moves forward at a volume close, after the close has audited the volume, and not before.

**Read this guide, then read the section for the volume you are actually working on, and do not read the whole thing. For the Volume 09 first prompt the useful reading is: this guide, `state/current.md`, `outline/volumes/volume-08-close.md` §§5, 6, 7, 9 and 10, the seventeen threads under *Threads the Volume 08 close hands to Volume 09* in `state/archive/continuity-volumes-01-12.md`, and items 984 to 1000 in `state/archive/continuity-volumes-01-12.md`. ## Hard canon

- World: Neyra, a late-medieval river-and-road world. Years count from the **Quiet Reckoning**; the story opens in **YR 312, Mudmonth**.
- Protagonist: Sir Aren Kest, thirty-two, a former Lantern Guard captain and current bridge repairer in Merefen. Estranged from Tovan; still idealizes Edric.
- Starting mystery: the Bellweather Sundering, **YR 301, Mudmonth**, erased Bellweather and seven villages. The story opens on the eleventh anniversary, in the same season. The Crown blames Aren’s failed bridge defense.
- The seven villages: Alder Cross, Saltbrim, Wrenlow, Cobbett, Nine Elms, Stonebarrow, Hushwater.
- Deeper answer: Marshal Edric Hunn and the Lantern Council ordered a forced cull to contain the Unwritten Wound; the order sacrificed a living city.
- Gatehouse rule: a living path cannot be reset or forcibly closed while conscious life exists inside it. Closing a route removes Aren’s access, not the world.
- **Local custody rule (added in bootstrap):** roads are opened by named local custodians, not by Aren. Two consenting custodians with paired anchors can open a road without him, and path communities can open roads to each other without the main world. Aren is a translator and temporary common anchor, never a necessary mechanism. The ending must show roads working without him.
- Path capacity: one at Stage 0/First Stitch, then two, three, five, seven, and ten at later stages. The final Unowned Road stage removes exclusive personal access.
- Path costs: roadmarks, memory loss, path bleed, lost time, failed workings, social obligations, and permanent local consequences. No path is a disposable map.
- Crossing budget: about three attempted crossings per season at any stage; **three days of fever after a return, at Stage 0 and at Stage 1 alike** — the first crossing in Volume 01 pays the same three days — growing to a month at Stage 6. A return stitch is consumed by use.
- Final enemy: Edric Hunn, using the established First Witness protocol to activate the One Road. No new final enemy is planned.

## Ending constraints

The ending must preserve Bellweather-Living as a living sovereign community, contain but not magically cure the Unwritten Wound, ratify the Charter of Many Roads, keep Aren and Mara’s relationship voluntary and independent, and leave Edric alive for public judgment.

## The window, at the Volume 13 close

**The window moved forward for Volumes 01 to 12 at the Volume 13 close, and it is the first move in this project that TRIMS the live file as well as copying it.** Every one of the five earlier moves, at the Volume 07, 08, 09, 11 and 12 closes, took an exact copy into `state/archive/` and then left the live file whole, and the live file grew back from the copy point every time. **The consequence of that convention, measured, is this: at the moment this cut was taken the five ledgers stood at 3.06 MB together, `state/continuity.md` at 984 KB holding every volume from Volume 09 forward, `state/open-threads.md` at 764 KB, `state/chapter-summaries.md` at 560 KB, `state/batch-summaries.md` at 468 KB, and `state/current.md` at 246 KB holding ten overlapping "What is true at the close of Chapter N" blocks for Chapter 560 through Chapter 650. That is roughly 780,000 tokens of state for a project whose live instruction is about 25,000 words, and Volume 14 cannot load it. The review of Volume 13 Batch 0005 recorded the arithmetic and called it a blocker, and it is one, and the fix is this cut.**

**Nothing is lost and nothing is renumbered.** `state/archive/continuity-volumes-01-12.md` is a byte-for-byte copy of this file as it stood immediately before this cut, so the entire pre-cut ledger, guides and all, is recoverable by concatenating the archive back onto the live file at the cut point, and item 412 and thread 412 and chapter 412 and batch 412 all mean exactly what they meant. **The cut moved whole sections only, at section boundaries, and it did not touch a single item number, thread number, chapter number or batch number.** What left the live file is: **the Volume 09, 10, 11 and 12 batch sections, every review-repair section, and both of those volumes' closes, which is `## The window, at the Volume 08 close` and every section from *Volume 09, Batch 0001* to *Volume 12 close* inclusive.**
