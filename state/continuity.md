# Continuity State

## How to read this file, and why the guide is at the top

**This file is a rolling window. It holds the global canon, the whole of the volume currently being written, and nothing older, and everything older is in `state/archive/continuity-volumes-01-06.md`, verbatim, with every item number exactly as it was here.** The reason is arithmetic and it is not a preference. As of the review of Batch 0004 this file was **1.94 MB and about 373,000 words, growing about 1,100 words a chapter, which is roughly a third of the manuscript**, and it was being carried whole into every phase, which is the opposite of what AGENTS.md asks a rolling summary to be. It is now about 170,000 characters, and it stays about that size for as long as the window is maintained.

**Three rules make this safe, and a phase that breaks one of them has broken canon rather than tidied it.**

1. **Items are never renumbered.** 1 to 869 are fixed and are what they were. 646 onward were appended by volume, by batch and by repair pass in the order they were established, and that is unchanged. **An item number means the same thing whether you find it here or in the archive**, and the archive carries the same numbers, so *item 412* is findable either way.
2. **Nothing is deleted, only moved.** The archive is a byte-for-byte copy of what was cut, and the four files under `state/archive/` reconstruct any earlier state of any of the four ledgers exactly.
3. **A volume is never archived while it is being read.** The window moves forward at a volume close, after the close has audited the volume, and not before.

**Read this guide, then read the sections for the batch you are actually working on, and do not read the whole thing. For Volume 07 Batch 0005 the useful reading is: this guide, `state/current.md`, items 860 to 869 in the section at the tail, and the fourteen threads under *Threads Volume 07 Batch 0004 carries into Batch 0005* in `state/open-threads.md`. Everything earlier in this file is Volume 07 and is the volume a close will audit. Everything before it is in the archive and is reachable by grep, not by reading.** **The highest item at any time is the newest, and the number is in `state/current.md`.**

**The archive index, so a number can be found without grepping a megabyte.** `state/archive/continuity-volumes-01-06.md` holds, in order and unaltered: the Volume 01 to Volume 05 batch sections, the Volume 01 to Volume 05 close sections, the six Volume 06 sections, the Volume 06 close, and the five Volume 06 and Volume 07 review-repair sections. **Items 1 to 645 are Volume 01 to Volume 05 and are fixed. Items 646 to 859 are Volume 06 and the Volume 07 Batch 0001 to 0003 sections, and item 860 opens the Batch 0004 block frame, and the live window begins at item 860**, which is the Volume 07 Batch 0004 block frame. **The live window begins at the section headed *Canon established by Volume 07 Batch 0001, Chapters 301 to 310*, and that heading and everything after it is live and is what a Batch 0005 writer and the Volume 07 close will read.**

**Items that are marked superseded in part are the point of them, and the markers are the point.** Item 746 is a block device ledger whose seven figures did not reproduce on their own stated basis, and item 748 reported a sweep of a block clean when three of the paragraphs it claimed to have repaired were still on the page. A writer who needs a Volume 06 Block 4 figure takes it from item 757, and a writer who needs to know what the block believed takes it from 746 and 748 and knows that nine figures in them were wrong.

**One warning that cost a real error. Do not trust a line-number pointer into a chapter file in this ledger.** Every repair pass inserts and removes lines, so a pointer written by one phase is stale by the next one. ## Hard canon

- World: Neyra, a late-medieval river-and-road world. Years count from the **Quiet Reckoning**; the story opens in **YR 312, Mudmonth**.
- Protagonist: Sir Aren Kest, thirty-two, a former Lantern Guard captain and current bridge repairer in Merefen. Estranged from Tovan; still idealizes Edric.
- Starting mystery: the Bellweather Sundering, **YR 301, Mudmonth**, erased Bellweather and seven villages. The story opens on the eleventh anniversary, in the same season. The Crown blames Aren’s failed bridge defense.
- The seven villages: Alder Cross, Saltbrim, Wrenlow, Cobbett, Nine Elms, Stonebarrow, Hushwater.
- Deeper answer: Marshal Edric Hunn and the Lantern Council ordered a forced cull to contain the Unwritten Wound; the order sacrificed a living city.
- Gatehouse rule: a living path cannot be reset or forcibly closed while conscious life exists inside it. Closing a route removes Aren’s access, not the world.
- **Local custody rule (added in bootstrap):** roads are opened by named local custodians, not by Aren. Two consenting custodians with paired anchors can open a road without him, and path communities can open roads to each other without the main world. Aren is a translator and temporary common anchor, never a necessary mechanism. The ending must show roads working without him.
- Path capacity: one at Stage 0/First Stitch, then two, three, five, seven, and ten at later stages. The final Unowned Road stage removes exclusive personal access.
- Path costs: roadmarks, memory loss, path bleed, lost time, failed workings, social obligations, and permanent local consequences. No path is a disposable map.
- Crossing budget: about three attempted crossings per season at any stage; **three days of fever after a return, at Stage 0 and at Stage 1 alike** — the first crossing in Volume 01 pays the same three days — growing to a month at Stage 6. A return stitch is consumed by use.
- Final enemy: Edric Hunn, using the established First Witness protocol to activate the One Road. No new final enemy is planned.

## Ending constraints

The ending must preserve Bellweather-Living as a living sovereign community, contain but not magically cure the Unwritten Wound, ratify the Charter of Many Roads, keep Aren and Mara’s relationship voluntary and independent, and leave Edric alive for public judgment.

In Chapter 2 Aren cannot raise six marks and signs a guild bond for the sum with his tool-chest as surety.
