## 1. Volume identity, and the question this volume is built on

**`outline/series.md`'s Volume 16 central pressure is a mass cull to prevent the Wound from spreading through the network of paths Aren has built.** The close of Volume 15 handed on three things in its own terms: a leaf with one thumb on it saying a place is shutting its own end before Hearthmonth, a Gatehouse that is mapped rather than hidden, and a request from an office four hundred miles off for the name of a person and a copy of a drawing that this county cannot answer.

**What Volume 15 asked and did not answer:** *who is going to hold nine hundred yards of wall.* **What Volume 16 asks and has to earn:** ***that a road which is to be shut for want of a name is not shut by a gate or a bar or a hurdle, it is shut by a roll, and that the roll arrives in the ordinary post, and that the question a county spent a year on becomes a question with a date on it in ninety days.***

**And the volume's real antagonist is not a person and is not the Wound.** It is the second thing a piece of paper can do to a road, which is the first thing it did. **Volume 15's engine was a fact four people heard and nobody was told. Volume 16's engine is that four and a half years of not being asked has produced a set of habits that are correct as answers and fatal as practice, and the habits belong to the people and not to anybody else.** The man who keeps the weigh-house floor has never written his figure down because nobody asked him what a sheet weighs. The roadkeeper of about fifty-two has never been paid for nine hundred feet of kerb. The man of forty-one has said ten words in eleven years. **All three of those are Volume 15's material and all three of them become the volume's costs, and none of them is anybody's fault, and the volume says so more than once.**

**What the volume is not.** It is not a volume in which a road is saved by a man walking up it. **It is not a volume in which the mass cull is defeated.** It is not a volume in which the Gatehouse is entered: it is taken, in Block 0005, by people with a Crown's warrant and about nine hundred yards of a stair, and nobody in this county sees it happen. It is not a volume in which a path community is rescued, exhibited, or explained, **and it is not a volume in which the closed place is found to be alive, which is Block 0003's finding and not this block's.**

## 7. The mechanics this volume runs on

- **The practice.** At the fourth hour before light, on the five mornings a week the room over the weigh-house is shut, a number is said out loud on the weigh-house floor by one person and then said again by another, and the same number is said on the next morning by the same two people. **A thing is held when two people have said out loud in the same hour that they are holding it, and both of them are still holding it on the next morning they said they would be.** Nothing about it is written down anywhere in Blocks 0001 to 0003, and that is the block's first failure and not an oversight.
- **A figure four short.** The floor counts loads as well as sheets. **A cart of lime has come up from Wethergill on every second Thursday for about nine years, about two hundred and thirty times, and one of them not arriving is a figure of one out of two hundred and thirty, and the man who keeps the floor has never once been one short in about four and a half years.**
- **A road with no name against it.** The notice. It is the volume's antagonist and it is paper.
- **Two documents asking for one name, ninety days apart, from one office, in two forms.** The request of the fifteenth of Frostmonth asked for the name of the person who holds that wall. The notice asks for the name of the keeper of every road. **This county has now been asked twice and has answered neither time.**
- **A column.** One footed money column per chapter, ten items, priced 1 to 41 pence, a mark at forty-eight pence in every conversion, and the coda that names the figures in the chapter that are not in it.
