## 1. Volume identity, and the question this volume is built on

**`outline/volume-05.md` §11 and `outline/series.md`'s Volume 6 row give two questions and they look like two questions and they are one room. This outline says so rather than choosing between them, because choosing between them would have produced a volume about classification with a coda about a hearing, and the volume this repository needs is a volume in which the two are the same object.**

`outline/volume-05.md` §11: **what happens to a refusal when somebody convenes a room to hear about it, and the room is the same shape as the thing that made the refusal?** `outline/series.md`'s Volume 6 central pressure: **the Crown convenes a public inquiry into Bellweather while the Culling Order attempts to classify every path as an emergency hazard.** `outline/series.md`'s Volume 5 next-volume cell, which is where the first of those came from: **can a path be alive enough to reject him, and what happens when the main world refuses to recognize that refusal?**

**Put the three together and every one of them is a room shaped like a form.** The Crown convenes a public inquiry — a room with a witness list and a form of oath. The Culling Order attempts to classify every path as an emergency hazard — a form with a column headed *Object* and a second column with no head. And what is being inquired into is **a body corporate in a fen county that has spent four years and a half refusing to be a body corporate**, whose only offence is that it has a door, a number, the capacity to sue and to be sued, and **a register in which five entries are questions and not one is a finding.** The office that made the offer has begun a public proceeding about a crossing it has never been to. And a city's refusal exists in the main world only as a figure of persons in a return against a district the office made up out of a sheet.

**So: the volume is a room, the room is convened about a refusal, and the refusal is in a column that has no head on it. The answer the volume is built on is the sentence a man of thirty-three with lime said in a glass yard in the last volume in a different register: a join with a person in it is a column, and a column can be filled in without asking anybody. Volume 06's sentence is the same sentence with a roof on it, and the roof is the part that does the damage, because the roof is what makes people go home believing they were heard.**

**What Volume 05 asked and answered:** *a place can be a person, and a person can be asked a second time and a column cannot.* **What Volume 06 asks and has to earn:** ***a room can hear a thing once. A person can be asked twice. And a refusal that has to be entered in a room in order to be refused has stopped being a refusal and become a finding, and a finding is the only kind of paper in this kingdom that can be sued for, entered against anybody, and produced in a court four hundred miles off — and the difference between a question and a finding is nine months and a deadline, and the deadline is not the Crown's, it is the form's, and nobody in the room knows which form they are in.***

**And the second half of the answer, which is the half that costs:** ***a cut made on a forged record is a cut made on somebody's lie, and the volume's argument is not the cut. It is what the lie costs the person the lie was about.***

## 2. Central pressure
