## 1. Volume identity, and the question this volume is built on

**`outline/series.md`'s Volume 12 central pressure is a path that offers a good life, and the last volume handed on a finding that a road may not be described without being found out about. The two are the same argument seen from opposite ends.**

The series says: the Quiet Orchard offers material prosperity, durable peace, and a warning that peace bought by enforced agreement is not freedom. The close of Volume 11 handed on **a man a town can find, a page he kept at the cost of a road he did not walk, a finding in a room's book under a head that is not its own, and a rule that an order may not act on a road without that road's own answer to a question put to it in its own words.**

Put them together and the volume is this: **a road that has never answered a question, being offered to a man as the proof that his kind of work has succeeded.** A prosperous valley where nobody has argued for twenty-two years, where the fruit keeps, where the children are safe, and where every single thing anybody does there has been done the same way for twenty-two years by people who cannot tell you why. **The man is asked to put his name to it. And the thing he can see, and the only thing he can see, is that it is held rather than left alone, and a held thing is beautiful from the outside and it is owned, and he has spent eleven years learning the second half of that sentence.**

**What Volume 11 asked and answered:** *a road which answers a question about itself has told somebody where it is.* **What Volume 12 asks and has to earn:** ***that the road which cannot answer is not thereby innocent, and that the person who cannot make it answer is the person who wants it certified, and that a certificate is a piece of paper with one man's name in a line on it, which is the exact object Volume 11 spent five blocks refusing to produce.***

**And the fourth head is the argument in one ruled line, and it turns over.** The Crown's form's fourth head has been empty for five months and nobody in nine counties has filled it, and a head that is empty is a question a form has learned to ask by itself. **A valley with nothing written on it at all is the same danger at the other end of the scale: a place with no line in it cannot be refused, cannot be produced, and cannot be argued with, and a mark at the top of a leaf with nothing under it is a document that says yes to anything a reader brings to it.**

**What the volume is not.** It is not a villain's valley and nobody in it is secretly wicked. It is not a utopia and nothing in it is free. It is not a volume in which a man is right on arrival; **he is wrong in Block 1 about the only thing he is sure of, and the woman he is wrong about is the one he has the least right to be wrong about.** It is not a courtroom and there is no bench in it. And **it is not a volume in which the protagonist solves it: the finding he carries home is that a road's own answer is the only evidence that can be shown, and that he is the man who most wants to be shown one.**

## 2. Central pressure

**Two parties want two different things, the two people in the middle are both right about a different part of it, and the man in the middle wants a third thing that nobody has offered.**

- **A man of about fifty-three who brings eleven people and a letter** wants **a road certified in public as the one road that is stable.** He has not lied once in this volume. **He is right about one thing and it is the most important thing in the volume: a road that can be found out about can be closed, and the only answer to that is a room where somebody is answerable, and a certificate is how a room gets an answerable person.** He is wrong about the size of a room and wrong about what a certificate is for. **He does not say whose plan it is, he is not a knave, and the eleven people he brought genuinely want that life and say so.**
- **A woman of about thirty-eight who keeps a block of ground in the Long Set** wants **to be able to say that a thing she did was wrong without being the one who has to move.** She has not been able to for four years. **Not because she is silent: because when she opens her mouth to say what she thinks, the sentence comes out in the oldest voice in the valley, and she can hear it, and it is not hers.** That is the volume in one physiological fact and it is not magic and it is not a curse and it is what happens to people in a place where one agreed voice was taught at their tables.
- **A woman of about forty-five from a river city** is **the only one of the eleven who can say what the word *road* means**, and she came to look at a place that had been left alone, and she says once, out loud, in a market town, a word nine people in that room have not heard in five months, and nobody there asks her what it means and the man with the badge says out loud that he is not going to ask.
- **A man of about fifty-eight with a chain of office that is a badge** wants **a question put to a road in the road's own words and the answer on a leaf he can hand to anybody.** He cannot certify anything and does not pretend to. **He says out loud on the first day of every month that the hearing stands, and it stands, and there is no bench in the room, and no chapter of this volume builds one.**
- **Aren Kest** wants **to be the man who finds the load-bearing problem in a place where nobody has permitted one to be found.** He can see it in about nine minutes. **And the thing he can see is that a valley's peace and a valley's unreadable record are the same object, and the only professional in nine counties who can act on what he can see is him, and being the only person who can see a thing is not a warrant to be the person who does it.**

**The volume question, in its operational form:** *a place where nothing has been decided for twenty-two years offers to prove that a man can keep roads open, and the proof requires him to sign — what is the smallest thing he can do that is not a certificate, and who has to be asked first?*

**The answer the volume gives is a question, carried by a person who was not sent, to a place that has not had an answer since before the man asking was born.**
