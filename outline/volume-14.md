## 1. Volume identity, and the question this volume is built on

**`outline/series.md`'s Volume 14 central pressure is legal, not military. Emergency law is invoked in the main world and forces are sent to take every anchor connected to the ferry city. The close of Volume 13 handed on a rule in nine hands with no copy, a hearing with no bench in it, and a badge-man's standing warning that in about four months somebody would want something out of the arrangement and there would be nobody to ask.**

Put them together and the volume is this: **the three clauses are about shutting roads, and nobody is shutting a road.** The instrument that arrives in Block 0001 is signed, sealed, printed, distributed, and obeyed, and it does one thing: it takes. It takes the works and the anchors and it asks for the persons, the horses and the goods of the district to give such aid to a marshal's officers as those officers shall require. Not one of the three clauses is touched by it. The notice is not required, because nothing is shut. The witness is not required, because no road is shut. The burden is not required, because nobody has said any road may be shut.

**What Volume 13 asked and answered:** *a road is kept open by being named, and naming it requires somebody local to stand up and be wrong in public.* **What Volume 14 asks and has to earn:** ***that a rule about shutting roads cannot be used against a man who never shuts one, that the whole apparatus nine counties built is a rule about one gesture, and that the second gesture is taking, and nobody in nine counties has a clause about taking because in nine counties nobody has ever taken a road.***

**And the volume's real antagonist is not the column. It is the shape of the thing the column is made of.** A sheet with a name at the top of it was obeyed in Volume 13 for nine days because a room could not show it false. In this volume a sheet with a name at the top of it is obeyed **and it is not false**, and the difference between those two facts is the entire distance the badge-man has to cross in front of about nine people.

**What the volume is not.** It is not a volume in which the rule is amended in a room by a vote. It is not a courtroom. **It is not a volume in which the protagonist is right on arrival**: he is right in Block 0001 about the hole in the rule, and he is wrong in Block 0003 about the only thing anybody can do with what he has found out, and the person he is wrong about is the one who has come the whole way from a city that was never severed. It is not a volume in which the paths fight, and **no path community marches anywhere in this volume.**

## 2. Central pressure

**Three parties want three different things and the two people in the middle are each right about a different part of it.**

- **A captain of about forty-four with a warrant** wants **a road to take and does not care which one**, and he is a competent professional doing what a Crown officer does with a paper in his hand, and he has a genuine difficulty, which is that a warrant which names a place can be refused and a warrant which says *wherever the works are* cannot be argued with at a gate. **He is not a knave and he is not stupid and nobody in this volume calls him either.**
- **A woman of about thirty-eight off the coast of returning roads** wants **to know whether she has been sent here to be an army**, and she has come the whole way to ask it, and the reason she came is given out loud, and the answer she is given is not the answer to the question she asked.
- **A man of about fifty-eight with a badge and no head** wants **the arrangement to survive being wanted.** He has said in a street that there is no head with which to call the next meeting and that he is not going to make one, and this volume is the four months he named.
- **Aren Kest** wants **to be the man who finds the load-bearing problem in a place where nobody has permitted one to be found**, and this is the fourth volume running in which that is what he is wanted for, and in this volume somebody finally asks him a different question and he gives the wrong answer to it in Block 0003 in front of about nine people.

**The volume question, in its operational form:** *the rule covers shutting and this man is taking — what is the smallest thing that is not a petition, and who has to be asked before anybody does anything at all?*

**The answer the volume gives is a coalition, and the coalition is offered a Crown charter, and the coalition says no, and the reason it says no is given by the person the protagonist has known longest.**

## 7. The mechanics this volume runs on

- **A rule about one gesture.** The three clauses govern closing. The instrument takes. **The whole of Blocks 0001 and 0002 is the discovery that this is true, and it is discovered out loud in a market street by a man with a badge in about four minutes, and nobody thanks him.**
- **A warrant that says *wherever the works are*.** The instrument names no road in nine counties. **The chapter on which that is said aloud is the chapter in which the volume's engine is handed to the next block.**
- **A delegation that has to go home along the road the column is on.** The cost block. **The badge-man says out loud that nobody in nine counties can protect a person on a road that is being taken, and that he will not send anybody, and nobody argues with him.**
- **A three-week contact**, the longest in the volume, spent on one paragraph from the ferry city. **A path dateline carries an open-crossing count and an hour and carries no month and no weekday.**
- **A hearing with no bench in it**, said out loud on the first of every month. **It has no power to declare anything and about nine people in nine counties know that, and this is the volume in which somebody needs it to.**
- **The copies in the county are abridged and the captain's copy is not.** The sheet on the boards is a quarter of the instrument and about nine pages long it is not, and the district clerk said nine pages because he had been told nine pages. **This is established on the page in Chapter 0655 and paid in Chapter 0660 and it is the volume's first hard fact about the emergency office, and it is not explained and no writer after the one who writes Chapter 0660 may trace it.**
- **A column.** One footed money column per chapter, 10 items, priced 1 to 41 pence, a mark at forty-eight pence in every conversion, and the coda that names the figures in the chapter that are not in it.

## 8. Escalation sequence — five blocks, Chapters 651–700

**The five-block shape is the same one Volumes 09 to 13 used — gift, test, cost, climax, consequence — and the content under each head is nothing like the last volume's. This is the first action volume since Volume 08 and the prose must move like one: bodies in weather, hands on things, distance, cold, and men who are tired.**

### Block 1 · Chapters 651–660: The Instrument That Shuts Nothing

**Days 2041 to 2094, fifty-four days inclusive, and no day of it falls between two chapters.** A Crown instrument arrives in a market street, printed, sealed, and signed, and it takes rather than shuts. **The badge-man reads it aloud and then reads the three clauses back by two people who each hold one of them and are willing to say so, and finds out loud that not one of the three is touched.** A delegation is in the town from the coast, having come eleven days on a cart, and asks whether it has been sent to be an army, and nobody can answer, because nobody has asked them anything and the Crown has not asked them anything either. **The captain reaches the town's own gate on the last day of the block and asks the badge-man for the name of the road the instrument refers to, and is told that nine counties have no head with which to give him one, and says he does not need one, because his paper says *wherever the works are*.** The block's gift: **a woman of about thirty-eight says out loud that she has been sent to ask one question and that the answer she has been given does not answer it.** The block's cost: **the delegation has to go back along the road the column is on, and no clause in nine counties covers that, and the badge-man will not send anybody.**

### Block 2 · Chapters 661–670: Where the Delegation Goes

**Days 2095 to 2148.** The four days of road, in both directions, with the column on it. **The man of about thirty-six who keeps a road in the county where a list of names was burned comes back into the volume**, and what she has to say is not a complaint and is not an argument and is a third thing. **A Crown clerk of about fifty-six who drafts instruments is named and is a professional doing the least bad thing available to her, and her want is unrelated to Aren and is that nothing she drafts should ever be obeyed without somebody choosing to obey it.** The cost of the block is **a horse, and a man who is not in the column and is on the road because of the delegation and is not got back.** And the small road, which has been a name on a list since Block 0001 of Volume 13, sends a letter for the first time and the letter is about a horse.

### Block 3 · Chapters 671–680: The United Front, and the Word in It

**Days 2149 to 2202. The midpoint reversal, and it is the series row's reversal: *Aren's plan for a united front requires the paths to accept Crown authority; Sera Quen exposes that this would repeat the Sundering in a new form.*** He writes it out in about nine hundred words and it is the best thing anybody in nine counties has written in a year and it is wrong. **He does not know it is wrong until a ferrywright from a city that was never severed stands up in a room with about nine people in it and says four sentences about a gate.** **THE STANDING DEFECT PAYS DUE HERE** at about the four months, and it pays twice, because Block 0001 took the first half of it: in Chapter 0656, on the sixteenth of Longlight, the badge-man counts out loud in a market street that what he called about four months on the twenty-fifth of Greenmonth was twenty-one days, and says that being right early about a thing you were right about is the same as being wrong about how long you had, and says he is not moving the figure. **Block 0003 gets the second half: at about the four months the thing arrives that he was early about, in a form nobody in nine counties can refuse at a gate, and the last unanswered question of Block 0001 is whether being early was a kindness.** And the cost of the block is **that Aren Kest has been wrong in front of the one person who came the whole way, in a room, and she does not soften it for him and he does not ask her to.**

### Block 4 · Chapters 681–690: The Coin That Was Not Offered

**Days 2203 to 2256.** The coalition that exists without a charter. **Two roads send writing and two roads send nothing, and the two that send nothing are the two the column is standing on.** The register gains its tenth question and still no finding. **The bar of wood at the Low Gate is not lifted and nobody is scheduled to lift it, and one road in nine counties says out loud that it will refuse to name a witness and that the man who said the road might be shut has not shown that it may be shut, and this volume is the four months in which that sentence is used for something and is not softened.** The recovery beat is here and it is long, and it is the road crew work at the gate, and the fourth course is not laid.

### Block 5 · Chapters 691–700: The Crossings Held

**Days 2257 to 2310. The climax and the resolution.** The coalition holds the river crossings for nine days and the column does not take the ferry city and does not take the works, **because nobody in nine counties will name a road to him.** **Aren Kest lets a bridge go, on his own authority, in about nine minutes, to stop a forced cull, and Tovan Kest stands in a street four days later and refuses to blame the path communities for it in public and refuses to let anybody blame Aren Kest either.** The Gatehouse is exposed. **Aren reaches the practical limit of the Witness Crown: he cannot be near the works any more without the road answering for him, and the last page is him on the made road four days off with a stick, hearing a bell answer across water that he did not ring.** The next-volume question: **what did the First Quiet do to the Gatehouse's original consent, and why is the road his mother came through still sealed?**

## 9. Volume climax

**Nine days of held crossings, and a bridge let go, and a Gatehouse that cannot be hidden again.**

- **Aren Kest spends the volume being the man who sees the load-bearing problem and then, for the first time in four volumes, acting on it in a way that costs somebody who is not a criminal.**

## 10. Concrete resolution

1. **The march fails and the Gatehouse does not.** The main world and the paths survive Volume 14. **The works are exposed, which is worse for everybody than the works being hidden, and no chapter says it is better.**
2. **The Law of Unopened Doors holds, and its known hole is now a fact in general use rather than a defect in a room**, and it is named in a street by a person who is not the protagonist, and nobody calls it small.
3. **Aren Kest loses safe distance from the Gatehouse**, which is the volume's power cost and is permanent and is not cured in Volume 15.
4. **The paths are not an army and say so, and the volume's political consequence is that a coalition of people who will not be soldiers is very hard to defeat and very hard to supply.**
5. **Tovan Kest and Aren Kest work together and keep the argument**, and the kin-term returns in this volume under a cap (see §16 item 5).
