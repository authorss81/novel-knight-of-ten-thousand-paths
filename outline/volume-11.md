## 1. Volume identity, and the question this volume is built on

**`outline/series.md`'s Volume 11 central pressure is an inquiry, and the last volume handed on a piece of paper, and the two are the same object seen from the other end.**

The series says: a public inquiry into the Gatehouse's oldest protocol threatens to expose every path community's location and history. The close of Volume 10 handed on **a form in a market town four days up a made road, with three heads filled and a fourth head empty, and nobody in nine counties has filled it, and a coast that put a word in a fourth head on a different document and said so on the page, and a woman of about thirty-four who told the man of about thirty-four with a sheet that if somebody gives a name to say then it is a form with a name on it and not the form on the sheet.**

Put them together and the volume is this: **a form that finds out, and the finding is the ordinary part.** A return with four heads goes out to nine counties. It asks for a place, a figure of people, and a trade. **Every road in nine counties can fill those three, and about nine hundred thousand ordinary lives will fill them without anybody meaning any harm, and the inquiry will then have a book in which the ordinary roads and the four that are not roads are in the same hand, in the same column, under the same three heads, and the only difference between them is which county clerk happened to be in the post.**

**What Volume 10 asked and answered:** *a road that works and cannot be found about will be taken by the first office that puts a chain on it.* **What Volume 11 asks and has to earn:** ***that the danger is not the list and is not the leak, and is not even the office, and is that a road which answers a question about itself has told somebody where it is, and cannot un-tell them, and did not know it was being asked.***

**And the fourth head is the whole of the argument in one ruled line.** It is empty in the Crown's form in the market town four days up. It is a word on the coast's own sheet, cut into a different piece of paper, in a different county, in a different month, and that word is the coast's name for the road. **Block 1 establishes that a fourth head is in every form in nine counties and that nobody in nine counties can say who cut it, and that the reason it is dangerous is not that it is empty and that it is dangerous because an empty line does not go away: it goes out with the sheet, and it comes back, and every return that comes back has it on it, and eventually somebody fills it in out of nothing but the fact that a line is there to be filled in.**

**What the volume is not.** It is not a courtroom and there is no judge on a bench and nobody is sworn and nobody is sentenced. It is not a volume about a villain in a chamber; the antagonist is not on a page in this volume in his own person, **and the inquiry is not his, and no chapter says whose inquiry it is.** It is not a volume about a battle and it has no fight in it. **And it is not a volume in which the protagonist is the person who solves it: he is in four of Block 1's ten chapters, he is a person the room can now call, and being called is what it costs him.**

## 2. Central pressure

**Two parties want two different things, the two people in the middle are both right about a different part of it, and the man in the middle wants a third thing that neither side has offered and is the reason it does not happen.**

- **A man of about fifty-one in a grey coat who is arranging an inquiry** wants **the returns in one room, read by people who have signed something.** He is not the marshal, he does not say whose plan it is, and he is not a villain. **He is right about one thing and it is the most important thing in the volume: an uncontrolled return goes to anybody who asks in a room with a door on a lane, and there are about nine hundred sheets a month coming out of one such room in this county alone, and the people asking are not the people being written about.**
- **A woman of twenty-nine who keeps a room over a weigh-house with a door on a lane, two hundred miles off the coast, out of a deed of thirteen words, at fourpence a year** wants **that no copy of a road be in a room she has to answer for**, which is the exact inverse of what a return in one room produces. **She is the only character in this volume who is asked to do the thing she is best at by people who need it, and she refuses the sheets and does the other part, and the other part is the volume's first real act.**
- **A copyist of about twenty-six in a hired room over a saddler's** has **a line to rule and no instruction to rule it** and has said once, in another county, that the fourth head ought not to be there. **She is the volume's conscience, she is right, and nobody in fifty chapters agrees with her out loud.**
- **A man of about twenty-nine who keeps an inn and has a tally-stick** has **a count of the carts that come into a town in a week** and it is the most useful number in the room and it is useless, because a tally of carts counts the carts and not the roads and the two are not the same figure. **He is the volume's first ordinary man and he is not a keeper of anything and nobody puts him on anything.**
- **Aren Kest** wants **to be the man who is not asked.** He cannot be, **and Chapter 510 is the chapter in which he is asked, and he answers once, and after Chapter 510 he is a person an inquiry can call, which is the first time in this book that has been true of him and it is the worst thing that has happened to him in eleven years.**

**The volume question, in its operational form:** *a return with a place, a figure of people, a trade, and an empty fourth head goes out to nine counties — what does the office do about the four roads that are not roads, without asking the people who live on them, and without putting a person in the fourth head?*

**The answer the volume gives is that the office cannot, and that the thing which can be built is not a keeper and not a refusal and not a list of what is not on the list, and that the first instrument found anywhere in five blocks is a woman in a room two hundred miles off who sends four ruled lines to a town four days off a coast and writes one sentence under the fourth one saying that it is empty and that she does not know why.**
