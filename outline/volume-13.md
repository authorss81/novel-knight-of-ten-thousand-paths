## 1. Volume identity, and the question this volume is built on

**`outline/series.md`'s Volume 13 central pressure is arithmetic: seven active roads on one set of works, and a controlled closure that would save six of them. The close of Volume 12 handed on a document with a mark at the top of it and nothing under it, and a person who could be refused, and a sentence said in a lane: *a beautiful place is still an owned place.***

Put them together and the volume is this: **a man who is the only person who can answer for seven roads discovers that one of the seven has no number, and that the road with no number is the cheapest thing on any list anybody draws.** A road may be described only by its own answer; a road with nobody to answer cannot be described, cannot be refused, and cannot be defended, because there is nothing on the page a defender can point at. **The same hole that made the Crown's form dangerous for six months made a whole community cheap, and the man who spent Volume 12 refusing to fill a head is the man who has to decide what to do about a road he cannot describe.**

**What Volume 12 asked and answered:** *a beautiful place is still an owned place, and the person who may speak for a road is a person and not an office.* **What Volume 13 asks and has to earn:** ***that a road nobody has counted is a road nobody is obliged to keep, and that the man who is only able to see the load-bearing problem will be asked to decide which road to shut, and that being the only person who can see it is the exact reason nobody asks whether he wants to.***

**And the forged sheet is the same object as the empty head, at a different end of the scale.** A leaf with a mark at the top and nothing under it says yes to whatever a reader brings to it. **A sheet with a name at the top and no sender says *shut* to whatever a reader brings to it, and it is obeyed, and it is obeyed precisely because a world in which no sheet with a name at the top is obeyed is a worse world, and that argument is true, and the sheet is false.** The man who refused to sign a certificate in Volume 12 spent that volume refusing one kind of unfilled head. **This volume spends it on the other kind, and the second kind is obeyed, and nobody thanks anybody.**

**What the volume is not.** It is not a volume in which a forged order is traced to a named man in four chapters; the man is not named and the forgery is not explained in this volume and the price of it is not stated. It is not a volume in which the protagonist is right on arrival; **he is right about the works in Block 0001 and is wrong about the cheapest thing on the list, and the person he is wrong about is the one who has come the whole way to tell him.** It is not a courtroom and there is no bench in it. And **it is not a volume in which he solves it: the finding he carries is that a road is kept open by being named, and that naming it requires somebody local to stand up and be wrong in public, and he cannot be that person and there is no arrangement that makes him able.**

## 2. Central pressure

**Two parties want two different things, the two people in the middle are both right about a different part of it, and the man in the middle wants a third thing that nobody has offered.**

- **A man of about forty-seven who brings seven lines and a page** wants **six roads kept and one road shut, and he has done the arithmetic and the arithmetic is good.** He is not a knave and is not an officer of anything and does not say whose plan it is. **He is right about the strain, which is real, and about the shape of the answer, which is a closure with a figure attached to it, and he is wrong about one thing only, which is which road is cheap.**
- **A woman of about forty who has crossed twice in two years and was sent by nobody** wants **her road counted.** She is the contact point of a road with no number on any sheet in nine counties, and the room has never had a figure for it because nobody has ever asked, and she says out loud that she is not going to be the first to say it. **She has no want about the works and no opinion about the other six and does not want to be rescued and cannot be.**
- **A man of about fifty-eight with a chain of office that is a badge** wants **the room to hold what comes into it and to say out loud, once a month, that the hearing stands.** He cannot certify anything and does not pretend to, and he is the only man in nine counties who says the true thing about the forged sheet, and what he says is that it is not a forgery until somebody shows it false.
- **Aren Kest** wants **to be the man who finds the load-bearing problem in a place where nobody has permitted one to be found, and he is still that man, and this is the third volume in a row in which that is what he is wanted for.** What he has not been asked, in two volumes and about a hundred and thirty days, is whether he wants it.

**The volume question, in its operational form:** *seven roads and one set of works and one road with no figure on it — what is the smallest thing he can do that is not a closure, and who has to be asked before he does anything at all?*

**The answer the volume gives is a rule, and the rule is fought over by the people the rule is for.**

## 10. Concrete resolution

1. **The Law of Unopened Doors exists as a first common principle and not as a law of anybody's court.** The finding is that no road may be shut, or made subject, without local notice in the road's own words and a human witness who can be named and found. **The burden of proof belongs to the person proposing the closure, and that clause is the one the volume earns and the one every later volume leans on.**
2. **Aren and Mara separate.** Not a quarrel and not a betrayal and not a scene. **The work continues, the alliance continues, the two of them keep separate allies, and nothing between them is described in a document, and the four and a half years are never referred to as four and a half years.**
3. **The forgery is not explained and the man who wrote it is not named.** What is established is what it did: one road stood down for nine days, one count changed, and the price is not on a page. **The sheet is entered into the public record as a thing that was obeyed, which is the first time in this manuscript that a forgery has been filed as an event rather than as an error.**
4. **The fruit valley's right to refuse is read into the text aloud and the reading is not softened.**
5. **The stage is not printed and no panel is printed.** A sweep of the fifty chapters must return zero for *stage*, zero for *ledger* and zero for *qualification*, and the leaf with a seventh line that says *a person* is not looked at, and the palm is not looked at, and no chapter names the mechanism.
