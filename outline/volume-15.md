## 1. Volume identity, and the question this volume is built on

**`outline/series.md`'s Volume 15 central pressure is an expedition to the oldest surviving path, where the First Quiet began.** The close of Volume 14 handed on three things in its own terms: a man of about forty-one who keeps a drove and a byre and knows the age of a lane he does not keep, a Gatehouse that is exposed and can therefore be looked at, and two refusals that path communities have already given in writing.

**What Volume 14 asked and did not answer:** *where the oldest road in this county goes, and who has been keeping it.* **What Volume 15 asks and has to earn:** ***that a road which has been shut for eleven years is not shut by a gate, it is shut by nobody having a reason to be at the top of it, and that the only way to find out is to put somebody up there on their own business and wait for them to come back down.***

**And the volume's real antagonist is not a person and is not the wall.** It is the four-and-a-half-year habit this county has of knowing a thing four times over and writing none of it down. Volume 14's finding was that a road cannot be named without somebody local standing up and being wrong in public. **Volume 15 asks the other half of that: what happens when the thing that has to be found out about is nine miles up a lane that nobody keeps, and the only people who know anything about it have never been asked and have no reason to say.**

**What the volume is not.** It is not a volume in which the protagonist opens a door by wanting to. It is not a volume in which a path community is a museum. **It is not a volume in which the oldest path's people are found and rescued or explained: they are the descendants of the first roadkeepers and they are working, and in Block 0002 and Block 0003 they are difficult in three separate trades.** It is not a volume in which the Gatehouse is entered, and **no chapter of Blocks 0001 to 0004 puts a person through a gate in a wall.**

## 7. The mechanics this volume runs on
