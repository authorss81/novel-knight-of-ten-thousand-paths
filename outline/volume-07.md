## 1. Volume identity, and the question this volume is built on

**`outline/series.md`'s Volume 7 central pressure is a path-wide fever that causes people to forget one another, and the Archive's copied records may be the only thing that preserves the dead. `outline/volumes/volume-06-close.md` §12 hands on a different question, and the two are the same room for the third volume running.**

The series says: people forget one another, and a copy of a record is the only thing left of a person nobody remembers. The close says: **a thing has been put in a column that has a head on it, and the column has a figure in it, and the figure is about a place, and now a room can be listed.** Put them together and the volume is this: **a machine that can be handed a place, and a world in which the place is the only thing anybody can still name.** A figure of persons against a district is a population and is never a person. A copy of a dead person's name is a name and is never a person. **Both are the only kind of paper that cannot be refused, and both are the only kind of paper that cannot be argued with, and a volume about both is a volume about the difference between a record and a memory, which is not a difference anybody in the book can state and which the volume states once and then spends.**

**What Volume 06 asked and answered:** *a room can hear a thing once, and a person can be asked twice, and the difference between them is a roof.* **What Volume 07 asks and has to earn:** ***a room can be listed, and a listing can be acted on, and the person whose room it is is not in the room when the act happens, and the person who acts on it is doing the only thing anybody has taught them to do with a column, which is to fill it in.***

**And the second half of the question, which is the half that costs:** ***a copy preserves the dead and replaces the living, and a community that cannot prove which of those a given sheet is doing has to choose, and the choice is a room, and a room is a shape, and the shape is what makes people go home believing they were heard.***

**What the volume is not.** It is not a volume about a fire set by a man with a face. **The office that made the classifications has gone out of the ordinary way; the man of forty-four is not in his building and his stove is cold; nobody has asked him where he is and nobody is going to.** What travels after an office goes underground is not an order. **It is a form.** A sheet of a printed series, six columns and five heads, with a place in the first column and a figure in the second column under a head that says *Date*, and nowhere on the whole of it a place for the name of a person. **The antagonist of this volume is a form with a room in its object column and a figure against it, and the people who carry it out are ordinary people doing ordinary work in ordinary buildings, and the volume does not give the form a want because a form cannot have one and does not give a carrier a villainy it did not do.**

## 2. Central pressure

**Four parties want four different things, three of the four are right, and the man in the middle is the only one who can pay for it and the only one who cannot have what he wants.**
