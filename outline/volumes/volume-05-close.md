## 1. What the volume was about

**It is about a city that has spent four hundred years refusing to be found and has just been found in a file by accident, and about the fact that the thing that found it was not a person but a form with a space for a figure and no space for a name.**

A city of about nine thousand people on a river keeps a light standing that has not turned since a decision taken in a room four hundred years ago by eleven people, and it pays for that light with a roster and a shift and a man of fifty-two on the third shift of it for nineteen years, and it does all of this on purpose and it has never told anybody, and the reason it has never told anybody is that telling anybody is contact. It has been asked nine times in four hundred years and has said no nine times, and eight of the nine were given by eleven people in a room, and a room is a column, and the ninth was given on the nineteenth day of a count of days the city keeps by a child of nine who is not named and whose name is not given because a name in a book in that city is a person somebody can be shown. **A man of thirty-two with a limp went ninety days into it and came out with four sheets of printed paper in a press in his own county, and those four sheets are a refusal in writing, and it is the third refusal in a document in three volumes, and it is not going to be sent to anybody.**

**And the volume's most consequential page is a return with a number in it.** On the fourth of Goatmonth, YR 315, a Thursday and a market day, at about the fourth hour, a woman of thirty-three in a provincial town two hundred miles off filled in the second of four places ruled for a figure on a sheet that was not one of the ordinary nine, in a column with a head on it, against a district that does not appear on any of that office's own earlier forms and that is on the sheet because a sheet came in with it on. **The figure is the figure that office has been copying out of a county's roll into the same column every year since the year after the Sundering, and the number of persons that office would not print on an offer was asked for of a county in a return twice and did not come, and nobody in that room has ever put the two figures beside each other and a reader can.** She had been afraid of that column since she was twenty-two. She said one thing about it, in nineteen words, and then wrote the figure and put no name in anything, and four or five words of hers are the whole of what she wants and have been since she was twenty-two: *nobody enters a family by a name nobody entered.*

**The volume's best page is a bench refusing a word.** On the sixth of Goatmonth, a Saturday and a market day, at a special sitting called by a reeve on a printed sheet addressed to a district of about nine market towns, with a line at the top of the minute that says so and names no person and that the clerk of about sixty read out first and the asking second, a bench of nine was asked an eighth thing in nineteen years and said no in eleven words: *a market town is not a district and never has been.* Nobody argued with it for about nine seconds, and the reason went into the minute twice, and the clerk entered no name of anybody who voted, which is the first time in nineteen years that a refusal has gone into that minute with nothing against it. **That is a bench refusing a word and not a road, and it is the volume's best moment, and it is not a resolution, and §3 says why and §10 item 8 says what a close that called it one would have missed.**

**And the volume's ending is a strip of paper.** On a shelf eleven feet long in a room off a lane that goes down to a quay, about four hundred miles off, there is a strip of paper about a foot long lying with eight others of its kind, and in the corner of that strip there is a mark that is a line and a bar and a figure, and nobody in that room put it there, and nobody in that room has looked at it since the seventh hour of a morning in the spring, and nobody in the county has ever seen it, and it is a foot long and it is on the shelf and it is there. **A close that says Volume 05 ends on the man who was let in and then let go has misread it.** The model is `outline/volumes/volume-03-close.md` §10 item 3, and Volume 03's last line was a road four hundred yards long and it is there, and the two volumes end on the same shape of thing and for the same reason: a thing somebody else made is the only proof in this book that the work outlasted the man. **The child who put the mark is nine years old and is not named in this document either.**

### 2.4 The refusal count is nine in a dateline on the sixth day and eight on the nineteenth, and the two cannot both be right

## 3. The volume's argument, as it landed

**What Volume 04 pressed:** *a place can do the same thing to itself that an office does, with no office in it at all, and the difference between the two is not that it is gentler, and the second time round there is nobody left in the room who is able to refuse.*

**What Volume 05 answers:** *a place can be a person, and the proof is that a person can be asked a second time and can change its mind and can say no to the second asking in different words, and a column can do none of those three things.* Said once, at `220:37`, by a man of sixty-six in a bindle yard, in a sentence the volume does not improve on: *A man can be asked twice and can be asked it a different way the second time. A column cannot be asked anything at all.*

**Where it is proved, and this is the part a close has to be exact about.** Not in a speech. In a **document**, in four chapters, two hundred miles from the city, in a room with a stove in it. A sheet of an office's printed series comes into a tray in a provincial town with a place name in the object column, the word *eastern* in front of it, and four places ruled for a figure and nothing in any of them. **The word is the office's own: the type has been in a drawer in that press room since the form was made two years ago, set in the line with the heads of the five columns and never struck, and a man of about fifty went into the press room at about the sixth hour, looked at the drawer, and came out and said nothing.** A clerk of the press finds, on the sixth line of each of four sheets that have lain in a tray since the previous Embermonth, a county, a name that is not a market town, a number, and four dates five months apart in one fair hand — **and he does not enter it, because a line with no head is not a column, and he has entered about eleven thousand of these forms in thirty-one years and has never entered one of those lines.** He puts four sheets in a file and writes nothing on the wrapper. Two sheets from one place are in a pile together with two different figures in them, and he looks at the two for about a minute and does not add them and says, in nine words, *two figures out of one place, I am not going to add them and I am not going to ask* — **and a carrier of about forty-six had said the same shape of thing in a market in that province two years before, and the two men have never been in a room and two of them do not know the other's name.** Then the return, and the figure, and the district, and the woman of thirty-three.

**The volume's answer to itself, stated by a man of thirty-four with lime in a glass yard, once, in nineteen words, and not improved on:** *You are the only person in this city that is in a book somewhere else. Build this with you in it and two places have got a join in them, and a join with a person in it is a column, and a column can be filled in without asking anybody.* **The man off the road is not allowed to help, the reason is the finding and not the welcome, and it is said by the man of sixty-six and not by the visitor.** Nobody in that city saw him not do the thing he had a warm hand for, and about four hundred people in a city of about nine thousand people did not know that a man stood at the top of a lane for about a minute and a half with a warm hand and did not put it anywhere.

## 12. The question Chapter 251 opens on

**Not the city. Not the strip. Not the figure of persons, which is already on its way and does not need a question asked about it. Not the reach, the joint, the bell, the seam or the four hundred yards.**
