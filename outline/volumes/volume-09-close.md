## The figures, and where they come from

**The counting basis, settled, unchanged, and stated before the number and not after it: a word count is a plain whitespace split of the file with the title line included, each file counted on its own and the ten or the fifty added, and the sum over the files is the authority over any concatenation. Concatenating the files runs one file's last word into the next file's first and the total comes out low. A whole-word token count for a figure or a barred token is a whitespace token with punctuation stripped from its ends, so a hyphenated compound is one token, and a token count is reported as occurrences with the file count beside it. ## 1. What the volume was about

**It is about a name that nobody has to answer to, and about the fact that the only one of nine that was safe was the one that was not a practice.**

`outline/series.md`'s Volume 9 central pressure is a song, and the Volume 08 close handed on a rule — *a rule which names a keeper is a rule which makes a keeper findable* — and the two are the same object seen from the other end. Volume 08 learned that a name on a roll is a handle. **This volume goes looking for a record that has no handle, and it is not a document and it is not a hiding place: it is a thing people say out loud in the course of work, in nine lines, on the beat of a bar coming down on a rim, and eight of the nine are things people do and the ninth is the name of a road, and a road is not a place, has nobody in it, is on no map, and cannot be entered on a form at all, because there is nobody to enter in the fourth column.**

**And the whole of what happened is that the thing worked for about four hundred years in nine places, until a man with a barrow said it out loud in a market in front of strangers.** Chapter 0402 puts the eight on a bench-board and rules a ninth line under them and leaves it empty. Chapter 0407 is a market Saturday and about forty people hear the ninth word and about nine of them hear it properly, and it is out. **What Volume 08 asked and answered** was *a rule that names a keeper makes that keeper findable.* **What Volume 09 asked and had to earn** was whether there is a form of the same protection that names nothing anybody can be asked about. **The volume's answer is yes, and the price was paid at a stile in a market in about nine minutes, and the price is that the one form of it that had no keeper has now been said in front of nine people who cannot un-say it, and one of them said it back three times and is not keeping it.**

**And the volume's last page is a room, and the man is not in it.** Day 1140, the twenty-fifth of Wolfmonth, YR 318, a Sunday and a day the county does not trade on at all, in an upper room over a store in a township of about nine hundred people, where a market comes in off six villages on two days in seven. Nine people were in the room and four of them had come nine miles and one had come thirty miles and one had come in a cart hired for the day. They decided they would not do it again. **The ninth word was said once more in a mouth that is not keeping it. A mason of about sixty-eight was in that room and did not say it. A chair stood empty on a platform at the top end. A woman of about fifty went nine miles home to four feet of board with no head on it. And about nine miles west of that township a man of about nineteen had his hand on a lever at about the seventh hour and then went and had his supper, and the lever and the fulcrum were cold, and the twenty-sixth of Wolfmonth is a Monday, and at the second hour of that morning he will put his shoulder under nine feet of oak and take it down in about four seconds and at the sixth hour he will bring it up again, and there is not one crown on anything in that township or on that road.** Volume 08's last page was a road with a bar on it and nobody on the road; this one is a room with nine people in it and nobody on a road. Same weekday, opposite contents, and the rhyme was chosen and is not a copy.

**What the volume asked and did not answer.** It asked, in its own outline, *can independent paths form a stable commons without sacrificing their right to refuse* — and the page's answer is a loose council of nine that agreed in seven words that nothing about it goes on any sheet, and then agreed there would be no ninth meeting and no tenth. **A council that has no ninth meeting has not answered the question, and the page does not say it has.** The question the volume actually leaves open is a question in a second drawer down on the left in a room two hundred miles off, and it is twenty-three weeks old, and nobody carried it in and nobody is going to: *Is the first of the eight still done anywhere.* The answer is nine miles from her door, and a man of about sixty-eight has asked the far side of a wall about four hundred times and has never once had the sentence carried the four miles that would answer it. §10.

**And the deepest change in the volume is a want and not a habit, and there are two of them and neither is the protagonist's.** A woman of about forty-four at a kiln has sung nine lines for nineteen years and cannot write and will not dictate and has a room with no door, and her want is to be left alone with her work and to be asked for nothing, and the volume costs her exactly that. And a woman of about fifty with four feet of board and about three hundred and seventy figures on it and no head on it matched a hand in about nine minutes and has not said it and is not going to and has never been thanked by anybody. **Neither is thanked. The protagonist's own want — to be the person who holds the ninth word — is stated once out loud in Chapter 0404, refused in the same scene by a woman with a door and a number and a deed of thirteen words, and never restated.**

**Did a volume with a path in it hold?** It is the third volume running with a sustained path on the page and it held, and what held it was a kiln draw, a voussoir, a boundary of two miles and nine stones, a receipt under a door, and a bar of nine feet of oak. **The path's unit is a practice and a person; the county's unit is a market, a Saturday and a Thursday; and the two are added nowhere in fifty chapters.** The volume is careful never to put a main-world month name or a main-world weekday inside a room at the kilns, in the fold, or in the far fen county.

### 2.3 The volume's idiom for a remark it does not print is applied to two remarks it does print, and both are wrong by a wide margin

**`outline/volume-09.md` §15 item 15 is a rule of this volume and it is explicit: a speech-length figure is either footed on the page or it is inside a character's mouth as a claim about that character, and the word *about* may not precede a figure attached to a speech the page prints.** The volume has four sites carrying the phrase *about nine words*, and they divide cleanly into two kinds.

**Legitimate, two sites, and the close confirms both.** `chapter-0406.md` line 85: *that is the whole of what my father taught me in about nine words before he died* — the referent is a dead man's speech that the page does not print and never will. `chapter-0410.md` line 29: *He said, in about nine words, that he had not got to the bottom of what he said in that market* — the referent is what he said in Chapter 0407 and it is not printed there. **Both go to the unprinted-referent class and neither is added to it, and the class was fourteen entering this volume and is fourteen leaving it.**

**Defective, two sites, and both are in Block 0001.**

- **`chapter-0402.md` line 50:** *she said, and then she said why, and the why was **about nine words** and it is worth having exactly.* **The why is printed in the next span of the same line and is twenty-seven words**: *If I have a figure and you have a figure, then there is a number of me, and I am a woman who puts a pot on.* The preceding span, *I am not going to tell you*, is seven words and is the refusal and not the why, and the sentence separates the two explicitly with *and then she said why*.
- **`chapter-0405.md` line 33:** *he said the thing that made him stop coming up that road, and he said it in **about nine words** and he did not know that he had said anything.* **The thing is printed in full at line 35, in two spans of one speaker, and is 5 + 54 = 59 words**, and it is the chapter's best sentence.

**The word *about* is not a hedge here, and the defect is not only the number. In both sentences the volume is using its own reported-remark idiom on a remark it is about to print in full, and a reader who counts the printed speech finds a figure out by eighteen words and by fifty words respectively.** Repaired: *about nine words* to *twenty-seven words* at `chapter-0402.md` line 50 and to *fifty-nine words* at `chapter-0405.md` line 33. **These two repairs removed two whitespace tokens and are the entire reason Block 0001, the volume and the manuscript are two words lower than the figures carried on the prompt page** — §11.1 and the head table.

### 2.12 What the bar's arithmetic looks like when a reader checks it against the calendar, and why the volume is right

**This is the cleared column, and it is the second most useful result in this section, because the reading that produced it was wrong in an interesting way and the volume turned out to be right.** `chapter-0448.md` line 19 prints: *the one day over is a Wednesday, because the bar went up on a Wednesday and fifty weeks lands on a Wednesday, and a Wednesday is up and a Wednesday is paid.* **And the same chapter's line 7 prints that the bar is down on a Thursday, and Chapter 0448's own dateline is the fifteenth of Wolfmonth, a Thursday and a market morning.** The inference as written — fifty weeks lands on a Wednesday, therefore the day over is a Wednesday — does not follow, because the day after a Wednesday is a Thursday, and a Thursday is a day the bar is down and the boy is not paid.

**So the figure was checked against the calendar, and it is right, and here is why, and the reason is a convention the volume declares and applies uniformly at all seven printings.** The bar's interval on a given day is the number of days *before* that day, and the method takes whole weeks of four up-days in seven with the Sundays out and takes the remainder block from the weekday of the start day, so the remainder block always begins on a Wednesday. **Run on that convention the method reproduces six of the volume's own canon figures exactly: 73 paid days on day 949, 83 on 972, 111 and 999 pence on 1038, 129 and 1,161 pence on 1080, 151 and 1,359 pence on 1130, and 155 and 1,395 pence on 1140. **The finding is not a defect.
