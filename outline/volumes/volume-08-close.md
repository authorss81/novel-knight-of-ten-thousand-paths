## 1. What the volume was about

**It is about a bar that a community put in itself, and about the fact that nobody may take it out again.**

`outline/series.md`'s Volume 8 central pressure is an annexation: the Crown annexs the Salt Verge to control path anchors, displacing people and starting a trade war. `outline/volumes/volume-07-close.md` hands on a rule: about nine sets of about forty sheets in four counties, none of them complete, each held by one person by name, and everybody in the four counties knows which person, and nobody made it. **Put the two together and the volume is one sheet.** An office that has to put a price on a crossing has to put a person at it, and it has a column headed *Keeper* that takes a person by that person's trade and not by that person's name, and about nine people in four counties who hold a set of forty sheets are, by their own trades, keepers of something else. **The named-holder rule is not broken in fifty chapters. It is never tested. It is *used*, by an instrument that does not need a name, and the finding is the whole volume.**

**And the whole of what happened is a question and an answer and neither of them was a lie.** A man with a sheet asks a woman of forty-four what her trade is, at a landing, on a Friday, in the ordinary way, and she answers it in two words, and the answer is a trade and not a name, and a name would have been the wrong answer and would have cost her the set. **Forty sheets go into a press two hundred miles off that Friday, entered under a head, by consent, with nothing seized and nothing lied to and nobody compelled and no hand raised and no door shut** — and the debt Volume 07 handed on is paid in one sentence by a woman saying yes, and the payment is not a failure and the word is not used.

**What Volume 07 asked and this volume answered.** Volume 07 asked *a copy is a person who was there.* **Volume 08 asked and answered: *a rule that says a record has one keeper has made that keeper a thing an office can be asked about, and a person can be asked a second time and a column cannot be asked at all, and the person is findable because the person is on a roll nineteen years old that did not burn with a store.***

**What the volume asked and did not answer.** It asked, in its own outline, *what is a rule that lets four thousand people in four counties keep a thing each of them cannot be got at, and who writes it, and what happens to the writer.* **The volume's answer is on the page and it is not comfortable.** A clerk of about sixty wrote four lines again in a second hand, with the same figures, and put the date of the day against it, and that is the only mark in that building that says anybody has ever wondered about a place — **and it helps a man who has to decide and not a person who is not there.** The close does not improve on it and Volume 09 inherits it as an open question.

**And the volume's last page is a bar, and it is not a document.** Day 930, the twenty-fifth of Mudmonth, YR 318, a Sunday and a day the county does not trade, at the head of a made road four miles west of Marl, where a bar of nine feet of oak has been going down at the second hour and coming up at the sixth on four days in seven since the twenty-fourth of a month in Wolfmonth, and a man of about nineteen has been standing at one end of it for a hundred and fifty-one days, and **his four months came out on the twenty-fourth of a month in Thawmonth and nobody has said so to him, and he is still there, and about eight people in a town of about two thousand know it and none of them has ever said a word.** And the last line is the page's own: *a road is a line and two hands and a date, and a sheet is a list*, and then the bar, and then a man putting a lever against the wall of a shed, and nobody has ever told him when the four months end, and he has not asked, and he is going to be there in the morning. **The clause *and it is not a schedule* is `outline/volume-08.md`'s promise and not the page's sentence, and the first printing of this section quoted the plan and called it the last line, which is the failure this document was written to catch and this document caught in itself** — §11.3. **Volume 07 ended on a street with nobody standing in it. This volume ends on a bar with a man on it who is not paid for the months when nobody crosses, and the inversion is exact and it was chosen.**

**And the deepest change in the volume is a want and not a habit, and there are two of them and neither is the protagonist's.** A woman of about fifty has kept one list of nine crossings for nineteen years and has never taken a line out of it, and the line she will not take out is a place about four miles north with a ditch against it that about four hundred people could use and about nine do. **A woman of about thirty-four on a coast two days west keeps eleven boats and has been asked for four and has not said yes, and says her want once, in sixteen words, before anybody has asked her a second question.** Neither is thanked. The protagonist's own want — nine crossings held as a position — is stated once, refused once, and never restated.

**Did a volume with a path in it hold?** It is the second volume in a row with a sustained open path on the page and it held, and what held it was a river crossing that runs four months of the year and a bar in a kerb at the top of a rise and a woman of about forty who keeps the only account of the worst winter in eighteen years. **The path's unit is a season and a crossing and a steward; the county's unit is a market, a Saturday and a Thursday. The two are not added anywhere in the fifty chapters, and the volume is careful never to put a main-world month name or a main-world weekday inside a room in that community.** The cost is about nine people on the wrong side of a shut road in a place of about four hundred, and the volume does not tell them, and nobody is going to.

## 8. The standing debt, the interest, and the second debt this volume declines

**The debt is one debt denominated twice, and this close pays it in both currencies because they are not the same currency and only one of them was ever promised.**

**What the threads call it:** Volume 07's rule made nine sets of records findable, and this volume learned that a rule which names a keeper is a rule which makes a keeper findable.
