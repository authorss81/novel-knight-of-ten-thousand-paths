## 1. What the year was about

**The road engine is open and nobody is in charge of it, and a road can be owed to people who are not in the room.**

That is the sentence the year was built on and it is the year's own, and the fifty pages spend three hundred days showing what the second half of it costs. **The year opens with a man walking into a place nobody in this manuscript had walked into, nine hundred yards of stair, a bar held at the other end by somebody else, a river loud enough to cover a floor being opened, and a date he says out loud once at the end of the eighth page because it is the anniversary and nobody else says it first.** The year closes on the same kind of work: nine feet of coping, one sill stone, a staple, a bar, a line, a wedge and nine feet of cord, at the fifth hour, on a bridge, with the boards down on the near side and four people on them and a tool bag on the stone.

**And the year's real problem is not a person.** Every party around that engine can be right about what a road owes and unable to say it in a form another party will sign. A man in a corridor with a lamp cannot open a route in nine days and says so. A woman who keeps a distributed record wants a claim that somebody who was not in the room can check and says that the hardest person to convince of a record is the man it is about. A bridgewright runs about nine hundred crossings and has no power to open a path at all and says so in a room with a form in it. A steward wants a guarantee that will survive whoever signs it and has said in a room that a guarantee given by a person dies with the person. A clerk of sixty-one declines to report a closure because nine households at the far end asked for the reason in her own book. A boy of eleven at `chapter-0896.md` is asked to read a record he had told four people he would read and nobody had asked him, and a girl of nine at `chapter-0900.md` puts her hand on nine feet of iron after asking whether she has to. **All of those are on the page, out loud, in rooms, by people who are not him, and the year does not decide between any two of them. The year builds the thing underneath them, and the thing underneath them is that no road on the network has a single keeper, including him.**

**And the answer the year gives is negative, local and narrower than either thing a reader expects.** It is not that all paths should be open forever. It is not that no path may ever close. **It is that no path may be closed or made subject by force, and that the burden of showing that one should be belongs to the person proposing that it does.** That sentence is on the page at `chapter-0882.md` in the fourth of four things and again in the year's second and last System panel at `chapter-0888.md`, and it is not softened on any of the fifty pages.

## 4. The year's one question, its answer, and whether the year kept it

**`outline/series.md`'s row for this year asks, in its own words and not improved on: what happens when the road engine is opened to thousands of people who disagree about what a road owes them. The answer the year builds is that the answer is not a keeper. It is not that a better keeper can be found and it is not that a worse one should be removed. It is that a road can be owed to people who are not in the room, and that the burden of showing that one should close belongs to the person proposing that it does.**

**THE ANSWER WAS BUILT IN SEVEN PLACES AND CONNECTED AFTERWARDS AND NO SINGLE VOTE CREATED IT, and this is a fact and not a tribute.** The seven are on the face of one page, `chapter-0888.md`, being a coast that put its tide and its wreck out in public and gave a second holder to a man who had never read it; an assembly that filled a chair it had been asked to fill and entered its own refusal in its own book; two places that wrote their own terms and the second of which came last; a valley that printed every rule it has including one about its own faults; a hall that let a man refuse in it and wrote the refusal down; an archive that wrote down what happened and the name of one place's silence; and a community that guaranteed its right to say no and opened a trade road at the same time, which reached nine hundred yards up an occupied stair in the mouth of a carter. **That page also carries the year's second and last System panel, and the panel says the instrument is in force in seven places and not in one and that no single act of any of them created it, and no page of the year describes a named road opening at a named place because of a paper.**
