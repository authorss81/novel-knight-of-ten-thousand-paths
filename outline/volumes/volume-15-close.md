## 1. What the volume was about

**A fact four people have heard and nobody was told is not a fact anybody can act on, and this volume's whole method is that the only cure for it is to put somebody in the same place at the same hour and let them see it twice, and that this is a thing you have to ask four people for in four trades and be refused once.**

And the volume says out loud that the arrangement is not broken and is not going to be fixed by anybody in it. **The protagonist asked two communities for the same one thing in two places in twelve days, was refused twice by people with standing and no obligation, accepted both refusals out loud in a market street in forty-five words, lost the childhood he was going to come home with, and was thanked by nobody at any point of it.** Then he found the oldest surviving road in the county, which does not go anywhere, and a scar nine hundred yards long in a boundary that nine households cut and never walked, and he held ten roads against it once at four in the morning and could not get off the grass afterwards.

**And the volume's answer to its own question is negative and local and has no head: about nine people in nine counties can now point at a thing on the back of a board in a cart shed, and about nine hundred yards of an old road is still in about four hundred mouths and in nobody's hand, and those are two separate things, and the volume keeps them separate on the page in every one of its fifty chapters.**

## 11. What Volume 16 opens on
