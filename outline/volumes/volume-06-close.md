## 1. What the volume was about

**It is about a room, and the room is the same shape as the thing that made the refusal, and the volume's finding is that the shape does the damage and not the people in it.**

Two questions look like two questions and are one room. `outline/volume-05.md` §11 asked what happens to a refusal when somebody convenes a room to hear about it. `outline/series.md`'s Volume 6 row says the Crown convenes a public inquiry into Bellweather while the Culling Order attempts to classify every path as an emergency hazard. **Put the two together and every one of them is a form.** A proceeding has a witness list, a form of oath, a table of three and a clerk. A classification has an object column and a second column with no head on it. And the thing being inquired into is a body corporate in a fen county that has spent four years and a half refusing to be a body corporate, whose only offence is that it has a door, a number, the capacity to sue and to be sued, and a register in which five entries are questions and not one is a finding.

**And the volume's most consequential act is a cut made in a chamber at the second hour on a forged witness, and it is not a triumph and it is not a failure and it is the whole of the volume.** He made the first cut in the manuscript that was not consensual. The machinery did not distinguish it from Chapter 195's. **The whole of the difference between the two was a man standing at his shoulder with a rope between them, and there was no man.** It took the anchor and not the link. Forty yards of tarred hemp went with it, four feet down, in a dressed block in the abutment of a crossing a hundred yards from the end of a market, and nobody has dug for it and nobody is going to. A crossing that has carried nobody for four years now carries nobody for a reason nobody in that county can name, **and that is the difference between two sentences and the whole of the volume: a crossing that carries nobody is a crossing that carries nobody, and it carried nobody for four years before anything happened to it, and a character who collapses those two sentences has taken the volume's argument away from the reader.** The word *failed* is at zero in the volume and the close does not use it about that crossing.

**And the volume's ending is not a man and not a city.** The testimony is Chapter 288 and the injunction is Chapter 289 and both are the volume's climax. **The last page is Chapter 300's, and it is a county of about four thousand people, and the sentence it ends on is the fact that there has never been a book in this kingdom with the two crossings in it and there is not going to be one.** The model is `outline/volumes/volume-03-close.md` §10 item 3, whose last line was a road four hundred yards long and it is there, and Volume 05's, which was a strip of paper on a shelf. **Volume 06's is two lines in two stones in one county, and the second of them is about nine inches long in the face of a dressed block four feet above forty yards of tarred hemp that nobody has dug for, and the only person in that county who could ever have got at it has a permanent limp and is four hundred miles from being able to try.**

**And the volume's deepest change is a habit and not a belief, and there are three of them, and none of them is the one the outline reaches for.** `outline/volume-06.md` §10 says he reaches the upper end of Threadkeeper and loses the illusion that one trial can restore justice. **What is on the page is different, and the difference is the finding.** First: **he entered his own failure into a record before anybody asked him for it, and it did not help.** Second: **a woman of thirty-eight who had asked for nine years for a room to show what was asked watched the best possible version of her want being performed in front of her by a witness, and it did not work, and she entered the room as a room in the minute anyway, and the finding at the end of it is a figure, and being correct is the most expensive thing available to a person in a chamber.** Third: **a woman of thirty-three who had been afraid of one column for eleven years asked a room to write down that she did not stop it, and there is nobody in that building she can ask.** A person who has an instrument and has not used it and cannot record the not-using is standing in this volume from Chapter 279 onward, and the close does not resolve her construction and does not let anybody enter it for her. **And the two of them are on opposite sides of a public proceeding, the breach is open, and it was not repaired in a scene, an aside, a document or an apology. The volume's relationship beat is two refusals of kindness in two rooms two hundred miles apart, and neither of them is the other one.**

**Did a volume with no path in it hold?** It is the first volume in the series with no sustained open path on the page at all, and `outline/volume-06.md` §17 consequence 2 says so in its own words: the valley is four hundred miles off and reachable only over a nine-day road in Block 2, after Block 3 it is not reachable at all, and the volume has to carry two hundred and forty days in rooms. **The close's answer is that it held, and that what held it was two hundred and forty feet of stone on a bridge in a valley four hundred miles off and four hundred yards of a road a man of thirty-four dug, and the second half of that answer is that neither of them is a crossing he made and neither of them cost anybody anything.** The recovery anchor was a public promise renewed in a field and a reach with a fourth bay pointed, and both are on the page, and the promise is the volume's cheapest and most dangerous page and it is fourteen pence and a sheet in a bag. **What the volume could not do, and did not pretend to, is put a path in a room: the two times the volume touches a place that is not the main world it does so in four chapters in a block of ten and in one chapter in a block of ten, and in both the touching is a room over a cross with about thirty people in it, and a room over a cross with about thirty people in it is a room, and that is the discovery and not a failure of it.**

## 12. The question Chapter 301 opens on

**Not the bench. Not the injunction. Not the figure of persons, which is already on its way and does not need a question asked about it. Not the reach, the joint, the bell, the two lines or the four hundred yards. Not the city.**
