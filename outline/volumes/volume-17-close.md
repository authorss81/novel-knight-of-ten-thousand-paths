## 1. What the volume was about

**The best offer anybody in this county has ever been made cannot be answered here, and the reason it cannot be answered is that answering it needs a name.**

That is the sentence the volume was built on and it is the volume's own, and it is printed in the outline at §1 and it is what the fifty pages are about. **The thing that came to this county in the ordinary post is not a threat and is not an order and is not a third copy of a roll. It is a sheet that asks for the name of one person to stand against the roads of nine counties, and it carries a date about nine weeks off the day it was nailed up, and about four people in nine counties said out loud in four trades that it is the best offer anybody in this county has ever been made, and about nine hundred people along eleven miles of made road have not heard it.** Here means this county and not the nine of them, because a person standing in a market street in this county is the only kind of person who can honestly have heard every offer this county has ever been made, and the volume says so on the page and does not widen the claim anywhere.

**And the volume's real antagonist is not a person and is not that place nine days off. It is the second thing a piece of paper can do to a county, which is the first thing it did, and Volume 16's engine was that a roll can shut a road and this volume's is that a paper can make people want to be governed, and that a county which has spent a year learning to refuse has no second answer in it, and that neither answer is a resolution.** A woman of about sixty-one keeps the county's one seal, its ribbon and a set of standard weights in a drawer eleven inches deep in a low room at the back of the office at the low end of that market street, and has kept them about nine years, and wants the paper this county is copying to be worth something so that the drawer matters, and says on the page that she would rather be the woman who kept it than the woman who gave it and that she knows that and it is not enough. A man of about thirty-eight runs a weekly wain up and down four miles of made road that have been the smoothest four miles in nine counties since the fifth of Wolfmonth and has said out loud in his own trade, once, to about nine people, that about nine hundred yards of a stair nine days off is worth four miles of made road any day, and the page says he has begun to think that not having been heard is a kind of safety and that he is right about that and it is not safety. **Both of those are true and neither of them is a reason, and the volume says so more than once and blames nobody.**

**And the answer the volume gives is a line with a trade on it, which somebody can be argued with, and a paper with no seal on it, which nobody has to obey, and by the last of the five parts both of those are a position and neither of them is a plan.** Nine trades went onto nine lines on the flap of a stall at the low end of that market street on the first of Goatmonth and no name went on any of them and nobody in nine counties has been asked whose trade is on any of the nine, and about four hundred places in nine counties have been writing the same paper out by hand for about four months and not one of the four hundred has ever met another of the four hundred and no two of about four hundred copies agree with one another because nobody has asked them to. **The roll is three hundred and fifty-three days on its frame with three copies of it and nine empty lines on each and the fourth line empty on all three, and the date at the foot of the sheet nailed up beside it is one hundred and forty-six days past and has shut nothing, and the drawer was not opened once in two hundred and nine days, and the scar is six hundred days old, and a bleed two hundred and sixty days old is in no document and has never been said out loud in a room in front of one other person, and the ninth thing behind nine hurdles is alive and unnamed and unheld and shut.**
