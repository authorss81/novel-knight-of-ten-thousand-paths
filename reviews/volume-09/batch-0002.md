## What this review adds to the pattern

**Across twelve batches, no review has found a plot defect in the delivered prose.** Every finding that changed a chapter has been a continuity, an arithmetic, a formatting or a voice slip, and every finding about a record has been a record about a record.

**What this one adds is that a repair pass can explain a defect away, and the explanation goes into a state file where the next writer reads it as a decision.** The second pass found the chair contradiction, did not repair it, and wrote *two chapters that are about two different chairs* into `state/batch-summaries.md`. That sentence was on the page for a whole phase as a finding already disposed of. **A finding that is disposed of with a reason instead of a change is a finding that has been closed without being answered, and a state file is where such a sentence is most expensive, because it is copied forward into the next prompt and inherits as a rule.** The other half of the same finding is the fifth row of the table above: the second pass *did* repair the boy in §Two, did not repair him in §Five, and recorded the repair as complete. **So one instance of one defect was handled twice in the same pass, once by explanation and once by a partial edit, and both were reported as done.**
