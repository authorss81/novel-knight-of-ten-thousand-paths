# Review — Batch 0003, Volume 01, Chapters 21–30, "The Second Crossing and the Refusal"

**This is a gap record, not a findings list.** The review of this phase ran and was applied, but its finding-by-finding output was written only to `logs/batch-0003.review.log`, which is gitignored. **No copy survives, and none is reconstructed here: inventing a findings list for a review nobody can read would put a false record into the canon's own audit trail.** The dispositions that do survive are in the manuscript, and this file points at them.

## What is known

- The phase ran and completed; `workspace/volume-01/batch-0003/.done` exists.
- **A repair pass followed the review, and its finding counts survive**: the review returned **sixteen findings applied to the prose and eleven to the state files** — twenty-seven in all. That count and the substance of the pass are recorded in the **Repair pass (review of Batch 0003)** paragraph of the Batch 0003 entry in `state/batch-summaries.md`. That paragraph is the durable record and it is specific: it names the chapters and the changes.
- The repair pass's own guarantees, as recorded: **no plot moved, no chapter was renumbered, no beat was removed, and no controller file was touched.**
- Substantively, that paragraph records the weekday fix that made the sixteenth of Greenmonth a Friday across two state files, Chapter 30's opening and the Market Square board in two places (the board line is now *Road warden in the cart on Friday*); the correction of Chapter 27's elapsed days to two; the rewrite of Chapter 26's arrival paragraph on page facts; Chapter 28 no longer dating the sworn account a year early; Captain Ruen's speech kept in his own voice; **the separation of Sera Quen from the woman who keeps the Hushwater crossing book, out loud, in a hall, so that the two cannot be merged by a later writer**; and two date errors of the same class found while repairing and also fixed.
- **Two findings were recorded rather than changed and are now canon**: Hollis Dree's second statement of the two hundred and eleven in a public hall in Ch 27 is a second source and not a substitution for the ferryman (continuity item 36), and the missing body of the Chapter 29 letter is a conscious cut (item 92).

## What is lost

The per-finding list: the finding text, the severity, the file and line, and the individual disposition of each of the twenty-seven. If a later phase needs to know *why* one specific change was made in this batch rather than what it was, that is not recoverable from this repository.

## Recommendation for the next phase

Treat the Batch 0003 entry in `state/batch-summaries.md` as the record of record for this phase, and do not claim a review file for it. If the Volume 01 close wants an audit trail, it should say in its own document that Batches 0002 and 0003 have a surviving disposition and no surviving finding list.
