# Review — Batch 0002, Volume 01, Chapters 11–20, "What the Fever Cost Everyone Else"

**This is a gap record, not a findings list.** The review of this phase ran and was applied, but its finding-by-finding output was written only to `logs/batch-0002.review.log`, which is gitignored. **No copy survives, and none is reconstructed here: inventing a findings list for a review nobody can read would put a false record into the canon's own audit trail.** The dispositions that do survive are in the manuscript, and this file points at them.

## What is known

- The phase ran and completed; `workspace/volume-01/batch-0002/.done` exists.
- **A repair pass followed the review, and its finding counts survive**: the review returned **thirty-five findings applied to the prose and six to the state files** — forty-one in all. That count and the substance of the pass are recorded in the **Repair pass (review of Batch 0002)** paragraph of the Batch 0002 entry in `state/batch-summaries.md`. That paragraph is the durable record and it is specific: it names the chapters and the changes.
- The repair pass's own guarantees, as recorded: **no plot moved, no chapter was renumbered, no beat was removed.**
- Substantively, that paragraph records the cut of Ch 17's coda into Pei Tarrow's interior, the removal of Ch 15's second bell, the dating of Ottery's statement to the second of Greenmonth, the addition of Rell putting the twenty-seventh to the court and being shown frightened, a corrected weekday chain across all ten chapters, the removal of the four thousand printed beside the villages and of *in March* in Ch 13, Ch 18's forward-look dates, the removal of Ch 18's System display, Ch 19's YR 302 year and the refused permission to add the villages, Ch 20's money bag, nine shillings and the paragraph that performed the thing Aren's character had just refused, the ninth now said aloud on the bridge to Tovan and Quarren, and consistent `---` scene breaks.
- Two things the repair pass did not do, as far as the surviving paragraph records: **it does not list any finding that was measured and declined, and it does not list any finding recorded rather than changed** — so unlike Batch 0003, which records two such findings by name, this batch's declined and recorded-only findings are not identifiable from what survives.

## What is lost

The per-finding list: the finding text, the severity, the file and line, the individual disposition of each of the forty-one, **and any finding that was declined or recorded rather than changed.** If a later phase needs to know *why* one specific change was made in this batch rather than what it was, that is not recoverable from this repository.

## Recommendation for the next phase

Treat the Batch 0002 entry in `state/batch-summaries.md` as the record of record for this phase, and do not claim a review file for it. If the Volume 01 close wants an audit trail, it should say in its own document that Batches 0002 and 0003 have a surviving disposition and no surviving finding list.
