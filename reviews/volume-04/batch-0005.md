## What this batch adds to the pattern

`reviews/README.md` records that across seven batches no review had found a plot defect in delivered prose, and this file is the eighth and ninth data points and they agree. **But the ninth review is the one that changes the pattern's shape: it reviewed a repair rather than a batch, and what it found was that the repair had missed the one place that mattered most — the last paragraph of the last chapter — and had carried the miss into the next phase's prompt as an instruction, and had asserted three of its own figures instead of counting them.** The three classes are the classes this project already knows: a continuity slip, a false claim about an artifact, and an unmeasured number. **What is new is only the target.** A repair pass is not a safer thing to review than a batch; it is a thing with the same failure modes and a smaller sample, and a project that reviews its batches and not its repairs will keep finding the same three defects in the same three places and will keep inheriting them.
