## Findings and dispositions

**1. A refusal that argued against its own premise, in 0584, and it is the chapter's whole argument.** The woman of about thirty-one tells the man of about fifty-one, at line 21, that the ditch about two paces wide on the top of the fourth terrace is what has kept the water off the four lowest for twenty-two winters, and that the reason the water reaches the first terrace on the ninth day and all four on the sixteenth is that the ditch is being cut this year by one person instead of six. Nine lines later she refuses his offer of four men, and as printed she refuses it on the ground that **putting four men on her ditch would leave the four of us under for sixteen instead of nine** — that is, that the help would make it worse. **That is the opposite of what she has just told him the ditch does, and the speech is a woman's refusal of help and it now argues for the harm.** Repaired by rewriting the two clauses that carried it and nothing else: four men on the ditch now gives her the ditch in four days instead of sixteen and the four of them dry this year, she says she would take them and is not going to take them, and she gives the reason the page already supports — a ditch is cut every sitting and the channel has not been mended in about nine years, and four men on her ditch buys one year off him. **The seven days of a season nobody is going to give back stays, and it is now stated once instead of twice.** No plot moved, nobody thanked, the barrow-wheel still goes down in the grass. Continuity 1398 quoted the defective arithmetic and has been corrected to the repaired page.

**2. A figure of persons that is wrong against the page, in the 0584 coda.** The coda said **the four who said no and the two who said yes**. Chapter 0582 has the four sets answer **yes and no and yes and no** and says so in a bold paragraph of its own, and 0590 repeats the order. **Four sets did not say no; two did.** This is the defect the project names as a figure checked against a number instead of against the page, and it is invisible to every sweep because the list still has four items and the claim still says four. Repaired to **the two sets who said no and the two who said yes**, count held at four.

**3. A sentence in 0585 that had come apart, and it is the pot of earth.** The page read *nobody in this room has been told that in nine days and has not asked*, which is not a sentence. Repaired to **nobody in this room has been told any of that and has not asked.** This is the same defect `state/continuity.md` 1403 records for a different sentence in the same chapter, and the general form is in thread 375: a repair that fixes the fact and breaks the sentence is the shape of a repair rather than the shape of a draft.

**4. **6. A typo in 0587.** *watching the channel hisself* → **himself**. Four words.

**7. The water's own clock, in 0590, against the chapter that counted it.** The page said **It came down the channel at about the seventh hour on the ninth day and the ninth day was a morning and the water went back to the four lowest terraces at about the fifth hour of the day after.** Three things are wrong with one sentence. Repaired to the water arriving on the first morning and going back off the four lowest at about the fifth hour of the day after the ninth, and to **the ninth was a morning as well as a day and that is on a slate in this valley and is not in a book**, which is the figure the block exists to have counted and is now anchored on the page instead of in a continuity file.

## What this pass did not repair, and why

**The turn of the five sittings arriving at the ninth hour of a Wednesday, in 0590, with nine men laying stone on a Sunday in 0589, is still where thread 394 put it.** The block's only stated weekday rule is that the room over a saddler's is shut on Sundays, that room is four days and about nine off, and nothing in 0589's Sunday happens in it, so the rule holds. Moving a day means moving it in a day list the volume outline fixed, that Batch 0005's prompt inherited, and that the volume close will need whole. **It is a shape question for the close and not a defect in the page, and it was not touched.**

**The card's fourth week of the four is not the fourth week of the four, and the page says so.** The chapter card for 0585 has the chapter falling in the fourth week of four weeks granted on the first of Harvestmonth, which is day 1746; the chapter is day 1776 and the four weeks were up on the twenty-eighth of Harvestmonth. **The page resolves this in the open rather than by pretending: it prints that the four weeks were up, that the Thursday after them was the first market morning past them and the room was shut on it, and that the man with the badge has left the door open for one more week and says in the street that it is his and not hers.** The block's own figures hold — 1776 − 1746 = 30, 1773 is a Wednesday, 1774 the Thursday that was shut, 1776 the Saturday that is open — and the page is self-consistent and foots. **A card is a plan and this is a page, and where they differ the page has to be the one that is true about the world. It is recorded here so that no successor reads the card and thinks the chapter is wrong.**

## What this pass adds to the pattern

**It is the fifth review of a repair rather than of a batch in this project, and the third of one in this volume, and the target again changed nothing about the failure modes.** Seven of the eleven are in the prose, and that is the first pass in this volume where a review found more prose than records, and **the seven are all one class: a fact described two different ways, or a figure checked against a number instead of against a page.** The ditch that keeps water off terraces is also the thing whose absence puts water on them, and the refusal argued for the harm. Four sets said no when two did. Four hundred paces to go when a hundred did. The water arriving on the ninth day when it arrived on the first. A dearest share given to two women. A penny counted as a person. A sentence copied out of an earlier chapter with its commas moved.

**Not one of the seven is visible to a token sweep, and every one of them was found by holding two or three chapters against each other and asking what the page has already said.** That is the finding this repository has now paid for in five consecutive blocks, and the block's own record of it at thread 393 is that the previous review verified everything its instruments were built to verify and missed all five of the defects that were actually there. **A review is a set of instruments plus a reader, and a set of instruments only finds what its phrases were built to find, and the four instruments written for this pass were each wrong at least once before they were right.**
