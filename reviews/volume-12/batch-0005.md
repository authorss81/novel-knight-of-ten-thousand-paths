## Findings and dispositions

**1. HIGH, and the root of findings 2 and 3: the block was written to survive the instrumentation rather than to be read, and the instruments all passed.** As written the batch was 12,621 words against 20,378 for the block before it, and the shortfall was hidden in the shape of the reporting. Every chapter carries a mandated cost coda of roughly three hundred words, so **the coda's share of a chapter's length moves inversely with the scene**, and it rose monotonically from 18 per cent in 0591 to 29 per cent in 0599. Scene bodies — the total minus the coda — were **733 to 1,121 words in nine of ten chapters**, and the volume's own floor for a body, set by Chapter 0568, is **1,161**. The review's figure of 1,518 is the floor for a *total*, which is a different number and a worse instrument. **0592 to 0600 were lifted by 295 to 320 words of body each and 0591 was left alone, because the review named it the best writing in the batch and it was already at 1,714.** Every body is now at or above the floor: **1,714 / 1,492 / 1,458 / 1,247 / 1,283 / 1,318 / 1,207 / 1,202 / 1,189 / 1,226.** The added material is event and physical work, not summary: a barrow that finds the wet on its second run, a corner of a board that goes into the soft edge and stays there, a white groove shown to be the post moving and not the cord, the back strip walked and stood on by the woman who gave it away, a cracked stone in the face above the uncut fan, a stop at a trough because his leg wanted it, a cart coming up the lane too fast.

**2. HIGH: one scaffold instantiated ten times, and the tell was measurable.** The coda opener was the same sentence with the slots swapped in nine of ten. *are not added to one another* ran ten in ten against **four in the preceding forty chapters**. Every chapter had **exactly three** bold-led lines against a 3-to-10 range across 551 to 590. The longest duplicated run inside the batch, 28 words, was an inventory frame shared by 0595 and 0600. **Repaired in place: the coda openers now take six distinct house forms and three of them restore the volume's own `And the cost of that <weekday> is a column and…` form; the persons lists now end in nine different ways, most of them saying what the list is *not*, which is the same claim as the old formula in the volume's own idiom; the bold-led counts are now 3, 4, 3, 3, 4, 3, 4, 3, 4, 3.** The duplicated 28-word run was the three-series inventory, and it is broken by ending a sentence at *about four hundred returns*, so the two chapters no longer share a string.

**3. HIGH: negation had displaced event, and the two worst cases were the block's set-piece and its last page.** As written, 0597 delivered the crossing as a list of refusals to narrate it — *nobody says what it does to anything a man carries, and nobody says why a man can stand in two places* — and 0600 stated the absence of a scene from inside the scene. **Both are now walked.** The crossing in 0597 is the lane in three turns losing about a mile of height, the last house and a dog that does not come back, the channel heard and not seen, the woman's remark that it will be louder again in a month, the stones of the made road in the last of the light, a stop at the trough, the mist coming up the lane and stopping, and the valley out of sight behind the ridge within about two hundred paces. The non-repair in 0600 is the key turned over once and not put into the lock, and a lane with room in it for him to go on and his not going on. **The mechanism is named nowhere, `stage`, `ledger` and `qualification` are at zero, no panel is printed, and each mandated fact is still stated exactly once.** A sentence in 0593 that addressed a reviewer rather than a reader — *this valley may neither quietly repeal it nor quietly vindicate it* — was rewritten so the two facts sit on either side of the rule on the page.

**4. HIGH, and this one the review could not see: five money columns collided with the prior manuscript, and both the writing pass and the review reported none.** The extractor in use was the house form `A mark is forty-eight pence and X pence`, and **it cannot read *a hundred and forty-seven* and the rest of the *a hundred* spellings, which is 40 of the 171 instances over the 590 chapters before this block, and it misses every chapter that words the conversion differently — 0561 writes *Forty-eight pence is a mark in this county and…* and 0566 writes no *A mark is* clause at all.** The blind set was 129 distinct where the true set is **163**, taken as the union of two independent extractors. The shipped totals collided as **145 with 0527, 153 with 0520, 155 with 0545, 175 with 0539 and 186 with 0548.** Repaired by the remedy the batch prompt prescribes — **add a pence to a column and re-foot, never edit a printed total** — which moved 0591, 0592, 0593, 0595, 0596, 0597, 0598, 0599 and 0600 and **left 0594's total of 176 standing unchanged.** Nine of the ten changed, the block's ten totals are now the ten lowest free slots in the range, and the collision set is empty. The item *lists* were re-wordered and re-worded and never the prices except by the penny that had to be added, and no price in any column is duplicated.

**5. MEDIUM: eight chapters were undated, so the calendar certification tested a table the prose does not carry, and printing the dates found a live error.** Only 0595 and 0600 carried a date, and the batch summary nonetheless assigned a day to each chapter and reported Calendar 0/0/0. **All ten datelines now print month, day of month, weekday and year**, every one re-derived from day 930 with the two halves derived separately from the number: **1796, 1799, 1801, 1803, 1806, 1811, 1816, 1821, 1826 and 1830**, on Fridays, Mondays, Wednesdays, Fridays, a Monday, a Saturday, a Thursday, a Tuesday, a Sunday and a Thursday. **1826 is a Sunday and 0599 is the only chapter on it, and 0599 is in the valley and not in the room over a saddler's, so the standing weekday rule holds.** The error that surfaced is in 0596, which claimed the three-name slate had lain on its stone **for nine days and a morning** — that figure is the water-cut count from 0587, and a slate in existence since at least 0581 and lying on that stone since the last day of the pick sitting has lain there seventeen days by 0596's own printed day. **Repaired to a claim carrying no number, *since before the bar sitting came in*, and the coda time list was corrected with it rather than the numeral alone.**

**6.
