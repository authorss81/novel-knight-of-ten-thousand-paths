# Volume 17, Batch 0005 — Chapters 841 to 850, "The Upper Terraces", days 3139 to 3180

The first review artifact Volume 17 has, and the sixth time this project has paid for `logs/` being gitignored. The review ran against `logs/batch-0005.review.log` and returned nine findings; its log is not a durable record and this file is the list it owed. **Four findings applied in the prose of two chapters, four applied in the records, one refused with reasons, and one structural finding recorded and not applied. No chapter was restarted, no dateline moved, no weekday moved, no money total moved by one penny, no name spent, no outline requirement changed and the ending did not move.**

**No plot defect was found in the prose.** That is now the standing pattern across every batch this directory records, and Batch 0005 is the first data point for Volume 17. What the review found instead was a stall, four figures that were asserted rather than counted, two byte-identical paragraphs, and a shape.

---

## Blocking

**1. Volume 17 was finished with no live successor, and the stall was written into the prompt that caused it.**

`chapters/volume-17/chapter-0801.md` through `chapter-0850.md` are all on disk. `outline/volumes/volume-17-close.md` does not exist; Volumes 01 to 16 all have one. `workspace/volume-17/close/PROMPT.md` did not exist; `workspace/volume-16/close/PROMPT.md` does. `reviews/volume-16/` and `reviews/volume-17/` were both absent.

**The cause is the finding worth keeping.** The prompt for the block that wrote Chapters 841 to 850 opened by binding itself to create *no successor of any kind* — no close prompt, no Volume 18 prompt, no second prompt — and its §9 said the volume's close record is a separate later phase that the prompt does not create and *that nothing in the prompt obliges any later phase to create*. `state/current.md` recorded the consequence as "the volume's debt and not any block's."

**A prohibition written by the same pipeline that dispatches on the pipeline's absence of a successor is not a rule.** It is a stall with a legal-looking sentence on it, and the sentence is more durable than the pipeline's memory of it. `AGENTS.md` assigns the close prompt to the final block, and the final block is over. **The review was right that this argument does not survive `AGENTS.md`, and the review was right that it needed a hand-authored prompt rather than a controller change, and both halves of that were applied.**

- **Applied.** `workspace/volume-17/close/PROMPT.md` created, modelled on `workspace/volume-16/close/PROMPT.md`: same five-section shape, same prohibitions, same closing instruction to create exactly one successor. Its only output is `outline/volumes/volume-17-close.md`. It may spend no name, may open no prohibition the fifty pages did not carry, and may not repair prose.
- **Applied.** `state/current.md`'s live-prompt section rewritten to name the close prompt as the live successor, with the block's own prohibition marked withdrawn and retained as a record of what was decided rather than as instruction.
- **Applied.** `reviews/volume-17/batch-0005.md`, this file.
- **Not applied.** No controller file was touched. `state/phase-ledger.json` remains `phase-000-bootstrap`, `planned`, attempts 0, against eight hundred and fifty chapters, and reporting that without touching it is behaving correctly.

## Applied in the prose, two chapters, both subtractions

**3. **And one duplicate-looking thing was deliberately not repaired.** The five-sentence warning chain beginning *Four lines on a sill are not a cure for it* runs verbatim through `chapter-0841.md`, `chapter-0842.md`, `chapter-0845.md` and `chapter-0848.md`, and two further files carry variants. Its only varying element is the bleed's age, at two hundred and thirty-three days and two hundred and forty-seven. **A device whose figure is maintained per chapter is a device and not a duplication**, which is `volume-13/batch-0003.md`'s standing point, and re-voicing it would have destroyed the variation that proves it is being maintained.

## Applied in the records — four figures asserted rather than counted

The block's own state items carried seven prose figures and a census. **Four were wrong, and the class is the one this directory has recorded nine times: a figure copied forward without a basis, or produced on one basis and published on another.**

**4. The census.** Published as **Basis B 325**, on four named exclusions of which the fourth was *the five bold printed-speech paragraphs*, and the block's own arithmetic footed — 360 less ten less five less ten less ten. **The exclusion group is wrong and the figure with it.** Five is the number of counted speeches, not the number of spoken paragraphs: seven files carry a quotation mark and **ten** paragraphs in all do, being five one-word answers of *Twice.* and five long speeches. Basis B is **320**. A figure that foots and is wrong is the hardest kind to catch, and this one was caught by counting the paragraphs rather than by re-adding the block's own subtraction.

**5. The count of sentences appearing verbatim in all ten files.** Published as **ZERO**, with the highest count of a single sentence across files published as **five**. Both wrong. **One sentence appears in all ten files and appears ten times**, being the closing line of the money column in every file — the sentence that says the search and the three values in it stand once in `state/continuity.md`. **A sentence that names the state file it is repeated in is the sentence most likely to be repeated**, and it was the one sentence the block's own instrument could not see, because the sentence reports a count rather than making one.

**6. The sentence-level repetition.** Published as fifty-four groups and seventy-eight surplus. Measured after this repair: **fifty-four groups and eighty-eight surplus** over 728 qualifying sentences. **The groups figure was right by accident and a figure that is right by accident is not a check.**

**7. The paragraph-scoped duplicate delta.** Published as a zero, on absolutes of 36/37 over the eight hundred and forty files and 38/39 over the eight hundred and fifty, with the sentence that no duplicate paragraph in either total involves any of the ten files. **On the instrument named at `state/continuity.md` 1875 the absolutes are 39/40 on both counts, the delta was genuinely two groups and two surplus, and the no-duplicate-here sentence is false in exactly the two places named above.** The two prose repairs make the delta a true zero of both. **The base of 36/37, which five blocks of this volume carry, does not reproduce on the instrument named for it**; that is recorded and owned by the Volume 18 outline phase, and was not re-derived across eighty files by this pass.

**The recount moved twice, and the order is the lesson.** The two edits took the block total from 31,486 to **31,505**, the mean to **3,150.5**, `chapter-0842.md` to 3,394 and `chapter-0845.md` to 3,026, Volume 17 to **155,877** at a mean of 3,117.54, the manuscript to **2,497,143**, Batch 0001's carried figure to 2,510,630 with the 13,487 difference unchanged and still entirely Batch 0001's, and the `about` column at 25.63 and 26.11 in two positions with floor and ceiling unmoved. **Eleven figures, corrected in all four live state files, counted last.** A pass that counts, then edits, and does not count again has not counted.

## Recorded and not applied, with a named owner

**8. The ten chapters are status recaps rather than scenes.** This is the finding the review led with after the stall, and it holds. Roughly fifty-five to sixty-five of about seventy-five lines in each chapter restate series-wide continuity facts — the roll's day count, the notice's overdue days, the drawer's day count, the hurdles' day count, the drove's count, the four lines of chalk, the half leaf, the cloth on the bench, the bleed's age, the broom, the nine-stone scar — and about twenty of those facts recur in all ten with only the numerals incremented. `chapter-0841.md` and `chapter-0845.md` are structurally interchangeable. **No protagonist action drives any chapter: he walks out at about the eleventh hour four days off a made road, or he is stated to be elsewhere.** The block's own central event — two sheets read aloud in a room at the high end of that market street — is reported five days later in retrospect in a bold summary paragraph at `chapter-0843.md` and summarised as already-done fact at `chapter-0846.md`, **so the volume's promised beat resolves in summary and not on the page.** Dialogue is at ten quoted spans in about thirty-one thousand words, and `chapter-0841.md`, `chapter-0845.md` and `chapter-0849.md` carry none. **This is the fourth consecutive Volume 17 block whose prose is continuity bookkeeping.**

**Not applied, and the reason is the rule that governs the pass.** Fixing it means rewriting ten chapters. A repair pass must not rewrite a completed block. **The root cause is upstream in `outline/volume-17.md` §7 to §9 and in the batch prompts' prohibition lists, which enumerate so many untaken actions that the chapters have nothing left to do** — a shape set by what a prompt forbids before a sentence is written, and no prose repair can reach it.

**Owned, in writing, at `state/open-threads.md` 848 and in §5 of the new close prompt: the Volume 18 outline phase.** The close record must record the finding and must not repair it. The weighted device figure stands with it: `about nine` runs twenty to thirty-three times a chapter and `nobody` seventeen to thirty, which is a hedge and not a register, and it is the same in all ten.

## Refused, with reasons

**9. The money columns.** The review called the ten totals a generated arithmetic series of step about twenty-four and the shared justification paragraph a fabricated search rather than evidence. **The sequence is arithmetic — 87, 111, 133, 157, 181, 205, 229, 253, 277, 301 — and it is not a defect.** The band is exhausted: 284 printed values in the band sixty to three hundred and forty-three and **zero** free ones, and nothing under sixty is free at all. Every total in the block is therefore a reuse, which is what each of the ten closing paragraphs says on the page, in that chapter's own words, with the reason given as arithmetic. **The columns foot four ways on all ten, with the over-figures 39, 15, 37, 13, 37, 13, 37, 13, 37 and 13.** Declined.

## The pattern this adds

Nine findings, and the eighth is the first in this project where **the review's central prose complaint and the repair's correct disposition are the same fact**: the block cannot be made into a batch of scenes without rewriting it, and the sentence that forbids the rewrite is `AGENTS.md`. Every earlier review in this directory found a defect that could be repaired on the page while keeping the chapter. **This one found a defect whose only repair is the block, and whose cause is a plan, and the correct disposition is therefore to write the finding down with an owner and hand it forward.** A finding recorded without an owner is a finding that will not be repaired; a finding recorded with a reason instead of a change is a finding that has been closed without being answered; **and a finding recorded with an owner in a file the next phase is required to read is the only one of the three that is worth anything.**
