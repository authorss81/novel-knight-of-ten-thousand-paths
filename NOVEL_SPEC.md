# Novel Specification

Title: The Knight of Ten Thousand Paths
Repository slug: novel-knight-of-ten-thousand-paths
Genre: simulation fantasy / medieval
Male lead: A disgraced knight who enters persistent complete simulations
Length target: 900 chapters across 18 volumes
Relationship policy: **Decided — one slow-burn relationship.** Aren Kest and Mara Vey, developed through contested work, secrets, and the right to refuse each other. No harem structure.
Power rule: Growth must be earned through practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. The lead must not become instantly overpowered.

## Premise

A knight can enter complete magical worlds whose conscious lives and consequences persist after he leaves them. He cannot reset them, own their people, or use their history as a disposable answer.

## Ending

He builds a world that can exist without being managed by him. At the end of the series he is the last person who could open a path alone, and he gives that away, because a road that needs one keeper is the failure the Gatehouse was built to prevent.

- `bible/themes.md` — authority, consent, grief, infrastructure, memory, and the thematic answer.
- `bible/terminology.md` — calendar, currency, measure, custody titles, names, and language conventions.
- `outline/series.md` — the full 18-volume arc, reveal ladder, knowledge control, antagonist ladder, power stages, relationship milestones, and the time ledger.
- `outline/ending.md` — the final volume in detail, the setup-to-payoff map, mystery resolution, and the planned final image.

`outline/volume-01.md` and `outline/batches/` are produced by the next phase, not by bootstrap.
