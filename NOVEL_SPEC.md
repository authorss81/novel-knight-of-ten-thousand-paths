# Novel Specification

Title: The Knight of Ten Thousand Paths
Repository slug: novel-knight-of-ten-thousand-paths
Genre: simulation fantasy / medieval
Male lead: A disgraced knight who enters persistent complete simulations
Length target: 900 chapters
Relationship policy: One slow-burn relationship or no romance, decided in the bible phase.
Power rule: Growth must be earned through practice, cost, failure, relationships, and changed decisions. The lead must not become instantly overpowered unless the approved genre premise requires it.

## Premise
A knight can enter complete magical worlds whose conscious lives and consequences persist after he leaves them.

## Ending
He builds a world that can exist without being managed by him.
