## 0. The standing warnings, because a close is the phase that most trusts the figures it inherits

**First, a collision check that prints 0 without the set size beside it is no result.** Four blocks of this volume shipped real collisions under a parser that could not read a hyphenated pence total, because the house form of a footed total spells most of its totals hyphenated and a parser that drops them reads a quarter of the set. Extract hyphen-tolerantly, accept twenty-one and one hundred and sixty-one alike, and print the hyphenated and unhyphenated match counts beside the total match count every time. The set over the five hundred and ninety chapters before Batch 0005 is 144 distinct house-form totals; Batch 0005's ten totals — 145, 148, 153, 155, 170, 175, 179, 186, 188 and 193 — collide with none of them. A second independent extractor over the same chapters returns its own set; print both.

**Second, a coda counter built on one phrase misses the claims built on the others, and a script that cannot count its own target is a blind figure.** This volume's codas enumerate their figures in several forms, and one block's counter matched only one form and reported twenty-four claims clean while missing the one that was wrong. Count every enumeration by hand item by item against the list it describes, move the numeral and never the list, and cut an unsupported item rather than counting it. The Batch 0005 codas foot as shipped; verify them rather than trusting them.

**Third, the cheapest defect is a figure that is correct for a different day, and no calendar instrument can see it.** A calendar check asks whether a date is right and not whether a duration is right for the day the chapter is set on. Derive every interval from the day number the chapter is set on, and check it against the chapter it recalls. This volume's standing shape questions — a Sunday of ordinary work on a slope, and a five-sitting year turning mid-week — are recorded at `state/open-threads.md` 394 and are not calendar errors; do not quietly re-day them.

**Fourth, no prompt written from here hands a writer a sentence in the author's register to put on a page.** A prompt may name a failure in its own words; it may not hand the words over. If a chapter must be told something about persons standing near other persons, the chapter says it in a character's mouth or in the room's own idiom, and the prompt says only that the rule exists. A rule about register cannot be enforced by a token count.

## 1. What this close is, and what it is not

**It is a valley of terraced fruit above a river, four days and about nine of hard going from the nearest made road, where nothing was decided for twenty-two years because the people who stopped deciding put the reason in the ground, and where a man of thirty-two with a limp was asked to certify it as the one road that is stable and refused, and where the valley then reopened grief, argument and choice on purpose and accepted the instability, at the cost of about nine households' ground for a season.** It is a finding with three names on a slate, four sets answering yes-no-yes-no, nine days and a morning counted by a woman with a stick, a ditch two paces wide cut by one, cloths coming down uncounted, a person who can be refused and is on no roll, a delegate whose first written act refuses thanks, a hearing that stands with no bench, a fourth head still empty, a floor washed by somebody nobody knows, and a half leaf still face down. It is a woman of about twenty-nine, two hundred miles off, saying that a beautiful place is still an owned place.

**It is not a villain's valley and nobody in it is wicked. It is not a settlement, not a reconciliation and not a reunion, and it is not a new office and not a new order and not a new mechanism and not a new enemy, and it did not name the antagonist in any of its fifty chapters and did not say whose plan the letter is.** It did not fill the fourth head, turn the half leaf, lift the bar, count the two in three, or thank anybody. It printed no System panel and no stage, ledger or qualification word. It spent two of five names and left three unspent. It did not resolve the three words for one lane. It did not cut the twenty-second notch and did not send anybody to that coast. It did not reopen the shut road. It did not restore a city, and the city's three protected numerals are at zero for the whole volume.
