## 1. What this block is, and the one-sentence argument

**`outline/volume-12.md` §8 Block 2: the road opens, the protagonist crosses, a local protagonist with a want that is not his carries the volume's ordinary work, and the price is a night he cannot place and a habit that no longer fits.**

**The argument in one sentence: a question written by a person under the fourth head of a leaf, carried home by a person who was not sent, comes back with a person in it, and what is in it is not a name for a road and not a promise of peace and not a list — it is a woman saying that nobody in her valley has been asked anything in twenty-two years and that she is asking on her own account, which is the first time in this manuscript that a road has answered instead of being described.**
