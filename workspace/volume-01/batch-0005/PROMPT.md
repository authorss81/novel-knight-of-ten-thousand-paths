### Chapter 0041

**POV:** Aren. **Time/place:** the twenty-sixth of Longlight, Merefen: the Old Toll abutment, the yard, the road.

**Goal:** Make the middle of the year happen. There is a date, there is a crossing, there is a third attempt, and there is no seat.

**Resistance:** The date is four days after he decided in Chapter 40 not to spend the crossing, and he has not changed his mind and is not going to. The seat has been used twice. **The town has put four things in a creditor's roll that are not tolls and one line in a yard's book that says a paper exists.** He is instructed not to have contact, and the instruction requires no court. And on the Wednesday morning, at the lowest water, **two men from the guild's own bench go up to the Old Toll abutment to clear the moss out of the swept rectangle and the bed of the stone, because the bench has voted to keep the crossing and that is what keeping a crossing means in a dry month.** They are four hundred yards from a seat with two used discs in it and they do not know he is on the bridge.

**Information revealed:** that the fourth of Greenmonth's finding and the fifteenth of Longlight's minute and the twenty-first's report are in one chain and a man can be shown all three; that a bench that has voted to keep a crossing starts by taking a bell-shaped hole out of a parapet; that a man who has been four months without work he is needed for is not rested and is not ready.

**Action/decision:** He goes out. Not in the storm, not at the ninth hour on an anniversary, **in daylight, on a working morning, with a tool bag, because there is a date and a bench and a woman with a book.** He tells two people where he is going and in what words, and one of them writes it down. And he takes the bar, because it is the only object he has ever carried into Bellweather-Living and it is the only thing in this county that knows the trade.

**Emotional change:** the third crossing is not a rescue and he has stopped trying to make it one; what he is going out for is a date.

**Resource change:** the third of three, and a bar, and a sentence in a day-book in Merefen that he will not be there to defend.

**Ending type:** decision. **Pull:** the air off his own sill on a dry Wednesday afternoon, and somebody four hundred yards away knocking moss out of a rectangle with a cold chisel and not knowing.
