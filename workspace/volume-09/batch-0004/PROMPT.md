## 1. The block in one paragraph

**The last person who kept a two-room store in a yard that has been clear for a circuit died in a winter, and nobody appointed anybody after her, and the office that could appoint her does not know the building exists.** Four men arrive with a crowbar and a warrant, and there is no lock, because there has not been a lock on it since before the woman died, and they take about forty sheets, and they write down what they took, and the receipt is accurate. **A man of about thirty-four with a sheet is on the strength of a county's own road and is one of the four, and he is not a villain, and he is the same man who invented a place, a figure and a trade for a road in Block 3, and the volume never says whether the two facts are connected and must never say it.** And the thing that happens is not the taking: it is that **eleven people in eleven places, none of whom has met any of the other ten, are connected well enough that the taking is visible in nine counties inside a fortnight, and that is the first time a thing has been done in this book by people who had never met.**

## 3. On measuring this block, and why this prompt is quiet
