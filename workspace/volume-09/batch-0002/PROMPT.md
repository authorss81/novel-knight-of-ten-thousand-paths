## 1. The block in one paragraph

## 3. On measuring this block, and why this prompt is quiet

1. **There is no target for any count of any device in this prompt, and there is no band.** You are not asked to hit a rate for *about*, a count for *the whole of*, a count for the reporting clause, a count for rules, or a table of words to avoid. **The last volume wrote four blocks to a metrics table and every chapter landed inside every target, and the fifth block, written after the quotas were lifted, came in at a rate a quota would have forbidden and found sixteen defects by reading that four block-scoped passes with quotas had not.** A number should matter because of what it changes in the story, not because the number exists.
2. **The narrator's reporting frame is retired outright, with a hard stop and not a target.** *Have said since that*, *have worked out since that*, *have been saying since that*, and the *about four people have worked out since that* construction, are gone. **Do not convert one form into another form.** A batch that lifted its quotas is the batch that found the repairs.
3. **Punctuation.** The first ten chapters of this volume have no question mark, no exclamation mark and no semicolon, and one em dash, and that is the house convention and it is not a quota. **A question asked aloud is set with a full stop inside the quotation.** Use a semicolon, a dash or an exclamation mark if a sentence wants one. **There is no zero-punctuation mandate and there is no zero-punctuation target.**
4. **Sentences are yours.** Vary on purpose. Some sentences under ten words, some over thirty, a paragraph of two sentences next to a paragraph of five.
5. **Paragraphs are yours, and the bold dateline frame is yours to vary.** The frame is a real convention and it is the source of every long frame-run in the duplication measurement. **Vary the hour-lists and the location clauses across the ten datelines.**
6. **The bare adverb and the participial tail.** The fillers this project watches are the sentence that ends *and not one of the two of them has ever said a word about it*, the clause that ends *and that is the whole of what anybody asked for*, and the paragraph that closes *and nobody in that town knows*. Those are not banned and they are sometimes exactly right. **They are not right forty times, and the first block of this volume used the last of them once in ten chapters and that is roughly the rate.**
7. **What is still measured, because it is about whether the book is true and not about how it sounds, and it is a short list.** The calendar. The money. The names. The barred proper nouns. The antagonist. The refusal counts. The speech-length figures. The duplication. And a person reading the files at the end. **That list is the whole of it, every item on it is a fact about the world, and none of them is a target.**
