## 3. On measuring this block, and why this prompt is quieter than the last four

**The last four prompts each set a numeric target for a style device — a rate for the word *about*, a count for the phrase *the whole of*, a count for *about four people*, a count for the narrator's reporting clause, a count for the `---` rules, a table of filler words to avoid, and a mandate that semicolons, dashes, exclamation marks and question marks be zero.** **A review of Block 0004 found that the ten chapters landed inside every one of those targets, that all ten fell inside a two-and-a-half-point band on a twelve-point scale, and that nobody in twenty-four thousand words asks a question aloud, and it concluded that the writer had been steering to a ledger rather than telling a story, and that conclusion was correct.** **A number should matter because of what it changes in the story, not because the number exists.** So:

1. **There is no target for any count of any device in this prompt, and there is no band.** You are not asked to hit a rate for *about*, a count for *the whole of*, a count for the reporting clause, a count for rules, or a table of words to avoid. **If you want to know what the previous four blocks did with these, the numbers are in the state files, and reading them is allowed and copying them is not what this block is for.**
2. **The narrator's reporting frame is retired outright, with a hard stop and not a target.** *Have said since that*, *have worked out since that*, *have been saying since that*, and the *about four people have worked out since that* construction, are gone. **Volume 08 stands at 220 of the first kind across forty chapters and 66 of the second, and that is a habit and not a style, and it carries nothing a scene cannot carry in dialogue or in action.** When a bystander has worked something out, put it in somebody's mouth, or let the reader have it without the frame, or cut it. **Do not convert one form into another form. That was tried in Block 0003, forty conversions took eight hundred and four words out of the block, and the block went under its own floor.**
3. **Punctuation is yours.** There is no zero-punctuation mandate. **Ask a question aloud somewhere. A world in which nobody asks a question for fifty chapters is not restrained, it is unnarratable.** Use a semicolon, a dash or an exclamation mark if the sentence wants one.
4. **Sentences are yours.** The block before this one had a mean sentence of about fifty-six words and more than half its sentences over forty. **That is a page a reader stops reading.** Vary it on purpose: some sentences under ten words, some over thirty, and a paragraph of two sentences next to a paragraph of five.
5. **Paragraphs are yours, and the bold dateline frame is yours to vary.** The volume's dateline frame is a real convention and it is a good one, and it is also the source of every long frame-run in the duplication measurement. **Vary the hour-lists and the location clauses across the ten datelines. Block 0003 beat Block 0001's sixty-three-word frame run that way and a reviewer cannot do it for you.**
6. **The bare adverb and the participial tail.** The filler this project watches is not `nodded` and `plain` and `steady`, which are already at zero across four blocks and will stay at zero because they are not words anybody reaches for. **What has been creeping in instead is the sentence that ends *and not one of the two of them has ever said a word about it*, and the clause that ends *and that is the whole of what anybody asked for*, and the paragraph that closes *and nobody in that town knows*.** Those are not banned and they are sometimes exactly right. **They are not right forty times.**
7. **What is still measured, because it is about whether the book is true and not about how it sounds, and it is a short list.** The calendar. The money. The names. The barred proper nouns. The antagonist. The refusal counts. The speech-length figures. The duplication. And a person reading the files at the end. **That list is the whole of it, and every item on it is a fact about the world, and none of them is a target.**
