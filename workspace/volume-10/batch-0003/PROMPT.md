# Volume 10, Batch 0003 — Chapters 471 to 480

## 0. What this prompt is

**Chapters 471 to 480, Block 3 of Volume 10, *The Sea of Returning Roads*. This is the block where the volume's own argument turns against itself. The coast has a road that works and cannot say who is responsible for it. The main world has a trade route that works and cannot say what it costs anybody. It arrives as arithmetic.**

**Read `outline/volume-10.md` whole first; §8 Block 3 and §9 and §10 and §12 and §13 and §14 and §15 and §17 are this block's authority and §13's name budget is binding on every chapter in it, and there is one slot left in it. **The manuscript at the moment this block opens is 1,420,010 words in four hundred and seventy chapters, counted file by file on the chapter files with the title line included. Count each of your ten new files on its own and add. Do not concatenate and count the stream. And if any figure in this prompt does not reproduce when you count it, the figure is the error and not the count, and this prompt prints no per-chapter figures, no device rates, no market-day list and no target for any count, and that is deliberate, and §3 says why in the only place you need to hear it. **The 1,420,010 is a recount: the figure this prompt first printed was 1,419,702 and it did not reproduce, and a count that has to be corrected by the person who inherits it is a count nobody can check, including the person who wrote it.**

**The counting basis is settled and a writer who does not use it will get a wrong total and not know it. A word count is a plain whitespace split of the file with the title line included. **This prompt was written by the phase that wrote Batch 0002 and it is deliberately quiet. The quotas are gone and they are not coming back. What is left is the world, and this block is the middle of a volume whose last page is a road with a man on it.**

## 3. On measuring this block, and why this prompt is quiet

1. **There is no numeric target for any count of any device in this prompt, and there is no band, and the reason is the reason it has always been and not an excuse.** You are not asked to hit a rate for *the whole of*, a count for the reporting clause, a count for rules, or a table of words to avoid. **Batch 0002 found twenty-two defects in ten chapters by reading and not by measuring, and the two instruments that found anything were the bold-parity sweep and the curly-quotation balance run across a block boundary.** A number should matter because of what it changes in the story, not because the number exists. **Items 3, 4 and 5 of this section are the exception that proves the rule and they are craft, not counting: a chapter with no dialogue in it, a protagonist with no name on the page, and a sentence where a man has become an estimate of a man are three defects that no number in this prompt would ever have found, and all three shipped.**
2. **The narrator's reporting frame is retired outright, with a hard stop and not a target.** *Have said since that*, *Have worked out since that*, *Have been saying since that*, and the *about four people have worked out since that* construction, are gone. **Do not convert one form into another form.** A self-reference sweep must test the bare noun: *this volume*, *this book*, *this chapter*, *this block*, *this batch*, *this manuscript*, *the volume*, *the chapter*, *the block*, *the batch*, *thirty chapters* and *about four hundred chapters* are all at zero across four hundred and seventy chapters and stay at zero. **In Batch 0002 eleven occurrences of the four-word sequence *have said since that* were written into nine new files and into one completed chapter of the previous block, and all eleven were removed by reading and not by a sweep, because a sweep tests the narrator's subject and the sequence is identical either way. **And this block converted the frame rather than removing it: it wrote *have said since then that*, *said since then that*, *has said since then that* and *have worked out since then* twenty-six times across nine of its ten files, which is the conversion this item forbids, and it did it with the rule standing in front of it.
