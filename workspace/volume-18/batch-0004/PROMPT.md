## 4. What this part must contain, one card a chapter

**EVERY CARD HAS A GOAL, A RESISTANCE, A CHANGE AND AN ENDING, AND A WRITER WHO CANNOT NAME ALL FOUR FOR A PAGE HAS A PAGE THAT IS NOT READY. Every page also carries at least one physical action of the protagonist's that is irreversible, and no two consecutive pages in this part share a room.**

1. **0881 · he does not refute the argument with certainty.** *Goal:* get two days out of a man who will not be argued out of anything. *Resistance:* the argument does not need to be refuted, it needs to be answered, and the only answer available is not available yet. *Change:* he stops trying to win the argument and starts carrying it. *Ending:* on him handing the argument itself to somebody who is going to have to hold it, in public.

5. **0885 · an assembly that accepts representatives.** *Goal:* get one body to take a seat it did not ask for. *Resistance:* a seat is a thing a body can be asked to fill and this one will be asked, and accepting it looks like consent to everything that body has ever refused. *Change:* representatives are admitted with a standing to refuse and with the refusal written down. *Ending:* on the first refusal made in that room and on it being recorded as a thing the room did.
