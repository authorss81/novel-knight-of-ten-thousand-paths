## 4. What this part must contain, one card a chapter

**EVERY CARD HAS A GOAL, A RESISTANCE, A CHANGE AND AN ENDING, AND A WRITER WHO CANNOT NAME ALL FOUR FOR A PAGE HAS A PAGE THAT IS NOT READY. Every page also carries at least one physical action of the protagonist's that is irreversible, and no two consecutive pages in this part share a room.**

1. **0871 · the terms on which a place may refuse contact.** *Goal:* get one page written that a person can refuse and that does not turn a refusal into a closure. *Resistance:* a procedure that treats a path refusing contact as an enemy is the only procedure three provinces have, and the person who has to draft it is a woman with a court mandate and no army and no soldier. *Change:* a refusal acquires a form and the form puts the burden of showing that a road should close on the person proposing it. *Ending:* on the form being given to a community that has not asked for it, and on nobody at the boards signing anything.
2. **0872 · the first anchor moved to a person that place chose.** *Goal:* move one more anchor without the middle. *Resistance:* the second place that chooses anybody chooses somebody the first place's form would not have admitted, and the two choices do not agree about what a keeper is. *Change:* two live routes have keepers chosen by their own places and the two places have begun to argue with each other about it in public. *Ending:* **the thirty-second first of a month arriving at those boards with nothing standing at it.**
3. **0873 · a woman who keeps records and the thing she will not do for him.** *Goal:* get one claim of his into the record as true. *Resistance:* she will not enter it and she is right, and the row stays empty with a reason beside it, and about four of the people who want it entered are people he likes. *Change:* a claim of his about himself stands in the record as a claim, and that is the second empty row inside a month. *Ending:* on the reason written beside it in six words, and on him not arguing.
4. **0874 · the steward of Bellweather-Living and a condition in her community's own hand.** *Goal:* get a community's own condition into the paper before anything else goes in. *Resistance:* she will not speak for the delegation and she is sent by it, and every word she says is read as the delegation's, and she has not corrected that once. *Change:* a guarantee that survives whoever signs it is written into the paper in the community's own words. *Ending:* on her reading her own condition aloud and nobody in the room correcting her for it. **One name is spent on this page: `Bryenna Faal`.**
5. **0875 · the selector records unsealed.** *Goal:* get the records at the bottom of that stair open. *Resistance:* the man in the lower archive does not come out to stop them and does not have to, because what is in the records is enough. *Change:* the records are open and the working is described by the people who built it and the man in the archive says one thing. *Ending:* on him saying it, once, and it being the better of one argument and not all of it.
6. **0876 · the whole of how it works, shown by the people who built it.** *Goal:* learn it from the people who made it rather than from the man using it. *Resistance:* the people who built it are three trades and two of them will not speak to each other and one of them is sixty-eight and has not left her county. *Change:* he learns the whole of it and two of the three tell him things he did not know about his own road. *Ending:* on two of the three refusing to be in the same room as each other.
7. **0877 · the evidence, which is real.** *Goal:* get rid of the argument by answering it. *Resistance:* **he cannot get rid of it, because the evidence is good and it is against him, being three regions that lost their own anchors the first time a path was closed and cannot show what they were since.** *Change:* he stops trying to explain it away and says out loud, before other people, that the risk is real. *Ending:* on him being asked whether the paper he is holding open makes it more likely or less, and answering it in one sentence.
8. **0878 · a glasswright's bench and a rule published.** *Goal:* get the rules for making anchor-glass published in full. *Resistance:* two cantons will not publish two of the four ways a batch can be wrong, and the woman who wrote the four down is the assessor and she has been overruled from a distance for nine years. *Change:* the four ways are published with two of them attributed. *Ending:* **the thirty-third first of a month, and a bench with four mistakes on it and nobody's name on any of the four.**
9. **0879 · the coast and a record of sea routes.** *Goal:* get one record held in two hands. *Resistance:* one man has kept it in one hand for nineteen years and has once refused a copy to a man he liked and tells the year that he refused for a good reason and cannot produce it. *Change:* the record is shared and the sharing is the point rather than the loss. *Ending:* on the copy going to a person rather than to a place, addressed to a name, carried in the ordinary post.
10. **0880 · the list of what is left and who is standing on each piece of it.** *Goal:* write down what remains and who holds it. *Resistance:* a list of nine hundred crossings and eight fragments and two keepers gone and one narrow path anchor out of use for good is not a list anybody can finish, and the people who have to read it are the ones standing on it. *Change:* the list is finished enough to be argued with and is published with the gaps in it printed as gaps. *Ending:* on one line of it that has no keeper on it yet and is not going to get one this year.
