## 4. What this part must contain, one card a chapter

**EVERY CARD HAS A GOAL, A RESISTANCE, A CHANGE AND AN ENDING, AND A WRITER WHO CANNOT NAME ALL FOUR FOR A PAGE HAS A PAGE THAT IS NOT READY. Every page also carries at least one physical action of the protagonist's that is irreversible, and no two consecutive pages in this part share a room.**

1. **0861 · the vote called in three places at once.** *Goal:* get one question put to three rooms on the same morning so that nobody can say the answer was arranged. *Resistance:* three places have three different words for the same thing, the rooms are nine days apart by road, and the copies that travel are copies. *Change:* the question is put and three answers come back and two of them are close enough to be counted as one. *Ending:* on the fourth answer arriving from a place that was not one of the three.
