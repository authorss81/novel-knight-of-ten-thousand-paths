# SYNOPSIS PHASE

The manuscript is finished. Write ONE file, `state/synopsis.md`, and create no phase after it.

Read `NOVEL_SPEC.md`, `outline/series.md`, `outline/ending.md`, `state/current.md`, and enough of the
finished chapters to be accurate about the opening situation, the central problem, the turn the
volume structure puts on the reader, and how the lead actually lives at the end.

`state/synopsis.md` must contain exactly these two headings and nothing else:

## Back cover

Sixty to one hundred and twenty words, the blurb a reader sees before buying. It states the promise of
the book and the situation it happens in. It must not spoil the ending, name later volumes, or reveal
any resolution. No exclamation marks, no "in a world where", no rhetorical question.

## Synopsis

Two hundred and fifty to four hundred and fifty words. It gives the premise, the lead, the central
pressure, the kind of opposition the book actually uses, and what the reader gets to watch over the
length. It may name the final movement's subject, but it must not narrate the resolution.

Write plain prose. Do not edit chapters, outlines, bibles, `NOVEL_SPEC.md`, any controller file, or
any existing state file other than creating this one.
