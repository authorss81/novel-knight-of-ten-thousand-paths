## The ten chapter cards

**Chapter 0661 — the batch's promise.** Days 2095 to about 2100. **The delegation is four days of road away and a month has gone by, and nobody here can go and ask her what she said to about nine hundred people, and the arrangement that existed in Mudmonth consisted of a copyist who would read out anything written and sent to that room and it would go nowhere else.** Open in the market town. **Establish that the question she was sent to ask has been answered twice and neither answer was it, and that the badge-man's answer was the truest thing anybody had said and is still not an answer to it.** Somewhere on the page: **a first hand report that the copyist has had something, and it did not come from the coast road.** That is your engine. Nobody thanks anybody.

**Chapter 0662 — the small road writes.** **The small road, which keeps its count in nine open crossings and four shut weeks in two years in a boy of about eleven, sends a letter for the first time, and the letter is about a horse.** It comes by a hand nobody in nine counties knows and the copyist recognises the hand and does not say where she has seen it. **The letter asks for nothing.** It says that the column's forward party has taken a horse off the mile and a half that stops being made, and that the horse belonged to nobody and that a horse off that lane is a horse off about nine households, and it ends in the only sentence in it that matters. **Do not count the small road's people. Do not print a figure for it. Do not convert its count into days.** The person who wrote it must want something that is not the horse and must say what it is.

**Chapter 0663 — the two counties.** **The instrument in a second county, and the same thing happening, and the difference being that in the second county there is a woman who will be named and she has been a clerk for about six years and she drafts the thing and she has a want that is unrelated to Aren, and the want is that nothing she drafts should ever be obeyed without somebody choosing to obey it.** She is honest about what she has done and she is not a villain and she is not safe. **Her hands are the chapter.**

**Chapter 0664 — the woman who is not a knight.** **The woman of about thirty-six who keeps a road in the county where a list of names was burned returns, and what she has is not a complaint and not an argument and is a third thing, and the badge-man asks her the question nobody has asked anybody, which is whether anybody has ever offered her a job.** The answer is no and it costs her to say it out loud. **Put the two holes in the arrangement in the open again, both of them, and have neither of them called small, and have somebody who is not the protagonist name the second one.** Nobody thanks her.

**Chapter 0665 — the field, and the labour, and the mid-batch turn.** **The column is on the made road and is working, and the middle of this block is a scene of ordinary work in bad weather at a place where about nine people keep a road that nobody has written down.** **This is an action volume and this is the chapter where it is one.** A culvert, a drain, a bank, a raised cart, cold, hands, and a man who does not want to be helped and is helped. **The turn is this: somebody from the column asks the road's own people for a barrow, and gets it, and does not pay for it, and does not think it needs paying for, and about four people in that lane understand in about four seconds what is about to happen to this county, and nobody in that lane says anything.**

**Chapter 0666 — what the four marks were for, in the wrong county.** **Take the fourth-mark logic one step and do not repeat it.** Not a level, not a tide line, and not a survey. **A man puts something on a gate because somebody told him to, and what he has put there is a thing that will let a person come back in about nine years and know that a thing was asked of this gate.** The person who put it there is a person of about thirty-one with a good opinion of his own handwriting and he does it correctly and he does it quickly and about nine people watch and about four of them understand it and about five do not. **Nobody says the word. That is the chapter.**

**Chapter 0667 — the cost of the block.** **The horse is not got back, or something else of equal weight is not got back, and it is a person and not a horse, and nobody made a decision about it and about nine people had a reason.** **Do not kill anybody for shock. The absence is on the page in the way this manuscript does absence: a road-keeper who is not at something, and a carter who says nothing has come from the end of it, and a badge-man who asks once and does not ask twice.** **AND THE QUIET ROAD FROM VOLUME 13 IS NOT THIS ROAD AND MUST NOT BE NAMED, VISITED, SENT TO, OR RESOLVED. Read `state/open-threads.md` item 525 again if you are tempted.** The absence here must be a different road and must not be described in a way that could be confused with that one.

**Chapter 0668 — the road-crew's answer.** **The recovery beat, and it is long, and it is ordinary labour on made road in weather, and it is done by the people who keep the road, and the money is counted out loud at the boards and one household puts in nothing and is not argued with, and nobody is thanked.** The fourth course of stone on the bank at the town's own gate with the mallet on top of it is still lying there and **nobody picks the mallet up and no chapter schedules anybody picking it up and no chapter lays the course.**

**Chapter 0669 — the question nobody in nine counties can answer yet.** **A person from the ferry city, not the one who wrote in Block 0001, and not a delegation.** One letter, or one messenger, or one thing said out loud in a street by somebody who has come a long way and is going straight back. **It must not be an army and it must not be a refusal to be an army.** It is a third thing and it is worse for the county than either, and the protagonist is not told what it is in time to act on it. **Nobody has asked the paths anything and the second half of the volume is going to be about somebody deciding to ask them.**

**Chapter 0670 — the batch's climax and its cost.** **Halvard Rees takes the works, or he does not, and the block ends with something physical happening and something in the block's engine getting worse rather than better.** The captain is competent and honest and defends his own instrument and **his difficulty is now real and is on the page, and it is that a paper that compels an answer needs somebody in the county to answer, and nine counties has nobody, and the reason it has nobody is a rule in nine hands that says a thing with a name at the top of it is obeyed until somebody shows it false.** **And the batch ends on a decision and not a summary:** somebody local, on the page, by name or by age and trade, decides to answer the question. **Nobody thanks anybody and the last line is a thing that happens.**

- **Zero em dashes. Zero en dashes. Zero semicolons. Zero exclamation marks. Zero italic single asterisks. Zero straight double quotes. Straight apostrophes only, never curly.**
