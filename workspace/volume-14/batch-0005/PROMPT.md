## The ten chapter cards

**Chapter 0691 — the block's promise, and the coin spent without being asked.** Days 2257 to about 2263. **The coin that Block 0004 named is spent, and nobody asked for it and nobody called it, and about nine people turn up at nine gates over nine days without any two of them knowing about the other eight.** A market morning in the market town, and then the first crossing held, and the shape of how nine counties holds nine crossings with no head: **not a muster, not a list, not a watch, not a rota, and not a name at the top of a thing.** The badge-man says the whole of the procedure out loud in a market street and it is nine words, or about nine, and it is that nobody says anything to anybody. **Chapter 0692 — the ferry city, and the question of asking it, and the decision not to ask.** A non-market day, the room over the weigh-house shut behind its own door. **The question that a woman of about thirty-eight asked in a market street in Fallowmonth and that nobody was ever able to establish who asked, which is whether to ask a city four hundred miles off, has come back round, because the city said in Fallowmonth that it will name the place if anybody in nine counties asks it.** Somebody must decide whether to ask, and **the decision is not the protagonist's and the decision is no**, and the reason is given by a person who has standing in their own trade and no standing in this: **a city that has said it will name the place has put its name to nothing, and the day anybody asks it, it is in the taking, and the reason nine counties has not been able to refuse a thing a city says about it is that nobody in nine counties has ever had to, and the only way to keep not having to is not to ask.** The question of who sent anybody is asked and is not answered. Nobody thanks anybody and the city is not named and nobody is sent.

**Chapter 0693 — the serjeant, the sheet, and one road read out of a book.** A market morning. **A serjeant of about thirty-eight arrives somewhere with a sheet and about four men, on a three-day interval, with a copy of a line of a warrant and not the warrant, and reads a road out of a book, and the road is four miles long and belongs to about nine households and to a man of about fifty-one who owns a field on the low side of a cut.** **Nobody in nine counties can say that road is anybody's, including the nine households, and that is the whole of why it holds, and nobody in nine counties has worked that out in advance and a reader must not be told it in a sentence.** Have the local people arrive one at a time, on their own errands, for their own reasons, and be there, and the badge-man say one thing about it afterward. **Nobody thanks anybody and nobody gives the serjeant an answer and the serjeant is competent and honest and is not called either.**

**Chapter 0694 — the cull, and what a cull is in this world, in a chapter of its own.** A non-market day. **A written order, lawful, sealed, with a name at the top of it, requiring a stated figure to be given up at a crossing, and the figure is people, and the giving-up is not a punishment and is not an arrest and is a number with a date on it, and a district that does not deliver it delivers it in a different way.** Write the order's shape, its arithmetic, and the ordinary way it is carried out at a gate, in about four hundred words, without a lecture and without a set piece. **Nobody is killed in this chapter and nobody is told anything and the order is obeyed, or is obeyed in part, by people who have no standing to refuse it and no clause in about nine hands that covers it.** A local person says the one true thing about a figure with a date on it, out loud, in the open, and it is that a number is a thing nine counties has never been able to answer and this one can be answered on Friday.
